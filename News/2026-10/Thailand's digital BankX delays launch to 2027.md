# Thailand’s digital BankX delays launch for second time

The WeBank-backed new entrant was supposed to launch in June but that has slipped to 2027

byKimberley Long

Share the article

[](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?text=https://www.thebanker.com/content/0ee41180-aed0-4256-b4f9-609ff67af754)[](https://www.thebanker.com/content/0ee41180-aed0-4256-b4f9-609ff67af754#)[](https://www.thebanker.com/content/0ee41180-aed0-4256-b4f9-609ff67af754#)

[](https://www.thebanker.com/content/0ee41180-aed0-4256-b4f9-609ff67af754#)

SCBX, KakaoBank and WeBank are partners in Thailand’s delayed BankX venture © Chalinee Thirasupa/Bloomberg

Thailand’s BankX has pushed back its launch for a second time and is now looking to go live in 2027.

The digital bank, a joint operation between Thailand’s SCBX Bank, South Korea’s KakaoBank and China’s WeBank is now looking at a go-live date next year, according to two sources with knowledge of the matter.
