## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened (dated).** On **15 October 2026**, at the anniversary forum-congress of the "Delovaya Rossiya" (Business Russia) association, President Vladimir Putin instructed Bank of Russia Chair Elvira Nabiullina and the government (Cabinet) to work out ("prorabotat") the creation of an **"ecosystem of long money"** — a system of **growth funds / direct-investment funds** that would channel long-term domestic capital into companies past the early stage (working businesses not yet ready for IPO). The idea was pitched at the forum by HeadHunter deputy CEO Maria Komarova; Putin endorsed it. The target capital sources are money currently sitting in deposits and bonds held by pension funds (NPFs), insurers, banks and households — an explicit attempt to find a **domestic replacement for the foreign capital** that left after 2022. ([Vedomosti summary of Putin's statements](https://www.vedomosti.ru/economics/articles/2026/10/15/1237466-glavnie-zayavleniya-putina), [1prime](https://1prime.ru/20261015/putin-874297389.html), [ABN](https://abn.agency/2026/10/15/putin-podderzhal-sozdanie-fondov-rosta-i-pryamyh-investiczij-v-rossii/))

**[1] Who must act / mechanism.** Two addressees: **Bank of Russia** (regulation) and the **government/Cabinet** (tax incentives, legal framework). Nabiullina set out the CBR's stance and three conditions:
- ⚪ **Pension/insurance money must not be seed capital** — NPF and insurance-fund money can be a *supplementary* resource, but initiative and the bulk of investment must come from private investors. ([1prime/Nabiullina](https://1prime.ru/20261015/nabiullina-874298455.html), [Rambler](https://finance.rambler.ru/economics/57241815-nabiullina-prizvala-ostorozhno-privlekat-pensionnye-nakopleniya-v-investitsionnye-fondy/))
- ⚪ **Funds must be more liquid than ordinary private-company equity and structured as an exchange-traded product**, so investors can exit (sell units) before the fund's term ends.
- ⚪ Such funds should help investee companies **build reporting and prepare for IPO** (a pipeline into public markets).

The CBR also said it is ready to **adjust NPF regulation** to give an impulse to the sector ([1prime](https://1prime.ru/20261015/nabiullina-874301948.html)). On the government side, Putin agreed that a **deferred investment tax deduction** ("otlozhennyi investitsionnyi nalogovyi vychet") could be a real support tool and told the Cabinet to work out its parameters (volumes, instruments). Putin stressed there is **no established legal/normative base** for such funds yet — rules must be written first — and demanded **"reinforced-concrete" (iron-clad) guarantees** for citizens' pension savings. ([RG](https://rg.ru/2026/10/15/investicii-i-posledstviia-atak-o-chem-putin-govoril-na-vstreche-s-biznesom.html), [PNP](https://www.pnp.ru/economics/putin-potreboval-nadezhnykh-garantiy-sokhrannosti-pensionnykh-fondov.html))

**[2] Deadline / size.** No hard deadline or ruble figure attached to this specific instruction — it is a "work it out" order, not a decree with dates. Size/instruments are explicitly left for the Cabinet and CBR to define.

**[3] Why it matters (context & prior "long money" moves).** This sits under Putin's Feb-2024 directive to **double stock-market capitalization to ~66% of GDP by 2030**. In early Oct 2026 the CBR publicly admitted that target is **unreachable** — capitalization was ~26-27% of GDP, and 7-10 trn rub of new share issuance over five years would be needed vs ~1 trn rub over the prior decade. ([Vedomosti](https://www.vedomosti.ru/investments/news/2026/10/08/1235566-kapitalizatsiya-ne-smozhet), [The Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/10/09/russias-central-bank-says-putins-stock-market-target-is-out-of-reach-a93931)) The growth-fund push is the authorities' answer: build a pre-IPO funding layer domestically. It extends a run of "soft mobilization of private savings" initiatives — see [[Финтехно Russia approves new private-savings mobilization law]] (housing-savings accounts, from 2027) and the long-term savings program (ПДС) in [[Russia's long-term savings program reignites pension-fund competition]], where NPF Sberbank captured >80% of switchers. It also complements the CBR's IPO-quality drive in [[Bank of Russia proposes 3bn ruble minimum for IPO listing tiers]] — growth funds would feed companies toward those stricter listing tiers.

**[4] Watch-list.** (a) Whether a concrete legislative framework and the deferred tax deduction actually get drafted vs staying at "study" stage; (b) how much NPF money the CBR ultimately permits into illiquid private equity despite its own cautions; (c) whether an exchange-traded liquid wrapper for private-company stakes is technically/legally feasible; (d) demand side — high key-rate deposits still out-compete risk capital.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian capital-market deepening / domestic long-term ("long money") capital formation. The structural problem is explicit: after 2022 foreign capital exited, leaving a financing gap for post-early-stage, pre-IPO private companies, and the state wants a domestic replacement. Size of the pool being targeted (household deposits + NPF/insurance reserves): the long-term savings program (PDS, launched Jan-2024) had attracted ~717bn rubles (~$8.8bn) from ~10m participants over two years — per bne/iz.ru, as of 2026 — described as "significantly below the levels needed to meet state financing goals." Moscow Exchange's own pre-IPO fund line committed ">10bn rubles" with the exchange's stake capped at ≤20% of each fund (per MOEX, Mar-2025). Structure: nascent and fragmented — a thin pre-IPO layer (MOEX Start launched mid-2024; single raises like Digital Habits at 900m rubles) sits between early-stage VC and a recovering but small IPO market (retail-driven, ~3.6m active accounts, policy rate ~21% in 2025, per bne IntelliNews). Barrier is regulatory (NPF investment limits) plus a high-rate environment that makes risk-free deposits/OFZ compete directly with growth equity. Why now: the HeadHunter proposal (deputy CEO Maria Komarova, Business Russia meeting with Putin, 15-Oct-2026) to build an "ecosystem of growth funds" as an IPO pipeline was what Putin tasked the CBR/government to work up — plus a stated goal of a "new investment cycle" to restore growth "from 2027" (Interfax). This is a presidential instruction / concept stage, NOT live regulation.

**Competitive landscape.** The sector's KPIs are AUM channelled into non-public equity, NPF equity-allocation ceiling, and the pre-IPO→IPO conversion funnel (deal count/volume). Key actors: Bank of Russia (regulator/gatekeeper of NPF rules), NPFs + insurers + banks (capital providers), Moscow Exchange (MOEX Start + a ≤20%-stake pre-IPO fund line, the de-facto incumbent infrastructure), and private VC/PE as the mandated lead investors. Recent moves: NPF equity ceiling raised from 5%→10% of a name's float, banking-asset cap cut 40%→30%, new "real-sector" investment rules (per CBR). Competing/adjacent domestic pre-IPO plays already live: [[Indie GO launches 5bn-ruble venture fund for gaming IPOs]], [[Lender Invest offers up to 14.5% via pre-IPO on SPB Exchange]], [[SPB Exchange plans crypto spot trading and settlement depositary]], and the broader state-IPO push in [[Mergers.ru Gref advises state companies on successful IPOs]]. Protagonist's position: this is state/regulator-led capital-formation policy, not a product — ahead on political backing, but behind on mechanism; the three CBR conditions (pension money as top-up not seed capital; fund must be more liquid than ordinary private-equity i.e. exchange-traded with an exit; protection of citizens' funds) are a moat against mis-selling but also a constraint on deployable scale (analysis).

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation/round/deal attached — this is a policy instruction, so trading multiples do not apply (no data). Comparable mechanisms for sizing the ambition: PDS pool ~717bn rubles / ~10m participants = ~72k rubles (~$880) average per participant (bne/iz.ru) — small-ticket retail money, not venture-scale. MOEX pre-IPO fund line >10bn rubles at ≤20% stake implies a targeted ~50bn-ruble total fund envelope if fully matched (arithmetic: 10bn / 0.20 = 50bn rubles) — still tiny vs the gap left by departed foreign capital. Internal comps as above ([[Indie GO...]] 5bn-ruble single fund; [[Lender Invest...]] pre-IPO at up to 14.5% yield). Historical precedent to weigh skeptically: Rusnano, the state's prior "finance tech scaleups" vehicle, ended in financial distress/near-default — a reason the "private money leads, pension money tops up" framing is being stressed this time (analysis).

**Risk flags.**
1. Pension-saver loss risk / political reversal: channelling NPF + PDS household money into illiquid non-public equity risks capital loss; the CBR's three conditions are precisely to contain this, but the PDS pool is already undersized vs targets — a single high-profile failure could trigger a regulatory retreat and freeze the whole ecosystem.
2. Crowding-out by a ~21% policy rate: risk-free deposits/OFZ out-yield uncertain growth equity, so voluntary long money may not show up at scale regardless of the structure — the mechanism can exist and still be unfunded.
3. State-directed-capital / precedent risk: "internal replacement for foreign capital" plus state-adjacent infrastructure (MOEX, state IPOs) risks politically-steered rather than return-driven allocation — the Rusnano precedent shows how a state scaleup-financing vehicle can impair capital.
4. Execution/vaporware risk: presidential instruction + industry proposal at concept stage, no draft NPF-regulation or fund launch yet — "announced" not "live"; what's silent = ticket sizes, loss-sharing, and who bears the exit-liquidity promise if the exchange-traded wrapper is thin.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** If delivered, this is a re-rating catalyst for the Russian pre-IPO layer — it would thicken the MOEX Start → IPO funnel and give NPFs a sanctioned growth-equity channel, benefiting incumbents like Moscow Exchange and existing pre-IPO fund sponsors (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: watch for a concrete CBR draft amendment raising/clarifying NPF non-public-equity limits AND a first exchange-traded growth fund with real NPF participation within ~6–12 months; absence of either (or a PDS inflow that stays below target) would confirm the mechanism is political signalling that stays unfunded under high rates.
