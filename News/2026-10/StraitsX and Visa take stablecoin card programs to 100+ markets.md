StraitsX Partners Visa to Expand its Reach to over 100 Markets Globally on Stablecoin-Backed Card Programs
 SINGAPORE , 15 October 2026 – StraitsX , the stablecoin-native settlement layer powering global finance, today announced that it is partnering with Visa, the world’s leader in digital payments to expand its card issuing capabilities globally to more than 100 markets. 
The milestone extends StraitsX’s card issuing infrastructure beyond its established footprint in Asia, enabling the company to support eligible card issuance programs globally and is subject to applicable regulatory, compliance and Visa requirements. This expanded capability is designed for fintechs, Digital Payment Token (DPT) service providers, wallets, and other businesses looking to build card programs that connect stablecoin balances and digital asset payment flows with Visa’s global acceptance network.
 From rapid growth in Asia to global card issuing 
The expansion follows a period of significant growth for StraitsX’s card issuing business in Asia. StraitsX reached an annualised card GTV of more than US$6 billion across its stablecoin-backed card programs.
With the partnership with Visa, StraitsX can now extend the infrastructure and experience behind this growth to businesses building card programs across global markets.
“Stablecoin-backed cards have become one of the clearest examples of how stablecoins can move beyond settlement and into everyday payments,” said Tianwei Liu, CEO and Co-Founder of StraitsX . “We have seen significant growth in this use case across Asia. Expanding our issuing capabilities globally allows us to bring the same infrastructure to businesses looking to build and scale card programs across markets.”
 Connecting stablecoins with global card acceptance 
Stablecoin-backed cards provide a practical bridge between digital value and existing payment networks, allowing businesses to extend the utility of stablecoins without requiring merchants or consumers to adopt an entirely new payment experience.
StraitsX enables fintechs, DPT service providers and wallets to build card programs where stablecoins can support the underlying funding and settlement flow, while cardholders continue to transact through familiar Visa credentials and its global acceptance network.
For businesses operating across multiple markets, this brings together stablecoin-native infrastructure and global card issuing capabilities through a single infrastructure partner.
This collaboration will also take into account the continuously evolving nature of money-laundering and terrorism financing typologies and ensure that such risks arising from this integration are properly mitigated.
“At Visa, we are focused on bringing the benefits of new payment technologies into the global financial system in a way that is secure, scalable and trusted,” said Rubail Birwadker, Global Head of Growth Products & Partnerships, Visa. Working closely with our partner, StraitsX, we are unlocking more ways to use stablecoins for real-world payment flows, while connecting them to the reach and reliability of Visa’s global network.”
 Building global payment infrastructure on stablecoin rails 
The expansion of StraitsX’s card issuing capabilities is part of a broader strategy to connect stablecoins with the financial and payment infrastructure businesses already use today.
Beyond card issuing, StraitsX provides stablecoin-native infrastructure for payments and settlement, enabling businesses to move and use digital value across markets. As new forms of commerce emerge, including payments initiated by AI agents, StraitsX is building the infrastructure to connect these new payment flows with existing financial networks.
By expanding its Visa card issuing capabilities globally, StraitsX is extending this infrastructure to businesses looking to launch and scale stablecoin-backed card programs across markets.
 About StraitsX 
StraitsX is the stablecoin-native settlement layer driving global finance, and a Major Payment Institution licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore. As the issuer of XUSD and XSGD stablecoins, StraitsX leverages the power of blockchain and stablecoin technology to drive payments interoperability while offering innovative tools for liquidity management, stablecoin integration, and cross-border transactions. Partnering with leading financial institutions, global liquidity providers, and payment technology partners, StraitsX delivers cutting-edge payment solutions in a highly regulated and reliable environment, bridging the gap between traditional finance and the digital economy.
 About Visa 
Visa (NYSE: V) is a world leader in digital payments, facilitating transactions between consumers, sellers, financial institutions and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories. Our mission is to connect the world through the most innovative, convenient, reliable and secure payments network, enabling individuals, businesses and economies to thrive. We believe that economies that include everyone everywhere, uplift everyone everywhere and see access as foundational to the future of money movement. Learn more at Visa.com .
