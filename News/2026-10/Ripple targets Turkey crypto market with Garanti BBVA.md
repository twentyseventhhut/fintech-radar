# Ripple targets Turkey crypto market with Garanti BBVA

> [!info] 2026-10-07 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇹🇷 Ripple targets Turkey's $200B crypto market with bank custody and dollar backed stablecoins. Ripple is partnering with Garanti BBVA and local exchanges to offer regulated custody and stablecoins like RLUSD. The move addresses demand for dollar pegged assets as Turkish users seek protection against lira depreciation and inflation.
