« Т-Банк » вводит в социальной сети для инвесторов «Пульс» отдельный статус для авторов — «Легенды Пульса». Новый статус поможет пользователям быстрее оценивать авторов финансового контента и находить тех, у кого есть большой инвестиционный опыт, стабильная история публикаций и высокая репутация внутри платформы. Об этом CNews сообщили представители «Т-Банка».
«Легендами Пульса» могут стать авторы, которые прошли отбор экспертов команды «Пульса», а также имеют подтвержденный опыт и положительную доходность от инвестирования (портфель от 5 млн. рублей с положительной доходностью, сумма подписок на стратегии автоследования автора — от 200 млн рублей), длительную историю публикаций на платформе (от двух лет, аудитория от 10 тыс. подписчиков) и никогда не нарушали правила сообщества.
Среди авторов площадки, которые уже стали «Легендами», такие известные в инвестиционном сообществе блогеры, как Василий Олейник (Vasiliy_Oleynik), Яков Николотов-Бородин (Cocklomax), Мурад Агаев (Agaev1003) и другие. 
На платформе «Пульс» изначально работают инструменты, которые позволяют площадке отслеживать качество контента. А новый статус добавляет еще один уровень навигации — визуальный ориентир, который помогает читателям быстрее находить авторов с большим подтвержденным опытом и устойчивой репутацией. Таким образом, при оценке финансового контента инвестор сможет учитывать не только сам текст или популярность автора, но и весь контекст: историю активности на платформе и реальные инвестиционные результаты. 
Авторы со статусом «Легенда Пульса» получают дополнительную медиаподдержку в «Пульсе», доступ к образовательным и отраслевым мероприятиям, возможность прямого общения с экспертами рынка и эмитентами. 
Сегодня в «Пульсе» публикуется более 140 тыс. авторов, у 500 из них аудитория превышает 10 тыс. подписчиков. Ежемесячно пользователи размещают более 1 млн постов и комментариев, а среднемесячная аудитория платформы составляет 1,5 млн человек. Все посты, независимо от опыта автора, оцениваются модераторами площадки для предотвращения нарушения правил платформы и попыток манипулирования рынком. Начать вести блог в «Пульсе» может любой пользователь: для этого необходимо зарегистрироваться или авторизоваться на платформе и начать публиковать материалы.
 Павел Володин , руководитель социальной сети для инвесторов «Пульс», «Т-Банк»: «Когда инвестор ищет качественный финансовый контент, важно не только, насколько убедительно звучит автор, но и какой реальный опыт стоит за его мнением. Поэтому в «Пульсе» изначально действуют единые правила публикаций, модерация и механизмы, которые помогают ограничивать недобросовестный контент. Новый статус не делит авторов на «хороших» и «плохих», а добавляет еще один ориентир для читателя — помогает быстрее находить тех, у кого есть значимый подтвержденный опыт и сильная история внутри сообщества. Для нас это следующий этап развития «Пульса» как среды, где качество инвестиционного контента поддерживается не только правилами платформы, но и понятной системой роста для авторов».

# Context-enrichment: T-Bank Pulse adds "Legends" status for proven investors
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
T-Bank (ex-Tinkoff) added a curated verified-author tier, "Legends of Pulse" («Легенды Пульса»), to **Pulse** — its in-app social network for retail investors inside the T-Investments app. This is a **badge/status feature, not a product, round, or regulated service**. No money changes hands, no new instrument, no API.

Entry criteria (from the cnews primary source — unusually specific, treat as the real substance):
- Passed a manual "expert selection" by the Pulse team (discretionary gate — not purely algorithmic).
- Verified investing track record with **positive return**: portfolio ≥ RUB 5m with positive return, OR auto-follow ("автоследование" = copy-trading) strategy subscriptions ≥ RUB 200m.
- Publishing history ≥ 2 years, audience ≥ 10k followers.
- Never violated community rules.

Named first "Legends": bloggers Vasily Oleynik (Vasiliy_Oleynik), Yakov Nikolotov-Borodin (Cocklomax), Murad Agaev (Agaev1003).

Perks for Legends: extra media support inside Pulse, access to educational/industry events, direct contact with market experts and issuers.

Platform scale cited by T-Bank: 140k+ authors, ~500 with >10k followers, >1m posts/comments monthly, ~1.5m average monthly audience. All posts are moderated for rule breaches and market-manipulation attempts.

**Why structured this way / what it reveals:** The criteria are a trust-signalling and anti-finfluencer-noise mechanism, not a growth lever. The dual threshold (own RUB 5m portfolio OR RUB 200m of copy-trade subscriptions) is the tell: the second branch rewards authors who already drive **auto-follow volume**, i.e. people whose signals move retail money — exactly the cohort T-Bank monetises via copy-trading. The "positive return" gate is PR-flattering and survivorship-biased: it certifies a snapshot of past performance, not skill, and silently excludes anyone who had a bad year. The real function: give readers a curated shortlist of high-influence authors while T-Bank retains discretionary control over who gets amplified.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Within Russia:** Pulse is positioned (by T-Bank's own claims) as the largest investor social network in Russia, launched ~2019–2020. Direct state-bank rivals **Sber (SberInvestments)** and **VTB (My Investments)** compete on brokerage flow but have **no comparable at-scale social/community layer** — their 2026 news flow is about regulated crypto trading (launches Nov–Dec 2026), not community curation. So on social-investing specifically Pulse is effectively unrivalled domestically → "Legends" is incremental polish on an existing moat, not a competitive catch-up.
- **Global analogues:** eToro (Popular Investor program, verified copy-traded accounts), StockTwits, Public.com, Commonstock (shut down). eToro's "Popular Investor" is the closest prior art — a tiered, performance-gated, paid status for copy-traded authors. T-Bank's "Legends" is a **softer, non-monetised, reputation-only** version (no direct payout tier disclosed) → behind eToro on author monetisation, comparable on verification intent.

**Why the landscape is this way / second-order:** Social-investing moats are network effects + the broker's own order flow. Sber/VTB can't easily copy Pulse because community requires years of accumulated authors and content, and their brands skew conservative/mass-retail, not trader-enthusiast. The margin in the stack sits with the **broker that captures copy-trade order flow**, and Pulse feeds exactly that funnel — so "Legends" quietly reinforces where T-Bank already captures value.

## [2] Company history / fit
T-Bank's 2026 fintech output around investing has been steady and incremental: T-Investments loyalty platform paying shares for purchases (Jul 2026, see [[T-Investments launches loyalty platform paying shares for purchases]]), exclusive analyst feed from 30+ experts for T-Private/T-Premium (Apr 2026), and — the strategically important one — a stated pivot to make **Pulse a social network "for everyone," not just investors** (topics: business, financial literacy, travel). The CloudPayments valuation dispute ([[CloudPayments co-founder Spiridonov wins valuation suit vs T-Bank]]) is unrelated corporate litigation.

**Why the company acts this way:** Fits logically. If Pulse is broadening beyond investors to chase audience/engagement, the risk is content-quality dilution and loss of the serious-investor core. "Legends" is the **counterweight**: as the top of the funnel widens to "everyone," T-Bank fences off a high-signal tier so the investing use-case (and the copy-trade revenue tied to it) doesn't get drowned. Structural logic: broaden reach for ad/engagement upside while ring-fencing the monetisable investor cohort.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
Novelty: **low.** Verified/tiered author badges are standard on social-investing platforms (eToro Popular Investor predates this by years). Within Russia it is new only in that no rival has a social layer to add it to.

Traction: **the feature itself has none to report** — it launched with a handful of named authors (3 cited). Pulse's ~1.5m MAU and 140k authors are pre-existing platform stats, not evidence the badge changes behaviour. No metrics on reader trust, copy-trade conversion uplift, or author retention — because it just shipped (announced ~6 Oct 2026). "Announced," not "proven adoption."

**Why value-add is marginal (deeper):** The badge's economic value is the copy-trade funnel — if a "Legend" label lifts auto-follow subscriptions, T-Bank captures the take-rate. But the criteria **certify past returns, not forward skill** → a "Legend" with a bad quarter who lost followers money becomes a liability/complaint vector that T-Bank has now implicitly endorsed. That is the hidden downside: by curating and amplifying authors, T-Bank partially **takes on reputational/implied-advice risk** it previously disclaimed. Who captures the margin: T-Bank (order flow); who carries the tail risk: T-Bank's brand + the follower. The badge quietly moves the platform from neutral venue toward implied endorser.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Backdrop that makes this timely and raises its weight slightly: MOEX retail base is huge and still growing — **42.7m individuals with brokerage accounts (Sep 2026, +244k MoM), ~81.9m accounts, ~3m monthly active traders** — so the addressable audience for curated investor content is real. Critically, the **Bank of Russia (CBR) has proposed finfluencer rules** (a finfluencer register, qualification requirements, liability for content quality, mandatory ad-labelling), because finfluencers currently bear no liability for subscriber losses.

**Why the market goes this way / counterintuitive second-order:** "Legends" reads less like a product idea and more like **pre-emptive self-regulation** ahead of CBR finfluencer rules — a private register/qualification layer that lets T-Bank say "we already verify and gate our top authors." That reframes the central question from "is a badge interesting?" (no) to **"is T-Bank building a compliance moat around finfluencer content before regulation forces it?"** (plausibly yes). Counterintuitive risk: a formal "Legend" endorsement could be the *worst* place to stand if CBR imposes liability — the clearer the endorsement, the harder to disclaim responsibility for a Legend's bad call. Sentiment: low market impact, no analyst coverage (minor in-app feature of a private, sanctioned, non-listed Russian bank); relevance is strategic/regulatory, not financial.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian retail investing is a mass-scale, still-expanding pool: MOEX reported 41.1M registered retail clients on the securities market and 6.3M individual investment accounts (IIS) as of Q1 2026 (per Moscow Exchange, moex.com, as of Q1 2026); note that the industry itself flags most accounts are small/dormant ("40 million accounts but only a fraction hold real assets", per FinanceMagnates). T-Bank/T-Technologies is one of the two dominant ecosystems: FY2025 group revenue ~RUB 1.4tn, operating net profit RUB 174.4bn (+43% y/y), 34.3M monthly active customers, ~54M total customers (per T-Technologies IFRS FY2025 via intellinews/tinkoff-group.com). "Why now": with a large but shallow retail base, the competitive frontier is no longer acquisition but engagement/retention — Pulse is T-Bank's owned attention/content layer on top of the T-Investments brokerage, and a "Legends" status is a low-cost engagement/credibility lever rather than a new product line. Structure: the social-investing-content niche inside RU brokerage is effectively a duopoly-of-ecosystems (T-Bank Pulse vs Sber), with high barriers from network effects (author base + followers) and distribution (bundled inside the broker app).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs for a social-investing layer: MAU/monthly readers, number of authors, posts/mo, and — economically — AUM steered via copy-trading/autoследование (the note gives "subscriptions to an author's autoследование strategies ≥RUB 200M" as a Legends threshold, confirming copy-trading is the monetizable backbone, not just content). Pulse scale (note + tbank.ru): >140k authors, 500 with >10k followers, >1M posts/comments per month, ~1.5M average monthly audience (tbank.ru marketing claims a wider ">4.5M monthly readers" — de-PR: "readers" ≠ active posters; treat the 1.5M audience figure as the tighter metric). Basis of competition: network effects + content quality/trust, not price (Pulse is free). Key players: T-Bank Pulse (incumbent leader, "крупнейшая соцсеть для инвесторов в России" per tbank.ru) vs Sber's investor app/community (SberInvestor), which has redesigned its app but has no comparably prominent public social-content network (per fintechfutures/zamin). Protagonist position: ahead / niche leader in RU social-investing (analysis) — moat is network effects (author–follower graph) + switching costs (an author's 2-year+ history and follower base don't port). Separately, note the stated direction to widen Pulse "для всех, а не только для инвесторов" (per vc.ru) — a pivot from a brokerage feature toward a broader social platform.

**Comps & multiples.** No deal/valuation/round in this news (product/status feature), so no transaction multiple to compute — "no data" on a Pulse-specific valuation. Internal comps (same company/ecosystem, engagement-monetization theme): [[T-Investments launches loyalty platform paying shares for purchases]] (Jun 2026 — issuer loyalty platform gifting shares, same "turn users into shareholders/engaged investors" playbook) and [[T-Bank multibanking pilot reaches 700k users]] (Jul 2026 — engagement/retention metric). Parent-level anchor (not a Pulse multiple): T-Technologies 1Q2026 net revenue RUB 197.5bn, operating net profit RUB 46.5bn (margin ≈ 46.5/197.5 = 24% operating-net margin on net revenue) (per tinkoff-group.com 1Q2026 IFRS). External peer multiples (Sber, MOEX): not computed — no clean, comparable Pulse-level segment disclosure → `[UNSOURCED]`. Distribution not computed, qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. Conflict-of-interest / mis-selling risk (regulatory). "Legends" is a platform-conferred trust badge gating on past realized returns + ≥RUB 200M of autoследование subscriptions — i.e. it amplifies exactly the authors whose signals move the most retail money. Past performance is not predictive; a badge steering followers into copy-trades raises CBR/consumer-protection exposure if a "Legend" blows up, and the broker both ranks the author and earns on the resulting flow (why: second-order — reputational/regulatory liability lands on T-Bank, not the author).
2. Selection/survivorship bias in the criterion. Gating on "positive returns" over a 2-year window favors authors who were lucky in a specific market regime; the status can entrench crowded trades and herding (why: concentrated retail follow-the-leader flows amplify volatility in thinly-held RU small/mid caps).
3. Engagement-layer, not revenue-layer, dependence. Pulse's value is captured only if it converts to brokerage flow/AUM; disclosure is silent on monetization economics of the status (media support, event access are costs, not revenue). If the "для всех" broadening dilutes the investor focus, the funnel from content to trades can weaken (why: margin stays at the broker; the social layer is a cost center unless it demonstrably lifts active-trader retention).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** (analysis) This is an incremental retention/credibility move in a maturing RU retail-brokerage market where the land-grab for accounts is largely over and the contest shifts to engagement and copy-trading AUM — expect Sber and others to answer with their own curated-author / verified-track-record features. Falsifiable thesis: if Legends is real moat-building, T-Bank will later disclose Pulse-driven copy-trading AUM or a lift in active-trader retention; trigger to watch — a CBR rule or guidance on influencer/finfluencer accountability and copy-trading suitability, which would reprice the economics of any "verified author" program. What would make the thesis wrong: a visible "Legend" portfolio drawdown that triggers follower losses and regulatory scrutiny, turning the badge from an asset into a liability.
