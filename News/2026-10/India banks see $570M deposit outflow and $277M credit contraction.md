Synopsis

Kolkata's banking sector faced a Rs 5.19 lakh crore withdrawal of deposits in the fortnight ending October 15. Credit outstanding also contracted by Rs 2.52 lakh crore during the same period. Consequently, credit growth decreased to 18.2% from 19.4% in the prior fortnight. Meanwhile, deposit growth recorded a decline to 16.7% compared to 17.8%. The overall outstanding deposits reached Rs 278.76 lakh crore and outstanding credit was Rs 227.

Kolkata: The banking sector saw a Rs 5.19 lakh crore withdrawal of deposits on net basis in the fortnight ending October 15 while credit outstanding too contracted by Rs 2.52 lakh crore, Reserve Bank of India data showed.  
  
Accordingly, the credit growth slowed to 18.2% from the 19.4% in the preceding fortnight while deposit growth recorded at 16.7% against 17.8%
