Apex Fintech Solutions Partners with EDX Markets to Support Digital Markets Expansion
PR Newswire
CHICAGO, Oct. 26, 2026
 CHICAGO , Oct. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- EDX Markets , a digital asset technology firm that combines an institutional-only trading venue with a central clearinghouse, and Apex Fintech Solutions  ("Apex"), the infrastructure powering modern investing, today announced a strategic relationship that will integrate EDX's trading technology with Apex's financial infrastructure.

 
 
Through the partnership, Apex will leverage EDX's trading and clearing solutions to support its digital markets offering. The collaboration pairs EDX's purpose-built technology with Apex's established capabilities and experience across traditional finance, establishing an efficient digital asset trading environment built on institutional standards.
"As participation in crypto continues to evolve, firms increasingly expect the same standards they rely on across traditional finance," said Tony Acuña-Rohter, CEO of EDX Markets . "Apex shares our focus on applying proven financial market principles to this space, and we're pleased to deliver the technology supporting the next phase of their digital markets strategy."
EDX's institutional-only marketplace is designed to provide transparent price discovery, deep liquidity and reliable execution while maintaining separation between trading and custody. Its trading and clearing model is designed to deliver the performance and operational standards required by sophisticated firms entering and operating in digital assets.
"As digital assets become an increasingly important part of the global financial ecosystem, our focus is on expanding our offerings while maintaining the high standards our partners have come to associate with Apex," said Travis McGhee, Global Head of Digital Markets at Apex Fintech Solutions . "To achieve this, we need trusted partners like EDX that understand the robust infrastructure supporting institutional markets and can integrate seamlessly with our existing capabilities across traditional finance, allowing us to deliver new solutions with the reliability and efficiency our clients expect."
EDX and Apex will continue to build on the partnership to support secure, efficient trading and broader participation in digital asset markets.
 About EDX 
EDX is a digital asset technology firm that combines an institution-only trading venue with a central clearinghouse. EDX Markets, our flagship marketplace, is designed to emulate the world's most sophisticated exchanges, with deep liquidity, firm prices and low trading costs. EDX has structured its business to minimize risk for its members while providing a diverse array of operational and capital efficiencies. Backed by some of the world's leading trading and venture capital firms, EDX is actively developing new features and expanding its geographic presence to deliver trusted, liquid and efficient crypto trading experiences for all institutions. To learn more, visit  edxmarkets.com .

 About Apex Fintech Solutions 
Apex Fintech Solutions provides the tools and services that enable hundreds of clients to launch, scale, and support digital investing for tens of millions of end investors. The company provides essential infrastructure and a comprehensive ecosystem of cloud-based products to enable and streamline trading, wealth management, cost basis, tax reporting, and, through its subsidiary Apex Clearing™, custody and clearing. Apex Clearing Corporation is registered with the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) as an Futures Commissions Merchant with the National Futures Association (NFA), operating under their regulatory oversight for futures, CFTC-regulated cleared swaps/event/prediction market contracts.
