# Nayax completes acquisition of IPS Group

> [!info] 2026-10-05 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇮🇱 Nayax completes its acquisition of IPS Group, expanding its unattended payments footprint into smart parking and urban mobility. The deal adds IPS Group's parking meter and sensor technology to Nayax's global payment platform, which serves vending, EV charging, and other self-service verticals across over 80 countries. Terms of the transaction were not disclosed.
