Vivi Money launches Vivi Shop & Pay, letting AI shop for you without the keys to your wallet
Byron Bay fintech Vivi Money is bringing shopping and payments into one conversation with Vivi Shop & Pay, an AI assistant that finds products, compares options and completes purchases with customer approval, without ever handing over your existing card details or control of your money.
 Vivi has already completed real AI initiated purchases through Visa ‘s payment network. “Right now the agent says, here’s your shirt, go and get it. You still have to do it yourself,” says founder and CEO Will Cormack (pictured above). “We wanted to close that gap. Find it, then pay for it.”
Two videos on Vivi Money ‘s LinkedIn page show Vivi Shop & Pay in action, from purchasing an Afends T-shirt to completing a flight booking involving seat selection, baggage and payment, with both transactions successfully settled through Visa ‘s payment network.
As the world’s biggest AI companies race to make their assistants capable of shopping, Vivi has taken a different approach to payments. With Vivi Shop & Pay customers can ask Vivi’s AI Assistant to find something within a budget and deadline, compare the options and approve a purchase. With their details already securely stored in Vivi, there’s no need to fill out checkout forms or enter payment information again. Customers simply review and approve the purchase inside the Vivi app, and Vivi handles the payment through Visa, the AI never sees the card number used for purchase.
 The Vivi Account: AI that actually knows your money 
Most AI assistants start from scratch, asking who you are, where to deliver and how you’ll pay. Vivi already has a head start. “Our customers are identity verified, we know their details and account balance, and through Open Banking we can see the other accounts they choose to connect,” says Cormack. “So when you ask Vivi to buy something, it already understands your finances and what you can afford.”
That means one conversation can do what used to take four apps. Ask “how much is in my account” and then “I need a black tee, medium, under $100, here by Friday”, and Vivi handles both. Banking and shopping sit in one place, with the same context.
Vivi can also search across retailers and compare prices. Ask for “a black tee with pink writing” and it looks at product images to find a match. And when booking flights, Vivi goes far beyond comparing airfares, handling everything from choosing airlines and fares to selecting seats, adding baggage or even a surfboard, before completing the purchase with the customer’s approval.
 Text it the way you text a friend 
Cormack believes people shouldn’t have to open a banking app just to chat. Customers can message Vivi in the app or through iMessage, with conversations synced across devices. “You tell Vivi what you need, it texts you back when it’s found it,” he says.
The conversation can happen anywhere, but payments stay inside Vivi. When it’s time to buy, customers return to the secure app, where Face ID and the full purchase details let them review exactly what they’re approving before any money moves.
 Security first: Hold, don’t tap 
Security is built into every Vivi Shop & Pay transaction. Customers are identity verified and can review the merchant, price and delivery details before approving a purchase inside the secure Vivi app. And approval isn’t a quick tap. “You press and hold, so there’s no accidentally authorising a payment,” says Cormack. “The AI can find something and ask to pay, but the customer stays in control.”
Only after approval does Vivi generate a single-use Visa card, restricted to the authorised merchant and amount and deactivated after use. The AI never receives card details or access to the customer’s money. “We’ve deliberately separated intelligence from control of the money,” Cormack says. “The AI doesn’t get the keys to your wallet.”
 Your AI, powered by Vivi Money 
Vivi has its own AI assistant, but its payment technology will also let customers connect their preferred assistant, such as ChatGPT or Claude to their Vivi Account. Vivi handles payments securely, with every purchase requiring approval through the Vivi Money app.
“Vivi doesn’t have to be the AI you choose,” says Cormack. “Your agent pays with Vivi.” The company aims to provide the identity-verified accounts and payment infrastructure that enable AI assistants to transact.
 Vivi Money: live now, with much more on the way 
Vivi Money is already live with its in-app AI Personal Assistant, Visa card, Open Banking and Australia’s lowest FX rates. Through Open Banking, Vivi can also monitor accounts customers choose to connect from other banks, helping identify opportunities for better savings and mortgage rates.
Vivi Shop & Pay will be available to all Premium Metal customers from November, with the waitlist now open. Customers will be able to find, compare and purchase products using AI while retaining control of every payment. Premium metal cards and Vivi’s Boost Saver high interest savings product are also planned for rollout next month.
The developments build on Vivi’s earlier AI initiated payments over Australia’s New Payments Platform and Snap & Pay technology which turns invoice photos into ready-to-approve payments.
For Cormack, it all comes back to one idea: bringing banking, payments, shopping and financial management together in one place. “The end goal isn’t an AI that talks about your money. It’s one that can do something about it.”
