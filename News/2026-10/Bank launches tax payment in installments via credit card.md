# «Т-Банк» запустил оплату налогов в рассрочку с кредитной карты

Комиссия за услугу и другие условия рассчитываются «индивидуально для каждого клиента».

Источник: «Т-Банк»

*   В рассрочку можно оплатить имущественный, транспортный, земельный налоги, НДФЛ с отдельных видов дохода (например, с процентов по банковским вкладам) и другие начисления, которые входят в единый налоговый платёж, рассказали в банке.
*   Оплатить налоги в рассрочку можно тремя способами: через мобильное приложение «Т-Банка», а также с помощью сервиса T-Pay на сайте ФНС или на Госуслугах. Функция доступна в версиях приложения не ниже 7.29 на iOS и 7.30 на Android.
*   В приложении «Т-Банка» нужно открыть раздел «Платежи», ввести свой ИНН и выбрать счета на оплату. До конца 2026 года добавят возможность оплаты по QR-коду из налогового уведомления.
*   За использование рассрочки по кредитной карте предусмотрена комиссия, отмечают в банке. Её размер и другие условия рассчитываются «индивидуально для каждого клиента», их можно увидеть перед оформлением.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** This is Russian consumer lending, specifically the credit-card / installment (BNPL-adjacent) subvertical, wrapped around a recurring tax-payment event. Note this is NOT regulated BNPL: it is a credit-card commission product, so it sits in unsecured consumer credit, not the new "installment-services operator" regime. Sector size: Russia's regulated installment/BNPL market reached ~RUB 940bn at end-2025 (+2.5x YoY) and is projected to ~RUB 1.2-1.25tn in 2026 (+~30%), ~1.4% of retail payments (per TAdviser, as of 2026). The protagonist's own rails are far larger: T-Technologies (parent, MOEX: T) posted 1Q2026 operating net profit RUB 46.5bn (+40% YoY) on net revenue RUB 197.5bn (+25% YoY), with ~RUB 1.4tn revenue, 54m customers and ~17% of Russian retail turnover (per T-Technologies IFRS 1Q2026 release / bne IntelliNews). "Why now": (1) the single tax payment (единый налоговый платёж) falls due in autumn with larger balances because of high deposit-interest income now taxed; (2) the CBR's hard macroprudential filter on unsecured lending — 0% share of credit-card issuance allowed at debt-load (ПДН) >80% from Q3 2026 — pushes banks to find NEW credit demand pockets that pass underwriting rather than chase saturated POS/card limits (per internal [[Финтехно Q3 brings debt-load and installment-market rule changes]]).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: credit-card loan book, net interest margin, cost of risk, and here the per-client commission on the installment (undisclosed — "individually calculated"). Key players in the tax-payment-on-credit niche are already present: Sovcombank let customers pay taxes with Halva borrowed funds + installment since at least 2024; Alfa-Bank allowed credit-card tax payment inside the grace period (per internal [[T-Bank tests selling credit card via tax-payment flow]]). So T-Bank is catching up on the mechanic itself, but ahead on packaging — it builds a full credit product (equal payments, per-client commission, distribution via its app + T-Pay on the FNS site + Gosuslugi) around the tax event rather than treating it as a grace-period side effect. Broader installment peers: Dolyame (T-Bank), Yandex Split, Podeli (Alfa), Wildberries "Частями"; Yandex has just registered a second BNPL legal entity, NewPay, with the CBR (per internal [[Yandex NewPay added to Russian installment-services registry]]), signalling the market is splitting into dedicated regulated operators — the opposite structural move from T-Bank, which keeps this product inside the credit card. Moat: distribution/scale and the T-Pay integration into state payment flows (FNS, Gosuslugi) — switching cost is low, so the edge is reach, not lock-in (analysis).

**Comps & multiples.** T-Technologies is listed (MOEX: T) but no clean, freely verifiable EV/Revenue or P/E pair is sourceable here for the parent, and this specific product discloses no economics (commission "individually calculated", portfolio size not given) — so product-level unit economics and valuation multiples: no data / [UNSOURCED]. Growth anchor only: parent operating net profit +40% YoY (1Q2026), 2026 guidance ≥+20% (per T-Technologies IFRS). Internal comps / precedent: [[T-Bank tests selling credit card via tax-payment flow]] (same mechanic, Oct 2026), [[Yandex NewPay added to Russian installment-services registry]] (peer BNPL regulatory split), [[Финтехно Q3 brings debt-load and installment-market rule changes]] (ПДН / macroprudential context). Distribution not computed — <3 comparable public multiples; qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
- Opaque, per-client commission ("рассчитываются индивидуально"): the bank is silent on APR, so the effective cost can exceed a straight card grace-period usage — reputational/mis-selling risk and regulatory attention (CBR is actively tightening consumer-credit disclosure and dark-pattern rules), second-order: potential forced disclosure that compresses the economics.
- Regulatory / macroprudential: this is unsecured credit-card debt into a ПДН regime where >80%-debt-load issuance is being driven to zero — if the tax-payment flow onboards already-stretched borrowers, cost-of-risk and underwriting rejection both rise, capping the addressable pool.
- Thin moat / fast imitation: Sovcombank and Alfa already do variants and incumbents can copy the "life-scenario" packaging cheaply; value accrues to whoever owns distribution (state rails are shared via T-Pay/FNS/Gosuslugi), so the first-mover lead is short-lived (analysis).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** (analysis) T-Bank is productising a new, underwriting-friendly credit-demand category — financing a non-discretionary, legally-due obligation rather than a discretionary purchase — which, if it converts, is higher-quality credit demand than POS installment. Falsifiable thesis: this becomes a repeatable seasonal acquisition channel (credit-card sell-through via the tax flow, cf. [[T-Bank tests selling credit card via tax-payment flow]]); it is wrong if the CBR forces standardized APR disclosure that kills the "individual commission" economics, or if peers' faster copying commoditizes it within one tax cycle. Watch: CBR consumer-credit disclosure rulings and whether T-Bank discloses take-up/portfolio by the next IFRS print.
