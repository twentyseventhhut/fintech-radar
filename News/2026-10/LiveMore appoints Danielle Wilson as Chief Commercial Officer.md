Specialist lender LiveMore has appointed Danielle Wilson as Chief Commercial Officer to lead its commercial and partnership strategy. For fintech professionals, this move signals an aggressive growth phase for a lender targeting the underserved 50-plus demographic, leveraging Wilson’s experience in AI and SaaS to scale its mortgage solutions for older borrowers.

LiveMore has appointed Danielle Wilson as Chief Commercial Officer, marking one of three major leadership hires intended to accelerate the lender's growth trajectory. Wilson joins the executive team from pensions software provider Mantle, where she also served as Chief Commercial Officer. Her professional background includes senior commercial and partnership roles at AI firm V7, as well as fintech companies GoCardless and Liberis. At LiveMore, she is tasked with shaping commercial and partnership strategy across sales, product development, marketing, and risk management.

The lender focuses on the later-life market, providing mortgage solutions for individuals aged 40 to over 90. LiveMore currently supports more than 6,000 customers and maintains a network of over 3,700 registered broker firms. Its product suite is extensive, featuring more than 250 options including Capital & Interest, Term Interest Only, Retirement Interest Only (RIO), Part & Part, and Equity Release solutions. To assist intermediaries in navigating this range, the company utilizes its proprietary Mortgage Matcher® tool. This technology calculates maximum borrowing potential based on a client’s specific income and expenditure, aiming to simplify the affordability assessment for brokers working with older borrowers whose financial profiles may be more complex than those of younger applicants.

> "What attracted me to LiveMore was the opportunity to combine a strong commercial growth opportunity with a mission that really matters. My experience spans fintech, lending, SaaS and AI, and I've spent much of my career building partnerships, products and new routes to market that help businesses grow."

Danielle Wilson, Chief Commercial Officer at LiveMore

LiveMore, also known as LiveMore Capital, is a specialist lender founded by CEO Leon Diamond. The firm has carved out a specific niche within the UK mortgage market by addressing the "later life" segment, which has historically been underserved by high-street banks due to age-related lending restrictions. Unlike traditional lenders that often implement strict age caps on mortgage terms, LiveMore provides a range of products designed for borrowers from age 40 through to 90 and beyond.

The company positions itself as a challenger to the traditional lending model, emphasizing flexibility in how it assesses affordability and risk for older homeowners. By offering an integrated range of products—from standard interest-only mortgages to equity release—LiveMore aims to provide a continuous path for borrowers as they move through different stages of retirement. The firm operates primarily through an intermediary model, relying on a large network of registered broker firms to distribute its products. This strategy is supported by internal technology like the Mortgage Matcher®, which is designed to handle the nuances of pension income and diverse expenditure patterns common in older demographics.

FF News has previously tracked LiveMore’s efforts to secure diverse funding sources to support its lending activities. The firm recently reached a significant financial milestone when LiveMore Secures £212 Million in Third Social Bond Securitisation for Older Homeowners. This transaction represented the company's third residential mortgage-backed security (RMBS) issuance, highlighting its ability to attract institutional capital to the later-life lending sector. These securitisations are a core component of the company's strategy to maintain a stable liquidity pool, allowing it to offer a broad range of products to borrowers who are often excluded from the mainstream mortgage market due to their age or income structure.

The appointment of a Chief Commercial Officer with deep roots in AI and SaaS suggests that the later-life lending sector is moving beyond simple product diversification and into a more tech-heavy competition. As the UK population ages, the "silver economy" represents a massive, yet complex, opportunity for fintechs. Traditional lenders are under increasing pressure to adapt their rigid affordability models or risk losing this high-equity demographic to specialists like LiveMore. The challenge for the sector remains the integration of complex pension data into automated underwriting. Wilson’s background in pensions and AI will likely be the litmus test for whether specialized lenders can truly scale through automation without losing the nuance required for older borrowers.

Original Press Release

Specialist lender LiveMore has announced the appointment to its executive team of Danielle Wilson as Chief Commercial Officer. Danielle’s appointment strengthens its senior leadership team as the lender accelerates its growth through innovative solutions and new strategic partnerships.

Danielle is an experienced commercial leader with a track record spanning fintech, lending, AI and SaaS. She joins LiveMore from pensions software provider Mantle, where she was Chief Commercial Officer, having previously held senior commercial and partnership roles at AI company V7, GoCardless and Liberis. Across these businesses, she has built strategic partnerships, launched new products and developed scalable routes to market to drive revenue growth.

Danielle will now help to shape LiveMore’s commercial and partnership strategy, working across sales, products, marketing and risk to help propel LiveMore’s growth.

The appointment is one of three key leadership appointments that LiveMore is making as it accelerates its growth trajectory and evolves its proposition for borrowers from 40 up to age 90 plus.

“What attracted me to LiveMore was the opportunity to combine a strong commercial growth opportunity with a mission that really matters. My experience spans fintech, lending, SaaS and AI, and I've spent much of my career building partnerships, products and new routes to market that help businesses grow.” said Danielle Wilson, Chief Commercial Officer at LiveMore.

“Having worked in the pensions sector, I've also seen first-hand some of the financial challenges people face as they approach retirement. Mortgage needs don't stop at 50 or 60, yet the options available to many borrowers can become more limited as they get older. LiveMore is challenging that, with a broad range of solutions designed around customers' changing needs.” said Danielle Wilson, Chief Commercial Officer at LiveMore.

“I'm excited to help build on the company's momentum, working across sales, product, marketing and risk to reach more customers, develop new partnerships and make it easier for brokers to find the right solution for their clients.” said Danielle Wilson, Chief Commercial Officer at LiveMore.

“Our growth has been built on challenging the traditional approach to lending and working closely with partners to offer a better choice to borrowers. Danielle brings a strong track record of driving revenue growth and building strategic partnerships in fast-paced high-growth environments. Her experience will be invaluable as we look to develop new opportunities for the next phase of our growth.” said Leon Diamond, CEO and founder of LiveMore.

LiveMore brings new and innovative solutions to the later life lending market. It supports more than 6,000 customers and works with over 3,700 registered broker firms. It is the only lender offering an integrated range of more than 250 products across Capital & Interest, Term Interest Only, Retirement Interest Only, Part & Part and Equity Release solutions for borrowers aged from 40 up to 90 plus.

LiveMore’s Mortgage Matcher® clearly shows brokers the affordability of different lending options available to a borrower over 40. It makes a maximum borrowing calculation based on a client’s income and expenditure. It was developed to help intermediaries cut through the complexities of the wide range of products available in later life lending.
