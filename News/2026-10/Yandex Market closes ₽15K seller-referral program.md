# «Яндекс Маркет» закрыл реферальную программу для продавцов — маркетплейс платил по 15 тысяч рублей за привлечение новых партнёров

Причины закрытия в компании не раскрыли.

Источник: скриншот vc.ru

*   «Маркет» запустил программу в ноябре 2022 года: маркетплейс платил партнёрам за привлечение новых продавцов на платформу — сначала 10 тысяч, а потом 15 тысяч рублей.
*   5 октября 2026 года «Маркет» программу закрыл, обратил внимание Telegram-канал «Клуб партнёров на маркетплейсах». Об этом говорится на сайте маркетплейса.
*   В пресс-службе «Маркета» рассказали «Т-Бизнес секретам», что с 5 октября маркетплейс перестал подключать новых участников.
*   При этом площадка выплатит деньги тем, кто успел выполнить условия до завершения программы. За результаты с 1 по 30 сентября 2026-го выплаты должны прийти 20-25 октября 2026-го, а за приглашения с 1 по 4 октября — 20-25 ноября.
*   Сколько продавцов поучаствовало в программе, в «Маркете» не раскрыли. В компании объяснили vc.ru, что «регулярно пересматривают действующие программы» и решили «завершить работу реферальной программы в текущем формате».

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] TL;DR.** On 5 Oct 2026 Yandex Market shut its four-year-old seller-referral program, which paid partners ₽15K (originally ₽10K) for each new seller they brought onto the marketplace. The company stopped onboarding new participants but will honor payouts for those who qualified before closure (results from 1–30 Sep 2026 paid ~20–25 Oct; referrals from 1–4 Oct paid ~20–25 Nov). Yandex gave no public reason beyond saying it "regularly reviews active programs" and chose to "end the referral program in its current format."

**[1] What happened (dated timeline).**
- **Nov 2022** — Yandex Market launches the referral program, paying partner shops ₽10K per newly-attracted seller; payout triggered once the referred seller sold ≥₽10K of goods (via FBY/FBS/Express models). Later opened to any Russian legal entity / sole proprietor, not just existing shops.
- **Later** — reward raised from ₽10K to ₽15K; qualification threshold tied to the referred seller shipping ≥₽15K of goods.
- **5 Oct 2026** — program closed to new participants; flagged first by the Telegram channel "Клуб партнёров на маркетплейсах," confirmed on Market's own site and to T-Bank Business Secrets / vc.ru.
- **20–25 Oct & 20–25 Nov 2026** — scheduled final payouts to those who met conditions pre-closure.

**[2] Why it matters / interpretation.** The ₽15K bounty was a classic seller-acquisition subsidy — a direct customer-acquisition-cost (CAC) line to grow Market's third-party seller base against Wildberries and Ozon. Closing it signals a shift from land-grab growth toward unit-economics discipline. Context: Yandex Market's segment cut its EBITDA loss from roughly −₽56.2B (2024) to −₽39.6B (2025), and group guidance targets adjusted EBITDA ~₽350B in 2026 — i.e., e-commerce is explicitly in a "narrow-the-losses / break-on-investment" phase as CAC normalizes and repeat-purchase rates rise. Cutting a per-seller cash bounty fits that cost-discipline narrative. It may also reflect diminishing returns: paid-referral channels tend to attract low-quality/dormant sellers, and Market has pivoted to higher-leverage acquisition (e.g., reported drive to recruit ~50K Chinese sellers, reduced-commission trials) rather than retail cash bounties to Russian partners.

**[3] Market context — Russian marketplace competition.**
- Russian marketplace sales grew ~32% YoY to ₽8.59T; Ozon + Wildberries together control a majority (~60%) of e-commerce, with Yandex Market the clear #3.
- Ozon H1-2025 turnover grew ~45%, outpacing Wildberries (~38%); competitive pressure on Market is intense.
- Commission environment is rising elsewhere: Ozon announced category commission hikes >10pp (Feb 2026); Wildberries ran six tariff increases through 2025 — giving Market room to court sellers on lower commissions rather than one-off cash.
- Market has leaned on seller incentives historically (reduced-commission trials, points, international seller recruitment); the ₽15K referral was one of the oldest and most direct of these.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

**Red-team questions**

1. Is this a full *closure* or only a *pause*? Sources diverge: some headlines say "закрыл/прекратил" (closed/ended), others "приостановил" (suspended). Market itself said "end in its current format" — leaving the door open to a relaunched format. Don't overstate finality.
2. Did Yandex give any official reason? No — "причины не раскрыли." The CAC/cost-discipline framing here is *analyst inference* from segment EBITDA trends, not a stated motive. Flag as interpretation.
3. How material is a ₽15K-per-seller bounty to a company guiding ~₽350B EBITDA? Likely tiny in absolute terms — so the signal (strategic posture) matters more than the savings. Avoid implying this moves financials.
4. How many sellers were ever acquired via the program? Market declined to disclose ("не раскрыли") — so we can't size its effectiveness or the payout liability being wound down.
5. Could the closure reflect *fraud/abuse* rather than cost discipline? Referral bounties are notoriously gamed (shell sellers, self-referral). The vc.ru comments hint at partner-program blocking/moderation issues. This alternative explanation is plausible and under-sourced.
6. Is the ₽56.2B→₽39.6B EBITDA-loss figure correctly attributed to Market alone vs the broader e-commerce/mobility/delivery cluster? Verify segment boundaries before leaning on it.
7. Timeline precision: closure date is 5 Oct 2026 per Market's site; the vc.ru article is dated 8 Oct; note date is 12 Oct. Make sure the digest uses 5 Oct as the event date, not the publish date.
8. Does "stopped onboarding new participants" mean existing referrers can still earn on pending referrals, or is all future earning frozen? Payout schedule suggests only pre-5-Oct qualifiers are honored — confirm no grace window for in-flight referrals.
9. Is the "50K Chinese sellers" recruitment drive current (2026) or stale (2025)? If stale, the "pivot to international acquisition" framing weakens.
10. Competitive-share figures (~60% WB+Ozon, Market #3) come from secondary aggregators — are they 2025 or 2026 data? Date-check before citing shares.
11. Does closing a seller-acquisition subsidy risk *slowing* Market's seller growth right when Ozon/WB are raising commissions (a moment Market could exploit)? Possible strategic cost, not just savings — worth noting as a two-sided read.
12. Any regulatory/tax angle? Cash payments to individuals for referrals can carry tax-reporting burden; unclear if that factored in. Unsourced — don't assert.
13. Source quality: primary is vc.ru (reposting a Telegram channel) + T-Bank Secrets. No Yandex IR filing or press release confirms the closure. Single-thread sourcing — treat as trade-press, not disclosed.
14. Is this fintech-relevant at all, or pure e-commerce ops? It's marketplace seller economics, tangential to the fintech lens — relevant mainly as a Yandex strategy/unit-economics signal.

**Importance: 2/5** — A minor, undated-rationale operational tweak: a single marketplace quietly winding down one legacy acquisition subsidy, with no disclosed figures, no financial materiality, and only trade-press sourcing. It is a mildly useful datapoint on Yandex Market's shift to cost discipline amid the WB/Ozon squeeze, but it is not a standalone market-moving event and only loosely fintech-adjacent.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian online retail reached ₽13.2tn in 2025 (~21% of total retail), per Minpromtorg via TAdviser. The top-3 marketplaces (Wildberries, Ozon, Yandex Market) booked combined GMV of ₽8.59tn in 2025, +32.2% YoY but decelerating as consumer spending on non-food cools (Kommersant, 2025). Structure is a consolidating duopoly-plus-one: Wildberries ~45% and Ozon ~32% of e-commerce by end-2026 (combined ~77%), with Yandex Market a distant third (per bne IntelliNews, 2026). Barriers are heavy — fulfilment capex, PVZ/logistics density, and network effects between buyers and sellers — which is exactly why a #3 player historically paid to acquire supply. **Why now:** the land-grab phase is ending. Across all three platforms seller monetisation is being tightened — reported effective commissions rose over the year to Ozon 47.8%, Wildberries 42.7%, Yandex Market ~40.1%, while seller profitability fell from ~15% to ~5% (Deliver2, 2026). Closing a ₽15K seller-referral bonus (launched Nov 2022 at ₽10K, raised to ₽15K; shut 5 Oct 2026) is the supply-side mirror of that shift: subsidy-to-grow → extract-the-base.

**Competitive landscape.** Marketplace KPIs: GMV, take rate (commission + ads + logistics), active buyers/sellers, and segment adj-EBITDA/GMV margin. Players & basis of competition: Wildberries (scale/price leader), Ozon (logistics + fast-growing fintech/ads), Yandex Market (ecosystem bundling — Plus loyalty, Delivery/Lavka, Yandex Pay/NewPay BNPL). Yandex Market: ~141K sellers and ~18M active monthly buyers as of mid-2025; 1P (own) sales nearly doubled ₽44.3bn→₽79.6bn in 2025 (new-retail/axdigital). Recent peer moves: Ozon extended "Ozon Select" into electronics (Apple/Dyson/Garmin, Oct 2026) and Wildberries opened self-serve direct ads at a ₽1K minimum (Oct 2026) — both pulling supply/ad spend onto their own rails. Yandex reportedly cut up to ~15% of marketplace staff (Jul 2026, Meduza) and is testing a reduced-commission seller subscription. Position: **niche/catching-up** #3, leaning on ecosystem cross-sell rather than standalone GMV scale (analysis); moat is intangible (Plus/search/fintech bundle), not logistics scale.

**Comps & multiples.** Internal comps (same sector/geo): [[Ozon Select adds electronics and Apple, Dyson, Garmin goods]], [[Wildberries opens direct marketplace ads with ₽1K minimum budget]], [[Ozon Bank registers digital financial assets platform with CBR]], [[VTB takes large Ozon share stake as collateral]], [[Axenix study marketplace banks hit trust ceiling despite traffic]].
- **Yandex (IR, PRIMARY — filed Q1 2026, 2026-04-28):** group revenue ₽372.7bn (+22% YoY); adj EBITDA ₽73.3bn (+50%, 19.7% margin). E-commerce services revenue ₽123.4bn (+18% YoY) with adj EBITDA loss halved to **−₽6.4bn**; Yandex Market adj-EBITDA/GMV margin improved +3.2pp YoY on "a more efficient marketing model and lower operating costs"; e-commerce ad revenue +28% (Yandex IR Q1 2026 press release; yandex.ru/company/news/28-04-2026). The referral-program closure is directly consistent with this filed "more efficient marketing model" language.

EV/Revenue ≈ ₽677bn / ₽424bn FY26e ≈ **1.6x** (mkt-cap proxy, net cash not verified → treat as P/S, `[UNSOURCED]` clean EV).
- Yandex Market standalone GMV not separately disclosed in the filing → segment EV/Rev **no data**. Distribution not computed (only 1 clean external comp); qualitative: at ~1.6x P/S on 30–50% growth Ozon screens in-line-to-cheap for Russian e-com, so Yandex Market's loss-making unit carries a discount vs that.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Supply stickiness / disintermediation risk** — cutting acquisition subsidies while #1/#2 raise commissions to 40%+ can slow or reverse seller onboarding; a #3 with no logistics moat is most exposed to sellers consolidating onto Wildberries/Ozon (second-order: GMV-share erosion).
2. **Margin extraction vs seller health** — sector seller profitability already ~5%; pushing take rates + removing bonuses risks seller attrition and regulatory attention (FAS already flagged WB/Ozon over unequal seller terms, Mar 2026).
3. **Segment still loss-making** — e-commerce adj EBITDA −₽6.4bn (Q1 2026); the referral cut is a cost lever toward break-even, so execution risk is "trim too hard, choke growth" — watch GMV growth vs the deceleration already visible sector-wide.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Read it as the Russian marketplace sector flipping from subsidised supply-acquisition to supply-monetisation across all three leaders simultaneously — a margin re-rating, not a growth story (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: Yandex Market's e-commerce segment keeps narrowing its loss (toward the ₽123bn-revenue base) without GMV growth collapsing below sector ~30%. **Trigger/what breaks it:** Q3 2026 Yandex IR — if e-commerce GMV growth undershoots peers (Ozon guide +25–30%) while the loss shrinks, the "efficiency over growth" pivot is working; if seller counts fall and GMV stalls, the subsidy cut was premature.

## Earnings Review

<!-- enrichment:earnings_review -->
> **Framing.** The seller-referral closure is an incentive/marketing change, not an earnings event. The relevant financial context is Yandex's latest reported quarter — **Q1 2026 (period ended 31 Mar 2026, press release 28 Apr 2026)** — where management explicitly pivoted Yandex Market to a "more efficient promo and marketing model," cutting subsidies to narrow e-commerce losses. The ₽15K seller-referral pullback fits that documented margin-over-growth shift. Primary source: Yandex's own filing (semsearch IR DB was unavailable — OpenRouter 402 — so figures taken directly from the Q1 2026 RUS press release PDF).

**Verdict: IN-LINE with Yandex's stated strategy** — the subsidy pullback is a direct, expected extension of the Q1 2026 e-commerce profitability drive, not a surprise.

### Group (Q1 2026 vs Q1 2025)
- Revenue **₽372.7bn, +22% YoY** (₽306.5bn prior).
- Adjusted EBITDA **₽73.3bn, +50% YoY** (₽48.9bn), margin **19.7%** (+3.7pp).
- Adjusted net income **₽34.7bn, +171%** (₽12.8bn).

### E-commerce segment ("Сервисы электронной коммерции" — Market, Lavka, Eda/Delivery)
| ₽bn | Q1 2026 | Q1 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| GMV (GTV) | **293.8** | 291.0 | **+1%** |
| Revenue | **123.4** | 104.8 | **+18%** |
| Adjusted EBITDA | **(6.4)** | (13.9) | **loss narrowed 54%** |
| Adj. EBITDA margin (% of GTV) | **-2.2%** | -4.8% | **+2.6pp** |

For reference, the broader City Services block (Ridetech + E-commerce + Delivery/O2O) posted revenue ₽210.5bn (+18%) and adjusted EBITDA ₽21.5bn (+161%).

### Why this explains the seller-subsidy pullback (direct quotes)
- Management states e-commerce GMV growth decelerated **because of** the subsidy pivot: *"Замедление динамики роста валового оборота сервисов Электронной коммерции связано прежде всего с переходом к более эффективной промо- и маркетинговой модели развития Яндекс Маркета."* (Slowdown in e-commerce GMV growth is driven primarily by the transition to a more efficient promo and marketing model for Yandex Market.)
- Market specifically improved its margin via lower marketing + opex: *"Маркет улучшил маржинальность по скорректированной EBITDA как процент от GTV на 3,2 п.п. год к году за счет перехода к более эффективной маркетинговой модели развития, а также снижения операционных затрат."* (Market improved adjusted-EBITDA margin as % of GTV by +3.2pp YoY via a more efficient marketing model and lower operating costs.)
- Group-level EBITDA growth was attributed in part to a *"более чем двукратное улучшение маржинальности в сервисах Электронной коммерции, прежде всего Яндекс Маркете"* (more than 2x improvement in e-commerce margin, chiefly Yandex Market).
- Stated 2026 priority for City Services: *"повышение качества экономики платформ ... оптимизацией затрат и улучшением экономики сервисов"* (improve platform unit economics — cost optimization and better service economics).

### Thesis flags (Market seller-subsidy pullback)
- **Consistent with reported direction, not a new risk.** Closing the ₽15K-per-seller referral program on 5 Oct 2026 is a textbook step in the same subsidy-reduction playbook that already cut Market/e-commerce losses by more than half YoY in Q1 2026. Payouts for qualifying Sept/early-Oct referrals still honored (per the source note), so it is a clean wind-down.
- **Growth-vs-margin tradeoff remains the key watch item.** E-commerce GMV already slowed to +1% YoY as subsidies came out; removing a seller-acquisition incentive risks further decelerating new-seller onboarding / assortment growth. The question for the thesis is whether margin gains persist without re-accelerating subsidy spend.
- **No dedicated forward GMV/e-commerce guidance** in the Q1 2026 release beyond the qualitative "improve platform economics" priority; Yandex has not disclosed seller counts or program participation.
