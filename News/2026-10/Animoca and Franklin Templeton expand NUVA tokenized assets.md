*269 слов · direct*

Animoca Brands and Franklin Templeton are expanding NUVA’s planned access to institutional tokenized real-world assets, broadening the marketplace beyond products linked to the Provenance Blockchain.
The companies announced the strategic collaboration Oct. 9, 2026, and launched a four-part research series focused on tokenization, real-world assets and the infrastructure used by institutional investors. They are also exploring the co-design of tokenized cultural assets, with further details expected later in 2026.
NUVA is a vault marketplace co-incubated by Animoca Brands and Nuva Labs. It was developed as a distribution platform connecting issuers of real-world assets with investors and was designed to support tokenized assets across blockchain networks.
NUVA’s earlier launch plan centered on vaults backed by Figure Technologies’ YLDS and home equity line of credit assets on Provenance. Provenance held more than $30 billion in total value locked as of Sept. 24, 2026.
Franklin Templeton had $1.83 trillion in assets under management as of Aug. 31, 2026. The firm has also developed blockchain-based investment products, while the new research series examines how tokenization may affect asset access, portfolio construction and institutional workflows.
“The convergence of traditional finance and digital assets has gone from the theoretical to being a real infrastructure play,” Yat Siu, co-founder and executive chairman of Animoca Brands, said.
Sandy Kaul, head of digital assets and innovation at Franklin Templeton, said tokenization is “moving beyond simply putting traditional assets on blockchain rails.”
The collaboration’s next disclosed milestone is the release of further details on the potential tokenized cultural assets later in 2026.

# Context-enrichment: Animoca Brands × Franklin Templeton expand NUVA tokenized assets
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5. Freshness: fresh (new FT counterparty on NUVA), but pre-product._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On Oct 8-9, 2026 Animoca Brands and Franklin Templeton (FT) announced a "strategic collaboration" to broaden **NUVA** — a RWA vault marketplace co-incubated by Animoca and **Nuva Labs** — beyond products linked to Figure Technologies' Provenance Blockchain. Three concrete deliverables: (a) a stated *plan* to integrate FT tokenized products into NUVA; (b) a live **four-part research series** on tokenization; (c) an *exploration* of co-designing "tokenized cultural assets," details "later in 2026."
- **This is a planning/intent-stage announcement, not a live product.** No FT product is named (BENJI/FOBXX not cited), no chain, no ticket size, no fee/economics, no committed capital, no AUM target, no launch date.
- **Why framed this way — and what it reveals:** The PR leans on two flattering anchor numbers — FT's **$1.83T AUM** (Aug 31, 2026) and Provenance's **">$30B TVL"** (Sep 24, 2026). The $30B figure does NOT reconcile with independent trackers: **DeFiLlama puts Provenance TVL at ~$1.2-1.3B** (ATH ~$1.2B early 2026). The $30B most plausibly conflates cumulative loan originations / notional represented-asset value with locked value (Figure separately claims ~$15B of the ~$20B tokenized private-credit market and >$50B lifetime volume). So the headline anchor is ~20x inflated vs on-chain TVL. (analysis)
- **Hidden provenance:** "co-incubated by Animoca + Nuva Labs" obscures that **Nuva Labs = the former Provenance Blockchain Labs / ProvLabs**, rebranded Sep 30, 2025. So the party "expanding beyond Provenance" is itself Provenance-rooted — the "neutral multichain" framing is softer than it sounds. (analysis)

## [1] Competitors / peers
Tokenized RWA market (ex-stablecoins) ≈ **$26-32B** distributed value mid-2026 (methodology varies). Direct peers in tokenized yield/Treasuries:
- **BlackRock BUIDL** (via Securitize): ~$2.25-2.8B, 9 networks (declined intra-month Sep→Oct 2026).
- **Ondo** (OUSG + USDY): ~$3.5-3.8B total; USDY ~$2.15B. Ondo also just launched **Ondo Private Markets** [[Ondo launches Ondo Private Markets for tokenized exposure]] (Oct 7, 2026) — tokenized pre-IPO exposure, a more concrete, shipped product than NUVA's announcement.
- **FT BENJI/FOBXX** itself: ~$2.5B on-chain across ~9 chains in 2026 — i.e. FT already has a live, larger tokenized rail of its own.
- **Figure / Provenance:** the source of NUVA's launch vaults (nvYLDS / nvPRIME).
**Position:** NUVA is a *distribution venue* (marketplace), not an asset. It is behind BUIDL/Ondo/BENJI on live AUM (NUVA's own TVL is undisclosed → likely small). **Second-order:** an asset manager with its own live rail (BENJI, $2.5B) choosing to also distribute via a third-party vault venue signals NUVA's value prop is reach/composability, not scale — and FT's commitment here (research + intent) is cheap. (analysis)

## [2] Company history / fit
- **Franklin Templeton** ($1.83T AUM) is a genuine tokenization pioneer: FOBXX (Franklin OnChain US Govt Money Fund) with the **BENJI** token launched 2021 on Stellar, now ~9 chains, >$2.5B on-chain in 2026; exec Sandy Kaul (quoted here) said in 2025 FT expects all products on-chain within a decade [[Franklin Templeton expects all products onchain within a decade]]. FT has a serial-partnership pattern: Kraken/Payward [[Payward and Franklin Templeton collaborate on tokenized assets]], DigiFT in Asia [[Franklin Templeton and DigiFT expand tokenized securities access in Asia]], DBS, Binance. NUVA is one more distribution tie-up in that pattern — low marginal cost, optionality.
- **Animoca Brands** is a HK Web3/gaming conglomerate (The Sandbox, Mocaverse, 600+ portfolio cos). RWA is an adjacency; it is building regulated institutional capability (ADGM in-principle fund-manager approval, Dec 2025 [[Animoca Brands gets ADGM in-principle fund manager approval]]). NUVA (with Nuva Labs) is its main institutional-RWA vehicle. **Why:** Animoca needs an institutional, non-gaming revenue narrative; attaching a $1.83T brand to NUVA is distribution credibility. (analysis)

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novelty:** low-to-moderate. The genuinely new fact is a *new counterparty* (FT) publicly named on NUVA — not a new capability. NUVA has been **live on Ethereum since ~May 2026** with nvYLDS (Figure YLDS) and nvPRIME (Figure HELOC) vaults; this is expansion of issuers, not a new primitive.
- **Traction:** the only shipped deliverable is a research series (marketing). The "cultural assets" angle is pure exploration — no asset, issuer, or regulatory wrapper defined.
- **Who captures margin:** NUVA aims to be the distribution/marketplace layer earning on flow; but FT already owns BENJI rails and could list on Ondo/Securitize/its own stack — so NUVA has little leverage and unclear exclusivity. If FT's assets are fungible across venues, NUVA is a commoditized storefront, not a toll road. (analysis/hypothesis)

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- Next disclosed milestone: more detail on tokenized cultural assets "later in 2026"; research-series parts; eventual (unscheduled) FT product listing in a NUVA vault.
- **Sentiment:** RWA tokenization remains a hot institutional theme (BUIDL/Ondo/BENJI all scaling), which is the tailwind this announcement rides. **Risk/counterintuitive:** reliance on Provenance/Figure anchor assets ties NUVA to Figure, which has faced a short-seller report (Morpheus Research) — a reputational/disclosure overhang for NUVA's base assets. Until a named FT product goes live with disclosed size/terms, this reads as a low-commitment relationship + marketing announcement. Upgrade to 3-4 if a concrete FT listing with AUM lands.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** RWA tokenization (tokenized treasuries / money-market / liquidity funds + private-market exposure) is the subvertical. Size: on-chain RWA value (ex-stablecoins) was ~$32bn in May 2026 and ~$38.8bn by mid-Sept 2026, up >200% YoY; treasuries are ~40% (~$12.9bn, largest/most-liquid category) per RWA.xyz data via pymnts/coincentral. Long-run TAM is the much-cited BCG/Standard Chartered "$16tn by 2030" figure (analyst projection, not a fact; treat as directional). Structure: still fragmented and early — issuers (asset managers), tokenization/chain platforms (Provenance, Stellar, Ethereum, Solana, BNB), and distribution/vault layers (NUVA, Securitize, Ondo) sit in separate hands; value today accrues mostly to the issuer + the chain, with distribution/marketplace economics unproven. Barriers: regulation (securities law, accredited/institutional gating), custody/administration (J.P. Morgan admin+custody in the Sygnum/Fidelity deal), and distribution/network. Why now: regulatory thaw for on-chain securities + asset managers (BlackRock BUIDL >$3bn AUM May 2026; Franklin's BENJI/FOBXX) racing to build on-chain distribution, so a neutral multi-issuer vault marketplace is the logical next layer.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: AUM/TVL on-chain, number of issuers/assets listed, holders, yield (APY), and — for a marketplace specifically — TVL that is actually live/traded vs merely "planned access." Key players & basis of competition: Securitize (BlackRock BUIDL issuance/transfer agent), Ondo (tokenized treasuries + new Ondo Private Markets, [[Ondo launches Ondo Private Markets for tokenized exposure]]), Superstate, Sygnum/Desygnate (distributing Fidelity's tokenized liquidity fund, [[Sygnum brings Fidelity tokenized liquidity fund to Singapore]]), Figure/Provenance (NUVA's original rail). Compete on issuer relationships, regulatory licensing, and breadth of asset menu rather than price. Recent peer moves: Ondo Private Markets launched week of 2026-10-07; Sygnum brought Fidelity's first tokenized liquidity fund to Singapore 2026-10-06 (category sized by Sygnum at >$15bn). Protagonist's position: NUVA is catching-up/niche (analysis) — a distribution/vault marketplace, not an issuer. Its differentiator is pairing a Tier-1 asset manager (Franklin, $1.83tn AUM as of 2026-08-31 per the note) with Animoca's Web3/distribution + a cultural-asset angle. Moat is thin today (analysis): the real moat would be exclusive issuer supply or network effects on the vault side, neither yet demonstrated.

**Comps & multiples.** Public market-cap/EV multiples are not meaningfully computable here — the news is a partnership, not a round, and the two protagonists are private (Animoca) / a segment of a listed parent (Franklin Resources, NYSE: BEN), so EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA = no data for the NUVA JV. Reference points instead:
- Franklin Templeton: $1.83tn group AUM (2026-08-31, note); its tokenized flagship BENJI/FOBXX grew ~$594m (Jan 2026) to >$2.5bn (Jul 2026), across 9 chains, ~3.66% 30-day APY — i.e. Franklin already ships a rival tokenized product, so NUVA is complementary distribution, not a new capability.
- Animoca: last disclosed primary raise $358.9m at >$5bn valuation (2021); has nine-figure revenue, profitable 4 yrs; pursuing a Nasdaq reverse-merger (with Currenc Group) at up to ~$5bn (announced Nov 2025). Round-valuation only, not a market cap — pre-revenue-multiple [UNSOURCED] for the tokenization unit.
- Internal funding comp: [[Superstate raises $82.5M Series B for tokenization]] (2026-01) — Series B size is a traction read on the tokenization-platform layer; no post-money disclosed, so no multiple. Peer treasury/liquidity comps: BlackRock BUIDL >$3bn AUM (May 2026); Sygnum/Fidelity (above). Distribution not computed (no ≥3 comparable valuation figures) — qualitative only.

**Risk flags.**
1. "Planned access," not live TVL. The note/PR describe *expanding NUVA's planned access* and a research series + "exploring" cultural assets "later in 2026" — no live AUM/flows on the new assets disclosed. The widely-cited "$30bn TVL" is Provenance Blockchain's TVL (2026-09-24, note), NOT NUVA's own booked assets; several outlets conflate the two. De-PR: who's silent on NUVA's actual on-platform assets, fees, and economics. (analysis)
2. Disintermediation / margin capture. As a marketplace layer, NUVA sits above issuers (Franklin, Figure) and chains (Provenance). If issuers distribute direct (as Franklin already does with BENJI) or via incumbents (Securitize), the vault layer can be bypassed and marginal economics squeezed — classic middle-of-stack risk.
3. Regulatory/eligibility concentration. These are securities gated to accredited/institutional investors; distribution scales only as fast as licensing across jurisdictions, and a single securities-law reclassification or issuer pullback would hit the asset menu.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is less a re-rating than a land-grab for the *distribution* layer of RWA: asset managers now have on-chain products (BUIDL, BENJI) and are picking marketplaces to reach Web3-native and institutional buyers (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: NUVA becomes a credible neutral RWA marketplace only if it reports actual on-platform AUM + ≥2 live Tier-1 issuers within ~2 quarters; trigger/what breaks it — the "cultural assets later in 2026" milestone slips or arrives with no hard AUM, signaling the deal is research/marketing rather than live distribution.
