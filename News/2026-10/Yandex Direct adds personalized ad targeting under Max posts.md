 «Яндекс» запускает персональный таргетинг рекламы в мессенджерах
 
 
Рекламодателям «Яндекса» стали доступны новые сценарии продвижения: реклама в мессенджерах будет показываться не только в постах каналов, но и в интерфейсе самого приложения — под контентом каналов. В новом сценарии продвижения рекламное объявление таргетируется на конкретного пользователя мессенджера: это значит подписчики одного и того же канала могут видеть разную рекламу. Новым форматом продвижения смогут воспользоваться не только бизнесы, но и владельцы каналов и инфлюенсеры для продвижения своего блога . Об этом CNews сообщили представители « Яндекса ».
Например, рекламодатели «Директа» смогут запустить продвижение под постами каналов в мессенджере Mах: на старте рекламодателям «Директа» доступно продвижение в нескольких десятках тысяч каналов по более 50 тематикам.
«Бизнес закладывает продвижение в мессенджерах в свою стратегию: приоритетными каналами до конца 2026 г. рекламодатели «Директа» назвали Telegram (67%) и MAX (56%). «Рекламная сеть Яндекса» открыта для площадок, готовых к монетизации своей аудитории, — и с каналами Mах мы тоже уже работаем с апреля этого года. Новый формат — следующий шаг усиления этого канала продвижения: и теперь, когда объявление таргетируется не на аудиторию канала, а на конкретного пользователя, оно начинает работать как перформанс-инструмент — и со временем будет конкурировать не только с продвижением на других контент-площадках, но и с поведенческой рекламой на других поверхностях», — сказал Олег Иванов , директор по развитию продуктов «Рекламной сети Яндекса».
Сегодня через « Директ » бизнесы могут запускать рекламные кампании сразу на всех ключевых контент-площадках — VK , Mах, Telegram , RuTube , YouTube , « Дзен ». C 2024 г. инвестиции рекламодателей «Яндекс Директа» в продвижение на этих площадках выросли в семь раз — во многом благодаря постоянному росту инвентаря.
Раньше ключевым критерием для прогноза эффективности продвижения в мессенджерах было наличие целевой аудитории среди подписчиков того или иного отдельного канала. Теперь рекламная система Яндекса ищет целевых пользователей среди читателей всех каналов мессенджера, а не ограничивается аудиторией одного — и сама находит внутри нее тех, кто подходит под задачи кампании.
Новый формат полезен и для продвижения самих блогов: в «Директе» можно привлекать подписчиков — на канал бренда или на конкретные посты — и платить только за клики по рекламе, а не за ее показы. Достаточно при запуске кампании указать комфортную целевую стоимость подписчика, а дальше рекламная система «Яндекса» сама оптимизирует размещения под этот показатель. Продвигать канал можно не только в самом мессенджере Mах, но и за его пределами: в «Поиске» и на площадках « Рекламной сети Яндекса ». Для этого достаточно указать ссылку на канал — «Директ» сам подберет настройки кампании и даже бесплатно создаст лендинг для переходов с рекламы.
Запустить рекламную кампанию с новым форматом продвижения можно в «Директе»: в «Единой перформанс-кампании» и в «Простом старте».

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** On 2026-10-07, Yandex added **per-user (personalized) ad targeting** to the "ads in messengers" format inside its **Yandex Direct** platform, aimed at channels in the **Max messenger**. Two things changed: (a) ads can now appear not only inside channel *posts* but in the app interface *under* a channel's content, and (b) each ad is now targeted at an *individual user* rather than at a channel's whole subscriber base — so two subscribers of the same channel can see different ads. Yandex's system now searches for suitable users across *all* Max channels' readers (not just one channel's audience) to match a campaign's goals. At launch the format covers "several tens of thousands" of Max channels across 50+ topic categories. A companion simplified flow ("channel promotion") lets channel owners/influencers acquire subscribers on a cost-per-click basis against a target cost-per-subscriber, with Yandex auto-optimizing placement and even auto-generating a landing page; promotion runs both inside Max and across Yandex Search + the Yandex Advertising Network (РСЯ). Campaigns launch via Direct's "Unified Performance Campaign" and "Simple Start." (Sources: cnews.ru 2026-10-07; sostav.ru "Яндекс добавил персональный таргетинг" 2026-10; Yandex B2B adv news; adindex.ru 2026-10-07.)

**[1] Why it matters (fintech/infra angle).** This is the **demand-side** counterpart to VK's supply-side move the same week ([[VK opens Max channel ads to accounts over 1K subscribers]]). Max is Russia's state-backed "national messenger"/super-app (messaging + gov services + digital ID + planned payments), pre-installed on devices sold in Russia since ~Sept 2025. The ad layer on top of it is now genuinely **two-sided**: VK sells its own channel inventory via VK Реклама, while Yandex Direct pipes its performance-advertiser demand and РСЯ ad-tech into the same surface. By shifting from channel-audience buying to **per-user performance targeting**, Yandex reframes Max inventory as a performance (behavioral-style) channel that will "compete not only with other content platforms but with behavioral advertising on other surfaces" (Oleg Ivanov, head of product development, Рекламная сеть Яндекса). For the Russian digital-infra beat, this is the ad-tech monetization of a state-mandated distribution surface.

**[2] Numbers / scale.** Advertiser investment via Yandex Direct into content-platform promotion has grown **~7x since 2024**, driven by expanding inventory. Yandex's own 2026 survey of Direct advertisers put priority messenger channels through end-2026 at **Telegram 67%** and **MAX 56%**. Yandex has worked with Max channels since **April 2026**. The broader Max beta via Direct reportedly spans 300k+ public and 6.7M+ private channels (РСЯ auto-selection), with Max cited at 60M+ daily active users. Context on Yandex's size: Q2 2026 group revenue ~₽386bn (+16% YoY), with ~half still from contextual advertising (Direct) — so Max channel ads are a small incremental inventory add onto Yandex's core ad engine, not a needle-mover at group level (per AOL/Global Banking & Finance on Yandex Q2 2026).

**[3] Timeline / sequence.** **April 2026** — РСЯ begins working with Max channels. **May 18, 2026** — "ads in messengers" (Telegram + Max) launches in Direct beta, pay-per-click, topic/geo targeting at channel level. **Sept 25, 2026** — Max itself opens ad impressions testing inside channels. **Oct 2026** — VK launches the self-serve targeted cabinet via VK Реклама for 1,000+ channels ([[VK opens Max channel ads to accounts over 1K subscribers]]). **Oct 7, 2026** — *this item*: Yandex Direct adds per-user personalized targeting + under-content placement + channel-promotion flow. Same week Yandex also launched a travel-vertical ad network ([[Travel services launch joint ad network with Yandex and Tutu]]). Regulatory backdrop: the Bank of Russia in **March 2026** called confirming financial operations via Max "premature" ([[Russia's central bank calls MAX transaction approval premature]]) — the payments leg of the Max super-app is still gated, so this remains an ad-tech, not a payments, story.

**[4] Open questions.** CPM/CPC pricing and whether per-user targeting delivers real performance lift vs. the old channel-audience buying; how Yandex's РСЯ demand interacts/competes with VK's own VK Реклама cabinet on the same Max inventory (duplicate impressions, price war, or complementary demand?); whether the April-2026 "tens of thousands of channels" are the same inventory VK gates at 1,000 subscribers; ad-marking/ОРД (RF ad-registration) compliance on Max; brand-safety on a state-linked, unencrypted messenger; and whether the "7x since 2024" growth figure is content-platforms broadly (VK/Telegram/RuTube/YouTube/Дзен) rather than Max specifically. No disclosed Max-specific revenue.

Overlap is the shared Max surface, not the event.
3. "Personalized targeting" — is this genuinely behavioral/per-user, or just better channel-topic matching dressed up? Yandex says ads target a specific user across all channels' readers, implying behavioral; but no disclosure of the data/signals used.

7. Is Max's "60M+ DAU" organic or inflated by pre-install mandates and the blocking of WhatsApp (Feb 2026) / Telegram (Mar 2026)? Reach may not equal engaged, ad-receptive attention.
8. CPM/CPC and performance evidence are undisclosed — without them, the "works like a performance tool / competes with behavioral ads" claim is a vendor assertion, not a result.
9. How do VK Реклама and Yandex Direct demand coexist on the same Max inventory? Risk of double-counted impressions, bidding conflicts, or one platform reselling the other — unclarified.
10. Does this touch fintech/payments at all, or is it purely ad-tech? Max's payments leg is still gated (CBR "premature," Mar 2026) — relevance to the fintech beat is via infra/super-app, not payments.
11. Ad-marking/ОРД compliance: is per-user targeted advertising on Max registered under RF ad-marking law, and who is the operator of record (Yandex or Max/VK)?

13. Single-primary-source risk: cnews/sostav/adindex all trace to Yandex's own press statement on the same day — is there any independent corroboration beyond the vendor?
14. Sequencing confusion: note date is 2026-10-12 (ingest) but the event is 2026-10-07 (announcement) — ensure the dated framing uses Oct 7.
15. Does "channel promotion on CPC with target cost-per-subscriber + auto-landing-page" overstate automation, or is it a genuinely new self-serve creator-acquisition flow? Materiality for the creator-economy read.

**Importance: 3/5 — rationale.** A real product/ad-tech step: Yandex Direct turning Max channel inventory into a per-user performance surface is the demand-side half of the Russian state-messenger monetization story, pairing with VK's supply-side cabinet the same week. It has dated specifics (Oct 7, April-2026 РСЯ start, 7x growth, 50+ topics) and a clear competitive read (two-sided Max ad market; Telegram-playbook mimicry). But it is single-origin (Yandex's own announcement), early/incremental against Yandex's ~₽386bn/qtr ad engine, limited to a sanctioned-market messenger, and only tangential to core fintech (payments still gated). Solidly relevant to the Russian digital-infra/ad-tech beat; not a top-tier global fintech event.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian digital/online advertising. Per AKAR (via tadviser.com), the total ad market crossed ₽1tn for the first time in 2024 and ~₽1.236tn incl. retail media; the online segment reached ₽470bn in 2024 (~52% of total). VK Ads cited +20% online-ad growth in 2025 (per VK Ads, via imarcgroup, as of 2025) — so a large, still-growing base. Structure: concentrated at the top (Yandex + VK), with a long tail of channels/platforms; value sits in the demand-side ad engine (targeting/auction + attribution), not in any single surface. Why now: the forced exit of Google/Meta ad inventory from Russia since 2022 re-routed budgets to domestic platforms, and the state-backed Max messenger (pre-installed since ~2025) is opening a brand-new, under-monetized inventory pool. This specific move — moving Max ads from channel-level to **per-user** targeting — is the pivot from a reach/brand buy toward a performance product.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: ad turnover/gross billings, inventory volume (impressions), CPC/target-cost-per-action, and share of the search/ad-tech stack. Key players: Yandex (Рекламная сеть Яндекса / Direct) as the dominant demand engine; VK (VK Реклама) on the supply/social side, which on the *same day* (2026-10-12) opened targeted ads in Max channels with a 1,000-subscriber floor — the mirror move on the publisher side [[VK opens Max channel ads to accounts over 1K subscribers]]. Basis of competition: breadth of inventory + quality of targeting/auction, not price. Recent moves: Yandex also launched a travel-vertical ad network with Tutu, Level Travel et al. [[Travel services launch joint ad network with Yandex and Tutu]], and per the note has worked with Max channels since April 2026; advertiser investment in these content surfaces via Direct is up ~7x since 2024 (per Yandex, via cnews.ru). Protagonist position: **ahead** on the demand side — Direct aggregates VK/Max/Telegram/RuTube/Dzen inventory into one cabinet, so it monetizes Max regardless of who owns the surface. Moat: network effects (largest advertiser + inventory pool) + the search/ad-tech data stack feeding per-user targeting `(analysis)`.

**Comps & multiples.** Yandex is listed (MOEX: YDEX) but does not break out a clean "advertising-only" line or an ad-segment EV, so pure ad multiples = no data. IR grounding (PRIMARY, Yandex Q1 2026 press release, 2026-04-28): group revenue **₽372.7bn, +22% YoY**; advertising-&-promotion turnover **₽115.4bn, +9% YoY** (growing slower than the group — ads are now the *mature* core, not the growth engine); "Search services & AI" segment revenue **₽122.6bn, −1% YoY** with adj. EBITDA margin **43.1%** (down 2.7pp); Yandex Russian search share **69.8%**. Group adj. EBITDA ₽73.3bn (+50%), 19.7% margin; 2026 guidance ~20% revenue growth, ~₽350bn adj. EBITDA. Comparability: no listed Russian pure-play ad peer of similar scale; VK is private-market/less comparable and loss-making on this layer. Distribution not computed — qualitative comparison only. Flag: the ad line growing (+9%) well below group (+22%) is **in-line with a saturating core**; the new Max performance format is an attempt to re-accelerate it.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Ad turnover decelerating / core saturation.** Advertising-&-promotion turnover +9% vs group +22% and Search-segment revenue −1% YoY (Yandex Q1 2026 IR) — the ad engine is maturing; Max per-user targeting is needed to defend growth, and if adoption is slow the cash-cow margin (43%) is at risk. Second-order: pressure to over-monetize messenger surfaces could dent user experience/regulatory goodwill.
2. **Dependence on someone else's rails (Max).** Yandex sells ads *inside* a state-backed messenger it doesn't control; VK/the Max operator sets inventory access, floors and rules (see the same-day VK channel-ads launch). If Max tightens terms or builds its own demand engine, Yandex's performance-ad economics on that surface get squeezed. Second-order: disintermediation — the surface owner captures the margin.
3. **Regulatory / targeting-data risk.** Per-user behavioral targeting inside a "national messenger" is politically sensitive in RU (ad-labelling/ERID rules, data-localization, possible limits on cross-channel profiling). Second-order: a rule change could force reversion to channel-level (reach) targeting, collapsing the performance thesis.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is Yandex converting Max from a brand/reach surface into a *performance* channel that competes with behavioral ads elsewhere — a potential re-rating of messenger inventory from CPM-brand to CPC/CPA-performance economics `(analysis)`. Falsifiable thesis: if the format works, Yandex's advertising-&-promotion turnover growth re-accelerates above group revenue growth within 2-3 quarters (watch the Q4-2026 / Q1-2027 IR ad line). Trigger/catalyst: Max active-user and inventory scale + whether VK (supply) and Yandex (demand) stay complementary or start competing for the same ad dollar. What breaks it: ad turnover growth stays stuck <10% or regulators curb per-user targeting.
