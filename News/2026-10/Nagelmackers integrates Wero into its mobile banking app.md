# Nagelmackers integrates Wero into its mobile banking app

Nagelmackers blijft zijn digitale dienstverlening verder ontwikkelen om u een steeds eenvoudigere, gebruiksvriendelijkere en veiligere bankervaring te bieden. Vanaf 20 oktober 2026 is een eerste Wero-functionaliteit beschikbaar in de Nagelmackers Mobile Banking-app: het betalingsverzoek. Dankzij Wero kunt u rechtstreeks vanuit uw vertrouwde bankomgeving in enkele seconden betalingsverzoeken aanmaken en betalen.
 Wero, de Europese betaaloplossing  
 Wero is een op Europese schaal ontwikkelde betaaloplossing die dagelijkse betalingen eenvoudiger maakt. Wero wordt geleidelijk uitgerold, en vandaag stelt Nagelmackers alvast de functionaliteit voor betalingsverzoeken ter beschikking. 
Concreet kunt u: 
 een betalingsverzoek aanmaken en delen via een QR-code of betaallink;  
 een ontvangen betalingsverzoek betalen door de QR-code te scannen of op de betaallink te klikken, waarna u het verzoek kunt goedkeuren of weigeren.  
Op termijn zal Wero onder meer ook de mogelijkheid bieden om: 
 betalingen in winkels uit te voeren;  
 online aankopen te doen;  
 geld rechtstreeks te verzenden naar of op te vragen via een gsm-nummer.  
Het doel is een moderne Europese betaaloplossing aan te bieden die rechtstreeks geïntegreerd is in bankapplicaties en ontworpen werd om tegemoet te komen aan nieuwe digitale gebruiksvormen. 
 Het proces is eenvoudig, snel en verloopt rechtstreeks vanuit uw vertrouwde bankomgeving. 
Wanneer kunt u Wero gebruiken? 
De functionaliteit voor betalingsverzoeken is bijzonder handig om dagelijkse uitgaven te delen.
 Bijvoorbeeld om: 
 een restaurantrekening op te splitsen;  
 een gezamenlijk cadeau te organiseren;  
 de kosten van een reis of uitstap te verdelen;  
 uw aandeel van een gezamenlijke aankoop terug te krijgen;  
 uitgaven tussen vrienden of familie eenvoudig te beheren.  
 U hoeft geen bankgegevens of rekeningnummers meer uit te wisselen. Enkele klikken volstaan. 
De voordelen van Wero
Door Wero te gebruiken via uw Nagelmackers Mobile Banking-app geniet u van een oplossing die de volgende voordelen biedt: 
Snelheid
Maak en deel een betalingsverzoek in enkele seconden.
Eenvoud 
Een QR-code of betaallink volstaat om een betaling te starten. 
Veiligheid
Alle verrichtingen gebeuren binnen een beveiligde bankomgeving.
Integratie in uw bankapp
U hoeft geen extra applicatie te installeren. Wero is rechtstreeks beschikbaar in uw Nagelmackers Mobile Banking-app.
 Een oplossing die geleidelijk verder wordt uitgebreid  
De lancering van het betalingsverzoek vormt de eerste stap in de uitrol van Wero bij Nagelmackers. 
 In de komende maanden worden stapsgewijs nieuwe functionaliteiten toegevoegd om de gebruikerservaring verder te verrijken en de mogelijkheden van deze Europese betaaloplossing uit te breiden.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** From 20 October 2026, Nagelmackers — Belgium's oldest private bank (founded 1747) — switched on its first Wero functionality inside its Mobile Banking app: peer-to-peer **payment requests**. Users create and share a request via QR code or payment link; the recipient scans/opens it and approves or declines, all inside the bank app with no account-number exchange. Store payments, online purchases and GSM-number money transfers are promised "in the coming months" as later phases. Source: [nagelmackers.be](https://www.nagelmackers.be/nl/onze-inzichten/betaaloplossingen/wero-komt-naar-nagelmackers-de-europese-betaaloplossing-geintegreerd-in-uw-mobile-banking-app), [thepaypers.com](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/nagelmackers-integrates-wero-payment-requests-into-mobile-banking-app).

**[1] Who is Wero / EPI.** Wero is the mobile wallet and instant account-to-account (A2A) payment solution of the European Payments Initiative (EPI), built on top of SEPA Instant. Launched in Germany (July 2024), France (Sept 2024) and Belgium (Nov 2024), it had ~52–58M registered users by mid/late-2026. EPI explicitly frames Wero as a tool of European "payment sovereignty" — a counterweight to US-controlled Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Internal: [[Belgian banks join EPI to expand Wero wallet across Europe]], [[Wero launches e-commerce payments in Belgium]], [[Europe's local payment systems gain ground with Wero]].

**[2] Why this matters — new bank, not a founding member.** Belgium's original EPI member banks already had Wero in their apps from the Nov-2024 launch: Belfius, BNP Paribas Fortis, Fintro, Hello bank!, ING, KBC and CBC ([[Belgian banks join EPI to expand Wero wallet across Europe]]). Nagelmackers was NOT in that group. This is therefore a *widening of the adoption base to a smaller, private/wealth-focused bank* — the same "long-tail bank joins Wero" pattern seen with [[Nickel integrates Wero into its app]] and [[BoursoBank integrates Wero into its payment solutions]] in France. It signals Wero is moving past the big founding retail banks toward near-universal Belgian bank coverage.

**[3] Belgian backdrop — Payconiq→Wero migration.** Belgium is mid-transition from the domestic Payconiq by Bancontact system to Wero + Bancontact Pay through 2026–2027; the Payconiq brand is being retired and QR flows routed to Wero/Bancontact. Wero also went live for Belgian e-commerce in March 2026 ([[Wero launches e-commerce payments in Belgium]], Ahold Delhaize/Veepee as first merchants), and EPI joined nexo standards in Oct 2026 to push A2A acceptance into physical retail ([[EPI joins nexo standards to support Wero acceptance]]). Nagelmackers turning on P2B/P2P requests slots into this national shift. Source: [bnpparibasfortis.be](https://www.bnpparibasfortis.be/en/public/article/wero-bancontact-replaces-payconiq).

**[4] Ownership angle (watch-item).** Nagelmackers passed from Delta Lloyd to China's Anbang (2014/2015, ~€296M), then to state-run Dajia Insurance after Anbang's seizure. In 2024–25 Dajia agreed to sell it to **Caisse d'Epargne Hauts-de-France (Groupe BPCE, France)** as part of Anbang's final global divestment. So a Chinese-owned→French-owned Belgian private bank adopting a Franco-German-Belgian sovereign wallet is a tidy illustration of EPI's "keep European payments in Europe" thesis. Source: [caixinglobal.com](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2025-07-04/dajia-insurance-closes-book-on-anbangs-global-empire-with-final-asset-sales-102337873.html), [investmentofficer.com](https://cms.investmentofficer.com/en/news/bpce-acquire-belgian-bank-nagelmackers), [Wikipedia: Banque Nagelmackers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banque_Nagelmackers).

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team / skeptical review**

1. *Is this a material event or routine PR?* — Mostly incremental. It is one small private bank enabling ONE Wero feature (payment requests). Low standalone significance; matters only as a datapoint in Wero's Belgian adoption curve. [answered]
2. *Was Nagelmackers already a Wero/EPI member?* — No. It is absent from the Nov-2024 Belgian founding list (Belfius, BNP Paribas Fortis, Fintro, Hello bank!, ING, KBC, CBC), so this is a genuinely new bank onboarding, not a re-announcement. [answered]
3. *Is this a duplicate of an earlier note?* — No. No prior note mentions Nagelmackers; prior Belgian Wero notes cover the 2024 launch, 2026 e-commerce and the nexo/acceptance story. Fresh. [answered]
4. *What exactly went live vs. promised?* — Live: P2P/P2B payment requests via QR/link. Promised later ("coming months"): in-store payments, online purchases, GSM-number transfers. Press release gives no dates for phase 2+. [answered]
5. *How many customers does this reach?* — Nagelmackers is a wealth/private bank (~€13B AUM, local-office network); customer base is small vs. KBC/BNPPF. So the volume impact on Wero's ~52–58M users is marginal. [answered]
6. *Does it displace Bancontact/Payconiq here?* — Not directly stated. Belgium-wide Payconiq→Wero/Bancontact Pay migration is underway through 2026–27, but the Nagelmackers release only describes adding Wero requests, not retiring anything. [open]
7. *Is "European sovereignty" claim clean?* — Partly undercut: reports note parts of Wero's infrastructure run on AWS (US cloud). Sovereignty is governance/scheme-level, not fully stack-level. [answered]
8. *Ownership — Chinese or French now?* — In transition. Historically Anbang→Dajia (Chinese state); announced sale to Groupe BPCE (Caisse d'Epargne Hauts-de-France). Deal timing/closing status not confirmed in the Wero press item — treat ownership as "being transferred to BPCE." [open]
9. *Does Wero actually beat cards here?* — Unproven for retail. A2A P2P requests are strong (free, instant), but in-store/e-com acceptance vs. entrenched Bancontact/cards is still nascent (EuroShop reports retailer reticence in 2026). [open]
10. *Source quality?* — Primary is Nagelmackers' own marketing page (promotional, Dutch). Corroborated by thepaypers. No independent volume/uptake data. [answered]
11. *Any regulatory/PSD angle?* — None flagged; standard SEPA Instant / EPI scheme rails. [answered]
12. *Is the "oldest Belgian bank (1747)" framing load-bearing?* — Color, not substance; useful narrative hook (old private bank + modern sovereign wallet) but no business impact. [answered]
13. *Follow-ups to watch.* — (a) BPCE deal close + whether it changes Wero strategy; (b) phase-2 rollout dates; (c) whether remaining non-EPI Belgian banks follow, nearing full coverage. [open]

**Importance: 2/5 — routine incremental adoption by a small private bank of a single Wero feature; meaningful only as another tick in Wero/EPI's Belgian rollout and the broader card/PayPal-vs-A2A sovereignty story, not as a standalone event.**

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Wero sits in European A2A (account-to-account) instant payments, EPI's sovereign-wallet push against Visa/Mastercard/PayPal. Size: Europe's real-time-payments market ~$8.11bn in 2026, projected ~$19.46bn by 2034 (~11.56% CAGR 2026–34, per Market Data Forecast). A2A transaction value forecast to exceed ~€850bn in Europe in 2026, and A2A was ~17% of domestic e-commerce transaction value in 2024 (per Databook/industry citations, via WebSearch). Structure: fragmented national A2A silos (iDEAL NL, Bizum ES, BLIK PL, Vipps MobilePay Nordics, Bancontact BE) that Wero is federating, not displacing; barriers = bank-consortium distribution, scheme governance, regulatory capital. "Why now": the EU Instant Payments Regulation (Reg (EU) 2024/886) mandated SEPA-Instant origination + Verification-of-Payee from 9 Oct 2025 in euro-area PSPs — this hands Wero cheap, universal instant rails and removes the "instant costs extra" friction. A2A on SEPA rails typically costs merchants 30–70% less than cards (approx., via WebSearch).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: registered users, active users, A2A transaction value/volume, P2P→merchant conversion, merchant acceptance footprint. Players: incumbents Visa/Mastercard (interchange ~0.2–0.3% EU regulated for cards) + PayPal; the EPI consortium (16 banks/PSPs) as challenger; national A2A schemes being absorbed. Basis of competition = distribution via bank apps + cost + sovereignty narrative, not product novelty. Recent moves: Wero reported ~44m users at year-one (Sep 2025, [[Wero wallet reaches 44 million European users in year one]]); ~52m by Mar 2026 ([[Nickel integrates Wero into its app]]); ~56–58m by summer/Sep 2026 with ~€7.5bn moved (per EPI, via WebSearch). Belgian-bank rollout cadence: Beobank Pay (Jul 2026, [[Beobank launches Beobank Pay with Wero integration]]), e-commerce live in BE (Mar 2026, [[Wero launches e-commerce payments in Belgium]]), plus BoursoBank/Nickel in FR. Nagelmackers' position: niche late-follower — a small Belgian private bank adding only the P2P payment-request feature (QR/link) on day one, with store/online/phone-number transfers "to follow." No moat of its own; it rides EPI's network effects (analysis). The real moat accrues to EPI/Wero (consortium distribution + cross-border A2A reach), not to any single integrating bank.

**Comps & multiples.** Both Wero (EPI Company) and Nagelmackers are private/consortium — no public market cap, no disclosed round valuation, no revenue/TPV split attributable to this integration → EV/Revenue, P/E, price-per-user all **no data**. Internal comps (same Wero-integration template, all private, no valuations): [[Nickel integrates Wero into its app]], [[BoursoBank integrates Wero into its payment solutions]], [[Beobank launches Beobank Pay with Wero integration]], [[iDEAL-to-Wero migration hits milestone in Netherlands]]. Only usable "multiple" is user-base trajectory: ~44m (Sep-25) → ~52m (Mar-26) → ~56–58m (Sep-26) ≈ +27–32% YoY on registered users; registered ≠ active, and no active-user or per-user-value figure is disclosed → engagement multiple **[UNSOURCED]**. Distribution not computed (no comparable valuation figures); qualitative only.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Registered-vs-active gap / adoption quality.** Headline user counts (~58m) are registrations, not active payers; critics have called Wero "a solution in search of a problem" ([[Opinion Wero wallet a solution in search of a problem]]). Each bank integration (incl. Nagelmackers) inflates reach but not necessarily usage — the second-order risk is a large but dormant base that never reaches card-displacing volume.
2. **Merchant economics undisclosed.** Nagelmackers launches only consumer P2P payment-requests; the contested part — merchant per-transaction pricing and chargeback/fraud-liability for store/online payments — is silent (same silence flagged in the Dutch iDEAL migration, [[Retailers and payment firms clash over iDEAL successor Wero]]). Until merchant economics and fraud-liability rules are public, the commercial model is unproven.
3. **Dependence on someone else's rail + integrator commoditization.** Nagelmackers has zero control over Wero's roadmap, pricing or governance; it is one of many near-identical bank integrations. If EPI stumbles (governance friction among 16 banks, or the Dutch iDEAL migration slips), every integrator inherits the risk with no independent fallback.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** For Nagelmackers specifically this is table-stakes parity, not differentiation — Belgian A2A is becoming a utility every bank must offer or look dated (analysis). The watch-item is EPI's conversion from registration to merchant-accepted volume: the falsifiable thesis is "Wero displaces card interchange at scale in BE/NL by end-2027." Trigger to confirm: disclosed A2A merchant transaction value + acceptance footprint (e.g., successful iDEAL→Wero cut-over). What breaks it: continued reliance on registered-user vanity metrics with no active-payment or merchant-TPV disclosure through 2027.
