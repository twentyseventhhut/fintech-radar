Yuno Becomes the first AI-Native Intelligence System for Global Payments to Integrate Coinbase Payments Acceptance for One-Click Stablecoin Checkout 
Merchants using Yuno can now enable stablecoin payments acceptance through Coinbase – with no additional development work – unlocking one-click stablecoin checkout for consumers worldwide.
 NEW YORK CITY, New York — October 5, 2026 — Yuno , AI-native intelligence system of global payments and financial services, today announced its partnership with Coinbase , the most trusted name in crypto, bringing stablecoin acceptance to merchants worldwide. The integration is complete and available globally to Yuno’s rapidly expanding merchant base.

Stablecoins are rapidly becoming part of everyday commerce: stablecoin transaction volume reached $10.2 trillion over the last 12 months, up 63 percent year over year . For consumers, particularly those in markets where card access is uneven or local currencies are volatile, digital currency is an increasingly preferred way to pay. For merchants, the challenge has been capturing that demand without rebuilding a payments stack tuned over years for performance, fraud, and acceptance.

Yuno's integration with Coinbase removes that barrier entirely. Because Yuno has fully-integrated payment acceptance through Coinbase, merchants can enable one-click stablecoin checkout through their existing Yuno integration with no additional development required.

Through the partnership, merchants on Yuno's platform gain:
 Acceptance Uplift: Stablecoin payments as an additional option alongside cards, bank transfers, and local payment methods converting consumers who already hold digital currency and reducing lost sales in markets underserved by traditional payment rails.
 Global Reach: Merchants wanting to expand quicker and want to offer an APM alternative to cards for ease of market entry and quicker settlement
 One-Click Stablecoin Checkout With Zero Additional Dev Time: Merchants enable Coinbase Payments Acceptance directly through their existing Yuno integration — no new build required . 
 FasterSettlement: As fast as same-day settlement.

The partnership is expected to deliver particular value in high-growth verticals where stablecoin demand is strongest including digital goods, gaming, travel, and cross-border commerce.

The announcement marks the next step in Yuno’s expansion of digital currency acceptance, following partnerships with Crypto.com , BVNK , and Triple-A earlier this year. As Coinbase’s first global commerce orchestration partner, Yuno will help bring one-click stablecoin checkout to merchants worldwide.

"Merchants shouldn't have to choose between staying ahead of how consumers want to pay and the stability of the payments infrastructure they've already built. By fully integrating with Coinbase we've made their entire offering available to our merchants with zero additional development work,” said Juan Pablo Ortega, Co-founder and CEO, Yuno. “This is what being ahead of the curve looks like: not just adding crypto as a checkbox, but making the most trusted name in the space a native part of every merchant's payment stack."

"Stablecoins are moving from the margins of commerce to the mainstream, and platforms like Yuno are how they reach merchants at scale,” said Peter Shenk, Head of Infrastructure Sales, Coinbase. “Yuno's global reach across payment methods, PSPs, and acquirers makes it a natural partner to extend stablecoin acceptance to businesses around the world through a single integration."

 About Yuno 
Yuno is the AI-native operating system of global payments and financial services, powering the financial infrastructure of enterprise merchants, banks, and wallets. Through one API, Yuno connects them to payins, payouts, fraud prevention, KYC/KYB, and stablecoins at global scale, so they can operate everywhere through a single integration.

Agnostic by design, Yuno offers connectivity to 1,000+ payment methods and 460+ integrations in 190+ countries, optimizing acceptance rates, reducing costs, and strengthening security through specialized AI agents that learn from every transaction on the network. Global brands run their payments on Yuno, including McDonald's, NetEase Games, GoFundMe, inDrive, and Rappi. Banks, acquirers, PSPs, and wallets use Yuno to modernize their infrastructure without replacing core systems, and to prepare for the next generation of commerce, including agentic payments.

Yuno Gives Merchants More Control at Checkout by Bringing Multi-Acquirer Routing to In-Store Payments
In-store and online payments now run on one AI-native omnichannel stack, with one ledger and one shared view of known customers.
POS Orchestration puts several acquirers and alternative payment methods (APMs) behind one compatible terminal, so merchants can choose who processes each sale, manage their merchant discount rate (MDR), and approve more sales.
Tap to Pay turns a compatible Android phone or iPhone into a card reader – no POS terminal needed.
Both products run on the same routing engine, vault, and ledger as Yuno's online payments, so merchants can start in-store only or run both channels as one.
