Kenyan fintech Cloud9 has received $500,000 from Alliance, a New York-headquartered accelerator, in its ongoing pre-seed round. This brings its total disclosed funding to $1 million.
Its pre-seed round has attracted investments from Techstars and undisclosed angel investors. The company plans to use the new funding to expand into cross-border payment corridors, improve the user experience, launch virtual and physical cards, and attract more customers.
Founded in late 2025 by Tesh Mbaabu and Mesongo Sibuti, the co-founders of Y Combinator-backed Marketforce , Cloud9 operates as a digital bank that combines banking, global payments, and business tools in one place tailored for informal businesses and retailers across Africa.   
Both founders previously co-founded Chpter, which Cloud9 acquired in an all-stock deal in August 2026. It previously acquired MTickets, a ticketing platform, for about KES 100 million ($773,000) in an all-stock transaction.
