 Gerald Segal

 FNG Exclusive … FNG has learned more details of the UK client business of Retail FX and CFDs broker FXCM, which was sold last week to rival online broker Trade Nation. FXCM UK was sold to Trade Nation ahead of the overall sale of (most of) the rest of FXCM to CFDs broker AvaTrade , announced just several days later.
FXCM UK stats
Via regulatory filings made by FXCM UK operator Stratos Markets Limited, FXCM UK brought in $10.8 million in Commission Revenue in 2025 , up by 73% from $6.3 million in 2024. We’d note that formally FXCM UK reported Revenues of $4.8 million in 2025 (and just $104K in 2024), but that was following an approximately $6 million intercompany “transfer pricing adjustment” made in each year. The $6 million adjustment is basically a significant allocation of parent company Stratos Global LLC expenses to FXCM UK.
 FXCM UK held client cash of $110.3 million as at December 31, 2025, up by 24% from $88.8 million the previous year.
Retail trading volumes at FXCM UK came in at $340 billion in 2025, or $28 billion monthly , up by 40% from 2024.
Intercompany charges
FXCM UK reported a Net Loss of $2.2 million in 2025. However it is somewhat difficult to ascertain the “real” profitability level of the company, due to intercompany charges included in its reported results. The company did report more than $12 million in Admin Expenses in 2025, consisting mainly of Staff Costs of $5.5 million for its employee base totaling 48 people, and Legal, Management and Professional fees of $3.6 million. The overall Admin Expenses are more than FXCM UK’s aforementioned $10.8 million in Commission Revenue.
FXCM UK employees
We understand from sources at the companies that no staff are moving from FXCM UK to Trade Nation with the purchase. FXCM ran much of its group function and other business lines from the FXCM UK entity.
What did Trade Nation acquire?
FNG has learned from sources at the companies that the figures reported by FXCM UK noted above include some other material business lines that are not included with the Trade Nation acquisition.
We’d also note that while the trading volume and client cash figures reported above are “real”, when it comes to Revenues the Company (i.e. Stratos Markets Limited) hedges its client trades with affiliates and is compensated in return with a commission, based upon a relative contribution to the total Stratos Group profits.
FXCM UK also earns other forms of revenue such as fees from white label arrangements with third parties to provide platform, back office and trade execution services, FX market prices and various ancillary FX and CFD related services. The Company also earns interest income on its cash balances.
We believe that most of the client cash and represented trading volume noted above will be moving to Trade Nation. But again, what that means in terms of Revenues is somewhat unclear.
