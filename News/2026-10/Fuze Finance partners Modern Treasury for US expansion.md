Fuze Finance Partners With Modern Treasury's Payments Infrastructure for U.S. Expansion
The partnership gives Fuze's customers across 100+ countries Global USD Accounts, access to U.S. payment rails, instant settlement, and stablecoin orchestration through a single integration.
 Las Vegas, NV — Money20/20 USA — [October 19, 2026] — Modern Treasury today announced that Fuze Finance, a regulated digital asset payment and wealth infrastructure provider, has selected the company’s Payment Service Provider (PSP) to accelerate Fuze’s U.S. expansion.
Fuze operates a regulated infrastructure platform that lets banks, neobanks, marketplaces, payment service providers, and payroll platforms build stablecoin-powered payment products without managing the underlying compliance and engineering complexity themselves.
Through its stablecoin orchestration, cross-border payments, and crypto payment gateway offerings, customers can move money across countries on stablecoin and fiat rails, while Fuze's embedded trading and yield products give those same customers a way to offer digital asset trading and yield generation directly inside their own apps. Fuze currently processes $10 billion in annual volume.
Fuze was founded with a primary focus on the MENA region, of which it is now the champion, and has since expanded its footprint globally. With this partnership, Fuze is now deepening its connectivity in the U.S. with broader access to U.S. payments infrastructure as a foundation for its next phase of growth.
The Modern Treasury integration allows Fuze to:
Give individuals and businesses in 100+ countries access to named U.S. accounts with unique routing and account numbers
Move funds between USDG, USDC, USDT, and dollars for users globally and in all 50 U.S. states
Offer wholesale stablecoin liquidity services
Orchestrate payins and payouts via ACH debits, ACH credits, checks, and wires
Settle payments instantly using RTP, FedNow, and push-to-card
Scale into the U.S. market on infrastructure already trusted to move over $600 billion
For Fuze, whose customers span financial institutions, fintechs and marketplaces, remittance platforms and exchange houses, businesses, and individuals, transacting across some of the most complex corridors in the world, consolidating U.S. rails, accounts, and stablecoin orchestration under one integration is a key step in scaling into the U.S. market.
"Our customers need dollar access that works, no matter where they are in the world," said Mo Ali Yusuf, Co-Founder and CEO of Fuze Finance. "Modern Treasury's software has moved over $600 billion across a myriad of rails, and that battle-tested infrastructure gives us the confidence to accelerate our growth in the United States."
Rather than spending months waiting on a bank sponsor, stitching together vendors, and building the infrastructure themselves, Fuze can now go live on U.S. rails through a single API in a matter of days.
"Fuze is solving a real problem for people and businesses who need reliable, instant access to dollars, wherever they are," said Matt Marcus, Co-Founder and CEO of Modern Treasury. "We're proud to support Fuze's growth in the U.S. market by helping them save months on their roadmap and put that time back into building for their customers around the globe."

Modern Treasury provides the most trusted infrastructure for global money movement. Trusted by leading enterprises and financial institutions, the platform helps businesses launch and scale payment experiences in days, not months, with best-in-class developer tools and a single API for both fiat and stablecoin transactions. With built-in compliance, ledgering, and reporting, Modern Treasury enables instant, reliable, and scalable money movement for companies of all sizes.

 About Fuze Finance 
Fuze is a regulated digital asset infrastructure provider that helps banks, neobanks, marketplaces, payment service providers, and payroll platforms build stablecoin-powered payment products without managing the underlying compliance and engineering complexity themselves. Through its stablecoin orchestration, cross-border payments, crypto payment gateway, and embedded trading and yield offerings, Fuze lets customers move money and offer digital asset products across more than 65 countries.
Founded with a primary focus on the MENA region, Fuze has since expanded its footprint globally and now processes $10 billion in annual volume. Fuze is backed by Galaxy Digital and e& capital, the venture capital and investment arm of technology group e&.
