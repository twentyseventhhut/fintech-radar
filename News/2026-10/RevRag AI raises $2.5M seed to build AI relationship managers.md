# RevRag AI raises $2.5M seed to build AI relationship managers

> [!info] 2026-10-15 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇮🇳 RevRag AI raises $2.5 million in seed funding to build AI relationship managers. Led by Accion Ventures and Cedar Hill Capital, the round will fund product development, more enterprise deployments and expansion into the U.S. The startup builds multilingual AI agents that help banks and financial service providers handle customer onboarding, sales and support.
