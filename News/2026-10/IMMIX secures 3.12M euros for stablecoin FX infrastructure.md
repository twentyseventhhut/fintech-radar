## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
### [0] What happened (one line)
London-based IMMIX raised €3.12M (~$3.5M) to build AI-priced FX infrastructure that lets payment providers convert between non-USD currency stablecoins more cheaply (announced 2026-10-08/09).

### [1] Facts (dated, sourced)
- Amount: €3.12M (~$3.5M). Round backed by Crane Ventures (Crane Venture Partners), crypto exchange BTSE, and Portfolio Ventures. UK press reports it as "£2.6m." (eu-startups, 2026-10; startupmag.co.uk; finsmes, 2026-10).
- Follows a prior ~€2.5M (~$2.7M) seed (reported led by MassMutual Ventures with Ripple Ventures participation, late 2023). Note: founder/round details below carry lower confidence — see [4].
- Founders: Dr. Andrew Mann and Dr. David Twomey, UCL Computer Science PhDs, ex-Morgan Stanley / JP Morgan / Virtu Financial (eu-startups).
- Product: "AI-native trading firm" building FX infrastructure for payment providers; deep-learning pricing models said to forecast conditions across thousands of instruments; claimed >$25B customer trading volume since 2024 (eu-startups; konsulteer).
- Problem framed: converting between non-dollar currency stablecoins is fragmented — a single conversion can fan out into 3–4 FX legs, each with spread, fee and prefunding; liquidity thin outside USD (eu-startups).
- Use of funds: AI pricing engine, expand liquidity, grow partner/distribution network.

### [2] Why it matters
Stablecoin payment rails are consolidating into a credible SWIFT alternative, but almost all liquidity is USD-denominated. IMMIX targets the non-USD FX seam — the exact friction that makes EUR/GBP/EM-corridor stablecoin payouts expensive. If AI pricing + aggregated liquidity genuinely collapse the 3–4-leg conversion, it is a picks-and-shovels layer under the PSPs rather than a competitor to them.

### [3] Internal links (prior notes)
- [[BVNK gets strategic investment from Citi Ventures]] — leading B2B stablecoin infra; Mastercard later agreed to acquire BVNK (reported up to $1.8B, early 2026) → the incumbent IMMIX plugs beneath.
- [[Euronet chooses Fireblocks for cross-border stablecoin payments]] — same "reduce pre-funding / liquidity management" thesis IMMIX sells.
- [[Krealo eyes B2B cross-border stablecoin payments]] — demand-side signal for B2B stablecoin corridors.
- [[Revolut launches fee-free 1 1 stablecoin conversions]] — consumer-side conversion commoditisation; contrast with IMMIX's wholesale/institutional FX angle.

### [4] Landscape / peers (external)
- BVNK (~$30B annualized volume; Mastercard deal reported up to $1.8B, 2026) and Conduit ($36M Series A, May 2025, Dragonfly/Altos; >$10B annualized volume) are the heavyweight cross-border stablecoin PSPs — potential customers OR competitors to IMMIX's FX layer. (bvnk.com; opendue.com; yespress.io)
- Bridge (Stripe-owned) is the other consolidating infra player.
- IMMIX's differentiator vs these: not the payment network itself but the non-USD FX pricing/liquidity engine underneath.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** IMMIX sits in stablecoin FX / cross-border payments infrastructure — specifically the non-USD "conversion rails" layer (converting between currency-denominated stablecoins). The broader stablecoin-infrastructure & cross-border-payment-platform market is sized at ~$7.43bn (2025) → ~$9.19bn (2026) at ~23.8% CAGR (per TheBusinessResearchCompany/GIIResearch, as of 2026). B2B stablecoin flows reached ~$226bn annualized, +733% YoY (per McKinsey/Artemis, Feb 2026, via crypto.news). The sub-vertical IMMIX targets is early and fragmented: USD-stablecoin rails are deep, but non-dollar conversion (e.g. EUR/GBP/SGD-denominated coins) is thin-liquidity, so one conversion can fan out into 3–4 FX legs, each with its own spread, fee and prefunding (per eu-startups, 2026-10-09). Why now: MiCA-driven EUR-stablecoin issuance (EURC, ODDO's EUROD) plus Asia non-USD flows are creating demand for a pricing/liquidity layer that didn't need to exist while USDC/USDT dominated.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: annualized transaction/trading volume, FX spread captured (bps), prefunding/liquidity depth, number of settlement currencies, partner distribution breadth. Basis of competition: liquidity depth + pricing quality (here AI-priced) + distribution into PSPs, not consumer brand. Key players operate one layer up (full stablecoin PSP/accounts): BVNK (~$30bn annualized volume; $750m valuation at 2024 Series B; Mastercard agreed to acquire for up to $1.8bn, Mar 2026, pending close), Bridge (Stripe-owned), Conduit (USD→local-fiat EM corridors), plus Circle/Fireblocks/Sphere. IMMIX's position: niche/upstream — it is not a PSP competitor but an FX-infrastructure supplier TO payment providers, explicitly non-USD-first. Claimed traction: >$25bn customer trading volume processed since 2024 (per eu-startups; company figure, unaudited) `[UNSOURCED beyond PR]`. Moat `(analysis)`: AI pricing engine + liquidity relationships are intangibles/network-effects that compound with flow — but thin today vs incumbents' balance sheets.

**Comps & multiples.** Pre-revenue-disclosure seed/A infra rounds; no revenue/EBITDA disclosed → EV/Revenue, P/E = no data. Round-size comps (internal, from base):
- [[Cybrid raises $10M for stablecoin infrastructure]] — $10m Series A, FI/B2B stablecoin+fiat rails (CA/US).
- [[Coinflow raises $25M to challenge Stripe]] — $25m Series A, stablecoin PSP (Pantera/Coinbase Ventures).
- [[Takenos raises $5 million seed for stablecoin wallet]] — $5m seed, consumer stablecoin wallet (AR).
- External: BVNK — $750m valuation at 2024 Series B on ~$10bn annualized volume → ~0.075x valuation/annualized-volume (round valuation, NOT revenue multiple; volume ≠ revenue, so treat as scale sanity-check only); Mastercard deal up to $1.8bn (Mar 2026).
IMMIX's €3.12m ($3.5m) is a small seed-stage round — in-line-to-small vs the $5–25m stablecoin-infra comps above; distribution not computed (mixed stages/models), qualitative comparison only. No multiple is computable; flag = no data.

**Risk flags.**
1. Thin non-USD liquidity is the whole thesis AND the whole risk — if EUR/GBP/local-coin volumes stay small, the 3–4-leg problem self-solves slowly and the addressable flow IMMIX monetizes stays tiny (second-order: revenue scales only if non-dollar stablecoins actually take off).
2. Disintermediation from the layer above — BVNK/Bridge/Circle already settle in 30+ currencies and could build FX pricing in-house or buy it, capturing IMMIX's margin; a €3.12m balance sheet can't out-fund a Mastercard/Stripe-backed stack.
3. Customer concentration + "volume ≠ revenue": $25bn "trading volume processed" is a flow stat, not disclosed take-rate or revenue; a few PSP partners likely drive most flow, so one churned partner is a cliff `(analysis)`.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` Signals early formation of a dedicated non-USD stablecoin FX layer — a "plumbing" bet that multi-currency stablecoins fragment enough to need a specialist pricer rather than each PSP DIY-ing it. Falsifiable thesis: if by ~2027 a major PSP (BVNK/Bridge) ships native non-USD FX pricing, IMMIX's standalone layer gets squeezed; conversely, a signed named-PSP distribution deal with disclosed volume would validate the wedge. Watch: next raise size/valuation and whether any partner is named with numbers.
