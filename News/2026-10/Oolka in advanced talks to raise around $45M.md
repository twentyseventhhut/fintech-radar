# Oolka in advanced talks to raise around $45M

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇮🇳 Oolka is in advanced talks to raise around $45M, with Bessemer Venture Partners and Prosus expected to invest alongside existing backers including Accel and Lightspeed. Oolka close to raising $45 million from Bessemer and Prosus
Oolka, a fast-growing AI credit management startup, is close to raising $45 million from Bessemer Venture Partners, Prosus, and existing investors like Accel and Lightspeed.
Bessemer is expected to chip in $20 million, with Prosus adding $10 million and the rest coming from current backers.
Oolka valuation rises to $200 million
Founded just two years ago by former Meesho executive Utkrishta Kumar, Oolka has quickly become a standout name in India's fintech scene.
This is their second big funding round in six months, helping boost their valuation from $90 million in April to about $200 million now.
The buzz also highlights how investors like Prosus are doubling down on AI-driven fintech startups across India lately.
