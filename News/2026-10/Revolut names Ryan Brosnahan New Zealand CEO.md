# Revolut names Ryan Brosnahan New Zealand CEO

> [!info] 2026-10-12 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🌍 Revolut appoints Ryan Brosnahan as New Zealand CEO and Piotr Bednarski and Radek Pluhar to its European Supervisory Council.Brosnahan, a former ANZ and Bendigo executive, will lead Revolut's New Zealand operations as it pursues local bank registration. Bednarski and Pluhar join Revolut Bank UAB's Supervisory Council to strengthen oversight of its European banking business.
