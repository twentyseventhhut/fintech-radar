# Archa secures A$61M in equity and debt funding

> [!info] 2026-10-14 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇦🇺 Australian FinTech Archa has secured A$61 million, comprising an A$11 million equity raise and a A$50 million debt facility. The business spend platform aims to help companies access working capital without personal guarantees, while developing AI tools to flag spending that breaches company policies. The funding will support product development and expansion.
