Selini Capital Joins zerohash's Liquidity Ecosystem, Extending Industry-Leading Execution Capabilities
On the page 
Get in touch
WASHINGTON - October 14, 2026 — zerohash, the leading onchain infrastructure provider, today announced that Selini Capital, a global digital-asset trading and investment firm, has joined zerohash's network of institutional liquidity providers, further enhancing execution quality, pricing depth, and market resiliency. Through the integration, Selini will provide continuous liquidity on zerohash's central limit order book (CLOB) and stream quotes into zerohash's Request for Quote (RFQ) system. The news was announced at DC Fintech Week .
Selini joins a growing roster of liquidity partners that provide the trading depth on zerohash's infrastructure. For zerohash's partners, this means deeper institutional liquidity, efficient execution, and greater market resiliency for end customers, without additional operational and technical complexity.
"Selini brings additional depth and sharp pricing to both our order book and our RFQ system, and that translates directly into better outcomes for everyone trading through zerohash,” said Edward Woodford, CEO and Founder of zerohash. “Our partners don't just need access to crypto markets, they need execution they can trust at scale, and adding a liquidity provider of Selini's caliber is a continued investment in that trust."
“Selini is delighted to partner with zerohash to offer our unique liquidity and competitive, resilient pricing to the zerohash liquidity ecosystem,” said Jordi Alexander, Founder of Selini Capital. 
 About Selini Capital 
Selini Capital is a leading digital-asset trading and investment firm specializing in institutional-grade OTC execution, liquidity solutions, and market connectivity. Selini leverages advanced trading technology and global networks to deliver secure and efficient digital-asset services to institutional investors worldwide.
 About zerohash 
zerohash is a leading onchain infrastructure provider, offering access to crypto, stablecoins, and tokenized assets. Its API, embeddable dev-kits and MCP suite enable innovators to seamlessly launch solutions across cross-border payments, commerce, trading, remittance, payroll, tokenization, and on/off-ramps. The company has a global regulatory footprint across the EU, Latin America, Bermuda, and the U.S., and operates regulated entities in 51 U.S. jurisdictions. For more information, visit zerohash.com .

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**What happened (2026-10-14, announced at DC Fintech Week; press release dated Oct 14, newswire Oct 15).** Zerohash — an onchain infrastructure provider offering crypto, stablecoin and tokenized-asset access via API — added Selini Capital to its network of institutional liquidity providers. Selini will stream continuous two-sided liquidity onto Zerohash's central limit order book (CLOB) and quote into its Request-for-Quote (RFQ) system. Framing from both sides: deeper institutional liquidity, tighter pricing and greater market resiliency for Zerohash's downstream partners (banks, brokerages, fintechs) "without added operational and technical complexity."

**Who is Selini Capital.** A global digital-asset trading and investment firm founded 2021 by Jordi Alexander (ex-quant trader and WSOP bracelet winner / former pro poker player), roots in TradFi high-frequency trading. ~45 employees. Core activities: systematic market-making, OTC execution, liquidity provisioning across CEX/DeFi, plus early-stage venture (reportedly ~21 crypto investments incl. Ether.fi, WOO Network, Arbelos, RedStone; was an investor in Backpack's 2024 Series A per [[Backpack Exchange raising at $1 billion valuation]]). Already an active market maker on other venues — e.g. seeding liquidity for Katana Perps alongside GSR and Auros per [[Polygon-incubated Katana acquires IDEX, launches perps platform]], and on the Talos provider network. So the Zerohash tie is an extension of an existing B2B2B liquidity-provider business, not a new strategic bet.

**Why this is a "growing roster," not a one-off.** Zerohash's liquidity ecosystem is a multi-LP aggregation layer: more LPs = more order-book depth + sharper RFQ pricing for the platforms embedding Zerohash. The direct comparable is Caladan, which joined the same ecosystem in July 2026 on near-identical terms ("aggregated digital-asset liquidity," two-sided pricing, streaming quotes). Selini is effectively the next name on that list — standard LP-onboarding cadence rather than a step-change.

**Where it sits in the Zerohash story.** Zerohash has been scaling aggressively on infra and regulation: the Mastercard ~$2B acquisition talks ([[Mastercard to acquire crypto firm Zerohash for ~$2 billion]], Oct 2025) that were later dropped, followed by an independent raise at >$1.5B valuation ([[Zerohash pursues funding above $1.5 billion valuation]], May 2026); US National Trust Bank charter application ([[Zerohash applies for US National Trust Bank charter]]); Dutch EMI license ([[Zerohash receives EMI license from Dutch central bank]]); Tempo validator seat for stablecoin rails ([[zerohash named a Tempo validator for stablecoin rails]]); and the Ava Labs "onchain readiness" survey pushing the institutional-adoption narrative ([[Zerohash and Ava Labs survey blockchain becoming standard infrastructure]]). Adding LPs deepens the execution layer that all of those distribution deals (Visa Direct, Marqeta, Worldpay, OnePay) ultimately rely on.

**Why liquidity depth matters here.** For embedded-crypto platforms, execution quality (spread, slippage, fill reliability at size) is a direct cost and a trust differentiator vs. routing to a single exchange. Aggregating multiple professional market makers reduces single-LP concentration risk and improves fills during volatility — the "market resiliency" claim. The economic win accrues to Zerohash (better product) and to Selini (more flow); end-platform customers get marginally better pricing.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** B2B crypto/stablecoin infrastructure, liquidity-provision sub-layer. Context: stablecoin market cap ~$323bn (May 2026, per news.market.us), annual on-chain volume ~$33tn; B2B share of stablecoin payments rose from 17.4% (early 2024) to 62.9% (per BeInCrypto Institutional 100) — the institutional, not retail, use-case is now dominant. The infra stack has fragmented into ~six layers (issuance / custody / orchestration / payment apps / tokenized assets / compliance); liquidity provision sits as a horizontal input feeding execution venues. Structure: Zerohash occupies the regulated B2B2C "embedded crypto" layer (API + dev-kits + MCP, 51 US jurisdictions, EU/LatAm/Bermuda footprint, >$65bn settled volume per Zerohash), and rents its order-book depth from external market-makers rather than warehousing risk itself. Why now: Wall Street onboarding (Morgan Stanley, Stripe, Interactive Brokers, BlackRock BUIDL are Zerohash clients) demands "execution you can trust at scale" — depth/latency become the competitive axis once regulatory access is table-stakes.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: order-book depth / spread tightness, settled volume, fill quality, number of LPs on the network (breadth of two-sided pricing). Basis of competition: Zerohash competes on *regulatory footprint + breadth of embedded rails*; Fireblocks competes on *MPC custody security* (1,500+ institutional clients, 50+ chains, per web); Paxos/BVNK on *issuance*. These are adjacent, not head-on — Zerohash even co-founded Fireblocks' Open Transaction Layer ([[Open Transaction Layer launches with Fireblocks, Robinhood, SoFi backing]]). Recent moves on Zerohash's LP network: Caladan joined (Jul 2026, per hokanews/enviromagazine); Selini joins Oct 14 2026 at DC Fintech Week streaming CLOB + RFQ quotes. Selini (crypto-native market-maker, HFT roots, founded 2021, Jordi Alexander; AUM not disclosed `[UNSOURCED]`) is one of several LPs, not an exclusive. Protagonist position: Selini = incremental depth supplier (niche input); Zerohash = ahead as the regulated aggregation layer. Moat (analysis): Zerohash's moat is regulatory/licensing + client switching costs, NOT liquidity — LPs are fungible and multi-home, so adding Selini deepens the book but confers no exclusivity.

**Comps & multiples.** Zerohash (private): Sep 2025 Series D-2 $104m led by Interactive Brokers at ~$1.0bn post-money; Oct 2025 Mastercard eyed acquisition at up to ~$2.0bn ([[Mastercard to acquire crypto firm Zerohash for ~$2 billion]]); Jan 2026 talks to raise $250m at $1.5bn ([[Zerohash in talks to raise $250M after dropping Mastercard deal]]); May 2026 raising above $1.5bn ([[Zerohash pursues funding above $1.5 billion valuation]]). Round-valuation re-rating ~$1.0bn → $1.5bn+ in ~8 months (+50%+), round figures not market cap. Revenue undisclosed → EV/Revenue multiple = **no data** / `[UNSOURCED]`. Internal trading-infra comps: [[Talos extends Series B to $150M in fundraise]] (institutional crypto trading infra), [[EDGE Markets raises $29.2M Series A led by CoinFund]]; Selini itself has no disclosed round. Distribution not computed (no comparable revenue multiples) — qualitative only.

**Risk flags.**
1. *LP counterparty / solvency risk.* Crypto market-makers can blow up and take venues/clients with them — see [[BlockFills suspends client withdrawals amid liquidity crunch]] → [[Susquehanna-backed BlockFills pursues formal restructuring]] → [[Keyrock to acquire BlockFills for $3.25 million pending court approval]]. A stressed LP on Zerohash's CLOB could pull quotes exactly when depth is needed most (pro-cyclical liquidity withdrawal); the PR is silent on any capital/risk vetting of LPs.
2. *Non-exclusive, low-moat announcement.* Selini is additive depth, not a strategic lock-in; Caladan did the same 3 months earlier. Marginal signal — watch for it being over-weighted as a "partnership win."
3. *Regulatory/disclosure gaps.* Zerohash disclosures note it is not SEC/FINRA-registered, no FDIC/SIPC protection, excluded in NY — so trust/resiliency claims rest on money-transmitter + VCBA licensing, not securities-grade oversight; concentration of execution through one unregulated-for-securities rail is a second-order systemic flag for its bank/broker clients.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** (analysis) Marks the maturation of Zerohash from an access/compliance layer into an *execution-quality* platform competing on book depth — a re-rating driver that helps justify the $1.5bn+ valuation push. Falsifiable thesis: if Zerohash keeps adding named LPs (Caladan, Selini, +more) and closes the raise above $1.5bn, the depth-as-moat narrative holds; it breaks if an LP defaults on the CLOB or if settled-volume growth stalls, exposing that fungible LPs give no durable edge.
