# Context-enrichment: Chinese token resellers build grey market for Claude access
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 3/5._

**FRESHNESS: fresh.** New angle (the reseller supply-chain mechanics), reported by The Information (Jing Yang, Juro Osawa, Qianer Liu, Oct 2, 2026), widely syndicated Oct 5. NOT a re-run of the Feb/Jun 2026 distillation accusations ([[Anthropic accuses Alibaba of stealing Claude]]) or the export-ban saga ([[Linas Newsletter GLM-5.2 ships 1M-token context amid Claude export ban]], [[Asian AI startups launch Mythos-like models amid Anthropic export ban]]) — those are the backdrop, this is a distinct investigation into HOW access keeps flowing despite the ban.

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
The Information documented a large, openly-operating grey market in China that resells access to US frontier models — chiefly Anthropic's Claude — despite Anthropic having no official China presence and having, on 5 Sep 2025, barred any entity >50% owned by firms headquartered in "unsupported regions" (i.e. China), a move Anthropic itself said would cost "low hundreds of millions of dollars" in revenue.

De-PR'd mechanics (from reporting, not vendor claims):
- **"Transfer stations" (中转站):** API proxies hosted on servers OUTSIDE China. A Chinese developer sends a request to the proxy; the proxy forwards it to Anthropic as if from a supported location, relays the answer back, and bills the user in RMB via WeChat/Alipay. No VPN or foreign card needed on the buyer side.
- **Pricing:** tokens sold at ~10% of official list price, i.e. roughly 70–90% off (some reports cite up to ~93%). This is NOT genuine arbitrage on Anthropic's real cost — it is subsidised by fraud/abuse upstream (see below).
- **Supply side (how the discount is manufactured):** bulk-registered Anthropic accounts (foreign SMS-verification platforms supply phone numbers; some operators stand up shell companies in SE Asia / Middle East / US to open enterprise or AWS Bedrock deals), farming the $5 free credits, exploiting education/enterprise discounts, and subdividing $200/mo Claude Max plans across dozens of users. Some accounts funded with USDT-loaded or stolen cards.
- **Scale (reported, single-source anecdote):** in one Haidian (Beijing) office building, ~6 of ~30 tenants resell US-model access; nationwide "tens of thousands" of such outfits (estimate, not audited).

**Why structured this way / what it reveals:** the market is deliberately **modular** — upstream account brokers, SMS platforms, reverse-engineering specialists, downstream Taobao/Telegram resellers — with each actor touching only 1–2 links. That makes it resilient to takedowns (killing one reseller doesn't kill the chain) and lets each layer plausibly deny the others. The 90% "discount" is the tell: it is not a pricing signal, it is the monetisation of ToS-violating account fraud and of the users' own data.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Supply-side peers (what's being resold):** Claude is the headline, but the same proxies front OpenAI/GPT and Google Gemini — a general US-model grey market, not Anthropic-specific.
- **Legit Chinese substitutes:** DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen, Zhipu GLM (e.g. [[Linas Newsletter GLM-5.2 ships 1M-token context amid Claude export ban]] — GLM-5.2 with 1M-token context, tops open-weight coding boards), Moonshot, MiniMax. The grey market exists BECAUSE a slice of Chinese developers still prefer Claude's coding/agentic quality over domestic models despite the ban.
- **Position:** the fact that a 70–90%-off black market persists is a demand signal that domestic models haven't fully closed the quality gap for certain workloads (coding, tool-use) — the exact capabilities Anthropic names as its differentiators.

**Why the lay of the land is this way (2nd order):** the grey market is a leaky-bucket proof that export controls on *software/API access* are far harder to enforce than on *chips*. Access is bits, re-exportable through any proxy; the only real chokepoints Anthropic controls are account-provisioning and payment, both of which the modular supply chain routes around.

## [2] Company history / fit
Anthropic's China posture hardened through 2026: Sep 2025 ownership-based ToS ban → Feb 2026 public accusation that DeepSeek/Moonshot/MiniMax ran ~24,000 fraudulent accounts generating 16M+ Claude exchanges to distill capabilities → Jun 2026 escalation accusing Alibaba (Qwen) of ~25k fake accounts / tens of millions of responses ([[Anthropic accuses Alibaba of stealing Claude]], importance 4), plus the US Commerce order to suspend global access to Claude Fable 5 / Mythos 5 ([[Linas Newsletter GLM-5.2 ships 1M-token context amid Claude export ban]]). This grey-market report is the "street-level" complement: the same fraudulent-account layer Anthropic complains about to Senators is what also feeds hobbyist/developer resale.

**Why Anthropic acts this way:** it is simultaneously (a) walking away from "low hundreds of millions" in China revenue for national-security/ToS reasons, and (b) unable to technically stop the leakage — so the policy is enforced via rhetoric, account bans, and lobbying for chip export controls rather than via a working access block.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novel here:** not that a grey market exists (VPN-fronted US-model access has existed since ChatGPT's China block), but the *documented supply-chain anatomy* and the **model-swapping fraud on the buyer side**: a CISPA Helmholtz study of 17 proxies found widespread silent rerouting — a request paid for as Opus 4.7 quietly served by a cheaper model; one "Gemini-2.5" endpoint scored 37% on a medical benchmark vs 83.82% official.
- **Traction / "did it fly":** the market is clearly live and at scale (multi-platform: Taobao, Telegram, GitHub), but all figures are reporting estimates, single-source, un-audited.
- **Who captures the margin:** the proxy operator — twice. Once on the token spread (arbitraging fraud-sourced cheap tokens vs RMB retail price), and again by **logging and reselling user prompts/outputs/tool-calls/reasoning traces** as training or distillation data. The buyer is simultaneously paying customer AND unpaid data source. Anthropic captures nothing and bears the brand/ToS/distillation risk.

**Why the value-add is fragile:** the "cheap Claude" is often not Claude at all (model-swap) and comes with total prompt-data exposure — so for serious/sensitive workloads the real cost (bad outputs + leaked IP) can exceed the nominal savings. (analysis)

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- Anthropic will keep tightening account provisioning and lean on chip export controls + Senator lobbying; expect more fraudulent-account purges. But the modular, cross-border structure means enforcement stays whack-a-mole. (analysis)
- The sharper the ban and the louder the distillation accusations, the more the grey market is both the *symptom* and the *mechanism* of the leakage Anthropic is trying to stop.
- **Counterintuitive 2nd-order effect:** the ban + grey market may accelerate Chinese model self-sufficiency — every month of leaky Claude access is also a month of harvested Claude traces feeding domestic distillation and of users migrating to GLM/Qwen/DeepSeek as those close the gap. The policy meant to deny capability may be subsidising its transfer. (hypothesis)
- **Fintech relevance (why this is in a fintech radar):** the payment rail IS the enforcement surface — WeChat/Alipay RMB settlement, USDT-funded and stolen cards, and shell-company AWS/enterprise contracts are what make the market work; it's an AML/KYC and sanctions-evasion story as much as an AI story.

## Red-team / challenge questions

1. **Is the "tens of thousands of resellers" figure real or anecdotal?** Open — it rests on a single reported Haidian-building observation (~6 of ~30 tenants) extrapolated nationally; no audit, no registry. Treat as directional, not a count.
2. **Is the ~90% discount genuine arbitrage or subsidised fraud?** Answered: it's fraud-subsidised — bulk/stolen-card accounts, farmed $5 free credits, split $200 Max plans, abused edu/enterprise discounts. Not a signal about Claude's true marginal cost.
3. **Are buyers actually getting Claude?** Often NO — CISPA study of 17 proxies found widespread silent model-swapping (one "Gemini-2.5" endpoint: 37% vs 83.82% official on a medical benchmark). "Cheap Claude" may be a cheaper model wearing Claude's name.
4. **How is this different from the Feb/Jun 2026 distillation story?** Distillation = Chinese labs mass-harvesting Claude to train Qwen/DeepSeek (an IP-theft accusation). This = a consumer/developer resale supply chain. Same fraudulent-account layer feeds both, but the event (an investigative map of the market) is new → fresh.
5. **Does Anthropic lose money here, or is revenue leaking IN?** Ambiguous: some RMB spend converts to real Anthropic API spend via proxy accounts (revenue Anthropic can't legally keep/attribute), but the net is brand + ToS + distillation + sanctions-evasion risk. Open how much net revenue, if any.
6. **Can Anthropic actually stop it?** Weakly. Only chokepoints it controls are account provisioning + payment; the modular cross-border chain routes around both. Enforcement is whack-a-mole account bans + chip-export lobbying.
7. **Is this an AI story or an AML/payments story?** Both — the enforcement surface is WeChat/Alipay RMB, USDT-funded/stolen cards, and shell-company AWS Bedrock/enterprise contracts. That's why it's fintech-relevant.
8. **Who captures the margin?** The proxy operator, twice: token spread + resale of logged prompts/outputs/traces. Buyer is customer and unpaid data source; Anthropic captures ~nothing.
9. **What's the data-exfiltration downside for buyers?** Every routed request exposes prompts, code context, tool calls, reasoning traces to the operator — resaleable as training/distillation data. Real cost can exceed nominal savings for sensitive work.
10. **Does the grey market help or hurt Chinese model self-sufficiency?** Counterintuitive: leaked Claude traces feed domestic distillation AND the quality gap's persistence (why pay for black-market Claude at all) keeps shrinking as GLM/Qwen/DeepSeek improve → policy may subsidise the capability transfer it meant to deny. (hypothesis)
11. **Is Claude uniquely targeted?** No — same proxies front GPT and Gemini; it's a general US-model grey market. Claude leads the headline because of its coding/agentic edge.
12. **How old is the phenomenon?** VPN-fronted US-model access predates this (since ChatGPT's China block); novelty is the documented supply-chain anatomy + model-swap fraud, not existence.
13. **Which numbers are hard vs soft?** Hard: Anthropic's 5 Sep 2025 ToS ban; "low hundreds of millions" revenue hit (Anthropic's own); CISPA 37%/83.82%. Soft/estimated: reseller counts, building ratios, 70–93% discount band.
14. **Does this change the central question?** Yes — from "can Anthropic block China?" to "is API/software access even blockable, or only chip access?" The grey market answers: software access leaks; the real lever is hardware + payments.
15. **Second-cycle duplicate risk?** None found — no prior note covers the reseller/transfer-station mechanics; closest internal notes are distillation and export-ban, which are backdrop, not the same event.

Importance: 3/5 — Well-reported, novel supply-chain anatomy with a genuine second-order angle (export controls on software are unenforceable; payments are the real chokepoint) and a legit fintech hook (AML/USDT/shell-company rails). Capped below 4 because the headline figures are single-source estimates, it's commentary not a corporate/market event, and the core finding (a leaky grey market exists despite the ban) is directionally unsurprising given the Feb/Jun distillation saga.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Market = access to US frontier-LLM APIs (here Anthropic's Claude) *inside* China, where Anthropic does not officially sell. The grey market exists because of a policy wall, not a product gap: per reporting on Anthropic's Sept-2025 terms update, Claude is barred in China and to entities >50% Chinese-controlled worldwide, closing the subsidiary/ownership loophole (via crnasia.com, techresearchonline.com). Anthropic is described as the *only* frontier lab restricting PRC-controlled firms even abroad (via Yahoo Finance). Stated rationale: distillation and misuse — Anthropic reported a distillation attack of ~25,000 fake accounts generating ~28.8M interactions between 22 Apr and 5 Jun [year unattributed in secondary sources → treat as `[UNSOURCED]` on exact window] (via Yahoo Finance). Demand driver "why now": Claude's lead in coding/agentic work keeps Chinese developer pull high even post-ban; supply driver: Anthropic's own revenue run-rate ~$65bn (Jul 2026) and ~$965bn valuation (via valueaddvc.com, explodingtopics.com) signal a price umbrella wide enough to arbitrage. Market size for the grey layer itself = **no data** (only anecdotes: ~6 of ~30 tenants in one Beijing Haidian building reselling, "tens of thousands" of outfits nationwide — The Information, via dealroom.co; not independently verifiable).

**Competitive landscape.** KPIs this sub-market runs on: effective token price vs official rate (discount %), account-farm throughput (accounts × interactions before ban), and uptime/latency of the proxy. Structure: "transfer stations" (中转站) — overseas proxy servers that accept RMB via WeChat/Alipay, forward prompts through pooled/mass-registered Anthropic accounts, and return outputs, hiding the real user (via the-decoder.com, medianama.com, X/@rohanpaul_ai). Cost base = account farming, free-credit abuse, resold unused quota, subscription-splitting of Max plans, shell companies in SE Asia/ME/US for AWS/enterprise deals, and — flagged by multiple write-ups — possible stolen cards plus resale of logged prompts/outputs/code as training data (via lavx.hu, memeburn.com). Distribution is open: GitHub, Taobao, Telegram, ranked by price/uptime. Protagonist's position: the "resellers" are a parasitic middle layer with **no moat** — zero switching cost, commodity service, survives only while Anthropic's detection (time-zone/proxy monitoring, per Yahoo Finance) lags.

**Comps & multiples.** This is a grey arbitrage, not an equity story — trading multiples do not apply (no listed entity, no disclosed revenue). The only quantifiable comp is the price spread:
- Official Anthropic rate (reference, via internal note): Opus 5.5 $4/$20 per M tok, Haiku 5.5 $0.10/$0.50; Opus 4.8 output ~$25/M — see [[FT Cheap new model could pressure OpenAI and Anthropic]].
- Grey price: "5–10% of official," i.e. **70–90% off**; some reports up to ~93% (via aiweekly.co, ibtimes.co.uk, explainx.ai). Arithmetic on the headline claim: if a user pays ~10% of list, discount = `1 − 0.10 = 90%`; range `70–90%` → effective price `0.10×–0.30×` of official.
- Domestic substitute comp: Chinese open-weight models now price *legitimately* far below Claude — Qwen3-235B output ~$0.10/M vs Claude Opus 4.8 ~$25/M = `25 / 0.10 = ~250x` list gap (via internal note [[FT Cheap new model could pressure OpenAI and Anthropic]]). On OpenRouter, Chinese open-weight models were ~61% of tokens consumed by May 2026; Qwen ~13.9%, Zhipu (Z.ai) and DeepSeek ~5.6% each in Q2 2026 (via digitalapplied.com) — **not fully verifiable**, single-vendor dataset. Internal rival context: [[DeepSeek nears $12B raise ahead of domestic IPO]], [[Huawei chairman urges Chinese firms to accelerate AI development]]. Flag: the grey market's existence despite a ~250x cheaper legal domestic option says the pull is Claude-specific capability (coding/agentic), not raw price.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Data-security / supply poisoning (why):** transfer stations log and reportedly resell user prompts, outputs and code; the same reseller layer is accused of feeding DeepSeek distillation — so the arbitrage doubles as a channel that erodes Anthropic's model moat, the second-order harm Anthropic's ban was meant to stop (via memeburn.com, dealroom.co).
2. **Enforcement cliff / fraud liability (why):** supply depends on fraudulent accounts, stolen cards and shell AWS/enterprise deals; a detection-and-ban wave or payment-rail crackdown collapses the economics overnight — no durable business, pure regulatory-arbitrage carry (via lavx.hu, Yahoo Finance).
3. **Disintermediation by domestic rivals (why):** with Qwen/DeepSeek/GLM within a few points of frontier scores at a fraction of price (via digitalapplied.com), the addressable grey demand shrinks to Claude-specific workloads — a structurally narrowing TAM, not a growing one.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` The grey market is a *symptom* that export-style AI access controls on software leak badly: a pure-software good re-exports through proxies far more easily than chips. Falsifiable thesis — if Anthropic's revenue/valuation keeps compounding, the ban costs it little direct revenue but the distillation-via-proxy leakage is the real tax; watch for (a) Anthropic disclosing proxy-driven account bans or a measurable crackdown, and (b) Chinese developers' observable migration off Claude onto Qwen/GLM. Thesis breaks if domestic models close the capability gap and the grey market withers on its own — in which case the story was transitory.
