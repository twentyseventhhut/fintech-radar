## Первоисточники

### kommersant.ru
<https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/9024921>
*400 слов · jina*

На российском рынке платформ для работы с корпоративными данными появится еще одно решение. Positive Technologies разработала свой продукт, в который вложила порядка 600 млн руб., рассказали источники “Ъ”. Подобные сервисы уже есть у крупных ИТ-компаний Yandex B2B Tech и VK Tech, и новой платформе будет сложно конкурировать с ними. При этом на такие системы фиксируется «устойчиво растущий спрос» в силу того, что они активно используются разработчиками как источники данных для обучения ИИ-моделей.

Разработчик решений информационной безопасности Positive Technologies (MOEX: POSI) выходит на рынок корпоративных платформ данных, рассказал “Ъ” источник на ИТ-рынке. Эти платформы собирают и хранят данные всей компании, чтобы ИБ-специалисты, разработчики и аналитики могли использовать их и обучать на них ИИ-модели. Решение будет называться PT Security Lakehouse, рассказал собеседник.

Коммерческий релиз намечен на осень 2026 года.

По данным источника “Ъ”, инвестиции в разработку платформы оцениваются в 400–600 млн руб., а целевая доля рынка к 2030 году — 10%.

В Positive Technologies на запрос не ответили.

На российском рынке корпоративных платформ данных работают несколько крупных игроков, в том числе Arenadata, Cloud.ru, Yandex B2B Tech и VK Tech. Рынок таких решений Центр стратегических разработок оценивает в 109 млрд руб. в 2027 году и 174 млрд руб. в 2030 году. В VK, Arenadata и «Яндексе» отказались от комментариев.

Появление нового игрока совпало с инцидентом начала октября, когда Yandex Cloud, включающий сервис Data Lakehouse, временно оказался недоступен после атак БПЛА на дата-центры компании (

см. “Ъ” от 11 октября 2026
года).

Компании хотят объединять данные из разных источников, работать с ними единообразно и сразу готовить их к аналитике и сценариям с ИИ, поэтому на такие решения растет спрос, объяснила директор департамента разработки Evolution Data Platform Cloud.ru Ася Грибанова.

По ее словам, Positive Technologies исторически сильны в ИБ, и их решение, вероятно, в первую очередь будет ориентировано на консолидацию и анализ данных служб ИБ.

Но для бизнес-заказчиков также критичны инфраструктурная основа, масштабируемость, широкий набор управляемых сервисов, интеграции с источниками данных и аналитическими инструментами, а также зрелая модель эксплуатации и поддержки, добавила госпожа Грибанова.

Cегмент уже занят платформами с промышленными внедрениями, и конкуренция решается не составом компонентов, а тем, как платформа справляется с узкими местами — производительностью объектного хранилища, эффективностью движков, сетью и эксплуатацией под одновременными нагрузками, подчеркивает собеседник “Ъ” в ИТ-отрасли.

В то же время смешение данных из бизнес-процессов и процессов безопасности создает дополнительные риски для управляемости, считает основатель и гендиректор компании Getmobit Мария Рукавишникова. Поэтому такие системы обязаны обеспечивать разграничение доступа, добавила эксперт.

# Context-enrichment: Kommersant — Positive Technologies enters the data market
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 3/5._

**Type:** product launch / adjacency expansion (single-sourced Kommersant scoop, "sources in the IT market"; PT itself declined to comment). Subject = Positive Technologies (MOEX: POSI), Russia. **Freshness: FRESH** — but note the hook is a RE-FRAMING of an already-disclosed asset (see [0]/[3]): the "Security Lakehouse" technology was publicly unveiled on 2026-09-02 (Positive Security Day run-up) as PT's *own internal defense* infrastructure; the NEW datapoint here is its *commercialization* as a product in the corporate data-platform market (PT Security Lakehouse), with investment ~400–600M₽ and a stated target of 10% market share by 2030. That commercial-positioning angle is not covered by any prior corpus note, so fresh — but low-confidence on the hard numbers (anonymous source, PR silent).

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
- Per Kommersant (sources, not PT on record): PT is entering the **corporate data-platform market** with a product called **PT Security Lakehouse**, commercial release slated for **autumn 2026**. Development investment ~**400–600M₽**; stated **target 10% market share by 2030**.
- **De-PR'd reality:** this is NOT a greenfield build. The same "Security Lakehouse" was shown on **2026-09-02** as PT's *internal* big-data layer — ingesting >50 bn anonymized/depersonalized records/day, up to ~1M events/sec, across all PT products/installs — used to spot cross-company attack campaigns and train detection AI (CNews 2026-09-22; Habr). So the Oct 14 story = **productizing an internal platform** PT already runs, re-badged for external sale.
- **Why framed this way (→ what it reveals):** the Kommersant piece anchors on the flattering "entering a growing market used to train AI models" narrative, while sourced *entirely* from anonymous IT-market people with PT declining comment. That pattern (→ why) signals a soft/early launch floated to gauge reception, not a firm GA with customers — "commercial release autumn 2026" is an intention, not a shipped product with references. The 10%-by-2030 target is an aspiration from a source, not PT guidance.
- **Second order:** PT's genuine asset is *security* telemetry, not general corporate data. Cloud.ru's own Gribanova (quoted in-piece) says PT's product will "first and foremost" serve consolidation/analysis of **IB (security) data** — i.e. a narrower ISM/security-datalake, not a true horizontal competitor to Yandex/VK/Arenadata lakehouses. The "enters the data market" headline over-reads the ambition.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Incumbents (named in-piece):** Arenadata, Cloud.ru (Evolution Data Platform), Yandex B2B Tech, VK Tech (VK Data Platform/Tarantool). These have *industrial* deployments; the segment is past the pilot phase. Market size (Center for Strategic Research, per Kommersant): **~109 bn₽ in 2027 → 174 bn₽ in 2030**.
- **Structural threat to PT's entry:** Yandex + VK are reportedly forming a cloud "supergiant" targeting ~1/3 of the market (cnews 2026-09/10); Cloud.ru leads IaaS (29.7%)/PaaS (44.6%). A sub-scale security vendor arriving late into a consolidating, capital-heavy infra segment is structurally disadvantaged (→ why: lakehouse wins are decided by object-store throughput, engine efficiency, concurrency/ops — Kommersant's IT source — not component lists, which favors hyperscalers).
- **Position:** catching-up/niche entrant. PT's edge is only credible in the **security-data** slice, not horizontal data management (where the 109–174 bn₽ TAM lives). Related internal note [[Alfa-Bank starts selling its cybersecurity system externally]] shows the same 2026 RU pattern: security players monetizing internal capabilities externally.

## [2] Company history / fit
- PT is a pure-play cybersecurity vendor (MaxPatrol, PT NGFW, PT ISIM, MaxPatrol EPP). FY2025 revenue ~30.9 bn₽ (tadviser); **H1 2026: revenue +41% to ~9.5 bn₽, shipments +45% to 10.7 bn₽, EBITDA +0.8 bn₽ (vs −2.5 bn₽ y/y), IFRS net loss −1.36 bn₽ (vs −5 bn₽)**; FY2026 shipments guided 40–45 bn₽. So PT is recovering from a weak 2025 (overshot 2024 guidance, heavy NGFW R&D) and leaning on NEW-product ramps.
- **Why PT does this (structural logic):** growth guidance depends on launching/scaling new SKUs (PT X, MaxPatrol EPP, NGFW, ISIM). Monetizing the Security Lakehouse it *already built for itself* is a low-marginal-cost way to add a revenue line and dress a security tool as a higher-multiple "data/AI" platform — classic adjacency to justify a software multiple. ~400–600M₽ is small vs revenue (≈1–2%), consistent with repackaging, not a new big bet.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Really new?** The *platform* is not new (internal since mid-2026, unveiled Sept 2). What's new is the *commercial positioning* into the corporate-data market. **No traction disclosed:** no named customers, no GMV, no pricing, no GA date beyond "autumn 2026" — and PT wouldn't confirm. So: announcement/intent, NOT adoption.
- **Where value-add is real vs not (→ deeper):** real only if the offering stays a **security data lakehouse** (consolidating SOC/IB telemetry) where PT's domain + existing data pipeline are a genuine moat. As a general corporate-data platform it is a me-too entrant against scaled, capex-heavy incumbents — value-add unproven. **Who captures the margin:** in lakehouse economics the margin accrues to whoever owns the object storage/compute substrate (hyperscalers Cloud.ru/Yandex/VK); a security vendor layering on top risks being a feature, not the platform.
- **Risk flagged in-piece:** mixing business-process data with security-process data raises governance/access-control risks (Getmobit's Rukavishnikova) → a reason enterprises may NOT want a security vendor as their general data lake.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- **Tailwind:** AI adoption is lifting demand for data-management + protection tooling in RU — see [[Vedomosti AI adoption lifts demand for corporate data protection]] (RU data-tooling market 2.3x by 2031 → ~198 bn₽; IB-software +13.1%/yr). The Oct Yandex Cloud outage (UAV strikes on data centers) is cited as making the segment topical.
- **Headwinds:** consolidating cloud oligopoly, late entry, governance concerns, single-source announcement with no customers. Expect a narrow **security-focused** GA rather than a true horizontal lakehouse; the 10%-by-2030 target is aspirational.
- **Second-order:** PT's safer win is cross-selling the lakehouse to its *existing security base* (SOC/MSSP) — not displacing Yandex/VK. If it over-pitches "corporate data platform," it invites a fight it is structurally set up to lose.

### Red-team / challenge questions

1. **Is PT Security Lakehouse actually new, or a repackaged internal asset?** Repackaged. The platform was unveiled 2026-09-02 as PT's *internal* defense/telemetry layer (>50 bn records/day); Oct 14 is the *commercial-positioning* announcement. Fresh as a corpus event, but not a new technology.

2. **Is this a shipped product or an intention?** Intention. "Commercial release autumn 2026," no named customers, no pricing, no GA confirmation. PT declined to comment entirely. Announcement, not adoption.

3. **How solid is the source?** Weak. Single-source Kommersant scoop citing anonymous "IT-market sources"; PT on record only by silence. All headline numbers (400–600M₽, 10% by 2030) are source-attributed, not company guidance. Treat as directional.

4. **Is "enters the data market" accurate, or over-read?** Over-read. Cloud.ru's Gribanova (in-piece) says the product will primarily consolidate/analyze *security (IB)* data — a narrow security-datalake, not a horizontal competitor to Yandex/VK/Arenadata. Headline inflates scope.

5. **What's the real TAM PT can address?** The named 109 bn₽ (2027) / 174 bn₽ (2030) is the *general* corporate-data-platform market. PT's credible slice is the security-data subset, materially smaller. The 10%-of-174bn (~17 bn₽) target applied to the whole market is aspirational/implausible for a late niche entrant.

6. **Who captures the margin in lakehouse economics?** The object-store/compute substrate owner (Cloud.ru, Yandex, VK) — not a security vendor layering on top. PT risks being a feature/application, not the platform. (analysis)

7. **Why now?** AI demand for training data + the Oct Yandex Cloud outage (UAV strikes) made the segment topical; and PT needs new-SKU revenue to hit FY2026 shipments guidance (40–45 bn₽). Timing is opportunistic. (analysis)

8. **Can PT fund/scale this vs capex-heavy incumbents?** ~400–600M₽ is tiny (≈1–2% of revenue) — consistent with repackaging an existing platform, not a serious infra build. Against a consolidating Yandex+VK cloud "supergiant," PT is structurally sub-scale on the horizontal play. Open on exact capex roadmap.

9. **Does this fit PT's strategy/finances?** Yes logically. H1 2026: revenue +41% (~9.5 bn₽), shipments +45% (10.7 bn₽), EBITDA back positive (+0.8 bn₽), net loss narrowed to −1.36 bn₽. Recovering vendor leaning on new products; a low-cost adjacency to add a "data/AI" revenue line and multiple story.

10. **Biggest adoption blocker?** Governance: mixing business-process and security-process data in one lake raises access-control/manageability risk (Getmobit's Rukavishnikova, in-piece) — a reason enterprises may avoid a security vendor as their general data platform.

11. **What already did the same (prior art)?** Arenadata, Cloud.ru Evolution Data Platform, Yandex Data Lakehouse, VK Data Platform/Tarantool — all with industrial deployments. PT arrives after the pilot phase, into an occupied segment.

12. **Internal duplicate?** No. [[Vedomosti AI adoption lifts demand for corporate data protection]] is market-sizing (iKS-Consulting), different event; [[Alfa-Bank starts selling its cybersecurity system externally]] is a different company/product. Same theme, distinct events → not a reprint.

13. **What's PT silent about?** Everything commercial: customers, pricing, true GA, how it separates security vs business data, competitive differentiation beyond "we have the telemetry." Silence is the tell that this is early.

14. **Realistic outcome?** Likely a narrow security-data lakehouse cross-sold to PT's existing SOC/MSSP base — a modest incremental line — rather than a genuine horizontal data-platform contender. (analysis)

15. **What would change the verdict to higher importance?** Named enterprise customers at GA, disclosed pricing/traction, or PT confirming it on record with real guidance. None present yet.

**Importance: 3/5** — a real, fresh *commercialization* move by a notable listed RU vendor into a large, topical (AI/data) adjacency, with a genuine data-pipeline asset behind it. Capped at 3 because it is single-sourced, PR-silent, announcement-not-adoption, over-framed as a horizontal "data market" entry when PT's credible scope is the narrower security-data slice, and it lands late into a consolidating, capex-heavy segment dominated by Yandex/VK/Cloud.ru.

**Sector & drivers.** Positive Technologies (MOEX: POSI), a Russian cybersecurity leader, is entering the adjacent **corporate data-platform / lakehouse** market with PT Security Lakehouse (commercial launch autumn 2026), investing ~₽400–600m (Kommersant, 2026-10-14; abn.agency, 2026-10-13).
- Size/growth: per the Centre for Strategic Research (CSR / ЦСР), cited in the note's primary source (Kommersant), the Russian corporate-data-platform market = **₽109bn in 2027 → ₽174bn by 2030** (implied ~**+17%/yr** 2027–30 (analysis)). This sits inside the broader data-management/dev-tools software segment that iKS-Consulting forecasts to grow **2.3x to ~₽198bn by 2031, ~+15.1%/yr** (see [[Vedomosti AI adoption lifts demand for corporate data protection]], 2026-10-06).
- Structure: **already occupied by scaled incumbents with industrial deployments** — Arenadata, Cloud.ru, Yandex B2B Tech, VK Tech (per Kommersant). Entry barriers are operational, not component-list: object-storage throughput, engine efficiency, networking, and ops under concurrent load (per the note's IT-industry source). This is a **consolidated, late-stage** segment — the opposite of a greenfield.
- Drivers + "why now": (1) **AI adoption** — enterprises consolidate heterogeneous data to train/feed models, lifting demand for governed lakehouses (the explicit industry thesis); (2) **import substitution** — the structural tailwind feeding all Russian enterprise-SW vendors post-2022. Second-order: Positive is extending from "data protection" into "data management" — monetizing its IB install base, not opening a new frontier (analysis).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: revenue growth vs market, licence/recurring mix, shipments ("отгрузки"), and — for lakehouses specifically — throughput (PT claims PT Security Lakehouse processes **>50bn records/day**, per abn.agency/Habr; unverified vendor metric).
- Key players & basis of competition (not price, but maturity + ecosystem): **Arenadata** (MOEX: DATA) — pure-play data-platform leader; **Cloud.ru** (ex-SberCloud, IaaS #1 ~30% share per TAdviser) with Evolution Data Platform; **Yandex B2B Tech** (Data Lakehouse) and **VK Tech** (VK Tech FY25 revenue ₽18.8bn, +38% y/y, per VK). Positive is a **new entrant into an occupied field** — ahead on IB credibility, behind on infrastructure maturity, scalability, managed-service breadth and integrations (the gaps Cloud.ru's Evolution Data Platform director explicitly flagged, per Kommersant).
- Recent moves (dated): early-Oct 2026 drone strikes knocked out Yandex Cloud (incl. its Data Lakehouse), forcing migrations to VK Cloud/Selectel/K2Cloud ([[Yandex Cloud helps clients migrate to rival providers after outages]], 2026-10-09) — a timing window Positive's entry coincides with; [[Alfa-Bank starts selling its cybersecurity system externally]] (2026-10-08) and [[Vedomosti AI adoption lifts demand for corporate data protection]] (2026-10-06) show the same "IB/IT players extend into adjacent monetization" pattern.
- Position + moat: Positive's moat is in **IB** (brand, SOC/SIEM install base), NOT data infrastructure. A likely-defensible niche = consolidating/analyzing **security-service data** (SIEM-adjacent security data lake), which Cloud.ru's expert predicted as PT's probable focus. A general-purpose enterprise lakehouse play would put it head-to-head with better-resourced infra incumbents — a weaker position (analysis).

**Risk flags.**
1. **Late entrant into a consolidated market** — the segment already has industrial deployments from Arenadata/Cloud.ru/Yandex/VK; competition is won on ops/throughput maturity, where Positive has no track record. A 10%-by-2030 share target against entrenched infra leaders is aggressive and unproven (why: displacing production lakehouses carries high switching costs — Positive must win net-new or security-niche workloads, not rip-and-replace).
2. **Capability/positioning mismatch** — Positive's moat is IB, not data infrastructure (scalability, managed services, integrations — the exact gaps named by a Cloud.ru expert). Stretching the brand into general data management risks a sub-scale product that is neither best IB nor best data platform (why: brand equity doesn't transfer across unrelated technical stacks — margin/share accrue to whoever owns the harder infra layer).
3. **Governance / data-commingling risk** — mixing business-process and security-process data in one platform creates manageability and access-control risks (Getmobit CEO, per Kommersant); a security vendor shipping a data platform that leaks the security/business boundary would be reputationally self-damaging (why: an IB firm's credibility is its core asset — a governance failure in its own data product undercuts the parent franchise).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** (analysis) Signals that **Russian IB/enterprise-SW vendors are converging on the data layer** as the AI-era monetization battleground — cybersecurity, cloud and data-platform categories are blurring. Falsifiable thesis: Positive's entry only works as a **security data lake** (SIEM-adjacent), not a general lakehouse. Trigger/what breaks it: if PT Security Lakehouse's autumn-2026 launch is positioned as a general-purpose enterprise data platform (vs a security-data store), it is likely over-reaching — watch the launch framing and first reference customers; conversely, named security/SOC design-wins would validate the niche-moat read.
