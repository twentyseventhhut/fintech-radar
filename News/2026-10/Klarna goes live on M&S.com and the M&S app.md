**London, October 15, 2026** — Klarna, the everyday finance network, is now available as a payment option at Marks & Spencer for Fashion, Home & Beauty purchases online, bringing shoppers at the UK’s most trusted retailer* access to the country’s largest flexible payments provider.

Shoppers on M&S.com and the M&S app can now pay with Klarna for Fashion, Home & Beauty purchases, splitting the cost into three equal, interest-free payments with transparent terms and no revolving balance.

"People deserve flexibility without paying interest for it, so our goal is simple: meet them wherever they shop," said Klarna Head of UK, Ireland & Western & Southern Europe, Raji Behal. "M&S is a beloved British icon that brings second-to-none quality and value to millions of shoppers every day. We’re proud to now offer them a transparent way to spread the cost with no interest and a clear end date."

Katherine Carlson, Director of Financial Services at M&S, said: “At M&S, we are always looking for ways to make it easier for our customers to shop with us. Customers shopping on M&S.com and through our app can now spread the cost in three interest-free payments with Klarna, offering more flexibility in how they pay for the things they love.”

M&S puts customers at the heart of everything it does, and Klarna’s delighted to be working with a retailer that shares that focus. M&S joins more than 1.2 million merchants globally that partner with Klarna, including all of the UK’s major department stores.

**About Klarna**  
Klarna is the everyday finance network. With over 120 million global active Klarna users and 3.8 million transactions per day, Klarna’s AI-powered payments and commerce network is empowering people to pay smarter with a mission to be available everywhere for everything. Consumers can pay with Klarna online, in-store and through Apple Pay & Google Pay. Over 1.2 million retailers trust Klarna’s innovative solutions to drive growth and loyalty, including Uber, H&M, Saks, Sephora, Macy’s, Ikea, Expedia Group, Nike and Airbnb. Klarna is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: KLAR). For more information, visit Klarna.com.

**About M&S**  
M&S is a leading British retailer bringing exceptional quality, great value food, clothing and homeware to millions of customers around the world.

_*GlobalData’s UK most trusted retailer ranking 2025_
