Allaria y Circle se alían para ampliar la liquidez y el acceso a USDC en Argentina
BUENOS AIRES, Argentina, 20 de octubre del 2026. Allaria S.A., una reconocida firma argentina del mercado de capitales, anunció hoy una alianza con Circle Internet Group, Inc. (NYSE: CRCL) («Circle»), una de las principales empresas de plataformas financieras de internet del mundo y emisora de USDC¹ a través de sus entidades reguladas, para ampliar la liquidez institucional y el acceso a USDC en Argentina.
En el marco de esta alianza, Allaria Digital S.A. permitirá que los participantes habilitados accedan directamente a USDC, sujeto a los requisitos regulatorios aplicables y a sus condiciones de elegibilidad y servicio, mientras que Allaria S.A. ofrecerá cotizaciones de compra y venta de USDC/ARS en los mercados locales correspondientes. A través de su infraestructura de negociación existente, el ecosistema Allaria contribuirá a aumentar la profundidad del mercado y a facilitar una ejecución más eficiente de las operaciones para quienes busquen comprar o vender USDC en el mercado argentino.
Allaria ocupa el primer puesto entre los agentes bursátiles argentinos por volumen negociado en BYMA, tanto en renta variable como en renta fija, y desarrolla internamente su tecnología de negociación algorítmica, que utiliza para actuar como creador de mercado (market maker) en los principales mercados de Argentina y Brasil. Esta combinación de escala en el mercado regulado y una cultura de ingeniería orientada a la negociación le permite a Allaria aportar liquidez tanto para la compra como para la venta y profundidad al mercado de USDC/ARS.
Argentina cuenta con un ecosistema de mercado de capitales consolidado, en el que las instituciones financieras exploran cada vez más cómo la infraestructura basada en blockchain puede complementar los mercados tradicionales. La alianza combina la infraestructura de USDC con las capacidades de Allaria en el mercado local y en la negociación de activos para contribuir a satisfacer esa necesidad.
Juan Politi, CEO de Allaria, afirmó: «Esta alianza con Circle refleja una visión compartida sobre cómo están evolucionando los mercados financieros. Por nuestro volumen de operaciones y nuestra posición como principal proveedor de liquidez del mercado de capitales local, Allaria es un socio natural para este desarrollo en Argentina. También nos entusiasma formar parte de un mercado que evoluciona rápidamente y seguir incorporando nuevas tendencias que están transformando el sistema financiero».
Kash Razzaghi, Chief Commercial Officer de Circle, afirmó: «Argentina ha estado a la vanguardia del uso práctico de dólares digitales. A medida que ese mercado se desarrolla, contar con una sólida liquidez local y conexiones con los mercados financieros establecidos cobra cada vez mayor importancia. Trabajar con Allaria combina USDC con capacidades locales de probada eficacia y contribuye a fortalecer la conexión entre el mercado de capitales argentino y el sistema financiero de internet».
¹ USDC es emitido por entidades afiliadas reguladas de Circle. Consultá la lista de autorizaciones regulatorias de Circle en circle.com/legal/licenses.

## [0] What happened
On 20 Oct 2026, Allaria S.A. — Argentina's top-ranked capital-markets broker (ALyC, CNV N°24; #1 by BYMA traded volume in equities and fixed income) — announced an alliance with Circle Internet Group (NYSE: CRCL), issuer of USDC, to deepen **institutional liquidity and access to USDC in Argentina**. Mechanics: **Allaria Digital S.A.** (a CNV-registered Virtual Asset Service Provider, PSAV N°132) will let eligible, KYC/regulatory-qualified participants access USDC directly, while **Allaria S.A.** will quote two-sided **USDC/ARS buy and sell prices** in local markets, using its in-house algorithmic trading / market-making stack (also used in Brazil) to add depth and improve execution. Framed as institutional, not retail. (Source: [allariainversiones.com.ar press release](https://allariainversiones.com.ar/Novedades/View/62))

## [1] Who are the players
- **Allaria S.A. / Allaria Digital** — leading Argentine brokerage ("casa de bolsa"), market leader by BYMA volume, acts as a market maker in AR and BR. CEO Juan Politi. Allaria Digital is its regulated crypto arm (CNV PSAV). The group spans investment vehicles, financing, agro, SMEs, real estate and insurance brokerage.
- **Circle** — issuer of USDC, the world's #2 stablecoin; publicly listed (CRCL). CCO Kash Razzaghi fronted the deal. This continues Circle's aggressive LatAm/emerging-markets push (see [4]).

## [2] Why it matters / strategic logic
Argentina is one of the most stablecoin-intensive economies on earth: ~**94% of peso-denominated crypto transactions flow into stablecoins** (a16z/Chainalysis-style data), driven by chronic peso weakness and a long dollarization reflex. Even as monthly inflation cooled sharply in 2026 (BCRA projecting ~30.5% full-year), stablecoin use has persisted/accelerated as a dollar-savings and payments rail. The twist: **USDC is the challenger here — Tether's USDT dominates Argentina/LatAm** (USDT ~68% regional share; ~60% of global stablecoin market cap). So this is Circle buying local USDC/ARS liquidity and credibility against an entrenched incumbent, by partnering with the single largest regulated local market maker rather than going retail. For Allaria, it extends its market-making franchise from traditional securities into digital dollars within a CNV-regulated perimeter.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
Red-team questions (skeptical; press-release-sourced, so scope claims are the company's own):

1. **Is this just a market-making/quoting arrangement dressed up as a "partnership"?** The release describes Allaria quoting USDC/ARS and Allaria Digital granting access — no equity, no revenue-share, no exclusivity disclosed. How deep is the integration really?
2. **Institutional-only?** It says "eligible participants" / "institutional liquidity." Does retail ever touch this, or is it a narrow B2B/accredited rail? If institutional-only, impact on Argentina's mass stablecoin habit is limited.
3. **Does it actually move share from USDT?** USDT owns ~68% of LatAm and is the de-facto parallel dollar. Local institutional quoting may not dislodge entrenched retail/P2P USDT usage. Is this a wedge or a niche?
4. **Liquidity source?** Who provides the ARS and USDC inventory, and what spreads/fees? "Improved execution" is unquantified — no volume commitment, no price benchmark.
5. **Regulatory fragility.** Allaria Digital is a CNV PSAV (N°132) — a young regime. ARCA already taxes wallet transactions. Could tax/FX controls blunt USDC/ARS flow? Coinbase exited this exact service in Jan 2026 — what makes Allaria durable where Coinbase retreated?
6. **Why did Coinbase leave and Allaria enter?** Is Allaria stepping into a regulatory gap, or did Coinbase exit for reasons (compliance, profitability, FX rules) that will also constrain Allaria?
7. **Redundancy with Grupo BIND.** Circle already signed BIND (July 2026) for institutional USDC/ARS. Is Allaria incremental liquidity or overlapping coverage? Are these competing or complementary rails?
8. **Reserve/counterparty risk.** USDC access depends on Circle's regulated minting entities and redemption; any USDC depeg or banking-rail stress (recall 2023 SVB episode) hits Argentine users with no local backstop.
9. **Verification gap.** Primary source is Allaria's own site; the loaded text is mostly a garbled login/ToS scrape. Independent crypto-press corroborates the headline but not the economics. Treat scope/metrics as unconfirmed.
10. **FX-control / official-rate arbitrage.** USDC/ARS quoting intersects Argentina's multi-rate FX history. Which ARS rate, and is there regulatory risk if it implies a parallel exchange rate?
11. **Does Argentina's cooling inflation (→~2.1%/mo, 30.5% annual) reduce the dollar-demand thesis?** Evidence says adoption persists, but the savings-hedge urgency may ease — does that cap growth?
12. **Allaria's crypto track record?** It is a securities market leader, not a crypto-native. Execution/custody risk in a new asset class, and brand/reputational exposure if USDC stumbles.
13. **Is the Brazil market-making mention relevant or filler?** The release cites AR+BR market making — any concrete BR/USDC angle, or is it credential-stacking?
14. **Who benefits most — Circle PR or real AR adoption?** This fits Circle's "40 emerging markets / $1.4B" narrative; risk it is more announcement than throughput.

Importance: 3/5 — Genuinely relevant: Argentina is a top global stablecoin-use economy, this fills a real USDC/ARS liquidity gap left by Coinbase's Jan-2026 exit, and it ties into Circle's broad emerging-markets offensive against USDT. But it is an institutional, single-country, local-market-making deal with no disclosed economics or volumes, overlapping an earlier BIND partnership, and Circle remains the challenger to dominant USDT. Material as a data point in the USDC-vs-USDT LatAm contest, not a market-mover on its own.

## Market Research

**Sector & drivers.** Argentina is the sharpest dollarization-driven stablecoin market in LatAm: per an a16z-cited report (via blockchainreporter/cryptonews, 2026), ~94% of peso-based crypto transactions flow into stablecoins and adoption reached ~19.8% of citizens in Jan 2026 — demand is structurally "savings in digital dollars," not speculation. Chainalysis repeatedly ranks Argentina LatAm's #2 crypto market by volume, ~$88–94bn over recent 12-month windows (per Chainalysis, via cryptonews, 2026) — treat as order-of-magnitude, methodology-dependent. Key structural wrinkle: the market is USDT-dominated at the retail/P2P layer (Tether ~58% of global stablecoin cap vs USDC ~24%, per stockanalysis/tradingkey, Oct 2026); this deal is Circle's attempt to win the *institutional/capital-markets* layer where USDC's regulatory posture is an advantage. Why now: Argentina's central bank ruled out near-term crypto-into-banking integration (crowdfundinsider, Oct 2026), so the opening is via regulated capital-markets intermediaries (CNV-licensed ALyC/PSAV), exactly Allaria's lane — not the banks.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: USDC/ARS on-exchange depth & spread, local trading volume, reserve-return capture (issuer side). Basis of competition for the issuer is distribution + regulatory trust, not price. Allaria is #1 Argentine broker by BYMA volume (equities + fixed income) and runs its own algo market-making tech (per the Allaria PR — a self-claim, unverified externally) — so as a *liquidity/market-maker* partner it is credibly positioned, niche-to-ahead in the institutional USDC/ARS niche. For Circle this is one of a fast-cadence string of distribution deals: [[Circle and Cassava partner on stablecoins in Africa]] (Mar 2026), [[Nium and Circle connect USDC settlement with global payouts]] (May 2026), [[SAP-backed Tereina launches payments inside SAP software with Circle]] (Oct 2026), [[Deutsche Börse and Circle collaborate on stablecoins in Europe]]. Moat is USDC's network + reserve-income economics `(analysis)`; Allaria's moat is local regulated scale. Retail incumbents it does NOT touch: Lemon Cash (2m+ users), [[Argentina's Cocos launches standalone Cocos Crypto app]] — USDT-heavy, still the mass-market battleground.
