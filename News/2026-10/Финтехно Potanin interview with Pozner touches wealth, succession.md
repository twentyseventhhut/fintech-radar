[0] WHAT HAPPENED (the item)
On 2026-10-15/16, Vladimir Potanin (president and controlling owner of the Interros holding; controls Norilsk Nickel + a large fintech stack) gave a long-form interview to veteran TV interviewer Vladimir Pozner. Per the "Финтехно" channel recap, the conversation was deliberately personal and "nothing directly about financial assets," but carried three business-relevant theses: (1) sanctions pressured both the economy and the social sphere, and Russia's core problem is being cut out of parts of international production/technology chains and losing external markets — positions that "will have to be rebuilt, or our economy will stall"; (2) Russia withstood sanctions on a buffer of accumulated economic resilience and an ability to live "under siege," but the economy is too small to self-finance large ambitious projects — import-substitution needs huge long-term capex while the domestic RU market is too small to recoup much of it (explicitly flagged as relevant to Interros); (3) AI as a theme. The item is fintech-adjacent (commentary/macro), not a deal.

[1] WHY IT MATTERS / SO-WHAT
Potanin is the ultimate beneficial owner of one of Russia's largest private fintech stacks, so his macro/strategic framing is a read on the owner's mindset, not a transaction. Per the Potanin Foundation / press disclosures, the Foundation + Interros structures hold T-Bank (≈100%), Rosbank (≈99.4% — merged into T-Bank as a branch from 1 Jan 2025), a ~41% economic stake in T-Technologies (MOEX: T), Tochka Bank, T-Insurance and a fintech platform, plus ~9.95% of Yandex (bought 2025). The "wealth/succession" headline ties to Potanin's long-standing Giving Pledge stance: give the majority to philanthropy, not heirs; in March 2026 he transferred a ~3.49% Nornickel package into his Foundation's endowment (Foundation NAV reported ≈RUB 93.3bn). So the succession angle is primarily a philanthropy/endowment-vehicle story, with the Foundation increasingly the formal holder of the fintech assets.

[2] COMPANY/ACTOR HISTORY & FIT
Interros is Potanin's investment holding (Norilsk Nickel is the anchor; ~37% held via Interros + Foundation as of Mar 2026). Fintech leg assembled rapidly post-2022: bought Rosbank from Société Générale (2022), acquired control of Tinkoff/TCS from Oleg Tinkov (rebranded T-Bank, June 2024), built up Tochka (via the 2023 Trust bad-bank consortium, then the April 2026 buy of VK's 25% for ≥RUB 21.2bn, pass-through to T-Technologies for full consolidation at the RUB 84.8bn mark — see [[Tochka Bank valued at 84.8bn rubles ahead of share consolidation]]). Potanin + Interros are under US blocking (OFAC SDN) sanctions since Dec 2022. Strategic VC leg: the Voskhod fund (Potanin/Interros-anchored), e.g. RUB 250m into Am.Tech additive-manufacturing — see [[i3D raises 50m rubles from Moscow Venture Fund]] and [[Indie GO launches 5bn-ruble venture fund for gaming IPOs]]. The Tinkov→Potanin forced-exit pattern recurs in RU fintech M&A commentary — see [[CloudPayments co-founder Spiridonov wins valuation suit vs T-Bank]].

Why: structurally compresses the forward returns and exit optionality of the whole portfolio.
