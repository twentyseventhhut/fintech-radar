ApprovalMax has launched ApprovalMax Pay for QuickBooks Online users in the United States, integrating bill approval and payment into a single workflow. For fintech professionals, this move addresses the persistent security and audit gaps created when finance teams are forced to jump between separate approval systems and bank portals to release funds.

Announced at the Intuit Connect event in Las Vegas, ApprovalMax Pay is now available to US customers using QuickBooks Online. The tool allows finance teams to approve and pay bills within the same platform, maintaining a continuous audit trail from the initial request to the final release of funds. This launch follows a successful rollout in the UK, where businesses have processed more than £98 million in payments through the system.

The platform uses pre-funded wallets that support local payment rails across more than 30 currencies. In the US market, businesses can pay vendors via ACH or Real-Time Payments (RTP), either individually or in batches. Security measures include role-based permissions that separate the preparation, approval, and release of funds, alongside mandatory two-factor authentication. To prevent fraud, the system automatically checks vendor payment details against previous successful transactions and flags any changes. Furthermore, if a bill is altered after its initial approval, the system requires it to be re-approved before payment can proceed. There is no additional subscription fee for the Pay feature, and USD wallet top-ups are provided at no cost, with transaction fees displayed transparently before execution.

ApprovalMax is a specialist in financial workflow automation, focusing on multi-step approval processes for accounts payable and receivable. The company has established itself as a key player in the cloud accounting ecosystem, particularly for businesses requiring more granular control than standard accounting software provides. It frequently operates alongside major platforms like Xero and QuickBooks to provide an additional layer of compliance and audit readiness.

QuickBooks Online, developed by Intuit, is a dominant force in the small and medium-sized business (SMB) accounting market. As a cloud-based platform, it serves as the central financial record for millions of businesses globally. The integration of third-party tools like ApprovalMax Pay into the QuickBooks environment is part of a broader trend where specialized fintech providers build "last-mile" financial services—such as payments and advanced approvals—directly on top of the core ledger. This ecosystem approach allows SMBs to access enterprise-grade financial controls without migrating to complex ERP systems.

FF News has closely followed ApprovalMax’s expansion within the accounting software ecosystem. The company was recently recognized for its market position when it was named winner in the Xero Global App Awards 2026. Beyond standard approvals, the firm has also moved into proactive spend management; we previously covered how ApprovalMax Debuts AI Agents to Combat Rogue Spending for Xero and QuickBooks Users. This focus on automated oversight is becoming a competitive necessity in the US market, where other players are also tightening their integration with QuickBooks. For instance, we reported on how Apron Launches Integrated QuickBooks Online Payments to Automate Supplier Billing, highlighting the intensifying race to own the end-to-end accounts payable process.

This launch signals a shift in the US SMB market away from fragmented "best-of-breed" software toward unified financial operations. By embedding payments into the approval engine, ApprovalMax is directly challenging traditional bank portals, which often lack the contextual data—like purchase orders or approval history—needed for secure B2B transactions. The inclusion of Real-Time Payments (RTP) is particularly significant, as US businesses increasingly demand the same speed in B2B settlements that they experience in consumer fintech. For the wider sector, the pressure is now on traditional banks to improve their API connectivity or risk losing the primary interface with their corporate clients to integrated workflow platforms.

Original Press Release

ApprovalMax has launched ApprovalMax Pay for QuickBooks Online in the US, bringing bill approval and payment into the same workflow. Announced at Intuit Connect in Las Vegas, the launch follows more than £98 million in payments made by UK businesses through ApprovalMax Pay.

For many US finance teams, approval and payment still happen in separate systems, making it harder to maintain a continuous audit trail and tightly control who can release funds. The payment stage can create further control gaps. Bank portals may give payment staff broader access to company funds than necessary, while checks depend on manual sign-off and can be intercepted or altered outside the company’s systems.

With ApprovalMax Pay, the controls that govern bill approval now apply to payment too. Finance teams can approve and pay bills in ApprovalMax without a separate payment system, and keep one audit trail from approval to the release of funds.

ApprovalMax Pay links each payment to the approved bill and, where applicable, its purchase order. Approved bill information carries into the payment workflow, reducing the need to re-key information between approval and payment.

ApprovalMax users can pay bills from pre-funded wallets, which support payments in more than 30 currencies via local payment rails. Role-based permissions separate who can prepare, approve and release payments. Two-factor authentication is required to release payments, and ApprovalMax checks vendor payment details against the last successful payment, flagging new or changed details before funds are released. Changes made to a bill after approval must be re-approved before payment.

Payment activity is recorded throughout, creating an audit trail of who raised, approved and paid each bill. Completed payments also sync back to QuickBooks Online, keeping the record connected from approval through to reconciliation.

Businesses can pay US vendors by ACH or Real-Time Payments (RTP), individually or in batches. ApprovalMax Pay works alongside existing payment methods rather than requiring finance teams to replace them.

There is no additional Pay subscription, maintenance or wallet holding fee, and USD Wallet top-ups are free. Transaction fees are shown before payment.

"ApprovalMax started with one job: making sure every bill is approved exactly as a business’s rules say," said Konstantin Bredyuk, CEO of ApprovalMax. "With ApprovalMax Pay, those rules no longer stop at approval. Finance teams can take a bill from approval to payment in one controlled workflow, with one record of who approved it and who released the money."

ApprovalMax Pay is available now to ApprovalMax customers using QuickBooks Online in the US.

"UK businesses have already paid more than £98 million through ApprovalMax Pay, and bringing it to the US is a big step towards our goal of giving finance teams robust control over accounts payable from start to finish," said Bredyuk.
