# Fibank to integrate crypto with Bitpanda Enterprise in 2027

> [!info] 2026-10-08 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇧🇬 Fibank will integrate cryptocurrencies with Bitpanda Enterprise in 2027. Fibank will become the first Bulgarian bank to offer crypto investing directly inside its mobile app, My Fibank. The service launches in early 2027 and will reach more than a million users alongside their traditional banking products.
