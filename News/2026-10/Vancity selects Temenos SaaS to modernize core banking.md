Vancity Selects Temenos SaaS to Modernize Core Banking 
Canada’s largest credit union strengthens its technology foundation to increase efficiency and support its next phase of growth.
VANCOUVER, Canada and GRAND-LANCY, Switzerland – [OCTOBER 26, 2026] – Temenos (SIX: TEMN), a global leader in banking technology, today announced that Vancity has selected Temenos SaaS to modernize its core banking platform and support its next phase of growth.
Serving the needs of more than 588,000 members across more than three million accounts and reporting CAD $42.4 billion in assets, Vancity is Canada’s largest credit union and one of the country’s most significant member-owned financial institutions.
With Temenos, Vancity will follow a build-and-migrate strategy, enabling it to introduce new digital banking capabilities while progressively modernizing its existing operations. This approach will help Vancity transition to Temenos’ modern SaaS platform at a pace that protects members, operations and business continuity.
The transformation program will support core banking operations across Vancity’s main business lines, including retail deposits, retail lending, corporate banking, and payments. The scope includes Temenos Core, Temenos Payments Hub and Temenos Data Hub.
Deployed on Microsoft Azure, Temenos SaaS will provide Vancity with a scalable and resilient foundation while simplifying the management of its core banking technology and supporting its ESG commitments through a more efficient technology environment.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector context — core-banking SaaS modernization / credit unions.** Vancity (Canada's largest credit union, CAD $42.4bn assets, 588k members) picking Temenos SaaS on Microsoft Azure fits the dominant incumbent pattern: legacy mutual/credit-union cores being re-platformed to cloud-delivered vendor SaaS rather than rip-and-replace. Temenos frames the engagement as a phased "build-and-migrate" (strangler-fig) spanning retail deposits/lending, corporate banking and payments via Temenos Core + Payments Hub + Data Hub. Industry norms set expectations: a full Temenos core rollout typically runs 18–36 months and $50M–$300M, and 45% of banks cite business-interruption risk / 42% integration complexity as the top transformation hurdles (fintechfutures/aspiresys, The Asian Banker). The "progressive, no big-bang cutover" language is the standard risk-managed playbook, not a differentiator.

**In-base comps (sem).** Temenos SaaS/Azure wins are a steady drumbeat in the DB — Peru's Mibanco (2026-02, Temenos SaaS core upgrade on Azure), UK Reliance Bank (2026-05, Temenos SaaS), FAB Saudi Arabia Shariah-compliant (2026-05), and Cognizant–Temenos Australia partnership (2025-08, partner-led delivery). Vancity is the first *Canadian credit union* comp in-base and the largest asset base of this cohort. Rival-stack comps for the same "modernize the core" job show the competitive field: Akbank's German unit live on **Mambu** (2026-02), Infosys **Finacle** cloud migration for Uniting Financial (2025-08), and **10x Banking**/audax (2026-01) — i.e., Temenos is defending share against Mambu, Finacle, Thought Machine and 10x in cloud-core deals.

**Vendor financial posture.** Temenos (SIX: TEMN) is executing, not distressed: Q2-26 ARR $881M, +11% YoY cc; FY-26 guidance reconfirmed (~12% ARR growth, ~9% subscription/SaaS). But Q2-26 subscription & SaaS revenue *declined 13%* as several large deals slipped out of the quarter (mgmt says signed, not lost; Q3 S&S guided +30%). 316 customer go-lives reported in 2025. Takeaway: healthy franchise, but recent lumpy deal timing underscores that signed logos like Vancity convert to recognized revenue over years, not quarters.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Announcement ≠ delivery.** This is a vendor press release at *selection* stage — no go-live date, no contract value, no migration milestones disclosed. Executive quotes in the source are empty placeholders. Core migrations of this size routinely slip multi-year; benefit realization and member-impact claims are unproven.
2. **Execution/business-continuity risk on a large mutual.** 588k members / 3M accounts across retail + corporate + payments simultaneously is a broad scope; build-and-migrate reduces but does not eliminate the ~45%-cited interruption risk, and credit-union member tolerance for disruption is low.
3. **Vendor delivery dependence.** Temenos increasingly leans on a hybrid partner-led delivery model (cf. Cognizant/Australia); no SI partner is named for Vancity, leaving delivery capacity/accountability unclear.
4. **Promotional framing / unquantified ESG & efficiency claims.** "Scalable, resilient, ESG commitments, simplified management" are vendor marketing with no metrics, baseline or independent validation.
5. **Competitive switching risk pre-go-live.** The core-modernization field (Mambu, Finacle, Thought Machine, 10x) is contested; selections at this stage can be re-scoped or re-tendered before contract/go-live.
