## Контекст

### [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On **2026-09-29**, VTB first deputy chairman **Dmitry Pyanov** said VTB expects to **close its purchase of a 5% stake in WB Bank** (Wildberries Bank, the credit institution of the merged Wildberries&Russ / RWB group) **"closer to the end of October" 2026**. The retail.ru source note (dated 2026-09-29) reports the same: buyer (VTB) + RWB plan to close by end of October.

De-PR'd substance:
- This is **not a new deal** — it is the **closing-date confirmation** of the 5%-stake acquisition first announced on **2026-05-26** (strategic partnership). The headline "buyer to close 5% stake deal by October" is a **timeline update on a known deal**, originally targeted for "summer" close (Kostin, May), then **slipped to September, then to end-October**.
- **Price / valuation UNDISCLOSED.** Neither party has published the consideration for the 5% or the implied WB Bank valuation. Any ruble figure for "5% cost" is **[UNSOURCED]**. (analysis) Opacity here is the real story — a 5% minority sale to a state incumbent with an option to increase, priced privately, is closer to a strategic foothold than a mark-to-market event.
- **Why it slipped:** the delay was attributed to RWB management/HR being pulled onto "extraordinary events" — i.e. the **Ukrainian drone/strike damage to Wildberries warehouses**; Russian authorities reportedly explored support for Wildberries after the attacks (per MarketScreener/Moscow Times). So the close date is a function of **operational disruption at the target's parent**, not deal-term renegotiation per sourced reporting.
- **Financing side (VTB):** VTB funds the broader partnership push via an **SPO/additional issue of up to ~6.29bn shares at ₽87/share**, targeting **₽300–400bn** (closer to the lower bound). The 49%-headline new-issue authorization and the ₽87 price are VTB-capital-raise parameters, **not** the 5% WB Bank price.

### [1] Competitors / peers
Russian "marketplace-bank" subvertical — fintech arms embedded in e-commerce platforms:
- **Ozon Bank** — materially ahead on scale: universal licence, >31.7m active customers, fintech deposits ₽348.6bn (Jun-2025), fintech pre-tax profit ₽13bn Q1-2026 (~37.5% of group EBITDA). DFA platform registered with CBR; ATM build-out to 2,000 by end-2026 (see [[Ozon Bank to double ATM network to 2,000 by end-2026]], [[Ozon Bank registers digital financial assets platform with CBR]]). VTB separately **took a large Ozon share stake as collateral** (see [[VTB takes large Ozon share stake as collateral]]) — VTB is wiring itself into *both* leading marketplace fintechs.
- **Yandex Bank** — third BigTech fintech in the ~40%-of-electronic-payments cohort.
- **State incumbents (Sber, VTB)** — the counter-party, not a peer, here. The VTB tie-up is the incumbent **co-opting** the challenger's distribution.

Position: WB Bank is **smaller and later into lending than Ozon Bank** (assets ~₽125bn, ~45th by assets per 2025 Expert RA/cbonds). Its moat is the Wildberries 80m-user traffic/data funnel, now plus VTB's balance sheet and branch/ATM/mortgage rails. **+ Why:** (analysis) the lay of the land is "captive distribution beats price" — none of these banks competes on rate; they compete on who owns the checkout and the seller settlement flow. Second-order: by selling 5% to VTB, WB Bank concedes that it **cannot scale unsecured retail on its own balance sheet/rails** and needs an incumbent's infrastructure — the opposite of the Ozon Bank path (which built its own licence and ATM network).

### [2] Company history / fit
- **2024-10-01** — Wildberries merges with Russ (advertising group) into RWB after a violent control dispute (Vladislav Bakalchuk opposed the merger; a September-2024 HQ shootout left two dead; Tatyana Kim/Bakalchuk prevailed with Kremlin/Kadyrov-faction dynamics in the background). On merger day RWB paid Russ's ~"billion-ruble" debts to VTB and the tax service — **so VTB has been a creditor to this group from the start.**
- **2025** — WB Bank balance sheet grows >3x; Expert RA assigns ruBBB (Jul-2025). Ex-VTB Gorshkov becomes WB Bank board chairman (Aug-2025).
- **2026-05-26/28** — VTB–RWB strategic partnership announced: VTB buys 5% of WB Bank (option to raise), WB Bank gets VTB branches/ATMs/mortgages/investment products; VTB gains access to ~80m marketplace users (doubling its ~31m client base); economics not expected before 2027 (Pyanov).
- **2026-06** — WB Bank launches savings (12% p.a.) + consumer loans up to ₽500k (see [[WB Bank launches savings accounts and consumer loans]]); Expert RA upgrades to ruBBB+; VTB sells the former Otkritie office (Vivaldi Plaza) to RWB (see [[VTB to sell former Otkritie office to Wildberries-Russ]]).
- **2026-07-07** — Partnership architect Gorshkov returns to VTB; Emelyanov named acting WB Bank chairman (see [[WB Bank names Svyatoslav Emelyanov acting board chairman]]).
- **2026-09-29** — Pyanov confirms end-October close target (this note).

Fit: logical and continuous. **+ Why:** (analysis) WB Bank's historic ~200% ROE rested on *risk-free commission* income from seller settlements. To grow it must pivot into **unsecured retail credit** — which imports credit risk it has never cycled through a downturn and needs funding/distribution it lacks. Selling 5% to the state's second-largest bank is the structural answer: VTB rails + a risk-pedigree operating chief (Emelyanov, ex-Post Bank/Home Credit) hedge the exact asset-quality risk Expert RA flags.

### [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novelty of THIS item: low-to-moderate.** The *deal* is 4+ months old; what is new is only the **confirmed end-October close window** and the implicit signal that drone-attack disruption did not kill the deal. No new terms, no disclosed price, no new reporting period.
- **Traction (real, not announced):** WB Bank's consumer-lending launch (Jun-2026) is live; VTB's claimed prize is access to ~80m marketplace users vs its ~31m base, with "little audience overlap." But **the integration's P&L impact is explicitly not expected before 2027** (Pyanov) — so there is **no near-term traction number** to point to. The 5% + option structure is a foothold, not a controlling play.
- **+ Who captures the margin:** (analysis) VTB captures distribution and a cheap optionality on the fastest-growing retail-credit funnel in Russia; RWB captures funding, branches, ATMs and mortgage product it would otherwise take years to build. The open question is whether the eventual **stake increase** (the option) is where the real value transfer happens — a 5% close is deliberately small and low-information. What breaks it: if unsecured-retail cost-of-risk spikes as the loan book seasons, the high-ROE thesis that justifies any WB Bank valuation collapses.

### [4] What's next / market sentiment
- **Immediate:** close targeted end-October 2026; watch for (a) any disclosed consideration/valuation at close, (b) CBR non-objection, (c) confirmation vs further slippage given ongoing security disruptions.
- **Medium:** the **option to increase the stake** is the real forward catalyst — a move from 5% toward a larger minority would reprice the relationship from "foothold" to "co-ownership." Economics expected to show up in VTB numbers from 2027.
- **Financing overhang:** VTB's ₽300–400bn SPO at ₽87/share carries **dilution risk** for VTB shareholders — a recurring complaint in retail-investor channels. (analysis) The partnership's cost to VTB equity is arguably larger and more certain than the (undisclosed) 5% cheque.
- **+ Why the market goes this way:** (analysis) Russia's sanctioned, concentrated banking system pushes state incumbents to buy into the few high-growth consumer funnels (marketplaces) rather than build organically. Counterintuitive second-order effect: embedding into Wildberries also imports **Wildberries' physical-infrastructure war risk** (warehouses are drone targets) and its governance/control-dispute history into VTB's strategic exposure — concentration here is a source of fragility, not just growth.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian "marketplace banks" (банки маркетплейсов) — embedded-finance arms of e-commerce platforms (Ozon Bank, WB/Wildberries Bank, Yandex fintech). Structure: a small set of platform-captive banks sitting on top of large-incumbent-dominated Russian retail banking (Sber, VTB). Value is created at the point-of-sale moment inside the app (wallet, card, BNPL/рассрочка, cashback), not from being a primary bank. Entry barriers are high on the banking side (CBR licence, capital) but the marketplaces cleared them by acquiring/standing up licensed banks and bolting them onto existing traffic. Drivers / "why now": (1) VTB–Wildberries-Russ (RWB) strategic partnership announced 26–27 May 2026 — VTB takes an initial 5% of WB Bank with an option to raise it; the Oct-close news is the execution leg of that May deal (per TASS/Interfax/Moscow Times). (2) VTB framed the rationale as distribution: access to "over 80mn active marketplace customers," said to roughly double its client base (per www1.ru/Interfax, 27 Jun 2026) — i.e. the state incumbent is renting the marketplace's reach rather than the marketplace building a standalone bank. Second-order: this is the opposite of the global "fintech disintermediates the bank" story — here the incumbent co-opts the embedded-finance channel.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: active clients / platform MAU, share of GMV paid via own wallet/BNPL, attach of cashback, off-platform usage (the "trust" metric). Key players: Ozon Bank, WB Bank, Yandex fintech (disruptors riding platform traffic) vs Sber/VTB/Alfa (incumbents on balance-sheet and primary-banking relationships). Basis of competition: distribution + discounts/cashback, not rate or product depth. Recent moves: WB Bank launched savings accounts + consumer loans up to ₽500k, 24–60mo (22 Jun 2026, [[WB Bank launches savings accounts and consumer loans]]); Ozon Bank registered a DFA platform with the CBR ([[Ozon Bank registers digital financial assets platform with CBR]]) and is doubling its ATM network to 2,000 by end-2026 ([[Ozon Bank to double ATM network to 2,000 by end-2026]]). WB Bank's position: large by client reach, small by balance sheet — ranked ~49th in Russia with RUB149bn assets and RUB64.6bn capital as of 1 Apr 2026 (per Moscow Times/Intellinews) vs VTB's RUB35.4tn assets. Moat: network effects / embedded distribution from the Wildberries marketplace `(analysis)` — but Axenix's Jul-2026 survey argues the moat is shallow: 85% see these as embedded services not banks, 60% don't use them off-platform, ~65% would quit if cashback stopped ([[Axenix study marketplace banks hit trust ceiling despite traffic]]) — treat Axenix percentages as consultancy marketing-grade, direction only.

**Comps & multiples.** This is a 5% minority stake with no disclosed price → **transaction multiple: no data** (no deal value published; VTB funds the broader partnership partly via a separate ₽300–400bn additional share issue, which is VTB capital-raising, not the WB-Bank price). Scale anchor for WB Bank: RUB149bn assets, RUB64.6bn capital (1 Apr 2026). Note: web summaries citing WB Bank "RUB64bn profit last year" / "RUB34.6bn Q1-2026 net profit" are **not credible** — a ~RUB64bn profit on ~RUB64.6bn capital implies ~100% ROE; this looks like a garbled source (profit/capital conflation) → `[UNSOURCED]`, do not use. Internal comps (base, directional — different geos/structures, so qualitative, distribution not computed):
- [[TBC Bank Group acquires majority stake in Uzbek firm Billz]] — bank buying platform (reverse direction).
- [[Emirates NBD to take 60% stake in RBL Bank for $3 billion]] — control stake, implies ~$5bn equity value; not comparable to a 5% embedded-bank minority.
- [[Kenro Capital eyes up to $40M stake in Pine Labs]] — secondary minority in a platform-fintech, typically at a discount — closest in *type* (minority) but different asset.
Peer operating comp (not a price): Ozon fintech client base 34.4mn, +43% YoY; Ozon Q1-2026 group revenue RUB300.9bn / net profit RUB4.5bn (per Intellinews) — shows marketplace-bank economics scale with platform GMV, not standalone banking. Verdict: can't mark rich/cheap — price undisclosed.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Disclosure gap / price opacity** — no stake price published; the headline deal is strategically material but financially unquantifiable, and the ₽300–400bn VTB issue muddies what is actually being paid for WB Bank. Can't underwrite value.
2. **Shallow deposit franchise (concentration on cashback)** — Axenix + the off-platform-usage data suggest the client base is promo-driven and single-platform; if VTB's thesis is "80mn customers," the second-order risk is that those customers are transactional, not primary-banking relationships, so the acquired reach may not convert to sticky balances.
3. **State/geopolitical & regulatory concentration** — a sanctioned state incumbent (VTB) taking a position in the dominant Russian marketplace's bank concentrates the sector under state-adjacent control and raises CBR/political-direction and sanctions-exposure risk for the combined entity.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This looks like the start of **consolidation, not a re-rating**: the Russian marketplace-bank model is bending toward incumbent-capture (VTB renting Wildberries' distribution) rather than platforms disintermediating banks — watch whether VTB exercises its option to lift the 5% toward control, and whether Ozon/Yandex respond with their own incumbent tie-ups or push to become standalone primary banks. Falsifiable thesis: if WB Bank's off-platform / primary-banking usage doesn't rise post-deal (still a cashback wallet), the "80mn customers" rationale is oversold; trigger to watch = disclosed stake price and any option-exercise toward a larger VTB holding.
