# Noda launches BIN-to-Bank to cut online card payment costs

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇪🇺 Noda launches BIN-to-Bank to reduce online card payment costs. Available across 26 European countries, the open banking checkout solution identifies customers’ issuing banks from their card details and redirects eligible transactions to bank payments.
