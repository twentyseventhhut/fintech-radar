 Государство продало 67% переработчика цинка и свинца «Дальполиметалл» 
АО «Русская промышленная компания» (РПК) признано победителем торгов по продаже 67% акций ГМК «Дальполиметалл», производителя цинкового и свинцового концентратов с содержанием серебра в Приморском крае. Итоги аукциона опубликованы 14 октября. РПК стала единственным участником торгов, предложив 553,74 млн руб., что соответствует начальной цене. Об этом пишет " Коммерсант ". 
Акции «Дальполиметалла» были обращены в доход государства в 2025 году по иску Генпрокуратуры к Эдуарду Янакову, занимавшему пост депутата Госдумы в 2008–2011 годах, а также другим ответчикам. По данным ведомства, господин Янаков приобрел предприятие в период исполнения полномочий депутата в нарушение закона. В пояснениях к отчетности «Дальполиметалла» за 2025 год говорится, что РФ владеет 67% акций компании, перерегистрация еще 33% была не завершена.
«Дальполиметалл» включает два рудника на Николаевском и Партизанском месторождениях и обогатительную фабрику, способную перерабатывать около 1,2 млн тонн руды в год. По данным отчетности, в 2025 году компания увеличила добычу руды на 65% год к году, до 483,66 тыс. тонн, выпуск концентратов вырос на 70%, до 22,53 тыс. тонн. Весь выпущенный свинцовый и цинковый концентрат экспортировался в Китай, сказано в отчетности. Выручка «Дальполиметалла» за 2025 год снизилась на 23%, до 2,51 млрд руб., чистый убыток составил 6,96 млн руб. В феврале 2025 года в отношении компании была введена процедура наблюдения.
РПК учреждена в мае 2026 года, акционеры не раскрываются, гендиректор РПК — Феликс Нахшунов. С ноября 2023 года по 2025 год по поручению Росимущества он руководил Дальнегорским ГОКом (сегодня входит в «Росхим»), единственным в РФ производителем борной кислоты, а также возглавлял недействующие сегодня структуры «Росхима» — «РХ-Ресурс» и «Радамант». В 2018–2020 годах господин Нахшунов также был гендиректором кемеровского угольного актива итальянской Coeclerici.
В РПК сообщили, что рассматривают «Дальполиметалл» как стратегический актив.
На первом этапе новый инвестор намерен вывести компанию из процедур банкротства и провести финансовое оздоровление: урегулировать обязательства перед кредиторами, восстановить платежеспособность, стабилизировать производство и зарплаты. Параллельно РПК входит в режим территории опережающего развития (ТОР) и в программы развития Дальнего Востока и Арктики, говорят в компании.
В 2027 году планируется вывести фабрику на проектную мощность 1 млн тонн горной массы в год и выпуск около 50 тыс. тонн цинкового, свинцового и серебросодержащего концентрата. Цель — к 2029 году выйти на объем 100 тыс. тонн концентрата в год, заявили в РПК. В реновацию фабрики, геологоразведку и новое горное и обогатительное оборудование РПК намерена инвестировать 5 млрд руб.
Как отмечает источник в отрасли, с переходом к российскому владельцу компания сможет получать краевые субсидии и преференции в рамках ТОР.
«Ресурсная база в 14,7 млн тонн руды позволит осваивать месторождения в течение 15 лет без учета прироста запасов за счет геологоразведки. И прогнозы рынка благоприятные»,— добавляет собеседник “Ъ”. Как говорится в обзоре Kept, в сентябре цена на цинк на мировом рынке впервые с мая 2022 года превысила $4,18 тыс. за тонну, увеличившись на 35% с начала года. Причина — сокращающееся предложение рафинированного металла за пределами Китая, а также снижение глобальной добычи руды, уточняют аналитики. Советник управляющего фондом «Индустриальный код» Максим Шапошников замечает, что концентрат «Дальполиметалла» исторически поставлялся на Челябинский цинковый завод, но сегодня первоочередной целью компании, видимо, будет экспорт в Китай, что проще с точки зрения логистики.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened (dated).** On 2026-10-14 the Russian state (via Rosimushchestvo/the Federal Property Agency) published the results of an auction for 67% of GMK Dalpolimetall, a Primorsky Krai producer of zinc, lead and silver-bearing concentrate. The sole bidder, AO "Russkaya Promyshlennaya Kompaniya" (Russian Industrial Company, RPK), won at the 553.74M-ruble (~$6.8M) starting price. The 67% stake had been "обращён в доход государства" (confiscated to the state) in 2025 following a Prosecutor General's suit against ex-State Duma deputy (2008–2011) Eduard Yanakov, who allegedly acquired the asset in violation of the law on deputies' commercial activity; re-registration of the remaining 33% was not yet complete. The company had been in bankruptcy observation (наблюдение) since February 2025. (Sources: [Mergers.ru](https://mergers.ru/news/Gosudarstvo-prodalo-67-pererabotchika-cinka-i-svinca-Dalpolimetall-87647), [Kommersant](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/9026346), [Deita](https://deita.ru/article/591681).)

**[1] Who the buyer is.** RPK was founded only in May 2026; its shareholders are undisclosed. Its CEO is Felix Nakhshunov, who from Nov 2023 to 2025 ran Dalnegorsky GOK (Russia's sole boric-acid producer, now part of Roskhim) on Rosimushchestvo's mandate, and headed now-defunct Roskhim structures "RKh-Resurs" and "Radamant." Russian and Ukrainian outlets link RPK to the Rotenberg family: Roskhim's board is chaired by Igor Rotenberg, son of Putin ally Arkady Rotenberg ([NV](https://english.nv.ua/nation/russia-nationalizes-one-of-largest-producers-of-lead-and-zinc-50498808.html), [ua.news](https://ua.news/en/world/poviazanii-iz-rotenbergami-biznesmen-otrimav-kolishni-vugilni-aktivi-akhmetova-v-rosiyi)). This makes the deal a textbook "seize-then-sell-to-a-connected-buyer" transfer at (low, single-bidder, starting-price) valuation.

**[2] The asset / numbers.** Dalpolimetall: two mines (Nikolaevskoye, Partizanskoye deposits) + a concentrator that can process ~1.2M t ore/yr; resource base 14.7M t ore (~15 years of mine life). 2025: ore mined +65% YoY to 483,660 t; concentrate output +70% to 22,530 t — all lead/zinc concentrate exported to China. 2025 revenue −23% to RUB 2.51bn; net loss RUB 6.96M. RPK plan: exit bankruptcy, stabilize payroll/output, join a TOR (priority development territory) regime for subsidies; ramp the plant to 1M t ore/yr in 2027 (~50k t concentrate), reach 100k t concentrate/yr by 2029; invest RUB 5bn in plant renovation, exploration and equipment.

**[3] Market backdrop.** Zinc hit multi-year highs in 2026 — LME ~$3,824/t in late August (highest since June 2022), with forecasts of ~$4,100/t by Q4; Kept's review cited >$4,180/t in September (+35% YTD) ([Industry Spread](https://theindustryspread.com/zinc-4100-q4-2026-west-versus-china-split/), [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com/zinc-price-supply-fears-speculators-short-squeeze-2026/)). The driver is a regional (ex-China) refined-metal squeeze and falling global mine output, with China poised to turn net refined-zinc exporter. Favourable prices + China-facing logistics underpin the strategic-asset thesis; concentrate historically went to Chelyabinsk Zinc Plant but is now steered toward China export.

**[4] Why it matters / fintech-M&A angle.** This is a small ticket but a clean data point in Russia's post-2022 nationalization-then-recycling machine: the state has confiscated an estimated ~RUB 6.5tn (~$80–90bn) of private assets since 2022 ([Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/07/30/prosecutor-general-ousts-official-overseeing-wartime-corporate-asset-seizures-a93378)) and is now disposing of them via opaque, often single-bidder auctions to politically connected buyers — same mechanism as [[PSB fails third auction for nationalized Vektor Rail]] (Taicher assets, Genprokuratura suit), [[PSB auctions Agropromkredit bank for 2.85bn rubles]] (Bikov/Bobrov bank seized by court), [[Shishkarev seeks to buy SASCO shipping from Rosatom]], and the foreign mirror-image in [[Germany begins privatization of nationalized Gazprom unit]] (SEFE). Note the timing coincidence: on the same day (2026-10-15) prosecutors withdrew their seizure suit against Timur Turlov (Freedom), underscoring how discretionary and politically mediated this asset-redistribution process is.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team / second-order questions**

1. Is RUB 553.74M a fair price for a producer with a 14.7M t resource base and a RUB 5bn capex plan? No — single bidder at the starting price, asset in bankruptcy; the price reflects distressed/controlled-sale dynamics, not fair value. The ~$6.8M headline is trivial vs the resource and vs the RUB 5bn committed capex. (Answered.)

2. Who really controls RPK? Undisclosed shareholders; CEO Nakhshunov's Roskhim track record and press reporting point to Rotenberg-orbit control. Beneficial ownership is formally open but practically attributable to connected parties. (Open on legal proof; strong circumstantial.)

3. Was this a competitive auction? No — RPK was the sole participant, bid = starting price. Classic signal of a pre-arranged buyer. (Answered.)

4. Does the state actually get clean 100% control to sell? No — only 67% was confiscated and sold; the remaining 33% re-registration was incomplete. Minority/legacy claims could complicate the restructuring. (Open.)

5. Is the "strategic asset" framing credible given 2025 financials? Revenue fell 23% to RUB 2.51bn with a small net loss and a bankruptcy observation — weak standalone, but output was up sharply (+65–70%) and zinc prices are at multi-year highs, so the turnaround thesis is plausible if capex lands. (Answered.)

6. How sanctions-exposed is the deal? All output goes to China; a Rotenberg-linked buyer is already OFAC/EU-designated territory. Western financing/off-take is irrelevant; this is a fully domestic+China-facing asset, insulating it from sanctions impact but closing it to Western capital. (Answered.)

7. Is the 2027/2029 ramp (50k→100k t concentrate) realistic? Requires near-doubling+ of 2025's 22.5k t output and RUB 5bn capex on a plant exiting bankruptcy — ambitious; depends on exploration, equipment delivery under sanctions, and sustained zinc prices. (Open.)

8. Does TOR/Far East subsidy eligibility materially change economics? Краевые субсидии + TOR preferences lower tax/infra burden and are a stated reason a Russian owner (vs confiscated structure) was needed — meaningful but not quantified in sources. (Partially answered.)

9. Is this genuinely new vs prior coverage? Yes — no prior Dalpolimetall/Yanakov note in the DB; the Oct-14 auction result is the first coverage. The 2025 confiscation is referenced only as background. (Answered — fresh.)

10. Could the price understate a hidden debt assumption? The buyer must "settle obligations to creditors" out of bankruptcy, so true acquisition cost = RUB 553.74M + assumed liabilities + RUB 5bn capex. Headline price is misleadingly low. (Answered.)

11. Any read-through to listed/fintech names? Low direct read-through; relevant as an M&A/capital-markets pattern (state asset recycling) rather than an investable fintech event. Chelyabinsk Zinc Plant loses a historical feed source. (Answered.)

12. Is the zinc-price tailwind durable? Driven by an ex-China regional squeeze that could reverse if China turns net exporter at scale; forecasts ($4,100–4,450/t) are not guaranteed. Turnaround economics are price-sensitive. (Open.)

13. Reputational/governance signal for the Russian M&A market? Strongly negative for minority-rights/rule-of-law perception; reinforces that ownership can be expropriated via Genprokuratura suits and redistributed. (Answered.)

14. Timing coincidence with Turlov suit withdrawal — meaningful? Both on/around 2026-10-15; illustrates the discretionary, politically mediated nature of the seizure/release machine rather than a direct link. (Answered — contextual.)

**Importance: 2/5 — rationale:** Tiny ticket (~$6.8M), single-bidder distressed sale of a niche regional mining asset with no listed/fintech read-through. Its value is purely as a clean, well-documented data point in the Russian state's seize-and-recycle M&A pattern (Rotenberg-linked buyer, Genprokuratura-confiscated asset), which is a recurring theme in the DB — hence a modest, context-only 2/5.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** This is a Russian non-ferrous mining (zinc/lead/silver concentrate) asset sold under the state's deprivatization-then-reprivatization cycle, not a fintech deal — the fintech-relevant layer is the state-asset-management / M&A mechanism. Market backdrop is favourable on price: per Kept (via Kommersant, as of Sep 2026) zinc on the world market topped $4,180/t for the first time since May 2022, +35% YTD, driven by shrinking refined supply outside China and falling global mine output. By early Oct 2026 LME zinc had cooled to ~$3,710–3,781/t cash (per LME via EBC/DiscoveryAlert, as of 8–9 Oct 2026), so the "+35% YTD" print is already partly unwound. Sell-side is split: J.P. Morgan sees ~$3,400–3,500/t through end-2026; Wood Mackenzie ~$3,350 with a projected ~80kt deficit and thin ~100kt LME stocks (per Mining Weekly/Wood Mackenzie, 2026); ILZSG models only a ~19kt refined deficit for 2026 (per ILZSG). So the thesis rests on a tight-but-not-dramatic deficit and a fragile stock buffer, not a runaway bull market. **Why now:** the asset was seized into state ownership in 2025 (Prosecutor-General suit vs ex-MP Eduard Yanakov) and Rosimushchestvo is under pressure to offload a large seized-asset inventory — ~RUB 2.5tn on its balance sheet as of early 2026 (per Russia Post / SZRU-cited data) — so the state is a motivated, time-constrained seller.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs for a concentrate miner: ore throughput (t/yr), concentrate output (t/yr), metal content/grade, cash cost per tonne, and realised price vs LME (minus treatment charges — TCs are at record lows per Fastmarkets LME Week 2026, which *helps* miners). Dalpolimetall: 2025 ore +65% YoY to 483.66kt, concentrate +70% to 22.53kt, concentrator nameplate ~1.2Mt ore/yr, resource base 14.7Mt (~15 yrs). Target: ~50kt concentrate by 2027, ~100kt by 2029, RUB 5bn capex. **Position:** sub-scale, distressed (bankruptcy observation since Feb 2025), 100%-export to China — a niche far-eastern player, not a peer of integrated majors (UMMC, Chelyabinsk Zinc / Chelyabinsk historically its offtaker; Nornickel is nickel/PGM not a true comp). **Basis of competition:** cost/logistics, not product — the moat (such as it is) is resource life + TOR (priority-development-territory) tax/subsidy preferences the new Russian owner unlocks, plus proximity to Chinese buyers (analysis). No disclosed cash-cost or LTV-type KPIs → `[UNSOURCED]`.

**Comps & multiples.** Deal economics: 67% sold for RUB 553.74M at the starting price (sole bidder) → implied 100% equity ≈ `553.74M / 0.67 = RUB 826.5M` (~$10M at ~RUB 81/$). Against 2025 revenue of RUB 2.51bn that is EV/Revenue ≈ `0.83bn / 2.51bn = 0.33x` (equity-value proxy; no public net-debt, so true EV likely higher — the firm is in bankruptcy, so this is a distressed, not a market, multiple). The RUB 5bn capex pledge is ~6x the implied equity value — i.e. the price pays for optionality on the resource, not the current (loss-making: net loss RUB 6.96M, revenue −23% YoY) business. Internal comps (same Russian seize-then-sell mechanism): [[PSB fails third auction for nationalized Vektor Rail]] and [[PSB auctions Agropromkredit bank for 2.85bn rubles]] (state bank PSB disposing seized assets), [[Russian prosecutors drop asset-seizure claim against Timur Turlov]] (seizure reversed), and the cross-border analogue [[Germany begins privatization of nationalized Gazprom unit]]. Distribution not computed (different sectors/sizes) — qualitative: single-bidder-at-floor pricing is the recurring signature of these auctions, confirming thin demand. Flag: **cheap, but correctly cheap** — distress + control-transfer friction, not a mispricing.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Reflexive-seizure / title risk.** Only 67% was conveyed; re-registration of the remaining 33% was unfinished per 2025 accounts — incomplete title and a precedent that the state can seize *and* has reversed claims ([[Russian prosecutors drop asset-seizure claim against Timur Turlov]]) means ownership is not final. Second-order: raises the cost of outside capital/offtake financing.
2. **Single-buyer concentration + opaque owner.** 100% of concentrate exports to China and the buyer RPK (founded May 2026, undisclosed shareholders, director Feliks Nakhshunov) is unknown — pricing power sits with Chinese smelters; any China demand/policy shift hits the only revenue line.
3. **Execution vs. balance sheet.** Scaling from 22.53kt to 100kt concentrate (2029) on RUB 5bn capex while exiting bankruptcy and restoring solvency is a steep ramp; if zinc retreats to the forecast $3,350/t band, the cushion for a sub-scale, high-logistics-cost mine is thin.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** (analysis) The single-bidder-at-floor sale is the tell for the whole Russian reprivatization pipeline: seized assets clear only to pre-aligned buyers at the reserve, so Rosimushchestvo's ~RUB 2.5tn inventory is a low-velocity, administratively-priced book rather than a competitive market — watch the 33% re-registration and whether RPK actually funds the RUB 5bn and the 2027 ~50kt milestone. Falsifier: a genuinely contested auction (≥2 real bidders above floor) on a comparable seized asset would break the "distressed, captive-buyer" read.
