_PayPal brings its agentic commerce services to Glance, providing the commerce capabilities that turn AI-powered product discovery into trusted transactions_

SAN MATEO, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Glance, an AI-powered personal shopping platform backed by global technology company InMobi, today announced an agentic commerce integration with PayPal, bringing real-time product data and native checkout directly to Glance for tens of millions of consumers in the United States. Now consumers can discover and visualize products, build a cart, and complete their purchase by leveraging PayPal’s trusted offerings without leaving the experience.

“The lines between where people discover, shop, and buy are disappearing,” said Mike Edmonds, Vice President of Agentic Commerce at PayPal. “Our goal is to help prepare merchants for a future where commerce can happen anywhere. As emerging AI shopping agents like Glance change how people shop, PayPal gives a reliable way to buy with confidence.”

Pre-installed on millions of phones by telecom operators and device manufacturers, and available for download on additional devices, Glance brings AI-powered shopping directly into consumers’ hands. From a single photo, shoppers using Glance can generate images of themselves to virtually try on clothes with an AI avatar and share looks with friends. When ready to purchase, shoppers can use PayPal to complete their purchase without being redirected to another site. As the only partner to provide native in-app checkout on Glance, PayPal is accelerating the path to purchase for consumers.

For retailers, the integration provides a simple way to extend their storefronts into Glance’s platform. PayPal’s agentic commerce services allow merchants to easily connect to multiple AI-powered shopping experiences through a single integration, surface real-time product information such as availability and pricing, and complete transactions while maintaining control of their customer relationship – enabling trusted commerce between consumers, merchants and AI agents.

“Glance meets consumers at the moment of inspiration, before they've even thought to search. Bringing native PayPal checkout into that experience means we can now carry consumers from the moment something catches their attention through purchase in the same flow,” said Mansi Jain, Chief Operating Officer at Glance. “That makes Glance more valuable to merchants because they can reach consumers earlier, help them evaluate what to buy, and give them a direct way to complete the transaction. As AI takes on more of the shopping journey, connecting discovery with commerce in the same experience becomes increasingly important.”

Glance is already embedded across more than 60 million mobile and CTV devices, through relationships with leading device manufacturers, including Samsung, Motorola, DirecTV, Google’s Android TV, and two of the top three U.S. mobile operators. With the addition of PayPal, Glance can bring full-stack agentic commerce to more merchants, connecting real-time product data, cart and checkout in one experience. Merchants interested in bringing their products to Glance’s AI-powered shopping experience can get started at paypal.ai.

# Context-enrichment: Glance partners PayPal for AI-powered shopping and checkout
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On 2026-10-15 Glance (an AI "personal shopping agent" operated by Glance InMobi Pte. Ltd., an InMobi associate backed by Mithril Capital, Google and Jio) announced an **agentic-commerce integration with PayPal** for US consumers. The real, concrete deliverable: **PayPal native in-app checkout** inside Glance — shoppers can discover/visualize products (incl. an AI "virtual try-on" from a selfie), build a cart and **pay via PayPal without leaving the surface**. PayPal is positioned as "the only partner to provide native in-app checkout on Glance." On the merchant side it is PayPal's existing **Agentic Commerce Services** (launched 2025-10, see [[PayPal launches Agentic Commerce Services for AI checkout]]): one integration exposes real-time inventory/pricing to multiple AI surfaces; merchants onboard at paypal.ai.

**Why framed this way / what it reveals:** This is a *distribution* win for PayPal, not a new product. PayPal supplies the same agentic rails it already ships to ChatGPT, Google and Microsoft Copilot; Glance supplies reach. The PR anchors to a flattering metric — "**more than 60 million mobile and CTV devices**" (Samsung, Motorola, DirecTV, Android TV, two of the top-3 US mobile operators) — but that is a *pre-installed/device-capable footprint*, not active shoppers (external coverage flags it as vendor-reported; Yahoo/AI: ~11% of SMBs are "agent-ready", only ~28% expose full inventory to agents). Note the geographic sleight: Glance's headline global scale (300M+ MAU, 450M+ installs) is overwhelmingly **India via the lock screen**; this deal is explicitly **US-only, "tens of millions."** So "tens of millions of devices with a capability" ≠ "tens of millions buying through an AI avatar." Announced capability — **no GMV, no live-merchant count, no conversion disclosed.**

## [1] Competitors / peers
Agentic checkout is now a crowded 2025–26 land-grab, and PayPal is on *every* surface:
- **OpenAI Instant Checkout / ACP** (Stripe) — launched 2025-09-29 with Etsy; [[Walmart partners OpenAI for ChatGPT checkout]], [[Stripe enables payments in ChatGPT with OpenAI]]. But adoption underwhelmed: OpenAI **retired Instant Checkout in March 2026** in favor of a discovery/redirect model (ACP persists as the rail). A direct cautionary tale for Glance's "discovery→purchase in one flow" thesis.
- **PayPal's own agentic stack:** [[PayPal adopts Agentic Commerce Protocol, expands in ChatGPT]], [[PayPal supports trusted AI checkout with Google]], [[Google and PayPal sign multi-year commerce partnership]], [[Microsoft launches Copilot Checkout with PayPal and Stripe]], [[PayPal acquires Cymbio to accelerate agentic commerce]], [[PayPal adds Honey to new agentic commerce features]].
- **Network rails:** [[Mastercard and PayPal partner on agentic commerce]] (Agent Pay in PayPal wallet), [[Visa works with over 100 partners on agentic shopping solutions]], [[Mastercard unveils Agent Suite for agentic AI]], [[Revolut Pay adds compatibility with Google's AP2]].

**Why the landscape looks this way / second-order:** PayPal has chosen a **"be on every agent" distribution strategy** rather than own the agent. That hedges against any single surface failing (as OpenAI's did) but means PayPal captures only the thin checkout/transaction take-rate, while the discovery/attention layer (Glance, ChatGPT, Google) and the payment-network rail (Visa/Mastercard) sit above and below it. Glance is the *novel surface* here (ambient lock-screen/CTV vs chat), but it is the weakest brand — a surface, not a destination.

## [2] Company history / fit
Glance began as InMobi's **lock-screen monetization** play (raised $145M from Google/Mithril in 2020; targeted 1B users). It pivoted the lock screen into an **AI shopping agent** ("meet consumers at the moment of inspiration, before they've searched"). For PayPal, the fit is clean and cheap: it's a bolt-on of the Cymbio-powered Agentic Commerce Services onto yet another surface, consistent with CEO Alex Chriss's agentic push amid a broader turnaround ([[PayPal plans job cuts under new CEO turnaround plan]], [[PayPal reorganizes into three business units with leadership changes]]).

**Why they act this way:** Glance needs **commerce monetization** to justify its ad-funded lock-screen model and the "shopping agent" rebrand — it has distribution but no native pay rail, so it rents PayPal's. PayPal, facing a stalling core and an FCA competition probe ([[FCA opens competition probe into PayPal, Mastercard and Visa]]), needs **agentic volume + merchant lock-in via a single integration**. Mutual dependency, but Glance needs PayPal (trust + rail) more than PayPal needs Glance (one of many surfaces).

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Genuinely new:** (a) the *surface* — ambient lock-screen/CTV agentic checkout rather than a chat box; (b) PayPal's exclusivity as the sole native in-app checkout on Glance. **Not new:** the plumbing (ACP-style agentic commerce services PayPal has shipped since 2025-10) and the "discovery→buy in one flow" pitch (which OpenAI already tried and pulled back on).

**Why the value-add is likely thin (1–2 levels deeper):** Traction is **unproven** — zero merchant count, GMV, or conversion disclosed; "60M devices" is capability, not demand, and skewed CTV/lock-screen placements (the known Glance UX friction/opt-out complaints undercut intent-quality). In the stack, **margin is captured elsewhere:** Visa/Mastercard own the rail and risk, merchants keep the customer relationship, Glance owns attention — PayPal is the plumbing in the middle on a transaction take-rate. The sharpest open issue is **fraud/merchant liability**: external analysis (Yahoo/AI) notes 93% of merchants think the AI provider should eat losses on wrong purchases, yet nobody in this announcement names who's liable — a nascent insurance/trust layer (Harbor, Cequence) is forming precisely because this is unsolved. So the "value-add" is distribution optionality for PayPal, not a defensible moat.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Expect PayPal to keep stacking surfaces (chat, search, CTV, lock screen) and tout aggregate reach; Glance will try to convert device footprint into merchant sign-ups at paypal.ai. Watch for the first **real metric** (live merchants, GMV, conversion) — until then this is an announcement.

**Why the market goes this way / counterintuitive second-order:** The industry narrative ("commerce happens everywhere, agents do the buying") is running **ahead of merchant readiness and liability clarity** — the OpenAI Instant Checkout retreat (Mar 2026) is the tell that distribution velocity ≠ adoption. Counterintuitively, PayPal being on *every* agent may make its agentic revenue **look diversified but stay structurally thin**: if agents commoditize the checkout step, the pure rails/attention owners capture the economics and the middle layer compresses. A new ambient surface raises the stakes on the unanswered fraud-liability question rather than resolving it.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Agentic commerce — AI agents that discover, evaluate and transact on a consumer's behalf — is the fastest-growing sub-vertical in payments. Per Morgan Stanley, agentic commerce could touch $190bn–$385bn (10–20% share) of US e-commerce by 2030 (morganstanley.com); Bain puts it at $300–500bn / 15–25% of US e-commerce (digitalcommerce360.com, Dec 2025); IDC pegs the global 2025 base at ~$18.4bn growing ~59% CAGR. Treat these as third-party TAM estimates with wide dispersion — not a settled figure. Structure: the value chain is fragmenting into (a) discovery/agent surfaces (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Amazon, and here Glance), (b) protocol/orchestration rails (OpenAI's ACP, Google AP2/Universal Commerce Protocol, Visa Intelligent Commerce, Mastercard Agent Pay), and (c) checkout/payment execution (PayPal, Stripe, the networks). The economic question is which layer captures the take rate. Why now: every major network shipped agentic rails in 2026 (Visa Intelligent Commerce Connect Apr-2026; Mastercard Agent Pay / AP4M live in Singapore & Malaysia) and the surface land-grab for pre-purchase "moment of inspiration" is on.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: for the payment layer — Branded Checkout TPV growth, attach/conversion at agent surfaces, per-transaction take rate; for the agent surface — installed base / MAU and discovery-to-purchase conversion. Key players and basis of competition: Stripe powers OpenAI Instant Checkout in ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot Checkout (distribution via the biggest agent surfaces); Visa (Intelligent Commerce) and Mastercard (Agent Pay) compete on trusted credentialing/tokenization; PayPal competes on a ready consumer wallet + merchant network + "trusted buy" branding. PayPal's own moves: Agentic Commerce Services launched Oct-2025, adopted OpenAI's ACP / in ChatGPT, Mastercard Agent Pay integration, acquired Cymbio (Jan-2026) for merchant catalog discoverability, Microsoft Copilot Checkout (Jan-2026). Protagonist position in THIS deal: PayPal is the checkout execution layer, not the agent; it is "the only partner to provide native in-app checkout on Glance" (businesswire, 2026-10-15) across Glance's 60M+ mobile/CTV devices (Samsung, Motorola, DirecTV, Android TV, two of top-3 US carriers). Moat = PayPal's installed consumer wallet + fraud/trust rails and now exclusivity on a large pre-installed surface (analysis); Glance's moat = distribution (60M+ devices) and pre-search discovery, but it is a new, unproven agent brand.

**Comps & multiples.** PayPal is public (PYPL) and is the grounded comp here. Per Yahoo Finance (as of Oct-2026): market cap ~$48.9bn, Enterprise Value ~$47.06bn, LTM revenue ~$34.13bn → EV/Revenue = $47.06bn / $34.13bn ≈ 1.4x; Price/Sales ~1.42x; Trailing P/E ~9.9x, Forward P/E ~9.1x. That is cheap vs payments peers — a low-growth, re-rating-candidate multiple (growth ~5% YoY), not a high-multiple growth name; agentic commerce is PayPal's attempt to re-accelerate Branded Checkout, which grew only +2% cn in Q1-2026. PayPal scale/IR grounding — Q1 2026 (8-K/earnings release 2026-05-05, s205.q4cdn.com; also 10-Q 2026-05-05): TPV +11% to $464.0bn ($1.86tn annualized run-rate), transaction revenue $7.5bn, 439M active accounts (+1%), 6.5bn payment transactions (+7%), Branded Checkout TPV +2% cn; latest IR filing is the 8-K dated 2026-05-21 (sec.gov). **Risk flags.**
1. Fraud / chargeback-liability gap in agentic checkout. No network rule yet assigns loss to the agent/platform; the merchant of record (the retailer) absorbs chargebacks and dispute cost by default, and 93% of merchants (PYMNTS, Sep-2026) think agent/AI providers should pay. If a Glance agent mis-buys, liability lands on merchants, chilling merchant adoption — the exact supply side both Glance and PayPal need (forkast.news, chargeflow.io, 2026).
2. Trust/adoption is the binding constraint, not technology. Worldpay's 2026 report: consumers fear identity theft (55%), unauthorized purchases (55%) and fraud (53%) in AI shopping — a 60M-device install base ≠ transacting users; "announced," not "adopted" (grep: [[Worldpay report consumers wary of agentic AI shopping fraud]]).
3. PayPal is the executor, not the agent — disintermediation risk. Value in agentic commerce may accrue to the agent/surface and the networks' tokenized credentials; PayPal-as-checkout can be swapped if Glance later adds Stripe or network rails. Exclusivity ("only native checkout") is the mitigant but is a commercial term, not a moat (analysis).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is a distribution land-grab, not a re-rating event for PayPal (too small to move a ~$47bn EV name). Thesis (analysis): PayPal is trading surface exclusivity for position in pre-search discovery as agent surfaces proliferate beyond ChatGPT; watch whether CTV/pre-installed agents convert at all. Falsifiable trigger: if PayPal's Branded Checkout TPV growth (+2% cn in Q1-26) does NOT inflect in the next 2–3 quarters, the agentic-commerce bets (Glance, ChatGPT, Copilot, Cymbio) are optionality, not a growth driver — thesis wrong.
