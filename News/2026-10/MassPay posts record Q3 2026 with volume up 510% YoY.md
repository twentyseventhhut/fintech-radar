Q3 2026 Record Results
In Q1 we called 317% volume growth “a signal, not a spike.” In Q2, 408% answered. Q3 answered again.
Volume grew 510% over Q3 2025. Gross revenue grew 112% year-over-year. That’s three record quarters in a row.
‍
 What the numbers actually mean 
One record quarter can be a big client. Two can be momentum. Three is a pattern.
The number we watch closest isn’t volume - it’s revenue keeping pace with it. Revenue growth went from 98% in Q2 to 112% in Q3. That tells us clients aren’t just moving more money through MassPay. They’re using more of the platform, in more corridors, on more rails. The model gets stronger as it scales.
‍
 Who’s driving the growth 
The growth isn’t coming from one client or one industry. It’s coming from every kind of business that moves money: enterprises paying suppliers and vendors, marketplaces paying sellers, direct selling companies paying distributors, gaming platforms paying players and partners, creator platforms paying creators, and workforce and payroll platforms paying contractors and employees.
Different industries, same truth: the people and partners they pay keep the business running. A late or failed payment isn’t a back-office problem. It’s a broken promise. That’s the problem we’re built to solve.
 The problem we set out to solve in Q3 
Most businesses that pay people and partners globally are stitching together several providers: one to collect funds, one for domestic payouts, one for cross-border, each with its own contract, dashboard, and reconciliation headache. Every handoff adds cost, delay, and risk.
So in Q3, we closed the loop.
‍
 MassPay Collect launched in the US. Businesses can now bring money in on the same platform they use to pay out. Europe, Canada, and the UK are next.
 Cash App payouts went live. Payees who want their money fast now have one of the most popular options in the US.
 Our partnership with Bottomline brings Paymode virtual card and Premium ACH into MassPay, so clients can pay suppliers and vendors through the same integration they already use for everyone else.
Money in, money out, domestic, cross-border. One platform, one contract.
‍
 Stablecoins keep scaling 
The Circle and Coinbase rails we put into production in Q2 keep growing alongside everything else. And for clients, nothing about how they work has changed: every payout goes to the rail that’s fastest, most cost-effective, and most reliable for that payee. Stablecoins aren’t a separate product. They’re one more rail in the mix.
‍
 One quarter left in the growth year 
We said 2026 would be all about growth. Three quarters in, we’ve posted three straight records and expanded from payouts into full money movement.
Behind every number is a person who got paid on time, in the way they chose. That’s the promise we’re scaling.
Want to see what one platform for all your money movement looks like? Talk to our team or explore the platform at masspay.io.
