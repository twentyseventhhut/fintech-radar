 Amsterdam, October 7, 2026 - Adyen N.V. (AMS: ADYEN) announces that its shareholders approved the appointment of Chris Vogelzang as a member of the Supervisory Board at the Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM), held on October 7, 2026.
Effective November 1, 2026, Piero Overmars will formally retire from the Supervisory Board. Herna Verhagen will succeed Piero Overmars as Chair of the Supervisory Board on the same date. 
The EGM also discussed a governance update to Adyen’s executive leadership model, which will take effect January 31, 2027 and will be updated on its website accordingly. 
Read more about Adyen’s governance structure on its website , including biographies and current terms.
 About Adyen 
Adyen (ADYEN:AMS) is the financial technology platform of choice for leading companies. By providing end-to-end payments capabilities, data-driven insights, and financial products in a single global solution, Adyen helps businesses achieve their ambitions faster. With offices around the world, Adyen works with the likes of Meta, Uber, H&M, eBay, and Microsoft.
