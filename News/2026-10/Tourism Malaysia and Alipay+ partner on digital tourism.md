# Tourism Malaysia and Alipay+ partner on digital tourism

_Collaboration will connect seamless payments, in-app digital engagement and AI-powered travel services to enhance the visitor journey, empower businesses of all sizes and unlock wider cross-border commerce opportunities_

**KUALA LUMPUR** – Tourism Malaysia and Alipay+, the unified wallet gateway of Ant International, today signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to accelerate cross-border commerce and digitalise merchant engagement across Malaysia's tourism economy.

As Malaysia welcomes visitors for Visit Malaysia 2026–2027 (VM2026–2027), this partnership merges Tourism Malaysia’s destination marketing with Alipay+’s global wallet ecosystem and AI capabilities. It aims to create a highly intelligent digital journey, connecting international travellers directly with local operators as well as Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs), strategically distributing visitor spending beyond major hubs to support the broader economic goals of VM2026–2027.

**Mr. Mohd Amirul Rizal Abdul Rahim, Director General of Tourism Malaysia**, said: “Digital technologies continue to reshape the way people plan and experience travel, and Tourism Malaysia remains committed to embracing innovative solutions that enhance visitor convenience and engagement. This initiative makes it easier for travellers to discover Malaysia and access tourism-related benefits through a seamless digital journey, supporting our broader efforts under VM2026–2027 to strengthen Malaysia's competitiveness as a smart tourism destination. By leveraging digital platforms with extensive international reach, we can engage travellers more effectively while showcasing Malaysia's diverse tourism experiences to a wider global audience.”

The MoU builds on Alipay+'s significant momentum within Malaysia's digital commerce ecosystem. In the first half of 2026, over 600,000 merchants, primarily SMEs, received payments from Alipay+ partner wallets and bank apps, marking a year-on-year increase of over 70%. Furthermore, traveller spending via DuitNow surged by more than 60%, establishing Alipay+ as the largest contributor to inbound QR transactions nationwide. Currently, Alipay+ connects over 2 billion user accounts with 150 million merchants across 210 destinations, and operating through major national schemes like PayNet.

**Mr. Michael Guo, Alipay+ General Manager for Southeast Asia, South Asia and ANZ, Ant International**, added: “Cross-border commerce is evolving beyond the moment of payment. AI can now connect a traveller's intent with the right destination, business and offer. Through our collaboration with Tourism Malaysia, we are helping local businesses not only accept international payments, but also to actively engage global customers, creating more opportunities for Malaysian businesses, particularly SMEs, to participate in the growth of cross-border travel and commerce.”

A core component of the collaboration is Alipay+ Voyager, an AI travel agent embedded within travellers' digital wallets. Alipay+ Voyager will surface Tourism Malaysia-verified cultural, culinary, and experiential offerings tailored to individual interests, creating a new digital channel to reach international customers at the moment of discovery. To further incentivise exploration, Tourism Malaysia will also launch an exclusive AI-powered travel subsidy programme, allowing visitors to converse directly with the digital agent to unlock benefits for accommodation, transportation, and attractions. Alipay+ Voyager also simplifies the day-to-day experience – for example, visitors can access local ride-hailing services directly via their embedded AI agent, without having to download additional apps, and also get triple protection covering timeout,no-shows,and long waits.

**Tangible Benefits for Malaysian Businesses**

As part of the MoU, Malaysian merchants, big and small, all across the country will benefit from the growing number of visitors for VM2026-2027:

·**Direct access to global travellers**— Tourism Malaysia and Malaysian businesses become visible directly within leading global e-wallets in the Alipay+ ecosystem

·**From payments to active engagement**— AI agent Alipay+ Voyager surfaces verified offerings, connecting travellers with businesses beyond major hubs; and with personalised deals across the travel journey driving higher intent engagements

·**SME growth engine**— Exclusive campaign with 20% savings via PayNet’s DuitNow encourages discovery and transactions at small local shops

Through these initiatives, Tourism Malaysia reinforces its commitment to cultivating a connected, inclusive digital commerce ecosystem that benefits both the national economy and the everyday traveller, beyond the Visit Malaysia 2026–2027 campaign.

**About Tourism Malaysia**

Malaysia Tourism Promotion Board, also known as Tourism Malaysia, is an agency under the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture Malaysia (MOTAC). It focuses on promoting Malaysia as a preferred tourism destination and has, since its establishment, played a significant role in strengthening the country’s presence and competitiveness in the global tourism landscape.

Visit Malaysia 2026 (VM2026) underscores Malaysia’s commitment to sustainable tourism development, in line with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UNSDGs). The campaign aims to showcase the nation’s diverse natural attractions, rich cultural heritage, and vibrant tourism offerings, while driving inclusive growth and long-term value for local communities and industry stakeholders.

For more information, visit Tourism Malaysia’s website www.tourism.gov.my and social media accounts onFacebook, Instagram,X,YouTube, andTikTok.

**About Alipay+**

Ant International's Alipay+ is a unified wallet gateway with cross-border payment and digitisation services that help connect global merchants to consumers. Consumers enjoy seamless payment, a broad choice of deals and the convenience of digital services using their preferred payment app while travelling abroad. Small and medium-sized businesses may use Alipay+ digital tools to enhance efficiency and achieve omni-channel growth.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

**Red-team questions**

1. It's an MoU — is anything binding? No disclosed fees, targets, duration, or go-live date beyond the VM2026–2027 window. How much of this is signalling vs. committed deployment?
2. Payment rails already existed: Alipay/Weixin DuitNow QR acceptance (2.5M merchants) and a Tourism Malaysia↔Weixin Pay deal (Jan 2026) predate this. What is genuinely new beyond the Voyager AI layer and the marketing framing?
3. Alipay+ Voyager launched Jul 2025 (Alipay, AlipayHK, GCash). Is it actually live/available to travellers heading to Malaysia yet, or still a roadmap item inside most partner wallets?
4. The "600k merchants, +70% YoY; DuitNow traveller spend +60%" figures are Alipay+'s own, unaudited. What's the base, and do they double-count the PayNet/Weixin promos?
5. Who funds the "AI-powered travel subsidy" and the 20% DuitNow savings — Tourism Malaysia (taxpayer), PayNet, or Alipay+? Sustainability after the campaign?
6. Does embedding a foreign (Ant/China) AI agent as the discovery layer create platform-dependence and data-sovereignty risk for Malaysia's tourism demand funnel?
7. Competitive response: Weixin Pay already has CIMB (Dec 2025) and DuitNow support (Apr 2026). Does an Alipay+ "exclusive" MoU actually lock out Tencent, or is Tourism Malaysia multi-homing?
8. Which local wallets (TNG eWallet, GrabPay, Boost) participate? If Alipay+ only routes Chinese/overseas wallets, domestic reach is limited.
9. "Distributing spend beyond major hubs to SMEs" — any mechanism/metric to verify dispersion, or just aspiration layered on QR volume?
10. Does Voyager's merchant-surfacing create pay-to-play / ranking bias against SMEs that can't fund deals — contradicting the SME-inclusion narrative?
11. Reliance on the China corridor (4.7M arrivals 2025, visa-exemption-driven) — geopolitical/visa-policy reversal or China outbound softness would undercut the whole thesis. How exposed?
12. Ride-hailing in-wallet and "triple protection" (timeout/no-show/long-wait) — who is the service provider, and is this live in Malaysia or a generic Voyager feature?
13. Any conflict with Malaysia's own DuitNow-native / PayNet sovereignty push to reduce foreign-wallet dependence?
14. Revenue model: how does Ant monetise Voyager (MDR, lead-gen, ad/placement fees)? Unclear who pays for the "marketing to 2bn wallet users."

**Importance: 3/5** — A credible, strategically interesting move: Ant converting payment acceptance into an AI-driven travel-marketing funnel at the moment of discovery, tied to a flagship national campaign (VM2026–2027) and the hot China-outbound corridor. But it is a non-binding MoU with no disclosed economics, the underlying QR rails and a prior Weixin Pay tie-up already exist, and the headline vendor metrics are self-reported — so it is an incremental, directional story rather than a market-moving one.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Cross-border mobile-wallet acceptance in SE Asia is a scale-driven, network-economics business sitting on top of national instant-payment rails (DuitNow/PayNet, SGQR, PromptPay, QRIS). Demand driver "why now": Visit Malaysia 2026–2027 — Malaysia targets ~35.6m arrivals / RM147.1bn receipts (some campaign materials cite a stretch 47m / RM329bn) (per Skift/MIDF, as of Jan–Nov 2025). Inbound QR is already live and growing fast: traveller spend via DuitNow +60% and Alipay+ partner-wallet merchants >600k (+70% YoY) in H1-2026 (note text / Alipay+ PR); Alipay+ reportedly accounts for >80% of Malaysia's cross-border inbound DuitNow QR volume (per Malay Mail, Mar-2025). Structure: concentrated at the gateway layer (a few wallet networks aggregate hundreds of regional e-wallets), fragmented at the merchant/acquirer layer; barriers are network effects (2-sided wallet↔merchant) + regulatory access to national schemes, not capital.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: inbound QR TPV, number of accepting merchants, cross-border MDR/take-rate, YoY transaction growth. Alipay+ scale (dated): >2bn user accounts, ~150m merchants, 300+ payment methods across 220+ markets, >20m txns/day avg (Ant International MoMents 2026, Apr-2026 — [[Fintech Wrap Up Ant International connects 150M merchants for AI commerce]]). Basis of competition = distribution/network breadth + national-scheme interop, not price. Key players: WeChat/Weixin Pay (China-tourist-centric, also PromptPay/DuitNow-linked — [[Weixin Pay and Alipay link to Thailand's PromptPay]]), UnionPay, plus domestic rails (TNG eWallet — >RM1bn inbound spend in first 6 months of VM2026). Recent Alipay+ moves: PayNet global DuitNow expansion (Jun/Aug-2025 — [[PayNet and Ant International expand DuitNow QR globally]]), ShopeePay interop (Mar-2026 — [[ShopeePay integrates Alipay+ for cross-border QR payments]]), STB renewal with +36% YoY 2025 txns ([[Singapore Tourism Board and Ant International renew Alipay+ partnership]]). Alipay+ position: clear inbound leader in Malaysia (ahead); moat = cross-side network effects + embedded scheme access `(analysis)`.

**Risk flags.**
1. **MoU, not execution.** It's a memorandum — subsidy programme, Alipay+ Voyager AI agent and the "20% DuitNow savings" campaign are announced, not proven live/adopted; no committed TPV, merchant-count or spend targets disclosed. Why: tourism-board PR optics can run ahead of measurable merchant onboarding.
2. **Take-rate / economics silence.** Neither Tourism Malaysia nor Ant discloses MDR, who funds the 20% subsidy, or fraud-liability split. Why: cross-border QR margin is thin and sits on PayNet rails — value could be captured by the scheme/acquirer layer, not Alipay+.
3. **Geopolitical / data + concentration.** Heavy reliance on a China-headquartered gateway (Ant) for national inbound-tourism infrastructure raises data-sovereignty/regulatory exposure; >80% inbound-QR share is a single-vendor concentration that regulators may want diluted (cf. competition from Weixin Pay/local wallets). Why: policy or interop mandates could erode the dominant position.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Payments is shifting from "accept the tourist's wallet" to owning pre-trip discovery/intent via embedded AI agents (Alipay+ Voyager) — moving the battle upstream from checkout to itinerary `(analysis)`. Falsifiable thesis: if Voyager + subsidy materially lift per-visitor DuitNow spend during VM2026–2027, expect peer NTOs (Thailand/Indonesia) to sign similar Ant deals → further Alipay+ inbound consolidation. Breaks if: campaign metrics go undisclosed, subsidy lapses post-campaign, or regulators push multi-wallet mandates. Trigger to watch: first disclosed VM2026 inbound-QR TPV/merchant figures.
