# This Week in Fin: 56% of firms lose revenue to AI fraud on biometrics

> [!info] 2026-10-19 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: This Week in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[This Week in Fintech] According to an April report by biometric tech company Aware, 56% of organizations reported revenue loss when it came to AI fraud impacting biometric security. Deloitte’s Center for Financial Services predicted fraud losses in banking could rise to as much as $40 billion in the U.S. by 2027, from US$12.3 billion in 2023, as generative AI becomes more advanced and pervasive.
