## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** MTS Link (МТС Линк), MTS's online-collaboration platform (meetings, webinars, chats; formerly Webinar.ru / Webinar Group), added real-time **Chinese speech recognition** with **live subtitle translation into 19 languages** (incl. Russian, English, Japanese, Korean). The feature was unveiled at the "Цифровые решения" (Digital Solutions) forum on **2026-10-09**; the note is dated 2026-10-12 from a single Sber digest mention. Subtitles can be combined with the platform's synchronous-translation feature — a listener can independently pick an audio channel and a subtitle language. The system accounts for Chinese tonal semantics (word meaning shifting by tone).

**[1] Why it matters.** Positions MTS Link as an import-substitution videoconferencing stack for RU businesses with growing China trade ties (regional MTS press notes explicitly pitch it to Kurgan/Chelyabinsk/south-Urals firms translating meetings with Chinese counterparts). Multilingual real-time captioning is a differentiator vs domestic rivals and a localized answer to Zoom/Teams AI-captioning. Narrow fintech relevance — it is an enterprise-productivity/AI product from a telco, not a payments/banking story; it lands in the pipeline via MTS ecosystem coverage.

**[2] Scale / numbers.** MTS Link group revenue grew **+39% YoY in 2025 to RUB 2.53bn** (2024: RUB 1.815bn, +65% vs 2023). Meetings MAU +75.7% in 2025; Chats MAU and monthly messages ×2.9; corporate-messenger sales ×3; comms-equipment sales +59% YoY. Q1 2025 Meetings MAU +58%, online meetings +54% YoY. 19 target subtitle languages. (Feature-level usage numbers not disclosed.)

**[3] Context / mechanism.** This sits inside a broader MTS/MWS AI speech push: MWS AI opened **RESON**, a speech-recognition model-evaluation tool (10+ metrics) [[MWS AI opens RESON speech-recognition model evaluation tool]]; MTS Secretary learned to answer calls in the subscriber's own voice [[MTS Secretary learns to answer calls in subscriber's voice]]; and MTS bundled enterprise comms/cloud/AI/IIoT under the "МТС КорпТех" brand [[MTS bundles enterprise solutions under MTS CorpTech brand]]. Competitive set: Yandex Telemost, Контур.Толк (SKB Kontur), SberJazz/SaluteJazz, VK Звонки, TrueConf.

**[4] Open questions / risks.** No independent accuracy/latency benchmarks for Chinese ASR or the 19-language translation; claims are vendor/PR-sourced (single Sber-digest mention, no full first-party text in note). Availability/tier (all plans vs enterprise), on-prem vs cloud, and whether MWS AI's own models power it are unstated. Rivals offer comparable AI captioning, so the moat is unproven.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** MTS Link (ex-Webinar Group) is a Russian unified-communications / collaboration-software platform (video meetings, webinars, corporate messenger "Чаты", boards, LMS); this news adds Chinese-speech recognition + real-time subtitle translation into 19 languages, shown at the "Цифровые решения" forum on 2026-10-09 (per cnews.ru, abireg.ru). Sector = the Russian videoconferencing (ВКС) / collaboration market, which was structurally remade post-2022: domestic services now control **>90% of the large-enterprise segment** (per TelecomDaily, as of 2025-06), i.e. an import-substitution-driven market after Zoom/Teams/Webex exits. Size/growth: the ВКС **software** market reached **~RUB 24.9bn in 2024 (+88% over four years)** (per CNews/TAdviser, as of 2025); a narrower "server-side ВКС software" cut was **~RUB 13.6bn in 2025, projected ~RUB 24.6bn by 2028** (per TAdviser, as of 2025 — treat the two figures as different scope definitions, not a contradiction). Top-20 players' combined revenue grew **+40% YoY** (per CNews, 2025). "Why now": the 2022 foreign exit created the demand vacuum; the 2026 layer is AI (real-time ASR/translation) and the specific China-trade pivot — the feature is explicitly pitched at Russian firms negotiating with Chinese counterparts without a language barrier (per ampravda, cnews).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: MAU/DAU, paying-seat / subscription revenue, on-prem vs cloud deployment mix, ВКС-terminal (hardware) sales. Basis of competition is **product + data-residency/on-prem compliance + distribution**, not price — note **66% of companies deliberately choose on-prem deployment and only 12% treat cloud as the ВКС base** (per anti-malware/CNews, 2025), which favours vendors shipping domestic hardware terminals. Key players by 2025 share: **IVA MCU (IVA Technologies) 33%, TrueConf 21%, VK Звонки 16%** (per CNews, 2025) — MTS Link is **not** in that top-3 on the pure-ВКС share table. On the consumer/usage side, **Yandex Telemost** ended 2025 as the most-used video-call service (~1mn DAU, MAU ~8mn, +3.5x YoY per rb.ru/MegaFon analytics); **СберJazz** and **Контур.Толк** (up to 400 participants, traffic +56% YoY) round out the leaders. Protagonist's position: **challenger / niche-leader in the broader "collaboration platform" framing** (meetings + webinars + messenger + LMS + own hardware) rather than #1 in bare ВКS — MTS Link's moat is **breadth + MTS group distribution + first-mover on China-language AI**, a genuine differentiator none of the named peers were reported shipping `(analysis)`.

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation/round/deal attaches to this news (a feature launch) → trading multiples = **no data**. **IR (PRIMARY — MTS group):** MTS files quarterly; ir_latest.json lists Q1 2026 press release (2026-05-20, storage.ir.mts.ru/.../MTS_1Q_2026_Press_release_RUS.pdf) as latest; MTS's actual latest print is **Q2 2026**: group revenue **RUB 213.5bn (+9.2% YoY)**, OIBDA **RUB 84.6bn (+16.3%)**, B2B/retail the fastest-growing reported line **+29.5% YoY** — the segment that houses MTS Link (storage.ir.mts.ru/.../МТС 2 кв. 2026 Пресс-релиз.pdf). **MTS Link stand-alone (web-sourced, MTS Link blog/TAdviser):** FY2025 group revenue **RUB 2.53bn (+39% YoY)**; "Встречи" MAU **+75.7%**; "Чаты" MAU & messages **×2.9**; messenger sales **×3**; communications-equipment sales **+59%** (launched domestic ВКС-terminal VINTEO T2); quarterly top line was volatile (+103% Q1, +84% Q3 2025 YoY). Arithmetic/sanity: at ~RUB 2.5bn FY2025 revenue, MTS Link is **~10% of the ~RUB 24.9bn ВКS-software market** — a top-tier but not dominant slice, consistent with its absence from the IVA/TrueConf/VK top-3 share table (different scope: platform vs pure ВКС). **Internal comps** (`[[wikilink]]`): [[MTS bundles enterprise solutions under MTS CorpTech brand]] (same group B2B push; MTS Link named an IPO candidate alongside MTS Exolve/MWS), [[MWS AI opens RESON speech-recognition model evaluation tool]] (the group's ASR/speech stack that this feature leans on), [[MTS Secretary learns to answer calls in subscriber's voice]] (sibling MTS voice-AI product), [[Russian AI startup Mymeet.ai raises 16.5m roubles]] (Russian meeting-AI peer, ~$1.3M ARR / ~$4M valuation 2025 per getLatka, SberJazz-integrated). Distribution not computed (comps are a listed-group parent + private micro-peers, not comparable public pure-plays) → qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Feature, not a moat — commoditizing fast.** Real-time ASR + multilingual subtitles is being shipped sector-wide (and natively by global suites); subtitles arrived in MTS Link only in 2025 and real-time translation in Q1 2026, so the China add-on is an incremental catch-up/extension, not a defensible platform edge — watch whether it converts to paid seats, not just press.
2. **China-feature TAM is narrow and policy-sensitive.** The value proposition is explicitly tied to Russia-China trade flows; demand is a bet on that corridor staying strong — a second-order dependence on geopolitics/trade policy, concentrated in a sub-set of exporters, not a broad MAU driver.
3. **Not top-3 in core ВКС share + on-prem preference.** With 66% of buyers choosing on-prem and share concentrated in IVA/TrueConf/VK, MTS Link's cloud-platform breadth may under-index the largest, most compliance-driven enterprise deals; its IPO-candidate status (gated on key rate <10%) means the revenue-growth story (+39% FY2025, but decelerating from +103%→+84% intra-2025) must keep compounding to justify a standalone multiple.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` The move signals Russian collaboration vendors racing to layer AI (ASR/translation) on top of the import-substitution land-grab, and pivoting feature roadmaps toward the China trade axis specifically. Falsifiable thesis: if AI features are a real differentiator, MTS Link's paying-seat / "Встречи" MAU growth should stay well above the ~+40% group ВКС-market pace and the company should enter ВКС share tables; what breaks it — decelerating MTS Link revenue (already +103%→+84% within 2025) or no share-table entry, which would mark these launches as marketing. Trigger to watch: MTS Link FY2026 revenue/MAU disclosure and the gated МТС Линк IPO (key rate <10%).
