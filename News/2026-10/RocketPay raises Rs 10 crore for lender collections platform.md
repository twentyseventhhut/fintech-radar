Key Points
RocketPay raised Rs 10 crore in a funding round led by Sandeep Jain and Group.
The funding will accelerate RocketPay's expansion into recovery infrastructure for lenders.
This expansion includes technology-enabled recovery of financed devices, aligning with new RBI guidelines.
RocketPay aims to achieve an annualised revenue run rate of Rs 100 crore by FY28.
Investors believe RocketPay is well-positioned to become a category-defining platform in the device-financing market.
