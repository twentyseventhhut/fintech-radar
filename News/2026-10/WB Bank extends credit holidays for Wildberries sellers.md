[Финтехно] WB Банк и WB Финанс ещё на месяц продлили кредитные каникулы продавцам Wildberries, пострадавшим от атак на логистические центры маркетплейса в июле. В России могут легализоватьонлайн-казино по аналогии с регулированием букмекерской деятельности. Об этом в кулуарах Веронского Евразийского экономического форума рассказал заместитель министра финансов Иван Чебесков.

## Доводы за

"Во многих странах действует также практика легализации [онлайн-казино], то есть регулирования этой деятельности. Так же, как мы регулируем букмекеров, мы сейчас все опции эти изучаем", — сказал он.

Он указал, каким может быть потенциал бюджетных поступлений.

"Мы оцениваем, что легальный рынок букмекеров сейчас приносит, ну, скажем так, и бюджету, и спорту, — в том числе целевые отчисления— порядка 100 млрд рублей в год. То есть с учетом полученных налоговых ставок, которые заработали в этом году. Мы считаем, что нелегальный рынок сопоставим с легальным. Понятно, что потенциал бюджетных поступлений от нелегального рынка тоже может быть сопоставим с легальным рынком. Это сумма порядка 100 млрд рублей. Это огромные деньги, которые сейчас, естественно, бюджет недополучает, которые идут в том числе на различные криминальные цели", — обратил внимание замминистра.

## Доводы против

В то же время заместитель секретаря Общественной палаты (ОП) РФ Владислав Гриб высказался против.

"Я против легализации онлайн-казино. Да, доходы бюджета — это важно, но важно и понимать, сколько людей будут вовлечены в онлайн-казино ввиду его доступности в отличие от офлайн-казино в России и соответствующей рекламы. Для людей с низкой финансовой грамотностью эти казино являются легким видом заработка", — сказал Гриб.

Он отметил, что в свое время государство не просто так закрыло казино почти по всей стране и создало несколько игорных зон. "Сейчас это теряет смысл! Доступность казино в нескольких нажатиях кнопок на компьютере — не есть хорошо", — подчеркнул замсекретаря ОП.

## Борьба с нелегальными казино

Чебесков подчеркнул, что Минфин получает и изучает различные предложения от легальных организаторов азартных игр по борьбе с теневым онлайн-сектором. Он выделил два ключевых метода противодействия.

"Первое — это, конечно, различные способы блокировки платежей: сейчас нелегальные казино используют довольно современные платежные механизмы, скажем так, с которыми мы тоже боремся. Второе — это, конечно, блокировка сайтов. Обе эти задачи довольно сложные", — подчеркнул Чебесков.

Рассказал он и о рекламе. По его словам, в Минфине считают важным продолжать работу по борьбе с рекламой нелегального казино в России. "Важно еще гражданам понимать, что когда они видят рекламу нелегальных казино, то эта деятельность для граждан несет риски <…> Здесь нужно продолжать эту работу", — указал он.

Замминистра отметил, что вопрос легализации онлайн-казино связан и с социальными рисками. "Это вопрос сложный, потому что мы говорим, что в азартных играх, конечно, есть положительный экономический эффект от этого вида деятельности. Но есть и социальные риски, которые с этим связаны, и мы ни в коем случае не упускаем этого <…> И тут вопрос, [как] правильно управлять этими рисками, чтобы граждане, подверженные рискам лудомании, чтобы они имели возможность самозапрета — не играли в азартные игры, — сказал он. — Поэтому это не только вопрос про деньги <…> мы стараемся посмотреть на ситуацию комплексно".

При этом, как отметил замминистра, самозапреты на ставки не являются методом борьбы с теневым сектором казино, это скорее профилактика и лечение лудомании, которые работают только в легальном поле.

По его словам, на форумах обсуждались различные подходы к работе с нелегальными онлайн-казино. Экспертам теперь предстоит оценить их.

# Context-enrichment: WB Bank extends credit holidays for Wildberries sellers
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

> NOTE ON SOURCE MISMATCH: the dumped primary-source text in this note (TASS 28245185) is a DIFFERENT story — about legalising online casinos (Chebeskov/Minfin). The real event is only in the aggregated Финтехно digest line. Enrichment below is grounded in that digest line + external press (RIA 2026-10-23, BFM, Lenta, Fontanka, RB.ru, CNews, Expert, Retail.ru). The TASS cache dump should be treated as a mis-attached source, not evidence for this item.

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
De-PR'd: WB Банк (the VTB/RWB-controlled Wildberries bank) and the sister MFO МКК «WB Финанс» extended by one month a pre-existing credit-holiday program for Wildberries sellers whose goods/operations were hit by the July 2026 fires at WB logistics centres. Total deferral window goes from 3 → 4 months. During it, BOTH principal repayment AND interest accrual are suspended; the next payment is now due 23 Nov 2026 (was end-October). The extension is applied automatically — sellers file no applications and submit no documents.
- The "4 months, interest also frozen" framing is the one genuinely load-bearing fact: a standard СМП/retail restructuring or the statutory СМСП credit holiday typically PAUSES principal but KEEPS accruing interest (which capitalises). Freezing interest too makes this a cost-absorbing subsidy by WB's own captive lenders, not a mere schedule shuffle. (This is the differentiator the digest correctly isolates.)
- Why structured this way: the lenders (WB Банк + WB Финанс) are IN-HOUSE to the marketplace. The "borrowers" are the same sellers whose inventory the marketplace lost in the attacks. So the holiday is really Wildberries/RWB cushioning its own ecosystem — protecting GMV and seller retention — using its captive credit arms. It is a retention/goodwill cost booked as forgone interest, not a regulatory concession.
- Scale is vague by design: press says "thousands of sellers" affected; external reporting puts estimated fire damage to Wildberries at ₽20–35bn and "tens of thousands" of SMBs using the platform were hit by the drone strikes. No disclosure of the loan book size under holiday, number of accounts, or forgone-interest cost. That silence is the PR gap.

## [1] Competitors / peers
Marketplace-bank peers in RU: Ozon Bank, Yandgo/Yandex (Yandex Bank), Avito. The structural play is identical across them — convert marketplace traffic + seller cash flows into embedded credit/deposits (see [[WB Bank launches savings accounts and consumer loans]], [[Axenix study marketplace banks hit trust ceiling despite traffic]]). None of the peers faced a comparable physical-asset shock, so there is no direct comp for "captive bank absorbs interest after its own warehouses burn". Closest analogue is a classic bank forbearance/payment-holiday after a disaster, but here the lender and the harmed party share a parent — a tighter alignment than any third-party bank-merchant relation.
- Why this way: embedded-lending economics depend on a captive, non-defaulting borrower base. A wave of seller defaults after the fires would (a) spike WB Банк's unsecured-SMB NPLs and (b) accelerate seller churn off the platform. Freezing interest is cheaper than either. Second order: the move quietly validates the "marketplace bank = subsidised ecosystem utility" critique (Axenix: 85% see these banks as an embedded platform service, not a standalone bank) — the holiday reinforces exactly that embedded identity rather than building standalone trust.

## [2] Company history / fit
WB Банк is the rebranded bank inside RWB (the Russ–Wildberries merger), with VTB as partner; Georgy Gorshkov was the VTB-side architect, Svyatoslav Emelyanov the risk-oriented operating head (see [[WB Bank names Svyatoslav Emelyanov acting board chairman]]). Through 2026 it has been scaling consumer loans (up to ₽500k) and savings accounts (June) while Expert RA flagged asset-quality as a forward risk as corporate loans season and unsecured retail scales. In Oct a 5% stake deal in the bank was set to close (see [[Buyer to close 5% stake deal in Wildberries bank by October]]). A one-month holiday extension fits logically: a young, fast-scaling captive lender with flagged asset-quality risk choosing forbearance over a default wave is textbook — it defers, not resolves, the credit stress.
- Why: the bank's whole thesis is monetising marketplace data/flows. Its "assets" are only as healthy as its sellers. After an exogenous shock to those sellers, protecting the borrower base is self-protection of the loan book — consistent with Emelyanov's risk-curator background.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
Novelty is LOW. This is an incremental extension (+1 month) of a program launched ~24–25 July 2026 — same beneficiaries, same mechanism, just a longer window and a pushed-out next-payment date. The only "new" datum is the 3→4-month total and the 23 Nov due date. Real value-add to sellers is genuine (free, interest-frozen, auto-applied deferral), but it is a continuation, not a new capability.
- Why value-add is real but limited: it is real cash relief for hit sellers (no interest drag), and the auto-enrolment removes friction. BUT it captures no margin, builds no new product, and discloses no traction metrics. Who absorbs the cost? WB Банк/WB Финанс forgo interest — i.e. the captive ecosystem eats it. There is no structural shift in where value is created; it is a defensive subsidy whose size is undisclosed.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Watch: (a) whether a 5th-month extension follows (signals seller cash flows still not recovered → deeper credit stress), (b) any disclosure of holiday-book size or forgone-interest cost, (c) warehouse-capacity recovery, since the underlying driver is physical (the July drone/fire attacks on WB logistics centres across Podolsk, Tambov, Elektrostal, Volgograd, St Petersburg/Leningrad). Regulatory backdrop: statutory SME credit holidays exist, but this is a voluntary, more generous private program. Risk: repeated extensions turn forbearance into hidden evergreening — deferred, not cured, defaults — exactly the asset-quality zone Expert RA flagged.
- Counterintuitive second order: the more generous and open-ended the holiday, the more it signals that seller losses are severe and recovery slow — i.e. "support" headlines are a lagging read-through of balance-sheet stress at the captive bank, not a sign of strength.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Marketplace-embedded lending to merchants (seller-financing): the platform owns the GMV flow and uses it as both collateral and underwriting signal, then lends via a captive bank/MFO. In Russia this is now a core "second P&L" as commission growth moderates. Market structure is a near-duopoly: Wildberries and Ozon are projected to hold ~77% of Russian e-commerce by end-2026 (Wildberries ~45%, Ozon ~32%; per bne IntelliNews, as of Oct 2026), so captive seller-lending concentrates inside two ecosystems. WB's parent RWB (Wildberries & Russ, 65% Kim / 35% Stinn) reported FY2025 GMV of RUB 6.1tn, +49% y/y (per oborot.ru/SelSup, FY2025). "Why now": (1) marketplaces are racing to monetize the merchant base before growth slows (H1-2026 non-food turnover RUB 10.8tn, growth decelerating per IntelliNews); (2) the July 2026 drone attacks on logistics centers (Nevinnomyssk warehouse fire, night of 21–22 Jul) turned seller-financing from a growth lever into a crisis-management / forbearance obligation. Barriers: banking license + the proprietary GMV/cashflow data moat that a standalone lender can't replicate.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: loan portfolio growth, seller loan penetration (% of sellers financed), NPL/overdue on the merchant book, and GMV take-rate uplift from fintech attach. Key players and basis of competition — distribution, not price:
- **WB Bank + MCC WB Finance** (Wildberries/RWB captive): consumer loans up to RUB 500k launched Jun 2026 [[WB Bank launches savings accounts and consumer loans]]; VTB buying 5% of the bank with option to raise, giving access to VTB branches/ATMs/mortgages [[Buyer to close 5% stake deal in Wildberries bank by October]].
- **Ozon Bank** (Ozon captive): fintech customer base 34.4m (+43% y/y), total loans issued RUB 100.2bn (+73% y/y) in Q1-2026; Ozon.Invest seller lending at 16–17.5% via MTS Bank, unsecured up to RUB 3m / 24 months (per corp.ozon.com + intellinews, Q1-2026). Ozon runs the more mature, disclosed fintech book.
WB's position: catching up to Ozon on disclosure/scale but with an aggressive recent ramp — WB Bank's IP (sole-proprietor) loan book jumped >5x in June 2026, from RUB 2.4bn to RUB 12bn, topping T-Bank (+RUB 4.2bn) and Alfa (+RUB 1.4bn) that month, partly by buying portfolio from WB Finance (per RBC via Forbes, Aug 2026). Moat = the GMV-data underwriting + captive distribution (seller onboarding funnel); not a pricing or balance-sheet moat.

**Comps & multiples.** No public EV/Revenue or P/E computable — WB Bank/RWB is private and does not disclose standalone financials; Ozon's fintech segment is not separately listed. Multiple = **no data**; qualitative comparison only.
- Internal comps: [[WB Bank launches savings accounts and consumer loans]] (product build-out), [[Buyer to close 5% stake deal in Wildberries bank by October]] (VTB 5% stake, implies an external valuation anchor but price undisclosed → `[UNSOURCED]`), [[Ozon Bank adds free cash delivery to Ultra premium tariff]], [[Ozon Bank tops banks on customer experience]], [[Ozon to launch brokerage arm Ozon Investments in autumn 2026]] (peer fintech trajectory).
- Portfolio arithmetic (sourced): WB Bank IP book RUB 12bn in Jun-2026 vs RUB 100.2bn total loans issued at Ozon Bank in Q1-2026 → WB's merchant book is ~1 order of magnitude smaller and far more recently inflated. Growth-multiple read: a 5x single-month jump is not "healthy hyper-growth" to be rewarded — it precedes the shock, so it reads as risk, not re-rating.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Concentration / wrong-way risk.** The loan book is 100% exposed to one marketplace's sellers, whose revenue depends on that same marketplace's uptime. The July warehouse attacks hit both the borrowers' sales AND the lender's collateral simultaneously — classic wrong-way exposure the standard SME credit-holiday regime (which keeps accruing interest) wasn't designed for, hence the non-standard "freeze principal AND interest" terms.
2. **Pro-cyclical build-up right before the shock.** The >5x June ramp (RUB 2.4bn→12bn), partly via buying WB Finance's portfolio onto the bank balance sheet, means the freshest, least-seasoned vintage is the one now in forbearance — overdue/NPL recognition is merely deferred to the 23-Nov payment date, not resolved.
3. **Captive-lender conflict / forbearance cost.** WB/RWB absorbs the carry of a 4-month interest-free holiday itself; repeated one-month extensions (3→4 months) signal seller distress isn't normalizing and raise the odds of further extension or loss recognition.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Marketplace-embedded lending's core selling point — "we know the seller's cashflow, so credit is safe" — is only as strong as the platform's physical resilience; a logistics shock converts the data moat into correlated, un-diversifiable loss (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: WB Bank's merchant-loan NPLs step up materially once the 23-Nov resumption lands; trigger to watch = whether a third extension is announced in November, and whether CBR flags the captive concentration. What would break the thesis: sellers' GMV recovers and the holidays end cleanly on 23-Nov with no further extension.
