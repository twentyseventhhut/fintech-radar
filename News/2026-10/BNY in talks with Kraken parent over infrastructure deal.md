# BNY in talks with Kraken parent over infrastructure deal

> [!info] 2026-10-06 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇺🇸 BNY is in talks with Kraken parent Payward over an infrastructure partnership, according to two people familiar with the matter. The potential agreement could span crypto products, custody, wealth management, trading and payments through Payward Services, its platform for banks, exchanges and asset managers. Discussions are ongoing, and there is no guarantee an agreement will be reached.
