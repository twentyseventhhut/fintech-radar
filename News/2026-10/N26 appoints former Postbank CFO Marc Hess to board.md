# N26 appoints former Postbank CFO Marc Hess to board

> [!info] 2026-10-13 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇩🇪 N26 appoints former Postbank CFO Marc Hess to complete its Management Board. Hess will serve as CFO and managing director of N26 SE and N26 Bank SE, replacing Arnd Schwierholz, subject to regulatory approval. His appointment comes as the digital bank remains under heightened scrutiny from German regulator BaFin.
