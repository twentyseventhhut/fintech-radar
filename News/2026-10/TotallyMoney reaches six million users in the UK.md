TotallyMoney has reached a significant milestone by serving six million people, accounting for 11% of the UK adult population. For fintech professionals, this growth highlights the increasing demand for credit-building tools and transparent financial data, as consumers seek to navigate a tightening lending market and improve their eligibility for lower-cost financial products.

## What was announced

TotallyMoney has reached a significant scale milestone, reporting that six million people have used its platform since the 2017 launch of its free credit report service. This figure represents approximately 11% of the UK adult population. The service is designed to provide consumers with a pathway to improve their credit status, which in turn allows them to qualify for lower-cost credit and a wider variety of financial products.

The platform functions by providing users with live credit scores and reports powered by TransUnion, a major UK credit reference agency. Beyond simple reporting, the app offers guidance on score fluctuations and provides personalized tips for credit improvement. Data from the company indicates that this active engagement leads to tangible results; 45% of new customers who joined with a "low" TransUnion credit score (defined as sub-556) saw an improvement within six months.

The impact is even more pronounced for those starting from the lowest baseline. Among customers with a score below 400 at sign-up, 86% managed to boost their score within the same six-month window. Frequency of use also appears to be a critical factor in financial progress. Users who check their reports on a monthly basis achieved a score increase that was, on average, 133%—or 2.3 times—larger than that of less frequent users.

> "This is a massive TotallyMoney milestone! Six million people in the UK have taken control of their financial future and gained the knowledge they need to understand and improve their credit status with our free app."

Nik Charalampous, TotallyMoney CEO

## The companies involved

TotallyMoney is a UK-based fintech company that operates a credit-building platform and financial app. Since its inception, the company has positioned itself as a tool for financial inclusion, specifically targeting consumer segments that may be underserved by traditional banking models. This includes individuals without an established UK credit history, those recovering from financial setbacks, and workers with non-standard income patterns that do not align with traditional lending criteria.

The company has established a significant presence in the UK market, with its core offering revolving around credit transparency and personalized financial management. By partnering with TransUnion, one of the three main credit reference agencies in the United Kingdom, TotallyMoney provides a bridge between complex credit data and consumer-facing financial products. The platform's growth to six million users places it among the more widely adopted financial health apps in the British market, where FF News has tracked the company’s development across 17 previous reports. Its model focuses on helping users prepare for major credit commitments by unlocking better deals through improved credit status.

## What this means

The scale of TotallyMoney’s user base suggests that credit scores have moved from being a back-office banking metric to a primary consumer concern. For the wider industry, this shift creates a virtuous cycle of data transparency that puts immense pressure on traditional lenders. Banks can no longer rely on consumer ignorance regarding their own eligibility; instead, they must compete in an environment where the customer knows exactly how they are perceived by credit bureaus. This announcement also highlights a shift in the fintech business model from lead generation to long-term financial coaching. The challenge for the sector now lies in whether these score improvements translate into actual long-term financial stability or merely temporary access to more debt.

Original Press Release

TotallyMoney, the credit app, announced today that it has served six million people since it launched its free credit report service in 2017. One-in-ten (11%) UK adults have now used TotallyMoney, which helps people improve their credit status, qualify for lower-cost credit and unlock a broader range of financial products.

TotallyMoney provides people with access to their live credit score and credit report from TransUnion, one of the UK’s leading credit reference agencies, as well as guidance when their credit score changes and personalised tips about how to improve their credit status.

TotallyMoney analysis shows that almost half (45%) of new customers with a “low” (sub-556) TransUnion credit score improved their credit score within six months of signing up to TotallyMoney. The biggest gains were achieved by people with the very lowest scores, with 86% of customers scoring less than 400 on sign-up boosting their score over the same period.

Customers that made a habit of checking their report every month achieved a 2.3x (133%) bigger score increase than less engaged customers, on average.

"This is a massive TotallyMoney milestone! Six million people in the UK have taken control of their financial future and gained the knowledge they need to understand and improve their credit status with our free app." said Nik Charalampous, TotallyMoney CEO.

"There are many reasons people use TotallyMoney to improve their credit status. Some don’t have an established credit record in the UK, some are rebuilding their finances after difficult life events, some have work patterns that don’t suit traditional lenders, and some are preparing to make major credit commitments and don’t want to miss out on the best deals." said Nik Charalampous, TotallyMoney CEO.

"Whatever your circumstances, once you know the score you can take steps to improve it and unlock better financial products, lower-cost credit and bigger opportunities." said Nik Charalampous, TotallyMoney CEO.
