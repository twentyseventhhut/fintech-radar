Ozon Банк запустил доставку наличных для премиальных клиентов
 
 На первом этапе можно заказать от 2 до 30 млн рублей в месяц с доставкой по Москве и Московской области
 
 Ozon Банк запустил бесплатную доставку наличных для премиальных клиентов Ultra. В зависимости от уровня обслуживания можно заказать от 2 до 30 млн рублей в месяц; на первом этапе сервис доступен в Москве и Московской области.

 Оформить доставку можно в приложении банка. Деньги привезут инкассаторы на бронированном автомобиле с охраной в выбранные день и время, средства будут упакованы в сейф-пакеты. Сервис позволяет безопасно получить крупную сумму без посещения банкомата. Кроме того, клиент может заказать инкассацию, чтобы передать наличные на личные счета; получать и передавать деньги может только сам владелец услуги. Число доставок в пределах лимита не ограничено, но при этом активной может быть только одна заявка.

 Также Ozon Банк расширил набор привилегий для премиальных клиентов. На уровнях Gold и Platinum появилась круглосуточная приоритетная поддержка в Ozon Travel, помощь в подборе индивидуальных поездок и организации тура. Бизнес-залы повышенной комфортности стали доступны в аэропортах в Москве, Санкт-Петербурге, Новосибирске, Тюмени и других городах. Для Ultra новым партнером по бизнес-залам стал Phoenix Pass, что, по данным банка, обеспечивает самое широкое покрытие среди премиальных программ в России.

 Ozon Банк запустил премиальное обслуживание в мае 2026 года. К началу сентября число клиентов Ultra превысило 85 тыс.

_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
Ozon Bank added **free in-person cash delivery/collection** (доставка + инкассация наличных) to its **Ultra** premium program: **₽2M–₽30M per month**, tier-dependent, by armored vehicle with armed security, cash in sealed safe-pouches, ordered in-app, **Moscow + Moscow Oblast only (phase one)**. Unlimited deliveries within the monthly limit, but only **one active request** at a time; the account holder alone may send/receive. The bank also padded Gold/Platinum perks (24/7 priority support in Ozon Travel, trip concierge, expanded business lounges across MSK/SPb/Novosibirsk/Tyumen, and **Phoenix Pass** as Ultra's new lounge partner — claimed "widest coverage in RU," a bank claim, unverified).

**Why structured this way / what it reveals:** This is a **logistics perk bolted onto an existing capability** — Ultra already carries a ₽30M/month fee-free cash-withdrawal limit, so "delivery" just changes where you collect it, not how much. The ₽2M floor and Moscow-only scope mean this targets a thin slice of HNW clients, not the mass base. The press release leads with "armored vehicle / up to ₽30M" imagery to make a narrow, catch-up feature sound premium. De-PR'd: a **me-too amenity for a new-entrant premium tier**, operationally live but geographically confined.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **T-Bank (Tinkoff) Premium** is the clearest prior art and offers a **near-identical service**: cash transport (withdraw or deposit), **free for Premium**, **₽2M minimum** (up to ₽600M), armored vehicle + armed guards, sealed envelopes, app-ordered in MSK/SPb and manager-arranged elsewhere. T-Bank's version predates Ozon's and is **broader (nationwide vs Moscow-only)**.
- **Alfa-Bank A-Club / Alfa Only** (HNW, ~14 cities, personal advisor): courier card delivery is standard; a specific in-home cash-delivery service is **not confirmed — open**.
- **Sber СберПремьер / СберПервый**: personal manager, premium rates, free transfers confirmed; home cash delivery **not confirmed — open**.

**Position:** **catching up / parity at best, behind on geography.** **Why the lay of the land is this way:** Russian premium banking competes on convenience-amenity bundles (lounges, concierge, cash logistics) because rate/fee differentiation is thin and HNW switching is sticky. Cash delivery is **table stakes among the digital-premium leaders (T-Bank)**, so Ozon is buying parity, not a moat. Second-order: for a bank that just launched premium in May 2026, matching T-Bank's amenity list fast is the point — the feature's value is **signaling seriousness up-market**, not winning clients on this perk alone.

## [2] Company history / fit
Trajectory: Ozon acquired **Oney Bank** from Sovcombank (CBR approval Apr 2021, close May 2021, ~₽615M) → basic license → **universal/general license via "Ecom Bank" (2022)** → unified under the **Ozon Bank** brand (2023). Fintech is now a genuine growth engine, not a wallet sideline: fintech active users **34.4M (Jun-2025, +43% YoY)**; fintech **deposits ₽348.6B (Jun-2025)** up from ₹193B (Dec-2024); fintech **revenue ₽46.2B Q2-2025 (+164% YoY)**; **profit before tax ₽13.1B Q2-2025 (+197% YoY)**. **>55% of Ozon Card volume is now off-marketplace (Q2-2025)** — the strongest "becoming a real bank" signal. **Ultra premium launched May 2026; >85,000 Ultra clients by early Sep 2026.** Expansion continuing: registered a CBR **digital-financial-assets (ЦФА) platform** ([[Ozon Bank registers digital financial assets platform with CBR]]), a ₽1bn debut retail digital-asset placement, a **brokerage arm (Ozon Investments, autumn 2026)**, and **doubling its ATM network to 2,000 by end-2026** ([[Ozon Bank to double ATM network to 2,000 by end-2026]]).

**Why the company acts this way:** Embedded-finance economics push Ozon up-market — a marketplace wallet earns thin take-rate, so Ozon is deliberately adding **premium tiers + brokerage/depositary licenses + physical cash infrastructure (ATMs, delivery)** to monetize deposits and become a full-stack neobank rivaling T-Bank. Cash delivery fits the pattern: closing amenity gaps so Ultra is credible against incumbents. (Context: Ozon shares themselves became collateral in a VTB stake deal — [[VTB takes large Ozon share stake as collateral]].)

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Not novel.** T-Bank already offers the same mechanism more broadly. **Precise mechanism delta vs T-Bank: none material** — Ozon is Moscow-only, T-Bank is effectively nationwide; both free, both ₽2M floor, both armored/sealed/in-app. **Traction on this feature: none disclosed** (announced day one; no uptake numbers). The only traction figure is program-level — **85,000 Ultra clients** — and that is a **self-reported, un-audited PR number**, "early September," with no split between ₽2,990/mo payers and the far more likely **₽2M-balance fee-waiver qualifiers**. Against Ozon's 34.4M fintech users, 85K Ultra is trivially small (~0.25%).

**Why the value-add is thin, deeper:** Who captures the margin? Cash delivery is a **cost center** (armored logistics, insurance) offered free to retain HNW deposits — the economics only work if it anchors the ₽2M+ balances that fund Ozon's **₽348.6B deposit book**. So the real value-add is **deposit stickiness**, not the perk itself. What breaks it: T-Bank's broader footprint and Ozon's Moscow-only scope mean the feature **can't yet move HNW clients outside the capital**; until national rollout it's a retention tool for existing MSK premium holders, not an acquisition lever.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Expect **geographic expansion** of cash delivery beyond Moscow (phase one framing signals more), continued Ultra perk-stacking, and the broader up-market build-out: **Ozon Investments brokerage (autumn 2026)**, ЦФА, 2,000 ATMs by end-2026, pursuit of fund-management/NPF licenses. Market read: Ozon's fintech momentum is real and fast (deposits +~80% in two quarters; PBT +197% YoY), and independent evidence supports the up-market narrative — a **ВЦИОМ/WCIOM customer-experience survey (Oct 2026) ranked Ozon Bank #2 on customer experience behind only T-Bank** ([[Финтехно Ozon Bank tops banks on customer experience]]).

**Why the market goes this way / second-order:** Russian retail banking is consolidating around a few scaled digital players (Sber, T-Bank, VTB, Alfa, now Ozon); embedded-commerce origin gives Ozon a **low-cost acquisition funnel** incumbents lack. Counterintuitive risk: racing to match T-Bank amenity-for-amenity on HNW logistics is **capital-intensive and low-ROE** — the premium segment is prestige/retention, not where the embedded-finance margin actually is. The durable value is the 34.4M-user deposit-and-payments graph; cash delivery is a rounding-error feature on that thesis.

**No independent sell-side commentary** on the cash-delivery move was found in accessible sources — the narrative is company IR + Russian trade-press reprints of the PR (open).

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian retail banking premium-subscription / mass-affluent tier. No clean third-party TAM for the premium-segment — "no data"; qualitatively the move sits inside two converging trends. (1) Ecosystem neobanks racing to bundle affluent clients: Ozon Bank launched premium "Ultra" only in **May 2026** and passed **>85k Ultra clients by early September 2026** (per Ozon Bank, via bosfera.ru / rb.ru) — ~4 months to 85k is fast adoption for a de-novo premium tier, though off a tiny base. (2) Ozon Bank sits atop a hyper-scaling fintech arm: group **Fintech revenue +164% YoY to RUB 46.2bn in Q2 2025**, **deposits RUB 348.6bn at 30-Jun-2025** (≈4x the RUB 94.4bn a year earlier), **34.4m fintech active users (+43% YoY)** (per Ozon Q2 2025 release). Group turned a **Q3 2025 net profit of RUB 2.9bn on RUB 258.9bn revenue** (per press, via WORLDEF). Why now: premium is the highest-margin, stickiest retail slice, and an ecosystem (marketplace + travel + bank) can cross-subsidise perks rivals pay cash for — so the land-grab is for deposits and primary-bank status, not fee income from the service itself.

**Competitive landscape.** KPIs the premium tier runs on: premium-client count, assets-under-service / deposit balance per client, subscription attach & monthly fee, and (for the ecosystem) primary-bank share. Players and basis of competition — competition is on **perks-density and distribution**, not price (fees cluster at the same point): **T-Bank** (T-Premium 2,990₽/mo, free at ≥200k₽/mo spend + ≥1M₽ assets; per tbank.ru), **Alfa-Bank** (Alfa Premium, ~1M₽ asset threshold, dedicated advisor; per premiumbanking.info), **Sberbank** (SberPremier/SberFirst). Ozon Bank's Ultra fee **2,990₽/mo, waived from 2M₽ assets** matches T-Bank's headline fee but sets a higher asset bar. Recent moves: cash delivery to Ultra, 2–30M₽/mo, Moscow region only (2026-10-05); Gold/Platinum Ozon Travel concierge + Phoenix Pass lounges added same release. Protagonist position: **niche challenger, catching up** — it entered premium last (May 2026) and is cross-selling off marketplace reach; moat is distribution / ecosystem data (intangibles + switching costs via Ozon loyalty), not scale in private banking `(analysis)`. On overall CX it already ranks #2: WCIOM 2026 — 58% of Ozon Bank clients rate interaction highly, behind only T-Bank, ahead of Sber 56%, VTB 53%, Alfa 52% (see [[Финтехно Ozon Bank tops banks on customer experience]]).

**Comps & multiples.** No deal/round/valuation in this news (product launch) → trading multiples **not applicable**; Ozon fintech is a segment of listed Ozon (MOEX/OTC), not separately valued → EV/Revenue, P/E for the bank alone = **no data / [UNSOURCED]**. Qualitative peer comp on the actual variable (price): Ozon Ultra 2,990₽/mo = T-Premium 2,990₽/mo (in-line on fee), but Ultra's fee-waiver asset bar 2M₽ vs T-Bank's 1M₽ → Ultra is **pricier to get free**, i.e. aimed at a wealthier/more-committed client. Internal comps (base) for Ozon fintech trajectory and peer premium/ecosystem moves: [[Ozon Bank to double ATM network to 2,000 by end-2026]], [[Ozon to launch brokerage arm Ozon Investments in autumn 2026]], [[VTB takes large Ozon share stake as collateral]], [[T-Investments launches loyalty platform paying shares for purchases]], [[T-Bank opens home insurance to all customer categories]]. Distribution not computed — only one price point is directly comparable; qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Negative unit economics on the perk, by design.** Free armoured-car delivery of 2–30M₽ is a genuine cost (cash-in-transit, insurance, security) booked as an acquisition subsidy. If Ultra scales beyond the current ~85k and heavy users concentrate the high-ticket deliveries, the "free" perk erodes premium-tier margin — second-order: pressure to re-tier or cap, denting the headline differentiation.
2. **Geographic/scale concentration = PR-grade, not national.** Service is Moscow-region only and early-stage ("first stage"); "announced" ≠ "live nationwide." The release is silent on per-delivery cost, loss/fraud liability, and whether limits hold at scale — classic de-PR gaps.
3. **Deposit-funding dependence & regulatory overhang.** Ozon Bank's draw is deposit growth (RUB 348.6bn, +~4x YoY); premium perks exist to defend that funding base. A rate turn or CBR scrutiny of ecosystem-bank concentration (and the VTB collateral situation over Ozon shares) could raise the cost of holding affluent deposits faster than the perk retains them.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This is an ecosystem neobank attacking traditional private-banking's last moat — physical/concierge service — with marketplace economics, not a re-rating event. Falsifiable thesis: if Ozon Bank sustains Ultra client growth and lifts affluent-deposit share while keeping fintech EBITDA positive, premium is net-accretive; **watch the next quarterly fintech disclosure for per-client deposit balance and any Ultra count update, plus geographic rollout of cash delivery**. Thesis breaks if the perk stays Moscow-locked and Ultra count stalls near 85k — then it's a marketing flag, not a franchise shift.
