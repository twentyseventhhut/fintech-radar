Gr4vy Partners with Unlimit to Give Enterprises Access to Local Payment Methods Worldwide
Merchants can offer locally preferred payment methods without building a separate integration for each new market, helping them launch faster. Through the partnership, they gain access to payment methods including Pix, Mercado Pago, PicPay, OXXO and Efectivo in Latin America, M-Pesa and Airtel Money in Africa, WeChat Pay, GrabPay and UPI in Asia-Pacific, and MB WAY and SEPA Instant in Europe.
“ Merchants expanding into new regions need the flexibility to add the right acquiring partners and payment options without rebuilding their financial stack,” said John Lunn, Founder and CEO of Gr4vy. “ Partnering with Unlimit gives our customers greater flexibility to adapt their business strategy to local needs while accessing a broad portfolio of ways to pay across different countries .” 
“Payments remain fundamentally local. Customer preferences, currencies and processing requirements differ from market to market, creating complexity for businesses expanding internationally. Our partnership with Gr4vy addresses that complexity by connecting its enterprise merchants to Unlimit’s global payment coverage and local capabilities,” said Irene Skrynova, CEO of Global Payments at Unlimit.
