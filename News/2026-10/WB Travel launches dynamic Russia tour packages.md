 На Wildberries запустили продажу динамических туров по России
 
 
На Wildberries (входит в группу RWB) запустили продажу динамических туров по России. Они формируются в режиме реального времени под запрос туриста из доступных на сервисе WB Travel вариантов прямых перелетов и проживания на внутренних направлениях. Бронирование уже доступно в приложении и на сайте Wildberries . Об этом CNews сообщили представители RWB.
Для бронирования необходимо зайти во вкладку «Туры», выбрать направление внутри России и даты поездки — после этого сервис сформирует доступные предложения под заданные параметры. Тур состоит из перелета и проживания. Оплатить тур можно банковской картой, через СБП или « WB Кошелек ». На всех этапах путешествия пользователям доступна круглосуточная поддержка WB Travel.
«Мы продолжаем расширять ассортимент WB Travel и делать сервис удобнее для путешественников с самыми разными запросами — и теперь можем предлагать пользователям более гибкий формат поездок по России : турист сам выбирает направление и даты, настраивает отдых под себя и при этом получает туроператорские гарантии. Кроме того, динамическое пакетирование позволяет охватывать и те направления, куда классические туроператоры не всегда формируют готовые турпакеты», — отметил руководитель WB Travel Тарас Демура . 
WB Travel ранее вошла в реестр туроператоров для запуска динамического пакетирования. Статус туроператора позволит WB Travel оказывать комплексные туристические услуги напрямую и расширить предложение. Также на WB Travel представлены туры стратегического партнера сервиса — туроператора FUN&SUN.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
### [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On **2026-10-05** Wildberries (part of the merged RWB / Wildberries&Russ group) switched on **dynamic tour packaging for domestic (Russia) trips** inside its **WB Travel** vertical. The user picks a Russian destination + dates in the «Туры» tab, and the service assembles a package — **direct flight + accommodation** — in real time from WB Travel's own inventory, bookable in the Wildberries app/site and payable by card, SBP, or «WB Кошелёк», with 24/7 WB Travel support. FUN&SUN tours (the strategic partner) also sit on the shelf.

De-PR'd: this is **not a new service** — it is **one feature added to an already-running OTA/travel showcase**. The hard enabler happened earlier: the operating entity **ООО «РВБ ТРЕВЕЛ» entered the federal registry of tour operators (реестр туроператоров) in June 2026**, which is the legal prerequisite for selling dynamically-packaged tours (a packaged tour = regulated «туристский продукт», sellable only by a licensed туроператор). So the real story is **Wildberries converting a marketplace storefront into a licensed tour operator** and now using that licence to do dynamic packaging — the thing marketplaces previously could not legally sell. The PR framing ("more flexibility for travelers") is secondary to the structural move: WB can now cover routes classic операторы ignore, and capture operator margin, not just affiliate/agent commission.

### [1] Competitors / peers
Russian online-travel is crowded and consolidating. Hotel-booking OTA shares (early–mid 2026): **Yandex Travel + Acase ~34.9%** combined (Yandex Travel alone ~15.4% of bookings Jan–Jul), **Островок ~24.8%** (Yandex is in the process of **acquiring Островок**, reportedly ~₽15–20bn — the defining consolidation move), Bronevik ~5.5%. ~59% of RU bookings now go through OTAs. On the bank-ecosystem flank, **T-Bank** runs **Т-Путешествия** and literally days earlier (2026-10-07) added short-term apartment rentals (see [[T-Travel adds short-term apartment rentals at home and abroad]]); Yandex/Tutu/Level Travel/Travelata just formed a joint travel ad network (see [[Travel services launch joint ad network with Yandex and Tutu]]).

But WB Travel's closest analog is **not** the OTAs — it is the **tour-operator / dynamic-packaging** niche. Global pioneers of dynamic packaging are **Expedia (2000s), Priceline, Orbitz**; it is now standard OTA functionality (lifts conversion + average booking value). WB's differentiator vs. OTAs: it holds a **туроператор licence** (so it sells guaranteed packages, not just bookings) **plus** the WB super-app traffic, wallet, and payments. Position: **late but structurally armed challenger** — scale and rails it has; travel-ops credibility it is renting from FUN&SUN's Тарас Демура.

### [2] Why it matters / stakes
This is the clearest signal yet of **Russian marketplaces moving from selling goods to selling services/experiences**, financed by their payment ecosystems. For WB specifically it monetises super-app traffic into high-ticket travel and routes it through «WB Кошелёк»/SBP — a fintech-adjacent payments and lending funnel (same group that owns WB Банк — see [[WB Bank launches savings accounts and consumer loans]], [[Buyer to close 5% stake deal in Wildberries bank by October]]). The incumbent-pain angle is real: traditional турагенты fear WB will **"задушить скидками"** (undercut on price) while customers consult agents then buy on the cheaper marketplace. The Mineconom-backed «витрина впечатлений» (excursions/guides marketplace) shows the state sees WB as a legalisation channel for grey-market tourism supply — regulatory tailwind, not headwind.

### [3] What to watch / unknowns
- **Volumes & take-rate**: no disclosed GMV, package count, or operator margin — "dynamic tours" could be a thin pilot. Watch for first booking numbers.
- **Supply depth**: dynamic packaging needs real flight + hotel inventory and contracted allotments; unclear how much is WB's own vs. re-sold FUN&SUN / third-party. Direct-flight-only constraint limits coverage.
- **Offline push**: WB Travel plans **branded offline турагентства in 2026** + a WB/FUN&SUN own-hotel chain (5 in Turkey, 1 in Egypt) — capital-heavy omnichannel bet; execution risk.
- **Regulatory/financial-guarantee load**: tour-operator status carries фингарантии / Турпомощь obligations and liability if a trip fails — a different risk profile than marketplace commerce.
- **WB Банк 5% stake deal** closing ~end-Oct 2026 ties the travel+payments ecosystem story together.

### [4] One-line takeaway
Wildberries is using its freshly-obtained **tour-operator licence** to bolt **dynamic packaging** onto WB Travel — turning a marketplace into a licensed, super-app-distributed travel operator and escalating the marketplace-vs-OTA-vs-turagent fight in Russian domestic tourism.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
### Challenge / red-team questions
1. **Scale unknown** — is "dynamic tours по России" a full rollout or a thin pilot? No GMV, no number of packages, no destinations count disclosed. Could be a press feature, not a business line.
2. **Own vs. re-sold inventory** — how much of the flight+hotel supply is WB's own contracted allotment vs. re-packaged FUN&SUN / third-party? If re-sold, WB is an aggregator, not a true operator, and margin is thin.
3. **Direct-flight-only limit** — real-time packaging requires only direct flights; that excludes most domestic routes needing connections. How wide is actual coverage vs. the "cover routes operators ignore" claim?
4. **Is dynamic packaging actually novel in RU?** Yandex/OTAs already bundle flight+hotel. What does WB's licence add beyond legal cover — real price advantage or just marketing?
5. **Tour-operator liability** — туроператор status brings фингарантии, Турпомощь obligations, and legal liability if a trip collapses. Has WB sized this risk, or is it a latent balance-sheet exposure?
6. **Does the licence belong where it's used?** Registry entity is ООО «РВБ ТРЕВЕЛ» — confirm it is the same consumer-facing "WB Travel" and the licence truly covers dynamically-packaged domestic tours.
7. **Turagent "задушит скидками" narrative** — is WB actually undercutting, or is that incumbent FUD? No evidence of below-market pricing in the source.
8. **Monetisation path** — does this meaningfully route payments through «WB Кошелёк»/WB Банк (the fintech thesis), or is card/SBP the dominant method, making the ecosystem-funnel angle speculative?
9. **"Second attempt" risk** — Profi.Travel calls this Wildberries' *second* run at domestic tourism. What failed the first time, and what's different now?
10. **Demura/FUN&SUN dependency** — WB's travel-ops credibility is rented from one partner. Key-man / single-partner concentration risk if the FUN&SUN relationship sours.
11. **Source quality** — core facts come from RWB's own press to CNews/BFM (vendor PR). No independent audit of inventory or pricing. Treat claims as company-stated.
12. **Consolidation threat** — with Yandex absorbing Островок (~35%+ combined), does a late WB entrant have room, or is domestic OTA/packaging already locking up?
13. **Regulatory framing** — Minэкономразвития backing the «витрина впечатлений» is cited as tailwind; but is that the same product as dynamic tour packaging, or a different WB Travel feature being conflated?
14. **Capex of offline agencies + own hotels** — branded турагентства + WB/FUN&SUN hotel chain are capital-heavy. Is the dynamic-tours launch a genuine strategy or a marketing wrapper around a costly omnichannel bet with unproven ROI?

**Importance: 3/5** — A concrete, correctly-dated product step that advances a genuinely strategic theme (Russian super-apps/marketplaces + bank ecosystems invading travel; marketplace-vs-OTA-vs-turagent disruption; WB converting to a licensed tour operator). It is more than noise because of the tour-operator-licence angle and the WB Банк/payments ecosystem tie-in. But it is capped below 4/5: it is a single feature on an existing vertical, volumes/economics are undisclosed, facts are vendor-PR, and it is incremental to WB Travel's already-reported 2026 build-out rather than a step-change event.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian online-travel / OTA sector, package-tour subvertical via *dynamic packaging* (real-time flight+hotel assembly). Market size: Russia online-travel ~USD 11.1bn in 2024 → ~USD 25.8bn by 2033 at ~9.8% CAGR (per IMARC, via imarcgroup.com, as of 2025). Structure: consolidating around domestic super-platforms after Booking.com/Airbnb/global OTAs exited in 2022. On the OTA/booking side, 2026 shares: Yandex Travel ~29% (with Acase ~35% combined), Ostrovok ~21-29% (~24.8% of hotel bookings May 2026), with Tutu.ru the most-visited RU travel site (>20yr incumbent, per SimilarWeb Q1'26) and Ozon Travel the other marketplace-owned OTA (per datainsightsmarket / similarweb, as of 2026). The *packaged-tour* layer is distinct and still broker/tour-operator-dominated (FUN&SUN: 20,000+ agencies, 2.7M tourists in 2025, per web). "Why now": (1) the post-2022 vacuum pulled travel demand onto domestic marketplaces; (2) *dynamic packaging* is a secular global shift (~+31% y/y globally, per dataintelo) that lets a marketplace bypass pre-built operator packages and assemble tours on its own inventory — and critically lets a non-operator capture the tour-operator GMV/margin once it enters the registry. Barriers: supply aggregation (flight+hotel inventory) + distribution + *regulatory* (tour-operator registry entry, fin-guarantees) + the touroperator liability stack. Low capital barrier for a super-app bolting travel onto a captive base; higher legal barrier to becoming the operator-of-record.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: GMV/booking value, take rate (commission), supply breadth (directions/inventory), attach/repeat into the parent ecosystem, and — for packages — share of own-assembled vs resold-operator volume. Basis of competition is distribution + supply, not price. Key players: **Yandex Travel + Acase** (scale leader, ~35% OTA), **Ostrovok** (hotels, Yandex in process of acquiring it — major consolidation), **Tutu.ru** (traffic leader), **T-Путешествия** (bank-OTA, just added STR apartments — [[T-Travel adds short-term apartment rentals at home and abroad]]), **Ozon Travel** (rival-marketplace OTA), plus classic operators FUN&SUN / Anex / Pegas. Recent dated moves: WB Travel entered the federal tour-operator registry (RWB Travel LLC, June 2026; internal/inbound/outbound), appointed FUN&SUN owner **Тарас Демура** as head, and on 2026-10-05 launched real-time *dynamic Russia tours* (flight+hotel, bookable in the Wildberries app/site, 24/7 support, payment via card/СБП/WB Кошелек); FUN&SUN is both WB Travel's anchor supply partner and its boss's own operator. Strategy also includes branded offline travel agencies in 2026 and branded hotels (per vc.ru / Interfax / tourprom). Protagonist position: **late entrant / catching up** on OTA supply and brand (Yandex/Tutu/Ostrovok lead), but **structurally advantaged on distribution** — RWB reaches a very large share of RU e-commerce buyers and owns payments (WB Кошелёк) + ad (RWB Media) + logistics rails. Moat `(analysis)`: captive-audience + payments/loyalty switching cost of the all-in-one app, NOT a travel-supply moat; the tour-operator license is the one genuinely defensible new asset (lets it earn operator economics, not just agent commission).

**Comps & multiples.** Product launch, no round/valuation/deal metrics → trading multiples **no data**. IR grounding: `wildberries` not in ir_coverage → IR skipped. Parent scale anchor (press, not IR): RWB Group (Wildberries & Russ) 2025 GMV >RUB 6.1tn (+49% y/y from ~RUB 4.1tn 2024), net profit ~RUB 175bn (per new-retail / rb.ru / oborot.ru, 2026-04-01). Against that base, travel/tour GMV is immaterial near-term — this is an ecosystem-engagement + payment-flywheel play, not a P&L mover. Internal comps (RU travel + marketplace-into-travel precedents, not valuation comps): [[T-Travel adds short-term apartment rentals at home and abroad]] (bank-OTA super-app widening — same playbook, different parent), [[Travel services launch joint ad network with Yandex and Tutu]] (Yandex OTA dominance + travel-media), [[X5 updates ad formats on 76K Pyaterochka and Perekrestok checkouts]] (adjacent retail-media monetization). Peer standalone valuations for Yandex Travel / Ostrovok / Ozon Travel: **no data** (embedded/private; Yandex-Ostrovok deal price not disclosed in corpus). Distribution not computed — qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Supply/brand disadvantage & FUN&SUN dependence** — WB Travel is a late demand platform entering an OTA market Yandex/Tutu/Ostrovok already lead, and its tour supply leans on a single anchor operator (FUN&SUN, owned by WB Travel's own head Тарас Демура). Second-order: concentration + related-party conflict risk (who gets priority inventory/pricing), and thin own-assembled volume until it signs more operators — bookings can leak to incumbents.
2. **Regulatory / tour-operator liability** — becoming an operator-of-record (registry entry) brings fin-guarantee, Turpomoshch-style reserve, and consumer-protection obligations that a pure agent avoids; a tour-failure/refund event now lands on RWB's balance sheet and brand, not just a partner's. Second-order: travel losses could dent the marketplace's trust halo.
3. **Immaterial economics vs headline, super-app over-reach** — travel take-rate on a RUB 6.1tn-GMV parent is a rounding error; risk the segment is marketed as GMV/engagement growth while never earning real margin, and that offline agencies + branded hotels (announced for 2026) pull RWB into capital-heavy, low-return hospitality far from its logistics core (execution risk).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` Confirms the RU pattern of *marketplaces/banks turning travel into an engagement + payment flywheel* rather than standalone OTAs — WB now joins T-Bank and Yandex in absorbing travel intent into a super-app. Novel wrinkle: unlike T-Travel (agent/aggregator), WB took the tour-operator license + dynamic packaging, so it can capture operator GMV directly. Falsifiable thesis: WB Travel is distribution-led, not supply-led; it would be *wrong* if WB discloses own-assembled tour GMV/take-rate or signs multiple large operators beyond FUN&SUN. Watch next: (a) offline-agency/branded-hotel rollout (signals a real vertical push vs feature-tick), (b) non-FUN&SUN operator onboarding (de-risks related-party concentration), (c) any WB Кошелёк-tied travel-credit/BNPL — that would be the fintech catalyst that makes this a radar-relevant super-app move rather than an e-commerce tab.
