# Lloyds names Javier Rodríguez de Colmenares as group chief risk officer

Rodríguez de Colmenares succeeds Stephen Shelley, who has retired after managing the bank's entire risk function since 2017.

Javier Rodríguez de Colmenares, formerly of Santander, has officially taken up the role of group chief risk officer at Lloyds Banking Group.

The appointment follows Stephen Shelley's decision to retire after 15 years at Lloyds, where he started in 2011 as CRO for commercial banking before taking charge of the bank's entire risk function in 2017.

Shelley's call to step down, first announced in April, brings to a close a 37-year career in financial services. Before joining Lloyds, he held a variety of coverage and risk roles within Barclays' wholesale banking division.

Commenting on Shelley's tenure and Lloyds, group CEO Charlie Nunn states: "He has overseen a fundamental reshaping of the group's risk management function and culture amidst significant regulatory change and economic turbulence".

"I know that the board and the entire leadership team owe him a great debt of gratitude and we wish him all the best for a well-deserved retirement," adds Nunn.

Related:Verisave hires Chris Lefebvre as new CEO

Shelley's departure follows shortly after long-time group chief technology officer Victor Weigler announced his retirement. HSBC's Jane Gilmour has been named as Weigler's replacement, and is set to join Lloyds in January.

Rodríguez de Colmenares joins from Banco Santander, where he spent the past three years as global CRO for Corporate and Investment Banking (CIB). His career at the Spanish banking group, split across two periods, includes a further ten years at its CIB unit in various senior roles, as well as a spell in Brazil heading up credit operations before serving as deputy CRO.

His career began in 2001 at BBVA within its capital markets division, and later included a role as chief of the infrastructure and energy division at IDB Invest, which he joined after initially leaving Santander in 2016, before returning to the bank in 2020.
