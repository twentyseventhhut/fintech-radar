From followers to founders: Beast Industries and Mastercard help the next generation own their path to prosperity 
A new collaboration with Beast Industries aims to give creators the tools and support to turn big ideas into sustainable businesses.
 From left, Mastercard's Shamina Singh chats with Jimmy Donaldson, the creator known as MrBeast, and Jeff Housenbold, CEO of Beast Industries, at a company event earlier this month.  
Ben Fox Rubin
Vice President,
Global Communications,
Mastercard
A great idea can start anywhere: in a friends group chat, behind a camera, at a kitchen table or wherever inspiration strikes in today’s digital world.
For a generation that learns, shops, creates and builds online, the digital economy is more than a place where money moves. It is where communities form, ideas find an audience and a single spark can become a business.
But getting started is only part of the journey. Building something that lasts takes more than followers or a viral moment. It takes confidence, know-how and a community to turn that momentum into meaningful opportunity.
That’s why Mastercard and Beast Industries are partnering on new consumer and creator co-brand card programs, experiences, tools and education to help creators and the next generation turn ideas into opportunity and build stronger financial futures on their own terms.
Founded by Jimmy Donaldson, known globally as MrBeast, the world’s most-subscribed to creator, Beast Industries has grown from the early days of Jimmy and  his YouTube channel  to a global business spanning media, consumer products, technology platforms, mobile and financial services — all with philanthropy at the center. Its reach reflects how a new generation discovers ideas, develops skills, gives back and imagines what’s possible.
For Mastercard, the collaboration extends its work in financial education and access, securing digital participation and small business growth in the creator economy. It also advances the company’s commitment to connect and protect 500 million people and small businesses on their pathways to financial health by 2030.
That’s where the right support can make all the difference, helping more people turn digital possibility into progress they can build on. That possibility is priceless.  
“The next generation is shaping culture and creating new forms of commerce at the same time,” said Jill Kramer, chief marketing and communications officer, Mastercard. “Through our work with Beast Industries, we're meeting people where they are, bringing culture and commerce closer together, and opening new pathways from ideas and influence to meaningful economic opportunity.”
Donaldson and his teams see that same drive across a community that is not only watching, but building.
“At Beast, we innovate boldly and are constantly trying things that have never been done before. Making the impossible possible, while also making kindness viral, is a core part of our brand,” said Jeff Housenbold, Beast Industries CEO. “That’s why this partnership is so important, it gives us a chance to dream big about how we help people gain practical skills, build that confidence and take their ‘impossible’ ideas further.”
 
 From left, Shamina Singh, president, Mastercard Center for Inclusive Growth; Mastercard CEO Michael Miebach; Beast Industries CEO Jeff Housenbold; Jimmy Donaldson, aka MrBeast; Ted Fike, Beast Industries chief business officer; Jon Huntsman, Mastercard vice chair and president, Strategic Growth; and Jill Kramer, Mastercard chief marketing and communications officer. 
 
Turning inspiration into ownership
The partnership will introduce co-brand offerings, exclusive benefits and Priceless experiences that bring cardholders closer to the world of MrBeast. More than access, the ambition is to turn inspiration into participation — giving fans new ways to engage, learn and see how creative energy can become something they build and own.
 
Helping creators grow from influence to enterprise
Creators today are also founders: hiring teams, launching products and reaching customers around the world. By combining Mastercard’s payments network, digital capabilities and loyalty expertise with Beast Industries’ creator ecosystem, the organizations plan to help creators manage and scale their work — turning reach into resilient businesses and momentum into longer-term financial opportunity.
 
Expanding access and education for the next generation 
Through the Mastercard Center for Inclusive Growth and Beast Philanthropy , the partnership will expand access to practical financial education and community-based resources. The goal is to meet people where they are — with information that connects to everyday decisions and helps them earn, save, protect what they build and make informed choices for the future.
 
Building wealth rarely comes from one product or one milestone
It grows through access, security, knowledge and the confidence to keep moving forward. In an economy where opportunity increasingly begins online, empowerment means helping more people do more than follow what comes next — it means giving them a stronger foundation to create it, own it and benefit from it.
