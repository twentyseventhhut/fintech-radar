Zcash developers set January target for quantum-resistant payments after ‘bunker mode’ scare
The plan concerns public payments, where about seven in 10 ZEC sit. A separate wallet tool would keep balance checks from revealing which addresses belong together.
Zcash developers aim to add support for quantum-resistant, hash-based signatures in January, though no network activation date has been confirmed.
The upgrade to the privacy coin would initially protect transparent transactions, which account for about 70% of issued ZEC, while shielded payments require separate safeguards.
Zakura, a program used to run the Zcash network, has also developed a private lookup tool that lets wallets check balances without revealing linked addresses to a server, and an experimental version is available in the Vizor wallet.
Developers of privacy-focused Zcash (ZEC) plan to add protections designed to withstand quantum computers within months as warnings about AI-assisted attacks put the broader security of cryptocurrency wallets under scrutiny.
“Our team plans for post-quantum signature opcodes to land in Zcash in January,” Roman Akhtariev wrote in an engineering post for Zakura , one of the programs used to run the Zcash network and validate its transactions.
Those opcodes, or instructions, would give Zcash the ability to check hash-based signatures, a method of approving payments designed to resist quantum attacks. While January is the team’s target, no confirmed network activation date was mentioned.
The aim is to prevent attackers from using today’s public keys to work backward to forge a wallet owner’s approval and spend their coins. 
The upgraded software would produce a signature that the network checks using hashes, digital fingerprints made from secret data. Hash-based signatures use a different way to prove permission to spend.
Forging an approval would require defeating those hashes, so a breakthrough against today’s wallet keys would not automatically defeat the replacement. 
Using fresh addresses keeps keys hidden before spending, and the proposed signatures are intended to protect the payment when spending makes its approval public.
Zakura’s post comes on the heels of a warning by Ethereum researcher Justin Drake, who on Thursday urged holders to prepare for a so-called “bunker mode,” warning that AI could find a shortcut through the mathematics protecting bitcoin and ether wallets “in months, not years” in the worst case. 
Ethereum’s own broader migration to quantum-resistant cryptography has a target date of December 2029 . No practical attack on their wallet keys has been demonstrated so far.
ZEC ended Thursday around $1,185, down about 11% over the preceding week, leading a drop among top cryptocurrencies after Drake’s post went viral on X.

Fresh addresses, revealing balance checks
Zcash has two kinds of payments. One is shielded payments, which conceal the sender, recipient and amount. Transparent payments, in contrast, work much like bitcoin BTC $84,048.79 , with addresses and amounts visible on the blockchain.
About 11.96 million of the 16.98 million ZEC issued sat in the transparent pool on Thursday, roughly seven in 10 coins, according to CoinDesk’s calculation from ZecStats data. The January plan focuses on that public side of the network.
A wallet uses a secret key to approve spending and a corresponding public key that lets the network check the approval. Working backward from the public key to the secret is supposed to require an impractical amount of computing. Drake’s concern is that a breakthrough could make it affordable, as CoinDesk reported Thursday.
A standard Zcash transparent address initially hides the public key behind a hash, a digital fingerprint designed to be difficult to reverse. Spending reveals that key — and moving leftover funds to a fresh address puts them behind another key that has not been disclosed.
But checking those fresh addresses can give something else away.
Someone who spreads savings across 10 addresses still needs their balances. A typical wallet asks a server for those records, giving it a way to connect addresses that may look unrelated on the blockchain.
Zakura’s lookup tool uses so-called private information retrieval, which lets a database return a record without learning which one was requested. The wallet can check its balances without handing the server a readable list of addresses, while the payments themselves remain public.
An experimental version is already available through the “Private queries” setting in Vizor, a Zcash wallet.
The shielded side needs separate work, however. Zcash’s quantum recovery design warns that an attacker who obtains a recipient’s address could save encrypted payment records today and try to decrypt them after a future mathematical breakthrough.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] TL;DR.** On 2026-10-09 Zcash core developers (via Zakura, a Zcash full-node/validation implementation) said they are targeting **January** to land **post-quantum signature opcodes** — support for hash-based signatures — on the privacy coin. The upgrade would **initially protect transparent transactions** (~70% of issued ZEC, ~11.96M of 16.98M coins) rather than shielded pools, and **no network activation date is confirmed** ("target," not a hard fork schedule). It follows a viral "bunker mode" warning that AI/quantum could break elliptic-curve wallet keys "in months, not years." ZEC ended that Thursday ~$1,185, down ~11% on the week after the scare.

**[1] Dated timeline.**
- **2026-01-15** — Project Eleven raises $20M specifically to build post-quantum cryptography for Bitcoin/crypto wallets (early signal the Q-threat was being funded). [[Project Eleven raises $20M for post-quantum cryptography]]
- **2025-10-13** — India's SEBI proposes a quantum-safe cryptography strategy for capital markets (regulators already treating Q-day as planning-horizon risk). [[SEBI proposes quantum-safe cryptography strategy]]
- **2026-05-27** — BitGo runs its first post-quantum MPC transaction simulation (custodian-side PQ readiness). [[BitGo completes first post-quantum MPC transaction simulation]]
- **2026-06-17** — Zcash discloses a separate critical vulnerability, ZEC falls sharply (distinct event — NOT this one). [[Zcash discloses critical vulnerability, ZEC falls sharply]]
- **2026-09-08** — Ethereum Foundation sets a **December 2029** target for a quantum-safe L1 (Hegotá upgrade; account-abstraction-based migration).
- **2026-10-07/08** — Ethereum researcher Justin Drake urges holders to prepare "bunker mode" (move funds to fresh, never-signed addresses); warns AI could shortcut ECDSA "in months, not years." Post hit ~6M views; Vitalik Buterin and cryptographers pushed back. Covered by CoinDesk 2026-10-08.
- **2026-10-09** — **This story:** Zakura's Roman Akhtariev writes that PQ signature opcodes are planned to land in Zcash in January; Zakura also ships an experimental private-information-retrieval (PIR) balance-lookup tool in the Vizor wallet.

**[2] Why it matters.** This is the first concrete **near-term** (months, not 2029) post-quantum signature deployment schedule from a major privacy chain, landing exactly as the market's AI-breaks-ECDSA anxiety peaked. It is a positioning move: Zcash reframes its privacy brand as *quantum-resilient* privacy — differentiating from Bitcoin/Ethereum, whose PQ migrations are years out (ETH ~2029). But the scope is narrow (transparent ~70% first; **shielded pools unaddressed**, which is the part users actually buy Zcash for), and the hardest problem — a "harvest-now-decrypt-later" attacker saving encrypted shielded records today — is explicitly left for separate future work.

**[3] Mechanics / key terms.**
- **Hash-based signatures (e.g., WOTS/XMSS-style):** approval scheme whose security rests on hash preimage resistance, not the discrete-log hardness of elliptic curves; believed resistant to Shor's-algorithm quantum attacks. Trade-off: larger signatures, statefulness concerns.
- **Signature opcodes:** VM instructions that let the network *verify* a new signature type; adding them is the enabling step before wallets can use PQ keys.
- **Transparent vs shielded:** transparent ZEC works like BTC (addresses/amounts public); shielded uses zk-SNARKs to hide sender/recipient/amount. Only transparent is in scope for January.
- **Fresh-address hygiene + PIR:** a transparent public key stays hidden behind a hash until first spend; moving change to a fresh address re-hides it. The new PIR "private queries" tool lets a wallet check balances without revealing which addresses it owns to the lookup server — closing a metadata leak that address-rotation alone doesn't fix.
- **"Bunker mode":** proposed mass migration of funds to never-signed addresses so public keys remain hidden; a mitigation, not a cryptographic fix.

**[4] Watch next / open questions.** (a) Whether January slips — it is a dev target with no confirmed mainnet activation/hard-fork date. (b) Timeline + design for **shielded**-pool quantum resistance (the harder, more important half). (c) ZEC price reaction given its outsized 2026 run (reported ~+2,300% YoY, privacy-sector cap ~$33.6B, Grayscale ZCSH spot-ETF push) — PQ narrative could re-rate it or the "bunker mode" fear could keep weighing. (d) Whether competitors respond: QRL (hash-based/XMSS from genesis), Algorand (Falcon-1024, opt-in since 2025, broader PQ by end-2027), and Ethereum (2029) set the comparison set.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team questions**

1. **Target vs commitment:** "January" is a dev target with *no confirmed network activation date.* How often do crypto hard-fork dates slip? Is this effectively a roadmap PR move timed to the bunker-mode news cycle?
2. **Wrong 70%:** The upgrade protects *transparent* ZEC first — but Zcash's value proposition is *shielded* privacy. Does securing the public, BTC-like side actually protect the users who chose Zcash for confidentiality?
3. **Shielded punt:** The hardest threat — harvest-now-decrypt-later against saved encrypted shielded records — is explicitly deferred. Is there even a design, let alone a date, for shielded PQ?
4. **Threat realism:** Drake's "bunker mode" rests on AI/quantum breaking ECDSA "in months, not years" worst-case. No practical attack has been demonstrated. Is Zcash marketing against a speculative threat?
5. **Opcode ≠ adoption:** Adding verify-opcodes doesn't migrate existing coins. How many of the 11.96M transparent ZEC would actually move to PQ keys, and who coordinates that?
6. **Hash-sig trade-offs:** WOTS/XMSS-style signatures are larger and statefulness is error-prone (key-reuse = catastrophic). What's the on-chain bloat and UX/wallet risk?
7. **PIR tool scope:** The private-information-retrieval balance lookup is "experimental" in one wallet (Vizor). What's its performance/trust model, and does it leak via timing/size side channels?
8. **Competitive framing:** QRL has been hash-based since 2018; Algorand shipped opt-in Falcon in 2025. Is Zcash late rather than first — and is "January" just catching up?
9. **Price confound:** ZEC is up ~2,300% YoY on privacy-narrative + Grayscale ETF + DCG buying. Is the PQ story a genuine driver or a post-hoc justification for a momentum trade?
10. **Governance:** Zakura is one of several node implementations. Does a Zakura engineering post represent a ratified Zcash protocol decision, or one team's intent?
11. **Source concentration:** Nearly all coverage traces to one CoinDesk piece + Zakura's own post. Any independent technical review of the opcode spec?
12. **Interaction with existing vuln history:** Zcash disclosed a separate critical vulnerability in June 2026. Does the team have the review bandwidth to ship novel PQ crypto safely on a tight timeline?
13. **Downgrade path:** If PQ and legacy opcodes coexist, can an attacker force fallback to the breakable elliptic-curve path?
14. **Regulatory overhang:** Dubai banned privacy tokens (Jan 2026); several venues restrict shielded assets. Does hardening transparent (compliant) payments subtly steer Zcash toward the de-shielded, exchange-friendly form?

**Importance: 3/5** — Materially interesting as the first *near-term* post-quantum signature schedule from a major privacy chain, and well-timed against peak "bunker mode" anxiety, giving Zcash a differentiated quantum-resilient-privacy narrative while BTC/ETH migrations sit years out (ETH ~2029). But it is capped below 4/5 because it is a *target*, not a confirmed activation; it covers only transparent (~70%) coins while leaving the defining shielded-privacy problem unaddressed; the underlying threat is still speculative (no demonstrated ECDSA break); and single-chain, single-source. Relevance to core fintech/payments rails is indirect.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Privacy coins are a small but newly re-rated crypto subsector: total privacy-coin market cap ~$33.6bn, with Zcash alone >60% of that share (per CoinDesk/DailyCoin, as of Oct 2026). ZEC trades ~$1,222 with a ~$20.7bn market cap (world's 7th-largest crypto), up ~2,496% in 2026 and at levels unseen since 2016 (per Kraken/CoinMarketCap, Oct 14 2026). The sector is concentrated, not fragmented: Zcash + Monero (XMR ~$10bn mcap, #14–15, per CoinDesk, Oct 13 2026) dominate; entry barriers are cryptographic/protocol credibility and exchange listings, not capital. "Why now" = a convergence of (1) the Oct 7–8 2026 "bunker mode" scare, where EF researcher Justin Drake warned AI could shortcut the math protecting Bitcoin/Ether wallets "in months, not years" worst-case (no practical attack demonstrated), and (2) privacy repositioned as "the trade of the year." The quantum angle rides a broader wave: Ethereum targets base-layer PQ by Dec 2029, Bitcoin advances BIP-360/361 (per DL News, ethereum.org); a US ~$300m quantum hardware push (CoinDesk, Sep 9 2026) sharpens the clock.

**Competitive landscape.** The KPIs here are not revenue multiples but network-security posture and privacy-adoption: shielded-pool share and PQ-readiness timeline. Zcash's shielded pool has climbed to ~29–30% of supply (~4.9–5.0m of ~16.9m ZEC, up from ~8% in early 2024; per The Block, crypto.news, zecstats). The January plan targets the OTHER ~70% (transparent pool, ~11.96m ZEC on Oct 9) via hash-based (WOTS) signature opcodes. Players on the PQ dimension: Ethereum (foundation-led, 2029 target, 10+ client teams on devnets), Bitcoin (no foundation, loose coalition, slower governance), and infra/custody peers already shipping — BitGo + Silence Laboratories ran the first post-quantum MPC custody simulation (May 2026), and Project Eleven raised $20m Series A (Jan 2026) for PQ self-custody ahead of network upgrades. Basis of competition = credibility + speed to live protection. Protagonist's position: **ahead on timeline** for a major privacy chain — January vs Ethereum's 2029 — but **narrow on scope** (transparent payments only; shielded side explicitly needs separate work, and "harvest-now-decrypt-later" risk on shielded records is acknowledged by Zcash's own recovery design). Moat = protocol-level privacy credibility + first-mover PQ narrative `(analysis)`.

Cap-structure arithmetic instead: ZEC market cap ~$20.7bn; FDV ~$25.4bn on 21m max supply → circulating/max = 16.9m / 21m = ~81%, i.e. mcap/FDV ~0.81x (dilution modest). External peer: Monero ~$10bn mcap (~half of ZEC), no comparable published PQ roadmap found `[UNSOURCED]`. Multiple verdict: ZEC's 2026 run (+2,496%) and 7th-rank are **rich vs its own history and vs Monero**, driven by narrative (privacy + quantum) rather than disclosed cashflow — not a valuation-vs-growth test that applies cleanly to a token.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Target ≠ activation (execution/vaporware risk).** Developers explicitly stated January is a *target* with *no confirmed network activation date*; the opcodes protect only transparent payments. Marketing the "quantum-resistant" label before mainnet activation could unwind the narrative-driven premium if the date slips.
2. **Scope gap on shielded funds (harvest-now-decrypt-later).** The ~29–30% in shielded pools is NOT covered by the January plan; Zcash's own design warns attackers could store encrypted records today and decrypt post-breakthrough — so the headline doesn't protect the privacy use-case that defines ZEC.
3. **Regulatory disintermediation.** Privacy coins face active delisting/bans (Dubai DFSA, Jan 2026); the subsector's >$33bn cap can be stranded by exchange/jurisdiction bans regardless of cryptographic merit — a second-order channel that bypasses the quantum story entirely.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` Zcash is converting an abstract, 2029-horizon quantum fear into a near-term, shippable differentiator — potentially a mini re-rating catalyst for privacy coins IF it ships on time and others (Monero, Bitcoin) remain slow. Falsifiable thesis: if the January opcodes activate on mainnet on schedule AND shielded-side PQ work follows, ZEC holds its premium over Monero; the thesis breaks if (a) activation slips past Q1 2026 with no date, or (b) a major exchange delisting (post-Dubai contagion) hits before activation. Watch: the mainnet activation announcement and any peer PQ-timeline pull-forward.
