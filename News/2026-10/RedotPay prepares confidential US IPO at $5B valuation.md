## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**What's new (2026-10-26).** Bloomberg reports RedotPay — a Hong Kong crypto/stablecoin payment-card fintech — is preparing to file *confidentially* for a US IPO before year-end at a valuation of **at least $5B**, working with JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Jefferies and (newly) Barclays. The note's headline figures: **9M+ customers**, **$180M+ annualized run-rate revenue**, ~280 employees. Separate reporting adds: 9M of 2026 revenue (first three quarters) up >50% YoY, operating margin >50%, and a parallel pre-IPO round being discussed at a **$4–5B** valuation.

**Why it matters.** A confidential S-1/draft filing is a procedural step that preserves optionality and timing flexibility; it is *not* a commitment to list. It lets RedotPay lock in bank engagement and SEC review while keeping the actual listing date open — important because, per the same news cycle, the public debut may slip to **2027 or later**.

**Internal storyline (this is a long-running, heavily-covered arc, not a new story):**
- [[RedotPay reaches unicorn status with $47M round]] — Sept 2025, unicorn, backers Coinbase Ventures, Galaxy, Vertex.
- [[RedotPay raises $107M Series B for stablecoin payments]] — Dec 2025; $194M total raised in 2025; led by Goodwater, with Pantera, Blockchain Capital, Circle Ventures, HSG; 6M users, $10B+ annualized volume, $150M+ ARR.
- [[RedotPay explores US IPO at $4 billion valuation]] — Feb 2026; first IPO signal, $1B raise / $4B valuation, same bank syndicate.
- [[RedotPay plans $1bn US IPO at $4bn valuation]] — Mar 2026; concrete $1B / $4B plan.
- [[RedotPay churns executives ahead of $4 billion US IPO push]] — Mar 2026; 5+ senior departures in 12 months, no CFO, seeking up to $150M more; revenue doubled to $158M.
- Product/distribution build-out through 2026: [[RedotPay partners Nium to expand stablecoin off-ramp]], [[RedotPay picks OpenPayd for stablecoin payment infrastructure]], [[RedotPay launches Connect for instant fiat stablecoin settlement]], [[RedotPay secures Mexico virtual asset service provider registration]], [[RedotPay integrates Tempo for agentic stablecoin payments]], [[Sui unveils RedotPay-powered Slush stablecoin credit card]].

**Valuation ladder.** Unicorn (~$1B, Sept 2025) → "$4B" IPO target (Feb–Mar 2026) → now "$5B+" (Oct 2026). The headline valuation has risen ~25% in ~7 months while revenue grew from ~$150–158M ARR (Dec/Mar) to $180M+ (now) and users from 6M to 9M.

**Material overhang not in the note — the Binance lawsuit.** Binance affiliates (Nest Trading, Distributed Technologies, Chaintecs Consulting Singapore) sued RedotPay co-founders (Gao Zhangpeng/Michael Gao, Chan Wa Choi, Yao Chao) in Hong Kong for ~**$473M**, alleging RedotPay diverted 470,000+ Binance Card users via Binance Pay funding in breach of a commercial agreement (damages derived from a $925 lifetime-value-per-user assumption). RedotPay is contesting the "unfounded allegations." Reporting in Aug–Oct 2026 tied this litigation plus regulatory hurdles to a possible IPO slip to 2027+; RedotPay publicly disputed any "deferral." The confidential-filing framing fits a company keeping options open while this resolves.

**Comparable crypto IPOs (2025 class).** Circle ($1.05B IPO, +168% day one, stablecoin-float economics), Bullish (~$13B valuation, Aug 2025), Gemini ($GEMI, Sept 2025), Figure, eToro, BitGo. The wave soured fast: by late 2026 Gemini was reportedly down ~89% from debut, Bullish ~71%, BitGo ~77%, and the pipeline froze. Circle held up best because stablecoin reserve/float revenue is more durable than exchange/custody revenue — a useful read-through: RedotPay's card-interchange + stablecoin-payment economics are closer to a payments fintech than to float-driven Circle, so the comp set and durability question are open.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team questions**

1. "Confidential filing by year-end" is not a listing. If the actual debut is being pushed to 2027+ (per Aug–Oct reporting on the Binance suit and regulatory hurdles), is the $5B valuation a filing target, a pre-IPO round mark, or press-sourced speculation? The note conflates "filing" with "IPO."
2. The headline $5B is "at least $5B" / "$4–5B" per different outlets. Which number is the actual confidential-filing basis vs. a pre-IPO round vs. a banker's aspiration?
3. Is the $180M+ ARR a reported figure or investor-materials self-disclosure? In Mar 2026 the figure was ~$158M; the jump to $180M+ is only ~14% — does that square with the claimed >50% YoY revenue growth for the first three quarters of 2026?
4. 9M users vs 6M in Dec 2025 — are these *registered* users or *active/funded* cardholders? Registered-user counts are notoriously inflated for crypto apps.
5. The >50% operating margin claim is unusually high for a card/payments fintech. Is it GAAP, is interchange net of card-scheme/issuer fees, and does it include crypto-trading spread revenue that may not recur?
6. The Binance ~$473M lawsuit is a direct, material, undisclosed-in-note overhang: it targets the co-founders personally, equals ~9–12% of the proposed valuation, and the alleged conduct (user diversion) may be the very source of RedotPay's user/revenue growth. How does the S-1 treat it as a risk factor?
7. If 470,000+ of RedotPay's users were allegedly diverted from Binance Card, how much of the 6M→9M user growth and the revenue base is dependent on channels Binance now disputes?
8. No CFO as of Mar 2026 and 5+ senior departures in 12 months — has RedotPay installed audit-grade finance leadership required for an SEC registrant? Confidential filing without a CFO is a governance red flag.
9. Backer concentration/related-party risk: Circle Ventures is both an investor and a stablecoin counterparty (USDC rails). Does RedotPay's economics depend on favorable terms from an investor-supplier?
10. Comp durability: Gemini -89%, Bullish -71%, BitGo -77% from 2025 debuts; the crypto-IPO window is reportedly frozen. Is a confidential filing a way to be ready if/when the window reopens, rather than a signal of imminent listing?
11. Regulatory: Hong Kong stablecoin licensing, US GENIUS-era stablecoin rules, and RedotPay-as-non-bank (wallet provided by licensed third parties) — does the issuing/compliance stack survive US public-company scrutiny across 100+ markets?
12. Revenue mix: how much is sustainable payment/interchange vs. crypto-price-sensitive trading, yield, and P2P-marketplace fees that evaporate in a bear market?
13. Why Hong Kong-domiciled firm listing in the US (Rabbit7 Holding BVI structure per Series B disclaimer)? VIE/offshore-structure and US-China cross-border listing risks for a crypto issuer.
14. Source quality: this is single-sourced Bloomberg ("said to"), relayed by one Telegram channel with 1 mention and no extracted primary text — all figures are reported secondhand.

**Importance: 3/5** — Meaningful datapoint in a prominent, well-tracked stablecoin-payments IPO story (largest Asia stablecoin IPO candidate, rising $5B mark, strong fundamentals claimed), but it is an incremental, single-sourced, confidential-filing step on an arc already covered 4+ times, carries a major undisclosed litigation overhang, and faces a frozen crypto-IPO window — so it moves nothing concrete yet.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector context.** RedotPay sits at the intersection of two hot 2026 narratives: crypto/stablecoin payment cards (spend rails that let users pay merchants from stablecoin balances via Visa/Mastercard) and the 2026 crypto-IPO window opened by Circle's mid-2025 NYSE debut. That window has been uneven: Circle, Gemini and Bullish listed successfully in 2025, but Kraken repeatedly slipped its timetable (confidential S-1 Nov 2025, froze its multibillion IPO in Mar 2026 "amid market conditions," now eyeing 2H2026) and Grayscale delayed "as the crypto listing boom cools" (see in-base [[News/2026-03/Kraken freezes multibillion-dollar IPO amid market conditions]], [[News/2026-05/Grayscale delays IPO as crypto listing boom cools]]). So RedotPay is filing confidentially into a window that is open but choppy — a confidential filing preserves optionality to delay, which is exactly how peers have behaved.

On the product side, stablecoin card spend is the growth thesis: RedotPay says cumulative stablecoin card spend has passed ~$10.9B and projects the category could hit ~$50B/yr by 2028. The space is getting crowded (Bybit Card, KAST, Bleap, Wirex, Shakepay, Plutus), and card-only models have proven fragile — Infini shut its individual crypto-card business in mid-2025 and pivoted away, a cautionary comp for monoline card economics.

**In-base comps & valuation trajectory.** RedotPay's own history in the base shows a steep ramp:
- Sep 2025 — unicorn at a $47M round (Coinbase Ventures, Galaxy, Vertex): [[News/2025-09/RedotPay reaches unicorn status with $47M round]]
- Dec 2025 — $107M Series B; claimed ~$10B annualized stablecoin volume: [[News/2025-12/RedotPay raises $107M Series B for stablecoin payments]]
- Feb 2026 — explores US IPO at ~$4B (JPMorgan, Goldman, Jefferies): [[News/2026-02/RedotPay explores US IPO at $4 billion valuation]]
- Mar 2026 — plans $1bn raise at $4B: [[News/2026-03/RedotPay plans $1bn US IPO at $4bn valuation]]; exec churn reported ahead of the push
- Oct 2026 (this note) — confidential filing targeting ≥$5B (banks now JPMorgan, Goldman, Jefferies, Barclays)

So the headline $5B is a ~25% step-up from the Feb/Mar $4B target and ~>100x the Dec-2025 Series-B scale of fresh capital — i.e. a private mark that has moved fast and is not yet validated by a priced round (a pre-IPO round at $4–5B is reportedly still being negotiated, not closed).

Nearest in-base/sector peer on fundamentals is KAST: $80M Series A at a $600M valuation (Mar 2026), ~1M users, ~$5B annualized volume, ~$100M expected ARR → roughly a 6x revenue multiple. RedotPay reports ~9M users, ~$10B+ annualized volume and ~$180M annualized run-rate revenue, with 9M2026 revenue up >50% y/y and operating margin reportedly >50%. Against KAST's ~6x, RedotPay's ~$180M revenue at $5B implies ~28x run-rate revenue — a large premium that bakes in scale, profitability and growth, but is rich versus the one clean private comp and leaves little margin for a growth or margin miss at IPO pricing.

**Risk flags (skeptical):**
1. **Confidential ≠ committed.** This is a confidential filing / pre-IPO sounding, not a priced deal. Direct peers (Kraken, Grayscale) have filed confidentially then frozen/delayed on market conditions; RedotPay could do the same.
2. **Unvalidated valuation step-up.** $5B is self-/bank-guided and ~25% above the Feb–Mar $4B figure, with the $4–5B pre-IPO round still being negotiated. Revenue is company-reported ($180M run-rate, >50% margin) and unaudited — no third-party / IR confirmation.
3. **Rich multiple vs comps.** ~28x run-rate revenue at $5B vs KAST's ~6x; premium depends on sustaining >50% growth and >50% operating margin that the market has not yet tested.
4. **Monoline card concentration.** Revenue skews to crypto/stablecoin card spend; Infini's 2025 card shutdown shows the segment's economics can break, and the field (Bybit, KAST, Wirex, Plutus, Bleap) is crowding fast.
5. **Governance / execution signal.** Earlier in-base reporting flagged executive churn ahead of the IPO push ([[News/2026-03/RedotPay churns executives ahead of $4 billion US IPO push]]) — a yellow flag for a pre-listing company.
6. **Regulatory / jurisdiction overhang.** A Hong Kong-domiciled, crypto-native issuer listing in the US faces stablecoin/VASP regulatory exposure across markets (it has been securing Mexico VASP registration, Nium/Circle off-ramps, etc.); SEC disclosure and cross-border stablecoin rules add listing risk.
