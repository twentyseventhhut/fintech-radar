N26 expands Flexible Cash Fund access to all membership tiers
 Competitive, market-aligned performance: Maximize cash potential and returns with our flexible cash fund – yielding up to 2.57%* p.a. A fund is not a deposit. The yield is variable and capital is at risk. 
 Tier-wide promotional access: Until 1 January 2027, every membership tier across all markets will receive an introductory yield of up to 2.57%* p.a., matching the Metal tier offer with no N26 service fee applied. Afterwards standard tier-specific N26 service fees will apply. 
 Low barriers to entry: Minimum investment starting from €1. 
 High liquidity: Unlimited deposits and withdrawals available flexibly with no transaction fees or restrictions. 
 Diversified exposure: Direct access to short-term, high-quality money market instruments. 
 Daily valuation & monthly payout: Value accrues daily and is paid out on a monthly basis.
