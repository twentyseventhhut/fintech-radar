In brief
OKX has raised fresh capital at a $25 billion valuation, unchanged from its March round.
Circle, Ripple, Standard Chartered's SC Ventures and hedge fund Qube Research & Technologies took part.
The exchange's joint venture with ICE is seeking approval to offer tokenized stock in 63 U.S. companies.
 Crypto exchange OKX has raised new funding at a $25 billion valuation from a group that spans a stablecoin issuer, a British bank and a London quantitative hedge fund, Bloomberg reported. 
 Circle Internet Group, Ripple and Standard Chartered's investment arm SC Ventures all took part, alongside Qube Research & Technologies, which is new to the cap table. The exchange declined to say how much it raised. 
 The round extends one closed in March, when Intercontinental Exchange, the owner of the New York Stock Exchange, put around $200 million into OKX at the same $25 billion mark, a deal framed at the time around tokenized securities. Seven months on, the price has not moved. 
 The money "focuses on strengthening OKX's long-term market infrastructure," Haider Rafique, the exchange's global managing partner, said in a statement. 
 London quant firm QRT, a Credit Suisse spinout, already runs a crypto fund holding about $1 billion. Its investment reflects confidence in "the long-term growth of digital assets and 24/7 markets," said Thomas Eaton, a quantitative trading director at the firm. 
 ICE and OKX have been building together since March. Their joint venture, OKXICE LLC, said on Monday it is seeking approval to sell tokenized stock in 63 U.S. public companies, Nvidia, Apple and Coca-Cola among them. 
 The offering would run under the Securities and Exchange Commission's innovation exemption , introduced in September days after the Clarity Act stalled in the Senate. It lets qualifying venues trade tokenized U.S. equities on public blockchains without registering as national securities exchanges, for up to five years, and covers only tokens carrying the same rights as ordinary shares, including dividends and voting. Synthetics that merely track a price are excluded. 
 The exemption also caps how many stocks each venue can list, and gives issuers 30 days to object to a third party tokenizing their shares. On that timetable, Nvidia, Apple and Coca-Cola each hold a veto over whether their stock appears on the venue at all. 
 OKX has been building equity exposure by other routes too, having l isted perpetual futures on Magnificent Seven stocks and the S&P 500.

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
OKX raised an **undisclosed amount** of fresh capital. It declined to say how much. The round is explicitly an **extension of the March 2026 round** when ICE put ~$200M in at a $25B mark ([[ICE takes minority stake in OKX at $25bn valuation]]). New backers: **Circle, Ripple, SC Ventures (Standard Chartered), and Qube Research & Technologies (QRT)** — a London quant firm (Credit Suisse spinout) running a ~$1B crypto fund, and the only genuinely *new* name on the cap table. The others are strategic partners, not pure financial investors.
- **Price unchanged 7 months on.** The PR frame is "flat $25B valuation" = stability signal. De-PR'd, a flat markup 7 months later in a hot crypto cycle is ambiguous: it can equally read as "no fresh third-party price discovery" — OKX's own release specifies "$25 billion **pre-money**" valuation, which is a *lower* effective per-share price than a $25B post-money at an undisclosed check size. **The headline "flat" obscures that pre- vs post-money framing plus an undisclosed raise size means no real new price was set** — this looks more like a strategic/alliance round than a competitive priced round.
- **Why structured this way (→ why it matters):** the investor list is a *vertical-integration roster*, not a syndicate. Circle = USDC issuance; Ripple = RLUSD/payments rails (already listed on OKX); SC Ventures = custody (SC is custodian for BlackRock's BUIDL tokenized Treasury fund); QRT = market-making liquidity. OKX is buying **stack components for its tokenized-RWA/24-7-markets thesis**, not capital. → Second order: the raise is really a *governance and alliance mechanism* to lock partners into the OKX–ICE tokenized-equity venue, with the valuation as a secondary artifact.
- Alongside: the **OKX–ICE JV (OKXICE LLC)** filed a public notice to offer **tokenized stock in 63 U.S. companies** (Nvidia, Apple, Coca-Cola, Tesla, Microsoft) on **X Layer**, 24/7, settled in stablecoins (USDC/USDT/USDG), tokens backed 1:1 with full dividend+voting rights, under the SEC's **Sept 17, 2026 "innovation exemption."** This is a *filing/notice*, not a live product — issuers get 30 days to veto; **Cerebras has already objected.**

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **The raise itself:** low novelty. Flat, undisclosed, partner-heavy extension of an existing round. Not a competitive priced round — closer to an alliance/strategic placement.
- **Real novelty = the OKXICE tokenized-equity venue**, but that is **announced/filed, not live** — explicitly a notice under the SEC innovation exemption with a 30-day issuer veto window and at least one objection (Cerebras) already lodged. Traction = **zero live tokenized shares traded**; OKX has meanwhile built equity exposure via perpetual futures on Mag-7 + S&P 500 (a synthetic workaround, not the real thing).
- **Who captures the margin (→ 2 levels deep):** if tokenized equities go live, the economics are thin and the rails (settlement, stablecoin, custody) are owned by the *investors* (Circle, SC, Ripple), while listing/matching leverage sits with **issuers (30-day veto) and ICE's NYSE relationships**. → OKX risks being the *venue operator* capturing a slim trading take-rate while Circle/ICE capture issuance/settlement/legitimacy rents. The central question shifts from "is OKX worth $25B" to **"in the tokenized-equity stack OKX is assembling, does OKX actually capture margin, or is it building a rail its own investors monetize?"**

## [4] What's next / market sentiment

- Sentiment: bullish crypto-funding tape (Kraken $20B, Circle public, stablecoin momentum). The flat valuation amid a hot cycle is the *skeptic's tell*.
- **→ Counterintuitive second order:** stacking strategic investors who are also *suppliers/partners* (Circle, Ripple, SC) concentrates dependency — if the tokenized-equity thesis stalls or the SEC exemption is challenged, OKX is left with an alliance of counterparties rather than durable diversified capital. Concentration here is **fragility, not safety**. Regulatory risk is binary: the Sept 2026 innovation exemption was a post-CLARITY-Act stopgap, 5-year, and legally untested.
