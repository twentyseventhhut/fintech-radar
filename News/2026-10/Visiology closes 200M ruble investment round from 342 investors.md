# Visiology closes 200M ruble investment round from 342 investors

> [!info] 2026-10-16 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Mergers.ru

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Mergers.ru] 💛 Visiology закрыла инвестиционный раунд на 200 млн рублей. В размещении приняли участие 342 инвестора, средний чек составил 584,8 тыс. рублей. Организатором инвестиционного раунда выступила компания BITL. Привлеченный капитал компания направляет на развитие продуктовой платформы, ИИ-функциональности и дальнейшее масштабирование бизнеса. Закрытие раунда совпало с опережением ключевых показателей финансовой модели Visiology. По итогам первого полугодия 2026 года выручка компании превысила собственный план более чем на 30%.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
[0] **What happened.** Visiology (AO "Viziolodzhi"), a Russian corporate business-intelligence / data-analytics platform vendor, closed a 200M-ruble (~$2.4M) equity-crowdinvesting round on 2026-10-16. 342 investors participated at an average check of ~584.8K rubles; the round was organized by BITL and collected on the Поток (Potok) investment platform. Proceeds go to product-platform development, AI functionality (Visiology Cortex) and business scaling. The close coincided with the company beating its financial-model plan: H1 2026 revenue reportedly exceeded plan by >30%.

[1] **Deal terms / valuation.** The offering (applications opened 2025-07-24) sold a 12.5% stake implying a post-money valuation of ~1.6B rubles. Minimum entry was 25,200 rubles = 180 shares at 140 rubles/share. This was a retail/pre-IPO-style equity raise through a CBR-licensed investment platform, not a MOEX direct listing — though Moscow Exchange is a shareholder of Potok, so there is an indirect exchange link. Note: 342 investors × ~584.8K avg check ≈ 200M, internally consistent.

[2] **Company financials (context).** FY2025: revenue 613M rubles (+32% YoY), EBITDA ~220M rubles (×2.5 YoY), net profit 129M rubles (+71% YoY). At a 1.6B-ruble post-money, that implies ~2.6x revenue / ~12x net profit — rich for a small-cap but typical of a growth-framed import-substitution software story. The round funds growth rather than rescuing a loss-maker (company is already profitable).

[3] **Market / competitive position.** Visiology positions as a full replacement for Microsoft Power BI, SAP BI, MicroStrategy, Tableau and Qlik — all effectively withdrawn/curtailed in Russia post-2022. It competes domestically with Yandex DataLens, AW BI, Luxms BI and others. Reference win: Mosenergo migrating from Power BI to a Visiology-based unified data/analytics platform (project valued ~613M rubles). Visiology cites the 2025 Russian BI market at ~65B rubles, growing faster than broader IT on import-substitution tailwinds, with consolidation squeezing third-tier vendors. Differentiator pushed in 2025: Visiology Cortex (embedded AI), 5 releases, 120+ new features.

[4] **Why it matters / significance.** Small by Western VC standards (~$2.4M) but notable as a retail equity-crowdinvesting / pre-IPO capital-formation event in Russia — an emerging domestic channel (Potok, ex-Alfa-Bank, CBR-licensed, MOEX-backed) stepping in where sanctioned Western VC/PE exited. Signals (a) maturation of Russian crowdinvesting for later-stage tech and (b) continued monetization of the Power-BI-vacuum import-substitution BI market. Limited standalone macro impact; mainly a data point on Russian domestic capital markets + BI consolidation.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
Red-team questions:

1. Is the 1.6B-ruble valuation self-reported by Visiology/BITL or independently set? Crowdinvesting pre-money is typically issuer-set — no external lead investor doing price discovery, so treat as marketing, not a market clearing price.
2. The note says "342 investors, 584.8K avg check" → ~200M. But a 12.5% stake for 200M implies 1.6B post-money. Is the equity actually 12.5%, or did some reporting conflate the offered tranche with the full raise? Confirm the stake/valuation link.
3. Are the FY2025 figures (613M revenue, 129M net profit) audited (RAS/IFRS) or management accounts? Russian small-cap "net profit +71%" claims are frequently unaudited.
4. Liquidity/exit: shares bought via Potok — is there any secondary market or lock-up? Retail investors in a private RU BI firm may have no realistic exit absent an actual IPO. Was an IPO timeline promised?
5. "H1 2026 revenue beat plan by >30%" — beat whose plan? Beating an internally-set budget is not the same as YoY growth; what is the actual H1 2026 YoY figure?
6. Is ~$2.4M even material? At a 65B-ruble market this is a rounding error; frame as capital-markets signal, not a scale event.
7. Competitive durability: Power BI/Tableau are legally withdrawn, not technically inferior. If sanctions ease, does Visiology's moat evaporate? How sticky are migrations like Mosenergo?
8. Yandex DataLens is free/bundled with Yandex Cloud and backed by a far larger balance sheet. Can a 613M-revenue vendor out-invest DataLens on AI (Cortex)?
9. Market-sizing (65B rubles, "fastest-growing") is sourced from Visiology's own research — circular/self-serving. Any independent (e.g., CNews Analytics, TAdviser) corroboration of share?
10. BITL as organizer — what's its track record and incentive? Placement fees can distort valuation framing.
11. Does the 12.5% dilution plus prior rounds leave founders in control, and does the cap table include state/strategic investors that could complicate future financing?
12. Currency/macro: ruble volatility and high CBR rates make ruble-denominated equity returns hard to compare; a 25% crowdlending yield on the same platform is the opportunity cost — is equity even attractive vs Potok's own lending product?
13. Any sanctions/compliance exposure for the underlying enterprise customers (likely state-linked) that could hit revenue?
14. Duplicate check: the round was announced July 2025 (CNews) and is only now "closed" (Oct 2026) — is this genuinely new news or a 15-month-old story finally completing? (Verdict: fresh — the CLOSE/final allocation is the new event; opening was prior.)
15. Sample-size on growth: one reference win (Mosenergo) does not establish a migration wave — how many net-new Power BI replacements in 2025–26?

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian BI/analytics software, driven by import-substitution after Western vendors (Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, Qlik, SAP) exited in 2022. Market size: ~65bn rubles in 2025 per Visiology's own research, or ~74bn per TAdviser; growth ~15–17% in 2025, up 28% in 2024 to ~132bn-rubles-cumulative-ICT context (BI-specific figure is the ~65–74bn). Pre-2022 foreign-vendor share was 70–80% depending on sector; Russian-software spend rose from 37.4% (2022) to 67.3% (2023), so the secular driver is a one-time-ish substitution wave now shifting into a consolidation phase ("укрупнение ведущих игроков", per TAdviser). Structure: fragmented domestic field (Visiology, Luxms, Foresight Analytical Platform, Yandex DataLens, Modus, FineBI-type stacks) consolidating around a few leaders. Entry barriers: enterprise reference logos + certification/registry inclusion (reестр отечественного ПО) rather than deep tech moats. "Why now": the easy substitution replacements are largely done, so growth decelerates from the 2022–23 spike toward mid-teens — the next leg must come from AI features and share-taking, which is exactly what the raise funds. (analysis)

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: ARR/revenue growth, license-segment share, enterprise logo count, EBITDA margin (this is a profitable, not blitzscale, software market). Key players & basis of competition: Visiology, Luxms, Foresight, Yandex DataLens, Modus — competition on enterprise-grade features, on-prem/sovereign deployment, and reference accounts, not price. Visiology's position: leader-tier — ~25% of the Russian BI *license* niche (per market data), 500+ client organizations incl. Rosneft, Gazprom, Samolet, Positive Technologies, Federal Treasury, Ministry of Health. Recent company moves: FY2025 revenue 613M rubles (+32% YoY), EBITDA 220M (x2.5 YoY), net profit 129M (+71%); 2026 guidance 900M rubles (+47%); H1 2026 revenue at 127% of half-year plan (as of 2026-10-16 round close). Moat (analysis): switching costs + tier-1 state/corporate reference base in a protected market; weak vs. a VK/Yandex-scale distributor if it decides to push DataLens harder.

**Comps & multiples.** Round terms: 200M rubles for a 12.5% stake → post-money equity valuation 1.6bn rubles (200 / 0.125 = 1,600M). Using equity value as a VC/private proxy (no net debt disclosed → [UNSOURCED], treating equity ≈ EV):
- P/Revenue (trailing, FY2025): 1,600 / 613 = 2.6x
- P/Revenue (forward, 2026E guidance): 1,600 / 900 = 1.8x
- EV/EBITDA (FY2025): 1,600 / 220 = 7.3x
- P/E (FY2025 net profit): 1,600 / 129 = 12.4x
Flag: these are **low/cheap** multiples for a software asset growing revenue 32–47% — a profitable, +40%-growth SaaS at <3x trailing revenue and ~7x EBITDA is well below the global software band (EV/Rev ~5–15x for that growth). This reflects the Russian risk/liquidity discount and the thin crowdinvesting exit market, not weak fundamentals. Internal comps (grep fallback; sem down 402): [[Indie GO launches 5bn-ruble venture fund for gaming IPOs]] (same RU pre-IPO/venture-for-retail channel), [[Kommersant Positive Technologies enters the data market]] (adjacent RU data-market peer, also a Visiology client), [[Putin orders 'long money' growth-fund ecosystem for scaleups]] and [[Mergers.ru Gref advises state companies on successful IPOs]] (the policy push behind retail pre-IPO funding). External public RU-software benchmarks (Positive Technologies, Astra, Diasoft, Arenadata) trade at richer multiples on MOEX than this private round — consistent with a private-round / crowdinvesting discount. Distribution not computed (fewer than 3 clean same-model public comps with verifiable numbers).

**Risk flags.**
1. **Liquidity / exit risk on the crowdinvesting channel.** 342 retail investors at a ~585k-ruble average check bought a 12.5% minority with no stated exit path; BITL/Zorko-type pre-IPO platforms in Russia are thin and the IPO window for mid-cap tech is uncertain — investors may be locked in a private, illiquid stake. Second-order: if an exit stalls, it chills the retail pre-IPO funding channel Visiology relies on for future rounds.
2. **Customer concentration in state/state-linked accounts.** Rosneft, Gazprom, Federal Treasury, Ministry of Health — revenue leans on budget/SOE IT cycles, so procurement freezes or budget cuts hit disproportionately, and pricing power is capped by state-buyer leverage.
3. **Growth decelerates as substitution saturates + platform-distributor threat.** The 2022–23 substitution spike is cooling to mid-teens market growth; Visiology's +47% guidance implies continued share-taking, which is harder once the field consolidates and a bundled Yandex DataLens / VK offering can undercut on distribution. Over-reliance on a continuing substitution tailwind is an execution risk if the tailwind fades. (analysis)

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is a re-rating/validation signal for the RU BI leaders: a profitable domestic BI vendor can now raise growth capital directly from retail via crowdinvesting at a disclosed 1.6bn-ruble mark, setting a public reference valuation for the niche. Falsifiable thesis: if Visiology hits the 900M-ruble 2026 target and a liquidity event (MOEX pre-IPO/IPO) materializes within 12–24 months, expect copycat raises across Luxms/Modus and multiple expansion toward listed RU-software peers. What breaks it: a missed 2026 number or a stalled/illiquid exit, which would re-widen the private-round discount and cool the whole retail pre-IPO channel. (analysis)
