 GCash users can fund their crypto accounts without cash-in fees and transfer digital assets to external wallets through GCrypto, ABS-CBN reported.
The service is powered by Philippine Digital Asset Exchange (PDAX), a licensed cryptocurrency platform.
Users can transfer money from their GCash balances into their GCrypto trading wallets at no additional cost.
Cryptocurrency purchase prices already include service and platform fees.
The platform also supports round-the-clock trading in up to 55 cryptocurrencies, according to GCash.

To register, users must be at least 18 years old and have a fully verified GCash account.

GCash says eligible users can complete registration through the app in as little as 30 seconds.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** On 21 Oct 2026 GCash — the Philippines' dominant e-wallet (owned by Mynt) — launched/expanded **GCrypto**, letting fully-verified users trade up to **55 cryptocurrencies** around the clock directly inside the GCash app. The service is white-labelled on **[[Coins.ph expands into all-in-one financial app|PDAX]]** (Philippine Digital Asset Exchange), a BSP-registered VASP. Key hooks: **zero-fee cash-in** from GCash balance to the GCrypto trading wallet, **external transfers** of supported assets to outside wallets/exchanges (without first selling), and ~30-second in-app registration (18+, verified GCash account, verified email, profile updated within 3 years). Purchase prices embed service/platform fees; outgoing-transfer fees vary by asset. (Sources: <https://fintechnews.ph/74075/blockchain/gcash-crypto-trading-wallet-transfers/>, <https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/21/gcash-simplifies-crypto-trading-transfers-through-gcrypto>)

**[1] Scale / why it matters.** GCash is the single largest distribution channel for crypto in the Philippines: ~**81M registered users** and ~**41.5M monthly active users** (~56% of the adult population). Putting a one-tap crypto wallet behind that reach dwarfs standalone-exchange funnels. Timing is pointed: parent **[[Mynt's GCash edges higher after record-breaking Philippine IPO|Mynt]]** just completed the Philippines' largest-ever IPO (₱60.9B raised, ~$7B valuation, listed 20 Oct 2026 at ₱6.60), so GCrypto is a fresh post-IPO monetisation/engagement lever added days after listing. (Sources: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GCash>, <https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/10/21/news/national/gcash-parent-makes-pse-history-with-p609b-ipo/2449279>)

**[2] Partner dynamics (PDAX).** GCash does not hold a crypto licence itself — it rents PDAX's BSP VASP licence and matching/custody engine. This matters because **BSP has frozen new VASP licence applications** (moratorium extended from 1 Sep 2025, no new entrants through 2026), so white-labelling an incumbent VASP is effectively the *only* fast route to crypto for a non-licensed super-app. PDAX remains on BSP's active VASP list alongside Coins.ph (Betur), Maya, UnionBank, GoTyme, etc. (Source: BSP VASP status via <https://bitpinas.com/learn-how-to-guides/ph-crypto-exchange-directory/>)

**[3] Competitive context.** GCrypto puts GCash directly against the two incumbents it had ceded crypto to: **[[Maya rolls out recycled-plastic Mastercard and Visa cards|Maya]]** (~20M+ users; crypto live since 2023 on Coinbase infra, 30+ tokens, own VASP licence + digital bank) and **[[Coins.ph expands into all-in-one financial app|Coins.ph]]** (widest BSP licence set, ~0.10% spot pricing, own peso stablecoin). GCash's edge is pure distribution scale; its handicap is that fees are bundled into the buy price rather than shown as a thin explicit spread. Note Coins.ph is simultaneously [[Coins.ph rebuts compliance allegations over customer assets|fending off compliance allegations over customer assets]], which may hand GCrypto a trust-timing opening.

**[4] Open watch-items.** (a) Fee transparency — bundled spreads vs. Coins.ph/Maya explicit pricing; (b) whether PDAX's capacity/custody can absorb GCash-scale volume; (c) product depth — 55 tokens trade-only vs. Maya's deposit-insured bank integration; (d) regulatory overhang — SEC CASP rules (MC 4 & 5 s.2025, effective 5 Jul 2025) now capture securities-like tokens and marketing, so GCrypto's listings/promo are in scope; (e) read-through for other remittance/IPO plays in [[Revolut plans digital bank and e-money licences in Philippines|the crowding Philippine fintech market]].

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team questions**

1. **Is this actually new, or a re-launch?** GCash/PDAX crypto ties predate this; the Oct 2026 news frames it as "expanding access"/simplified transfers + 55 tokens + external transfers. Treat as a material feature expansion, not a greenfield launch. (OPEN — unclear how much is genuinely net-new vs. repackaged.)
2. **Does GCash hold a crypto licence?** No. It white-labels PDAX's BSP VASP licence. GCash itself is not a VASP. (ANSWERED)
3. **Why not just get its own licence?** BSP VASP moratorium (extended 1 Sep 2025) blocks new applications through 2026, so partnering an incumbent is the only fast path. (ANSWERED)
4. **How many tokens / what scope?** Up to 55 cryptos, 24/7 trade + external send/receive. Trade-only wallet; no evidence of staking/yield/derivatives. (ANSWERED)
5. **Are the fees actually "zero"?** No — only GCash-balance cash-in is zero-fee. Buy prices embed service + platform fees; outgoing transfers carry per-asset fees. "No cash-in fees" ≠ cheap trading. (ANSWERED)
6. **How does pricing compare to rivals?** Likely worse on transparency: Coins.ph quotes ~0.10% spot; Maya shows fees; GCrypto bundles spread into buy price. (PARTIAL — exact GCrypto spread not disclosed.)
7. **Real reach vs. headline users?** 81M registered is a vanity figure; ~41.5M MAU is the credible base, and crypto adoption will be a small fraction of that. (ANSWERED — directionally.)
8. **Can PDAX handle GCash-scale volume/custody?** Unknown — capacity, custody segregation, and incident history at GCash-scale inflows untested publicly. (OPEN — key risk.)
9. **Regulatory exposure?** SEC CASP rules (MC 4 & 5 s.2025) now capture securities-like tokens, public offers, and marketing; GCrypto's token list + in-app promo fall in scope. BSP oversees the VASP side. Dual-regulator risk. (ANSWERED — exposure real, enforcement effect TBD.)
10. **Is this a reaction to losing crypto share to Maya/Coins.ph?** Plausibly — both had crypto live for years; GCash is catching up on its own turf. (OPEN — intent not stated.)
11. **IPO-timing coincidence?** Launched days after Mynt's record IPO (20 Oct); reads as a post-listing engagement/monetisation signal to investors. (PARTIAL — correlation clear, causation asserted not confirmed.)
12. **Custody / who holds the keys?** PDAX custodies; users can transfer out to external wallets, but default is custodial. Counterparty risk sits with PDAX, not GCash. (ANSWERED.)
13. **Any deposit/asset protection?** Crypto is not PDIC-insured (unlike Maya Bank deposits). No investor-protection backstop for GCrypto holdings. (ANSWERED.)
14. **Could a Coins.ph-style compliance scare hit GCrypto?** Possible — Coins.ph is currently rebutting customer-asset allegations; any PDAX custody issue would transmit straight to GCash's brand. (OPEN — contagion risk.)
15. **Is 55 tokens meaningfully differentiated?** No — Maya lists 30+, Coins.ph/global exchanges list far more. Differentiation is distribution, not product breadth. (ANSWERED.)

**Importance: 3/5** — Materially important for the Philippine crypto/super-app race: GCash's ~41.5M MAU is the largest crypto distribution surface in the country, and the launch lands days after Mynt's record IPO. But it is a white-labelled, fee-bundled, trade-only wallet on a rented VASP licence, catching up to Maya and Coins.ph rather than breaking new ground — incremental share-shift, not a structural shift. Not a 4 (no new licence, model, or asset class); clearly above a 2 given the scale and IPO context.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Crypto-trading-inside-an-e-wallet in a remittance-heavy, high-adoption market. 22–23% of Filipinos interact with digital assets as of 2026 — one of SE-Asia's highest ownership rates (per Disruption Banking, as of Nov 2025; corroborated getperk/liveinph). Structure: consolidated at the rails, fragmented at the front-end — the exchange/VASP licence layer is the scarce asset, and the BSP froze it: a moratorium on new VASP licences from 1 Sep 2025 (extended 20 Aug 2025 memo, per coingeek) locks the roster at 9 active VASPs (incl. Coins.ph, Maya, PDAX, UnionBank, GoTyme). Why now: GCash has no VASP licence of its own, so the only route into crypto was to white-label a licensed exchange — and the licence freeze means distribution (GCash's 94m registered / 41.5m Q2'26 MAU) is now the differentiator, not the licence. Second-order: GCash just IPO'd (debut 20 Oct 2026, ~$7bn valuation, ₱60.9bn raised — per FintechFutures/Nikkei/Rappler), so GCrypto is a monetisation/ARPU story layered onto a freshly public super-app two days later.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs for a VASP-distribution deal: trading GTV, take rate (spread + platform fee), funded accounts / conversion of wallet users to traders, assets listed. Players: Coins.ph (widest BSP licence set, own PHPC peso stablecoin, QR Ph crypto-pay since Apr 2026), Maya/PayMaya (in-app crypto), UnionBank + GoTyme (bank VASPs), and PDAX itself as a direct exchange (~4m users, >50 peso-priced assets, BSP-licensed since Sep 2018 — per bitpinas/trustfinance). Basis of competition: distribution and UX (30-sec onboarding, zero cash-in fees), not price — spreads are baked into the quoted buy price and external-transfer fees "vary", i.e. GCrypto competes on funnel, not on disclosed economics. Protagonist position: GCash is the distribution front-end (41.5m MAU dwarfs PDAX's ~4m), PDAX is the licensed back-end; GCash is ahead on reach, a follower on crypto product vs Coins.ph/Maya which launched in-app crypto earlier. Moat: network-effects + super-app switching costs on GCash's side `(analysis)`; the regulatory moat (VASP licence) belongs to PDAX, not GCash.

**Comps & multiples.** No deal size / revenue split disclosed for GCrypto → multiples "no data". Internal comps from the base (this is a GCash-distribution-adds-crypto event, not a round):
- [[Mynt's GCash edges higher after record-breaking Philippine IPO]] — ~$7bn valuation at IPO, 41.5m MAU (the distribution asset behind GCrypto).
- [[Mynt sets GCash IPO price at P6.60 per share]] — pricing of the same listing.
- [[GCash operator Mynt eyes $8 billion valuation in Philippine IPO]] — earlier $8bn target vs ~$7bn realised; a ~12% haircut into print.
- [[Binance and Mastercard launch crypto card in Brazil]] / [[m10 and Binance enable direct manat crypto-fiat in Azerbaijan]] — same template (licensed-exchange-plus-wallet/distribution partner) in other EM wallets.
Valuation flag: GCash's ~$7bn mark is set on the payments/super-app, not on crypto; GCrypto is incremental optionality, not priced in. Distribution not computed — no comparable crypto-revenue figures disclosed; qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. Rails dependence / margin capture — GCash owns the funnel but PDAX owns the VASP licence, the trading infrastructure and (likely) the bulk of the spread; GCash's economics are undisclosed and probably a referral/rev-share, so the crypto take rate may sit mostly with the partner — a classic disintermediation-of-your-own-distribution risk if PDAX later goes direct-to-consumer.
2. Regulation / volatility exposure — BSP's VASP moratorium plus "continuing concerns over consumer protection and cybercrime" (Aug 2025 memo) means a freshly-listed, retail-heavy issuer (140,725 GCash subscribers bought the IPO via the app) is now routing mass-market users into a 55-asset, 24/7 trading product; any retail-loss or fraud episode is a reputational/regulatory liability for a company two days into public markets.
3. Economics opacity / adverse selection — fees are embedded in quoted prices and outgoing-transfer fees "vary by coin"; silence on spread, custody and who bears fraud liability makes revenue quality unverifiable and invites regulator scrutiny on transparency.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Confirms the SE-Asia pattern: licence-frozen crypto markets re-rate toward whoever owns distribution, turning the super-app into the real crypto on-ramp and the licensed exchange into a back-office utility `(analysis)`. Falsifiable thesis: if GCrypto meaningfully lifts GCash ARPU, expect Mynt to either buy/deepen the PDAX tie or seek its own VASP route once the moratorium lifts. Trigger to watch: first disclosed GCrypto GTV / funded-account numbers in a post-IPO Mynt filing, or any BSP move on the VASP freeze — that is what would move this from "optionality" to priced-in. Thesis breaks if crypto stays a thin referral line with no ARPU signal.

## Earnings Review

<!-- enrichment:earnings_review -->
**Earnings review — GCash / Mynt, Inc.** (IR-covered; parent Mynt IPO'd on the PSE 2026-10-20). This GCrypto launch is a product event, not an earnings print — figures below contextualize GCash's financial standing from Mynt's latest disclosed results. No crypto-specific revenue is disclosed.

**Latest disclosed period — H1 2026 (six months to 2026-06-30, Mynt IPO disclosure):**
- Revenue: ₱43.27B, +10.4% YoY (Q2 2026 a record ₱22.41B).
- Net income: ₱10.82B, +7.3% YoY.
- EBITDA: ₱11.76B, −4% YoY.
- Payments GTV: ₱9.84T, +23% YoY.
- Monthly active users: 41.5M as of June 2026.
- Net cash pile: ~₱69B (post-IPO, pre-raise cushion).

**FY2025 (full-year baseline):**
- Revenue: ~₱79.8B. Net income: ₱17.25B, +55% YoY.
- Payments GTV: ₱17.03T. MAU: 39.1M; ~56.7M transactions/day avg.
- Net income trajectory: ₱6.38B (2023) → ₱17.25B (2025).

**IPO:** Priced at ₱6.60/share; raised ~₱60.9B (~$967M) — the largest-ever Philippine IPO. Shares edged modestly around the offer price at debut.

**Read vs prior / thesis flags:**
- Growth decelerating into maturity: H1 2026 net income +7.3% vs FY2025's +55%, and EBITDA actually fell 4% — margin pressure despite 23% GTV growth. Top line (+10.4%) is now outpaced by transaction volume, i.e. take-rate/profitability compression.
- The crypto push (GCrypto, 55 coins, no cash-in fees, via PDAX) fits the playbook of layering higher-engagement financial services (payments → lending → investments → insurance → crypto) onto a 41.5M-MAU base to defend growth as core payments mature. Monetization is indirect (PDAX runs the exchange; GCash earns via embedded service/platform fees in purchase prices, and transfer fees), so near-term P&L impact is likely immaterial relative to the ₱43B H1 revenue base.
- Risk: a zero-cash-in-fee, round-the-clock crypto offering adds volatility-linked, regulatory-sensitive activity just as the market is pricing GCash on profit-growth durability (stock hovering near the ₱6.60 offer).

**Verdict: in-line / no-data (product launch, not an earnings event; crypto revenue undisclosed).** Latest actuals (H1 2026) show healthy but clearly decelerating growth with EBITDA softness.
