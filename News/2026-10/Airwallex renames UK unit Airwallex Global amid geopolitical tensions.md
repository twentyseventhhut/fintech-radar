Airwallex strengthens London presence as geopolitical tensions rise 

Airwallex has established a global holding company in the United Kingdom, just as criticism in the US over its Chinese links intensifies.
Australian-founded fintech Airwallex has established a new global holding company in London as the business shifts its focus to European expansion as it faces increasing political pressure in the US due to rising geopolitical tension between existing major markets in the US and China.
Earlier this week, Airwallex updated the name of its Airwallex Group UK company to Airwallex Global, a global financial holdings company, with president and co-founder Lucy Liu exiting as a director as of October 3. Co-founder and CTO Jacob Dai was registered as a director in late September. The company also incorporated a new entity, Airwallex Treasury UK, to support its UK treasury offering in June.
The restructuring follows the business’s increasing focus on the United Kingdom as a gateway to Europe, which it sees as a key market for its ongoing growth as it leans into the opportunities of being an early mover in agentic business accounts.
While Airwallex is yet to label its UK operations as its main headquarters for its next stage of growth, both its co-founder and CEO Jack Zhang , and Chief Financial Officer Pranav Sood are based out of London. The company is currently hiring for more than 50 roles in London, including critical roles such as a Head of Onboarding and FinCrime, Head of Risk Operations and a range of senior strategy roles.
The company officially has two headquarters, in Singapore and San Francisco, US. Launched in Melbourne in 2015, the company has long had a Cayman Island-parented structure.
An Airwallex spokesperson told Capital Brief the company saw London as an important global financial centre and a strong base for its continued growth.
