Revolut has integrated its Revolut Pay solution into the Just Eat app across the UK and Ireland, marking a significant expansion of its merchant acquiring ecosystem. For fintech professionals, this move signals the increasing maturity of account-to-account (A2A) payments in the gig economy, leveraging biometric authentication to bypass traditional card rails at scale.

The partnership introduces Revolut Pay as a primary checkout option for Just Eat customers in the UK and Ireland. By utilizing account-to-account (A2A) technology, the integration allows users to bypass manual card entry, instead authorizing restaurant and grocery orders via biometric authentication such as Face ID or fingerprint recognition. This mechanism aims to increase transaction speed and reliability for Just Eat’s millions of users and its network of 362,000 partners.

To incentivize adoption, the companies have launched a promotional period running from 28 October to 27 December 2026. During this window, users earn a 5x boost on RevPoints for every purchase made through the app. Once the promotion concludes, a permanent 2x points boost remains in place. Furthermore, the integration allows for the instant redemption of RevPoints for cash discounts directly within the Just Eat app and web checkout. This collaboration leverages Revolut’s massive scale, which currently includes over 80 million retail customers and 850,000 business clients globally.

> "This partnership brings together two platforms with a shared focus on convenience and customer experience. By integrating Revolut Pay into Just Eat, we're making everyday purchases faster and more rewarding, giving customers the opportunity to earn extra RevPoints on every order and seamlessly redeem them for instant discounts at checkout. We're excited about the potential to build on this partnership and bring even more value to our customers."

Alex Codina, General Manager of Merchant Acquiring at Revolut

Revolut has evolved from a digital-only challenger bank into a global financial super-app. With a user base exceeding 80 million retail customers, the firm has aggressively expanded its suite of services to include merchant acquiring, business banking, and loyalty programs like RevPoints. Its business division now serves more than 850,000 companies, positioning it as a significant player in the B2B payments space alongside its consumer-facing operations.

Just Eat Takeaway.com is a dominant force in the global on-demand delivery market. Headquartered in Europe and owned by the multinational conglomerate Prosus, the company operates a vast network of 362,000 partners worldwide. It facilitates millions of orders through its platform, connecting consumers with local restaurants and grocery stores. The integration of Revolut Pay represents a deepening of ties between two of Europe’s most prominent technology firms, as Just Eat seeks to streamline its checkout experience and Revolut aims to embed its payment solutions into high-frequency consumer spending categories.

Revolut has maintained a high tempo of product launches and strategic shifts throughout late 2024. FF News recently covered how the bank is diversifying its ecosystem, including the launch of Revolut Mobile in the German market. The company has also been active in the loyalty space, recently establishing a partnership with Eurostar to allow customers to convert RevPoints into rail travel. On the regulatory front, the firm has been vocal about competition, notably backing the CMA’s move to open Apple’s NFC access to UK fintechs. Additionally, Revolut has expanded its social impact initiatives through a collaboration with the International Rescue Committee to facilitate global humanitarian donations.

This partnership is a direct challenge to the dominance of traditional card networks in the high-frequency delivery sector. By steering users toward Revolut Pay’s A2A infrastructure, Just Eat can potentially reduce its reliance on interchange-heavy card rails while offering a smoother biometric experience. The move puts pressure on legacy digital wallets that still rely on card-behind-the-scenes models. However, the long-term success of this integration hinges on whether consumers are willing to shift their habitual checkout behavior for the sake of loyalty points. It raises a broader question for the industry: can A2A payments finally achieve mass-market ubiquity by piggybacking on the "super-app" loyalty ecosystems?

Original Press Release

Revolut, the global bank with over 80 million customers and over 850,000 business customers, has today announced a strategic payments partnership with Just Eat Takeaway.com, a global leader in on-demand delivery with 362,000 partners and millions of users worldwide. Just Eat customers can now select Revolut Pay for a one-click checkout experience directly within the app in the UK and Ireland.

To mark the launch, users can earn a 5x RevPoints boost on every Just Eat purchase between 28 October and 27 December 2026 in the UK and Ireland. Following the promotional window, customers will retain a 2x points boost on every purchase, alongside the ability to instantly redeem RevPoints for cash discounts at the Just Eat app and web checkout. RevPoints T&Cs apply.

By selecting Revolut Pay, customers no longer need to manually input card details as direct account-to-account (A2A) transfers deliver transaction speed and reliability, allowing users to authorise restaurant and grocery orders instantly using biometric authentication (Face ID or fingerprint recognition) through their Revolut account.

"This partnership brings together two platforms with a shared focus on convenience and customer experience. By integrating Revolut Pay into Just Eat, we're making everyday purchases faster and more rewarding, giving customers the opportunity to earn extra RevPoints on every order and seamlessly redeem them for instant discounts at checkout. We're excited about the potential to build on this partnership and bring even more value to our customers." said Alex Codina, General Manager of Merchant Acquiring at Revolut.

"We're always looking for ways to make ordering from Just Eat faster and more rewarding. Revolut Pay removes a step from checkout, and the RevPoints boost gives customers something back on every order. This is two of Europe's leading technology companies building a better checkout together, and we see real scope to expand the partnership. Building a better checkout together is good news for consumers." said Michiel Verberg, Head of Global Strategic Partnerships at Just Eat Takeaway.com.
