 SINGAPORE , Oct. 8, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Anyflo, a stablecoin payments orchestration service built to make new payment rails work for enterprises, has today emerged from stealth at Sui Basecamp during TOKEN2049 Singapore.
Incubated within Mysten Labs, Anyflo was founded by CEO Lola Oyelayo-Pearson, former GM Product at Mysten. Anyflo is built on the premise that stablecoin payment rails need to become more accessible to the wider business world beyond crypto . Anyflo acts as an orchestration layer, offering access to stables across multiple chains, while handling the complexity of blockchain networks, liquidity and routing. Anyflo helps its customers stay focused on what matters to them: business outcomes.
The company enters the market bolstered by the acqui-hire of Native. Prior to acquisition, Native raised just under USD 2 million, built a market-ready Bitcoin lending platform on Sui and ranked among the top five out of 630 projects at Sui Overflow 2025. Native co-founder Robert Zaremba and team bring existing engineering expertise, cross-chain IP and a readiness for scale into the business.
 Lola Oyelayo-Pearson, Founder and CEO of Anyflo, said: "Most businesses aren't asking for stablecoins . They're asking how to reduce the cost of moving money, protect margins and build better experiences for their customers. The rails may be changing, but the business challenges haven't. Anyflo is built to meet customers where they are. Our job is to abstract away the ego, complexity and hype, and make the new payment rails make business sense."
The move brings together Lola's experience building products at Shopify Money, Capital One and Mysten Labs, the original contributor to Sui, with Native's blockchain infrastructure expertise. Anyflo is entering private alpha in October, with a production preview and liquidity onboarding, followed by beta in Q1 2027.
Embedded finance is changing who moves money. Marketplaces, neobanks, payroll and other businesses are increasingly building payments directly into their products, meaning the economics of moving money now sit inside the business model. Anyflo sees the missing layer as orchestration. The goal is to make an increasingly complex payment infrastructure work in the context of the business using it.
In addition to incubating Anyflo, Mysten Labs has invested USD 1 million in the company. The relationship gives Anyflo early backing from the team behind Sui, along with access to deep blockchain infrastructure expertise, built for mass adoption. 
 Kevin Boon, President of Mysten Labs, said: "Anyflo was born from the combination of deep technical expertise and a clear understanding of what businesses actually need, based on hard-won, first-hand experience. Lola and the Native team are building around a real challenge: making blockchain-based payments work for businesses without requiring them to become blockchain experts."
 About Anyflo 
Anyflo is a payments infrastructure provider that allows enterprises to make cross-border payments using stablecoins . Originally incubated within Mysten Labs, the team behind the Sui blockchain, Anyflo abstracts away the complexity of chains, liquidity and routing so customers can focus on the business outcomes they want to achieve. For more information, visit https://anyflo.io/
