 SINGAPORE , Oct. 8, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Anyflo, a stablecoin payments orchestration service built to make new payment rails work for enterprises, has today emerged from stealth at Sui Basecamp during TOKEN2049 Singapore.
Incubated within Mysten Labs, Anyflo was founded by CEO Lola Oyelayo-Pearson, former GM Product at Mysten. Anyflo is built on the premise that stablecoin payment rails need to become more accessible to the wider business world beyond crypto . Anyflo acts as an orchestration layer, offering access to stables across multiple chains, while handling the complexity of blockchain networks, liquidity and routing. Anyflo helps its customers stay focused on what matters to them: business outcomes.
The company enters the market bolstered by the acqui-hire of Native. Prior to acquisition, Native raised just under USD 2 million, built a market-ready Bitcoin lending platform on Sui and ranked among the top five out of 630 projects at Sui Overflow 2025. Native co-founder Robert Zaremba and team bring existing engineering expertise, cross-chain IP and a readiness for scale into the business.
 Lola Oyelayo-Pearson, Founder and CEO of Anyflo, said: "Most businesses aren't asking for stablecoins . They're asking how to reduce the cost of moving money, protect margins and build better experiences for their customers. The rails may be changing, but the business challenges haven't. Anyflo is built to meet customers where they are. Our job is to abstract away the ego, complexity and hype, and make the new payment rails make business sense."
The move brings together Lola's experience building products at Shopify Money, Capital One and Mysten Labs, the original contributor to Sui, with Native's blockchain infrastructure expertise. Anyflo is entering private alpha in October, with a production preview and liquidity onboarding, followed by beta in Q1 2027.
Embedded finance is changing who moves money. Marketplaces, neobanks, payroll and other businesses are increasingly building payments directly into their products, meaning the economics of moving money now sit inside the business model. Anyflo sees the missing layer as orchestration. The goal is to make an increasingly complex payment infrastructure work in the context of the business using it.
In addition to incubating Anyflo, Mysten Labs has invested USD 1 million in the company. The relationship gives Anyflo early backing from the team behind Sui, along with access to deep blockchain infrastructure expertise, built for mass adoption. 
 Kevin Boon, President of Mysten Labs, said: "Anyflo was born from the combination of deep technical expertise and a clear understanding of what businesses actually need, based on hard-won, first-hand experience. Lola and the Native team are building around a real challenge: making blockchain-based payments work for businesses without requiring them to become blockchain experts."
 About Anyflo 
Anyflo is a payments infrastructure provider that allows enterprises to make cross-border payments using stablecoins . Originally incubated within Mysten Labs, the team behind the Sui blockchain, Anyflo abstracts away the complexity of chains, liquidity and routing so customers can focus on the business outcomes they want to achieve. For more information, visit https://anyflo.io/

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On 2026-10-08, at Sui Basecamp during TOKEN2049 Singapore, **Anyflo** — a stablecoin-payments **orchestration** startup incubated inside **Mysten Labs** (the team behind the Sui blockchain) — exited stealth and simultaneously announced an **acqui-hire of Native**, a Sui-based startup. De-PR'd:
- **"Acquisition" = acqui-hire.** Deal terms undisclosed; the press release and all reprints describe it as a talent/IP pickup, not a priced M&A. Native's **co-founder Robert Zaremba and team** join Anyflo. Native had previously raised "just under $2M", built a Bitcoin-lending platform on Sui, and placed top-5 of 630 projects at Sui Overflow 2025 (a hackathon). → The headline frames a hackathon-stage team as a strategic acquisition; in reality Anyflo is buying a small engineering crew and cross-chain IP.
- **Anyflo itself is pre-product.** It enters **private alpha in October 2026**, a production preview with liquidity onboarding, then **beta in Q1 2027**. Nothing is live; there are no customers, no GMV, no volume numbers. The entire item is a **launch announcement**, not traction.
- **Funding is tiny and captive.** Mysten Labs incubated Anyflo *and* invested **USD 1M**. There is no external/independent round disclosed — the only capital named is from the parent incubator. CEO **Lola Oyelayo-Pearson** is ex-GM Product at Mysten (plus Shopify Money / Capital One background), i.e. this is a Mysten spinout staffed by Mysten alumni.
- **Why framed this way:** stacking "emerges from stealth" + "acquisition" + "TOKEN2049 launch" + a named Sui-ecosystem backer manufactures momentum for what is, financially, a ~$1M incubated pre-alpha. The PR anchors to the flattering "top-5 of 630" hackathon stat rather than to any product metric, because there is no product metric yet. (analysis)

## [1] Competitors / peers
The **enterprise stablecoin orchestration** category is already crowded and partly consolidated:
- **Bridge** (acquired by **Stripe** for ~$1.1B in 2024/25) — issuance + orchestration + on/off-ramps; also the issuer behind **USDsui**, Sui's own native stablecoin ([[Sui Network launches USDsui native stablecoin with Bridge]]). This is directly on Anyflo's home turf: Stripe/Bridge already orchestrate stablecoins *on Sui itself*.
- **BVNK** — licensed fiat↔stablecoin infra; **agreed to be acquired by Mastercard for up to $1.8B (March 2026)**. (web)
- **Conduit** — cross-border B2B stablecoin payouts to LATAM/Africa/SE Asia.
- **Circle** (CPN), **Fireblocks**, **Anchorage**, plus orchestration challengers like **Eco**.
- Corpus peers: **Modern Treasury acquired Beam** specifically for "stablecoin orchestration technology" ([[Modern Treasury acquires stablecoin startup Beam]]); **MoonPay acquired Entendre** to move into on-chain finance ops ([[MoonPay acquires Entendre for on-chain finance operations]]); multiple B2B stablecoin-settlement integrations (dLocal, BitGo, Mesh/Stellar).
- **Position: late and sub-scale.** Incumbents are regulated, funded in the hundreds of millions to billions, and already live. Anyflo is pre-alpha with $1M. **+ Why:** "orchestration" is exactly the layer that platforms (Stripe/Bridge, Mastercard/BVNK) are buying up because it sits closest to the merchant relationship and the routing margin — so the category is consolidating into distribution giants, which makes an independent $1M pre-alpha structurally fragile. (analysis)

## [2] Company history / fit
Anyflo is a **brand-new Mysten Labs spinout** (no prior corpus history; no earlier Anyflo/Native notes exist). Mysten's logic is clear: Sui needs real-world payment use cases to justify USDsui and its payments narrative; spinning out a founder-led orchestration layer, seeding it with $1M and a hackathon-winning Sui-native team, is a cheap option on Sui becoming a B2B-payments chain. **+ Why structured this way:** incubate-and-spin-out lets Mysten keep the payments bet off its own balance sheet while retaining upside and steering ecosystem volume toward Sui — the $1M is effectively ecosystem BD spend, not a conviction growth round. (analysis)

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novelty: low.** "Abstract chains/liquidity/routing so enterprises don't need crypto expertise" is the standard pitch of Bridge, BVNK, Eco, Conduit and Modern Treasury/Beam. No differentiated mechanism is disclosed beyond "multi-chain orchestration." The only arguably distinct angle is Sui-nativeness — but Bridge already serves Sui via USDsui, blunting even that.
- **Traction: none.** Pre-alpha, zero customers/volume disclosed. The $2M and "top-5 of 630" belong to the *acquired* hackathon team, not to a shipped Anyflo product.
- **Who captures the margin:** in stablecoin payments the durable margin accrues to whoever owns the regulated fiat ramps, the issuer relationship, and the merchant/distribution — which is why Stripe bought Bridge and Mastercard is buying BVNK. An unlicensed, pre-alpha orchestration layer sits in the thinnest, most disintermediable slice. (analysis)

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- **Roadmap:** private alpha Oct 2026 → production preview + liquidity onboarding → beta Q1 2027. Re-evaluate on real alpha/beta metrics (named customers, corridors, volume), not on this launch.
- **Backdrop:** US GENIUS Act and surging stablecoin settlement volumes are the tailwind every entrant cites; the same tailwind is driving the consolidation that squeezes small independents.
- **Risks / second-order:** (1) Mysten-only funding = dependency and concentration risk; a future external round is the real signal. (2) Category consolidation (Stripe/Bridge, Mastercard/BVNK) means Anyflo's most likely "exit" is itself being acqui-hired. (3) Single-chain (Sui) origin despite a "multi-chain" claim invites lock-in skepticism. **+ Counterintuitive:** heavy Sui/Mysten backing is presented as strength but makes Anyflo an ecosystem instrument rather than a neutral orchestrator — enterprises choosing orchestration specifically want chain-neutrality. (analysis)

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Sub-sector: stablecoin payments orchestration / cross-border B2B settlement infrastructure. Market: B2B stablecoin volume reached ~$226bn in early 2026, +733% YoY per Rise, but still only ~0.01% of the ~$1.6 quadrillion global B2B payments market (via x.com/rise_pay, as of early 2026) — i.e. almost all penetration is still ahead. Total stablecoin payment volume ran ~$401–527bn in the first 8 months of 2026, +≥42% on 2025, with businesses receiving 58–64% of flows and B2B settlement the single largest lane at ~$137–153bn (per crypto.news / stablecoininsider, as of Sep 2026). Structure: still fragmented and infrastructure-heavy — value sits in the stack layers between chains and bank rails (issuance/custody, FX, liquidity, routing, licensing). Entry barriers are regulation (EMI/payment-institution licences, SOC2/ISO) and banking-partner access far more than tech. Why now: stablecoin cross-border is reportedly growing ~7x faster than fiat rails, and incumbents are buying the stack — Stripe/Bridge (~$1.1bn, Feb 2025), Mastercard/BVNK (up to $1.8bn, Mar 2026) — which both validates the layer and compresses the independent-startup window `(analysis)`.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: annualized settlement volume (TPV), number of chains/corridors, take rate on FX/settlement, licence coverage, named enterprise logos. Key players and basis of competition: BVNK (~$30bn annualized, EMI + SEPA/FPS/SWIFT, now Mastercard-owned), Bridge (issuance+custody+FX via one API, Stripe-owned), Orbital (~$12bn annualized, 80+ currencies, FCA-authorized, SOC2/ISO), OpenFX (~$500m valuation, raised $94m Mar 2026) — competition is on licences + banking rails + volume, not on the "orchestration" concept itself. Anyflo's position: pre-product new entrant (private alpha Oct 2026, beta Q1 2027), Sui-centric, differentiated by multi-chain abstraction for non-crypto enterprises and by Mysten Labs incubation/distribution. Position = niche/catching-up, well behind licensed, volume-bearing peers. Moat `(analysis)`: today it is intangibles/access — founder pedigree (Shopify Money, Capital One, Mysten) and Sui-ecosystem backing — not network effects, scale, or switching costs, none of which exist pre-launch.

**Comps & multiples.** This is a seed-stage acqui-hire with no disclosed revenue/TPV, so EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA and P/E = no data (cannot compute). Funding facts: Mysten Labs invested $1m in Anyflo; acquired target Native had raised just under $2m pre-deal (deal price undisclosed — acqui-hire). Internal comps (base):
- [[BVNK raises $50 million for US expansion]] — direct stablecoin-infra peer, scale-stage.
- [[Orbital partners ClearBank Europe on euro payments and stablecoins]] — orchestration across 80+ currencies, licensed.
- [[OpenFX acquires Embed for EU and UK payments licences]] — stablecoin FX peer buying licences (the barrier Anyflo lacks).
- [[Conduit partners with Brazil's Braza Group for stablecoin FX]] — cross-border stablecoin FX comp.
External anchors: BVNK sold to Mastercard for up to $1.8bn on ~$30bn annualized volume → ~0.06x price/annualized-volume (`$1.8bn / $30bn`, a liquidity-flow ratio, NOT a revenue multiple — use only directionally); OpenFX ~$500m post-money (round valuation, not market cap). Anyflo flag: pre-revenue, pre-licence — **no meaningful multiple; distribution not computed, qualitative only**. Relative to the ~$30bn-volume / ~$1.8bn-deal reference point, Anyflo is several stages earlier and not comparable on economics.

**Risk flags.**
1. Pre-product, pre-licence execution risk — alpha only in Oct 2026, beta Q1 2027, no disclosed TPV/revenue/enterprise logos; peers already carry live volume and regulatory authorizations that take years to obtain. Why: the moat in this sector is licences + banking rails, exactly what Anyflo has not yet shown.
2. Disintermediation / stack-layer squeeze — "orchestration" is the same pitch BVNK/Bridge/Orbital already run at scale, now backed by Stripe and Mastercard balance sheets; a pure orchestration layer risks having its margin captured by better-capitalized, already-licensed incumbents. Why: thin, undifferentiated middleware is the first layer to be absorbed or priced to zero.
3. Ecosystem concentration / dependence on someone else's rails — tightly bound to Mysten/Sui (incubator, $1m investor, Native was a Sui project). Why: single-chain anchoring narrows the enterprise TAM and ties Anyflo's fortunes to Sui adoption and Mysten's continued backing; the PR is also silent on fiat on/off-ramp economics and fraud/settlement liability.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This is a small new-entry/acqui-hire datapoint in an already-consolidating sector, not a re-rating — the signal is that incumbents (Stripe, Mastercard) have set the exit comp high enough to pull capital and talent (incl. Mysten) into stablecoin orchestration. Falsifiable thesis: Anyflo either secures a payment/EMI licence and a named enterprise customer with real TPV by beta (Q1 2027), or it becomes acqui-hire fodder itself for a licensed platform. Watch: whether the Oct alpha converts to disclosed volume + a licence by Q1 2027; absence of either by mid-2027 would confirm the squeeze thesis.
