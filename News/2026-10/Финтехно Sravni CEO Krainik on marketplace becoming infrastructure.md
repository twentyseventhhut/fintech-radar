# Context-enrichment: Финтехно — Sravni CEO Krainik on marketplace becoming infrastructure
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

**FRESHNESS VERDICT: STALE (thesis re-run).** This is a commentary/interview item (TASS, 2026-10-07; via Финтехно), not a new event. Its headline thesis — "we stopped being just a business, we became infrastructure" — is a **repackaged slogan on a 2024-era idea**, not a new, substantive claim. Krainik made the same infrastructure + Open-API framing publicly in **Vedomosti 31.10.2024** (verbatim: _"Под капотом у финансового маркетплейса огромная инфраструктура… Открытые API решают вопрос масштабирования"_) and again in **BFM.ru 09.10.2025** (_"технологии — это основа, через которую мы доставляем до клиента ценность"_, arc "от лидогенератора до финансового маркетплейса"). The only genuinely new element in Oct 2026 is the sharper either/or tagline. Closest prior corpus note is [[Sravni helps microlenders embed mandatory biometric verification]] (2026-06) — a *different event* (biometric ЕБС SaaS product), but it already established the same underlying strategic logic (marketplace → identity/infrastructure toll). This item carries no new numbers, no new product, no new period — it is the PR-level articulation of a strategy already on record. (NB: the TASS text itself could not be independently retrieved — 403/unindexed — so exact quotes are reported-but-unconfirmed.)

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
Sravni's CEO Alexander Krainik gave a TASS interview (2026-10-07) built around the line "we stopped being just a business, we became infrastructure," reflecting on how the role of financial marketplaces is changing. **This is positioning rhetoric, not an announcement** — no new product, round, deal, metric or launch is attached to the item as surfaced. De-PR'd, the substantive backbone behind the slogan (all pre-existing, dated):
- **211-FZ financial-platform-operator licence** — АО "Финансовый Маркетплейс Сравни.ру" in the Bank of Russia register since **26.08.2021** (real "infrastructure" status: users can complete deals on-platform, not just click out).
- **#1 in online OSAGO since 2020** — ~1 in 10 Russian e-OSAGO policies sold via Sravni (RBC, 2024 results).
- **Open-API mortgage with Bank DOM.RF + FinTech Association**, piloted at Finopolis **Oct 2024**; OSAGO White-Label + API partner program (sravni.market).
- **Revenue ≈ RUB 13 bn in 2025 (+18%)**, top-5/top-10 RU fintech (Smart Ranking).

**+ Why structured this way / what it reveals:** The slogan is deployed to ride a **regulatory tailwind** (2026 open-API obligations for insurers; the broader ЕБС/biometric mandates) and to **re-rate Sravni's revenue narrative** from a commodity lead-gen/CPA take-rate toward a "recurring infrastructure" story. Interviews like this are a cheap way to assert a higher-multiple identity without new disclosure — which is exactly why the anti-PR read matters: ~30% of the pitch is real licensed/live product, ~70% is vision packaging of a strategy already stated in 2024–25.

## [1] Competitors / peers
Russian financial-aggregation / 211-FZ platform landscape (dated):
- **Banki.ru** — direct aggregator rival and **traffic leader**: ~20.8M monthly visits (SimilarWeb, Sep 2026) vs Sravni's ~12.1M (~1.7x). Also a registered 211-FZ operator (06.09.2022). Last disclosed revenue RUB 5.37 bn (2022). Owned by Elbrus Capital et al.
- **Finuslugi (MOEX)** — regulated 211-FZ platform; deposits opened +4.3x YoY H1 2025; total RU financial-marketplace turnover ~RUB 500 bn in 2025.
- **First-party ecosystems — the real structural threat:** Sber/Domclick (mortgage origination powerhouse), T-Bank (55.4M clients), Yandex (Pay/Bank/Split). All provide financial products directly rather than neutrally comparing them, and several just entered the **same 211-FZ register in late 2025** (Т-ОФП 21.08.2025; Яндекс Финансовая Платформа 25.12.2025; Avito Finance 27.03.2025).

**+ Why the lay of the land is this way / second-order:** No single dominant *pure-play* aggregator — Banki.ru leads on traffic, Sravni on disclosed revenue (different periods). But the "marketplace becoming infrastructure" move is partly **defensive**: as first-party ecosystems capture customers at the source and themselves register as platforms, a neutral aggregator's lead-gen funnel is squeezed from both ends. Re-branding as "infrastructure" (API/white-label distribution to partners) is an attempt to become a supplier *to* those ecosystems rather than a disintermediated comparison page — but it competes for that role against the ecosystems' own platforms. The slogan doesn't change who owns the customer.

## [2] Company history / fit
Sravni (founded 2009; online-OSAGO pioneer) grew revenue ~RUB 9 bn (2023, +131%) → ~RUB 10 bn (2024, small net loss) → ~RUB 13 bn (2025, +18%); ~23M+ users (2024). Owned by "Восток Инвестиции" (ex-Baring Vostok Russia successor) + Goldman Sachs since Tinkov's 2020 exit. Krainik became CEO **07.08.2024** (ex-Ingosstrakh/AlfaStrakhovanie; built Sravni's #1 insurance channel). The infrastructure framing has been his message since he took over.

**+ Why the company acts this way:** A marketplace lead-gen/CPA take-rate is a **commodity, low-multiple business** exposed to disintermediation by the very ecosystems it routes to. The structural pressure is to convert per-lead economics into **stickier per-transaction / API-distribution revenue** (platform licence, Open-API mortgage, OSAGO white-label, the biometric ЕБС toll from the June note). The "infrastructure" rhetoric is the narrative layer over that real, logically consistent re-rating attempt — but narrative ≠ completed shift.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Novelty of THIS item: essentially nil.** The thesis is on record since Vedomosti 31.10.2024 and BFM 09.10.2025; the licence dates to 2021, live Open-API products to Oct 2024. No new traction number is disclosed in the interview. The *real* assets (211-FZ licence, #1 OSAGO share, Open-API mortgage, ~RUB 13 bn revenue) are genuine but previously known.

**+ Why the value-add (of the thesis) is thin, deeper:** "Infrastructure" is doing marketing work. Where Sravni actually owns a rail it is usually a **thin, contestable toll** (e.g. the ЕБС biometric per-request fee of 4–19 rub, already cut 3–6x, over a rail the state's CBT owns — see June note). In aggregation it still doesn't own the customer; first-party ecosystems do. So the durable question is not "is Sravni infrastructure" but **"which layer captures margin"** — and on the evidence Sravni collects toll/take-rate at layers it doesn't control, while the customer-owning ecosystems control distribution. The interview asserts a re-rate it hasn't yet earned with disclosed, infrastructure-grade numbers.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Watch for substance behind the slogan: disclosed API-distribution revenue mix, named partners consuming Sravni's APIs/white-label at scale, the ЕБС biometric product's first live MFO integrations (Aug-2026 launch, per the June note), and whether 2026 insurer open-API obligations route volume through Sravni. Regulatory backdrop is the key driver (211-FZ platform regime; ЕБС mandates — МФК 01.03.2026, МКК 01.03.2027; open-API for insurers 2026). Risks: first-party-ecosystem disintermediation; the state can own/re-price the rails Sravni tolls; Banki.ru's traffic lead.

**+ Why / second-order:** The same regulatory tailwind Sravni rides also **invites the ecosystems onto the same 211-FZ register** (T-Bank, Yandex, Avito all joined 2025), so the "infrastructure" window is crowding fast. Counterintuitively, reframing as infrastructure makes Sravni *more* dependent on partners/ecosystems and the state (whose rails it resells) — a positioning that sounds like strength but increases structural dependence. For a dated commentary piece with no new data, the signal-to-noise is low.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Sravni sits in Russia's regulated "financial marketplace" (финансовый маркетплейс) segment — aggregators of deposits/loans/insurance that run on the 211-FZ "financial platform operator" (OFP/ОФП) regime (Bank of China России reg. 742-P; min. own funds 100M RUB; NFO as a JSC, cannot combine with a credit institution; entry via the CBR registry — per cbr.ru / consultant.ru / epam.ru). Deal volume across the regulated financial-platform segment reached **498.4bn RUB in 2025, +42% YoY**, with platform users at **8.4M (+58%, +3.1M) as of 31-Dec-2025** (per Interfax, 2026). Note this is the narrow regulated-platform tally — it is far smaller than the broader lead-gen/aggregation market where Sravni itself earns most revenue (microloans + OSAGO), so the 498bn figure understates the economic sector Sravni actually plays in. Structure: fragmented at the front end (several competing aggregators) but the regulated settlement layer is a capital/licence-gated barrier — that gate is exactly what Krainik's "we became infrastructure" framing leans on. Why now: the CBR has been pushing marketplaces up the value chain from lead-gen into licensed settlement and now into embedded compliance rails (e.g. UBS/ЕБС biometric delegation — see [[Sravni helps microlenders embed mandatory biometric verification]]).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: MAU/registered users, deal/transaction volume (GMV-equivalent), take rate on placed products, revenue mix by vertical. Key players and basis of competition (distribution + licence, not price):
- **Banki.ru** — largest RU financial marketplace, 20M+ users; new CEO Roman Halansky (Aug 2025); YandexGPT-based assistant (per tadviser/banki.ru).
- **Finuslugi (MOEX)** — regulator-initiated platform; platform revenue **1.3bn RUB in Q1 2025, +69.5%**, deposit-led; 70+ offers from 22 banks by Dec 2025 (per moex.com / interfax).
- **Yandex** — financial services its fastest-growing line, **26.7bn RUB revenue in Q4 2025 (>2x YoY)**; owns bank + distribution (per alfabank/kommersant).
- **Avito** — added to the CBR OFP registry **27-Mar-2025** (per RBC) — a new entrant with huge classifieds distribution.
- VK — ecosystem distribution play (no comparable disclosed marketplace figure — "no data").
Protagonist position: **niche-leader / catching up on the regulated-rail layer.** Sravni is a top-5 RU fintech by its own framing, MAU 23M+, 2025 revenue **12.98bn RUB (+18.3%)**, mix: microloans 5.78bn, OSAGO 1.17bn, e-OSAGO 1.01bn RUB (per tadviser). Moat `(analysis)`: OFP licence + lending/insurance funnel + the fresh UBS-delegation SaaS = switching/distribution moat on the compliance rail; but it is NOT scale-dominant vs Yandex/Banki.ru on users.

**Comps & multiples.** Internal comps (grep): [[Sravni helps microlenders embed mandatory biometric verification]] (June 2026 — Sravni as OFP selling UBS compliance as SaaS); [[Ozon Bank registers digital financial assets platform with CBR]] (June 2026 — marketplace→regulated-platform adjacency). External: Sravni is **private**, no post-money round disclosed in 2025 → valuation "no data". Revenue is public: Sravni **12.98bn RUB (2025)** vs Finuslugi **~1.3bn RUB Q1-2025 platform revenue** (~5bn RUB annualised, not strictly comparable — Finuslugi is settlement-only, Sravni is lead-gen-heavy). **EV/Revenue, P/E, EV/EBITDA = `[UNSOURCED]`** — no EV, no EBITDA, no market cap for any private peer; multiple distribution NOT computed (fewer than 3 comparable valuations). Qualitative only: Sravni's +18.3% revenue growth lags Finuslugi's +69.5% and Yandex financial +100%+ — i.e. Sravni is the slower-growing incumbent among the names, consistent with its "becoming infrastructure" pivot as a growth-reacceleration bet.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Disintermediation / toll-booth captured upstream** — the UBS biometric rail and settlement layer are controlled by regulator-adjacent operators (CBT/ЦБТ, CBR). Sravni rents, it does not own, the compulsory rail; a cheaper bank-delegated competitor (T-Bank, RSHB) or direct CBR tariff change compresses the "infrastructure" economics it is now branding around.
2. **Distribution asymmetry vs ecosystem players** — Yandex (26.7bn RUB/Q), Avito (new OFP), Banki.ru (20M users) have larger captive funnels; Sravni's 23M MAU and 12.98bn RUB revenue are grown off aggregation, and ecosystem rivals can embed finance at lower CAC, squeezing Sravni's take rate — a margin, not just share, risk.
3. **Revenue concentration + regulatory single-point** — ~45% of 2025 revenue is microloans (5.78bn of 12.98bn); MFO-sector tightening or CBR caps on microlending/aggregator marketing hit the biggest line directly, and the OFP licence makes the whole model regulator-dependent.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` The "marketplace → infrastructure" line is a re-positioning, not a reported re-rating — watch whether it converts into disclosed B2B/SaaS infra revenue (UBS delegation, embedded-finance API fees) as a growing share of the mix. Falsifiable thesis: if Sravni's non-lead-gen (infra/SaaS) revenue is still negligible at its next annual disclosure (early 2027) while Yandex/Finuslugi keep growing 2x+, the "infrastructure" claim is PR, not economics. Trigger to watch: any disclosed MFO client count / per-request UBS tariff, or an OFP-registry move by Avito/VK that out-distributes Sravni.
