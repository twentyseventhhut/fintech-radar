# Botim and Orange Egypt connect UAE remittances to Orange Cash

> [!info] 2026-10-28 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇦🇪 Botim and Orange Egypt connect UAE remittances to Orange Cash wallets. The partnership allows users in the UAE to transfer money directly to Egyptian mobile wallets, connecting more than two million Egyptian Botim users with approximately ten million Orange Cash customers.
