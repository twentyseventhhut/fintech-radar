 MIAMI , Oct. 8, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Securitize, Inc. (NYSE: SECZ ), the leading platform for tokenized assets, today announced the launch of Securitize Stocks , bringing some of the world's most widely held U.S. equities onchain for eligible investors in the United States, European Union (EU) and other permitted jurisdictions worldwide.

 The initial offering on Securitize will include security entitlements to shares of AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOG, TSLA, META, AMZN, NFLX, CRCL, SPCX, MSTR and PLTR . Securitize Stocks will initially launch on Solana and trade on Securitize's registered broker-dealer platform.
Securitize Stocks are also expected to trade on the new 24/7 digital trading venue being developed by the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) , subject to the venue's launch and applicable regulatory and operational requirements. The planned connectivity would create another pathway for tokenized U.S. equities to trade through infrastructure being developed by one of the world's leading exchange operators.

 Unlike products that primarily provide economic exposure to a stock's price, Securitize Stocks are backed 1:1 by underlying shares, structured to preserve the investor rights and economic benefits associated with those shares, and convertible, where available, to direct ownership on the books of the issuer's transfer agent. The offering is designed to accelerate adoption of issuer-sponsored tokenization as issuers increasingly embrace tokenization .
"Tokenized stocks should give investors more than a price on a wrapper that tracks a stock and is only offered offshore," said Carlos Domingo, Chairman and CEO of Securitize . "The opportunity is to bring equities onchain without leaving behind the ownership, investor protections and market infrastructure that make U.S. capital markets work. Securitize Stocks are designed around that principle, while creating a bridge to a future where issuers themselves can participate directly in tokenization ."
 Securitize Stocks give investors: 
 1:1 backing and security entitlements.  Each token is backed one for one by an underlying share and represents a security entitlement under UCC Article 8, preserving applicable rights and economic benefits including dividends and, where the underlying class carries them, voting rights. This sets Securitize Stocks apart from products that are primarily designed to track a stock's performance.
 Global availability.  Securitize Stocks will be available to eligible investors in the U.S., EU and other permitted jurisdictions, subject to applicable securities laws, sanctions requirements and other restrictions.
 No share lending.  Shares backing Securitize Stocks will not be lent out. The tokens are designed to support additional onchain utility over time, including supported lending and collateral markets like Aave.
 A bridge to issuer-sponsored tokenization .  Securitize Stocks use a convertible entitlement token (CET) structure. When a company adopts issuer-sponsored tokenization , investors can convert their security entitlements into actual shares recorded on the company's register, through Securitize's partnerships with leading transfer agents.
 Compatibility with evolving U.S. market structure . The offering is designed to be  consistent with SEC guidance on tokenized securities. Secondary market trading is offered for Securitize Stocks in a regulated manner through its broker-dealer, without the need for exemptions. Securitize Stocks are also expected to be eligible for trading on  Tokenized Securities Venues (TSVs) as contemplated by the SEC's Innovation Exemption.
Securitize Stocks are also expected to be available on the OKXICE Tokenized Securities Venue (TSV), which combines OKX's blockchain technology and global reach with the regulatory experience and institutional connectivity of Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), parent company of the New York Stock Exchange. The TSV will bring tokenized U.S. equities onchain in a compliant, permissioned environment, with plans to unlock 24/7 trading, instant settlement and self-custody of exchange-listed securities for a global audience.

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On **2026-10-08** Securitize (NYSE: SECZ) launched **Securitize Stocks** — 12 tokenized US equities on **Solana**, each a **convertible entitlement token (CET)** backed 1:1 by a real underlying share, settling in **USDC**. Stocks: AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOG, TSLA, META, AMZN, NFLX, CRCL, SPCX, MSTR, PLTR.

De-PR'd live-vs-announced split (the crux):
- **LIVE now:** secondary trading on Securitize's own SEC-registered **broker-dealer** (Securitize Markets, LLC), via a Solana **PropAMM** with **Jump Trading** as market maker — but **extended hours only, not 24/7**, gated by KYC/AML for eligible US/EU/permitted investors.
- **NOT launched (forward-looking):** 24/7 trading; the **NYSE 24/7 digital venue** ("subject to the venue's launch"); the **OKXICE TSV**. The release itself concedes "The NYSE and OKXICE venues have not launched." The Aave collateral/lending and Ripple Prime integrations are **aspirational quotes only** ("plans to", "excited about the potential"), not live.

**Why structured this way:** the headline leans on the NYSE/OKXICE/Aave names to borrow institutional credibility for what is, today, extended-hours trading on Securitize's own venue with one market maker. The real, shippable product is narrower than the press frame. The "no need for exemptions" claim applies **only** to this broker-dealer path; the 24/7 TSV routes explicitly rely on the SEC's Innovation Exemption — so the two regulatory claims in the same release point in opposite directions and should not be conflated.

## [1] Competitors / peers
Tokenized-equity rivals (internal corpus is deep here): Backed/xStocks [[Kraken acquires Backed Finance for tokenization]], Ondo [[Ondo launches 100+ tokenized US stocks across chains]] / [[Ondo Finance launches 24 7 minting for tokenized US stocks]], Robinhood [[Robinhood launches Robinhood Chain Mainnet with stock tokens]] / [[Analysis Why Robinhood and OpenAI clashed over onchain stock tokens]], Coinbase [[Coinbase launches 24 7 US stock trading with Yahoo Finance]], Gemini [[Gemini launches tokenized stocks in Europe]], Nasdaq [[SEC approves Nasdaq tokenized securities trading]], Bullish [[Bullish wins Gibraltar approval for tokenized securities trading]].

The PR's central contrast — "real shares with rights/dividends/voting + convertible to direct ownership" vs "offshore price-tracking wrappers" — is accurate **only against Robinhood** (economic exposure, no legal ownership/voting). **xStocks, Ondo and Dinari also use 1:1 real-share backing.** So 1:1 backing is NOT the differentiator. (analysis) Securitize's genuine, verifiable edge is being **onshore US-regulated as both broker-dealer AND transfer agent**, with a UCC Art. 8 entitlement that is **convertible to direct on-register ownership** — a feature aimed at issuer-sponsored tokenization, not at retail.

Traction reality (Sept 2026, CoinDesk report): on-chain tokenized-equity volume hit **$15.6B/month**, but is dominated by **Robinhood ($6.57B, 42%) and Binance bStocks ($5.42B, 34.7%)**; xStocks $2.11B, Coinbase $804M. By AUM, Securitize leads tokenized-equity issuance (~$1.62B) but that is **issuance, not trading** — Securitize is asset-heavy, activity-light vs the exchanges.

**Why it acts this way:** (analysis) Securitize's moat has been fund tokenization (BUIDL etc.), a commodity-ish infrastructure layer. Tokenized equities + the transfer-agent/convertibility angle is the move from "we mint tokens for asset managers" to "we are the on-chain plumbing issuers themselves use" — chasing a defensible position (issuer-sponsored tokenization) rather than competing on retail trading volume it cannot win against Robinhood/Binance.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
What's actually new vs the crowded field: the **CET + transfer-agent convertibility to direct ownership** path, wired to the planned NYSE Digital Trading Platform via the existing MOU. That is a real, issuer-sponsored structural bet, not just another xStocks clone. But as a **launch**, traction is thin: one market maker (Jump), extended hours, no 24/7, key venues (NYSE/OKXICE) not live, Aave/Ripple Prime not live. No launch volumes disclosed.

**Who captures the margin:** (analysis) if tokenized equities commoditize into cheap 24/7 trading, the value accrues to the venue/liquidity layer (Robinhood, Binance, OKXICE), not the issuer of the wrapper. Securitize's hedge is to be the **regulated transfer agent and broker-dealer** — the rails and the conversion chokepoint — so it earns regardless of which venue wins. The bet breaks if the SEC Innovation Exemption lets exchanges self-serve tokenization without an issuer-side transfer agent, disintermediating Securitize's chosen niche.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Regulatory backdrop is the real driver: the SEC **issued the Innovation Exemption on 2026-09-17** (two conditional 5-year exemptions: a TSV exemption from the "exchange" definition for AMM-based tokenized NMS stock, plus a dealer-registration exemption for AMM liquidity providers; conditions include permissioned access, US operator, OFAC compliance, 30-day pre-launch notice, and volume caps). It had been reported "delayed indefinitely" on 2026-08-18 before issuance. **No TSV is operational yet.** [[OKXICE notifies SEC of 24 7 tokenized securities venue]] (filed ~Oct 5) is the first filing under it — same infrastructure Securitize Stocks plans to trade on, so the two stories are linked but distinct.

Sentiment: real momentum but contested. (fact) IMF warned tokenized stocks are ~1.5x more volatile; concentration is severe (xStocks HHI ~2,565, ~3 tickers ≈ 78% of activity); off-hours liquidity is thin; third-party tokenized shares carry no SIPC coverage and contested enforceability of shareholder rights. **Counterintuitive second-order:** (hypothesis) the very institutional names Securitize invokes (NYSE/ICE, OKX) are building their own 24/7 venue — if OKXICE/NYSE succeed, Securitize's broker-dealer trading venue becomes redundant and its value collapses back to the transfer-agent/issuance layer. The partnership is also the competitive threat.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Securitize sits at the intersection of RWA tokenization (its core book) and the newer, much smaller tokenized-equities subsegment this launch targets. Total tokenized RWA onchain (ex-stablecoins) was ~$31bn as of Jul-2026 and ~$46.2bn across 36 chains later in 2026 per RWA.xyz (methodology/date-dependent), up from ~$8bn at the start of 2024 — but concentrated in private credit and US Treasuries, not equities. The tokenized-*equities* slice is tiny: ~$300M onchain AUM as of Q1-2026 (per eco.com). So the headline "RWA is huge" does not transfer to this product — equities are the frontier, not the base. Structure: issuer side still fragmented and early; the gating barrier is now regulatory clarity, not tech. "Why now": the SEC's Innovation Exemption (17-Sep-2026) created a temporary 5-year framework exempting Tokenized Securities Venues (TSVs) from the "exchange" definition and certain AMM liquidity providers from the "dealer" definition — enabling onchain trading of tokenized NMS stocks. Securitize explicitly frames this launch as "consistent with SEC guidance" and TSV-eligible; the regulatory window is the catalyst.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: tokenized AUM, onchain trading volume, holder count, token-catalog breadth, and (for Securitize-the-issuer) platform revenue. Key players in tokenized US equities (2026): Backed Finance / Kraken xStocks (~$25bn cumulative volume since Jun-2025, >$3.5bn onchain, >80k holders, ~100 names targeting 500+ by end-2026); Robinhood (200+ US stock/ETF tokens for EU/EEA, migrating to its own Arbitrum-Orbit "Robinhood Chain"); Ondo Finance (260+ tokens across Solana/Ethereum/BNB, onchain proxy voting via Broadridge); Dinari. Basis of competition: catalog breadth, liquidity/spreads, and — crucially — *legal structure* (economic-exposure wrappers vs. real security entitlements). Securitize's differentiator (analysis): 1:1 share backing + UCC Article 8 security entitlements + a convertible-entitlement-token (CET) path to registered ownership + a US-regulated broker-dealer wrapper (no exemptions needed for secondary trading) + NYSE/ICE (OKXICE) and NYSE 24/7-venue connectivity via the Mar-2026 MOU [[NYSE and Securitize sign MOU on tokenized securities]]. Position: catching up on *catalog/volume* (12 names vs. peers' 100-260+) but ahead on *regulatory/ownership rigor* and incumbent-exchange distribution. Moat (analysis): regulated digital-securities infrastructure in both US and EU, transfer-agent partnerships, and BlackRock/Apollo/KKR/VanEck issuer relationships — intangible/regulatory moat, not scale.

**Comps & multiples.** Securitize (NYSE: SECZ) post-SPAC [[Securitize to go public via $1.25B SPAC]] [[Securitize files for SPAC merger after 841% revenue jump]]: market cap ~$2.09-2.72bn (source-dependent, Oct-2026); FY2025 revenue $62.2M (vs. $18.6M FY2024); Q2-2026 revenue $14.4M, **-5% YoY** (growth stalled after the 841%/2025 surge); tokenized AUM ~$4.3bn (+9-16%), plus $24.3bn AUA in fund services. P/S ≈ $2.09bn / $62.2M ≈ **34x** (low end) to $2.72bn / $62.2M ≈ **44x** (high end), trailing. Even on an optimistic ~$60M NTM run-rate (Q2 annualized ≈ $57.6M), P/S stays ~35-47x — rich vs. a sanity EV/Rev ~0.5-20x band, and the usual growth justification is weakening (Q2 -5% YoY). No public, fully-comparable listed pure-play tokenization peer with verifiable multiples → distribution not computed; qualitative only. Private peers (Backed, Dinari, Ondo) trade as tokens/rounds, not P/S-comparable equity → [UNSOURCED]. Flag: **rich** on revenue, with decelerating growth = the outlier worth watching.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Valuation vs. decelerating revenue.** ~34-44x trailing P/S while Q2-2026 revenue fell -5% YoY — a high-growth multiple without current high growth; a re-rate risk if tokenized-equity adoption doesn't convert to platform revenue.
2. **"Announced" ≠ "live".** The release itself discloses the NYSE and OKXICE venues *have not launched* and are subject to regulatory/venue approval; only the Solana broker-dealer path is live at 12 tickers, extended-hours (not yet 24/7). Much of the thesis is roadmap, not product.
3. **Regulatory dependence + reversibility.** The SEC Innovation Exemption is *temporary* (5-year, conditional); the whole TSV/AMM pathway can tighten or lapse. Securitize also warns it may withdraw listings "as needed or required," and issuers have not sponsored/endorsed the tokens.
4. **Catalog/liquidity lag + rails dependence.** 12 names vs. peers' 100-260+; launch liquidity leans on a single market maker (Jump) and a PropAMM, and distribution depends on ICE/NYSE and OKX rails Securitize does not control — margin/value could be captured by the venue layer.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is less a volume play than a bet that *issuer-sponsored, ownership-grade* tokenization (CET-to-register conversion, UCC Art. 8) beats the exposure-wrapper model as the SEC framework matures — a potential standards-setting entry rather than a re-rate (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: if, within ~2-3 quarters, (a) the NYSE 24/7 / OKXICE TSV venues go live and (b) real issuers opt into sponsored tokenization + convert entitlements, Securitize's regulated moat compounds; trigger/catalyst = first issuer-sponsored conversion and TSV go-live. What breaks it: venues slip indefinitely, catalog/volume stays a fraction of xStocks/Ondo, and platform revenue keeps decelerating — in which case the rich multiple is unsupported.
