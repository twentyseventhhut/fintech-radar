# Pockit books nearly £4M in frozen customer funds as income

> [!info] 2026-10-28 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇬🇧 Pockit records nearly £4 million in frozen customer funds as income. According to The Times, the UK FinTech recognised unclaimed balances from closed accounts linked mainly to suspected financial crime or inactivity. Pockit defended the practice as lawful, saying claims expire after six years, while a consumer advocate called for an FCA review.
