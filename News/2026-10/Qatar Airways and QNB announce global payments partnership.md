Qatar Airways, QNB Group announce strategic global payments partnership
The collaboration will integrate QNB’s payment acceptance expertise with Qatar Airways’ digital sales channels
Doha: Qatar Airways and QNB Group have entered into a strategic global payments partnership that brings together the international reach and capabilities of two of Qatar’s most recognised institutions.
The collaboration will integrate QNB’s payment acceptance expertise with Qatar Airways’ digital sales channels to support secure, efficient and seamless transactions for customers across the airline’s global network.
The partnership agreement was signed by Qatar Airways Group Chief Executive Officer, Hamad Al-Khater, and Group Chief Executive Officer of QNB Group, Abdulla Mubarak Al Khalifa, in the presence of senior representatives from both organisations.
The collaboration reflects a shared commitment to innovation, resilience and the continued development of Qatar’s digital payments ecosystem. It also demonstrates how leading Qatari institutions can combine complementary strengths to support secure, scalable and globally connected commerce.
With both organisations operating across international markets, the partnership will support cross-border commerce and strengthen the infrastructure of digital transactions across Qatar Airways’ global sales network.
Under the partnership, QNB Group will process digital payments for Qatar Airways’ e-commerce transactions, while both organisations work together to enhance transaction visibility, operational efficiency and the overall payment experience across the airline’s digital sales channels.
The arrangement combines Qatar Airways’ global customer reach and digital commerce platform with QNB Group’s capabilities and payments expertise.
Qatar Airways Group Chief Executive Officer, Hamad Al-Khater, said: “As the aviation industry continues to evolve, Qatar Airways remains focused on building the digital capabilities required to support a modern, agile and globally connected business. Our partnership with QNB Group represents an important step in that journey, enabling us to further enhance the technology and financial infrastructure that underpins our operations. By combining our respective strengths, we are advancing our digital transformation agenda, increasing efficiency across our ecosystem and reinforcing our position as one of the world’s leading digitally enabled airlines.”
Commenting on the partnership and its broader significance, Group Chief Executive Officer of QNB Group, Abdulla Mubarak Al Khalifa, said: “This partnership reflects QNB’s continued focus on building globally connected financial capabilities rooted in Qatar. By combining QNB Group’s payments expertise with Qatar Airways’ international reach, we are strengthening the infrastructure required to support secure, scalable and seamless digital commerce, while contributing to the continued evolution of Qatar’s digital economy.
“It further reinforces QNB’s position as a leading payments innovator with a growing international footprint and the capabilities to support commerce across global markets. The Group continues to invest in next-generation acquiring capabilities, e-commerce infrastructure, payment technologies and advanced payment security frameworks the collaboration creates a powerful platform capable of supporting growing digital transaction volumes while delivering a secure, reliable, and frictionless payment experience for customers worldwide.”
Beyond payment processing, the partnership establishes a framework for continued collaboration across digital commerce, customer experience, emerging technologies, and evolving payment solutions. Both organisations will explore opportunities that support their shared international ambitions, while responding to the evolving needs of travellers.
