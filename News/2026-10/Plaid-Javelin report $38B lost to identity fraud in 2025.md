# Plaid-Javelin report: $38B lost to identity fraud in 2025

> [!info] 2026-10-07 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 📑 Plaid’s latest report with Javelin puts some striking numbers behind the fraud problem: $38B was lost to identity fraud and scams in 2025, while new-account fraud victims increased 31%. And in 56% of scam cases, people lost data rather than money. The report looks at why fraud can leave clues across onboarding, logins and transactions - and what happens when those signals are connected earlier. See how Plaid and Javelin are thinking about detecting fraud risk earlier.Connected Signals Catch Fraud Early
