Seeds Fincap raises Rs 100 Cr in Series B led by Michael & Susan Dell Foundation
 
Gurugram-based NBFC Seeds Fincap has raised more than Rs 100 crore ($10.4 million) in a Series B round led by Michael & Susan Dell Foundation.
 
 Follow Us
 Gurugram-based NBFC Seeds Fincap has raised more than Rs 100 crore ($10.4 million) in a Series B round led by Michael & Susan Dell Foundation. Existing investors Z47 and Lok Capital, along with Norinchukin Capital and Alteria Capital, also participated in the round. 
 Last year, Seeds Fincap raised Rs 50 crore ($5.5 million) in a round led by Z47 and Lok Capital. In July 2024, it raised over $8.5 million in a Series A round led by Lok Capital and Matrix Partners India, now Z47. 
 The company plans to use the fresh capital to expand into new markets and strengthen its branch network, technology and risk management capabilities. It is targeting Rs 1,000 crore in AUM by March 2027 while continuing to focus on credit assessment and portfolio quality. 
 Founded in 2021 by Subhash Chandra Acharya, Avishek Sarkar and Sumeet Dhall, Seeds Fincap provides credit to underserved individual and MSME borrowers. It offers loans ranging from Rs 50,000 to Rs 10 lakh and uses a cash flow based approach to assess borrowers. 
 Seeds Fincap closed FY25 and FY26 profitably and disbursed around Rs 620 crore in FY26. Its AUM stood at Rs 722 crore as of August 2026. The company has expanded to 164 branches and has more than 70,000 active members. 
 Seeds primarily serves nano enterprises and small businesses, where traditional credit assessment may not fully capture business cash flows. The company combines technology with field teams across sourcing, onboarding, credit assessment, documentation, collections and portfolio monitoring to provide loans to small business owners.
