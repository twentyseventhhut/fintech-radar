# Target Global invests in Spanish healthtech Health Force

> [!info] 2026-10-07 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Русский венчур

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Русский венчур] Target Global инвестировал в испанский healthtech‑стартап Health Force. В раунде на €4,2 млн также участвовали LUMO Labs, при участии Step Venture, Next Tier Ventures и Calm/Storm.

The operating system behind the agents

Evolving AI layer for your own hospital operations

How every agent does the work

Raw data becomes structured output. Claims formatted for submission. Reports compiled for registries. Trial data prepared for the sponsor's EDC system.
Agents carry out the work across your systems and external ones. Submitting claims. Filing reports. Updating records and schedules. The same work your team does, at scale.
