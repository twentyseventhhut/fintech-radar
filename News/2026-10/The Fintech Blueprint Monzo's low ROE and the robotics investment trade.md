# The Fintech Blueprint: Monzo's low ROE and the robotics investment trade

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[The Fintech Blueprint] The first is measured by return on equity (ROE), which is how much profit a company makes for every dollar its shareholders have invested. Monzo sits at a rather low 7%, which is even below incumbents Lloyds and Barclays (each around 10%) and a fraction of Revolut’s at 35%.Source: a16z, Blueprint analysis

[The Fintech Blueprint] There is still ample room, but investors would be less jumpy if Monzo can repeat its playbook abroad. Earlier this year, the company exited the US and cut 50 jobs (source). It initially filed an application for a Banking Charter in 2020 but withdrew it a year later. This ultimately led to shake-ups at the leadership level with then-CEO TS Anil stepping down. The company’s focus is now on Europe. It recently received an EU banking licence from the Central Bank of Ireland and has started serving both Ireland and Spain.Source: Company data, Blueprint analysis

[The Fintech Blueprint] We examine whether robotics is following the AI investment blueprint, from components and training data to industrial and household applications. We estimate that robotics-related companies represent roughly $3.8 trillion in value, although most of that sits inside large technology and legacy industrial companies rather than pure-play robot makers.
