# Hong Kong gold token project GoldZip adopts Chainlink CCIP

> [!info] 2026-10-28 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇭🇰 Hong Kong gold token project adopts Chainlink CCIP for cross-chain transfers. GoldZip is integrating Chainlink’s cross-chain interoperability protocol to enable blockchain transfers of its XGZ token, backed by one gram of 99.99% pure gold per token. The initiative is being developed with Midas Labs.
