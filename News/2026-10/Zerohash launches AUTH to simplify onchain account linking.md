zerohash Launches AUTH to Simplify Onchain Movements
On the page 
Get in touch
AUTH by zerohash turns onchain transfers into simple account-to-account experiences through 600+ connections.
 Money 20/20 USA, LAS VEGAS — Oct. 19, 2026 — zerohash , the leading onchain infrastructure provider, today announced the launch of AUTH by zerohash* , a paradigm-shifting onchain authentication and account-linking product. AUTH solves for customer usability of onchain movements including for crypto, stablecoin and tokenized securities. Customers do not have to copy alphanumeric addresses or make chain decisions. 
As onchain infrastructure scales across every asset class and moves into every mainstream financial product, usability and risk remain the biggest barriers to adoption. AUTH by zerohash solves for:
 Usability: While onchain rails are incredibly powerful, transfers rely on wallet addresses, chain selection, and manual workflows that create user friction. By structuring onchain asset movement around accounts instead of wallet addresses, AUTH lets users link self-custody and custodial accounts directly, transferring onchain assets without needing to use a raw wallet address or making a chain selection.
 Trust: AUTH replaces raw addresses with authenticated, user-permissioned accounts.
 Control: Every platform has their own risk appetite. AUTH provides the flexibility for partners to configure transfer types, onchain assets and enabled accounts.  Configurations put AUTH clients in control of the experience. 
AUTH offers 600+ connections across custodial and self-custodial accounts, representing approximately 250 million users across 190+ countries, including platforms such as Robinhood, Gemini, Coinbase (Exchange and Wallet), MetaMask , Phantom, and more. 
To lead this next phase of growth, zerohash has also named Ryan Beaudry as General Manager of AUTH. Most recently, Ryan was Executive Vice President of Global Product & Operations for Open Finance at Mastercard, where he scaled open finance across nearly 100 million connected accounts and 10,000+ applications, powering 15 billion API calls serviced globally annually. 
“Onchain rails scale when users stop thinking about the underlying technology. Usability, trust and control is the inflection point for mass adoption,” said Ryan Beaudry, GM of AUTH. “AUTH removes the sharp edges from onchain transfers and replaces them with experiences people already trust.”
 Deep Dive: How AUTH Works 
Once an account is linked, AUTH handles authentication and settlement automatically, with no wallet addresses or blockchain selection. Transfers follow a user-friendly, secure flow:
 Select connection: User selects a custodial or self-custodial account to link.
 Authenticate: Login with account credentials and multi-factor authentication.
 Transfer: Assets move directly onchain.
 Near-Instant movement: Onchain transfer completes.
