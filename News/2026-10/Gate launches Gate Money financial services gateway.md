Gate Launches Gate Money, a New Financial Services Gateway Connecting Assets, Funds, and Payments
 
On October 7, Dr. Han, Gate Founder and CEO, officially announced the launch of Gate Money during his keynote speech at the TOKEN2049 conference. Centered around the core philosophy of "One Gate, Everything Money," Gate Money leverages Gate's existing suite of diversified financial services to create a globally integrated financial platform. By connecting digital assets, equities, payments, and asset management under a single account, it seamlessly covers holding, receiving, transferring, paying, exchanging, and earning yield on assets—marking Gate's expansion from digital asset trading into a broader range of financial services. Users can now activate Gate Money with a single click by updating their Gate App to version 8.39.0.
 Diverse Assets, One-Stop Management 
Gate Money enables users to hold multiple fiat currencies and digital assets within the same account system, with a consolidated view of asset balances and fund movements. The platform supports fiat deposits and withdrawals as well as on-chain deposits and transfers. Fiat accounts can be managed independently by currency, while eligible users can also upgrade to obtain a bank account under the same name, allowing users to receive funds and make withdrawals using their personal account information, providing greater convenience for everyday and cross-border fund transfers.
Building on this foundation, Gate already provides access to traditional financial assets such as stocks and continues to expand its range of supported asset classes. For users holding different types of assets, Gate Money brings these services into a clearer account structure, making asset and fund management more centralized and convenient.
 Connecting Global Payments for Everyday Spending 
Gate Money also connects everyday payments more closely with the account experience. Through Gate Card, users can make online and offline purchases using fiat currencies or digital assets, with support for digital wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay. In certain countries and regions, users can also access ATM withdrawals and local QR-code payments to accommodate different payment methods across markets.
From online shopping to in-person purchases, users can choose the payment method that best fits their needs, making the assets held in their accounts more accessible for everyday spending.
 Flexible Allocation for More Asset Possibilities 
Beyond asset holding and payments, Gate Money also offers features including Convert and Earn. Convert enables users to exchange between different assets, while Earn provides a range of wealth management and earning products. Eligible balances such like USDT can earn up to 3.6% without a lock-up period, giving users additional options while maintaining access to their funds.
For assets that need to remain liquid, users can convert them as needed. For temporarily idle assets, users can select suitable earning options based on applicable product terms, providing more flexibility in how they manage and use their funds.
 Expanding Financial Services Across More Real-Life Scenarios 
Gate Money brings together Gate's product capabilities across digital assets, stocks, payments, and asset management, integrating asset holding, receiving funds, transfers, payments, conversion, and earning into a single account. This further connects asset management, fund flows, and everyday spending within the Gate platform.
From cash and digital assets to stocks and other financial products, and from asset management to everyday spending, Gate Money brings a broader range of financial needs into one platform, driving the shift from trading and asset management to payments, fund management, and daily financial needs, further expanding Gate's financial service boundaries.
 Global Compliance Framework and Financial Infrastructure 
In terms of global financial service capabilities, Gate continuously builds a compliant foundation covering multi-asset services, capital flows, and payment scenarios through financial, payment, and virtual asset licenses and authorizations obtained across key jurisdictions, providing underlying financial infrastructure support for Gate Money.
Gate’s entities have obtained a MiCA Crypto Asset Service Provider (CASP) license and a PSD2 Payment Institution license in Malta, a full Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) operating license from VARA in Dubai, and registered with AUSTRAC as a digital currency exchange service provider in Australia. Gate also operates through local compliant entities in key markets such as Japan, covering 81 regulated jurisdictions globally. These qualifications encompass crypto asset trading and custody, digital currency services, and payments, establishing a robust compliance foundation for Gate Money to connect multi-asset management, fiat accounts, fund receipts and transfers, and everyday payments.
Meanwhile, Gate has established partnerships with a range of international payment institutions, banks, custody providers, and clearing institutions, covering multiple areas of financial services including payments, funds, asset custody, and clearing, and providing foundational support for business operations across different markets.
As different asset classes become increasingly integrated with payment and everyday spending scenarios, Gate will continue to enhance its related products and services, providing users with a more convenient and flexible financial experience. By further connecting digital assets, traditional finance, and everyday financial needs, Gate ai ms to create more possibilities for innovation in financial service models.

## Контекст

### [0] What happened (one line)
On 2026-10-07, Gate (formerly Gate.io) founder/CEO Dr. Han announced **Gate Money** at TOKEN2049 — a "financial services gateway" unifying digital assets, equities, fiat, payments, and asset management under one Gate App account (v8.39.0), under the slogan "One Gate, Everything Money." (source: [gate.com announcement 102095](https://www.gate.com/announcements/article/102095); [TheBlock press release](https://www.theblock.co/press-releases/gate-launches-gate-money-a-new-financial-services-gateway-connecting-assets-funds-and-payments-417927); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/gate-launches-gate-money-a-new-financial-services-gateway-connecting-assets-funds-and-payments/))

### [1] What it actually is (mechanics)
- **One account, many asset classes.** Holds multiple fiat currencies + digital assets; supports fiat deposit/withdrawal and on-chain deposit/transfer; fiat accounts managed per-currency. Gate already lists stocks/ETFs/gold/metals/indices/forex/commodities (12,800+ stock assets per Gate).
- **Named bank account.** Eligible users can upgrade to a personal-name bank (electronic) account for everyday and cross-border transfers — i.e. receive/withdraw via own account details.
- **Spending via Gate Card.** Online/offline purchases in fiat or crypto; Apple Pay / Google Pay support; ATM withdrawals and local QR payments in some markets. The companion **Visa** crypto-linked card (see [[Gate partners Visa for crypto-linked card in 40+ countries]]) converts crypto→fiat at point of sale across 40+ markets; Instant Spend supports USDT/BTC/ETH/GT.
- **Convert + Earn.** Asset conversion; Earn products with eligible balances (e.g. USDT) earning up to 3.6% no-lock-up.
- **Compliance backbone.** Gate cites MiCA CASP + PSD2 Payment Institution license (Malta), VARA VASP (Dubai), AUSTRAC registration (Australia), local entities in Japan — claiming coverage of 81 regulated jurisdictions. 61M+ users claimed.

### [2] Why it matters / significance
This is Gate repositioning from a crypto exchange into a **crypto super-app / neobank-style everyday-finance platform** — the dominant 2026 strategic theme across major CEXes. It is the umbrella brand that packages Gate's payments, banking, cards, and yield into one consumer surface, deepening user lock-in and fiat on/off ramps.

### [3] Context / prior art (internal + external)
- **Internal / Gate's own trajectory:** builds on [[Gate Pay launches “National QR Code” feature]] (2025-10, Vietnam/Brazil local QR), [[Gate to Pay joins Mastercard Product Express in Jordan]] (2026-07), [[Gate and Flutterwave launch localized crypto payments in Africa]] (2026-05), and bundles the same-day [[Gate partners Visa for crypto-linked card in 40+ countries]]. Gate Money is the consumer-facing synthesis of these payment rails.
- **Competitive landscape (external):** mirrors the sector-wide CEX-to-banking push — **Binance** building a "WeChat-like" super app with stablecoin payments, tokenized stocks/ETFs and Binance Pay (21M+ merchants); **Bybit** launching "MyBank"/European super app with multi-currency IBANs (18 fiat) via Pave Bank; **Crypto.com** long-running card+app model. (sources: [CoinDesk Bybit](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/09/10/crypto-exchange-giant-bybit-to-offer-european-super-app-with-stocks-derivatives); [crypto.news Binance super app](https://crypto.news/binance-plans-crypto-super-app-with-payments-stocks-and-stablecoins/); [EconoTimes: super app race](https://www.econotimes.com/Gate-Launches-Gate-Money-as-Crypto-Super-App-Race-Heats-Up-1754233))

### [4] Open questions / what to watch
- Which jurisdictions actually get the named bank-account upgrade (who is the licensed bank partner)? Gate names partners generically, not specifically.
- Custody model of the "bank account" balances — are fiat deposits e-money/safeguarded or exchange-held? Not disclosed.
- Real availability vs. announcement: "81 jurisdictions" is a license-footprint claim, not a product-availability claim. US notably absent.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
### Red-team / skeptic pass

1. **Is this a genuinely new product or a rebrand of existing features?** Mostly a **repackaging** — Gate Pay, cards, Earn, and stock trading already existed; Gate Money is the umbrella brand + a new named bank-account feature. Partly new, mostly bundling.
2. **Is "launch" = GA or announcement?** One-click activation via App v8.39.0 is claimed live from 2026-10-07, but the headline bank-account and Visa card features are rolled out selectively / "expected" in markets. → partially aspirational.
3. **Who is the actual bank?** Open — Gate references "partnerships with banks/custody/clearing institutions" generically; no named bank partner disclosed. Material gap for a "bank account" claim.
4. **Are the license claims accurate?** MiCA CASP + PSD2 (Malta), VARA (Dubai), AUSTRAC (Australia), Japan entity are plausible and specific; "81 regulated jurisdictions" is a footprint claim not independently verified here. Treat as company-stated.
5. **US availability?** Not mentioned — consistent with Gate's historic US restrictions. Limits TAM narrative.
6. **Is 3.6% USDT yield notable?** Market-rate-ish, no-lock-up; a retention hook, not a differentiator. Regulatory risk if marketed in strict jurisdictions.
7. **Does the same-day Visa card announcement inflate this?** They are complementary but separate press items; risk of double-counting importance across the two notes. Gate Money is the parent; Visa card is one spending rail.
8. **Is it a duplicate of a prior archived note?** No — no prior "Gate Money" launch exists; closest priors are Gate Pay QR (2025) and the same-day Visa card. → **fresh**.
9. **How differentiated vs Binance/Bybit super-apps?** Not very — Gate is a fast-follower in a crowded CEX-to-neobank race (Binance super app, Bybit MyBank IBANs). Me-too, decent execution.
10. **Does "bank account under own name" imply a real IBAN?** Implied (receive/withdraw with personal account info, cross-border) but IBAN not explicitly confirmed in sources. → open.
11. **Any reported criticism/litigation?** Search surfaced an unrelated "Gate Money" (UC Berkeley bail/pretrial detention topic) — a naming collision, not this company. No substantive criticism of the product found yet.
12. **Scale credibility?** 61M users / 5,300+ cryptos / 12,800+ stocks are Gate's self-reported figures; directionally plausible for a top-10 CEX but unaudited.
13. **Second-order significance?** Reinforces the 2026 thesis that CEXes converge on embedded banking/payments; regulatory burden (sanctions, capital, resilience) rises as they approach full banking.
14. **Is the source independent?** Core facts trace to Gate's own announcement + press-release syndication (HackerNoon/TheBlock press/GlobeNewswire). Low independent verification; treat product claims as vendor-stated.
15. **Durability?** Umbrella brand likely persists; specific feature rollouts and the named-bank-account gating are the real signal to watch.

**Importance: 3/5** — Strategically meaningful (a top-tier CEX formalizing a crypto super-app / neobank play, bundling licensed payments + cards + named bank accounts across many jurisdictions), and it fits a clear sector trend. But it is largely a rebrand/bundle of pre-existing capabilities, a fast-follower vs Binance/Bybit, relies on company-stated/vendor claims with no named bank partner and no US presence — so not a category-defining or market-moving event.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Gate Money sits at the crossroads of the crypto-exchange super-app race and the crypto-/stablecoin-payments gateway market. Sizing (attribute carefully, mostly secondary vendor reports — treat as directional, not verified): per The Business Research Company (via futuremarketinsights.com / thebusinessresearchcompany.com, as of 2026) the crypto-payment-gateway niche is only ~$1.9–2.4bn in 2026 at ~13–19% CAGR — i.e. the pure "gateway" layer is small; the real prize is the stablecoin-settlement flow, estimated at ~$6–8tn annualized in payments-related activity in 2026 and growing ~4–5x since 2024 (per venlyfinance.com / insidedeeptech.com, as of 2026). Retail crypto payment volume is projected ~$600bn by end-2026 (per sqmagazine.co.uk). **Structure:** the exchange super-app layer is consolidating fast around a handful of large CEXs (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Gate) that already own the users, custody and liquidity; the payments/banking rails below them are fragmented and license-gated (card networks, EMIs, bank partners). Value accrues to whoever controls the account relationship AND secures the regulated rails — the hard, capital/regulation-barriered part. **Why now:** (1) a regulatory window — MiCA (EU), VARA (Dubai), AUSTRAC (AU) now give CEXs a lawful path to bundle payments/banking onto crypto, which Gate explicitly leans on; (2) stablecoins have become the actual settlement medium (Binance Pay reports 98% of merchant volume in stablecoins, per cryptobriefing.com), turning "crypto payments" into a real cross-border rail rather than a novelty. Second-order effect: the CEX-to-everyday-finance pivot is now a sector-wide land-grab, not a Gate one-off.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: registered users / MAU, Gate Pay or card TPV, stablecoin settlement volume, merchant count, take rate on payments, Earn AUM & yield spread. Gate discloses none of the payment KPIs (TPV, take rate, merchant count) — `[UNSOURCED]`. **Key players & basis of competition:** Binance (super-app frontrunner — 300m+ users, Binance Pay merchant volume +114% YoY, 21m merchants, bStocks for US equities; per cryptobriefing.com / coinlaw.io); OKX (ICE JV for tokenized US stocks); Bybit (publicly pursuing a payments/stocks "super app" in Türkiye, 2026-10-05; `[[Bybit eyes payments, stocks, crypto super app in Türkiye]]`); competition is on distribution + regulated breadth, not price. **Recent peer moves:** Gate itself paired this with a Visa crypto-linked card across 40+ markets the very next day (2026-10-08; `[[Gate partners Visa for crypto-linked card in 40+ countries]]`) — card + account launched together, which is the credible part of the story. **Gate's position:** catching up, not leading — Binance is ~1–2 years ahead on payments scale, and Gate (~61m users, per coinlaw.io/press) just passed Bybit into #3 by derivatives volume in Sep-2026 but trails on the payments franchise. Moat `(analysis)`: its genuine edge is the breadth of held licenses (MiCA CASP + PSD2 PI in Malta, VARA VASP, AUSTRAC, "81 jurisdictions" per PR) feeding a single account — a regulatory-intangibles + switching-cost moat, not network effects it can yet claim in payments.

**Comps & multiples.** No public financials for Gate (private) → EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E all = **no data**. Private-valuation reference point among peers: `[[OKX raises capital at flat $25B valuation]]` — OKX at a flat $25bn round valuation (2026-10-07, Circle/Ripple/SC Ventures backing); mark as round valuation, NOT market cap. Gate's own valuation: **no data** (Crunchbase/PitchBook show no verified 2026 round). With only one comparable figure, distribution not computed — qualitative only. Per-user sanity check `(analysis)`: OKX's $25bn over a user base broadly in Gate's order of magnitude implies a rough price-per-user in the low-hundreds-of-dollars range, but Gate's own figure is unsourced so no multiple is computed. Other internal context comps: `[[Bybit eyes payments, stocks, crypto super app in Türkiye]]`, `[[Gate partners Visa for crypto-linked card in 40+ countries]]`.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Execution / "announced vs live" gap.** The PR bundles a lot — fiat accounts, named bank accounts, Convert, Earn, card, ATM/QR — but is silent on economics (take rate, fees), fraud/AML liability split, and which features are actually live vs eligibility-gated ("eligible users can upgrade"). Second-order: launch optics may outrun real adoption; TPV is the only thing that matters and it's undisclosed.
2. **Regulatory concentration & "banking" claim.** Offering a personal "bank account" and 3.6% yield on balances across 81 jurisdictions invites bank-licensing, deposit-insurance and securities scrutiny; the yield product in particular can be reclassified (cf. prior US actions against exchange "earn" programs). A single jurisdiction pulling a license disrupts the whole single-account promise.
3. **Disintermediation / rails dependence.** The spend layer runs on someone else's stack — Visa for the card, external banks/custodians/clearers for fiat. Gate owns the account but rents the rails, so margin and continuity depend on partners who also serve Gate's rivals; the differentiated economics may be captured upstream.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This is a sector re-positioning signal, not a Gate-specific re-rating: the top CEXs are converging on the WeChat-style "one account for crypto + stocks + payments + banking" model, so the competitive axis shifts from trading fees to who assembles the regulated everyday-finance stack fastest. Falsifiable thesis: Gate converts trading users into payments users at scale within ~12 months. Trigger/watch: first disclosed Gate Money / Gate Card TPV, merchant count, or a named bank/EMI partner — and any regulator challenging the "bank account" or 3.6% yield framing. Absence of those disclosures a year out would falsify the thesis and mark this as PR-led.
