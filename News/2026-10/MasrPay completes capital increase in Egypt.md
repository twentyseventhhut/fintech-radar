MasrPay enters a new investment phase following completion of capital increase
The completed capital increase strengthens MasrPay’s financial and operational base
 Cairo –  MasrPay announced the completion of its capital increase following approval from the Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) and completion of the required procedures, marking the beginning of a new investment phase for the company in Egypt.
The completed capital increase strengthens MasrPay’s financial and operational base and supports its long-term strategy to expand its business, deepen partnerships with banks, businesses and merchants, and continue investing in its technology platforms and digital payment capabilities.
The move also reflects continued shareholder commitment to the company’s growth plans and its position within the wider GlobalPay regional ecosystem spanning the UAE, Egypt and Saudi Arabia.
 Tamer Shawer: The Capital Increase Reflects Shareholder Confidence and Commitment to Long-Term Growth 
Eng. Tamer Shawer, CEO of MasrPay, said:
“The increase in MasrPay’s capital following approval from the Central Bank of Egypt represents an important milestone in the company’s journey and reflects our shareholders’ confidence and continued commitment to investing in the growth of our business in the Egyptian market.”
 “With the capital increase now completed, we are entering a new phase of our investment plans focused on strengthening the company’s capabilities, expanding our business, and building long-term partnerships with banks, businesses, and merchants. We view Egypt as a key market in our strategy and our investment here as a long-term commitment to building a sustainable and scalable business.” 
MasrPay operates in Egypt as part of the regional GlobalPay ecosystem. Headquartered in the United Arab Emirates, GlobalPay focuses on investments and technology solutions related to payments and financial technology. The group’s regional presence also extends to Saudi Arabia through GlobalPay KSA – Alam Al-Dafaa.
 Ali Taha: Technology, Cybersecurity and Scalability Are Priorities for the Next Phase 
Ali Taha, Chief Business Officer of MasrPay, said the completed capital increase gives the company greater capacity to align commercial growth with continued investment in the technology and operating capabilities required for sustainable expansion.
“Our next phase is about converting investment capacity into scalable business growth. We are continuing to strengthen the technology, cybersecurity, operational resilience and payment capabilities that allow us to expand responsibly and support larger transaction volumes and new digital payment services.”
 MasrPay’s next phase will focus on deploying resources across business growth, technology development, cybersecurity, operational readiness and its payment gateway capabilities, while strengthening long-term partnerships across Egypt’s payments ecosystem.
 About MasrPay 
MasrPay is an Egyptian company operating in payment technology solutions and services. It focuses on developing digital payment acceptance and management solutions for merchants and businesses in the Egyptian market and operates as part of the regional GlobalPay ecosystem.
