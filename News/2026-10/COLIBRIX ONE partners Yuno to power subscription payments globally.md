COLIBRIX ONE Partners with Yuno to Power Subscription Payments Globally
October 16, 2026
3 min read
 Partnership connects COLIBRIX ONE’s acquiring and local payment methods to Yuno’s platform, giving merchants a simpler way to accept recurring payments from customers around the world and scale revenue. 
COLIBRIX ONE, a full-stack payments infrastructure provider for international digital businesses, announced a partnership with Yuno , the AI-native intelligence system of global payments and financial services, powering the financial infrastructure of enterprise merchants, banks, and wallets. Together, the companies will enable European merchants to accept subscription and recurring payments from customers in more than 130 countries through a single connection.
For many businesses, running subscriptions across borders is a challenge: every market has its own payment methods, often requiring separate integrations. The partnership simplifies that complexity. Through a single integration with Yuno, merchants can process payments with COLIBRIX ONE while accessing subscription management capabilities including customizable billing, smart routing, payment retries, card account updating and revenue recovery.
On the acceptance side, COLIBRIX ONE enables merchants to accept payments from customers in more than 130 countries, with support for major card networks, Apple Pay and Google Pay. As a principal member of Visa and Mastercard, COLIBRIX ONE also offers open banking options, PayPal and plugins for WooCommerce, PrestaShop, OpenCart, Magento and other platforms.
On the subscription management side, Yuno supports multiple pricing models, including fixed, per-seat and usage-based billing. Merchants can adjust billing cycles and amounts without rebuilding their setup. When a payment fails, Yuno can automatically retry the transaction and support revenue recovery. The platform also supports the subscription lifecycle from free trials through cancellations, with real-time analytics to monitor cash flow and subscription performance.
On top of this, merchants get a dedicated human partner — a personal account manager from COLIBRIX ONE who handles onboarding and stays with them from the first transaction onward. And with compliance verification and background checks completed within 48 hours, merchants can move to live processing quickly.
Subscriptions are where many of the world’s fastest-growing brands make their money, but getting paid reliably across borders is still far too complicated,” said Olga Oleha, Head of Partnership and Business Development at COLIBRIX ONE . “By combining our acquiring and plugins with Yuno’s orchestration, we’re giving merchants one simple way to accept recurring payments anywhere — and to keep more of the revenue they earn.
Subscription businesses shouldn’t have to rebuild their payments infrastructure every time they enter a new market,” said Mauricio Schwartzmann, Chief Financial Services Officer at Yuno . “By bringing COLIBRIX ONE into Yuno’s global infrastructure, we’re giving businesses another way to manage recurring payments across markets while keeping the flexibility to choose the providers and payment methods that work best for them.
 About COLIBRIX ONE 
COLIBRIX ONE is a global payments infrastructure platform providing merchant acquiring for one-time and recurring payments, as well as e-money/payment accounts with dedicated IBANs (SEPA/Swift) for international businesses, e-commerce, and corporate clients.
COLIBRIX ONE is a commercial brand operated within the Mellifera group by Colibrix Limited (FCA-authorised EMI, FRN 927920), Mellifera Kartiera Limited (MFSA-authorised EMI, C107685), and Mellifera Operations Limited.
 About Yuno 
Yuno is the AI-native intelligence system of global payments and financial services, powering the financial infrastructure of enterprise merchants, banks, and wallets. Through one API, Yuno connects them to payins, payouts, fraud prevention, KYC/KYB, and stablecoins at global scale, so they can operate everywhere through a single integration.
Agnostic by design, Yuno offers connectivity to 1,000+ payment methods and 460+ integrations in 190+ countries, optimizing acceptance rates, reducing costs, and strengthening security through specialized AI agents that learn from every transaction on the network. Global brands run their payments on Yuno, including McDonald’s, NetEase Games, GoFundMe, inDrive, and Rappi. Banks, acquirers, PSPs, and wallets use Yuno to modernize their infrastructure without replacing core systems, and to prepare for the next generation of commerce, including agentic payments.
Learn more at www.y.uno .
