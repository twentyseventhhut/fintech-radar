Платформа обновила раздел «Фильмы и сериалы», превратив его в полноценный инструмент для быстрого поиска и выбора контента. Выбирать фильмы и сериалы стало еще удобнее: теперь пользователям доступны кинокарточки тайтлов с обложкой, трейлером, рейтингом и основной информацией. Об этом CNews сообщили представители VK .
В обновленном разделе можно искать конкретный фильм или сериал, а также знакомиться с контентом по жанрам и тематическим подборкам в новом формате. К уже привычным рекомендациям и плейлистами добавились разделы с кинокаточками с информацией о каждом тайтле: актерах, режиссере и других характеристиках.
Еще одним новым инструментом стал саджестер — подсказка с кинокарточкой в поисковой строке , которая помогает находить нужные фильмы и сериалы уже при вводе запроса. Из поисковой строки пользователь может сразу перейти в карточку тайтла, познакомиться с основной информацией о нем и принять решение о просмотре.
До конца месяца в разделе появятся шесть тематических направлений по интересам: «Фильмы», «Сериалы»,«Шоу», « Мультфильмы », «Мультсериалы» и «Аниме». Внутри каждого будет доступен отдельный раздел, где можно быстро найти, и выбрать что посмотреть, — по жанрам или в редакторских подборках, в том числе в «Популярном», «Новинках» и других коллекциях.
Обновление уже доступно в приложении и веб-версии « VK Видео ». В течение месяца новые возможности появятся и на Smart TV .

# Context-enrichment: VK Video revamps Films and Series catalog section
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On 2026-10-08 VK announced (via CNews) a UI/UX overhaul of the "Films & Series" ("Фильмы и сериалы") section inside VK Video, turning a loose list into a unified content catalog. Concrete, shippable changes:
- **Title cards** ("кинокарточки") per title: cover art, trailer, rating, cast, director, metadata.
- **Suggester in the search bar** — a title card surfaces as you type, with a direct jump into the title page.
- **Six interest verticals promised "by end of month"**: Films, Series, Shows, Cartoons, Animated Series, Anime — each with genre/editorial collections (Popular, New Releases).
- Live now in app + web; Smart TV "within the month".

De-PR note: this is a **merchandising/discovery layer on top of existing catalog**, not new content, new rights, or a new paid product. The PR frames it as a "full-fledged search and selection tool," which overstates — it is a catalog-browsing redesign common to any OTT app (IMDb-style cards, typeahead). "By end of month" + "within the month" means much of what is announced is **not yet live** — announced, not fully shipped.
- **Why structured this way**: VK Video is an ad-monetized UGC+licensed-video hub, not a subscription cinema. A richer "Films & Series" showcase is a lever to raise **watch-time on long-form licensed content** (the surface that carries pre/in-stream video ads) and to compete for the "what to watch tonight" intent that otherwise goes to Kinopoisk/Okko. The one quantified claim — early experiments lifted viewing time on recommended films **+23%** — is the real tell: this is a time-on-platform / ad-inventory play dressed as a UX nicety.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Russian OTT / film catalogs**: Kinopoisk (Yandex), Okko (Sber), Ivi, Wink (Rostelecom), START, Premier — all mature subscription services with polished catalog cards, typeahead, and ML recs for years. On catalog UX, VK Video is **catching up to table stakes**, not leading.
- **Russian UGC video**: Rutube (see [[Rutube tests AI assistant for video creators]], same 2026-10-08 CNews batch) and Dzen — VK Video is far ahead on audience (42.2M DAU Jan-2026 vs Rutube ~8.3M).
- **Global reference**: YouTube's "Movies & TV" and IMDb/JustWatch-style discovery — again, prior art with years of lead.
- **Why the lay of the land is this way**: VK Video's edge is distribution (throttled YouTube pushing Russians onto VK) and ad scale, not catalog depth. Bolting cinema-grade discovery onto a UGC app is an attempt to annex the OTT "lean-back" use case without paying for an OTT content slate. Second-order: it blurs the line with sister/rival Sber's Okko and Yandex's Kinopoisk, intensifying intra-RU platform competition for the same shrinking pool of licensed titles.

## [2] Company history / fit
- Fits a clear trajectory. July 2026: VK rolled out **Discovery AI** neuro-search across VK Video, Dzen, Mail ([[VK launches Discovery AI neural search across products]]) — this catalog revamp sits downstream of that recs/ML push; the "+23%" ML showcase is plausibly the same AI VK stack.
- VK is simultaneously pushing monetization and ad plumbing: [[VK opens Max channel ads to accounts over 1K subscribers]] (ad inventory expansion), and divesting non-core assets — [[VK sells 40% stake in ticketing service Intickets]].
- **Why the company acts this way (the fintech-adjacent logic)**: VK Company 2025 revenue RUB 160bn (+8% YoY), EBITDA RUB 22.6bn, **net debt RUB 82bn, net-debt/EBITDA ~3.6x** (target 2-3x) — a **leveraged balance sheet under pressure to grow cash-generative ad revenue**. Video-ad revenue rose **68% YoY to RUB 6.5bn** in 2025; in-stream video ads +28% in Q1-2026; 2026 EBITDA guidance raised to >RUB 24bn. So every watch-time lever on VK Video feeds the segment VK is leaning on to de-lever. The catalog revamp is a small cog in a balance-sheet repair story — that is the (thin) fintech thread.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novelty: low.** Catalog cards, typeahead suggester, genre verticals, ML recs — all standard OTT features, present for years at Kinopoisk/Okko/Ivi and globally. The only differentiated asset is VK's distribution scale, not the feature.
- **Traction**: one internal, unaudited figure (+23% viewing time on recommended films, "initial experiments"). No DAU/subscription/revenue delta attributed to this feature. The six verticals and Smart TV rollout are **not yet live** at announcement.
- **Why value-add is marginal**: the margin in Russian video sits with (a) ad inventory owners and (b) rights holders of premium content. This feature marginally improves (a) by raising watch-time on ad-bearing long-form, but does not give VK new content or a new paid tier — so it does not shift who captures value. It is incremental ad-yield optimization, not a strategic unlock. (analysis)

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- Near term: six verticals + Smart TV completion "within October 2026"; expect VK to pair this with its new revenue-share creator program (50-80% ad share for 5k+ follower creators, launched mid-Sep 2026) to deepen the content supply side.
- **Structural backdrop**: Russia's deliberate YouTube throttling since 2024 is the single biggest tailwind — YouTube RU daily reach ~22M vs VK Video 42.2M (Jan-2026); VK Video overtook YouTube on RU monthly audience in mid-2025. Be skeptical of VK/state-media audience framing (VK is an interested party; "almost twice YouTube" rests on throttling, not product win).
- **Counterintuitive second-order**: VK Video's growth is **policy-dependent, not product-dependent**. A catalog redesign is VK trying to convert coerced, sticky-but-shallow YouTube refugees into genuine engagement before/if throttling eases. Fragile moat: the audience is on loan from a regulator.
- **Risk / honest read**: minimal fintech/markets consequence. Importance is carried only by the thin ad-revenue → de-leveraging thread; the event itself is a routine product update.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian online-video splits into (a) ad-funded UGC/long-form video platforms (VK Video, Rutube, ex-YouTube) and (b) subscription OTT "online cinemas" (Kinopoisk, Okko, Wink, Ivi, Kion). This news sits at the seam: VK Video rebuilding its "Films & Series" section with film cards, trailers, ratings and a search suggester pushes a UGC platform toward online-cinema UX. Market size: Russian online-cinema revenue reached ~RUB 120bn by end-2024 (per Tadviser/iz.ru, as of 2025-02); H1 2025 total users ~67m with ~52m paid subscribers, +44% YoY paid subs growth (per iz.ru, as of 2025-07). Structure — consolidated: the top-5 OTT (Kinopoisk, Okko, Ivi, Wink, Kion) serve ~86% of the audience, so VK Video enters a catalog race against entrenched, content-licensed incumbents. "Why now": the secular driver is YouTube's throttling in Russia (deliberate slowdown since July 2024, not formally blocked but access severely limited into 2026); VK's own daily VK Video audience hit ~42m in H1 2026 (per VK, via Tubefilter/Meduza, as of 2026) and first overtook YouTube on monthly reach in late 2024/early 2025. That migration is the tailwind; the catalog revamp is an attempt to convert captured attention into longer watch-time and ad inventory.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: for ad-video — DAU/MAU, watch-time, ad fill/CPM; for OTT — paid subscribers, subscriber share, ARPU. Key players and basis of competition: VK Video and Rutube (Gazprom-Media) compete on free, ad-/UGC-funded breadth and creator tooling; Kinopoisk (Yandex), Okko (Sber), Wink (Rostelecom), Ivi and Kion (MTS) compete on licensed/original premium content behind subscriptions. VK Video's move is a UX/discovery play, not a licensing push — it is aggregating/organizing content with cards and a suggester, not buying rights, which is cheaper but weaker as a moat. Recent peer moves: Rutube began closed testing of an AI creator assistant 2026-10-08 (same news cycle, same CNews reporter — see internal comp below); VK launched "Discovery" AI neural search across products (2026-07). Protagonist position: ahead on free-video reach (dominant DAU post-YouTube), but a late, niche entrant in the films-and-series/premium-catalog layer where Kinopoisk (~26% paid-sub share, per iz.ru) and Okko/Sber are entrenched. Moat `(analysis)`: network effects and distribution via the VK ecosystem / Max messenger, not content exclusivity.

**Comps & multiples.** This is a product/UX feature with no deal, round or disclosed VK Video-level economics, so segment multiples are not computable — "no data" at the VK Video unit level. Parent-level grounding (VK, FY2025, IFRS): revenue RUB 160bn (+8% YoY); EBITDA RUB 22.6bn (margin ~14%, vs negative in 2024); video advertising revenue +68% YoY to RUB 6.5bn — the clearest monetization signal that video traction is converting to ad RUB, though RUB 6.5bn is still small vs RUB 160bn group revenue (= ~4%). Net debt RUB 82bn, Net debt/EBITDA ~3.6x after a RUB ~112bn equity raise in April 2025 (per VK Q4/FY2025 release; akm.ru; Reuters). EV/EBITDA/P-E not computed — EBITDA only just turned positive, 2024 net loss ~RUB 94.9bn, so trailing P/E is meaningless `[UNSOURCED]` forward basis. Internal comps: [[Rutube tests AI assistant for video creators]] (2026-10-08, direct ad-video peer), [[VK launches Discovery AI neural search across products]] (2026-07, same company adjacent product). No OTT subscription comp note in base (grep found no Kinopoisk/Okko/Ivi dedicated notes) — qualitative comparison only; distribution not computed.

**Risk flags.**
1. Monetization dependence / thin economics — VK Video monetizes mainly via ad video (RUB 6.5bn, ~4% of group), and a catalog of cards/trailers does not by itself add subscription revenue; the feature improves discovery but the second-order payoff (watch-time → ad inventory) is unproven and unquantified here.
2. Content-cost / licensing gap — organizing "films and series" without the licensing spend of Kinopoisk/Okko risks a shallow catalog vs subscription incumbents; closing that gap means capex into rights, pressuring the just-recovered EBITDA margin (14%).
3. State-dependence / regulatory tailwind is exogenous and reversible — VK Video's reach gains rest heavily on YouTube throttling, a policy/infrastructure lever VK does not control; any easing of YouTube access would erode the migration thesis underpinning the audience numbers.
4. Parent leverage — Net debt/EBITDA ~3.6x (above VK's own 2-3x target) after a dilutive RUB 112bn raise; discretionary investment into video/content competes with deleveraging.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This nudges VK Video from a YouTube-substitute UGC feed toward a hybrid discovery hub blurring into the OTT layer — a feature-level land-grab, not a re-rating. Falsifiable thesis: if the catalog/suggester lifts watch-time it should show up as continued double-digit video-ad growth (building on +68% YoY in 2025) in the next VK release; watch Q1-2026 / H1-2026 segment ad figures. Thesis breaks if video-ad growth decelerates sharply or YouTube access normalizes, exposing the reach as borrowed rather than earned. Fintech relevance is modest — indirect, via VK's broader ecosystem monetization (ads, payments within VK/Max), not this feature.
