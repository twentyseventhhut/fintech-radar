# Birmingham fintech Lovey secures £250M credit facility

> [!info] 2026-10-28 · 2 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] Small businesses often struggle to get the financing they need to grow. Birmingham-based FinTech Lovey is looking to change that, securing a credit facility of up to £250 million from Pollen Street Capital, the largest funding deal in the company’s history.

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇬🇧 Birmingham FinTech Lovey secures £250 million credit facility. The funding from Pollen Street Capital comprises £50 million in committed capital and a £200 million accordion facility, enabling the SME lender to expand lending from its own balance sheet.

Birmingham fintech Lovey has secured a credit facility of up to £250m from alternative asset manager Pollen Street Capital, the largest funding deal in its history.

The senior secured facility is made up of £50m of committed capital and a £200m accordion, and will significantly increase the SME lender’s capacity as it scales up lending from its own balance sheet.
