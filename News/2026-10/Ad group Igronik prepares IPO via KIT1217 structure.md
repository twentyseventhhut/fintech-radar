# Ad group Igronik prepares IPO via KIT1217 structure

> [!info] 2026-10-12 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Mergers.ru] 🖥 Рекламная группа «Игроник» объявила о подготовке к IPO. Новая структура «КИТ1217», куда войдет «Игроник», может стать первой публичной российской компанией на рекламном рынке — холдинг запланировал провести IPO, сообщил основатель компании Никита Пипко. По словам Пипко, горизонт IPO будет варьироваться в диапазоне от шести месяцев до полутора лет. Практическая подготовка к размещению начнется в 2027 году. «Сейчас мне видится, что это будет Московская биржа, но могут быть варианты», — уточнил он. Всего, по словам главы компании, планируется вывод 20–25% от общего объема акций. Один из обсуждаемых вариантов — pre-IPO, в рамках которого может быть реализовано от трети до четверти этого портфеля. «Игроник» был основан в 1992 году. Компания обеспечивает рекламное сопровождение свыше 80 крупных компаний. Среди клиентов — Альфа-банк, ВТБ, «Почта России», ЛСР, «Ингосстрах», Минздрав, МТС и др.

# Context-enrichment: Ad group Igronik prepares IPO via KIT1217 structure
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On 2026-10-12, Nikita Pipko, founder of Russian advertising group "Igronik" (founded 1992), said he is creating a new holding **"KIT1217"** — bundling advertising, media and tech assets — and intends to take it public. If it happens, it would be **the first public Russian company in the advertising market**. Stated parameters:
- **Float: 20–25%** of total shares.
- **Timing horizon: 6–18 months**; *practical* preparation only begins **in 2027**.
- **Venue:** "I see it as the Moscow Exchange, but there could be options" — i.e. not fixed.
- **Pre-IPO** is one option under discussion, placing a **third-to-a-quarter of that 20–25% package** (so ~5–8% of equity) ahead of a full listing.
- **Indicative valuation ~15bn RUB at ~5.5x EBITDA**, with **expected EBITDA ~2.7bn RUB by 2026** (per "professional investment consultants" cited in RB.ru / iz.ru — *not* an underwriter mandate).
- Scale proof: **~48.8bn RUB of advertising-budget volume in 2025**, ranked **4th among Russia's largest media companies** (AKAR / Sostav). Serves 80+ large clients — Alfa-Bank, VTB, Russian Post, LSR, Ingosstrakh, Minzdrav, MTS.

**Why structured this way / what it reveals.** This is a **founder's verbal intention statement, not a filing** — every hard commitment is hedged ("could be", "I see it as", "options"). The de-PR'd read: a new holding wrapper (KIT1217) has been *created* precisely to make a fragmented agency business IPO-able, but no prospectus, no lead banks, no date. The headline "48.8bn RUB" is **advertising-budget volume (billings/turnover passed through the agency), NOT revenue** — agency revenue is a thin commission on that gross number. The economically honest anchors are the **~2.7bn RUB EBITDA and ~15bn RUB (5.5x)** figures, which imply a modest mid-cap, not a 48.8bn-RUB-scale business. Announcing a valuation multiple this early is itself marketing — it sets an anchor before any book is built.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Direct peer: none public.** The entire pitch is "first public ad company in Russia" — by definition there is no listed Russian comparable, which cuts both ways (scarcity premium vs. no read-across for pricing).
- **Russian media/ad landscape:** Igronik/KIT1217 is #4 among Russian media companies by AKAR/Sostav; the larger players (VK's ad machine, Yandex, MTS AdTech, Sber) are ad *platforms/owners*, not agencies, and are either already public or embedded in larger groups. See [[VK opens Max channel ads to accounts over 1K subscribers]] — the ad-inventory side is consolidating around big-tech platforms, which squeezes independent agencies toward services/execution rather than owned inventory.
- **Russian IPO-pipeline peers (process comparables, not sector):** [[Indie GO launches 5bn-ruble venture fund for gaming IPOs]] (another "prep-for-listing" vehicle in a non-financial niche) and the broader 2026 MOEX new-issue backdrop. [[Mergers.ru Gref advises state companies on successful IPOs]] frames the exact gating risk: Gref's "don't be greedy, price for the public investor" — a KIT1217 that anchors to 5.5x EBITDA pre-book risks the opposite.

**Why the lay of the land is this way.** No public ad-agency comparable exists because agency economics (thin, cyclical commission margins, client-concentration risk, no owned inventory) have historically been a poor fit for public markets globally — the listed names are holding networks (WPP, Omnicom) trading at depressed multiples. A 5.5x EBITDA ask is cheap vs. tech but **rich for a pure agency** in a declining ad market (Russian agency-market revenue growth was negative in 2026). The "scarcity" framing is the whole bull case; the bear case is that scarcity reflects structural unsuitability, not opportunity.

## [2] Company history / fit
Igronik is a **1992-vintage, founder-run services business** with a blue-chip but **state-heavy client book** (VTB, Russian Post, Minzdrav, Ingosstrakh). The IPO is the classic **founder-liquidity / brand-elevation** move: wrap operating assets into a fresh "KIT1217" holding (clean cap table, consolidated reporting) and float a minority (20–25%) while the founder keeps control.

**Why the company acts this way (analysis).** A 34-year-old private agency has few exit paths in the current Russian market: strategic buyers are scarce, cross-border sale is closed. An IPO is the **only realistic liquidity + growth-capital route**, and the 2026 MOEX window (retail-investor demand, import-substitution/"domestic champion" narrative) makes "first public ad company" a marketable story. The pre-IPO option signals the founder wants to **de-risk and price-discover** before a full listing — prudent, but also a tell that confidence in an immediate full float is limited.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novelty:** genuinely a "first" — no prior Russian listed ad agency. But novelty of *structure/listing*, not of business.
- **Traction:** this is an **announcement, not a transaction.** No banks, no prospectus, no confirmed venue, no date — practical prep is a **2027** item. The only hard traction is the operating business itself (48.8bn RUB billings, #4 rank, 80+ clients).

**Why the value-add is real-or-not (1–2 levels deep).** The durable question is **margin capture in the ad stack.** An agency sits between advertisers and inventory owners (increasingly VK/Yandex/MTS/Sber platforms). As inventory consolidates into big-tech ad platforms, **the platform captures the margin and the agency becomes a commoditized execution layer** — exactly the WPP/Omnicom multiple-compression story. So the IPO multiple rests less on "ad-market growth" and more on whether KIT1217 owns something non-commoditizable (data, tech, proprietary inventory) vs. reselling platform inventory on thin commission. Nothing disclosed yet confirms the former. Second-order: the heavy **state-linked client base** is a stability prop in the near term but a concentration/political risk for public minority investors.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- **Next:** holding assembly through 2026; practical IPO prep in 2027; possible pre-IPO round (~5–8% of equity) as a price-discovery step; venue decision (MOEX likeliest) still open.
- **Sentiment:** the 2026 Russian IPO market has been thin and discount-driven (early-2026 issues priced at heavy discounts), and Gref's public caution ([[Mergers.ru Gref advises state companies on successful IPOs]]) captures the mood: price for investors or get punished.
- **Risks:** declining Russian ad-market revenue; agency-margin commoditization vs. ad platforms; client concentration (state-heavy); high-rate environment compressing multiples; execution risk (nothing is filed); the founder may quietly let the 6–18mo horizon slip (as many announced-but-not-filed Russian IPOs do).

**Why the market will go this way (analysis).** In a high-rate, discount-heavy IPO window, a **pure agency with no public comparable and a "billings not revenue" headline** is a hard sell at 5.5x. The most likely path is a **small pre-IPO first** (test demand, set an anchor) rather than a quick full listing — and a real risk the full IPO drifts past the stated window. Counterintuitively, the "first public ad company" scarcity label could **hurt** pricing: with no comparable, institutional investors have no anchor and default to a discount.

**Sector & drivers.** Russian advertising market grew +8.5% to ~980bn RUB in 2025 (per AKAR, via smart-lab/tadviser) and is forecast +12.1% to ~1.138trn RUB in 2026 ex-retail-media (per ADPASS Nov-2025, via tadviser); online adtech/promo was the fast lane at ~1.569trn RUB, +28% YoY in 2025. Structure: fragmented agency landscape ranked by the AKAR/Sostav billings table — Igronik sits #4 on 2025 billings of ~48.8bn RUB (per RBC/Sostav, via rb.ru, as of 2026-10-12). Value in a media-agency sits in buying scale + client relationships, not capital; entry barriers are relationship- and trust-based, not regulatory — a thin moat. "Why now": a nascent 2026 Russian-IPO reopening (B2B-RTS listed Apr-2026 as the year's first deal) plus sanctions-driven exit of global holding networks (WPP/Omnicom/Publicis) leaving domestic agencies to consolidate the vacated client budgets — the de-PR'd rationale behind a "first public ad company" pitch.

**Competitive landscape.** Agency KPIs: billings/turnover (gross media handled), net revenue/commission, EBITDA margin. Igronik: founded 1992, services 80+ large clients (Alfa-Bank, VTB, Russian Post, LSR, Ingosstrakh, MinZdrav, MTS), #4 by billings (~48.8bn RUB 2025). Key peers are other top-5 AKAR agencies (incumbents) + in-house/retail-media platforms of Yandex, VK, Ozon, Wildberries capturing budget directly (disintermediation threat). Basis of competition: distribution/buying scale + client switching costs. Position: established incumbent, NOT a disruptor — "first public" is a structuring/branding first (new holding "KIT1217"), not a product edge (analysis). Moat: client relationships / switching costs, low on scale vs the platforms.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** A "first public Russian ad holding" would set the listed comp for the sector — but on 5.5x EBITDA it re-rates agencies as low-growth cyclicals, not tech, so it is a modest new-entry signal rather than a sector re-rating (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: this does NOT list on the stated horizon — watch for the KIT1217 AO re-registration + a lead-underwriter mandate in 2027 as the real trigger; absence of both by mid-2027 confirms it stays an aspiration.
