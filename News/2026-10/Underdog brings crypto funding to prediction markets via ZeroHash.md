Underdog to Bring Crypto and Stablecoin Account Funding to Prediction Markets, Powered by zerohash

 New integration with zerohash will extend Underdog’s prediction market exchange with onchain account funding, enabling instant money movement 
NEXTPredict NYC Summit - NEW YORK - October 22, 2026 - Underdog , the prediction market and fantasy sports pioneer, today announced that users will be able to fund their accounts with stablecoins and crypto, in a new partnership with zerohash, the leading onchain infrastructure provider. The new funding option is expected to be available to eligible users later this year. 
The new capability will allow eligible users to fund their Underdog accounts directly with supported stablecoins and digital assets, providing an additional funding option alongside existing rails, all natively within the Underdog app. Prediction markets and fantasy contests move quickly, and stablecoins allow for instant, real time settlement including on weekends and evenings when fiat rails are unavailable. By leveraging zerohash’s onchain infrastructure, users can move money into their accounts in near real time, so funding keeps up with the speed of the games and they can participate in live markets and contests the moment opportunities arise.
For Underdog users, the benefits include:
Near-instant account funding 24/7/365;
Simple, account-to-account funding with wallet addresses and chain selection abstracted away; and
More choice and flexibility in how accounts are funded.
“We launched our own exchange so we could move at the speed our customers expect, and funding needs to move at that same speed,” said Arthik Adla, Head of Payments at Underdog. “Stablecoins and onchain assets unlock greater choice and speed. Partnering with zerohash will allow customers to fund their accounts and get into the action faster, anytime, from anywhere.”
"Prediction markets never close, and users increasingly expect their money to move just as fast,” said Adam Tesan, Chief Revenue Officer at zerohash. “Stablecoins solve that by moving money globally, 24/7. We built zerohash to make that simple for companies like Underdog, so their customers can fund their accounts when the velocity of money matters most.”
The integration follows Underdog’s launch of its own federally licensed prediction market exchange , making Underdog the first sports company with the complete prediction market license stack, including a Designated Contract Market (DCM), Derivatives Clearing Organization (DCO), and Futures Commission Merchant (FCM), all wholly owned and operated by Underdog.

Built for fans, by fans. Visit underdogsports.com .

# Context-enrichment: Underdog brings crypto funding to prediction markets via ZeroHash
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
De-PR'd: Underdog (fantasy-sports + prediction-market operator) signed a partnership with ZeroHash to let eligible US users fund their Underdog accounts with stablecoins/crypto. **This is an announcement, not a live product** — the PR says the option is "expected to be available to eligible users later this year" (announced at the NEXTPredict NYC Summit, Oct 22, 2026). No live date, no supported-asset list, no volume, no fee disclosed. The deposit rail is ZeroHash's standard embedded "Account Funding" widget (wallet addresses + chain selection abstracted away), explicitly excluding New York (ZeroHash is not available there).

**+ Why structured this way / what it reveals:** This is a deposit-rail add-on, not a change to Underdog's core product. The real underlying event already happened — Underdog launched its own CFTC-licensed exchange on ~July 18, 2026 (acquired a DCM + DCO in March 2026, plus FCM registration), making it the first sports company to own the full prediction-market license stack. The crypto-funding bolt-on is a second-order consequence of owning that exchange: once you control settlement you want deposits to move at "the speed of the games" (24/7, evenings/weekends when ACH/wire are closed). The PR frames an off-the-shelf infrastructure plug-in as a strategic milestone; the substance is a payments UX improvement on top of a real, already-reported licensing achievement.

## [1] Competitors / peers
This is a near-exact replay of a well-worn ZeroHash playbook, not a first:
- **Kalshi × ZeroHash — Oct 2024.** ZeroHash launched the identical embedded USDC "Account Funding" widget for Kalshi (reportedly shipped in ~10 days). Underdog's integration is the same product, ~2 years later.
- **ProphetX, EDGE Markets, Tastytrade (Jul 2025), Interactive Brokers (24/7 USDC funding, Jan 2026)** — all received the same ZeroHash instant-stablecoin-funding product per ZeroHash's own press list.
- **Prediction-market incumbents:** Kalshi (~80-83% of regulated US notional, ~$20B valuation), Polymarket (~$20B; ~10% of US regulated share and rising), Robinhood (recently surpassed Crypto.com for #3 notional), Crypto.com (CDNA — Underdog's *former* exchange partner, see [[Crypto.com and Underdog partner on sports prediction markets]]).

**+ Why the lay of the land / second-order:** Underdog's edge is NOT the crypto rail (commodity infrastructure any competitor can rent from the same vendor) — it is owning the full license stack so it keeps exchange economics instead of paying a CDNA/Kalshi intermediary. Crypto funding is table stakes: Kalshi had it 2 years earlier. So this news positions Underdog at *parity* on deposits, while its genuine differentiation lives one layer down in the DCM/DCO/FCM ownership. The multiple gap vs Kalshi/Polymarket is about distribution + regulatory moat, which a deposit widget does not move.

## [2] Company history / fit
Underdog (founded 2020) trajectory: fantasy-sports operator → **first** sports-gaming operator to offer prediction markets (fall 2025, via third parties Crypto.com/CDNA and Kalshi) → acquired DCM + DCO (Mar 2026) → launched wholly-owned CFTC exchange (Jul 2026) → now adds crypto funding (Oct 2026). Reported traction: ~$1.4B trading volume in the 30 days ending Oct 21, 2026, +133% vs prior period.

**+ Why the company acts this way:** Structural pressure = disintermediation. As a third-party operator on CDNA/Kalshi, Underdog was a distribution front-end giving away exchange margin. Vertically integrating the license stack lets it capture clearing/exchange economics on its own flow. Crypto funding is the logical next move: owning settlement makes 24/7 deposits valuable (fiat rails don't settle on nights/weekends when live sports happen), and reducing funding friction directly lifts the deposit→wager conversion that monetizes the exchange it just built.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
Genuinely new here: **very little.** The crypto-funding capability itself is ~2 years old (Kalshi 2024) and is a rented ZeroHash product. What IS new is the pairing with Underdog's *own* exchange — Underdog is plausibly the first sports-company-owned full-stack prediction exchange to add native crypto deposits. But even that is announced, not live, with zero adoption numbers for the crypto rail specifically (the $1.4B/133% figure is total exchange volume, unrelated to crypto deposits).

**+ Why the value-add is thin, deeper:** In this stack ZeroHash captures the margin on the deposit rail (spread/fee on fiat↔stablecoin conversion), Underdog captures exchange/clearing economics on the trade. The crypto rail is a conversion-lift utility, not a revenue line Underdog owns — and it is commoditized, since every competitor can buy the identical widget. So the durable value is Underdog's license moat and sports distribution; crypto funding is a marginal UX tailwind that could be matched tomorrow. What breaks it: if the CFTC/courts reclassify sports event contracts as gambling, or states/tribes win jurisdiction, the whole exchange thesis (not the deposit rail) is at risk.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment

**Sector & drivers.** Prediction markets are in a vertical take-off: combined Kalshi+Polymarket monthly volume rose from <$5bn in Sep-2025 to ~$24bn in Apr-2026 and a reported ~$44.8bn in Jun-2026 (per Pew Research citing DeFi Rate, via pewresearch.org, as of 2026-09). CFTC began formal prediction-market rulemaking in 2026 (per Forbes, 2026-03). Structure: still fragmenting — two dominant exchanges (Kalshi, Polymarket US/Intl) plus a wave of sports-native entrants (Underdog, ProphetX, EDGE). The *funding-rail* layer under all of them is consolidating fast around one vendor (see below).

Two competitive planes: (1) the *exchanges* — Kalshi (~$31.5bn Jun-2026 notional), Polymarket (~$10.8bn global, per DeFi Rate), vs sports-first Underdog/ProphetX; basis of competition = contract breadth, liquidity/network effects, and license posture. (2) The *funding infrastructure* — this deal is really a ZeroHash win. ZeroHash has now stamped the on-ramp for Underdog, ProphetX (Jul-2026) and EDGE Markets (Sep-2026), i.e. it is quietly becoming the default stablecoin funding rail for the whole prediction-market vertical.
