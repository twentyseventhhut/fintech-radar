Getnet, the leading AI-payments company and part of Santander Group, partners with PingPong, the intelligent payments infrastructure for global growth, to support its expansion into Iberia and provide international businesses with access to local payment capabilities in Spain and Portugal
 
The collaboration brings together two established payments businesses to deliver a stronger payment capability in the region and a simple, streamlined solution for global businesses looking to expand operations in Iberia. By combining PingPong’s global cross-border payments infrastructure with Getnet’s European acquiring capabilities and access to leading local payment methods, including Bizum in Spain and MB WAY in Portugal, the partnership aims to simplify market access, reduce technical complexity and provide  premium support to merchants expanding into the region. 
“This partnership represents another important milestone in Getnet's strategy to expand our open-market business across Europe while strengthening the connections between Iberia and one of the world's fastest-growing commerce ecosystems. By combining PingPong's strong presence across Asia with Getnet's leadership in Europe and Latin America, we are creating new opportunities for international merchants to grow with a seamless and trusted payment experience.”
 Fabrice Mendez , Global Head of Partnerships at Getnet
With a network of more than 750,000 businesses across over 200 countries, PingPong brings an established international footprint to the partnership. Through this collaboration, Getnet complements PingPong’s global offering with local payment capabilities and connectivity, helping international merchants navigate the Iberian payments landscape and access new growth opportunities through a streamlined integration and dedicated support.
“Iberia is one of Europe's most valuable commerce markets, and the 750,000+ businesses in our network want to operate there on payment infrastructure they already trust. Our move into Iberia is the latest step in an ongoing global expansion, driven by rising customer demand and sustained investment in our international growth”
 Aaron Xu, General Manager, Europe and Americas at PingPong
 About PingPong 
Founded in 2015, PingPong is a global cross-border B2B payments platform supporting over 750,000 businesses across more than 200 countries. The company annually processes over $300bn and holds 80+ financial licences worldwide. PingPong operates from 44 offices across 18 countries.
For more information, visit www.pingpongx.com .
