# Context-enrichment: TODAY raises €2.8M seed for adviser AI workflow platform
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

**FRESHNESS: fresh.** No prior corpus note covers TODAY (usetoday.io) or this round; `company/today` matches only this note. This is a NEW, discrete Seed round (announced ~2026-10-08), distinct from the company's ~€1M pre-seed (Nov 2024, per Nordic Fintech Magazine). Adjacent-but-DIFFERENT European adviser/broker-AI seeds exist in the same corpus window — see [1] — but none is the same company or deal.

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
- **TODAY** (legal/brand usetoday.io; Copenhagen + Berlin) raised **€2.8M (~$3.1–3.2M) Seed**, **co-led by High-Tech Gründerfonds (HTGF) and Insurtech Gateway**, with **Seed X, Lucid Capital**, and angels **Herbert Rogenhofer** (ex-HDI CEO) and **Pierre Dubosq** (Tech11 founder). Use of funds: "development and commercialisation" (i.e. build + GTM/hiring) — no new product line, no M&A.
- Product, de-PR'd: an **"AI Sales Operating System" / agentic copilot for financial & insurance advisers** that transcribes client meetings, auto-drafts meeting notes, does back-office admin, **answers inbound calls**, and gives sales coaching — before/during/after the meeting. In plain terms this is an **adviser-specific AI notetaker + workflow/CRM-feeder with a voice-agent and a "compliance" wrapper**; the "operating system"/"agentic" framing is marketing on top of a notetaker-plus-automation stack.
- **Claimed traction:** **~3,500 advisers** across insurers, distributors and brokers; outcomes cited as **"up to 10% more revenue"** and **"~5 hours/week saved"** on admin. All vendor-reported, no independent audit; "up to 10%" is a ceiling, not an average.
- **Why structured this way / what it reveals:** the lead pairing is strategic-distribution money, not generic VC — **HTGF** (German state-backed deep-tech seed) + **Insurtech Gateway** (incubator that supplies insurer access) + an **ex-HDI-CEO angel** = the cap table is buying *insurer-distribution reach*, exactly the channel this product needs (advisers sit inside insurers/brokers). The number PR anchors to is **adviser count (3,500)**, not ARR, paying seats, or retention — a classic omission that lets a usage figure stand in for revenue. The currency the English note carries (€2.8M/$3.1M) vs Dealroom's "$3.2M" is just FX rounding of the SAME round (not two events).

## [1] Competitors / peers
Two circles, and TODAY straddles both:
- **US adviser-copilot / AI-notetaker incumbents (the category leaders, far ahead on funding & integrations):** **Jump AI, Zocks, FinMate AI** (plus Pulse360) — all purpose-built for advisers, compliance-aware, CRM-integrated; Jump and Zocks have raised materially more (Zocks is SOC 2 Type II; Jump is the category's fastest-scaling). TODAY is **behind on capital and CRM-ecosystem depth** but **differentiated on the inbound-voice-agent + insurance-adviser (not just RIA/wealth) focus and the EU/DACH compliance posture**.
- **European adviser/broker back-office-AI seeds (direct stage/sector comps in THIS corpus):** [[Portuguese insurtech FRANK raises 2.9m euros]] (€2.9M, "AI Operator" to free brokers from back-office), [[Konsileo raises £5M equity financing]] (UK tech-enabled broker with in-house platform + AI), [[Nettle raises $4.8M seed for insurer property risk AI]] (AI workspace, insurer-side loss control — adjacent, not adviser-facing). TODAY's €2.8M sits **right at the European seed median** for this wave (FRANK €2.9M, Nettle $4.8M), i.e. parity, not a standout cheque.
- **Why the landscape looks this way (2nd order):** the US "adviser copilot" category has already consolidated around 3–4 names because US wealth distribution is CRM-centric (Redtail/Wealthbox/Salesforce) and buyers are RIAs. Europe's adviser base is **insurer/broker-tied**, fragmented across languages and regulators, and CRM-light — so a US incumbent can't just port in, which is the opening TODAY and FRANK are exploiting. But that same fragmentation caps how fast any one EU player scales → the moat is distribution relationships (hence the insurer-heavy cap table), not technology.

## [2] Company history / fit
- Trajectory: **founded 2024** by **Michael Gackstatter (CEO)** and **Artem Demchenkov (CTO)**, alumni of **CLARK** (German insurtech distributor), **Funding Circle** and **Billie** → **~€1M pre-seed (Nov 2024)** → **€2.8M Seed (Oct 2026)**. ~2 years from founding to Seed with a claimed 3,500-adviser base is fast-ish but consistent with a notetaker land-grab.
- Fit: logical. **CLARK pedigree matters** — the founders come from insurance *distribution*, so building a tool for the adviser layer (not the carrier or the consumer) is their native wedge. The insurer-angel + Insurtech Gateway cap table is a deliberate reuse of that network.
- **Why it acts this way:** an adviser notetaker is a thin, easily-copied feature (OpenAI/Whisper + LLM); durable value requires **owning the adviser workflow and the compliant record**, so TODAY is racing to add voice-agent + coaching + back-office to become sticky "operating system" infrastructure before the feature commoditizes. The "agentic/OS" positioning is the structural response to notetaker commoditization.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Not technically novel:** meeting transcription + auto-notes + CRM push is the exact Jump/Zocks/FinMate playbook; the **inbound-call-answering voice agent** and **insurance-adviser (vs pure-wealth) focus** are the genuine deltas, plus EU-data/compliance framing.
- **Is the value-add real?** Partly. **Traction is a usage figure (3,500 advisers), not monetized ARR or retention** — "advisers use it" ≠ "advisers pay and keep paying." The "up to 10% revenue / 5 hrs saved" outcomes are **vendor claims, un-audited**. So value-add is *plausible* (clear admin pain, credible founder-channel) but **unproven on economics**.
- **Who captures the margin / what breaks it:** the transcription+LLM layer is commoditizing fast → whoever **owns the compliant adviser record and the inbound-call relationship** captures durable margin. TODAY's risk is that (a) carriers/CRMs build this natively, (b) US incumbents (Jump/Zocks) localize into EU, or (c) the "OS" never materializes and it stays a replaceable notetaker. The €2.8M is enough to prove the EU wedge, not to win a continent-wide platform race.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- Plans: spend the Seed on product + commercialisation (sales/hiring), deepen DACH/EU insurer distribution via HTGF/Insurtech Gateway/Rogenhofer. No stated geographic leap beyond Europe, no ARR target disclosed.
- **Sentiment/backdrop:** adviser-AI-copilot is one of the hottest 2025–26 wealthtech sub-sectors (US notetaker funding + the broader [[Portuguese insurtech FRANK raises 2.9m euros]] / [[Konsileo raises £5M equity financing]] European back-office-AI wave). Regulatory tailwind: EU insurance-distribution (IDD) + MiFID record-keeping make a *compliant* AI record a real buying trigger, which is why "compliant agents" is TODAY's lead phrase.
- **2nd-order / counterintuitive:** the strategic insurer-heavy cap table is both the moat and the ceiling — it buys distribution but can **tether TODAY to a few carriers' roadmaps and make it an acquisition target** (a CLARK/HDI/carrier buying the adviser-workflow layer) rather than an independent platform. In a category where US incumbents are better capitalized, the likeliest "win" for a €2.8M EU seed is a strategic exit, not category dominance.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** TODAY sits in insurance/financial-adviser distribution software — the "sales OS / AI copilot for advisers" layer, not underwriting or a consumer app. Global insurance-brokers-software market ~$3.91bn in 2026, projected ~$6.24bn by 2032 at ~8.06% CAGR, with Europe the #2 region at ~28.3% share (per Research and Markets / 360iResearch-cited figures, via web search, as of 2026 — note: these are vendor market-sizing reports, treat as directional, not audited). "Why now": Europe's adviser tooling is being pulled forward by compliance load — the Insurance Distribution Directive (IDD), GDPR and (for in-scope firms) DORA raise the cost of manual, unaudited advice workflows, which is exactly where an AI agent that logs/structures the interaction can claim a "compliant by construction" wedge (analysis). Structure: fragmented — tens of thousands of brokerages/banks/insurers across DACH + UK + France, no dominant adviser-copilot standard; value historically captured by broad orchestration platforms (FNZ, additiv) and legacy agency-management systems, so a lightweight front/back-office AI layer is a greenfield land-grab, not a share-steal (analysis).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: seats/advisers on platform, net revenue retention, attach of automation modules per seat, and (for this cohort) compliance-audit coverage. TODAY reports >3,500 advisers already using the product and is raising to expand across DACH and extend front-/back-office automation (per eu-startups / tech.eu / ArcticStartup, as of 2026-10-08). Named/adjacent players: **additiv** (Advisor Copilot embedded in a wealth-orchestration platform, Europe + APAC), **Neurons Lab / ARKEN** (compliance-aware co-pilot for regulated wealth/asset managers), **FNZ** (incumbent wealth-platform rails for advisers), plus wealth-side entrants like **Quartz** (UK, £2.75M pre-seed). Basis of competition: distribution/integration into existing adviser stacks and regulatory trust, not raw model quality — everyone rents the same LLMs. TODAY's position: niche/early challenger with real adoption (3,500 advisers) but tiny funding vs the orchestration incumbents; moat is thin today (data network effects from adviser interaction logs + switching costs once it sits in daily workflow) and not yet proven (analysis).

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation/post-money disclosed for TODAY, so revenue/EV multiples = no data. Round sizing only: €2.8M (~$3.1M) seed, co-led by HTGF + InsurTech Gateway (per htgf.de / eu-startups, 2026-10). Internal comps from the base (same insurtech-distribution / adviser-automation cohort, this month):
- [[Konsileo raises £5M equity financing]] — UK commercial-broking platform, ~£85M premiums/yr, 220 staff; later-stage, automates broker admin + compliance (2026-10-07).
- [[Nettle raises $4.8M seed for insurer property risk AI]] — $4.8M seed, AI workspace for insurance loss-control/inspections; closest by stage/raise size (2026-10-06).
- [[Portuguese insurtech FRANK raises 2.9m euros]] — €2.9M, European insurtech seed, near-identical cheque size.
Distribution not computed (no disclosed valuations across comps) → qualitative: TODAY's €2.8M is squarely in the European insurtech seed band (€2.8M–$4.8M) set by Nettle/FRANK; in-line, nothing rich or cheap signalled.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Thin, rentable moat / LLM commoditisation.** Core value is an agent over generic LLMs; competitors (additiv, Neurons Lab) and incumbents can bolt on the same copilot — defensibility must come from adviser-data network effects and workflow lock-in, neither proven at seed.
2. **Disintermediation by the incumbent stack.** FNZ / additiv / agency-management vendors own the rails and the data; if they ship "good-enough" copilots natively, TODAY risks being a feature, not a platform — margin captured one layer down.
3. **Regulatory double-edge.** IDD/GDPR/DORA create the demand, but putting an AI agent into regulated advice means liability for mis-advice, auditability and (for EU) the EU AI Act's high-risk-system scrutiny — a compliance miss is existential for an adviser-facing tool, and "3,500 advisers" says adoption, not certified compliance.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Signals a new entry wave into adviser-copilot distribution rather than consolidation — cheap LLMs mean the barrier is distribution + compliance, not tech, so watch whether TODAY converts the 3,500-adviser base into paid DACH seats and lands an insurer/bank channel deal (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: if an orchestration incumbent (additiv/FNZ) ships a native adviser copilot within ~12 months, TODAY's standalone wedge compresses; absent that, a Series A at a disclosed revenue/seat metric would confirm the land-grab is working. Trigger to watch: first named bank/insurer rollout and any disclosed paid-seat count.
