# Fraudio: new merchants hit by fraud within first 90 days

> [!info] 2026-10-14 · 2 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: This Week in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[This Week in Fintech] Fraudio followed 3,923 merchants from their very first payment across more than 127.5 million transactions between January 2025 and April 2026, and found that 10.5% experienced fraud within 90 days. Among those hit, the median time to first fraud was 33 days, and one in five saw it in their first week. The per-transaction fraud rate ran 2.9 times higher during those first 90 days, and 82% of the fraudulent transactions looked unremarkable on amount, currency and payment channel alone.

[This Week in Fintech] The card networks are moving on the same window. Mastercard's Scam Merchant Monitoring Program, in effect since July, requires acquirers and payment facilitators to open an investigation within 72 hours when scam signals hit a threshold, with extra scrutiny for merchants with less than six months of processing history. A new merchant has no history of its own to compare against, so detection has to come from network and peer signals from day one, and a 72-hour clock leaves no room to wait for chargebacks to make the case.

Fraudio Data Led Insights
The data behind Early Fraud 
Fraudio analysed a representative sample drawn from its wider transaction data, covering more than 127.5 million transactions processed between January 2025 and April 2026, following 3,923 merchants from their very first recorded payment.
The question was simple: how soon does fraud find a new business? The answer is sooner than most risk teams expect.
Fraud tends to arrive when a merchant has the least history to judge it against, and when it does, it rarely looks out of place. Here is how the first 90 days break down.
The first 90 days in numbers

 Problem with Early Fraud 
 
Every new merchant is a blind spot. Fraud can strike long before you know what normal looks like.
The risk isn't just earlier, it's heavier. The fraud rate per transaction was 2.9 times higher during merchants' first 90 days .
Fraud strikes when there is the least data to catch it. Nearly half of new merchants hit by fraud in their first 90 days saw it within their first 30 days, and one in five within their first week. That is long before any meaningful baseline of normal customer behaviour exists, so rules and models that rely on a merchant's own history are effectively running blind.
Without context from day one, payment companies are left learning what fraud looks like the expensive way: by absorbing the losses first.

 Main bottlenecks
 
The situation before Fraudio
Merchant acquirers and Issuers previously relied on the traditional approach of a rules-based engine for each transaction. Those rules were hard-coded rules with an “if-then” framework that resulted in many false positives and created disruption in the business of their customers. This system required a large team of people to check every alert, hence why they needed an AI-assisted system to handle the workload.
