DOWNERS GROVE, Ill., October 20, 2026 – Formidium, an AI-native global fund administrator, today announced the acquisition of Ci Fund Services S.A., a Luxembourg-based provider of Private Label Fund solutions. The deal gives Formidium a Luxembourg hub to bring AI-native fund services to Europe's largest fund domicile, home to €9.1 trillion in assets. The transaction was completed following approval by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier (CSSF).
The acquisition strengthens Formidium's ability to serve institutional clients and asset managers across Europe. For managers still navigating disconnected systems and manual workflows, it introduces a different approach: AI-native fund administration built on proprietary technology, backed by Ci Fund Services' Luxembourg expertise.
Luxembourg: A Gateway to Institutional Capital and Cross-Border Distribution
Luxembourg is one of the world's leading fund domiciles, offering global asset managers an established base to structure, manage and distribute funds across Europe.
By acquiring Ci Fund Services S.A., Formidium gains:
An established Private Label Fund platform for managers entering Europe
Local expertise in fund structuring, ongoing management, risk and compliance
Digitized ManCo services, fund marketing and distribution capabilities
Together, these capabilities establish a Luxembourg base for Formidium to expand its AI-native fund administration model across Europe.
Bringing Formidium's AI-Native Technology to Europe
"Managers increasingly want access to European investors without the cost and complexity of building a fund platform from scratch", said Nitin Somani , CEO of Formidium . "Ci Fund Services gives them that path, and our technology allows it to scale for institutional clients operating across borders."
"Over the years, we have built deep client relationships and an extensive network across the European fund industry", said Michael Sanders , CEO of Ci Fund Services S.A. "Joining Formidium strengthens our ability to support fund managers with institutional-grade technology and the operational scale required in today's market, while maintaining the personalized service our clients expect."
Ci Fund Services S.A.'s experienced team will integrate into Formidium's global operations while continuing to support clients under existing service frameworks.
As the businesses integrate, Ci Fund Services clients will gain access to Formidium's AI-native capabilities through ARYA, alongside Seamless for fund accounting and CommonSubDoc for investor onboarding.
The international law firm gunnercooke’s partners Dr Martin Liebi (Zurich) and Dr Matthias Döring (Berlin) acted as legal advisers to Formidium in connection with the acquisition.
About Formidium
 Formidium is an AI-native global fund administrator serving alternative investment funds. Since 2016, the firm has built a global presence with offices in the U.S., India, Canada, Singapore, the U.K., Hong Kong, Switzerland and now Luxembourg. Formidium supports funds across multiple asset classes, with more than $38 billion in assets under administration (AUA).
Formidium's proprietary technology is purpose-built for bespoke fund structures and supports NAV reporting, tax and financial reporting, cross-currency integration, compliance, and automated workflows, enhanced by its AI solution, ARYA.
About Ci Fund Services S.A.
 Ci Fund Services S.A. , formerly Alpina Fund Management S.A., is a Luxembourg-based fund services provider established in 1988. It offers Private Label Fund solutions, including fund structuring, administration, digitized ManCo services, and distribution support, to asset managers, family offices, and institutional investors across Europe.
