# Context-enrichment: VK sells 40% stake in ticketing service Intickets
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

> Note on the stored note's primary source: the extracted `mergers.ru` text is about an UNRELATED deal (giper.fm founder buying 25% of BBQ-goods maker 800 Degrees) — a mis-attached scrape. The real event comes from the "Русский венчур" blurb + the VK press release URL. All facts below are reconstructed from external research (EN + RU), since the stored source text is noise.

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
- On **2 October 2026** VK (ООО «Компания ВК») sold its **40% stake in Intickets** (ООО «Интикетс», cloud ticketing service Intickets.ru) to **ООО «ТКИ»**, a vehicle created in **August 2026** and 100% owned by **Tatyana Kondratenko** — who already held the other 60% of Intickets. Net effect: Kondratenko is now the **sole owner**; VK fully exits. (Interfax / Kommersant / RBC / CNews, Oct 2026.)
- VK had bought the 40% in **May 2024** for **472 mln RUB cash + up to 251 mln RUB deferred = up to ~723 mln RUB** (Interfax). Intickets 2023 financials: revenue **578 mln RUB**, net profit **174 mln RUB**. So VK paid ~4x earnings / ~1.25x revenue for a minority stake — a strategic, not financial, buy.
- Stated 2024 rationale: make VK a **"trusted information system" for the "Pushkin Card"** (state-funded cultural-spend card for 14–22 y.o.), improving program efficiency/security. Post-sale VK says it will keep developing its own **"VK Tickets"** and support Pushkin Card on its own infrastructure; Intickets stays in the Pushkin Card program independently.
- **Sale price/valuation: NOT disclosed** (open). No deferred-payment or earn-out terms published. The absence of a number, for a deal that generated this much press, is itself a tell (see below).
- **Why structured this way / what it reveals:** The buyer is the incumbent majority owner via a shell registered just weeks before close (Aug 2026 → Oct 2 close). That is the shape of a **fast, pre-arranged unwind to a related party**, not a competitive auction. Combined with (a) VK's broader 2026 asset-shedding and (b) the timing right as the **Kanye West / «YE» concert-cancellation scandal** broke (Intickets was the ticketing agent; refunds via Intickets open Oct 12, ~10 lawsuits filed against the organizer) — the clean read is **reputational + strategic distancing**, dressed in portfolio-management language. VK did not state the Kanye link as a reason; that connection is (analysis) but strongly implied by timing (deal closed Oct 2, cancellation confirmed Sep 30).

## [1] Competitors / peers
- Russian ticketing aggregators: **Yandex Afisha** (12.8 mln tickets in H1'26, +36% YoY), **MTS Live**, **Kassir.ru** (+10% volume / +30% RUB in 5 mo; expanding into cinema, 540+ cinemas / 69 cities; adding Pushkin Card + instalments). Combined top-3 share rose from **18%→35% over 5 years** — a consolidating market.
- Intickets is a **B2B SaaS / cloud box-office automation** player (venues, agents, organizers) — a different layer than consumer aggregators; its strategic value to VK was the Pushkin Card "trusted system" rail, not consumer GMV.
- **Why the landscape is this way (2nd order):** The platforms with captive distribution (Yandex, MTS — both own super-apps/telecom) are winning share; a standalone back-office automation vendor like Intickets has no consumer funnel of its own, so its upside depended on a patron (VK) plugging it into the Pushkin Card and VK's audience. Remove the patron and Intickets reverts to a modest, profitable-but-capped infrastructure vendor. Note: VK in 2024 also reportedly took a stake in **Kassir.ru** (>1.2 bn RUB total spent on ticketing that year, CNews) — so this is VK **retreating from a ticketing-consolidation thesis it built in 2024**.

## [2] Company history / fit
- VK (ex-Mail.ru Group): **6 straight annual losses**, net loss **24.9 bn RUB ($289M)** in 2025, cumulative ~195 bn RUB lost over 6 years; **first profitable quarter since 2022 in Q2'26** (328 mln RUB), aided one-off by asset sales. Net debt ~**62 bn RUB**, Net Debt/EBITDA ~2.6.
- 2026 divestiture wave: **Tochka Bank 25% → Interros for 21.2 bn RUB** (closed Apr 16, 2026; drove the Q2 profit); **RuStore sold** to its GM (Jul 16, 2026, partly to de-risk from EU sanctions). Intickets is the **third, much smaller** exit in the same sequence.
- **Why VK acts this way (structural):** A chronically loss-making, debt-laden, state-aligned holding under sanctions is being pushed toward **portfolio concentration on core (ads, VK ecosystem, VK Tech) and deleveraging**. Non-core minority stakes (a bank, an app store, a ticketing SaaS) are liquidity + risk-reduction sources. Intickets specifically: ~723 mln RUB is immaterial vs 62 bn RUB net debt, so the driver is **less cash, more de-risking** — shedding exposure to the Pushkin-Card-adjacent reputational hit from Kanye.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novelty of the event: low.** It is a routine minority unwind to the existing majority owner. No new product, no capability, no market structure change.
- **Did the 2024 thesis "fly"?** Partially/unclear (open). VK built "VK Tickets" and stays in Pushkin Card, but it is **abandoning the Intickets vehicle** it bought precisely to anchor that program — suggesting the integration value never materialized enough to justify holding through a scandal. That is the real signal: a **quiet admission the 2024 ticketing-consolidation bet underperformed**.
- **Who captures the margin:** Consumer aggregators (Yandex/MTS) capture distribution margin; back-office vendors like Intickets capture thin SaaS fees. VK's exit concedes it won't own the ticketing distribution layer.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- Kondratenko gets full control of a profitable (174 mln RUB NP in 2023) niche vendor, but now carrying the **Kanye refund/litigation overhang** (refunds open Oct 12; ~10 suits against the organizer — Intickets is agent, exposure unclear/open).
- VK continues deleveraging and leans on **VK Tech** (IPO repeatedly delayed, timing market-dependent) as the value story.
- **Why / 2nd order:** Each small exit marginally cleans VK's balance sheet and risk profile but also **shrinks the ecosystem breadth** that was the original Mail.ru/VK thesis. The counterintuitive effect: "portfolio discipline" under distress reads less like strategy and more like **forced simplification of a loss-making state champion** (cf. Carnegie/Moscow Times framing).

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** This sits at the intersection of (a) VK's balance-sheet repair and (b) the Russian cultural-events/ticketing market. VK's 2025 IFRS: revenue RUB 160bn (+8% YoY), adjusted EBITDA RUB 22.6bn (EBITDA-positive for the first time, margin ~14%), net debt roughly halved to RUB 82bn, net debt/EBITDA 3.6x against a stated 2–3x target; net loss RUB 25.4bn (vs RUB 95.5bn in 2024) (per VK FY2025 press release / Interfax / AKM, as of 2026-03-19). So VK is still over-levered and loss-making at the bottom line — the context for shedding non-core stakes. The Russian cultural-events ticketing market was ~RUB 227bn in 2024 and an estimated ~RUB 265bn in 2025 (+17% YoY), split across concerts (~RUB 53bn), cinema (~RUB 45bn) and museums/exhibitions (~RUB 38bn); the concert segment alone reached ~RUB 66bn in 2025 (+24%) (per tadviser citations, as of 2025). The state "Пушкинская карта" program — Intickets' original strategic rationale for VK — has cumulatively processed ~115m tickets for ~RUB 57bn since 2021 launch (per VTB/Interfax, 2026). **Structure:** fragmented, low-barrier SaaS-plus-distribution; value accrues to whoever owns the operator/venue relationship and the trusted-system status for state programs. **Why now:** VK is deleveraging by serial disposals — RRUB 21.2bn for its 25% in Точка Bank (sold to Interros, Apr 2026) and 100% of RuStore (Jul 2026) — and had an operational trigger: Intickets sold tickets to the cancelled Kanye West St. Petersburg concerts, a reputational event preceding the exit (per RBC/CNews/vc.ru, Oct 2026).

**Competitive landscape.** Ticketing KPIs: GMV (ticket value sold), take rate / commission, number of connected cultural institutions, repeat-venue retention. VK bought 40% of Intickets in spring/May 2024 as a "trusted information system" to run Пушкинская карта. The 40% now goes to ООО «ТКИ» (registered Aug 2026, owned by Tatyana Kondratenko, who also directly holds the remaining 60%) — i.e. VK sold back to the founder/control side, not to a strategic consolidator (per smart-lab/mergers.ru, deal closed 2026-10-02). Crucially VK keeps its own "VK Билеты" product and continues Пушкинская карта on its own infrastructure — so this is an un-bundling, not an exit from ticketing. **Protagonist position:** VK is a non-core minority seller retreating to its first-party stack (analysis); moat here is distribution/state-program access, which VK retains independently of Intickets.

**Comps & multiples.** No deal value disclosed for the Intickets 40% — **no data** on price, GMV, or implied multiple; any EV/Revenue or take-rate math is **[UNSOURCED]**. Internal comps (ticketing/event-fintech M&A and VK portfolio moves) from base: [[Nigeria's Wakanow acquires ticketing platform Nairabox]] (travel-fintech buying a ticketing platform, Oct 2025); [[Cloud9 acquires Mtickets to merge digital banking with event finance]] (neobank + event ticketing, May 2026); [[VK launches Discovery AI neural search across products]] (same protagonist, first-party product focus). None carry disclosed transaction economics → distribution not computed, qualitative comparison only: the cross-cutting pattern is fintech/platform players treating ticketing as a distribution/payments adjacency, consolidating or divesting around their core — consistent with VK's move. **In-line** read: a minority-stake divestiture to the controlling shareholder is a cheap, low-signal transaction, not a market-clearing valuation event.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Rationale downgrade / reputational trigger.** The exit follows the cancelled Kanye West concerts that Intickets served; selling a "trusted system" stake after a visible failure suggests the Пушкинская-карта thesis underdelivered — second-order: weakens the case that VK's 2024 bolt-on acquisitions created durable value.
2. **Buyer concentration / related-party optics.** The 40% went to a newly-formed entity (Aug 2026) wholly owned by the same person holding the other 60% — a non-arm's-length-looking buyer means the undisclosed price is a poor mark for the asset and gives no external validation.
3. **Deleveraging still incomplete.** At 3.6x net debt/EBITDA vs a 2–3x target, VK remains a forced seller of minority stakes; expect further non-core disposals, which caps re-rating until leverage normalizes (second-order: disposals may be price-insensitive).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** For the sector: nothing structural — Russian ticketing stays fragmented; VK competes via first-party "VK Билеты" rather than via a minority stake (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: VK's disposal program is leverage-driven, not strategic, so more non-core exits follow and net debt/EBITDA trends toward 2–3x by FY2026; it breaks if VK instead makes a fresh acquisition or discloses an Intickets price implying a strategic (not distressed) valuation. Trigger to watch: VK FY2026 results (EBITDA guided >RUB 24bn) and the next disposal.

## Earnings Review

<!-- enrichment:earnings_review -->
**Verdict (headline read).** M&A/divestiture, not an earnings event — but VK's reported financials are the material context. Latest reported period: **FY 2025 (audited IFRS, released 2026-03-19)**. Read: revenue **RUB 160bn (+8% YoY)**, EBITDA **RUB 22.6bn** (margin ~14%, +17 p.p., vs a negative result in 2024), but still a **sixth straight annual net loss of RUB 25.4bn** (narrowed from RUB 95.49bn in 2024) and **net debt RUB 82bn (net debt/EBITDA ~3.6×)** after a **RUB 112bn 2025 recapitalization / additional share issue**. A stretched, loss-making, de-levering balance sheet is the frame for why VK is shedding a non-core 40% stake in Intickets.

**Key figures (FY 2025 IFRS, with growth).**
- Revenue: **RUB 160bn, +8% YoY** (8M/2025 release headline).
- EBITDA: **RUB 22.6bn** vs negative EBITDA in 2024; EBITDA margin **~14%, +17 p.p. YoY** — first positive, operating-leverage-driven EBITDA after years of burn.
- Net loss: **RUB 25.4bn** (total IFRS net loss, Interfax/akm.ru), narrowed ~73% from **RUB 95.49bn** in 2024. (The Moscow Times cites **RUB 24.9bn / ~$288.8m**, the loss attributable to owners — the two figures reconcile as total vs owner-attributable.)
- Net debt: **RUB 82bn**, cut by more than half YoY; **net debt/EBITDA ~3.6×**, with a stated short-/medium-term target of **2–3×**.
- Interest burden: interest expense on loans and borrowings **RUB 23.25bn in 2025** — i.e. the interest bill alone is ~roughly the size of EBITDA (RUB 22.6bn), so operating earnings barely cover financing costs. Weighted-average debt rate **8.2%** at period end.

**By segment / driver.** FY2025 revenue growth was carried by VK's core Social Platforms/ecosystem advertising plus the B2B/EdTech segments (VK Tech, whose own 2025 IFRS profit rose ~37.56% per akm.ru). The EBITDA swing to positive is the key operating story — cost discipline + ad monetization turning the holding EBITDA-positive for the first time in years. Intickets (ticketing, tied to the "Pushkin Card" program, 40% bought spring 2024) is not a growth driver and sits outside this core — a logical divestiture candidate.

**vs expectations / prior period.** No public sell-side consensus for VK (sanctioned, Moscow-listed, thin external coverage) → beat/miss assessed qualitatively vs prior year: revenue **+8%**, EBITDA **negative → +RUB 22.6bn**, net loss **−73%**, net debt **−>50%** — a broad YoY improvement on every line, though still loss-making. Prior-period base in the corpus: [[VK launches Discovery AI neural search across products]] (2026-07, product, no figures). No consensus figures available — any Street estimate would be [UNSOURCED].

**Guidance / forward.** No formal quantitative guidance in the public releases beyond the **net debt/EBITDA 2–3× deleveraging target**. The RUB 112bn additional share issue was explicitly aimed at **reducing the debt burden** — recapitalization, not growth capex. Management tone: deleveraging-first. What they stay quiet on: a clear path to net-income breakeven (still six consecutive loss years) and the cash timeline for covering the RUB 23.25bn annual interest bill without further equity support.

**Thesis-flags.**
1. *Interest ≈ EBITDA.* Fact: interest expense RUB 23.25bn vs EBITDA RUB 22.6bn → why: financing costs consume essentially all operating earnings → why it matters: little/no free cash to self-fund → second-order: asset sales (Intickets 40%) and recapitalizations become the lever to cut debt, directly explaining this divestiture.
2. *Recapitalization dependence.* The RUB 112bn 2025 equity raise kept leverage falling; absent it, net debt/EBITDA stays punitive → signals VK monetizes non-core stakes (ticketing) rather than relying on organic cash generation.
3. *Positive EBITDA but still net-loss.* The EBITDA turn (+17 p.p. margin) is real operating progress, but the sixth straight net loss + 8.2% debt cost mean the thesis hinges on continued deleveraging — selling Intickets is consistent with that playbook, not a strategic retreat from fintech/infrastructure per se.
4. *De-PR.* Releases lead with "revenue +8%" and the EBITDA turn; they are quieter on the absolute net-loss persistence and the interest coverage — the divestiture is the market reading through to balance-sheet pressure the PR underplays.
