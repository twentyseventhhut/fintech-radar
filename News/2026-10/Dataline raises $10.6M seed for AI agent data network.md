Forecast Trend Report by Period
Dataline, a decentralized data network for AI agents, has raised $10.6 million in seed funding. The company plans to expand its data supply and verification capabilities and launch a token this year for network payments and data access.
The round was co-led by Lattice and Dispersion Capital, The Block reported on Oct. 21. Coinbase Ventures, Symbolic Capital, Anagram, and Robinhood co-founder and Chief Executive Officer Vlad Tenev also participated. The deal was signed in April through a SAFE, or simple agreement for future equity, with token warrants included. The valuation was not disclosed.
Dataline is a platform where AI agents can query on-chain and off-chain data and pay usage fees. Its in-house AI evaluates the reliability, recency, and consistency of information. It also provides context for decision-making alongside raw figures.
Dataline co-founder Nuo Xi said a simple price feed or API alone cannot determine whether a stablecoin trading at $0.97 is a buying opportunity or the start of a depeg. By combining price data with peg-stability signals and exchange outlier information, the platform helps bots, smart contracts, and human users understand the situation.
The platform currently supports about 50 data sources. Its first "AI Data Launch" group includes Hyperliquid, Polymarket, Kalshi, Base, Robinhood Chain, BNB Chain, Sui, and TON. Developers on Base and Robinhood Chain will receive free access.
Dataline plans to conduct a public token sale on Base using Uniswap's continuous clearing auction, or CCA, system. It will disclose the sale size and terms next week. The token will be used for payments and data access. The company also plans to let users stake tokens to maintain access to data feeds instead of paying subscription fees.
Dataline was founded after winning the BNB Chain AI Hackathon in 2025. It plans to use the new funding to expand data sources, improve verification technology, and move into robotics and AI training data.

### [0] What happened
Dataline, a decentralized data network for AI agents, raised a **$10.6M seed round**, first reported by The Block on **2026-10-21** ([cryptorank summary](https://cryptorank.io/insights/deals/dataline-seed-2026-10-21)). The round was **co-led by Lattice and Dispersion Capital**, with participation from **Coinbase Ventures, Symbolic Capital, Anagram, and Robinhood co-founder/CEO Vlad Tenev**; reporting also names a former CFTC head plus founders/partners from Venmo, Anchorage Digital, NEA, General Catalyst, Jump Capital and FalconX among undisclosed backers ([KuCoin flash](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/decentralized-ai-data-network-dataline-closes-10-6m-seed-round-plans-token-launch-by-year-end)). The deal was **signed in April via a SAFE with a token warrant**; valuation was not disclosed ([bloomingbit](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/122721)).

### [1] What Dataline does
Dataline is a platform where **AI agents query on-chain and off-chain data and pay per-use fees**. Its in-house AI evaluates data **reliability, recency and consistency** and supplies decision context (not just raw figures) — e.g., judging whether a stablecoin at $0.97 is a buy or the start of a depeg by fusing price with peg-stability and exchange-outlier signals (co-founder **Nuo Shi**, per The Block; some trackers spell "Nuo Xi"). The live platform connects to **~50 data sources**; the first "AI Data Launch" cohort includes **Hyperliquid, Polymarket, Kalshi, Base, Robinhood Chain, BNB Chain, Sui and TON**, with free access for Base and Robinhood Chain developers. Dataline was founded after winning the **BNB Chain AI Hackathon in 2025**.

### [2] Token / business model
A **token launch is planned by year-end**: a **public sale on Base via Uniswap's continuous clearing auction (CCA)**, size/terms to be disclosed "next week" (~late Oct 2026). The token is positioned for **network payments and data access**, with **staking to maintain data-feed access in place of subscription fees** — i.e., a crypto-native, usage-metered alternative to SaaS data subscriptions.

### [3] Why it matters / category
This sits in the fast-forming **"data layer for agentic commerce"** — infrastructure letting autonomous agents transact and act on trusted data. 2026 landscape maps describe a multi-layer agentic-commerce stack (payments, protocols, trust/verification) with heavy VC inflow ([stellagent landscape](https://stellagent.ai/insights/agentic-commerce-landscape-map-2026)). Dataline's bet is that agents need **verified, priced, machine-payable data feeds**, blending the oracle/data-marketplace model with pay-per-query AI economics. Internal comps:
- [[Tria raises $12M for self-custodial neobank for humans and AI]] — AI-agent-native payment/custody rails.
- [[Basis Theory raises $33 million for agentic commerce]] — agentic-commerce infrastructure raise.
- [[Nuvei supports Visa Trusted Agent Protocol]] — agent identity/trust plumbing.
- [[Bud Financial launches MCP server for AI banking]] — exposing financial data to AI agents.

### [4] Open / unknowns
- **Valuation undisclosed**; dilution and token/equity split (SAFE + token warrant) opaque.
- **Token-first revenue model is unproven** — real demand for paid agent data feeds vs. free/standard APIs is speculative.
- **Regulatory exposure**: a public token sale (even via CCA) carries securities-law and depeg/oracle-liability risk; a former CFTC head as backer is notable but not a safe harbor.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector context — data/oracle rails for agentic commerce (seed-stage climate).** Dataline sits in the "data & context" layer of the fast-forming AI-agent stack, below the payments/identity rails that are attracting the most 2026 seed capital (Basis Theory, Nekuda, Skyfire reportedly ~$50M combined for agent financial rails; The Block, Pantera). The 2026 agentic-commerce narrative is built around payment protocols — OpenAI/Stripe's Agentic Commerce Protocol (Shared Payment Token), Mastercard Agent Pay / Agentic Tokens, Stripe's Machine Payments Protocol, and Coinbase's x402 for account-less agent micropayments. Dataline's pitch is adjacent but distinct: it supplies the *data* agents query (on-chain + off-chain, ~50 sources at launch incl. Hyperliquid, Polymarket, Kalshi, Base, Robinhood Chain, BNB Chain, Sui, TON) with an in-house layer scoring reliability/recency/consistency — i.e. a context/oracle play rather than a payment rail. (Sources: [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/deals/2026-10-21-dataline-decentralized-data-network-ai-agents-funding-ico-421596), [Pantera](https://panteracapital.com/financial-rails-of-agentic-commerce/), [original note source](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/122721))

**Round mechanics (grounded, verified vs independent reporting).** $10.6M seed co-led by **Lattice** and Samsung-backed **Dispersion Capital**; participants include Coinbase Ventures, Symbolic Capital, Anagram, and Robinhood co-founder/CEO Vlad Tenev, plus angels/partners from Venmo, Anchorage Digital, NEA, General Catalyst, Jump Capital, FalconX and a former CFTC head (The Block, via BloomingBit). Structure is the notable part: the deal was **signed in April via a SAFE with token warrants**, and the company plans a **public token sale on Base using Uniswap's continuous clearing auction (CCA)**, with size/terms "next week." Valuation undisclosed. Company emerged from the 2025 BNB Chain AI Hackathon. This is an equity-plus-token hybrid, not a conventional venture seed.

**In-base comps (grep fallback — semsearch down, OpenRouter 402).** Recent agentic-infra raises in the DB frame the stage/size:
- **Basis Theory — $33M** (agentic commerce payment vault for merchants) — payments/identity layer, larger.
- **Paid — $21M seed** (AI results-based / outcome billing).
- **Zania — $18M Series A** (agentic AI compliance).
- **Tria — $12M** (self-custodial neobank + payments for humans *and* AI; also planning a public token allocation — closest structural analogue to Dataline's equity+token model).
- **TabSense — $5M** (agentic POS).
- **Paygentic — $2M pre-seed** (payments infra for AI-native businesses).
Dataline's $10.6M sits mid-pack for the cohort; it is the only one here whose core product is *data/oracle* rather than payments/billing/compliance, and shares the token-distribution angle only with Tria.

**Risk flags (skeptical).**
1. **Crowded, dominant-incumbent data layer.** The decentralized-data/oracle niche is led by Chainlink (TVS ~$75B, 1,000+ feeds, 2,100+ projects per DeFi trackers) and challenger Pyth (Data Marketplace with Fidelity/Euronext, targeting the ~$50B market-data industry). A 50-source seed-stage entrant must prove differentiated value beyond raw feeds — Dataline's bet is the reliability/recency/consistency scoring layer, which is unproven at scale.
2. **Token-first monetization risk.** Replacing subscription revenue with token-staking access ("stake to keep data access") plus a CCA public sale ties the business model to token-market conditions and invites regulatory scrutiny (utility-vs-security, public sale optics). A former-CFTC-head angel does not neutralize this.
3. **SAFE-with-token-warrant valuation opacity.** Undisclosed valuation, April signing only now public, and a token warrant structure make the true economics/dilution hard to assess — standard for crypto seeds but a transparency flag.
4. **Vaporware / execution gap.** Launch cohort names (Hyperliquid, Polymarket, Kalshi, Base, Robinhood Chain) are listed as supported sources, not disclosed paying design-partner commitments; free access for Base/Robinhood Chain developers suggests demand still has to be bought. Planned expansion into "robotics and AI training data" is scope creep for a seed-stage team.
5. **Data provenance/liability.** A scoring layer that tells agents whether "a stablecoin at $0.97 is a buy or a depeg" assumes liability for bad signals feeding autonomous trading/smart-contract actions — a thin, unbattested oracle is the known "fatal flaw" for agent decisioning.
6. **Concentration & reflexivity to crypto cycle.** Heavily crypto-native cap table and on-chain data focus make the venture correlated to a single market cycle; a down-turn hits both the token sale and the on-chain demand it serves.
