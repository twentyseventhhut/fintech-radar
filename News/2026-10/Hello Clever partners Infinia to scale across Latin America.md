Hello Clever partners with Infinia to scale across Latin America
 Hello Clever has partnered with Infinia to expand its payment operations across Latin America. The partnership gives Hello Clever access to local payment rails in Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Peru, Bolivia, Uruguay and Paraguay.
Latin America’s e-commerce market is projected to exceed US$215 billion in 2026, growing 1.5 times faster than the global average. As online commerce expands, merchants need payment experiences that reflect how consumers actually pay in each market.
Through Infinia’s infrastructure, Hello Clever will access virtual accounts, local pay-ins and payouts, and on- and off-ramp services through a single partner. This removes the need to manage multiple financial providers and separate local payment integrations in each country.
Local payment systems are central to this approach. From Brazil’s Pix and Mexico’s SPEI to Argentina’s Transferencias 3.0, Colombia’s Bre-B or Peru’s interoperable Yape payments, each market has its own established payment methods and infrastructure. Connecting to these systems helps merchants offer familiar ways to pay while simplifying the complexity behind cross-border operations.
For merchants, one integration with Hello Clever is now enough to sell into all eight countries. Shoppers in those markets can pay using the local methods they already know and trust. Merchants can grow their sales in the region without adding new payment providers.
“Expanding across markets requires reliable local infrastructure. Infinia came highly recommended, and it has deep local payment capabilities across Latin America. By connecting Infinia’s rails into our platform, we’re giving our merchants access to eight new markets without the complexity of adding new providers,” said Caroline Tran, Co-Founder and CEO of Hello Clever.
“Businesses operating across borders need more than international transfers. They need the local infrastructure to collect, convert and disburse funds in each market. We’re helping Hello Clever connect its platform to the financial rails needed to operate across Latin America,” said Ianai Urwicz, CEO and Co-Founder of Infinia.
The partnership combines Hello Clever’s payments platform with Infinia’s multi-market infrastructure. It provides a foundation for Hello Clever’s continued growth in the region, and both companies expect it to drive new partnerships and revenue as Hello Clever adds more payment corridors around the world.
“Every corridor we open makes it easier for our merchants to sell globally. Latin America is a major step in that journey, and we’re excited to be building it with Infinia,” said Tran.
The partnership comes as Hello Clever opens its new global headquarters in New York, marking the company’s next chapter of growth and a stronger global focus for its operations. Founded in Sydney, Hello Clever now has teams across Australia, Singapore, Japan and the US.
