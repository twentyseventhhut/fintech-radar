## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
### [0] What happened (the claim)
Sentora published its **"Fintech Onchain" report** (sentora.com, annual report 2026), arguing that balances moved into **onchain earn / savings programs tend to stay put** — i.e. high retention/stickiness of deposits. The report frames onchain infrastructure in 2026 as a *financial product layer* (savings, treasury, credit, payroll), not a speculative trading feature, and backs this with shipped-deployment case figures: **Kraken's ~$800M DeFi Earn program**, **Coinbase's ~$2.3B in crypto-backed originations**, **Deel's payroll wallet in 80+ countries**, and a **seventeen-bank tokenized-deposit consortium targeting a 2027 launch** (figures as stated in the source report; not independently audited here).
Source: <https://sentora.com/research/reports/annual-report-2026>

### [1] Who Sentora is
Sentora is the **institutional DeFi platform born from the May 2025 merger of IntoTheBlock (onchain analytics/risk) and Trident Digital (structured liquidity)**, which closed with a **$25M Series A** (backers incl. New Form Capital, Ripple, Tribe Capital). IntoTheBlock's legacy analytics API was sunset 15 Aug 2025, folded into Sentora Research.
- <https://www.theblock.co/post/353220/intotheblock-trident-merger-institutional-defi-platform-sentora-25-million-usd-series-a>
- <https://www.coindesk.com/business/2025/05/05/intotheblock-and-trident-merge-secure-usd25m-backing-to-build-institutional-defi-gateway>
- <https://medium.com/sentora/announcing-sentora-a-new-institutional-defi-platform-born-from-the-merger-of-intotheblock-and-1351c6f33182>

Sentora is not just a researcher — it is an active **vault curator/strategy designer**, so this report is also marketing for its own book.

### [2] Supporting / corroborating evidence
- **Kraken DeFi Earn (curated by Sentora)** crossed **$600M deposits in <6 months**; lifetime depositors ~**90,000 addresses by end-June 2026** (up from ~50k in the first 4 months); weekly net yield distributed scaled toward **~$360k/week late June 2026**. <https://sentora.com/case-studies/kraken-defi-earn-vaults-curated-by-sentora-hit-500m-deposits-milestone-in-under-6-months>
- **$700M+** across Sentora-curated **Morpho Vaults**; **sentUSD** vault (Ethereum) ~**$94.5M on 30 Sep 2026**, 30-day APY ~**7.63%**. <https://morpho.org/stories/sentora>
- The deposit-growth curve (balances + depositor count rising together, not churning) is the empirical basis for the "stickiness" claim.

### [3] Why it matters (context in the market)
The onchain-earn / curated-vault market is consolidating fast in 2026. **Morpho** (~$16.9B total deposits, 4 Oct 2026) is the dominant rails; curators (**Gauntlet, Steakhouse Financial, Re7, Block Analitica, Bitwise, Sentora**) compete to route deposits, typically beating Aave USDC by ~100–300 bps. Exchanges embed these: **Kraken DeFi Earn**, **Coinbase** (Morpho-powered BTC loans). Prior Fintech Radar notes track the same theme:
- [[OpenTrade secures $17 million for stablecoin yield infrastructure]] (stablecoin yield infra, May 2026)
- [[TruFin raises undisclosed round for onchain yield management]] (institutional onchain yield, Mar 2026)
- [[Theo launches thUSD, raises $100m on gold-backed yield strategy]] (onchain yield product, Mar 2026)
- [[Osero raises $13.5 million for stablecoin yield infrastructure]] (stablecoin yield infra, May 2026)
Sources: <https://morpho.org/stories/sentora>, <https://defiprime.com/defi-vaults-guide>, <https://coinstancy.com/article/best-stablecoin-yield-platforms-in-2026-coinstancy-vs-coinbase-nexo-aave-morpho-and-kraken/>

### [4] Open questions / caveats
- "Balances tend to stay put" is Sentora's own data on its own curated vaults — **selection bias / vendor-marketing**, measured in a rising market (sticky ≠ sticky through a drawdown or yield compression).
- The headline $800M / $2.3B / 17-bank figures are quoted from Sentora's report page, **not independently confirmed**; the $600M case-study URL literally says "500M milestone" (figure inconsistency between report copy and case study).
- Retention cohort definitions (addresses vs. unique users; dust balances) are unspecified.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
### Red-team (challenge)

1. **Is this an independent finding or vendor marketing?** Vendor marketing. Sentora curates the vaults it measures — the "stickiness" claim promotes its own book. Treat as directional, not independent.
2. **Does the data support "balances stay put"?** Partially. Deposits + depositor count both rose (Kraken ~$600M, ~90k addresses by Jun 2026), but growth ≠ retention; no cohort churn/half-life disclosed. **Open.**
3. **Tested through stress?** No evidence the stickiness held through a yield-compression or de-peg/drawdown event. Measured in a rising 2026 market. **Open.**
4. **Are the headline figures verified?** $800M Kraken / $2.3B Coinbase / 17-bank consortium are quoted from Sentora's own report page; not independently confirmed here. The case-study URL says "500M" while report copy says "$600M"/"$800M" — internal inconsistency. **Open / caution.**
5. **Is this new news or a re-run?** The Fintech Onchain *annual report 2026* is a fresh, first-time publication (dated 2026-10-08 retrieval). Prior Radar notes cover the theme but not this report. **Fresh.**
6. **Is the market real / sizable?** Yes — Morpho ~$16.9B deposits (Oct 2026); curated vaults are the dominant onchain-earn model; exchanges (Kraken, Coinbase) embed them. Theme is well-corroborated.
7. **Does Sentora have a defensible moat?** Weak-to-moderate. Curators compete on brand/risk track record (Gauntlet, Steakhouse, Re7, Bitwise). Sentora's edge = IntoTheBlock analytics + exchange distribution (Kraken), but curation is commoditizing.
8. **Counter-explanation for "stickiness"?** Inertia + withdrawal friction + lack of better alternatives, not product loyalty. Also DeFi gas/UX friction discourages churn. **Open.**
9. **Any conflicting prior Radar signal?** No contradiction; [[OpenTrade secures $17 million for stablecoin yield infrastructure]], [[TruFin raises undisclosed round for onchain yield management]], [[Theo launches thUSD, raises $100m on gold-backed yield strategy]] all point the same direction (growing institutional onchain yield).
10. **Is the source primary?** Yes — note's source is sentora.com's own report; external corroboration (TheBlock, CoinDesk, Morpho) supports the company/market backdrop.
11. **Regulatory risk unaddressed?** Yes — onchain "savings/earn" products face securities/consumer-protection scrutiny; report frames them as savings products without regulatory caveats. **Open.**
12. **Does the headline overstate?** Mildly. "Tend to stay put" is a soft, defensible claim; the surrounding report figures are the aggressive part.
13. **Actionable/novel for readers?** Moderately — confirms a known trend with fresh proprietary datapoints (depositor counts, weekly yield), useful as a dated marker, but no surprising reversal.
14. **Duplicate of an archived report?** No same-report archive found (grep: only this note tagged company/sentora). **Not a duplicate.**
15. **Would it move a decision?** For a fintech product team evaluating an onchain-earn build, yes as a reference; for an investor, it's confirmatory not catalytic.

**Importance: 3/5** — Credible, fresh, well-corroborated proprietary datapoints on a genuinely important 2026 theme (onchain earn going mainstream via curated vaults), but it is vendor-authored marketing restating a known trend, with unverified headline figures and no independent retention/stress evidence. Solid context marker, not a market-moving revelation.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Sentora sits in **institutional onchain yield / DeFi "earn" infrastructure + risk analytics** — the layer packaging DeFi lending/vault yield into compliant products for funds, treasuries and fintech front-ends. Size proxies (web, as of 2026): stablecoin market cap ~$308bn, +14.3% YoY (per reap.global, Aug 2026); ~$42bn sits in stablecoin yield-farming protocols (per DeFiLlama, via defiprime, 2026); tokenized RWAs surpassed $23.6bn in Mar 2026 (via reap.global). Curated-vault TVL concentrates in a few names: Morpho ~$5.8bn, Sky Savings Rate ~$4.92bn of Sky's $7.1bn suite (Aug 2026). Realistic onchain-earn yields are ~3.5–9% APY, e.g. USDC on Aave ~5–6% (via defiprime/eco.com). Structure: **fragmented at the protocol layer, consolidating at the access/curation layer** — value is migrating from raw protocols to curators/risk-managers and compliant front-ends (analysis). "Why now": the bulk of ~$312bn in stablecoins earns nothing between transactions, so the drivers are (a) the hunt for yield on idle stablecoins, (b) the generational wealth transfer Sentora's own report cites, and (c) institutions wanting risk-managed, compliant access rather than touching DeFi directly (e.g. Bitwise deploying Morpho vaults ~6% APY for institutional depositors, via defiprime).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: TVL / AUM, net yield (APY) after fees, and retention/stickiness of balances (the note's own thesis — "balances tend to stay put"). Key players: **risk/analytics peers** Gauntlet (quant risk models) and Chaos Labs (risk, analytics, oracles); **curated-vault/yield venues** Morpho, Sky, Aave, plus institutional packagers like Bitwise. Basis of competition: trust/risk-management credibility + distribution to institutions, not price. Recent peer moves: Gauntlet raised **$125m Series C in Jul 2026** ($170m total); Chaos Labs has raised **$75m** (Galaxy, Haun, PayPal Ventures). Protagonist's position: **niche but credible** — Sentora formed May 2025 from the IntoTheBlock + Trident Digital merger with a **$25m Series A** (led by New Form Capital; Ripple, Tribe among backers), bringing together **>$3bn of prior institutional DeFi deployments** under one brand (via theblock.co / finsmes, May 2025). Moat `(analysis)`: proprietary onchain behavioral data (the retention finding) + combined analytics-plus-structuring stack — switching-cost/intangibles moat, not yet network effects.

**Comps & multiples.** Private names; no public revenue/EV figures disclosed → **EV/Revenue, P/E, EV/EBITDA = no data** `[UNSOURCED]`. Funding-stage comparison only (round size, not valuation):
- Sentora — $25m Series A, >$3bn historical deployments (theblock.co, May 2025)
- Gauntlet — $170m total, $125m Series C Jul 2026 (Tracxn/Crunchbase, 2026)
- Chaos Labs — $75m total, Series A (Tracxn, 2026)

Sentora is the **least-capitalized of the three** institutional-DeFi risk/analytics peers; distribution not computed (no comparable valuation figures). Internal comps from the base (adjacent onchain-yield/earn deals): [[Kraken launches DeFi Earn vaults powered by Ink]], [[Ground raises $3.6M to pipe on-chain yield into fintech apps]], [[OpenTrade secures $17 million for stablecoin yield infrastructure]], [[TruFin raises undisclosed round for onchain yield management]], [[Otomato raises $2 million for real-time DeFi intelligence layer]].

**Risk flags.**
1. **Marketing report presented as independent data.** The source is Sentora's own "Fintech Onchain" annual report — the "balances stay put" retention claim is self-published, undated at the data level, and sells the product; treat as PR, not verified behavioral evidence. Why: a vendor with the measurement incentive reporting its own stickiness is unfalsifiable from outside.
2. **Under-capitalized vs peers in a trust-led market.** $25m Series A (2025) vs Gauntlet's $170m / Chaos Labs' $75m — in institutional DeFi where credibility and balance-sheet depth win mandates, Sentora risks being out-resourced on risk tooling and enterprise sales. Why: thin runway limits the audits/integrations institutions demand before allocating.
3. **Yield durability / disintermediation.** The 3.5–9% "earn" APY depends on protocol conditions and can compress or spike with leverage cycles; if balances are "sticky" only while yield holds, the thesis is cycle-dependent, and margin can be captured by the curator/front-end (Morpho, Bitwise) above Sentora's layer. Why: Sentora rides someone else's rails and rate environment.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` The signal is that onchain "earn" is re-rating from speculative DeFi into a **retention-driven savings product** — if balance stickiness is real, it shifts the defensible value to the curation/risk layer Sentora occupies, not the protocols. Falsifiable thesis: institutional onchain-earn AUM at curated venues keeps compounding even through a yield drawdown. Trigger/what breaks it: a sharp APY compression (sub-4%) or a vault-level loss event → if balances then leave, "tend to stay put" was a bull-market artifact, not behavior.
