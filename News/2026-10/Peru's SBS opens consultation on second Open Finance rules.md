# Peru's SBS opens consultation on second Open Finance rules

> [!info] 2026-10-28 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[This Week in Fintech] 🇵🇪 Peru’s SBS opened a consultation on the second part of its Open Finance rules, which sets out how banks and fintechs will verify each other and share customer data securely.
