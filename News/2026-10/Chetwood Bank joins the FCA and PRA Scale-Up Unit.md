Chetwood Bank has been selected for the second cohort of the Scale-up Unit, a joint initiative by the Prudential Regulation Authority and the Financial Conduct Authority. This selection provides the digital challenger with direct regulatory support, a crucial advantage for fintechs navigating the transition from early-stage innovation to large-scale, sustainable banking operations.

Chetwood Bank will join the Scale-up Unit to receive tailored guidance on regulatory processes, product innovation, and the impact of new policy proposals. The unit, which was originally announced by the Chancellor of the Exchequer in October 2025 as a pillar of the Government’s Financial Services Growth and Competitiveness Strategy, is designed to assist fast-growing firms as they enter new markets and scale their customer bases.

The bank enters the program following a period of significant balance sheet expansion. Over the last 18 months, Chetwood Bank reported a £1.7 billion increase in savings balances, representing 43% growth. This influx of retail savings has funded a broader expansion into mortgage and wholesale lending. During the same period, the bank’s treasury portfolio grew by 88%, or £1.6 billion. This includes a £1.1 billion rise in structured finance investments across Residential Mortgage-Backed Securities (RMBS), Asset-Backed Securities (ABS), and Collateralised Loan Obligations (CLOs).

The Scale-up Unit aims to provide a smoother regulatory experience for firms during critical growth phases. While participation does not imply an endorsement of Chetwood’s specific products by the FCA or PRA, it allows the bank to engage more closely with regulators to identify opportunities for sustainable growth and sector-wide competitiveness. The initiative is intended to help innovative firms grow sustainably at pace while navigating an increasingly complex regulatory environment.

> "As a rapidly growing bank, the Scale-up Unit offers us a valuable opportunity to engage more closely with both the FCA and PRA, helping us navigate an increasingly complex regulatory environment while supporting our growth ambitions."

Paul Noble, Chief Executive Officer at Chetwood Bank

Chetwood Bank is a UK-based digital challenger bank that focuses on making specialist banking simpler and more accessible through technology and strategic partnerships. The firm operates across several verticals, providing mortgage and wholesale lending solutions alongside retail savings products. Its client base includes individual savers, professional landlords, intermediaries, and institutional partners.

The bank has positioned itself as a tech-led alternative to traditional high-street lenders, leveraging its digital infrastructure to scale its lending and investment activities. By combining structured finance expertise with a growing retail deposit base, Chetwood has established a significant presence in the UK's specialist lending market. The firm’s recent growth in structured finance investments, particularly in RMBS and CLOs, highlights its role as an active participant in the broader financial ecosystem beyond simple consumer deposits. As a digital-first institution, its operational model is built around the ability to deploy capital efficiently while maintaining a strengthened liquidity position, a strategy that has seen its treasury portfolio nearly double in less than two years.

This announcement signals a maturing relationship between UK regulators and the challenger bank sector. For years, fintechs have suggested that rigid regulatory frameworks stifle growth once a firm moves past the startup phase. By bringing Chetwood into the Scale-up Unit, the PRA and FCA are acknowledging that high-growth digital banks require a different level of engagement than either tiny incumbents or established Tier 1 giants. This moves the needle for the industry by providing a blueprint for how middle-tier challengers can scale without the friction of regulatory misalignment. It puts pressure on other mid-sized lenders to seek similar bespoke engagement to remain competitive in product innovation.

Original Press Release

Chetwood Bank, the UK-based digital challenger bank, has been selected to participate in the second cohort of the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) and the Financial Conduct Authority’s (FCA) Scale-up Unit.

As one of the firms chosen to take part in the second cohort, Chetwood Bank will benefit from tailored support in areas such as regulatory processes, product innovation, impact of new policy proposals and sector engagement, helping them to grow sustainably at pace.

Announced by the Chancellor of the Exchequer in October 2025 as part of the Government’s Financial Services Growth and Competitiveness Strategy, the Scale-up Unit was created to provide support for fast-growing and innovative firms as they develop new products, attract new customers and move into new markets.

The Scale-up Unit is intended to provide a smoother and more supportive regulatory experience for firms as they go through a critical growth phase. It will also engage with the sector as a whole to identify opportunities for regulators to better deliver sustainable growth, competitiveness and innovation.

Chetwood Bank is focused on making specialist banking simpler and more accessible by combining technology, expertise and strong partnerships. It provides mortgage and wholesale lending solutions, funded by retail savings, serving individual savers, professional landlords, borrowers, intermediaries and institutional partners.

Over the last 18 months, Chetwood Bank’s savings balances have grown by £1.7 billion, an increase of 43%, reflecting growing customer confidence in the bank and supporting the continued expansion of its lending and investment activities.

Over the same period, the bank’s treasury portfolio has grown by £1.6bn, an increase of 88%, including a £1.1bn increase in structured finance investments across RMBS, ABS and CLOs, alongside a strengthened liquidity position.

“We’re delighted to have been selected to join the second cohort of the Scale-up Unit.” said Paul Noble, Chief Executive Officer at Chetwood Bank.

“As a rapidly growing bank, the Scale-up Unit offers us a valuable opportunity to engage more closely with both the FCA and PRA, helping us navigate an increasingly complex regulatory environment while supporting our growth ambitions.” said Paul Noble, Chief Executive Officer at Chetwood Bank.

“We believe participation in the initiative will support us in continuing that journey at pace, with the confidence that comes from strong regulatory engagement and understanding." said Paul Noble, Chief Executive Officer at Chetwood Bank.

Participation in the Scale-Up Unit does not imply endorsement by the FCA or PRA of any aspect of Chetwood Bank, its products or innovations.
