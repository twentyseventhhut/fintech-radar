## [0] What happened
On 2026-10-23 TEN Credit Union (Nashville, TN) announced it selected **Bankjoy** as its digital banking platform, to go live the SAME day the CU converts its core to **Corelation KeyStone** ([source](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261023532702/en/TEN-Credit-Union-Selects-Bankjoy-for-Digital-Banking-as-It-Moves-to-Corelation-KeyStone)). Members get consumer + business digital banking, real-time fraud prevention (Bankjoy **FraudSense**), and advanced card controls via a **Velera** integration. The deal also bundles **JoyCompass** (financial-wellness) and **Plaid**-powered account verification/aggregation, both activatable post-launch ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/111431/ten-credit-union-selects-bankjoy-for-its-corelation-keystone-conversion)).

## [1] Who is involved
- **TEN Credit Union** — founded 1950 by Nashville school educators; ~24,800+ members, >$410M in assets. A small-to-mid community CU. "TEN" = Teachers Educators Nashville lineage.
- **Bankjoy** — Detroit-based digital-banking vendor for credit unions and community banks. ~60+ financial-institution clients; platform reaches >1M end users. Raised ~$13.6M total, incl. a round led by **Curql Collective** (a CUSO) with CU clients (AEA CU, Community Wide FCU, Statewide FCU) investing ([ffnews](https://ffnews.com/news/bankjoy-raises-funding-from-curql-to-support-companys-continued-growth-ongoing-product-innovation)). Q4-2025: +10 new FI clients, 13 existing-customer expansions ([IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/bankjoy-expands-credit-union-community-bank-footprint/)).
- **Corelation** — core-banking vendor; KeyStone is its core. Bankjoy integrates via the **KeyBridge API** and was the first fintech certified under Corelation's vendor-certification program.

## [2] Why it matters
Thin-margin strategic significance: this is a single small-CU vendor win, not a market-mover. What's mildly notable is the **"same-day" play** — converting core (KeyStone) AND digital banking (Bankjoy) simultaneously, avoiding a separate later digital conversion. That requires tight Corelation-certified integration and is Bankjoy's core go-to-market wedge: lock up the Corelation installed base by being the pre-certified, launch-day-ready digital layer. The CU avoids a dual-migration project; Bankjoy deepens its Corelation moat.

## [3] Context / comparables
Fits the steady drumbeat of US CUs picking fintech digital-banking vendors on core conversions. Comparables in the vault:
- [[CTBC Bank USA selects Narmi digital banking platform]] — Narmi, bank side, similar vendor-selection shape.
- [[Sunwest Credit Union and Tyfone partner on nFinia platform]] — Tyfone/$583M CU, same "digital transformation" framing.
- [[Ukrainian Credit Union partners with VeriPark on digital banking]] — VeriPark/Canada CU.
- [[Bankjoy partners InvestiFi to offer digital investing]] — prior Bankjoy note (DIFFERENT deal: product/InvestiFi investing, not the TEN win).
Bankjoy competes with Alkami, Q2, Narmi, Tyfone, Mahalo, Lumin/Jack Henry in the CU/community-bank digital-banking stack; its differentiator is Corelation-native certification.

## [4] Open questions / what to watch

- Whether same-day core+digital cutovers become Bankjoy's repeatable sales motion across the Corelation base.
- Economics: contract size undisclosed; a ~$410M / 24.8k-member CU is a modest ARR contributor.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Digital-banking platforms for US credit unions / community FIs — a crowded SaaS layer that sits between the core (Corelation KeyStone, Jack Henry, Fiserv, FIS) and the member. Structure is fragmented and consolidating: public scale players (Alkami, Q2) sit alongside VC-backed challengers (Narmi, Bankjoy) and core-adjacent suites (MeridianLink, Lumin Digital, Backbase). "Why now": a wave of core conversions among small/mid CUs is forcing simultaneous digital re-platforming. TENCU (founded 1950, ~24,800 members, >$410M assets — per BusinessWire PR, 2026-10-23) is a textbook case: it launches Bankjoy *the same day* it cuts over to Corelation KeyStone, via the KeyBridge API, avoiding a separate digital conversion later. Barriers to entry are real (core certification, fraud/compliance stack, switching costs), which is why vendors race to pre-certify against specific cores. Demand signal: Alkami ARR reached $511.7M in Q2 FY2026, +21% YoY (per Alkami PR, 2026-08-06); Q2 Holdings FY2025 revenue $794.8M, +14%, ~$2.7bn backlog (per Datos/press, 2026) — double-digit growth confirms the digital-banking re-platforming cycle is live, not announced.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: ARR / registered-user count / revenue-per-registered-user / client logos live-vs-signed; CUs also watch core-certification coverage. Key players & basis of competition — product depth + core integration + price, not network effects:
- **Alkami (Nasdaq: ALKT)** — 23.6M registered users, RPU $21.69, 54 bank clients contracted / 42 live (Q2 FY2026) — the scale incumbent for mid/large FIs.
- **Q2 Holdings** — Datos-ranked consensus leader US retail digital banking 2026; bank + CU breadth.
- **Lumin Digital** — cloud-native, CU-centric; raised $115M at $1.6bn (2026-07) — the best-funded pure-play challenger.
- **Narmi** — bank+CU, multi-core; 2026 wins incl. CTBC Bank USA, Northrim.
- **MeridianLink** — loan-origination-led suite for community FIs.
- **Bankjoy (protagonist)** — modular consumer+business digital banking, FraudSense, JoyCompass wellness, Plaid-powered verification; >60 FIs, >1M end users; added 10 new FI clients + 13 expansion agreements Q4 2025 (per PRWeb, 2026). Position: **niche challenger** targeting small/mid CUs, competing on Corelation-KeyStone certification and conversion-day go-live as its distribution wedge `(analysis)` — moat is switching costs + core-certification coverage, not scale.

**Comps & multiples.** TENCU–Bankjoy is a vendor win, not a financing — no deal economics disclosed (contract value, term, per-member pricing all silent). Internal comps from base:
- [[Lumin Digital raises $115M at $1.6B valuation]] — CU-centric pure-play round valuation $1.6bn (2026-07); a private-market read on the subvertical.
- [[CTBC Bank USA selects Narmi digital banking platform]] (2026-04) and [[State Bank of Texas launches Alkami-powered digital banking]] (2026-02) — comparable vendor-selection wins, no economics.
- [[Alkami appoints Cassandra Hudson as CFO]] / Alkami Q2 FY2026: at ~$528–531M FY revenue guide, the public anchor for sector multiples.
Bankjoy is private (YC- and Bessemer-backed; recent round partly funded by CU clients) — no post-money disclosed, so **EV/Revenue, P/S = no data** for Bankjoy. Public-peer sanity: Alkami trades as a growth SaaS name but its market cap wasn't verifiable here → **[UNSOURCED]**, multiple not computed. Qualitative read: the subvertical commands growth-SaaS valuations (Lumin $1.6bn on an undisclosed small revenue base implies a rich forward multiple `(analysis)`), justified only if logo growth sustains.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Conversion execution risk** — launching Bankjoy *and* a new KeyStone core on the same day doubles cutover risk for TENCU; any core-migration slip drags the digital launch with it (second-order: member attrition / support spike if the single-day go-live stumbles).
2. **Core-vendor dependence / disintermediation** — Bankjoy's wedge is Corelation certification; if Corelation (or its acquirers) favors a first-party or bundled digital layer, the KeyBridge-API channel that wins Bankjoy these logos could be squeezed (margin captured upstream).
3. **Scale disadvantage vs funded peers** — with >60 FIs and ~1M users, Bankjoy is an order of magnitude smaller than Alkami (23.6M users) and less capitalized than Lumin ($1.6bn); R&D/AI-feature cadence (e.g. Alkami Code Studio, Narmi MCP) may outpace it, pressuring retention at renewal.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Reinforces the thesis that CU digital banking is won at the *core-conversion moment*, not on standalone RFPs — vendors pre-certified to the winning core (here Corelation KeyStone) harvest same-day digital wins `(analysis)`. Watch next: whether Bankjoy keeps converting its Corelation renewal base into multi-product (JoyCompass/business) expansion; trigger that breaks the thesis = a core vendor bundling its own digital layer, collapsing the independent-vendor channel.
