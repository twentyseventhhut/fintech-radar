Northpointe Bancshares, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Related Documents

 Announces Strategic Decision to Sell Distributed Retail and Consumer Direct Mortgage Origination Channels 


 GRAND RAPIDS, Mich.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--
Northpointe Bancshares, Inc. (NYSE: NPB) (“Northpointe” or the “Company”), the holding company for Northpointe Bank (the "Bank"), today reported net income to common stockholders of $13.9 million, or $0.39 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026. This compares to $21.3 million, or $0.60 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026, and $20.1 million, or $0.57 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.



 Chief Executive Officer Commentary 



“Our core operating performance remained strong, led by continued growth in our Mortgage Purchase Program (MPP) business and the successful execution of an opportunistic balance sheet acquisition strategy during the quarter,” said Chuck Williams, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “Our third quarter financial results were negatively impacted by fair value declines driven by higher long-term interest rates and expenses associated with an isolated deposit fraud within our retail banking center, which we promptly addressed through strengthened procedures and additional controls.”



The Company also announced today it plans to sell the distributed retail and consumer direct mortgage origination channels to CMG Financial and its affiliates (“CMG”). The Company and CMG have entered into a non-binding letter of intent regarding the proposed transaction. Subject to the satisfactory completion of due diligence, execution of a definitive agreement and customary closing conditions, the Company anticipates the proposed transaction should close by the end of 2026.



“Given today’s economic and operating environment, and the continued evolution of the mortgage distribution channels, we are refocusing our strategic priorities where we have the strongest competitive advantages,” Williams continued. “This allows us to deepen client relationships, generate attractive portfolio assets, and grow relationship-based deposits, which we expect will improve operating leverage, capital efficiency and create a more robust, stable and scalable earnings profile.”



The Company currently estimates restructuring charges of between $6 million and $7 million, with the majority realized during the fourth quarter of 2026. Loans currently in process will continue to be closed, and the Company will provide support to the affected team members. Further details on the timing and anticipated restructuring charges, expense savings, and potential transaction will be provided on the Company’s earnings call on October 21, 2026.



“We believe CMG would be a strong home for our origination team and a constructive path for our customers, referral partners and team members,” Williams concluded. “We remain highly committed to the mortgage banking industry, and believe allocating capital and resources to our businesses with the strongest client relationships and balance sheet opportunities provide us the best path forward for long-term growth and shareholder returns.”



 Third Quarter 2026 Highlights 



Current quarter results included two notable items:
 
 
$9.3 million combined decrease in fair value of loans held for investment (“HFI”) and lender risk account (“LRA”), partially offset by a $5.0 million increase in fair value of mortgage servicing rights (“MSRs”), collectively driven by an increase in market interest rates.



$3.1 million in operational losses within non-interest expense resulting from an isolated external deposit fraud incident within the retail banking center. The Company has notified its insurance carriers and plans to pursue all potential recoveries.



Financial performance and profitability metrics:
 
 
Return on average equity of 9.10%, compared to 14.36% in the prior quarter.



Return on average tangible common equity of 9.19%, compared to 14.69% in the prior quarter (see non-GAAP reconciliation).



Return on average assets of 0.75%, compared to 1.18% in the prior quarter.



Efficiency ratio of 64.47%, compared to 54.76% in the prior quarter.



Continued growth in the balance sheet, including:
 
 
Mortgage Purchase Program (“MPP”) balances increased by $262.7 million, or 27% annualized, from the prior quarter. This is net of $368.3 million in balances participated to other institutions at period end, which compares to $489.0 million in the prior quarter.



First-lien home equity lines which are tied seamlessly to a demand deposit sweep account (the Company commonly refers to these loans as “All-in-One” or “AIO” loans) balances increased by $100.4 million, or 50% annualized (including purchase noted below).



Purchased $85.4 million in seasoned AIO loans on September 30, 2026 ($69.9 million net of purchase discount).



Total deposits increased by $210.4 million, or 16% annualized, driven primarily by higher demand deposits and brokered CDs, partially offset by lower time and savings & money market deposits.



Asset quality remained stable:
 
 
Non-performing assets increased by $8.9 million from the prior quarter but loans past due 31-89 days decreased by $5.7 million.



Net charge-offs remained historically low at $429,000, or 0.02% of average loans (annualized).



Reduced current period income tax expense by $608,000 through acquisition of renewable energy investment tax credits.



Wholesale funding ratio increased to 63.94%, compared to 63.09% in the prior quarter.



The Company’s Board of Directors increased its regular quarterly cash dividend to $0.03 per share, from $0.025 per share, payable on November 4, 2026, to stockholders of record as of October 15, 2026.



 Net Interest Income 



Net interest income before provision was $43.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $1.2 million compared to the second quarter of 2026. The linked quarter increase reflects a $157.0 million increase in average interest-earning assets partially offset by a 1 basis point decrease in net interest margin. As compared to the third quarter of 2025, net interest income before provision increased by $3.3 million, which was driven primarily by a $987.3 million increase in average interest-earning assets partially offset by a 15 basis point decrease in net interest margin.



Net interest margin was 2.32% for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 1 basis point compared to 2.33% in the second quarter of 2026 and a decrease of 15 basis points compared to 2.47% in the third quarter of 2025. The linked quarter decrease was driven primarily by a higher average cost of deposits, partially offset by higher average yields on loans and an improvement in the funding mix. The decrease compared to the prior year quarter was driven primarily by lower average yields on interest-earning assets, which outpaced the decrease in average rates paid on interest-bearing liabilities.



Average interest-earning assets at September 30, 2026 increased by $157.0 million from June 30, 2026 and by $987.3 million compared to September 30, 2025. The increases from both comparable periods reflect the strong growth in MPP and AIO loan balances, which are the portfolios the Company is focused on strategically growing, partially offset by continued run-off in the remainder of the loan portfolio.



 Provision (Benefit) for Credit Losses 



The Company recorded total provision for credit losses (including both loans and unfunded commitments) of $845,000 in the third quarter of 2026, compared to provision of $210,000 in the second quarter of 2026 and provision of $828,000 in the third quarter of 2025. The Company’s quarterly provision (benefit) for credit losses reflects net loan charge-offs, along with factors such as loan growth, portfolio mix, reserves on individually evaluated loans, credit migration trends, and changes in the economic forecasts used in the credit models.



The Company’s allowance for credit losses was $10.0 million at September 30, 2026, $9.4 million at June 30, 2026 and $12.3 million at September 30, 2025. The allowance for credit losses represented 0.15% of loans HFI at both September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026, and 0.21% of loans HFI at September 30, 2025. The majority of the growth in the loans HFI portfolio has come from MPP and AIO balances, with continued run-off in residential mortgage, construction, and other consumer / home equity loans, which carry higher average loss rates. In total, at September 30, 2026, residential mortgage, construction, and other consumer / home equity loans have decreased by $49.6 million from June 30, 2026 and by $215.2 million from September 30, 2025.



Total provision for credit losses in the third quarter of 2026 reflected net charge-offs of $429,000, and an increase of $524,000 in allowance for credit losses, which was primarily attributable to higher forecasted loss rates resulting from an update to Moody’s credit models, partially offset by lower allowance from an improvement in qualitative factors. Total provision for credit losses in the prior quarter reflected net charge-offs of $528,000, and a decrease of $264,000 in allowance for credit losses, which was primarily attributable to lower levels of non-performing loans and continued change in the loan mix, partially offset by slightly higher loss rates from the economic forecasts used in the credit models. Total provision for credit losses in the prior year quarter reflected net charge-offs of $977,000 and a decrease of $125,000 in allowance for credit losses.



 Non-interest Income 



Non-interest income was $12.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $9.6 million compared to the second quarter of 2026 and a decrease of $11.8 million compared to the third quarter of 2025.



MPP fees, which are driven by total loans funded and participation balances, were $2.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $118,000 compared to the second quarter of 2026 and an increase of $731,000 compared to the third quarter of 2025. The decrease compared to the prior quarter reflects lower participation volume, and the increase compared to the prior year quarter was driven primarily by higher levels of funded loans.



Loan servicing fees were $7.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $5.3 million compared to the second quarter of 2026 and an increase of $6.5 million compared to the third quarter of 2025. The changes from both comparable periods reflect changes in the fair value of MSRs primarily attributable to the movement in market interest rates during the respective periods.



Net gain on sale of loans was $2.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $17.0 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $21.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. Net gain on sale of loans includes the capitalization of new MSRs, changes in fair value of loans, and gains on the sale of loans.



The net gain on sale of loans for the third quarter of 2026 included a decrease of $9.3 million from the combined change in fair value of loans HFI and LRA, which are both attributable to changes in market interest rates. Excluding these items (see Net Gain on Sale of Loans table below for a reconciliation), net gain on sale of loans was $11.5 million, a decrease of $4.9 million on a comparative basis from the second quarter of 2026 and a decrease of $6.0 million on a comparative basis from the third quarter of 2025. The decreases from both comparable periods reflect lower levels of residential mortgage interest rate lock commitments.



 Non-interest Expense 



Non-interest expense was $36.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $0.8 million compared to the second quarter of 2026 and an increase of $1.7 million compared to the third quarter of 2025.



Salaries and benefits expense decreased by $3.1 million on a linked quarter basis and decreased by $2.4 million compared to the third quarter of 2025. The linked quarter decrease was driven primarily by lower variable compensation on mortgage production, along with lower bonus and incentive compensation. The decrease compared to the prior year quarter was driven primarily by lower bonus and incentive compensation, lower base salaries and lower variable compensation on mortgage production.



Other non-interest expense increased by $3.8 million on a linked quarter basis and increased by $4.0 million compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increases from both comparable periods reflect the $3.1 million related to the isolated deposit fraud noted above.



 Taxes 



Income tax expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $4.6 million, compared to $7.1 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $7.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. The Company’s effective tax rate was 24.43% for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 24.72% for the second quarter of 2026 and 24.00% for the third quarter of 2025. The effective tax rate in the third quarter of 2026 includes a $608,000 benefit from the acquisition of renewable energy investment tax credits made during the quarter, partially offset by a higher state tax rate. The Company plans to acquire additional renewable energy tax credits during the fourth quarter of 2026 to help reduce its overall effective tax rate for the year.
