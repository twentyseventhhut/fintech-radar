Hong Kong Names Nasdaq-Listed Solana Company Strategic Enterprise to Support Blockchain Infrastructure
Summary
The Hong Kong government said Nasdaq-listed Solana Company was included in the seventh batch of its corporate support program as a strategic enterprise to foster the blockchain infrastructure industry .
The company said the designation will provide administrative support , help with hiring overseas specialists , and opportunities to collaborate with local institutional investors and business partners .
Solana Company said it plans to expand its Asia-Pacific blockchain infrastructure business through staking on the Solana (SOL) network , validator infrastructure , holding Solana as part of its digital-asset treasury strategy , and the Pacific Backbone project .
Forecast Trend Report by Period
Hong Kong has named Nasdaq-listed Solana Company a strategic enterprise, stepping up support for the blockchain infrastructure industry.
Cointelegraph reported on October 26 that Solana Company had been included in the seventh batch of companies selected for a support program run by OASES, the Hong Kong government office dedicated to attracting strategic enterprises. It is the only blockchain infrastructure company chosen for the program this year.
Hong Kong on October 22 designated 18 companies, including Solana Company, as new strategic enterprises. That brought the total number of participating companies to 142. The city runs the program to attract global companies that can contribute to economic growth.
The designation will provide Solana Company with administrative support for its local expansion. It will have a unified communication channel with government agencies and regulators. The company will also receive help with hiring overseas specialists and opportunities to collaborate with local institutional investors and business partners.
Solana Company operates staking and validator infrastructure for the Solana network. It also runs an advisory business that supports financial institutions adopting blockchain technology and holds Solana as part of its digital-asset treasury strategy.
The company plans to use Hong Kong as a base to expand its blockchain infrastructure business across the Asia-Pacific region. In particular, it plans to build validator operating infrastructure in Hong Kong through its Pacific Backbone project, which is designed to minimize latency across blockchain networks in the region.

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
- On **2026-10-22**, Hong Kong's **OASES** (Office for Attracting Strategic Enterprises, under the Financial Secretary) named its **seventh batch of 18 strategic enterprises** at a signing ceremony officiated by FS Paul Chan. One of the 18 is **Solana Company** — the only blockchain-infrastructure firm in the 2026 cohort. Total OASES partners now **142**; the new batch is pitched to bring ~**HK$84bn of investment and ~27,000 jobs** over coming years (note's "Oct 26 Cointelegraph" framing is secondary pickup of the Oct 22 government release).
- **What Solana Company actually gets:** administrative support, a single/unified communication channel with HK government agencies and regulators, help hiring overseas specialists, and introductions to local institutional investors/partners. It has set up a **Hong Kong office as its Asia-Pacific HQ** and plans to run **SOL validator nodes** in HK under its **"Pacific Backbone"** project (an ultra-low-latency validator mesh linking Seoul/Tokyo/Singapore/HK; a Tokyo cluster is reportedly already live, HK next).
- **CRITICAL ENTITY FIX — the enrichment prompt is wrong.** "Solana Company" (Nasdaq: **HSDT**) is **formerly Helius Medical Technologies**, a neurotech shell that pivoted to a SOL digital-asset-treasury (DAT) in mid-Sep 2025 ($500M raise backed by **Summer Capital, Pantera Capital**; rebrand announced ~29 Sep 2025; signed a non-binding LOI with the Solana Foundation). It is **NOT** "formerly DeFi Development Corp." DeFi Development Corp (Nasdaq: **DFDV**, formerly Janover) is a *separate, larger* SOL-treasury firm (~2.6M SOL). **Do not conflate HSDT and DFDV.** And neither is the Solana blockchain/Foundation (non-profit, Zug) — this designation is about the **listed corporate treasury/validator entity**, as the prompt correctly flags. HSDT holds ~**2.2–2.3M SOL** (~$525M + >$15M cash per its 2026-10-06 update).
- **Why framed this way:** both sides get cheap optics. For HK, adding "the only blockchain infrastructure company" to the roster props up its Web3-hub narrative at zero fiscal cost (OASES perks are administrative, not grants). For HSDT, a government designation is a credibility stamp for a company that is fundamentally a leveraged SOL-holding vehicle with a thin operating business — the real object is **narrative/legitimacy**, not a binding investment or revenue event.
