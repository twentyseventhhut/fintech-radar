# Airwallex announces inaugural Startup Finance 30 honorees

> [!info] 2026-10-28 · 2 упоминаний · 2 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] And speaking of people shaping startup finance, Airwallex has announced its first Startup Finance 30, recognising 30 finance leaders across the global startup ecosystem.

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🏆 Airwallexannounces inaugural Startup Finance 30 honorees. A committee of operating CFOs from Airwallex, Notion, Vercel and Deel selected 30 startup finance professionals from hundreds of nominations across the US, Europe, APAC and other markets. The cohort includes CFOs, heads of finance and financial controllers. Half of the honorees work at companies at Series B or later, while 11 represent businesses valued at $500 million or more. Individual profiles detail their roles and the companies they work with. Explore the full list.

 Finance is one of the most under-leveraged growth levers a startup has.
