Moomoo Launches Real-Time* Deposit Capabilities Through Visa and Checkout.com
Published
Oct 8, 2026 3:45pm EDT
 
 New Apple Pay and Google Pay deposit options enable eligible users to fund their accounts in real-time*, 24/7 
 JERSEY CITY, N.J. , Oct. 08, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Moomoo , a leading global investment and trading platform, today announced an expansion of its account funding capabilities through  Visa , one of the world leaders in digital payments, and  Checkout.com . Moomoo’s collaboration with Visa and Checkout.com now allows eligible users to make real-time* deposits through Apple Pay and Google Pay to invest in stocks, cryptocurrencies, ETFs and options and event contracts.
The collaboration expansion provides users with a fast and more convenient way to transfer funds into their Moomoo accounts and invest. By expanding beyond traditional bank transfer methods and business-hour limitations, eligible Moomoo users can deposit funds 24 hours a day, seven days a week and gain real-time* access to capital to provide flexibility when investment opportunities arise. Access to funds will be subject to funding availability.
“Investors expect the same speed and execution in their investing experience that they access through other market-leading digital products,” said Neil McDonald , CEO of Moomoo U.S . “Expanding our funding capabilities through Visa and Checkout.com makes it easier for users to quickly move money into their Moomoo accounts when they are ready to invest. Combined with the expanded market access and AI-enhanced investing tools on the Moomoo platform, this collaboration marks a transformative step in giving investors greater control over how and when they put their money to work.”
 Account Funding Fast and More Accessible 
The enhanced deposit system leverages Visa's global payments network and Checkout.com 's payment infrastructure to provide a streamlined funding experience within the Moomoo app. Users can select Apple Pay or Google Pay as their deposit method and complete transactions using an eligible debit card linked to their digital wallet, reducing the steps required to fund their account.
“Digital investment and trading platforms across the globe, have transformed the way consumers move funds in and out of their accounts,” said Jakub Petri , VP Business Development and Partnerships at Visa . “We’re extending that real-time experience to account funding, giving investors on the Moomoo platform a secure and convenient way to fund their accounts through Apple Pay and Google Pay, powered by Visa’s global network.”
“Investors need digital financial experiences that are fast, intuitive, and able to keep pace with their portfolio decisions,” said Jim Cho , VP of Revenue Growth in North America at Checkout.com . “Our collaboration with Moomoo and Visa represents an essential step in providing the infrastructure investors need to move money at the same pace that they make decisions in a market that is increasingly 24/7.”

24/7 real-time* account funding for eligible users

 Supporting an Around-the-Clock Investing Experience

Moomoo currently supports 24/5 trading of eligible U.S . stocks and ETFs, while its expanding product ecosystem provides additional ways for investors to engage with markets around the clock.***
In recent months, Moomoo has continued to enhance its platform with new capabilities, including  Web3 wallet integration , agentic investing through  Moomoo API Skills and  prediction markets through its partnership with Kalshi. ## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
### [0] TL;DR
On 2026-10-08 Moomoo (the US retail-brokerage subsidiary of Nasdaq-listed Futu Holdings) launched **real-time account deposits** funded via **Apple Pay and Google Pay**, running on **Visa**'s push-payment network and **Checkout.com**'s payment infrastructure. Eligible US users can now fund stocks, crypto, ETFs, options and Kalshi event contracts 24/7 with a debit card linked to their digital wallet, bypassing business-hours ACH limits. Deposits are free, capped at ~$10k/day and $50k/month across both wallets; eligibility requires a US SSN and US-issued debit card.

### [1] What's actually new / changed
- **Mechanism:** card-rail "push-to-account" funding (Visa Direct-style A2A card funding), not ACH batch transfers — funds credited in real time*, 24/7.
- **UX:** in-app selection of Apple Pay / Google Pay, debit card already in the wallet, fewer steps than linking a bank account.
- **Positioning:** paired with Moomoo's "always-on" narrative — 24/5 extended-hours trading, Web3 wallet, agentic "API Skills", and the June-2026 Kalshi prediction-markets integration. Funding speed closes the loop so capital can be deployed the moment an opportunity (or event contract) appears.
- **Sweetener:** 3.6% APY on uninvested cash (promo 8.1% for 2 months), making the fast-deposited cash productive while idle.

### [2] Why it matters / significance
- Instant debit-card funding is becoming **table stakes** for US retail brokers. Robinhood ($1k instant deposits + Apple Pay instant transfers since 2024) and Webull (provisional buying power, debit-card instant deposit) already set the bar; Moomoo is catching up to native US incumbents rather than leapfrogging them.
- For Visa and Checkout.com this is another proof point that **brokerage/crypto-wallet funding** is now a first-class Visa Direct use case alongside payouts and remittances — monetizing the "money-in" side of investing, a 24/7 flow that ACH structurally can't serve.
- Reduces a classic conversion-killer: the dead time between deciding to invest and funds settling. Lower funnel friction = higher activation and more frequent top-ups.

### [3] Open questions / what to watch
- **Real "real-time"?** The press release's triple-asterisk caveats ("actual fund availability depends on receiving institution and region") suggest crediting may still be provisional/limited. How instant is settlement vs. buying power?
- **Economics:** card-funding interchange/scheme fees are far higher than ACH. Who eats the cost given "free" deposits — Moomoo margin, or offset by the APY-sweep spread and trading revenue?
- **Caps:** $10k/day, $50k/month is modest for active traders; does this stay a convenience rail or scale?
- **Chargeback/fraud:** card-funded brokerage accounts invite card-not-present fraud and deposit-reversal (chargeback-then-trade) risk — hence debit-only, SSN-gated, US-only.
- **Rollout:** US-only at launch; will it extend to Moomoo's Singapore/Australia/Japan/Canada markets?

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
### Red-team questions
1. Is "real-time" real, or marketing? The release asterisks fund availability as depending on the receiving institution and region — is money actually settled instantly, or just shown as provisional buying power (like Robinhood/Webull)?
2. What is the catch on the 3.6%→8.1% APY sweep? Rate is new-user-only, drops to 0.03% without a paid subscription after the promo — is instant funding really a loss-leader for the paid tier?
3. Who absorbs the card-interchange cost of "free" deposits? Debit-card funding fees dwarf ACH; is this subsidized and therefore capped/temporary?
4. Are the $10k/day and $50k/month limits a technical/risk ceiling or a cost-control lever? They're low for serious traders — does that undercut the "deploy capital when opportunity arises" pitch?
5. Chargeback exposure: a user could fund via card, trade, then dispute the card charge. How is reversal/fraud risk mitigated beyond debit-only + SSN gating?
6. Is this genuinely differentiating, or catch-up? Robinhood offered Apple Pay instant transfers back in 2024 and Webull has had debit-card instant deposits — is Moomoo matching, not leading?
7. Why Checkout.com specifically, an enterprise ecommerce processor, rather than Visa Direct direct integration? What does CKO add, and is there concentration/dependency risk?
8. US-only, SSN-required, US-issued-debit-only — how much of Moomoo's claimed 30M+ global users does this actually serve?
9. Does crypto funding via card deposits create AML/KYC and gambling-adjacency concerns given the Kalshi event-contracts tie-in?
10. The "30 million investors" figure (press release) vs. Futu's ~3.37M funded accounts (end-2025) — which is the real, monetizable base, and is the headline number registered users not funded accounts?
11. Is there a regulatory angle? Card-funded brokerage deposits that immediately buy crypto/event contracts could attract FINRA/CFTC scrutiny on suitability and deposit sourcing.
12. Partnership durability: is this an exclusive, a pilot, or a non-exclusive add-on Visa/CKO will replicate for every broker, eroding any moat?
13. Does instant funding increase impulsive/overtrading behavior and consumer-harm optics for a platform already adding prediction markets?
14. What's the withdrawal side? Instant-in is easy to market; are withdrawals back to card equally fast, or is that where friction (and float) hides?

### Importance: 3/5 — rationale
A solid, well-sourced product launch that is strategically coherent with Moomoo's "always-on" roadmap (24/5 trading, Kalshi event contracts, Web3) and a notable proof point that brokerage funding is now a core Visa Direct / Checkout.com use case. But it is catch-up to US incumbents (Robinhood, Webull already offer instant/card funding), US-only, capped, and incremental rather than category-defining. Material for the Moomoo/Visa/Checkout.com narratives and the retail-funding-UX trend, not a market-mover.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Retail-brokerage account funding is being re-plumbed from ACH batch rails to card-based real-time push. The protagonist rail here is Visa Direct: in fiscal Q2 2026 (quarter ended Mar-31-26) Visa ran 3.7bn Visa Direct transactions, +23% YoY, across 18bn+ endpoints (per Visa Q2 FY2026 earnings release, 2026-04-28). Visa Direct advertises reach of ~195+ countries and 20+ real-time payment schemes (per corporate.visa.com, as of 2025-10). "Why now": the shift is demand-pull — 24/5 extended-hours US equity trading, 24/7 crypto/event-contract markets, and prediction markets (Moomoo's Kalshi tie-up) make T+1/business-hour ACH funding a competitive liability; whoever credits capital fastest captures the trade. Structure: the rail layer (Visa) is highly consolidated (duopoly with Mastercard); the brokerage layer is fragmented and competes largely on features. Value in a flow like this is split rail (Visa network fee) → PSP/orchestration (Checkout.com) → broker (Moomoo, which earns on the downstream trade/float, not the deposit). Entry barrier is low for the broker (buy the rail off-the-shelf) but high at the rail layer (network effects, licensing).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: funded/paying accounts, deposit conversion & time-to-first-trade, net new asset inflows, and uninvested-cash spread (float). Moomoo's parent Futu reported Q2 2026 revenue US$918.2M (+35.6% YoY), net income US$464.4M (+41.6%), 3.84M funded accounts and 31.25M registered users (per Futu Q2 2026 release). Basis of competition = funding friction + cash yield. Recent peer moves: Robinhood supports debit-card deposits (~30-min funding) and ~$1k instant buying power; Webull is ACH-only with ~$1k same-day access (per BrokerChooser / Wealthvieu, 2026). Moomoo's launch (2026-10-08) routes instant deposits through Apple Pay / Google Pay debit credentials over Visa Direct + Checkout.com — on funding rails this puts it at parity-to-ahead of Robinhood (digital-wallet front-end vs raw debit entry) and ahead of Webull (which lacks card funding). Moat `(analysis)`: thin at the funding layer — the rail is rentable by any competitor — so the durable edge is Moomoo's 8.1%-APY-for-2-months cash-sweep teaser (promo rate, drops to 0.03% after) plus product breadth (crypto, options, event contracts, Kalshi, Web3), not the deposit mechanic itself.

**Comps & multiples.** Futu (FUTU, Moomoo's listed parent): market cap ~$15.5bn (as of 2026-09-30), TTM net income ~$1.42bn → P/E = `$15.5bn / $1.42bn ≈ 10.9x` (trailing); stated P/E ~11.3x, forward ~7.2x (per stockanalysis.com). For a franchise growing revenue +35.6% YoY, a sub-11x trailing / ~7x forward P/E screens cheap vs a US retail-broker like Robinhood (typically 30x+ earnings) — the discount is the China/geopolitical + listing overhang, not an operating weakness `(analysis)`; the growth-multiple relation argues "cheap", though the China risk is the standing reason. Visa (rail provider, PRIMARY IR evidence): Q2 FY2026 net revenue $11.2bn, +17% (+16% cc) (per [Visa Q2 FY2026 8-K earnings release, filed 2026-04-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001403161/000140316126000077/q22026earningsrelease.htm); latest indexed Visa SEC filing in IR DB = 8-K 2026-05-12, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000119312526219432/d64238d8k.htm). "Other revenue" incl. value-added services +41% to $1.3bn — the segment that captures incremental flows like brokerage funding. Clean EV/EBITDA and ticket-level take-rate for this specific deposit flow = no data (neither party discloses). Internal comps (in-base precedents for the same brokerage/crypto instant-funding pattern): [[PayPal becomes funding option for Kraken US clients]] (2025-08, instant 24/7 deposits, no bank login — direct analogue), [[This Week in Fintech Visa Direct and Cross River on card-based speed]] (2025-10, Visa Direct card-credential funding thesis), [[Visa Direct partners with Lunar's Moonrise across Nordics]] (2026-03).

**Risk flags.** (1) Dependence on someone else's rails — Moomoo controls neither the network (Visa) nor the PSP (Checkout.com); economics, fraud liability and chargeback cost on debit-funded deposits are undisclosed, and card-funded instant deposits are a known vector for fraud/first-party abuse at brokers, which can force clawbacks or caps. (2) Disintermediation / margin capture — the deposit itself is a cost center for Moomoo; Visa and Checkout.com take the transaction economics, so the value only accrues to Moomoo if faster funding converts to higher trading/float, which is unproven at launch. (3) "Announced vs live" + fine print — the press release hedges every "real-time*" with "actual fund availability depends on receiving institution," the headline 8.1% APY is a 2-month promo collapsing to 0.03%, and PR is silent on fees, deposit caps and which users are "eligible" — all suggesting narrower economics than the framing implies.

**What this changes (idea-lens) `(analysis)`.** Instant card-based funding is shifting from differentiator to table-stakes for US retail brokers chasing a 24/7 trading calendar; Moomoo matches the leaders but doesn't leapfrog them. Falsifiable thesis: if the real payoff is real, Futu's next prints should show rising US funded-account conversion / net new assets, not just a feature checkbox — watch Futu's quarterly funded-account adds and client-asset growth. Thesis breaks if deposit caps, fraud losses, or the promo-APY expiry mean the rail adds cost without lifting funded-account or AUM growth. Second-order watch: more brokers renting Visa Direct for funding is a tailwind for Visa's "new flows" revenue regardless of which broker wins.
