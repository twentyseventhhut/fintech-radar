«Т-Технологии» утвердили оценку Точка Банка в 84,8 млрд рублей
 
 Для консолидации пакета акций будет использоваться смешанное финансирование: допэмиссия акций для «Каталитик Пипл» и денежная выплата для остальных акционеров
 
 «Т-Технологии» сообщили, что совет директоров утвердил оценку Точка Банка для сделки по консолидации банка с использованием допэмиссии акций. Оценка 100% одного из крупнейших цифровых банков для предпринимателей составила 84,8 млрд рублей.

 Для консолидации пакета будет использовано смешанное финансирование: допэмиссия акций «Т-Технологий» по 320 рублей за бумагу в пользу владельца 64% Точка Банка — компании «Каталитик Пипл» — и денежная выплата остальным акционерам.

 Алексей Малиновский, председатель совета директоров «Т-Технологий», заявил, что оценка учитывает справедливую рыночную стоимость бизнеса и предыдущие сделки с акциями Точка Банка. По его словам, в компании видят потенциал роста за счет синергии с экосистемой «Т», а инвестиция должна окупиться с доходностью выше 30%.

 Совет директоров также решил частично оплатить сделку деньгами, чтобы снизить размывание долей акционеров. Для финансирования планируется использовать заемные средства и допэмиссию: в целом может потребоваться выпуск 170 млн акций, а эффективный объем допэмиссии оценивается в 85 млн акций, или 3,2% капитала. Акционеры с преимущественным правом выкупа получат уведомления после корпоративных процедур, а неиспользованные дополнительные акции будут погашены.

 В компании подчеркнули, что допэмиссия не повлияет на дивидендную политику: «Т-Технологии» продолжат ежеквартальные выплаты, а в 2026 году общий размер дивидендов на акцию, как ожидается, вырастет более чем на 20%.

 Сейчас 64% Точка Банка принадлежит компании «Каталитик Пипл», в которой 50,01% долей находится у «Т-Технологий», поэтому экономическая доля группы в банке составляет 32%.

 Закрытие сделки и завершение допэмиссии ожидаются в конце 2026 года. Точка Банк сохранит бренд и текущую команду управления.

_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
T-Technologies' (ex-TKS Holding / Tinkoff group) board **approved a 84.8bn RUB valuation for 100% of Tochka Bank** (a Russian SME/entrepreneur digital bank) as the price basis for consolidating the bank. The consolidation uses **mixed financing**: an additional share issue (dopemissiya) of T-Technologies stock at **320 RUB/share** to Catalytic People (the vehicle holding 64% of Tochka), plus a **cash payout** to the remaining shareholders to limit dilution. Headline numbers: up to **170m shares may need to be issued**, effective dopemissiya ~**85m shares, i.e. ~3.2% of capital**; unused shares will be cancelled. The group says the deal will not change dividend policy (quarterly payouts continue; 2026 DPS expected to grow >20%) and targets **>30% IRR** on the investment via ecosystem synergies. Close expected **end-2026**; Tochka keeps its brand and management.

**Why framed this way / what it reveals:** This is NOT a fresh market event — it is the **board sign-off on a price that was already implied in April 2026**, when VK sold its 25% Tochka stake to Interros for "no less than 21.2bn RUB" for onward transfer to T-Technologies. 21.2bn / 0.25 = **84.8bn RUB** — the exact same number. So the "valuation" is a re-confirmation/formalization of the April implied price, not a step-up or new discovery. (analysis) The PR anchors to a flattering >30% IRR and "synergy" narrative precisely because the deal is dilutive to existing holders; the cash component and the "no dividend impact / >20% DPS growth" line exist to pre-empt the dilution complaint. The company's economic share today is only **32%** (T-Technologies owns 50.01% of Catalytic People, which owns 64% of Tochka = ~32%), so "consolidation" is the step from economic 32% toward full control/100%.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Russian SME-banking rivals:** the direct peers for Tochka are **Modulbank** (pure SME digital bank), **Delo Bank**, and the SME arms of **Sber (SberBusiness)**, **Alfa-Bank**, **VTB**, and T-Bank's own existing business banking. Tochka is one of the largest *independent* entrepreneur-focused digital banks; the deal removes that independence by folding it into the T ecosystem. Position: Tochka is a strong niche player being absorbed by a larger universal ecosystem (catching-up/absorbed, not leader).
- **Valuation comparables (corpus):** [[Revolut completes share sale at $75B valuation]] / [[Revolut reaches $75 billion valuation after share sale]] — a far larger neobank at a secondary-sale valuation; [[Chime shares surge 59% in IPO at $18.4B market cap]] and [[Chime IPO endorses consumer fintech with 8.6M members]] — public-market neobank pricing; [[Tata Capital trims valuation to $15.7B ahead of IPO]] — a valuation *cut* ahead of listing, the inverse signal. The relevant M&A comp for "SME bank sold at/below prior marks" is [[Access Bank UK to acquire Zempler Bank]] (SME bank bought for "less than half its previous valuation").
- **Why the landscape is this way (analysis):** Western neobank marks (Revolut $75bn, Chime ~$18bn) are not comparable — different scale, open capital markets, and FX. In a sanctioned, closed Russian market, valuations are set by domestic strategic buyers, not global VC rounds; 84.8bn RUB (~$1bn at ~85 RUB/USD) is a modest number for a "largest digital SME bank," reflecting the illiquid, captive-buyer dynamics rather than competitive tension.

## [2] Company history / fit
Tochka was founded in 2015; JSC Tochka was structured in 2018 (QIWI Bank 40%, Otkritie 50%+1, Catalytic People — the founders' vehicle — ~10%-1). Ownership has churned repeatedly: **2023** — Trust Bank (the CBR's bad-bank) auctioned 90.01% of Tochka for **41.5bn RUB** (start price 24.7bn) to a management-led consortium incl. Interros; **Q4 2023** — VK bought 25% for **11.6bn RUB** (1C also joined the consortium); **2025** — T-Technologies raised its Catalytic People stake to 50.01%, gaining control; **April 2026** — VK exited, selling its 25% to Interros for ≥21.2bn RUB (for onward transfer to T-Technologies), booking ~2x on cost. Now (Oct 2026) T-Technologies moves to full consolidation at the 84.8bn mark.

**Why the company acts this way (analysis):** T-Technologies has been methodically climbing the cap table (control of Catalytic People → absorb VK's exit → now full consolidation). The logic is straightforward: convert a ~32% economic, control-but-not-owned asset into a wholly-owned B2B SME franchise to bolt onto the T ecosystem (cross-sell, shared infra, deposit/transaction flow). The repeated changes of hands (Otkritie→Trust→consortium→VK→Interros→T) reflect the asset's origin inside the CBR's failed-bank cleanup, not organic strategic demand.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Low novelty.** No new product, no new market, no new financial disclosure for Tochka itself. The 84.8bn valuation is mathematically identical to the April-2026 VK-exit implied price — the board merely ratified it for the dopemissiya mechanism. Real traction data (Tochka's current client count, 2025 IFRS P&L) was **not disclosed** in this release; the only hard historicals are RAS net profit ~6.2bn RUB in 2022 and the 41.5bn RUB 2023 auction price. So the "value" rests on the unverified >30% IRR claim and generic "synergy."

**Why the value-add is real or not (analysis):** The step-up from 41.5bn (2023, 90%) to 84.8bn (2026, 100%) is roughly a doubling over ~3 years — plausible for a growing SME bank in a high-rate environment (Russian banks earn fat net interest margins at ~16-20% policy rates), but it could equally reflect inflation and a captive-buyer paying up to complete control. Who captures the margin: T-Technologies, which gets a fully-owned SME franchise and deposit base; minority sellers get cash; existing T shareholders bear ~3.2% dilution. The ">30% IRR" is a management assertion, not a modeled, source-backed figure — treat as marketing until the synergy math is shown.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Close and dopemissiya completion expected **end-2026**. Shareholders with pre-emptive rights get notices after corporate procedures; unused shares cancelled. Tochka keeps brand/team. The structural driver is consolidation of Russian fintech under a handful of ecosystems (Sber, T, Alfa) amid a closed, sanctioned market where cross-border exits are impossible — so absorption by a domestic strategic is the only liquidity path for minority holders.

**Second-order (analysis):** For T-Technologies the risk is paying a control premium for an asset whose standalone economics aren't publicly proven, funded partly by dilution during a period the group simultaneously promises >20% DPS growth — a slight tension. Counterintuitively, "consolidation under one ecosystem" can entrench a mid-tier number rather than unlock it: once Tochka is fully inside T, there is no external price discovery left for the SME franchise. Regulatory backdrop (CBR) is not a visible obstacle given T already controls the asset.

## Market Research

**Sector & drivers.** Russian SME digital banking. Tochka serves 700k+ business clients (per Tochka corporate profile, as of its 2025 10th anniversary), positioning it among the larger independent SME-focused digital banks in Russia; no sourced TAM for the RU SME-banking segment — no data. Structure: concentrated at the top (Sber, VTB, Alfa dominate corporate/SME), with a layer of SME-native challengers (Tochka, Modulbank, Tinkoff/T-Bank Business) competing on onboarding speed, pricing and value-added services (accounting, VAT, embedded tooling) rather than balance sheet. "Why now": the deal is a vertical consolidation — T-Technologies is moving from a 32% economic stake to 100% control (board valuation 84.8bn RUB / 8.48mn RUB per share, closing expected end-2026, per bosfera.ru/rb.ru) to fold Tochka into the "T" ecosystem and accelerate B2B lending to entrepreneurs (analysis). Driver: ecosystem/cross-sell economics in a market where SME acquisition is expensive and sticky.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs for SME banking: active business accounts, fee/subscription revenue per client, interest income on balances, and attach of value-added services (Tochka explicitly cites three streams — subscription fees, interest income, VAS, per its profile). Key players: incumbents Sber/VTB/Alfa (scale, balance sheet); challengers T-Bank Business, Modulbank, Tochka (product/distribution). Basis of competition: product + distribution + speed, not price-of-capital. Recent moves: Interros (Potanin) bought 25% of Tochka from VK for ≥21.2bn RUB (closed April 2026, per Interfax/AKM) as a pass-through to T-Technologies; VTB + Alfa rolled out a corporate "Multibank" service (2026-06, [[VTB and Alfa-Bank roll out Multibank service for corporates]]). Protagonist position: Tochka is a niche SME specialist being absorbed by a larger ecosystem owner (T-Technologies, MOEX: T) — moat is switching costs/embedded workflow for SMEs plus, post-deal, distribution via the "T" ecosystem (analysis).

**Comps & multiples.** Headline: 100% of Tochka = 84.8bn RUB (board-approved deal valuation, not a market quote).
- **Internal cross-check (arithmetic):** VK's 25% sale to Interros at ≥21.2bn RUB implies 100% = 21.2 / 0.25 = ~84.8bn RUB — the two independent data points reconcile, so the 84.8bn figure is internally consistent (not an outlier vs the most recent arm's-length-ish block).
- **Parent anchor:** T-Technologies market cap ~457-460bn RUB (Investing.com, date-stale — treat as indicative); the 84.8bn Tochka valuation is ~18-19% of parent market cap (84.8 / ~458 = 0.185). Effective dilution from the share leg is stated at ~3.2% of capital (~85mn shares), consistent with mostly-cash + partial equity funding (per note/bosfera.ru).
- **Multiples:** Tochka P/E, P/B, P/revenue = no data — Tochka's standalone net profit / equity / revenue are not publicly sourced here; cannot compute. For scale context only, T-Bank's 2025 RAS net profit was 112.1bn RUB (AKM) — a different, far larger entity, not a comp.
- Internal M&A comps (private digital-bank take-privates/acquisitions): [[Isracard to acquire Israeli digital bank Esh for $130m]] (100% via share allocation, mixed consideration — same structural template), [[NTT Docomo to acquire SBI Sumishin Net Bank for $2.9B]], [[EQB agrees to acquire PC Financial in $800 million deal]]. Distribution not computed (different geos/currencies/scale) — qualitative only. Verdict: in-line with its own recent block trade; standalone richness vs earnings = not assessable (no Tochka financials).

**Risk flags.**
1. **Related-party / minority-valuation dispute risk.** Mixed consideration (new T-Technologies shares at 320 RUB for 64%-holder Catalytic People vs cash for other shareholders) in a controlled, multi-layer structure invites fairness challenges. T-Bank/Tinkoff has prior form: it lost a ~4-year suit over undervaluing a founder's stake ([[CloudPayments co-founder Spiridonov wins valuation suit vs T-Bank]]). Why it matters: a contested valuation could reopen price, delay the end-2026 close, or create litigation overhang.
2. **Ownership-chain opacity.** The note states group economic stake is 32% (64% via Catalytic People, in which T-Technologies holds 50.01%); separate reporting (AKM/Interfax) describes Interros owning 89% / 1C 11% of Tochka at an interim stage. These are reconcilable as a layered/pass-through structure, but the inconsistency means the true pre-deal cap table and who is cashed-out vs diluted is unclear — governance/execution risk `(analysis)`.
3. **Valuation self-assessment, not market-tested.** The 84.8bn RUB is a board (related-party) valuation backing a ">30% IRR" claim from the acquirer; no independent fairness opinion is sourced. A self-interested controller setting the price for a buyout it controls is a structural conflict, not a de-PR'd market price.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Consolidation, not a re-rating: T-Technologies is converting an ecosystem partner into a wholly-owned SME-banking engine, removing a notionally independent challenger from the RU SME field and tightening the top-tier/ecosystem grip on business banking `(analysis)`. Falsifiable thesis: if the deal closes at ~84.8bn by end-2026 without a successful minority challenge, expect T to push Tochka cross-sell and entrepreneur lending hard in H1-2027. What breaks it: a shareholder valuation suit (cf. CloudPayments) or regulatory/corporate-procedure delay pushing close past end-2026. Watch: official close date, any dissenting-shareholder actions, and whether Tochka's financials are ever disclosed to justify the price.
