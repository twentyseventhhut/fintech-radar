# Max добавил возможность проводить опросы в каналах

Функция доступна в приложении Android, десктопной и веб-версиях мессенджера.

Источник: Max

*   Для создания опроса администратору канала нужно нажать на скрепку в нижней части экрана и выбрать «Опрос», рассказали в сервисе. Затем ввести вопрос и варианты ответа. Можно добавить обложку.

*   Завершить опрос сможет только его создатель или администратор канала с соответствующими правами.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** Max — VK's state-backed Russian "national messenger" — rolled out **polls inside channels**. Channel admins create a poll via the paperclip → "Опрос", enter a question and answer options, and can add a cover image. Admins can configure whether subscribers may **select multiple answers**, **change (re-vote) their answer**, and view results; polls can also be made anonymous. Only the poll creator or an admin with rights can close it. Availability: Android, desktop and web clients create polls natively; iOS users must create via the web version (a notable client gap). The feature was first opened for *testing* to "several hundred" channel authors around **2026-10-05/06** and framed by coverage as GA for channel owners shortly after. (Sources: vc.ru 2026-10-06 (note primary); rb.ru; iguides.ru; ixbt.com 2026-10-06; ppc.world.)

**[1] Why it matters (fintech/infra angle).** On its own, a poll feature is trivial; it matters only as one brick in Max's **state-mandated super-app build-out** (messaging + gov services + digital ID + planned payments), pre-installed on devices sold in Russia since ~Sept 2025. Engagement features like re-votable multi-choice polls exist to **thicken the channel surface** that VK and Yandex have just begun to monetize with ads — the supply-side cabinet ([[VK opens Max channel ads to accounts over 1K subscribers]]) and the demand-side per-user targeting ([[Yandex Direct adds personalized ad targeting under Max posts]]), both the same week. This is explicit **Telegram-playbook mimicry**: polls, channels, stories, comment threads. The fintech leg (in-chat payments, e.g. [[MTS Bank enables money transfers inside Max chats]]) remains the real prize but is still gated — the Bank of Russia called confirming financial operations via Max "premature" ([[Russia's central bank calls MAX transaction approval premature]]).

**[2] Numbers / scale.** Max's own CEO Farit Khusnoyarov (Digital Solutions forum, 2026-10-09) cited peak **~140M registrations and ~90M daily active users** — figures that supersede the lower ones in sibling notes (50M+ mid-2026; Mediascope ~86.2M MAU / ~69M DAU June 2026; VK's own ">90M daily" press release). By Aug 2026 there were 11.5M+ public/private channels. Caveat: ~70% of Max channels were registered by government agencies and public institutions at the authorities' behest (Agentstvo analysis via Meduza), and reach is partly a product of pre-install and the blocking of WhatsApp (Feb 2026) and Telegram (Mar 2026) — so "users" overstate organic, ad-receptive engagement.

**[3] Timeline / sequence.** **Apr 2026** — Yandex РСЯ starts working with Max channels. **May 18, 2026** — "ads in messengers" beta in Yandex Direct. **Sept 25, 2026** — Max begins testing channel ad impressions. **Oct 5–6, 2026** — *this item*: polls open in channels (testing → channel owners), multi-choice + re-vote + anonymous. **Oct 7, 2026** — Yandex Direct adds per-user targeting under Max posts. **Oct 9, 2026** — Khusnoyarov announces **stories, multi-pin and comment threads** for channels (stories/multi-pin "soon", threads Q1 2027) and the 140M/90M figures. So polls are one move in a dense Oct-2026 channel-feature + monetization sprint, not an isolated event.

**[4] Open questions.** Does multi-choice/re-vote change engagement materially, or is it feature parity theatre vs Telegram? Why the iOS-create gap (App Store policy friction vs a sanctioned-market app)? Do polls feed any ad/targeting signal (behavioral data) given Yandex's new per-user targeting on Max? Are the 140M/90M figures audited or self-reported, and how much is coerced/pre-install reach? And does any of this move the gated payments leg — the only part with direct fintech weight?

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team questions**

1. Is this a meaningful event at all, or a trivial feature-parity ship? A re-votable multi-choice poll is table-stakes functionality Telegram has had for years — materiality rests entirely on the Max super-app context, not the poll itself.
2. Is it fresh or a duplicate? The *polls* launch (Oct 5–6) is distinct from the same-week VK ad cabinet (Oct 12 note) and Yandex per-user targeting (Oct 7) — different features, same platform. But it overlaps heavily with the Oct 9 CEO announcement of stories/threads/multi-pin; risk of being one slice of a single feature-sprint story.
3. Testing vs GA: coverage says "several hundred authors" tested it first — is this a limited beta dressed as a launch? The note's vc.ru text implies broader channel-owner availability.
4. Why the iOS gap (create only via web)? App Store policy friction for a sanctioned/state app, or an engineering shortcut? Signals platform immaturity.
5. Do the multi-choice/re-vote options generate behavioral signal that feeds Yandex's new per-user ad targeting on Max? Unstated — potential privacy/monetization link.
6. Are the CEO's 140M registrations / 90M DAU figures audited or self-reported? They exceed Mediascope's June-2026 ~86M MAU; self-interested source.
7. How much of the channel base is organic? ~70% of channels are government/institutional (Agentstvo/Meduza) — engagement on polls may be hollow.
8. Does this touch fintech/payments at all? No — payments leg still gated (CBR "premature", Mar 2026). Relevance is only via super-app infra, not payments.
9. Single-ish sourcing: primary is vc.ru re-reporting a Max press statement; corroboration (rb.ru, iguides, ixbt) all trace to the same vendor announcement — any independent verification of actual usage?
10. Is "revoting" a genuine differentiator or a liability (poll manipulation, brigading in state-run channels)?
11. Does feature velocity (polls, stories, threads, multi-pin in one month) signal real product momentum or a rushed catch-up to justify the mandated roll-out?
12. Could the feature carry data-collection concerns given Max's lack of E2E encryption and reported FSB data-sharing? Poll responses are political-opinion-adjacent data.
13. Timeline hygiene: ingest/note date 2026-10-12 vs event ~2026-10-06 — framing should anchor on Oct 5–6, with the Oct 9 CEO roadmap as adjacent context.
14. Does the poll feature move any monetizable metric (ad fill, ARPU) or is it pure engagement cosmetics with no revenue read?

**Importance: 2/5 — rationale.** A minor channel engagement feature whose only weight comes from the broader Max state-messenger/super-app and ad-monetization narrative (where the VK cabinet and Yandex targeting notes already carry the 3/5 substance). It is dated and real, but low-materiality on its own, single-vendor-sourced, non-fintech (payments gated), and partly a feature-parity move vs Telegram inside a coerced-distribution market. Worth a line in the Russian digital-infra beat; not a standalone fintech event.

Foreign incumbents are being squeezed out: WhatsApp and Telegram are both largely blocked/throttled in Russia, yet residual usage persists — WhatsApp ~63.5mn (June 2026) and Telegram ~35.1mn Russian users, with the foreign-messenger audience still up 26% YoY per Kommersant (via Meduza, 2026-07-24), i.e. demand has not disappeared, only migrated around blocks. Into that vacuum VK's Max was designated the "national messenger" by government order (June 2025, PM Mishustin) and pre-installed on devices sold in Russia since ~Sept 2025 — a distribution mandate, not organic pull. Max reports >100mn registered users (March 2026) and a daily audience that VK/press put at ~68–77mn DAU in spring 2026, >1bn messages and 28mn calls/day (via vk.company press, The Moscow Times 2026-07-31). "Why now": the moat here is regulatory/distribution (pre-install + mobile carriers waiving data fees for Max), not product — which reframes a routine poll feature as catch-up, not differentiation. (analysis)

**Competitive landscape.** The relevant KPIs for a messenger-as-super-app are DAU/MAU and stickiness (DAU/MAU), messages & calls/day, and — once monetized — ARPU and ad load. Players: Max (state-backed, VK-built), Telegram (channels/creator economy, blocked), WhatsApp (Meta, blocked), plus VK's own VKontakte/Sferum. Basis of competition in Russia is now distribution and state backing rather than features. Recent moves: VK opened targeted ads in Max channels with a 1,000-subscriber floor delivered via VK Реклама ([[VK opens Max channel ads to accounts over 1K subscribers]], 2026-10-12); Yandex added per-user personalized ad targeting under Max posts across "tens of thousands" of channels ([[Yandex Direct adds personalized ad targeting under Max posts]], cnews 2026-10-07 — advertisers name Telegram 67% / Max 56% as priority channels to end-2026); VTB launched an in-Max digital bank and NSPK/СБП added QR + biometric ("pay by face") contactless payments as Max mini-apps; Max opened a Partner Platform (Oct 23) for third-party services and payments. This multi-choice-poll-with-revoting launch (2026-10-06, per vc.ru) is textbook feature-parity catch-up with Telegram/WhatsApp polls — Max's position is **catching up** on product while **ahead on mandated distribution**. Moat = switching costs engineered by the state (pre-install, carrier subsidy, gov-services bundling), not network effects earned on merit. (analysis)

**Comps & multiples.** Max is not separately valued; its economics roll into VK (IPJSC, MOEX: VKCO). VK is loss-making and does not break out Max revenue, so clean EV/EBITDA or P/E on the asset = no data / [UNSOURCED]. Internal comps (grep over News/): [[VK opens Max channel ads to accounts over 1K subscribers]] and [[Yandex Direct adds personalized ad targeting under Max posts]] (the monetization layer this feature feeds), plus [[VK Video revamps Films and Series catalog section]], [[VK sells 40% stake in ticketing service Intickets]] and [[Sferum launches Olympiads service for school competitions]] (VK ecosystem). External read-across for the monetization model: Telegram's channel-ads program uses the **same 1,000-subscriber eligibility floor** and shares 50% of channel-ad revenue (in TON) with owners (telegram.org/blog/monetization-for-channels) — VK is cloning that playbook to capture the migrating creator/advertiser economy. Distribution-not-revenue flag: Max's ~68–77mn DAU vs Telegram's blocked-but-sticky creator base means reach is bought (pre-install) while monetization is nascent; price-per-user comparison "not computed" — no disclosed Max revenue.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Distribution-funded, not demand-pulled.** Max's scale rests on pre-install + carrier data-fee waivers + national-messenger mandate; if any of those props is relaxed, DAU could prove soft — reach bought this way does not convert to engagement/ARPU the way organic adoption does. A poll feature does not fix that.
2. **Trust / surveillance overhang caps monetizable engagement.** Independent researchers (InterSecLab, Meduza 2026-05) allege no end-to-end encryption and extensive tracking; Max's security centre denied it ([[... VK opens Max channel ads ...]] source thread shows hostile user sentiment on vc.ru). Low trust suppresses the private-messaging and payments usage that monetization (ads + VTB bank + СБП pay-by-face) depends on — second-order: the super-app stack is only as valuable as the engagement it can defend.
3. **Value captured one layer up / by the ad network, not the messenger.** Yandex (Директ/РСЯ) and VK Реклама own the targeting + demand stack feeding Max channels; if the ad network captures the margin, Max supplies inventory (commoditized reach) while someone else books the high-margin perform­ance ad economics — classic disintermediation of the surface by the rails above it.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** For the sector this is a non-event on its own — a parity feature — but it is a marker that Max is being hardened from a mandated messenger into a monetizable channel/super-app surface (ads + payments + partner platform) on a Telegram-cloned economic model. Falsifiable thesis: if Max's ad + partner monetization is real, VK's "Social platforms & media content" segment (RUB 77bn 9M-2025 revenue, +6% YoY) should show an accelerating ad line with Max broken out or called out in VK's 2026 reporting; trigger/catalyst = VK's FY2026 release or the next quarterly disclosing Max monetization metrics. What breaks the thesis: Max DAU and ad inventory keep growing on paper while VK revenue growth stays single-digit and losses persist — i.e. distribution without durable monetization. (analysis)

Read-through: VK is pushing EBITDA positive at group level, but the engine is still VK-platform/SMB advertising and VK Tech (RUB 10.7bn, +39%), NOT a separately-monetized Max — confirming risk flag 1 that Max is currently a cost/distribution bet whose revenue has not surfaced in segment disclosure. ## Earnings Review

<!-- enrichment:earnings_review -->
**No full earnings report in the news** — this is a product feature (Max adds multi-choice polls/revoting in channels), not a results print. Folding in parent VK's latest reported figures as context; Max itself is pre-monetization and not disclosed as a segment.

**Parent context (VK IPJSC, FY 2025, reported 2026-03-19, audited IFRS).** Revenue RUB 160.0 bn (+8% YoY) — a deceleration vs the double-digit prints earlier in the year (Q2 revenue +17% YoY, 9M +10% YoY), so growth cooled into Q4. EBITDA RUB 22.6 bn vs a negative result in 2024; EBITDA margin ~14% (+17 p.p. YoY) — the real swing is the profitability turn, not the top line. Net loss narrowed to RUB 25.4 bn from RUB 94.9 bn in 2024 (loss cut ~3.7x), but VK still posted its 6th straight annual loss; cumulative six-year losses are ~RUB 195 bn.

**By segment.** Social Platforms & Media Content (the home of VK's social/messaging/ad assets, incl. Max): revenue RUB 109.8 bn (+5% YoY), segment EBITDA RUB 21.1 bn vs negative in 2024 — the margin turn is carried here, but at only +5% this is the slow-growth core. VK Tech: revenue RUB 18.8 bn (+38% YoY), EBITDA RUB 4.8 bn (+22% YoY), driven by VK WorkSpace (+75% YoY) and business apps (+66% YoY) — the fastest-growing, highest-quality engine.

**Max positioning (the subject's product).** Max is a strategic, state-backed "national messenger" bet, not yet a revenue line: ~RUB 4 bn ($53.2m) allocated to its development, management calls monetization "premature." Scale is being built fast — 85 mn registered users (mid-2025 disclosure) rising past 100 mn / 107 mn registered by Mar 2026, DAU >77 mn (Mar 2026); VK reported Max average MAU of 20.2 mn globally / 18.9 mn in Russia in Q4 2025. The poll feature here — and the recently launched channel ads (>1K subs) and Yandex Direct targeting under Max posts — are the engagement/monetization-infrastructure build-out that precedes any ad revenue showing up in the Social Platforms segment. (hypothesis) Expect Max ad monetization to land in that segment, not as a standalone disclosure, over 2026–2027.

**Thesis-flags.** (1) Margin inflection is real (EBITDA swung positive, +17 p.p.) but top-line growth decelerated to +8% FY and the core social segment to +5% — the bull case now leans on VK Tech (+38%) and eventual Max monetization, not the legacy social ad base. (2) Still loss-making 6 years running; a feature like multi-choice polls is adoption plumbing for a product that remains a cash cost, not yet a contributor — watch for the first disclosed Max ad revenue as the signal that the state subsidy is converting to P&L. (3) Max growth is substantially mandate-/state-driven (national-messenger push), so registered-user and DAU gains should be discounted for organic engagement quality before reading them as monetizable ad inventory.
