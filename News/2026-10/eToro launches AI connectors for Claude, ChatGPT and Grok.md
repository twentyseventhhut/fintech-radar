 Abu Dhabi, UAE – etoro, the trading and investing platform, has launched connectors for Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Grok Bot. Built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the connectors bring an investor’s etoro account into the AI assistant they already use with no API keys, code or technical setup required.
"Every few years, technology changes the way people invest. Social investing connected people to each other. Mobile put the markets in their pocket. Today it's AI, and the place people now think and plan is with their AI assistant, so that's where their portfolio needs to be. For many years this industry gave people access. The next phase is intelligence: investors combining their own judgment with AI. It's not the investor or the AI, it's both, with the investor always in control." commented Yoni Assia, Co-founder and CEO of etoro.
etoro’s latest Retail Investor Beat shows the appetite for this shift. 56 per cent of retail investors either already use or are open to using AI tools such as ChatGPT or AI agents to help pick or alter investments. 
The opportunity is no longer just asking AI what is happening in the market. It is asking what is happening in your portfolio. Once connected, users can ask questions about their actual holdings, positions, balances and performance, rather than a hypothetical investor. 
Users can choose read-only access for research and analysis, or read-and-act access where available. Any action is proposed for review and approval. Funding and withdrawals remain in etoro, never in the chat, and users can pause or disconnect the connection at any time. New users can begin with simple look-ups and test workflows on a demo account first.
Put simply: the assistant brings the analysis, etoro brings the market access, and control remains with the investor.
Guy Barkat, Director of API Partnerships said: “AI assistants are becoming part of everyday life. They help people plan, research, summarise information and get things done. Investing is moving into that same space. Connectors bring portfolio context into the AI tools people already use, while keeping the investor in control. It is an important step towards an agentic trading experience built around clear boundaries, transparency and approval.”
etoro is listed in the connector directory of select AI platforms, with more listings on the way. On those platforms, setup takes a few clicks: search for etoro, connect and sign in. Anywhere else, investors can add etoro manually in minutes by adding a custom connector. Tori, etoro’s AI agent, can help you through the process plus step-by-step guides for every assistant are available at https://mcp.etoro.app/.
Capabilities and availability vary by assistant and regulatory jurisdiction. Connectors are not currently available to clients in the US, Australia or Singapore.
etoro's Retail Investor Beat surveyed 11,000 retail investors across 13 countries between 13 and 28 August 2026. The research was conducted by Opinium. Respondents did not need to be etoro users.

# Context-enrichment: eToro launches AI connectors for Claude, ChatGPT and Grok
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 3/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On ~8 Oct 2026 eToro launched **MCP (Model Context Protocol) connectors** that plug a user's live eToro account into third-party AI assistants — Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Grok and "Grok Bot" (xAI) — with "no API keys, code or setup". Two scopes: **read-only** (query holdings, positions, balances, performance) or **read-and-act** "where available", with every action **proposed for user review/approval**. Funding/withdrawals stay inside eToro, never in chat; users can pause/disconnect anytime; a demo account is suggested for testing. Setup is a few clicks where eToro is listed in a platform's connector directory, or a manual "custom connector" elsewhere; guides + the Tori agent assist at mcp.etoro.app.
- **De-PR'd reality:** this is **not new trading capability** — it is a **distribution/surface move**. eToro already had in-house agentic trading ([[eToro lets investors delegate trades to AI agents]], Mar 2026, "Agent Portfolios" sub-accounts) and a conversational AI companion ([[eToro relaunches Tori AI investing companion with Grok]], Apr 2026). The connectors just expose that account to AI chat UIs the user already lives in via the open MCP standard. The press line "now it knows your portfolio" is the whole pitch: context portability, not a new engine.
- **Vagueness to flag:** "read-and-act **where available**" + "capabilities and availability vary by assistant and regulatory jurisdiction" = the actual trade-execution footprint is undefined per model/geography. **Not available in US, Australia, Singapore** — i.e. excluded from its own US-listed home market and from the markets it most recently entered. The "56% of retail investors use/open to AI tools" stat is from eToro's own Retail Investor Beat (Opinium, 11k respondents, 13 countries, 13–28 Aug 2026) — a self-interested survey, not independent demand proof.
- **Why structured this way:** building on the open MCP spec (vs a proprietary API) lets eToro ride the connector directories of Anthropic/OpenAI/xAI instead of fighting for the assistant layer it will never own. Keeping funding/withdrawals and final approval inside eToro is a **regulatory/liability firewall** — it keeps eToro the custodian-of-record and pushes the "AI did it" risk onto the user (see the unusually blunt disclaimers: "AI systems may perform poorly, act quickly and be difficult to stop… you are responsible for all trades, losses and third-party use of your data").

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Robinhood** ([[Robinhood lets AI agents trade stocks and issues agent credit card]], 27 May 2026): agentic trading beta via a dedicated sub-account + agent credit card — but Robinhood's model is its *own* agent framework, and US-centric. eToro is the mirror image: **open MCP into others' assistants, US excluded.**
- **The MCP/connector wave in fintech (all 2025–2026):** [[Coinbase launches Payments MCP for AI agents]], [[BitGo launches MCP Server to bring crypto infrastructure to AI agents]], [[Blend launches Autopilot MCP Server for FI-built AI agents]], [[Finix launches MCP integrations for AI-native developer workflows]]. eToro is **late-ish but first-of-kind for a retail brokerage** exposing a *consumer* portfolio (not dev/infra) into mainstream chat assistants.
- **Assistant side:** Anthropic has a Claude Connector directory (thousands of MCP servers) and [[Anthropic expands Claude for Financial Services with Excel add-in]] (Oct 2025) signals its finance push; OpenAI has ChatGPT "Apps" and [[OpenAI rolls out personal finance experience in ChatGPT]] (May 2026). eToro is one listing among thousands — **position: parity/early-mover on the brokerage sub-category, but a commodity participant in a crowded directory.**
- **Why the landscape is this way (2nd order):** the assistant platforms (Anthropic/OpenAI/xAI) own the user attention and the "front door"; brokers become one of many connected tools. The margin/relationship question shifts from "who has the best app" to "who the assistant *defaults to* when a user says 'buy X'." eToro gets reach but risks being disintermediated into a back-end execution venue.

## [2] Company history / fit
eToro (founded 2007; Nasdaq: ETOR since 14 May 2025, IPO at $52, +29% day-one, ~$4.2B) has run a consistent **AI-at-the-core** narrative: AI smart portfolios (UAE, Jul 2025), Agent Portfolios (Mar 2026), Tori relaunch on Grok 4.2 (Apr 2026), and now connectors. It also cut ~7% of staff (Jan 2026) and is exploring banking/M&A (Zengo crypto-wallet deal, Apr 2026; banking push, Jun 2026). Fit is logical: eToro's DNA is "social + accessible retail investing," and CEO Yoni Assia explicitly frames AI as the next access-paradigm after social and mobile.
- **Why it acts this way:** the stock is **down ~62% from IPO (~$25 vs $52 offer / $67 close)** and crypto drove ~91% of Q2'25 revenue — a volatile, commoditized take-rate base. eToro needs an **AI/software narrative to defend/re-rate the multiple** and diversify off crypto beta. Q2 2026 was operationally solid (net income +77% YoY to $53M, funded accounts +18% to 4.28M, AUA +10% to $19.2B) yet **analysts still cut price targets** — so the connector launch is partly a market-sentiment play, not just product.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Genuinely novel:** exposing a *retail brokerage portfolio* into Claude/ChatGPT/Grok via standard MCP appears to be a **first among listed retail brokers** (Robinhood kept it in-house/US). The value-add is real for research/analysis (read-only Q&A on your own holdings beats hypothetical prompts).
- **But traction = zero proven.** This is an **announcement, not adoption**: no user counts, no trade volumes, "more listings on the way," read-and-act only "where available," and the home US market excluded. No independent confirmation it is live/listed in the named directories beyond the PR.
- **Why value capture is shaky (deeper):** in the stack, the **assistant owns the user intent and the margin-bearing relationship**; eToro supplies execution + custody. If assistants later add their own multi-broker routing or default brokers, eToro's connector becomes a commoditized pipe — the same disintermediation risk Ramp faces vs Visa/Mastercard on agentic rails. The durable asset eToro keeps is **custody + regulatory license** (funding stays in-platform) — that, not the connector, is the moat.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
"More listings on the way"; expect a push to get read-and-act live in more jurisdictions and prominence in the Anthropic/OpenAI directories. Watch for (a) whether US/AU/SG restrictions lift, (b) any disclosed adoption/trade metrics, (c) regulatory reaction to agentic retail trading (the disclaimers pre-empt liability). Market sentiment on eToro is cautious (analyst target cuts post-Q2'26 despite good numbers); this is incremental narrative support, not a catalyst.
- **Counterintuitive 2nd order:** broad AI-assistant distribution could *increase* trade frequency and churn (good for take-rate) but also *commoditize* eToro's social/community differentiator — if the assistant is where you "think and plan," eToro's own app (and copy-trading social graph) loses attention. Reach may erode the very engagement moat eToro monetizes.

**Sector & drivers.** Subvertical: retail multi-asset brokerage, at the leading edge of "agentic finance interfaces" — brokers exposing accounts to third-party LLM assistants (Claude/ChatGPT/Grok) over Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP). No credible free TAM figure for the MCP-brokerage niche specifically — no data; treat it as an emergent layer on top of the ~$400-500bn+ global online-brokerage pool (unsourced, excluded). What IS sourced is the demand signal eToro cites from its own Retail Investor Beat (11,000 investors, 13 countries, 13-28 Aug 2026, via Opinium): 56% of retail investors already use or are open to AI tools to help pick/alter investments (per eToro PR, 2026-10). Structure: distribution is consolidating onto a handful of AI-assistant "front ends" (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI) that are becoming the new top-of-funnel; brokers compete to be *listed in the connector directory* of those platforms — a new, winner-takes-share shelf-space game. Why now: MCP standardised account connection (no API keys/code), lowering integration cost to "a few clicks", so the race is to be first in each assistant's directory before defaults harden. Why-ladder: standard protocol → zero-integration listing → assistant becomes the demand gateway → broker risks being disintermediated to a pure execution/custody back end if the assistant owns the user relationship.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: funded accounts, net contribution (eToro's top line, = total revenue & income less crypto cost-of-revenue & margin interest), AUA, trades/volumes, invested-amount-per-trade. Key players & basis of competition (product/distribution, not price):
- **eToro (protagonist):** Nasdaq-listed (ETOR), 75 countries, "millions" of users; launched MCP connectors for Claude/ChatGPT/Grok/Grok Bot on 2026-10-12 with read-only vs read-and-act modes, action-approval gating, funding/withdrawals kept in-app. Builds directly on its 2026-03 Agent Portfolios ([[eToro lets investors delegate trades to AI agents]]) — it has an in-house agent ("Tori") AND now opens to external assistants. Not available to US/Australia/Singapore clients.
- **Robinhood (closest comp):** launched third-party AI agentic trading + agentic account in 2026-05 ([[Robinhood lets AI agents trade stocks and issues agent credit card]]), then embedded in-app "Robinhood Agents" (OpenAI/Anthropic models) at HOOD Summit, Sep 2026 (per TechCrunch/CNBC/Robinhood newsroom, 2026-05/09). 28.4m funded customers Q2 2026, +7% YoY (per Robinhood Q2 2026 release).
Basis of competition: breadth of assistant integrations, the read-vs-act permission model, and jurisdictional coverage. Protagonist's position: *co-leader / fast-follower* on agentic interfaces — eToro is first-mover on the open MCP-connector pattern (vs Robinhood's dedicated-account approach), but Robinhood dwarfs it on US scale. Moat `(analysis)`: eToro's edge is its social/copy-trading network + multi-asset, multi-jurisdiction footprint; the connectors themselves are *not* a moat — MCP is an open standard any broker can list on, so first-mover shelf position is defensible only until peers list too.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Disintermediation / loss of top-of-funnel.** If OpenAI/Anthropic/xAI become where users decide trades, the assistant owns the relationship and the broker is pushed toward commoditised execution+custody — margin could migrate up the stack to the AI layer. Second-order: brokers may end up paying for directory placement or revenue-share, eroding net contribution per account (already seen falling invested-amount/trade).
2. **Execution / fiduciary & liability risk.** eToro's own disclaimer is unusually blunt: "AI systems may perform poorly, act quickly and be difficult to stop," trades "may differ from what you asked," and "you are responsible for all trades, losses and third-party use of your data." Read-and-act mode on live money via a third-party LLM is a mis-trade / mis-selling / data-leakage surface; a single public LLM-driven loss event could trigger regulatory scrutiny of agentic retail trading (FCA/ESMA/MiCA-adjacent).
3. **Jurisdiction gating caps the TAM.** Connectors are *not* available in the US, Australia or Singapore — i.e. eToro's single largest potential retail-AI market (US) is excluded, limiting the strategic payoff vs Robinhood, which is US-native.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This is a *new-entry / land-grab*, not a re-rating: being listed in each assistant's connector directory is the 2026 equivalent of app-store shelf placement, and eToro is making an early bid for it. Falsifiable thesis: if agentic connectors drive measurable engagement/net-contribution-per-account uplift, eToro's +17% funded-account growth converts into net-contribution re-acceleration and narrows the P/E discount to Robinhood. What would break it: (a) net contribution per account keeps thinning despite account growth (invested-amount/trade already -36% YoY), or (b) a regulator restricts third-party-LLM live trading. Trigger to watch: directory-listing breadth (how many assistants, and whether US unlocks) + whether Q3/Q4 2026 results show AI-connector cohorts trading more.

**Thesis-flags.**
1. **Crypto-dependence is unwinding fast → the AI-connector launch is a monetization hedge.** Crypto fell from ~50% to ~11% of trading commission in ~18 months; contribution -59% YoY. Why it matters: eToro's historic earnings beta was a crypto beta, now visibly derating. The connectors (Claude/ChatGPT/Grok via MCP) push engagement into equities/portfolio workflows — second-order: deeper, stickier multi-asset activity is exactly the Capital Markets line (+25% YoY) that is replacing crypto, so the launch defends the mix shift that drove the beat.
2. **Funded accounts +18% with net contribution only +9% → per-account monetization is softening.** Why it matters: growth is coming from accounts, not revenue per account; agentic/AI features that lift trades-per-user are the lever to re-couple the two. The connectors are a direct play on ARPU, not just acquisition.
3. **"Read-and-act" agentic trading = a new fee-generating surface, but also a new liability/suitability surface.** The release explicitly disclaims AI-originated trades and pushes responsibility to the user; funding/withdrawals stay inside eToro. Second-order: if agentic volume scales, it re-accelerates Capital Markets commission without added crypto risk — the cleanest read-through from the Q2 economics to this launch.
4. **Margin is flat (~34%), net income beat is leverage/one-offs, not pricing.** Why it matters: the 77% net-income jump is not a repeatable margin story; sustained EPS growth needs volume (more engaged, more-frequent traders) — again the AI-connector / App Store / Tori agentic thesis.
