# Context-enrichment: Ozon Q3 2026 net profit RUB 3.7bn amid ~RUB 30bn warehouse-attack provisions
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 4/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
Ozon (MKPAO Ozon — the re-domiciled Russian e-commerce + fintech group, ex-Cyprus Ozon Holdings) reported Q3 2026 IFRS results. These are GROUP consolidated results, with Ozon Bank reported as a *fintech segment* inside them (not the bank in isolation). De-PR'd numbers:
- GMV (incl. services) +42% YoY to RUB 1.6trn; orders ~1.2bn, +76% YoY (order growth outruns GMV growth → average order value is FALLING, i.e. more, smaller baskets).
- Revenue +63% YoY to RUB 421.2bn (services revenue +70% to 275.7bn; goods sales +46% to 91.3bn; interest income +57% to 54.3bn).
- Gross profit ~2x to RUB 122.1bn; adjusted EBITDA +124% to RUB 93bn (e-commerce segment adj. EBITDA RUB 65.4bn). EBITDA/GMV margin up from 3.8% to 6.0%.
- Net profit RUB 3.7bn, +25% YoY (vs RUB 2.9bn in Q3 2025). Active buyers +17% YoY to 73.5m (counting methodology not disclosed).
- Fintech segment revenue +57% to RUB 85.2bn; pre-tax profit +57% to RUB 27.1bn (fintech NET profit NOT disclosed).

**The structural tell — why net profit is tiny vs a doubling EBITDA.** The headline "net profit RUB 3.7bn" is almost entirely an artifact of a one-off: Ozon booked RUB 30.1bn of *estimated* loss provisions tied to UAV/drone attacks on 11 of its warehouses. These are provisional accruals recognised in advance, not realised cash losses. Two numbers tell opposite stories: adjusted EBITDA +124% (the real operating trajectory) vs net profit barely positive (dragged near zero by the one-off). The PR frame leans on "profit maintained DESPITE the attacks"; the honest read is that absent the ~RUB 30bn charge, bottom-line profit would have been an order of magnitude larger — exactly the sardonic in-source reader comment ("officially losses from attacks are 10x the profit?"). The provision is a soft number: an estimate revisable up or down as insurance/claims resolve.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Wildberries (RWB / Russ)** — #1 Russian marketplace by GMV and Ozon's direct rival; the same source cluster flags a sellers' union FAS complaint about WB's *delayed payouts to sellers hit by the same attacks* — i.e. the UAV-strike logistics shock is sector-wide, not Ozon-specific. Ozon's choice to pre-provision RUB 30bn vs WB's payout disputes is a governance/transparency contrast.
- **Yandex Market** — third major RU marketplace, backed by Yandex's ecosystem; competes on logistics + fintech bundling.
- **Fintech comps:** Ozon Bank is a captive fintech inside the marketplace — the same playbook as **MercadoLibre/Mercado Pago**, **Sea/Shopee + SeaMoney**, **Grab Financial**, Nubank-style embedded credit. The "+57% fintech revenue, pre-tax RUB 27.1bn" profile mirrors the MELI thesis: the marketplace is the acquisition funnel, the bank monetises the balance.
- Position: Ozon is **catching up to / near parity with Wildberries on GMV**, but ahead on *disclosure and fintech-integration maturity* (public MKPAO filer with a registered bank, DFA platform, insurers).
