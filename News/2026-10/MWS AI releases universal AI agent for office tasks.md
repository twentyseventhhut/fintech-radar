# MWS AI releases universal AI agent for office tasks

> [!info] 2026-10-27 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[42 секунды] Ведомости: MWS AI выпустила универсального ИИ-агента для решения офисных задач в периметре компании

## Первоисточники

### vedomosti.ru
<https://www.vedomosti.ru/technologies/industries_and_markets/news/2026/10/27/1240592-ii-agenta-dlya-ofisnih>
*342 слов · direct*

Компания MWS AI, входящая в МТС Web Services, запустила универсального ИИ-агента MWS AI Cowork для решения офисных задач. Сервис работает в режиме одного окна: ищет и анализирует информацию, взаимодействует с файлами и корпоративными приложениями, создает таблицы, презентации и отчеты, следует из пресс-релиза.
По утверждению компании, в отличие от Claude Cowork и ChatGPT Work, новый сервис учитывает требования российских компаний к безопасности корпоративных данных и гибкости ИИ-инфраструктуры. Его можно развернуть в закрытом контуре организации в России и использовать с различными ИИ-моделями, включая Cotype от MWS AI, модели Сбера, «Яндекса» и открытые зарубежные разработки.
Сотрудники смогут поручить сервису до 90% повторяющихся офисных задач по работе с информацией, прогнозирует MWS AI. Агент работает через чат-интерфейс и подключается к почте, календарю, мессенджерам, хранилищам, CRM и другим корпоративным приложениям. Он действует от имени пользователя в пределах его прав доступа.
Для выполнения типовых задач сотрудники могут создавать навыки – инструкции, которыми затем можно делиться с коллегами. Например, для подготовки ежемесячного отчета достаточно заранее указать источники данных, правила проверки и формат документа. Сервис также запоминает предпочтения пользователя и контекст задач, а сохраненные сведения можно просматривать, исправлять и удалять.
«Можно начать с отдельных задач – например, подготовки отчетов или сравнения предложений поставщиков, – а затем подключать новые отделы и переходить к процессам, в которых участвуют несколько команд», – отметил генеральный директор MWS AI Денис Филиппов. По его словам, компания может постепенно расширять автоматизацию по мере роста потребностей. При этом руководство сохраняет контроль над расходами, доступом к данным и используемыми инструментами.
По результатам пилотных проектов в подразделениях МТС Web Services, сотрудник, использующий ИИ-агента в 90% задач, экономил не менее часа рабочего времени в день. В компании оценили такую экономию примерно в 263 000 руб. в год на сотрудника. Годовая лицензия на продукт в 2026 г. стоит от 6 млн до 20 млн руб. в зависимости от комплектации и числа пользователей.
При тестировании отдельных рабочих сценариев в компании с 1000 сотрудников подготовка презентации на 10 слайдов занимала на 40–50% меньше времени, квартального финансового отчета – на 30–50%, протокола встречи – на 50–80%. В оценке учтено время на проверку и доработку результата сотрудником.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened (one line).** On 2026-10-27 MWS AI (the AI arm of MTS Web Services / MTS group) launched **MWS AI Cowork**, a "universal" enterprise AI agent that runs inside a company's perimeter and automates office knowledge-work — searching/analysing information, working with files and corporate apps, and producing tables, presentations and reports from a single chat window. The company claims it can take on up to 90% of repetitive office information tasks. (vedomosti.ru primary; also CNews, RB.ru, RG.ru, ITWeek, Sostav.)

**[1] Why it matters / so-what.** This is MWS AI's move up the stack from *tools to build agents* to a *ready-made horizontal agent for end users* — explicitly positioned as the Russian, data-sovereign answer to Anthropic's Claude Cowork and OpenAI's ChatGPT Work (both named by MWS AI). The pitch is on-prem deployment in a closed Russian contour plus model choice (MWS AI's own Cotype, plus Sber and Yandex models and open foreign models). It sharpens the enterprise-AI contest inside Russia where Sber (GigaChat Enterprise / GigaChat Business / GigaCowork) and Yandex (AI Studio, B2B Tech) are pushing the same "AI employee" narrative. For MTS, enterprise AI/cloud (MWS) is a declared growth pillar alongside its fintech/bank and telecom base.

**[2] Prior coverage / internal links (freshness check).** This is the first note on a horizontal end-user office agent from MWS AI; prior MWS AI/MTS notes cover adjacent-but-distinct products, so this is **fresh**, not a duplicate:
- [[MTS adds dialog-based agent creation to MWS AI Agents Platform]] (2026-10-19) — the *platform* to build/run custom corporate agents (AI Force v4.0, scan reading, corporate-rules memory). Cowork is the packaged horizontal agent, not the builder.
- [[MTS adds AI assistant to MWS Tables]] (2026-10-19) — narrow spreadsheet/app assistant.
- [[MWS AI opens RESON speech-recognition model evaluation tool]] (2026-10-12) — speech-model benchmarking.
- [[MTS bundles enterprise solutions under MTS CorpTech brand]] (2026-10-12) — the B2B umbrella brand (connectivity, cloud, AI, IIoT) Cowork ultimately sells under.

Related external AI-agent comps in the DB: [[Revolut builds AI agents for sales and customer service]], [[HSBC partners with Mistral AI for generative AI]], [[This Week in Fintech Cloudera report on enterprise AI agents]].

**[3] Numbers / specifics (vendor-reported, unaudited).**
- Claimed coverage: up to **90%** of repetitive office information tasks.
- Pilot result (inside MTS Web Services units): an employee using the agent on 90% of tasks saved **≥1 hour/day**, valued by MWS AI at **~₽263,000/employee/year**.
- Annual licence in 2026: **₽6M–₽20M** depending on configuration/user count.
- Test scenarios (1,000-employee company, incl. human review time): 10-slide deck **−40–50%**, quarterly financial report **−30–50%**, meeting minutes **−50–80%**.
- Architecture: chat UI; connects to email, calendar, messengers, storage, CRM and other corporate apps; acts on the user's behalf within their access rights; reusable shareable "skills"; remembers user preferences/context (viewable/editable/deletable).
- Models: Cotype (MWS AI) + Sber + Yandex + open foreign models; on-prem in a closed Russian contour. CEO: Denis Filippov.

**[4] Watch-items.**

- Real customer traction/logos outside MTS, and conversion given the ₽6–20M/yr price.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russian enterprise/generative-AI, specifically the agentic "AI coworker" layer (chat-driven agents that execute multi-step office workflows end-to-end, not just answer). Market size: Russia's GenAI market reached ~RUB 58bn in 2025, >5x the RUB 13bn of 2024; Onside projects ~RUB 778bn by 2030 at ~68% CAGR, with the B2B segment driving ~77% of volume (per Onside / market press, via samreshuuu.com & tadviser, as of 2026). Adoption is past the pilot phase: 71% of large Russian firms used generative AI in ≥1 business function (Yakov & Partners / Yandex, Dec 2025); Yandex AI Studio processed 597bn commercial tokens in H1 2026 (~18x YoY) and hosts 30k+ agents built on it. **Structure:** consolidating around a handful of sovereign full-stack players (Sber/GigaChat, Yandex, MTS/MWS, T-Bank) rather than fragmented — entry barriers are capital (own LLMs + GPU/datacenter), distribution into existing B2B accounts, and the "closed-loop / data-sovereignty" compliance requirement that fences out Claude Cowork and ChatGPT Work. **Why now:** (i) sanctions/data-localisation make on-prem "closed contour" deployment with domestic or open models a hard requirement — the explicit wedge MWS AI positions on vs. OpenAI/Anthropic; (ii) the agentic shift from "assistant that answers" to "agent that acts within user access rights" is the 2026 product frontier, and every major Russian vendor shipped an agent-building or agent-coworker product this month.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs (vendors mostly don't disclose): seats/paid users, token volume, number of agents built, measurable productivity lift, and license ACV. Basis of competition = distribution into B2B accounts + model/infra flexibility + security/closed-contour, not price. Key players & recent moves (all Oct 2026 unless noted):
- **Sber / GigaChat** — the direct analogue. [[Sber's GigaCowork platform used by thousands of companies]] (GigaCowork, "thousands of companies" already using, 1000+ agentic initiatives, many in production, per SVP Belevtsev, 2026-10-26); [[Sber unveils free GigaPlatform AI Studio for building AI agents]] (free no-code GigaBlocks agent builder, 2026-10-13); [[Sber adds BPMN generation to Giga Agentic RPA platform]]; GigaChat Enterprise launched Mar 2026.
- **Yandex** — Yandex AI Studio (597bn tokens H1 2026, 30k+ agents); [[RBK Yandex keeps AI plans despite data-center attacks]].
- **MTS / MWS (protagonist)** — MWS AI Cowork is the newest entry; sits alongside [[MTS adds AI assistant to MWS Tables]] and the [[MTS bundles enterprise solutions under MTS CorpTech brand]] B2B umbrella; model is Cotype plus third-party (Sber/Yandex/open) — a notable "we'll run competitors' models too" neutrality play.
- **T-Bank** — named sector peer in own-LLM/agent build-out.
Protagonist's position: **catching up / fast-follower**, not leader. Sber's GigaCowork already claims "thousands of companies" live; MWS AI Cowork is a same-month launch citing only internal MTS Web Services pilots. Moat `(analysis)`: distribution via MTS's existing B2B base + model-agnosticism (can wrap Sber's/Yandex's models rather than only compete on model quality) — thin vs. Sber/Yandex, who own both distribution and frontier models.

**Comps & multiples.** This is a product launch with **no deal/round/valuation** attached, so trading multiples are **not applicable** for MWS AI Cowork itself. Disclosed unit economics (vendor-stated, PR — treat as marketing, not audited): annual license RUB 6m–20m depending on config/seats; claimed saving ~RUB 263k/employee/yr and ≥1 hr/day for a user at 90% task coverage; task-time cuts of 40–50% (10-slide deck), 30–50% (quarterly financial report), 50–80% (meeting minutes), in a 1,000-employee test. **De-PR flag:** the RUB 263k/employee figure × seats can exceed the RUB 6–20m license, which is how the vendor implies ROI — but it is a self-reported pilot estimate, not an independent result. Peer trading multiples: no data (sector dominated by private/divisional units; MWS AI is not separately listed). Distribution not computed — qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Late to a consolidating market / Sber pre-emption.** Sber's GigaCowork is already at "thousands of companies" while MWS AI Cowork launches citing only internal pilots — second-order: in a winner-take-most B2B AI market, the distribution leader compounds data and reference accounts, leaving MWS AI fighting for the long tail.
2. **Dependence on third-party models = margin/moat captured elsewhere.** Running Sber's and Yandex's models inside MWS AI Cowork is a flexibility selling point but hands model-layer value (and a kill-switch) to direct competitors; the differentiator collapses to orchestration + closed-contour hosting, both commoditising.
3. **Unverified ROI / high ticket vs. adoption.** RUB 6–20m/yr license with savings claims sourced only from internal MTS pilots; if real-world lift underperforms the PR, the high ACV becomes an adoption ceiling, especially vs. Sber's free AI Studio funnel.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This confirms 2026 as the year the Russian enterprise-AI race moved from models to **agentic "coworkers,"** with every sovereign stack (Sber, Yandex, MTS, T-Bank) shipping one quarter-simultaneously — a crowding that points to consolidation and price/feature pressure, not a new high-margin category for any single entrant. Falsifiable thesis: MWS AI Cowork stays a niche/long-tail product unless MTS discloses external paying-customer counts comparable to Sber's "thousands." Trigger to watch: MWS AI naming named external enterprise logos / seat counts, or MTS breaking out MWS/AI revenue in a quarterly release — absent that, treat traction claims as PR.
