[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🌏 Bitpanda opens account access to AI investing assistants. Its MCP integration enables customers to connect tools such as ChatGPT and Claude to view portfolio information and initiate trades across supported assets, subject to explicit user instructions and platform permissions.
