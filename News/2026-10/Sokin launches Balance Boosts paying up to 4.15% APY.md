Questions and Answers 
How do I set up Balance Boost? 
From your Sokin dashboard, select Balance Boost, choose your currency and Boost type, enter your amount and confirm. Fixed term Boosts require selecting a term length. Once submitted, an account approver can review and activate.
What's the difference between easy access and fixed term?
Easy access Boost earns a variable daily rate; funds can be withdrawn at any time. Fixed term Boost earns a fixed rate for a set period (60, 90, 180 or 360 days) with the rate confirmed upfront. You can run both at the same time.
‍
When can I access my money? 
Easy access funds are available to withdraw at any time. Fixed term funds are locked until maturity, when your principal and interest are returned. Enable autorenewal to roll straight into a new term.
Are there any fees or limits? 
No fees. No maximum deposit. A minimum of approximately $1,000 (or currency equivalent) applies per Boost. ‍ Need a little more help? Visit our support centre for more information.
