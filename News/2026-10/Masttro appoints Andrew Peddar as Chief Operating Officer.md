Masttro has appointed Andrew Peddar as Chief Operating Officer to lead its global data operations and client experience teams. For fintech professionals, this move signals a scaling phase for a wealth tech firm reporting 43% ARR growth, as it seeks to professionalize its operational backbone to support AI-driven cross-border wealth management.

Andrew Peddar joins Masttro as Chief Operating Officer, a role that places him in charge of the company’s Data Operations and Client Experience teams. Based at the firm’s New York City headquarters, Peddar reports directly to CEO Jay McNamara. His remit focuses on unifying global operations, onboarding, and client service functions to maintain data reliability and seamless delivery as the platform scales.

Peddar’s career spans over 25 years in financial technology and enterprise software. He previously served as Group Chief Operating Officer at StatPro, where he managed commercial and delivery functions across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. He later held the position of Vice President and General Manager at Broadridge Financial Solutions, overseeing a global business serving major financial institutions. Most recently, he was Chief Revenue Officer at Next Matter, an AI and data orchestration platform.

The appointment follows a period of significant growth for Masttro, which reports a 43% increase in annual recurring revenue over the last 18 months. Since the beginning of 2025, the company has expanded its workforce by 20%, with staff operating across seven countries and serving clients in more than 40 markets. The firm’s technical infrastructure currently supports more than 750 direct custodial connections and tracks over 300,000 alternative investments. In the first nine months of 2026, Masttro’s AI-driven tools saw high engagement, with the Intelligence Assistant processing over 32,000 client requests. Additionally, its Documents AI and Alternatives AI products now support workflows across more than 10,000 funds. The company also secured ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification in September 2026.

Masttro provides a global operating system designed for the management of complex wealth. The platform is built to consolidate financial data across various institutions, entities, and asset classes, specifically targeting the needs of ultra-high-net-worth individuals and family offices. By integrating direct custodial connections with alternative investment tracking, the firm aims to automate the reporting and analysis of diversified portfolios that often include non-traditional assets.

Masttro has positioned itself as a specialist in the "complex wealth" niche, where traditional retail or institutional tools often struggle with the lack of transparency in private equity, hedge funds, and other alternative holdings. Its market strategy relies heavily on the integration of artificial intelligence to handle document processing and data orchestration. This focus on security and operational standards was recently formalized through the achievement of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, reflecting its role as a critical infrastructure provider for global wealth owners and their advisors.

The appointment of a COO with deep enterprise experience suggests that the wealth tech sector is moving beyond the initial "visibility" phase into a period of industrial-scale operational maturity. As ultra-high-net-worth portfolios become increasingly weighted toward complex alternative assets, the pressure on legacy reporting providers to match the speed of AI-driven platforms is intensifying. The industry now faces a critical question: can automated data orchestration fully replace the manual reconciliation processes that have long defined family office operations? This move indicates that for platforms to win, they must pair advanced AI tools with the institutional-grade operational stability typically associated with much larger financial incumbents.

Original Press Release

Masttro, the global operating system for complex wealth, today announced the appointment of Andrew Peddar as Chief Operating Officer. Peddar will oversee the company’s Data Operations and Client Experience teams, strengthening the operational support behind its platform and reinforcing Masttro's capacity to deliver AI-powered wealth solutions to a growing, cross-border client base.

Peddar brings more than 25 years of experience in financial technology and enterprise software, with expertise in investment data, analytics and reporting. He will report to CEO Jay McNamara and work closely with the team at Masttro’s New York City headquarters. The appointment comes as Masttro reports 43% growth in annual recurring revenue over the past 18 months. Since the start of 2025, Masttro has grown its workforce by 20%, with employees now based in seven countries, serving clients in over 40 markets globally.

“As Masttro grows, it is critical that we ensure the quality of our data and the support our clients receive grows with it,” said McNamara. “It is also essential that our tools and support keep pace with our growth. Andrew has spent his career connecting technology, operations and client service in complex financial software businesses. He will help us scale those functions together, so clients can rely on Masttro as their portfolios and reporting needs evolve.”

Peddar previously served as Group Chief Operating Officer of StatPro, where he led commercial, client-facing and delivery functions across North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific. He later joined Broadridge Financial Solutions as Vice President and General Manager, overseeing a global financial technology business serving major financial institutions. Most recently, he was Chief Revenue Officer at Next Matter, an AI and data orchestration platform serving asset and wealth managers.

**Strengthening data operations and client support**

Peddar will oversee Masttro’s global operations, onboarding and client experience functions, bringing these teams together to deliver a seamless and consistent experience across the client lifecycle. His focus will be on strengthening collaboration, improving operational efficiency and ensuring that service quality, data reliability and client outcomes remain central as the business scales.

Central to this approach is Masttro’s commitment to working closely with clients as long-term partners, understanding their evolving needs and incorporating their feedback into ongoing platform innovation. This collaboration helps ensure Masttro continues to deliver solutions that create lasting value as both its clients and the business grow.

“For clients managing wealth across institutions, entities and asset classes, reliable data and responsive support are essential,” said Peddar. “My focus will be on building upon Masttro’s strong foundation, further strengthening how our teams work together to deliver the connectivity, reporting and support clients depend on every day.”

**Growing global connectivity and AI adoption**

Masttro continues to expand its global data network and AI capabilities, with more than 750 direct custodial connections worldwide and over 300,000 alternative investments tracked on its platform. This extensive network, combined with its integrated AI tools, enables clients to consolidate complex financial data, automate time-consuming processes and gain deeper insights across their holdings. During the first nine months of 2026, clients submitted more than 32,000 requests to the Masttro Intelligence Assistant, while Documents AI and Alternatives AI support document and alternative investment workflows across more than 10,000 funds. Masttro also achieved ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification in September 2026, reinforcing its commitment to data security as its global operations expand.
