# Context-enrichment: Mynt's GCash edges higher after record-breaking Philippine IPO
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 3/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On **2026-10-20** Mynt Inc. (operator of GCash) **began trading on the PSE Main Board** (ticker "GCASH") after its IPO — the listing-day DEBUT, a distinct event from the **2026-10-02 pricing** ([[Mynt sets GCash IPO price at P6.60 per share]]). President Marcos led the bell-ringing. De-PR'd facts:
- Shares **opened at P6.63 (+0.5% vs the P6.60 offer)**, touched an intraday high of **~P6.76 (+2.4%)**, then **gave back the gains and closed effectively flat/unchanged at ~P6.60** amid selling pressure and a broader Asian sell-off (Reuters/BusinessWorld/BusinessMirror). (One outlet, astig.ph, reports a P6.74 close — a minority figure; the Reuters/BusinessMirror consensus is **flat/unchanged**.)
- With the **overallotment fully exercised**, the deal totalled **9.23B shares at P6.60 = ~P60.9B (~$966M)** — confirming it as the **largest IPO in Philippine history**, surpassing Monde Nissin's P55.89B (2021). At base size it would have been #2, so the record is **real only because the greenshoe was fully taken up**.
- **~P50.3B secondary / only ~P10.6B primary** — i.e. **~83% cash-out** to existing holders (Ant, Warburg, Bow Wave, Globe, Ayala, execs); fresh capital to Mynt is small. Implied market cap **~P441.5B (~$7B)**; Mynt debuts as the **7th-largest PSE company**.
- **165% local oversubscription** (orders ~2.65B vs ~1.6B allocated to local trading participants).

**+ Why this framing matters:** the headline "edges higher" captures only the **opening/intraday tick**; the decisive signal — the full-session print — was a **flat/unchanged close**, i.e. the stock did NOT sustain a premium on day one. For a "record-breaking" listing that is a **muted, de-risked debut**, not a pop. The down-priced P6.60 (a ~34% cut from the P10 ceiling) did its job: it cleared, held the line, but left no first-day upside — the market neither punished nor rewarded. The real story is confirmation (the record is now booked, the greenshoe was exercised) plus a cautious aftermarket read.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Maya (PLDT/Voyager/KKR)** — direct domestic #2 (~50M registered vs GCash ~94M; GCash leads ~2:1). Bank-licensed, profitable since 2025, own IPO reportedly slipping to 2027. GCash's flat debut is the near-term benchmark Maya's bankers will now price against.
- **SE Asia / EM fintech IPO track record — the real comp:** GoTo (Indonesia) **~-90%** from 2022 IPO (hit a record low after IDX removed the price floor, Oct 2026); Grab **~-65/70%** vs debut; Paytm (India) **~-80%**. The lone winner is **Nubank (~+74%)** — profitable, huge domestic market. GCash debuting FLAT (not down) is, against that cohort, a modest success — it avoided an immediate GoTo-style crack.

**+ Why the lay of the land is this way (2nd order):** thin-float Asian super-apps that listed into shallow EM exchanges cratered; the profitable large-domestic name (Nubank) rewarded. GCash sits in between — **genuinely profitable** but listing into the **thinnest** of these exchanges with a ~12-15% float. A flat debit means the GoTo/Grab "thin-float discount" was already fully priced into the P6.60; the aftermarket is now a liquidity/float-fragility test, not a valuation test.

## [2] Company history / fit
Mynt: founded 2015 (Globe + Ayala + Ant). Valuation ladder: ~$2B (2021) → **$5B (2024, MUFG/Ayala)** → **~$7B IPO (2026)**. Process: considered 2025 → delayed to H2 2026 ([[GCash delays $1.5B Philippine IPO to second half of 2026]]) → filing authorized June 2026 ([[Ant-backed GCash operator Mynt prepares Philippine IPO filing]]) → PSE approval 2026-09-19 → final pricing 2026-10-02 ([[Mynt sets GCash IPO price at P6.60 per share]]) → **listing 2026-10-20 (this note)**. GCash profitable: FY2025 revenue ~P79.7B (+47% YoY), net income ~P17.2B (+55%); H1-2026 decelerated hard to ~+10%/+7% as the 2025 gambling-link removal washed out of the base.

**+ Why the company acts this way:** Mynt waited out 2025 and lobbied for the SEC float cut precisely to list at a premium with minimal dilution; the ~83%-secondary, thin-float structure is engineered to **let early backers realize gains**. The flat debut is the capstone: the exit is executed, the record booked — but the modest price action shows the market extracted the premium down to P6.60 before giving the sellers their liquidity.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Freshness:** This note is the **DEBUT / first-day-trading** event — a **genuinely distinct event from the 2026-10-02 PRICING note**. It carries NEW, citable hard facts absent from the pricing note: actual listing date (Oct 20), opening/intraday/closing price action (P6.63 open, ~P6.76 high, flat close), **confirmation the overallotment was FULLY exercised → P60.9B / 9.23B shares** (the pricing note only said "could reach" P60.93B *if* exercised), confirmed largest-ever status, 7th-largest PSE company, 165% local oversubscription, Marcos bell-ringing. **FRESH, not a duplicate** of the pricing note.

**+ Why the value-add (of the event) is real but limited:** the debut confirms the record and resolves the conditional ("could be largest" → "is largest, greenshoe fully taken"), and the flat close de-risks the "next GoTo" fear for now. But in pure news terms a **flat debut is close to a non-event** — no price discovery surprise, no pop, no crack. The durable equity story still rests on existing profitability (the raise funded exits, not a war chest), so the listing changed Mynt's ownership/liquidity, not its fundamentals.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Next watch-items: (1) **aftermarket liquidity / float fragility** — a ~12-15% float into a thin PSE (turnover ~$100-120M/day) makes the stock prone to volatility and manipulation, the GoTo risk that is still live post-debut; (2) **lock-up expiry overhang** — with ~83% of the deal secondary, insider selling pressure after lock-up is the key downside trigger; (3) **H2-2026 earnings re-acceleration** — Morgan Stanley pencils ~P21.2B 2026 NI (+~23%), which requires CreditTech (lending) to scale cleanly after a soft H1; (4) **read-through to Maya and other EM-fintech listings** — a flat-but-stable debut keeps the regional IPO window ajar rather than slamming it (a GoTo-style crack would have frozen it).

**+ Why the market may go this way (2nd order):** the flat debut is the "no news is good news" outcome for a de-risked, down-priced float — it validates the P6.60 as a clearing price. But the same thin float that protected insiders from dilution makes the aftermarket **fragile, not safe**: concentrated holdings + low free float mean the stock can gap on small order flow, so the debut's calm says little about where it trades once lock-ups roll off and daily liquidity is tested.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Philippine digital-wallet market ~$16.9bn in 2026, up from ~$13.7bn in 2025, projected to ~$62.7bn by 2034 (~17.8% CAGR) — per IMARC/openPR market report, as of 2026 (treat secondary analyst sizing as directional, not audited). Structure is a sharp **duopoly tilting to near-monopoly**: GCash ~89% domestic wallet share vs Maya ~5% (per digitalinasia/riverpe, 2026) — one of the most consolidated wallet markets in Asia. Barriers are network effects (merchant + agent two-sided network, ~150K cash-in/out agents) and BSP e-money licensing. "Why now": the banked rate is only ~56% while digital payments already clear ~57% of retail transaction volume — GCash monetizes the gap between a large unbanked base and a cashless-policy push (BSP digitalization targets), the structural reason a wallet can scale to >40M MAU before banks reach the same base. Second-order effect: inclusion economics (airtime, remittance, bills) convert into a lending/savings cross-sell flywheel.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: GTV (gross transaction value), MAU, take rate, and for fintech-expansion ARPU / active borrowers. GCash FY2025: **₱17.03tn payments GTV** (₱4.75tn in Q1-2026, +23.2% YoY), **40.4M MAU** (~55% of adult population, ~4x nearest rival) and ~94–100M registered users (per prospectus coverage / webgeek, 2026) — the 100M figure is *registered*, not active, so lean on the 40.4M MAU. Financial-services layer: 7.5M active borrowers, 16.9M savings users, 9M fund users, 1.9M equities users. Key players: **Maya** (PLDT/Voyager-backed, ~50M users, ~$3–3.5bn est. private valuation, distant #2 at ~5% share) and **Alipay+/Ant** (GCash shareholder + regional rails). Basis of competition: distribution/network, not price. GCash position = **dominant incumbent, extending moat**; notable structural gap — Maya holds a BSP *digital-bank* license (BSP-insured deposits) while GCash as of 2026 operates mainly as e-money, a regulatory asymmetry worth watching. Moat: two-sided network + agent scale + incumbency `(analysis)`.

**Comps & multiples.** IPO priced ₱6.60/share → market cap **~₱442bn (~$7bn)**; base offer ~₱53bn, up to ~₱60.9bn (~$970m) with greenshoe — the Philippines' largest-ever IPO (prior record ~$1bn Monde Nissin 2021). Arithmetic on FY2025 (adjusted rev ₱79.7bn; net income ₱17.2bn):
- **P/S = ₱442bn / ₱79.7bn ≈ 5.5x** (in-line-to-rich for a wallet; justified only if ~40%+ revenue growth holds — FY24→FY25 rev grew ~47%, ₱54.1bn→₱79.7bn, so growth supports it).
- **P/E = ₱442bn / ₱17.2bn ≈ 25.7x** — unusually sane for a scaled EM wallet because GCash is *actually profitable* (net income ₱6.4bn→₱11.1bn→₱17.2bn FY23–25), unlike most loss-making peers.
- **Maya** (private, ~$3–3.5bn round valuation — mark as round, not market cap): no public rev → EV/Rev "no data"; GCash is ~2x Maya's valuation on far larger scale.
Internal comps: [[GCash operator Mynt eyes $8 billion valuation in Philippine IPO]] (May 2026, $8bn target — priced ~13% below that at ~$7bn), [[GCash delays $1.5B Philippine IPO to second half of 2026]] (Oct 2025), [[GCash valuation soars to $5 billion]]-era (2024, ~$5bn → ~40% re-rate into IPO). Distribution not computed — only 1 truly comparable scaled, profitable EM wallet in-base; qualitative comparison. Peer EM-fintech P/S band ~3–8x; 5.5x sits mid-band, supported by growth + profitability, so **not** an outlier flag on its own.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Regulatory asymmetry / license gap** — GCash lacks a BSP digital-bank license that Maya holds; deposit-insured savings and balance-sheet lending economics could accrue to Maya and banks, capping GCash's move up the stack `(analysis)`.
2. **Single-market concentration** — ~100% exposure to the Philippines; GTV/revenue track one macro, one regulator (BSP), one FX. Any domestic consumption or remittance slowdown hits the whole book with no geographic hedge.
3. **Valuation-vs-growth dependence** — 5.5x P/S / 25.7x P/E only hold if ~20%+ GTV growth (Q1-26 +23.2%) persists; a deceleration re-rates the stock fast, and the IPO already priced ~13% below the earlier $8bn target, hinting demand sensitivity despite 20+ cornerstones (BlackRock, T. Rowe Price).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is the first large *profitable* EM super-app wallet to IPO on a home exchange — a live public read on whether inclusion-led wallet economics command bank-like multiples `(analysis)`. Falsifiable thesis: if GCash sustains >20% GTV growth AND secures a digital-bank license within ~18 months, 25x P/E looks cheap and it re-rates toward a bank-plus-growth multiple. What breaks it: GTV growth slips below ~15% or BSP keeps the deposit moat with Maya/banks — then 5.5x P/S compresses toward the low end of the band.

## Earnings Review

<!-- enrichment:earnings_review -->
**Verdict (IPO "reported" read — not an earnings print).** Record Philippine IPO raised ₱60.9bn ($0.97bn) at ₱6.60/share → market cap ₱441.5bn (~$7.0bn), 7th-largest listed PH company. Priced on prospectus financials that are genuinely strong at scale — FY2025 adjusted revenue ₱79.7bn (+47.4% YoY) and net income ₱17.2bn (+55% YoY) — but H1 2026 growth has sharply decelerated (revenue +10.4% YoY; net income +7.3% YoY) under regulatory pressure. De-PR flag: "record IPO" is 83% secondary stock (early backers cashing out), only ₱10.6bn fresh capital; stock closed flat at the ₱6.60 offer on debut.

**Key figures (prospectus / H1 2026, PHP).**
- FY2025 adjusted revenue **₱79.7bn, +47.4% YoY** (FY2024 ₱54.1bn).
- FY2025 net income **₱17.2bn, +55% YoY** (FY2024 ₱11.1bn).
- H1 2026 (to Jun 30): revenue **₱43.27bn, +10.4% YoY**; net income **₱10.82bn, +7.3% YoY** — margin healthy (~25% net) but growth roughly a quarter of the prior-year pace.
- GTV: **₱17.03tn** payments GTV in FY2025; **₱4.75tn in Q1 2026, +23.2% YoY** — volume still compounding faster than revenue, i.e. take-rate compression (payment take-rate −6bps after the Aug 2025 gambling-link removal).
- Users: **41.5M monthly active** (Q2 2026, ~56% of PH adults) on a ~94M registered base. Profitability is real and established (not a loss-making neobank).

**By segment / driver.** Payment Solutions is the cash engine but the pressured line: H1 2026 payments revenue **₱24.9bn, −3.7% YoY** after BSP ordered e-wallets to delink from online gambling (Aug 2025). The GLife/online-gaming flow collapse drove GCash's Globe-booked equity-income contribution down to ~₱800M in a recent quarter (−47% QoQ). Growth is being re-based onto **CreditTech** — 60% of net primary proceeds earmarked for lending expansion (incl. a stated push into informal "5-6" lending), i.e. shifting the thesis from payments take-rate to consumer-credit risk.

**vs expectations / prior period.** No sell-side consensus (IPO debut) — assess vs prior disclosures and peer. Valuation: $7.0bn cap vs the **$5bn** mark at the Aug-2024 round and a **$7bn** target set at the Jun-2026 filing — IPO landed at the top of the pre-deal range despite decelerating H1 growth [UNSOURCED on an implied P/E multiple]. Record size beats Monde Nissin's ₱55.89bn (2021). Peer **Maya** (Ascend/Voyager-adjacent competitor): FY2025 profit only **₱1.7bn** (first full-year profit) on ~10.7M bank customers and ₱68bn deposits — Mynt's ~9-month profit (~₱15bn) is ~10x Maya's, confirming GCash's scale/profit lead (89% wallet share vs Maya ~5%), though Maya has more lending/deposit depth per user.

**Guidance / forward.** No formal guidance (IPO). Forward read (analysis): the +47% FY2025 top line is NOT the run-rate — H1 2026's +10.4% is the live trajectory as the gambling-revenue base washes out (annualizes through H2 2026). CreditTech must carry growth, which trades payments take-rate risk for credit/provisioning risk in an untested informal-lending segment. Management tone in the prospectus leans on the ₱17tn GTV and 56%-of-adults reach headline while the payments-revenue decline and take-rate compression are the quiet lines.

**Thesis-flags.**
1. **Regulatory re-basing of the core.** BSP gambling delink → payments revenue −3.7% YoY (H1 2026) and take-rate −6bps. Why it matters: the pre-IPO +47% growth story is partly a one-off (gaming) that regulators have removed → second-order: FY2026 headline growth likely lands mid-single to low-double digits, pressuring the ~$7bn (top-of-range) valuation.
2. **Deceleration vs valuation.** Priced at the $7bn ceiling while net-income growth fell from +55% to +7.3% YoY — thin margin for error on the multiple.
3. **CreditTech pivot = new risk vector.** 60% of fresh proceeds to lending (incl. "5-6" informal credit) → unproven loss curve; converts a capital-light payments model toward balance-sheet/credit risk.
4. **Float / lock-up overhang.** Only ~12% public float (reduced from 15% under the >₱200bn-issuer rule); ₱50.3bn of the ₱60.9bn was secondary (early investors selling), and anchors Globe/Ant are not selling but are lock-up-restricted → limited true free float plus a post-lock-up supply overhang; flat debut shows demand already met near the offer price.
