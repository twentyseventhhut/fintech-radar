# UnionPay to issue cards in Argentina with PVS

> [!info] 2026-10-07 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: This Week in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[This Week in Fintech] 🇦🇷UnionPay, the Chinese card network with more than 2 billion cards issued, will issue cards in Argentina with Pagos Virtuales del Sur (PVS), the local fintech that also partners with Alipay. The move takes UnionPay from serving Chinese tourists to courting Argentine users, with cards that link to Alipay, WeChat, and Huawei wallets.
