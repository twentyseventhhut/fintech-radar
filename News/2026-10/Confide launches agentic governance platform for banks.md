# Confide launches agentic governance platform for banks

> [!info] 2026-10-07 · 2 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🌏 Confide launches an agentic governance platform for financial institutions, designed to help organisations manage AI agents and automate governance, risk and compliance processes. The platform provides continuous oversight and controls for AI-driven operations, helping financial institutions govern agentic systems as they scale.

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] I also couldn’t let today’s edition go by without mentioning Confide. Founded and led by CEO Pav Gill, the company just launched an agentic governance platform that brings GRC, risk and compliance into one place and, importantly, is built to govern AI agents as well as people. As more agents start making decisions inside businesses, I think this is a problem we’re going to hear a lot more about.
