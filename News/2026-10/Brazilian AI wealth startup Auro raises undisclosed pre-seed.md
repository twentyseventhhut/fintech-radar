### [0] What happened (one line)
Auro, a São Paulo-based wealthtech using AI to bring wealth management to Brazilians still *building* savings (rather than those who already hold them), raised an **undisclosed pre-seed** round from **Link Capital**, the early-stage fund of Link School of Business. (Source: [startups.com.br](https://startups.com.br/negocios/startup-quer-democratizar-o-wealth-management-com-ia/), [Radar Startups](https://radarstartups.com.br/materia/auro-capta-pre-seed-com-link-capital-para-levar-gestao/))

### [1] Who / what / numbers (dated, sourced)
- **Company:** Auro — founded **Feb 2026** by **Pedro Avena** (Link School of Business grad) and **Yuri Miyazaki** (ex-FEA-USP). Category it coins: "wealth building" (serving clients *before* they hit the R$500k+ minimums traditional private banks require).
- **Round:** Undisclosed pre-seed — the firm's *first* external investment.
- **Investor:** **Link Capital**, the early-stage venture fund of Link School of Business. (NB: distinct from the Chilean private-debt manager "Link Capital Partners" — see caveat in [4].)
- **Traction (as of Oct 2026 reporting):** **46 clients** on a **fixed R$79/month** subscription. Uses AI agents/automations to cut operational cost of a traditional wealth-management structure, blended with human support.
- **Date of coverage:** 2026-10-14 (This Week in Fintech; startups.com.br).

### [2] Why it matters / so-what
- Targets the *underserved* bottom of the Brazilian wealth pyramid — only ~a third of Brazilians hold any investment, and the "wealth building" framing deliberately contrasts with incumbent private banking's high minimums. AI-for-cost-reduction is the thesis that lets a ~R$79/mo price point be viable.
- Tiny, early experiment (46 clients, undisclosed/likely small check) — signal value, not scale value.

### [3] Prior art / internal links
- [[Link Capital Partners raises second private debt fund]] — CAVEAT: this 2025-08 note is a **Chilean private-debt fund (Cumplo I)**, a *different* "Link Capital" entity; do not conflate with Auro's backer.
- LatAm wealthtech / Brazil funding comparables in the vault: [[Brazil's Bull raises $1.8M pre-seed for worker credit]], [[Zippi raises ~$15.9M via securitized structure in Brazil]], [[Argentina crypto fintech Lemon raises $20M Series B round]].

### [4] External landscape / comparables (dated)
- **Decade** — the headline Brazilian AI-wealth story of 2026: ex-Nubank CTO **Vitor Olivier** + **Felipe Meneses** (Hyperplane co-founder, acq. by Nubank 2024) raised **$85M seed** (LatAm's largest ever seed), backed by **Greenoaks, Benchmark, Diffusion**; closed Sept 2025, announced Aug 2026; R$200/mo; AI model + senior human advisor via WhatsApp/video. (Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-04/ex-nubank-executives-launch-ai-wealth-adviser-in-brazil), [Businesswire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260804082552/en/Decade-Raises-$85M-in-Latin-Americas-Largest-Seed-Round-to-Create-a-New-Generation-of-Millionaires-with-AI), [fintechfutures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/fintech-start-ups/ex-nubank-execs-launch-ai-driven-wealth-manager-decade-with-85m)) — Auro is a far smaller, cheaper (R$79 vs R$200), earlier echo of the same thesis.
- Incumbent digital wealth peers in Brazil: Warren, Magnetis, Nubank's wealth push. Macro: ~7% of Brazilians can fund retirement from savings → large structural TAM for low-cost advice.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Auro sits in Brazilian/LatAm AI wealthtech — specifically "wealth building" for mass-market savers below the private-banking threshold, the segment human advisors ignore. Market size: global wealthtech solutions ~$6.19bn (2025) → ~$7.08bn (2026), ~14.4% CAGR (per Research and Markets, via web, as of 2026). Brazil-specific wealth-management-software revenue projected ~$408.9M by 2033 at ~16% CAGR 2026–2033 (per Grand View Research, via web). Structure: fragmented at the low end — incumbent Brazilian digital players (Warren, founded 2017; XP; BTG) chase the affluent/active-investor tiers, leaving first-savings customers underserved; Magnetis, an early Brazilian robo-advisor, is now out of business (per web), signalling the pure-robo model struggled on unit economics. Why now: cheap AI agents let a startup absorb "heavy operational routines" (per founders, via startups.com.br) so a human-plus-AI advice model can profitably serve a R$79/mo ticket that was previously uneconomic; Open Finance integrations (in development) lower data-acquisition cost. Driver = cost collapse of advice delivery; headwind = thin per-user economics at low tickets.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: subscription ARPU, clients/subscribers, CAC payback, AUM/AUA, retention. Auro disclosed: 46 clients, R$79/mo flat subscription, founded Feb 2026 by Pedro Avena (Link School) and Yuri Miyazaki (FEA-USP) (per web). Players & basis of competition: incumbents Warren/XP/BTG compete on product breadth + brand; new AI-native entrants compete on price + conversational UX. Recent peer moves: Astor (YC-backed, Brazil-relevant) raised $5M seed led by Monashees, $15/mo AI-advice subscription (per Fortune, Apr 2026); Quartz (UK, Revolut/N26 alumni) raised £2.75m pre-seed for an AI wealth assistant (per fintech.global, Sep 2026). Auro's position: niche early-stage challenger, pre-product-market-fit (46 clients) — catching up, not ahead. Moat `(analysis)`: none proven yet; potential switching costs via Open Finance data lock-in + human-advice relationship, but intangible and unbuilt at this stage.

**Comps & multiples.** Round economics only — no revenue/valuation disclosed, so no trading multiples. Peer pre-seed/seed context for the AI-wealth-advice subvertical:
- Astor — $5M seed, $15/mo subscription, YC + Monashees (closest comp: AI advice, LatAm-adjacent, subscription).
- Quartz — £2.75m pre-seed, UK, AI wealth assistant.
- Auro — undisclosed pre-seed, R$79/mo (~$14/mo), Link Capital (Link School's early-stage fund).
Ticket (R$79 ≈ $14/mo) is in line with Astor's $15/mo and the $20–$50/mo mass-affluent band cited (per web) — not an outlier. Valuation/round size: **no data** (undisclosed). EV/Revenue, P/S: **no data** — pre-revenue-scale, no figures. Internal comps from base (round/valuation reference points for LatAm wealth/AI-advice): [[Chile's LAKPA gets SEC approval as US registered adviser]], [[Wallbit expands in LatAm with wealth management focus]], [[Y Combinator-backed Saturn raises $15M for advice AI]], [[Focal raises $5 million for AI advisor productivity]]. Distribution not computed — fewer than 3 comparable disclosed valuations; qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. Sub-scale, unproven unit economics — 46 clients at ~$14/mo is ~$7.7k MRR; the segment's defining hazard is that low tickets don't cover CAC + human-advice cost. Magnetis's failure (per web) is the cautionary precedent — second-order: pre-seed capital may fund growth that is structurally unprofitable unless AI genuinely removes the human cost.
2. Single-investor / small round concentration — backed solely by Link Capital (the founders' own school's fund), undisclosed size; limited external validation and a shallow runway raise financing-continuity risk into the next round.
3. Open Finance + advice-regulation dependence — the model leans on Open Finance integrations (not yet live) and on providing financial advice at scale, which in Brazil can attract CVM/suitability scrutiny; second-order: compliance cost could erode the thin-ticket economics further.

**What this changes (idea-lens) `(analysis)`.** A new low-ticket entry probing whether AI finally makes mass-market advice profitable where pure robo-advisors (Magnetis) failed — a format test, not a re-rating. Watch: does ARPU-×-retention beat CAC as clients scale past a few hundred, and does a priced follow-on (seed) with an external lead materialize? Falsifiable thesis: if Auro cannot cross ~1,000 retained subscribers before needing its next round, the human-plus-AI low-ticket model is not yet economic in Brazil. Trigger to revisit: a disclosed seed valuation or a client-count update.
