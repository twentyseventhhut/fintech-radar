# Mexico's Senate approves Digital Economy Law for digital payments

> [!info] 2026-10-20 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[This Week in Fintech] 🇲🇽 Mexico’s Senate approved the Digital Economy Law for Digital and Electronic Payments. The law lets the Finance Ministry name sectors where only digital payments are accepted and makes the digital CURP valid ID for opening financial services.
