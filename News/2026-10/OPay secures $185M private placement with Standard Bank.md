# OPay secures $185M private placement with Standard Bank

> [!info] 2026-10-09 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🌐 SoftBank-backed OPay secures a proposed $185 million private placement with Standard Bank. The investment would give Standard Bank a stake of up to 4.99% in OPay, subject to its planned US IPO and regulatory approvals. The companies also plan to explore merchant acquiring, digital banking and other financial services across Africa.
