Noah Closes $38m Seed Funding Round as Major Enterprises Move Cross-Border Payments onto Stablecoin

YTD 2026 revenues jump 538% compared to 2025, with 31% month on month growth.
LONDON, Oct. 07, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Noah , the stablecoin payments infrastructure provider, has closed its seed funding round at $38 million following the raise of an additional $16 million from existing and new investors. The raise follows strong growth that has seen revenues surge by 538% during year-to-date 2026 compared to the same period 2025, with recurring monthly growth of 31%. Noah has also signed over 150 new customers during 2026 including major enterprises such as they transition to stablecoin transfers.
The new funding will enable Noah to expand and accelerate its international money transfer services that enable the frictionless movement of funds across borders in seconds. Specific application of the funds will expand Noah’s regulatory footprint, recruit engineering and compliance specialists, and deepen its connections with local payment rails in its highest-volume markets. The business will also now be able to accelerate its US expansion through opening an office in New York.
 “Stablecoins combine the flexibility of digital currencies with the stability of fiat currencies to allow money to move at the speed of the internet,”  said Shah Ramezani, Co-Founder and CEO of Noah.  “Noah is building the infrastructure that will make one-click international transactions possible everywhere, with full compliance at both the origination and destination of funds. Just as neobanks have revolutionised domestic banking, we want to redefine global money transfer markets.” 
The new investment round has been made by Endeit Capital, FJ Labs, LocalGlobe, Felix Capital and a select group of high-profile angels. The capital raise follows strong sector growth for stablecoin as well as revenue growth for Noah as customers replace legacy correspondent banking with faster and cheaper stablecoin transfers.
Cross-border payments reached $208 trillion in 2025, generating an estimated $625 billion in annual revenue for banks and intermediaries. While Small and Medium Business (SMB) payments account for just 7% of this total flow, they generate 31% of banks' revenue, costing businesses $194 billion a year in fees, foreign exchange spreads and operational charges.
Stablecoin adoption within the SMB market is rapidly accelerating. B2B stablecoin payments have reached an annualised value of $226 billion, representing an increase of 733% year-over-year. Stablecoins today account for less than 1% of the $34.8 trillion in annual cross-border B2B payments, highlighting the opportunity for future adoption. 
Noah is live in more than 150 markets and supports over 60 currencies, where it enables seamless cross-border payments to replace legacy correspondent bank chains where multiple steps add delays and fees. Noah provides its services both directly to enterprises and individuals, and also indirectly through partnerships with consumer platforms, fintech companies, neobanks, and workforce platforms that aggregate large numbers of businesses, contractors, and individual users.
 About Noah 
Noah is a stablecoin payments infrastructure provider whose mission is to set money free. It is building the infrastructure that will make one-click international transactions possible everywhere, with full compliance at both the origination and destination of funds. Noah’s technology connects stablecoin settlement with local payment rails, enabling businesses to move money internationally faster and more efficiently without relying on traditional correspondent banking. Noah’s network supports more than 150 countries in excess of 60 different currencies.

# Context-enrichment: Noah closes $38M seed after extra $16M raise
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 3/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On 2026-10-07 Noah — a London-based stablecoin cross-border payments infrastructure provider (founded 2020 by CEO Shah Ramezani, ex-The Hut Group/Kingsway/Lazard/UBS, and President Thijn Lamers, Adyen founding-team/ex-EVP Global Sales) — announced it "closed" its seed round at **$38M total** after raising an **extra $16M**. This is a **seed EXTENSION**, not a fresh priced round: the $38M = the **$22M seed from June 2025** (see [[Noah raises $22M to build global stablecoin payments network]]) **plus the new $16M**. Axios and FinTech Futures both label it a "seed extension."
- New money: **Endeit Capital** (the one genuinely new name) + existing investors **FJ Labs, LocalGlobe, Felix Capital** + "a select group of high-profile angels" (June 2025 angels included Joe Lonsdale, David Helgason, Alexander Matthey (ex-Adyen CTO), Tom Stafford (DST Global)).
- **Not disclosed:** post-money valuation, the equity/structure of the $16M, the absolute revenue base, customer concentration, and whether "150+ customers" are live & paying or signed.
- Growth claims: **YTD-2026 revenue +538% vs 2025**, **+31% MoM**, **150+ new customers in 2026**. Live in **150+ markets / 60+ currencies**.
- Use of funds: expand regulatory footprint, hire engineering/compliance, deepen local-rail connections, **open a New York office (US expansion)**.
- **Why framed this way:** anchoring to the **$38M "closed" total** (rather than headlining "$16M extension") makes an incremental top-up read as a flagship round. A +538% number off a presumably **tiny 2025 base** is PR-flattering — triple-digit growth is trivial from a low denominator; no dollar figures are given to size it. The "150 markets / 60 currencies" is **network reach, not volume/GMV**. The extension (vs a priced Series A) plausibly signals the company wanted runway on existing terms rather than testing a fresh valuation in a crowded market — or simply opportunistic top-up on momentum.

## [1] Competitors / peers
Dense, well-capitalised B2B stablecoin-rails field:
- **Bridge** (acquired by Stripe for ~$1.1B, 2025) — the category's exit benchmark; now a Stripe-backed incumbent.
- **BVNK** — enterprise-grade virtual accounts, SLAs, compliance tooling; raised at ~$750M valuation (2025), Visa-backed.
- **Conduit** — emerging-market payout corridors (LATAM/APAC/Africa), Noah's closest direct overlap.
- **Circle / Paxos** — issuers Noah partners with (not pure competitors); **Circle** also pushes its own orchestration/CPN rails, a disintermediation risk.
- Payout/ramp peers: Zerohash, Fireblocks (custody/infra), MoneyGram ramps, Yellow Card (Africa).
**Position:** catching-up / mid-pack by capital. $38M total is small next to Bridge (Stripe) and BVNK (~$750M). Noah's differentiation is **emerging-market corridor density + a single fiat↔stablecoin↔local-rail + compliance layer**, and now **MiCA licensing**. **Why the field looks like this:** stablecoin cross-border is a land-grab for corridors + licences + enterprise logos; the moat is regulatory coverage and local-rail integrations, not the chain itself (which is commoditised). The gap to Bridge/BVNK means Noah competes on **specific corridors and compliance breadth**, not scale — a niche-leader path, not a category-winner path (analysis).

## [2] Company history / fit
Consistent trajectory: **June 2025 $22M seed** → steady 2025-26 partnership flywheel in emerging markets — Nala, Picnic (Brazil payroll), VelaFi/LATAM, Nafolo/Hurupay/Payd (Africa), PEXX (Asia), Cenoa (Pakistan freelancers), Opera MiniPay, Gnosis/Algorand/Hyperbeat (chains), Sumsub (compliance). See [[Noah and Nala launch instant stablecoin settlement network]], [[Noah and Nafolo partner to cut Africa remittance fees]], [[Noah and PEXX unlock borderless USD and EUR accounts in Asia]]. Crucially, **one day earlier (2026-10-06)** Noah received a **MiCA crypto-asset licence + payment-institution licence from Latvijas Banka** (see [[Latvijas Banka grants Noah LV crypto and payment licences]]) — EU passportable. **Why now / why structured this way:** the raise lands **immediately after the EU licence and alongside a US push (NY office)** — capital is being deployed into **regulatory footprint**, the real bottleneck in this business. The extension (not Series A) fits a company compounding via partnerships and licences rather than a single step-change; it keeps the cap table and terms stable while regulatory assets (MiCA passport, future US licensing) mature.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Nothing technically novel** — fiat↔stablecoin↔local-rail orchestration with compliance is the whole category's pitch. The genuine value-add is **(a) emerging-market corridor breadth (150+ markets, 60+ currencies) and (b) a fresh EU MiCA passport** — both are licensing/integration assets, not tech. **Traction signal:** +538% YTD revenue and 150+ customers are real directional momentum, but **unverifiable in absolute terms** (no GMV/revenue $, no live-vs-signed split, no concentration). **Who captures the margin:** Noah sits between enterprises and local rails; its take-rate is squeezed from above by **issuers (Circle/Paxos) adding their own orchestration** and from below by **local-rail/ramp commoditisation**. The durable asset is the **licence stack + hard-won EM rails**, not the orchestration code. The multiple rests on being an indispensable compliant layer in hard corridors; it breaks if Circle/Stripe-Bridge/BVNK bundle the same corridors, or if issuers disintermediate (analysis/hypothesis).

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Near-term: **US expansion via NY office**, more licences, more EM corridors, hiring. Sentiment is **positive but crowded** — stablecoin B2B payments is one of 2025-26's hottest fintech themes (Bridge/Stripe, BVNK, Circle CPN). Market size the PR leans on: $208T cross-border flow (2025), B2B stablecoin at ~$226B annualised (+733% YoY) but <1% of the $34.8T B2B cross-border total — a large-TAM, early-penetration story. **Why the market goes this way / second-order:** as the theme heats, **capital and logos concentrate** toward the scaled players (Stripe-Bridge, BVNK, Circle); a $38M-total extension-stage firm is **more fragile, not safer**, in that dynamic — it must win specific corridors before incumbents bundle them. The MiCA passport is the strongest near-term edge in Europe; the US (post-GENIUS-Act environment) is a far more contested arena where Noah is a late, small entrant (analysis). Key risk: growth decelerating off the low base, and margin compression from issuer/incumbent disintermediation.

## Market Research

**Sector & drivers.** Stablecoin cross-border payments infrastructure. Size/growth: the stablecoin-infrastructure & cross-border-payment-platform market was ~$9.19bn in 2026, up from ~$7.43bn in 2025 (per The Business Research Company, via GII/EIN press, as of 2026; ~23.8% CAGR, to ~$21.3bn by 2030). TAM anchor from the PR itself: cross-border payments reached $208tn in 2025 with ~$625bn of annual bank/intermediary revenue; B2B stablecoin payments hit an annualised ~$226bn (+733% YoY) yet are still <1% of the ~$34.8tn annual cross-border B2B flow — i.e. an early-penetration, land-grab market. Structure: fragmented, infrastructure/"rails" layer sitting between stablecoin settlement and local fiat rails; barriers are regulation (money-transmission/EMI licences per geography) + depth of local-rail connectivity (network effect), not capital alone. Why now: enterprises replacing legacy correspondent-banking chains (multi-hop delay/fees) with near-instant stablecoin settlement, plus a 2025–26 regulatory thaw (US GENIUS-era clarity, EU MiCA) de-risking enterprise adoption. Second-order: as SMB/B2B flow (7% of volume but ~31% of bank revenue, ~$194bn/yr in fees) migrates to stablecoins, the economic rent shifts from correspondent banks to the rails layer.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: TPV / annualised transaction volume, take rate (bps on flow), revenue growth, # markets & currencies, licence coverage. Key players (basis of competition = regulatory coverage + rail depth + enterprise trust, not price): BVNK (acquired by Mastercard, closed Aug 2026), Conduit, PhotonPay, VelaFi, plus Circle/Bridge(Stripe)-adjacent stacks; disruptors vs incumbent correspondent banks + SWIFT. Recent moves: Mastercard completed the BVNK acquisition in Aug 2026 (announced Mar 2026); Conduit raised a $36m Series A (May 2025, co-led Dragonfly/Altos). Noah's position: niche/early-scale challenger, catching up to BVNK/Conduit on scale but with credible operator pedigree (co-founder ex-The Hut Group/UBS/Lazard; Thijn Lamers, Adyen founding team & ex-EVP Global Sales).
