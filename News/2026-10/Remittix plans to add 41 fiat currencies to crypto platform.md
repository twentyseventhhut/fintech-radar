MAJURO, Marshall Islands, Oct. 21, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Remittix is preparing to bring 41 new fiat currencies online as part of a major expansion of its PayFi ecosystem. The rollout is designed to extend the project’s crypto-to-bank and bank-to-crypto infrastructure across more currencies, payment routes and potential user markets.
 
The expansion gives Remittix a stronger international proposition ahead of the November 24, 2026 RTX launch. The project has raised $33 million from more than 40,000 participants toward a $36 million hard cap, while RTX remains available at $0.28 before progressing toward its announced $0.46 final presale stage and minimum exchange launch price.
The announcement arrives as a bullish XRP price prediction targets $20 and JPMorgan’s expansion of JPM Coin to Ethereum strengthens the institutional case behind a $12,000 ETH forecast.
 Forty-One New Fiat Currencies Expand the PayFi Opportunity Remittix is primarily a PayFi project built to connect digital assets with conventional banking. Eligible users can fund transactions with supported cryptocurrency while recipients receive fiat through compatible bank accounts.
PayFi testing has opened to 1,000 invited holders from the wider Remittix community. Early routes support EUR and USD transactions using USDT and USDC across Ethereum, Celo, Base and Polygon.
The addition of 41 fiat currencies would substantially broaden this foundation. More currency coverage can open Remittix to international workers, freelancers, suppliers, merchants and crypto holders seeking a direct route between digital assets and everyday money.
 Remittix Builds a Connected Financial Ecosystem PayFi remains the central use case, while the wider Remittix ecosystem adds products designed to attract users and create recurring activity.
The iOS wallet has surpassed 10,000 downloads, providing an established onboarding layer for future PayFi functionality. Remittix Markets has processed more than $50 million in volume across its perpetual trading platform, while Remittix Earn is set to go live with advertised yields of up to 22% APY on selected crypto assets and stablecoins.
Together, these products give Remittix several routes into the digital finance market. Users can enter through payments, trading, wallet access or earning while remaining connected to the project’s primary crypto-to-bank infrastructure.
 XRP Price Prediction Targets $20 as Ethereum Eyes $12,000 XRP recently traded near $1.36, making a $20 target nearly fifteen times higher. The bullish case is tied to payment adoption and the continued expansion of the XRP Ledger , including work around native lending, vaults and permissioned financial markets.
Ethereum recently traded around $2,454, meaning a move to $12,000 would represent almost fivefold growth. The institutional narrative strengthened when JPMorgan brought JPM Coin to Ethereum , extending its tokenized deposit infrastructure to another public blockchain.
XRP and Ethereum represent established network opportunities requiring significant new capital to reach their bullish targets. Remittix offers an earlier-stage PayFi proposition with a defined launch date, expanding fiat coverage and a visible presale price path.
 Remittix Enters Its Final Pre-Launch Phase At $33 million raised, only $3 million remains before Remittix reaches its hard cap. The current $0.28 RTX price sits approximately 64% below the announced $0.46 final presale stage and minimum exchange launch price.
The 41-currency expansion gives the project a major product catalyst as November 24 approaches. For buyers comparing XRP and Ethereum price predictions with newer crypto opportunities, RTX combines a fixed launch timeline with a PayFi platform targeting practical international payment demand.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What actually happened (vs the headline).** On 2026-10-21 a GlobeNewswire press release — **authored and paid for by Remittix itself** (Source line "Remittix", media contact marketing@remittix.io, issued from Majuro, Marshall Islands) — announced that Remittix *plans/"is preparing"* to bring 41 new fiat currencies online for its "PayFi" crypto-to-bank platform "ahead of the November 24, 2026 RTX launch." This is a **roadmap/promise, not a shipped feature.** What is actually live is far smaller: PayFi testing opened to only ~1,000 invited holders, supporting **EUR and USD only**, funded with USDT/USDC on Ethereum/Celo/Base/Polygon. The "41 currencies" are not available. The release is wrapped in unrelated price-prediction hype (XRP→$20, ETH→$12,000) and a hard-sell of the RTX **presale** ($33M raised toward a $36M hard cap; token at $0.28 heading to a $0.46 "final presale/launch" price).

**[1] This is recycled, high-frequency paid PR.** External search shows Remittix publishing **near-daily GlobeNewswire releases** with the same template ("Remittix [does X] as [XRP/ETH/BTC/DOGE] price prediction targets [Y]"): Oct 9 & 11 ("opens PayFi testing to 1,000 holders"), Oct 14 ("$0.46 final tier"), **Oct 21, 22, 23** (three near-duplicate "41 new fiat currencies" variants), Oct 24 ("300M RTX burn at launch"). The embedded "Recommended Reading" block in the note itself links two more same-week Remittix releases. The "news event" here is a marketing drumbeat timed to close a presale, not a product milestone.

**[2] Prior coverage in the vault — the timeline keeps moving.** Remittix has a multi-note history in this DB, and the claims contradict each other:
- [[Remittix announces XRP-compatible on-ramp for cross-border payments]] (2025-07) — early roadmap.
- [[Remittix Web3 wallet beta launches Q3 after $18.7M presale]] (2025-08) — presale already at $18.7M, wallet still "beta."
- [[Remittix to launch global crypto payments platform in February]] (2026-01) — platform "scheduled to go live 9 February 2026."
- [[Remittix hits $30 million as crypto-to-fiat transfer platform goes live]] (2026-06) — claimed the platform "goes live with **real** crypto-to-fiat transfers... fiat delivered directly into a bank account."
Yet this Oct-2026 release says PayFi is still in invite-only *testing* for 1,000 users (EUR/USD only) with the token not launching until Nov 24, 2026. "Live" has now been announced at least three times (Feb, June, and implicitly now) while the presale has run continuously since Dec 2024. The raise has crept $18.7M (Aug 2025) → $30M (Jun 2026) → $33M (Oct 2026) with the finish line perpetually just ahead.

**[3] Live crypto-remittance rails for comparison (what a shipped product looks like).** The vault is full of genuinely operational stablecoin cross-border rails that contrast sharply with a presale: [[Revolut launches stablecoin remittances with Polygon in UK and EEA]], [[Zepz launches stablecoin-powered Sendwave Wallet for digital dollars]], [[TerraPay partners with Fipto for stablecoin flows]], [[RedotPay adds crypto-to-MXN payouts via Circle in Mexico]], [[MAJORITY launches Latin America stablecoin transfers with Privy]], [[Bitso lists Western Union USDPT stablecoin with live data]], [[Tether invests in EFY Finance for LATAM stablecoins]]. These involve named operators, licensed partners (Circle, Visa, Western Union, Privy), disclosed corridors and actual payout rails — none sell a token to retail to fund themselves.

**[4] So what / credibility.** Independent reviewers flag material red flags: **anonymous team** (no named executives/verifiable profiles), **Marshall Islands** domicile, **no disclosed money-transmitter licenses, KYC or jurisdictional approvals** for a product that moves fiat into bank accounts, and an apparently AI-generated whitepaper. The December-2025 CertiK audit (Skynet "A", 80.09) **covers only the ERC-20 token smart contract** — not the wallet, off-ramp, banking integrations or compliance stack that the remittance pitch depends on. Some reviewers allege tokens weren't delivered to presale buyers. Net: treat "41 fiat currencies" as an unverifiable marketing claim from a self-published presale PR, not a product fact.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team questions**

1. **Is the "41 fiat currencies" live?** No — the PR says Remittix "is preparing to bring" them online. Live scope is EUR/USD only, in invite-only testing for ~1,000 holders. The 41 are a promise.

2. **Who authored this "news"?** Remittix itself. GlobeNewswire Source = "Remittix," contact marketing@remittix.io, and an explicit disclaimer that the content is provided by Remittix and not endorsed/verified. This is paid PR, not journalism.

3. **How often does Remittix publish this?** Near-daily. Same template ran Oct 9, 11, 14, 21, 22, 23, 24 2026, with three near-identical "41 currencies" variants in one week. Not a discrete event.

4. **Does the timeline hold together?** No. "Platform goes live" was claimed for Feb 9 2026 and again at $30M in June 2026 ("real crypto-to-fiat transfers... into a bank account"), yet Oct 2026 says PayFi is still invite-only testing and the token won't launch until Nov 24 2026. "Live" has been announced repeatedly while the presale runs continuously since Dec 2024.

5. **Is there an identifiable team?** Open/negative — reviewers report no named founders or verifiable executive profiles; domiciled in the Marshall Islands.

6. **Any regulatory/licensing basis to move fiat into bank accounts?** None disclosed — no money-transmitter licenses, KYC policy, or jurisdictional approvals mentioned. Major gap for a remittance product.

7. **Does the CertiK audit cover the remittance product?** No — it covers only the ERC-20 token contract (Skynet A, 80.09, Dec 2025). It does not audit the wallet, off-ramp, banking integrations, or compliance backend.

8. **Are the usage metrics verifiable?** Open — "10,000+ wallet downloads," "$50M+ Remittix Markets volume," "up to 22% APY Earn" are self-reported with no third-party data; the 22% yield claim is itself a risk flag.

9. **Are the price-prediction claims (XRP $20, ETH $12,000) relevant?** No — they are unrelated hype bolted on to attract search traffic and frame RTX as "earlier-stage upside." Pure marketing.

10. **Has money actually been raised / tokens delivered?** Raise figures ($33M/40k participants) are self-reported; at least one reviewer alleges presale tokens were not delivered. Unverified either way.

11. **Is $0.28→$0.46 "64% below launch price" a real signal?** No — it's a presale pricing tier set by the issuer, not a market price; it functions as a buy-now urgency device.

12. **Does this merit a fresh note vs the existing Remittix coverage?** No — it is the same ongoing presale promo already captured by the June 2026 "goes live" note and earlier notes; the only new element is a larger roadmap number and a later launch date.

13. **What would change the verdict?** A post-Nov-24 independent verification that RTX listed, that non-EUR/USD corridors actually settle to bank accounts, named/licensed operators, and a compliance/audit scope covering the off-ramp — none present as of 2026-10.

**Importance: 1/5 — self-published, near-daily crypto-presale paid PR promoting an unlaunched token; the headline feature ("41 fiat currencies") is an unverifiable roadmap promise, the timeline has slipped repeatedly, the team is anonymous and unlicensed, and the item duplicates existing Remittix coverage in the vault. Logged for the record / promo-tracking only; not a substantive fintech milestone.**

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Global remittance flows run ~$750–860bn/yr, with the World Bank pegging the average send cost at 6.36% as of Apr 2026 (digital 4.59% vs non-digital 7.30%) — still >2x the UN SDG 3% target (per World Bank RPW, via remitroutes.com, Apr 2026). This cost gap is the entire sales pitch for crypto/stablecoin rails. But stablecoins remain a tiny slice of the pipe: only ~$135bn moved of ~$44.3tn retail cross-border in 2025 = 0.31% of volume (per Allium, 2026). "Why now": stablecoin regulation (US GENIUS-style frameworks, MiCA) plus visible incumbent adoption — MoneyGram does USDC cash-in/out in 170+ countries on Stellar; Western Union is launching a USDPT stablecoin (see in-base comp below). The structural point: the rails are being built by regulated, licensed incumbents — exactly the thing a presale token lacks.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: take rate / all-in send cost (bps), settled volume (TPV), corridor count, licensed-market count. Real players and basis of competition: **Wise** (self-built infra, ~40 licences, price+product), **Remitly** (RELY, distribution in migrant corridors), **Ripple ODL** (cumulative >$95bn processed by Jan 2026, 70+ corridors, ~80% of major remittance routes — per press citations), **Stellar/MoneyGram** ($5.5bn Q1-2026 volume, +72% y/y; 475k+ cash locations). Remittix's position: **not a competitor to these in any measurable sense — it is pre-revenue, pre-launch.** Disclosed "traction" is a 1,000-invitee PayFi test (EUR/USD via USDT/USDC on Eth/Celo/Base/Polygon), a 10k-download iOS wallet, and self-reported "$50m volume" on an in-house perps venue — none audited, none a remittance metric. Moat: none demonstrated `(analysis)`; the 41-currency "expansion" is an announcement, not live coverage.

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation can be computed for Remittix — RTX is a presale token at $0.28 (claimed $33m raised of $36m hard cap, >40k participants), with no revenue, no float, no market cap. That is itself the finding: "no data / not a going-concern comp." For scale, the legitimate listed peers:
- **Wise:** mkt cap ~$11bn, LTM rev ~$2.8bn → EV/Revenue ≈ $11bn / $2.8bn ≈ **3.9x**; EV/EBITDA ≈ 15.1x (EBITDA ~$713m) (per multiples.vc, Oct 2026).
- **Remitly (RELY):** mkt cap ~$5.22bn, LTM rev ~$1.81bn → EV/Revenue ≈ **2.5x** (per multiples.vc, Oct 2026).
- Internal comps (stablecoin/crypto remittance rails being built by licensed players): [[Revolut launches stablecoin remittances with Polygon in UK and EEA]], [[Zepz launches stablecoin-powered Sendwave Wallet for digital dollars]], [[Western Union plans USDPT stablecoin on Solana]].
Distribution not computed (only 2 comparable listed figures; both profitable, licensed, revenue-generating — qualitatively Remittix shares none of those attributes).

**Risk flags.**
1. **Promo-vs-substance gap / PR-as-source.** The sole source is a Remittix-authored GlobeNewswire release (with a disclaimer stating the platform "do[es] not endorse, verify, or guarantee" it and "accept[s] no liability"), padded with unrelated XRP-$20 and ETH-$12,000 price hype to drive token demand. The headline "41 currencies" is a plan, not a shipped capability — classic token-economics-driven marketing. Why it matters: the real signal (a 1,000-person test) is far smaller than the framing implies.
2. **Presale-project red flags.** Independent coverage flags Remittix for a presale running since Dec 2024 with repeatedly postponed listing dates, an anonymous/undoxxed team, no corporate entity or regulatory licensing, and claims that on-chain receipts allegedly don't match (per auriccrypto.com, coingabbar.com, coinspeaker 2026). Marshall Islands domicile adds regulatory opacity. Why it matters: no accountability, no legal recourse, and a $36m hard cap creates incentive to keep "launch" perpetually imminent.
3. **Disintermediation by licensed incumbents.** Even if Remittix ships, the stablecoin-remittance rail is being captured by regulated players (MoneyGram/Stellar, Western Union USDPT, Revolut, Ripple ODL) who already hold the licences, cash-out networks and corridors that are the actual moat. A new unlicensed token has no realistic path to the 6% cost pool `(analysis)`.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** For the sector: nothing — this is noise, not a re-rating. The genuinely watchable trend is incumbent stablecoin adoption (WU USDPT, MoneyGram/Stellar). Falsifiable thesis: Remittix is promo-first, product-thin; it would be falsified by a live, audited, multi-currency remittance product with real settled volume and a doxxed/licensed operator post the Nov 24, 2026 "launch" — absent that, treat traction claims as unverified `(analysis)`.
