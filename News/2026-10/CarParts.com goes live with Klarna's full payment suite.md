**New York, October 27, 2026**— Klarna (NYSE: KLAR), the everyday finance network, announced today that CarParts.com (NASDAQ: PRTS), a leading U.S. online retailer of automotive parts and accessories, now offers Klarna's full suite of payment options to its customers at checkout.

Across CarParts.com's more than 1.5 million parts and accessories, shoppers can now pay in full for everyday items like wiper blades or brake pads, split a new bumper cover or headlights into four interest-free payments, or spread the cost of larger repairs, like a catalytic converter or transmission, over time with Financing.

"CarParts.com shows how simple we want working with Klarna to be," said Sarah Kocina, Head of GtM Partnerships, North America. "With one integration, their customers get the full range of ways to pay with Klarna. Whether a driver needs a quick fix or a major repair, they can choose the option that fits that purchase. That's the setup we want every merchant to have."

"We want every trip to CarParts.com to be as seamless as possible, from finding the right part to paying for it," said Michael Huffaker, Chief Operating Officer of CarParts.com. "Klarna's flexible payment options give our customers more control over how they pay, so they can take care of their vehicle on their own terms. Integrating with Klarna was seamless, and we're already seeing how much our shoppers value having those choices at checkout."

**About Klarna**

Klarna is the everyday finance network. With over 120 million global active Klarna users and 3.8 million transactions per day, Klarna's AI-powered payments and commerce network is empowering people to pay smarter with a mission to be available everywhere for everything. Consumers can pay with Klarna online, in-store and through Apple Pay & Google Pay. Klarna is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: KLAR). For more information, visitKlarna.com.

**About Carparts.com**

CarParts.com,Inc.is a technology-led ecommerce company offering over 1.5 million quality automotive parts and accessories. Operating for over 30 years, CarParts.com has established itself as a premier destination for drivers seeking repair,maintenance, and upgrade solutions. With a commitment to delivering exceptional value backed by its nationwide,company-operated distribution network, fast shipping, and experienced customer service team, CarParts.com aims to eliminate the uncertainty and stress often associated with vehicle maintenance and repair. The company, headquartered in Long Beach, California, operates CarParts.com and a portfolio of brands, including JC Whitney®, Kool-Vue, Evan Fischer, Garage-Pro, and CarParts Wholesale.
