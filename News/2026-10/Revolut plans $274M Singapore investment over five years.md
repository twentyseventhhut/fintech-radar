> [!info] 2026-10-09 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇸🇬 Revolut plans to invest $274M in Singapore over five years, with a focus on profitability. The investment will support product development, regional expansion and hiring. The firm lso plans to open a permanent office in early 2027 and grow its local workforce to more than 300 employees within three years, strengthening Singapore's role as its Southeast Asian hub.
