Today, we’re introducing Ondo Private Markets, which gives eligible investors tokenized exposure to the economic performance of industry-defining private companies before they go public.
Starting with a pre-IPO AI company, the first market will launch this week with permissionless trading available on secondary markets. Exposure to leading names across robotics, cybersecurity, biotech, infrastructure, and other sectors will follow as Ondo builds out the broadest selection of private-company exposure onchain.
Exposure that has traditionally been opaque, gated by high minimums, and illiquid is now accessible in a permissionless token, fully composable and able to be traded 24/7.
Why Private Markets?
 
Private markets can offer even more in both breadth and performance than U.S. public markets, which are widely regarded as the global gold standard. 87% of U.S. companies with more than $100 million in annual revenue are privately held, including many shaping the next generation of AI, robotics, and more. They can also deliver superior long-term returns. Over the 20 years ending December 2025, U.S. private equity funds delivered annualized returns of 13.2%, net of fees, compared with 11.3% for an S&P 500 public-market equivalent.
Yet access to private markets remains out of reach for many retail and high-net worth investors, with high minimum investments create a substantial barrier to entry. Still, access is only half the challenge, with a lack of liquidity also holding back the asset class. Limited secondary markets make it difficult to exit, particularly for investors with smaller positions, who may have to wait years for an IPO. Getting in is difficult, and getting out can be just as hard.

 “A huge and growing amount of wealth-generating potential is out of retail investors’ reach, concentrated in a dramatically smaller number of institutional hands.” — Eric Pan, CEO of The Investment Company Institute (ICI), the trade association for the global asset management industry 

Open Access, With A Way In and Out
Ondo Private Markets brings together targeted exposure to individual private companies with permissionless 24/7 trading, giving investors exposure to top performing private companies, while unlocking the ability to transfer or trade their positions before the reference company goes public.
Specifically, Ondo Private Markets provides economic exposure to the economic performance of individual private companies through tokenized notes. Each note’s payout is linked to the per-share value realized on the referenced company’s common shares at a qualifying liquidity event. Eligible investors can trade the notes 24/7 on secondary markets or hold them in their own wallets.
Here’s why it matters:
1. Access industry-defining companies
Gain economic exposure to private companies shaping the next era of the economy, before they reach public markets. We're starting with AI, with plans to expand into robotics, cybersecurity, biotech, defense, energy, space, and more, bringing eligible investors access to the companies building out the modern economy.
2. Buy and sell, anytime
Private markets have historically offered limited liquidity. Ondo Private Markets enables trading venues to generate liquidity, bringing buyers and sellers together 24/7. Investors can increase, reduce, or exit their exposure through secondary trading without waiting for an IPO or other liquidity event.
3. Only the companies you want
Choose the individual businesses you have conviction in, without having to buy a broad fund full of companies you don't. Build exposure around your own view of a company's prospects, with greater control over which businesses you include and how much you allocate to each.
4. Self-custody and freely transferable
Hold your tokens in your own self-custody wallet and transfer them to other eligible holders. Onchain transferability gives you flexibility over where you hold your tokens and how you move them between supported wallets and trading venues.
5. Built on proven infrastructure
Ondo Private Markets is built on the same institutional-grade infrastructure that has made Ondo the leading issuer of tokenized stocks and Treasuries, with $3.9 billion in total value locked, over 1 million cumulative holders, and partnerships with the largest institutions in traditional markets.
Access the Fastest-Growing Startups, Starting With AI
Within private markets, the fastest-growing startups have delivered outsized returns. AI companies are leading the way, accounting for nearly half of all unicorn value. Together with SpaceX, the two leading AI companies are projected to generate more value than every U.S. venture-backed exit since 2000.
These private companies shaping the future of AI, space, energy, and robotics are staying private longer rather than going public, even as their valuations climb into the hundreds of billions or trillions of dollars. By the time these private companies IPO, a significant portion of their wealth creation may already be behind them.

 “Today, the top decile of high-growth companies
are almost exclusively in the private markets.”
— David George, General Partner at a16z 

Ondo Private Markets is built to provide a way to not only get access before industry-defining companies go public, but also a way to get out anytime if they want to reduce their exposure. The first asset, tokens referencing a leading pre-IPO AI company, is expected to begin secondary market trading this week, with robotics, cybersecurity, biotech, and infrastructure names to follow.
One Asset First, an Asset Class Next
What Ondo Stocks did for public companies, Ondo Private Markets will do for private companies.
Ondo Stocks became the number 1 platform for tokenized stocks with over $1 billion in TVL, bringing over 450 tokenized stocks and ETFs to global investors that previously never had access. Now Ondo Private Markets is bringing tokenized exposure to the most in-demand private companies, opening access to eligible investors worldwide, with 24/7 trading on secondary markets.
We're starting with one market, but we're on a path to redefine an entire asset class, bringing the broadest selection of private-company exposures trading 24/7.

# Context-enrichment: Ondo launches Ondo Private Markets for tokenized exposure
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 3/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On 2026-10-06/07 Ondo Finance announced **Ondo Private Markets**, a new line issuing **tokenized "notes"** that give *economic* exposure to individual pre-IPO private companies. First asset: a note referencing a single, **undisclosed** pre-IPO AI company, expected to begin **permissionless 24/7 secondary trading "this week."** Robotics, cybersecurity, biotech, defence, energy, space and infrastructure "to follow."

De-PR'd reality — what the token actually is:
- It is **NOT equity**, not a share, not a fund unit. Per external reporting, each note is an **unsecured contractual obligation of "Ondo Global Markets (BVI) Limited," a bankruptcy-remote SPV**, issued under **SEC Regulation S** — i.e. **non-U.S. persons only; U.S. retail is explicitly excluded.** Payout is linked to the **per-share value of the referenced company's common stock at a qualifying liquidity event** (IPO/acquisition). No ownership, no voting, no dividend, no share-registry entry.
- So three things the PR blurs: **(a)** the "democratize access for retail" framing collides with Reg S — the people most locked out (U.S. retail) still cannot buy it; **(b)** the holder's real credit exposure is to **Ondo's BVI SPV and its ability/willingness to source and honour the underlying**, not a clean claim on the company; **(c)** the headline company is **unnamed**, so buyers cannot do their own diligence and must trust Ondo's sourcing and valuation.
- **→ Why structured this way:** private-company shares carry transfer restrictions (ROFR, issuer consent) and are scarce. A synthetic note over an SPV is the only way to make a "freely transferable, 24/7, composable" instrument out of an asset that is legally sticky. **→ Why it matters:** the liquidity and composability are real at the *token* layer, but the *underlying* is still illiquid and gated — the SPV absorbs that mismatch, and so does the noteholder if Ondo can't deliver at the liquidity event. This is the same SPV-wrapper model that drew OpenAI's public "these tokens are not OpenAI equity" rebuke of Robinhood in July 2025.

## [1] Competitors / peers
Crowded, fast-moving "tokenized private exposure" field in 2026:
- **Robinhood** — fired the starting gun (June–July 2025, offshore EU) with SPV-backed OpenAI/SpaceX tokens; triggered the OpenAI "not equity" controversy — the template Ondo now follows.
- **Securitize** — the regulated heavyweight: SEC transfer agent/broker-dealer/ATS, FINRA nod for tokenized IPO underwriting/custody ([[FINRA approves Securitize for tokenized IPO underwriting and custody]]), tokenized the ~$1.3B ARK fund (SpaceX/OpenAI/Anthropic sleeves), NYSE MOU, $1.25B SPAC. Does it "properly" (real securities), but fund-wrapped, not single-name 24/7.
- **JPMorgan (Kinexys)** — tokenized a PE fund for private-bank clients ([[JPMorgan tokenizes private-equity fund on its blockchain]]); institutional, permissioned, not retail/24-7.
- **Bitget IPO Prime, Hecto, Jarsy, Republic** — various SpaceX/hectocorn index or single-name tokens.
- **Monark + Apex** — non-token "private markets for retail" via custody/clearing ([[Monark and Apex Fintech open private markets to retail investors]]).
- Tokenized *public* stock peers (Kraken/xStocks $25B+ volume, Robinhood) frame the demand but sit in the regulated-equity lane the Sept-2026 SEC "Innovation Exemption" (75 largest stocks, 0.25% caps) is building.
- **→ Position:** Ondo is **fast-follower, not pioneer**, on the structure (SPV note = Robinhood's 2025 move). Its edge is **distribution + brand + rails**: leading tokenized-stocks issuer, $3.6–3.9B TVL, >1M holders — so it can bootstrap secondary liquidity faster than a cold-start venue. **→ Second-order:** the real contest is not "who tokenizes first" but **who can source genuine underlying allocations in SpaceX/OpenAI-class names and build two-sided secondary liquidity.** Securitize wins on regulatory depth; Ondo bets on crypto-native liquidity and composability.

## [2] Company history / fit
Clean strategic line: OUSG/USDY tokenized Treasuries → **Ondo Global Markets / Ondo Stocks** (100+ tokenized US stocks Sept 2025, [[Ondo launches 100+ tokenized US stocks across chains]]; $1B TVL by May 2026, rebranded "Ondo Stocks") → 24/7 minting ([[Ondo Finance launches 24 7 minting for tokenized US stocks]]) → custodial securities w/ Broadridge ([[Ondo Finance launches custodial tokenized securities with Broadridge]]) → institutional Treasuries pilot w/ JPM/Mastercard/Ripple ([[Ondo, J.P. Morgan, Mastercard, Ripple complete tokenized Treasuries pilot]]). The 2025 Oasis Pro (SEC broker) acquisition and $250M Pantera fund, plus the SEC closing its Ondo probe ([[SEC ends investigation into tokenization platform Ondo Finance]]), set the regulatory runway.
**→ Why Ondo acts this way:** Treasuries/stocks are becoming **commoditized, low-margin** tokenization (everyone has them). Private markets are **scarcer, higher-margin, and brand-defining** — the natural up-market move to protect a software multiple on top of a commodity rails business. "What Ondo Stocks did for public companies, Ondo Private Markets will do for private" is the explicit thesis: reuse the same issuance + distribution stack on a scarcer asset.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Genuinely new?** The *mechanism* (SPV note / Reg S / liquidity-event payout) is **not new** — Robinhood did it in 2025. What's new-ish is **single-name 24/7 permissionless secondary trading of private-company exposure from the category-leading tokenized-stock issuer**, with self-custody and DeFi composability.
- **Traction = zero so far.** As of the announcement it is **"expected to begin trading this week"** — one undisclosed name, no AUM, no holder count for *this* product, no stated secondary-market depth. The $3.6–3.9B TVL / 1M holders belong to the *other* Ondo products, not Private Markets. **Do not read the TVL as adoption of this launch.**
- **→ Where value-add is real vs not:** real value is **liquidity + fractional access + self-custody** on an asset class that is normally illiquid and $1M-minimum gated. But **who captures the margin?** Ondo (issuance + venue fees) and whoever sources the underlying shares. The **noteholder bears basis risk** (note price ≠ true share value, which is unobservable pre-IPO), **SPV credit risk**, and **sourcing risk** (if Ondo can't actually acquire proportional underlying, the note is a bet on Ondo, not the company). **→ What breaks it:** an issuer (OpenAI-style) publicly disowning the token; a liquidity event where payout underdelivers; or regulators treating the note as an unregistered security for any U.S. nexus. The value-add is **conditional on sourcing credibility and secondary depth**, neither proven here.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- Roadmap: expand from AI to robotics/cybersecurity/biotech/defence/energy/space; "broadest selection of private-company exposures trading 24/7." Trading "this week."
- Sentiment: rides the dominant 2026 narrative — top-decile growth is now **private** (a16z's George quoted), companies staying private longer, AI+SpaceX projected to out-value all US VC exits since 2000. Strong tailwind for *demand*.
- Regulatory backdrop: **Reg S/offshore is the safe harbour but also the ceiling** — the big prize (U.S. retail) stays off-limits until a U.S. framework exists; the Sept-2026 SEC Innovation Exemption covers *public* NMS stocks, not private notes. Securitize's regulated path may age better.
- **→ Why the market goes this way + counterintuitive second-order:** scarcity of real pre-IPO allocation means several issuers may float notes referencing the *same few* names (OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceX pre-float) — **concentration makes the segment fragile, not safe**: a single issuer disavowal, a down-round, or a messy liquidity event could reprice the whole synthetic-pre-IPO category at once. The central question is therefore **not "is there demand" (clearly yes) but "can anyone source enough real underlying and survive the first contested liquidity event with payouts intact."**

## Market Research

**Sector & drivers.** Tokenized real-world assets (RWA) sit at ~$32-38bn on-chain AUM in 2026 (per aggregators via Bitget/yellow.com, as of Aug 2026), up ~9x from ~$4bn in Jan 2025; tokenized Treasuries lead (~$10bn), private credit ~$8bn, tokenized equities the smallest but fastest-growing slice. Tokenized private-company/pre-IPO *equity* exposure is a sub-vertical still measured in the low hundreds of millions, so Ondo is entering a thin but opening market, not a mature one. Structure: fragmented and early — issuers (Ondo, Securitize), access platforms (Monark/Apex, LSE/Crowdcube), and DeFi venues compete; value accrues to whoever controls distribution + a liquid secondary book. Barriers are regulatory (securities law, who can hold) and liquidity depth, not tech. "Why now": the staying-private-longer thesis (Ondo cites 87% of >$100m-revenue US firms private; a16z's "top-decile growth almost exclusively private") plus a crypto-rails regulatory thaw (Ondo's own SEC investigation closed Dec 2025 — [[SEC ends investigation into tokenization platform Ondo Finance]]). Why it matters: value creation migrates pre-IPO, so a product that fractionalizes and makes pre-IPO exposure *tradeable 24/7* targets a genuine gap — but only for eligible non-US investors (below).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: AUM/TVL, holder count, secondary-market liquidity (depth/spread), number of reference names. Players: (a) issuers/infra — Ondo (leading tokenized-stocks + Treasuries issuer: ~$3.7-3.9bn TVL, >1m cumulative holders per company PR/web; Ondo Stocks >$1bn TVL across 450+ tokenized stocks/ETFs), Securitize (RWA tokenization, BlackRock BUIDL plumbing); (b) private-market access rails — [[Monark and Apex Fintech open private markets to retail investors]] (SPV-based pre-IPO: SpaceX/Perplexity/Stripe, $5k minimums), [[LSE and Crowdcube open private markets to retail investors]] (regulated secondary venue); (c) TradFi-on-chain — [[JPMorgan tokenizes private-equity fund on its blockchain]] (Kinexys, permissioned, private-bank clients). Basis of competition: distribution reach + secondary liquidity + regulatory perimeter, not price. Recent moves: Securitize announced $1.25bn SPAC listing (Oct 2025); JPMorgan Kinexys PE tokenization (Oct 2025). Ondo's position: ahead on tokenized-RWA infra and holder base, first-mover on *single-name pre-IPO notes with permissionless secondary trading* (analysis). Moat: scale + holder network + "proven infrastructure" brand (intangible/network) — but thin on the one thing that matters here, a deep secondary order book, which is unproven at launch.

**Comps & multiples.** No clean trading comp — ONDO is a token, not an equity with disclosed revenue/EBITDA, so EV/Rev-type multiples = no data (do not compute). Reference points only:
- ONDO token market cap ~$2.38bn at ~$0.49 (per Coinbase/MetaMask price pages, as of ~Oct 8 2026); circulating ~4.86bn of 10bn max (~48.6%). This is a token valuation, NOT an enterprise/equity value tied to product revenue — treat as sentiment, not a multiple.
- Ondo TVL ~$3.7-3.9bn → token mcap/TVL ≈ `$2.38bn / $3.8bn ≈ 0.6x` (analysis; rough, TVL ≠ revenue and much is low-fee Treasuries — not a valuation anchor).
- Internal comp — closest private-company valuation: [[Securitize plans to go public via $1.25B SPAC]] = $1.25bn pre-money equity value (Oct 2025), a direct RWA-tokenization peer; and [[Ondo Finance to buy SEC broker Oasis Pro for $1.4B]] shows Ondo's own M&A scale.
- Reference AUM anchor: BlackRock BUIDL ~$2.5bn (May 2026, via web) — the single largest tokenized product, dwarfing the pre-IPO sub-vertical Ondo is now entering.
Verdict: distribution not computed — no comparable earnings-based multiple exists for a token issuer; qualitative only. `[UNSOURCED]`: Ondo product-level revenue, take rate on notes, and the pre-IPO book's AUM.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Jurisdictional gate undercuts the "democratization" pitch.** Notes are issued under SEC Reg S via a BVI SPV, explicitly *barring US retail* (per Forkast/CoinCentral, web). Second-order: the largest wealth pool the PR implicitly targets can't buy, capping TAM and inviting enforcement if US persons are reached.
2. **Synthetic structure = issuer counterparty risk, not equity.** Holders get a note referencing per-share value at a liquidity event — no shares, no voting, payout contingent on a *qualifying liquidity event* that may be years away or never. Second-order: if Ondo/SPV or its share-sourcing counterparties fail, or the reference company stays private indefinitely, the token can detach from NAV; "24/7 liquidity" depends on a secondary book that doesn't yet exist at depth.
3. **Opacity of the reference asset.** First note ties to an *unnamed* pre-IPO AI company; investors price a name they can't see, with no issuer-level private-company financial disclosure. Second-order: mispricing/mark-to-myth risk and reputational blowback if the referenced firm stumbles.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is a potential *new entry* that extends tokenization from Treasuries/public stocks into single-name pre-IPO equity — if it works, expect fast-following issuers (Securitize, access rails) and pressure on traditional SPV middlemen like Monark (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: Ondo Private Markets only matters if real secondary liquidity forms — watch for disclosed per-market AUM and bid/ask depth within 1-2 quarters. Breaks the thesis: thin/ no secondary trading, a regulator challenging the Reg S perimeter, or the first reference name delaying its liquidity event — any of which reduces this to a gated, illiquid wrapper no better than existing SPVs.
