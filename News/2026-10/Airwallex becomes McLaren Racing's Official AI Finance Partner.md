# Airwallex becomes McLaren Racing's Official AI Finance Partner

> [!info] 2026-10-22 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇬🇧 Airwallexrenews and upgrades its partnership with McLaren Racing, becoming its Official AI Finance Partner. Starting in January 2027, the expanded agreement will focus on AI-driven financial operations, while Airwallex continues supporting McLaren's cross-border payments and digital merchandise platform.
