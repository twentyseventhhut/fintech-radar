Stifel Makes Strategic Investment in Meow; Partners to Launch Next-Generation Digital Banking Platform
White-Label Treasury Management Platform Enhances Stifel’s Full-Service Offering for the Innovation Economy; Client Launch Expected in Early 2027



 
 
 October 15, 2026 08:30 ET 
 

 
   | Source:
 

 Stifel Financial Corporation 
 
 

 
 

 

 
 Stifel Financial Corporation 
 

 


ST. LOUIS, Oct. 15, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Stifel Financial Corp. (NYSE: SF) today announced that it has made a strategic minority investment in Meow Technologies, Inc. (“Meow”), one of the leading business banking fintechs in the United States, and has entered into a services agreement under which Stifel will offer through its subsidiary bank, Stifel Bank, a white-label version of Meow’s treasury management platform to its commercial banking clients, with an initial focus on Venture and Fund Banking.
Under the agreement, Stifel will have access to Meow’s current and forward capabilities, giving Stifel clients a modern digital experience for managing cash, payments, cards, and day-to-day treasury operations across operating companies and multi-entity fund structures. Stifel expects to begin onboarding clients to the new platform in early 2027.
“The innovation economy is one of Stifel’s most important growth opportunities, and we have invested accordingly – in talent, in our balance sheet, and now in technology,” said Stifel Chairman and CEO Ron Kruszewski.
Jake Moseley, Managing Director, Stifel Venture Banking added “Our investment in Meow pairs a best-in-class digital platform with the bankers, products, and capabilities of a full-service financial firm. We believe that combination is unmatched for founders, growth companies, and the investors who back them.”
The new digital banking platform rounds out Stifel’s offering for today’s entrepreneurial economy, which includes experienced bankers with deep sector expertise, a broad suite of cash management solutions with competitive yields and access to expanded FDIC insurance coverage through Stifel’s banking subsidiaries and their deposit placement arrangements, a balance sheet capable of supporting companies and funds with venture debt, growth lending, and fund finance, and full-service investment banking and wealth management capabilities that support clients from formation through IPO and beyond.
“Stifel has built one of the fastest-growing venture and fund banking franchises in the country, and we are proud to have them as both an investor and a partner,” said Brandon Arvanaghi, CEO of Meow. “Meow’s technology brings new capabilities to Stifel’s clients including expense management, bill pay, stablecoin payments and agentic infrastructure, all backed by Stifel’s bankers and balance sheet. That’s a powerful combination for the innovation economy and we look forward to building the future of business banking together.”
The partnership builds on strong momentum in Stifel’s Venture and Fund Banking business, where deposits have grown nearly six-fold since September 2023 to more than $10 billion. Stifel has recently expanded into Life Sciences and Healthcare, Energy Tech, and Project Finance, added senior coverage in New York and Boston, and allocated dedicated balance sheet capacity to venture and fund clients. The business now includes more than 125 client-facing professionals in every major U.S. technology market and serves more than 500 high-growth companies and more than 250 fund clients.
 Stifel Company Information Stifel Financial Corp. (NYSE: SF) is a diversified financial services firm providing wealth management, commercial and investment banking, trading, and research services to individuals, institutions, and municipalities. Founded in 1890 and headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri, the firm operates more than 400 offices across the United States and in major global financial centers. As a firm where success meets success, Stifel works closely with individual and institutional clients, aiming to transform opportunities into achievement.
Stifel Bank and Stifel Bank & Trust, Members FDIC, offer a full range of consumer and commercial lending solutions, including Venture Banking and Venture Capital Coverage. To learn more about Stifel, please visit the Company’s website at Stifel.com. For global disclosures, please visit www.stifel.com/investor-relations/press-releases.
 Meow Company Information Meow provides business banking, expense management and treasury technology to businesses and the banks that serve them. Extending white-label capabilities this way will allow other banks to offer the same modern digital experience that Meow’s business customers enjoy spanning cash management, cards, expense management, bill pay, invoicing, agentic banking, stablecoin payments, and back-office functionality like agentic transaction monitoring and enhanced due diligence under their own brand and without building the technology themselves.
