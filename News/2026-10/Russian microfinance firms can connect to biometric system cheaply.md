# Russian microfinance firms can connect to biometric system cheaply

> [!info] 2026-10-08 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Финтехно

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Финтехно] МФО могут подключиться к Единой биометрической системе (ЕБС) без многомиллионных затрат на собственную инфраструктуру — об этом на форуме «Цифровые решения» рассказал Магомед Гамзаев, директор по развитию финансовых продуктов «Сравни». По словам Гамзаева, прямая интеграция с ЕБС может стоить до 90 млн рублей, что для многих участников рынка слишком дорого. Делегированная идентификация через оператора финансовой платформы позволяет минимизировать эти затраты. При этом для МФО важно не только подключиться к ЕБС, но и сохранить удобный клиентский путь. Поэтому после интеграции финансовой организации остаётся контролировать аналитику и корректность оформления займов, а сам процесс подключения «Сравни» сократил до двух недель. Для клиента при этом ничего не меняется в привычном процессе дистанционного оформления займа: биометрия помогает подтвердить личность и дополнительно защищает от мошенничества. 💳 Финтехно в Telegram | в MAX

# Context-enrichment: Russian microfinance firms can connect to biometric system cheaply
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5. **FRESHNESS: STALE — re-run of [[Sravni helps microlenders embed mandatory biometric verification]].**_

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
At the "Digital Solutions" (Цифровые решения) forum, Moscow (6–10 Oct 2026), **Magomed Gamzaev, director of financial-product development at Sravni (Сравни)**, told attendees that MFOs can connect to Russia's Unified Biometric System (ЕБС / UBS) **without multimillion-ruble infrastructure spend**: direct integration "can cost up to 90M rubles," while **delegated identification via a financial-platform operator** minimizes cost, and Sravni has cut the connection process to "two weeks." The client-facing remote-loan flow is unchanged; biometrics confirm identity and add anti-fraud protection.

**This is a conference talking-point, not a new event.** There is no new regulation, no new pricing/tariff, no named MFO client, no volume. The statement is timed to the **ЦБТ–Sravni cooperation agreement announced 2026-10-07** (one day earlier) at the same forum — i.e. a marketing reiteration of Sravni's SaaS product that the corpus already captured in June 2026 ([[Sravni helps microlenders embed mandatory biometric verification]], 2026-06-26, importance 3, which carries the full [0]–[4] analysis, market research, the 1 March 2026 mandate, the "zero of 27 MFCs connected" gap, and the 4–19 rub/request delegated economics).

**+ Why structured this way / what it reveals:** The "up to 90M rubles direct / two weeks" framing is vendor marketing for *total cost avoided*, **not unique to Sravni** — T-Bank (Т-Банк) runs the identical "save up to 90M rubles, connect in 2–4 weeks" pitch for its own delegated MFO-identification service (Kommersant doc 8832436; Forbes). So this is an industry-standard delegated-identification sales line, recited at a forum, not a Sravni-specific disclosure.

## [1] Competitors / peers
Unchanged from the June note: this is a delegated-identification toll-booth race for a legally compulsory step.
- **T-Bank (Т-Банк / Т-Банк ID)** — launched delegated biometric identification for MFOs with the **same "up to 90M / 2–4 weeks" pitch**; already onboarded "ПСБ Финанс," Summit Group. Most direct competitor; arguably ahead on named integrations.
- **RSHB (Россельхозбанк)** — building delegated UBS identification (per June research).
- **Finuslugi (Финуслуги, Moscow Exchange)** — a bona-fide 211-ФЗ financial-platform operator already doing full delegated client identification.
- **Direct integration** (~15–30M, up to ~90–100M rubles all-in) vs **delegated ~4–19 rub/request** — same economics as June.
- **Position:** parity / slightly behind T-Bank; nothing in this item moves the competitive picture.

**+ Why this lay of the land:** Whoever owns the ЕБС connection levies a per-call toll on a mandated step; the contest is distribution + price, not tech (everyone hits the same ЦБТ API). The Oct forum changes none of this.

## [2] Company history / fit
Sravni — top-5 Russian fintech by revenue (Smart Ranking FY2024, +23% YoY), a financial marketplace converting its per-lead take-rate into a recurring per-transaction identity toll. Fit and logic are exactly as analysed in the June note. **Caveat flagged in June stands:** the June note called Sravni a "licensed OFP"; external research for THIS item could not confirm Sravni is a CBR-registered 211-ФЗ financial-platform operator — sources call it a "marketplace" selling a SaaS ЕБС integration, and Gamzaev's "via a financial-platform operator" phrasing blurs that category (the registered OFP exemplar is Finuslugi). Treat the OFP characterization as **unverified**.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**No new novelty vs June.** The capability (state-mandated ЕБС biometric ID, delegated via an intermediary, capex avoidance, ~two-week onboarding) was already presented by Sravni in June/August 2026 and covered in the corpus. The only genuinely new 2026-10 fact is the **ЦБТ–Sravni cooperation agreement (2026-10-07)** — a signed partnership to jointly develop biometric identification for online loans and extend ЕБС to other products; but that is a one-day-prior press item, not what this forum-quote note is about, and it still names **no client, no tariff, no volume** (announced, not traction).

**+ Why the value-add is (still) capped:** ЦБТ owns the rail; intermediaries collect a thin, price-war-compressed per-call toll (cut 3–6x in early 2026 to 4–19 rubles); MFOs multi-home across direct + delegated. Sravni is the toll collector, not the rail owner. Nothing here changes that.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Regulatory backdrop (unchanged): MFCs mandated to biometric remote ID from **1 Mar 2026**, MKCs from **1 Mar 2027**; CBR fine-forbearance floated through ~1 Jan 2027; Nov 2026 relaxation to **single-modality (face-only)** ID for MFOs (Kommersant 8991388). The real signals to watch remain a named live MFO integration with disclosed volume/tariff and whether Sravni out-distributes T-Bank into the larger 2027 MKC wave — none delivered by this item.

**+ Why this is stale, not fresh (analysis):** The central question ("who wins MFO distribution and the per-call price war on a toll the state can re-price") is identical to June's. This note adds a forum quote and a one-day-old partnership mention on top of a fully-enriched prior note with the same company, same mechanism, same figures, same spokesperson's product. No new reporting period, no new terms, no "talks → signed deal" delta that bears on the MFO-pricing claim itself.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russia's microfinance market is contracting from its 2024 peak: the total MFO loan portfolio fell to ~₽520bn in 2026, down from ₽623bn at end-2024 (+41% that year), and the number of MFOs dropped ~6.7% y/y to ~842 companies (per Bank of Russia data, via akm.ru / tadviser, as of 2026). Structure: highly fragmented (hundreds of small/regional players) and heavily regulated — the Bank of Russia sets a basic MFO standard, tightened the DSTI calc for small unsecured microloans (<₽50k) from early 2026, and from March 1, 2026 made biometric identification via the Unified Biometric System (EBS) mandatory for online microloans (per Izvestia / Kommersant / Vedomosti, 2026). "Why now": this is a regulatory shock, not a growth story — the biometric mandate is the immediate driver. Enrolment and payment adoption is ramping (biometric samples submitted +4x y/y; Sberbank reports ~900k "Smile to Pay" terminals toward 1m — biometricupdate / idtechwire, 2026), but MFO-side connectivity lagged badly: as of late March 2026 none of the ~27 largest microfinance companies had connected to EBS, and the CB suspended supervisory penalties for failed remote ID until Jan 1, 2027 (plusworld / finance.mail.ru, 2026). Second-order effect: facial-only verification (no voice) is slated to become available for MFO online flows from November 2026 (Vedomosti, 2026) — a direct unblocker for the delegated-identification model this note describes.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs that matter here: connection cost (capex), per-verification tariff (opex), time-to-integrate, and conversion/drop-off in the remote onboarding funnel. The protagonist is Sravni (a financial marketplace / comparison platform), positioning its SaaS "delegated identification" via a financial-platform operator so MFOs connect to EBS without building their own IB infrastructure, claiming a ~2-week integration. Basis of competition is distribution + cost-per-check, not product novelty — EBS is a single state rail, so vendors compete on how cheaply/quickly they intermediate it. Key competing move: T-Bank launched its own biometric-identification service for MFOs (Kommersant, July 2026), and banks/financial-platform operators broadly can act as the delegating counterparty (delegated-ID tariff quoted at ₽4–19 per check per vbr.ru / anti-malware, 2026). Sravni's position: one of several intermediaries catching up to the same regulatory window; moat is thin — any licensed financial-platform operator or large bank can offer the same delegation, so the edge is relationships + onboarding speed, not a structural lock-in (analysis).

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation/round/metrics in the item — this is a regulatory/product item, not a deal. Trading multiples: no data. Internal comps from the base (Russian lending / biometrics / regtech context, grep fallback as sem is down): [[MFO обязаны проверять заемщиков]] not in base; nearest in-base references — [[T-Bank enables money transfers inside Max chats]], [[Russia to require banks disclose digital-team ownership from 2027]], [[SberBank Online adds FSSP travel-ban check service]] (Russian regulatory/bank-rail notes); on biometrics globally [[UnionDigital Bank partners with iProov on authentication]], [[Razorpay and YES Bank launch biometric authentication for payments]], [[NPCI introduces biometric authentication for UPI]]; on microfinance sector dynamics [[India's microfinance sector grows 5.3% in Q4 FY26]]. Cost figures (as data, not multiples): note text cites Sravni's Gamzaev at "up to ₽90m" for direct EBS integration; external sources vary widely — ₽15–30m for IB compliance on direct integration, "up to ₽25m" by other estimates, and "up to ₽100m" all-in (anti-malware / vbr.ru, 2025–26). Figures are not reconcilable; treat the ₽90m as a PR-sourced vendor claim, [UNSOURCED] independently.

**Risk flags.**
1. Regulatory/deadline risk — the whole market opportunity rests on a mandate whose enforcement the CB has already deferred once (to Jan 1, 2027) and whose technical rails (facial-only ID) only go live ~Nov 2026; a further delay or rule change collapses the urgency Sravni is selling into.
2. Commoditization / disintermediation — EBS is a single state platform and delegated ID is priced at ₽4–19/check; with banks (T-Bank) and other financial-platform operators offering the same gateway, margins compress toward the tariff and Sravni risks being bypassed once MFOs integrate directly or via a cheaper partner.
3. Shrinking/attritioning customer base — up to ~25% of MFOs may exit the market over biometrics, and some are reclassifying as microcredit companies to defer the rule (to March 2027); the addressable base of paying MFO clients is both shrinking and actively avoiding the requirement, with a parallel risk of borrowers migrating to illegal lenders (>2m clients cited by Izvestia, 2026).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is a regulation-driven land-grab for a thin intermediation layer, not a re-rating of microfinance itself (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: delegated identification becomes the default MFO onboarding path, and 2–3 operators (a bank like T-Bank + 1–2 marketplaces like Sravni) capture most small/mid MFO connections before the Jan 2027 enforcement date. Watch/trigger: (a) the first disclosed count of MFOs actually live on EBS via delegation after facial-only ID ships in Nov 2026; (b) whether the CB extends the enforcement holiday again. Thesis breaks if direct integration costs fall (making delegation unnecessary) or the mandate is diluted.
