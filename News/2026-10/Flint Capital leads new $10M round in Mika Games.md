Инвестиционный фонд Mika Games, основанный сооснователем Wargaming Николаем Кацелаповым (Nick Katselapov), объявил о привлечении дополнительных 10 млн долларов. Деньги планируется направить на финансирование мобильных студий и их маркетинг.
 
Как сообщает GamesBeat, новый инвестиционный раунд возглавила венчурная компания Flint Capital. В нем, впрочем, участвовали и действующие инвесторы Mika Games.
В фонде объяснили, что одна из главных проблем небольших мобильных студий — отсутствие средств на масштабные маркетинговые тесты: чтобы получить финансирование на привлечение пользователей, разработчикам зачастую требуется доказать эффективность рекламных кампаний, однако это само по себе требует значительных сумм.
По данным Mika Games, специализированные компании, финансирующие закупку трафика, обычно ожидают от разработчиков:
▫️нескольких месяцев успешных рекламных кампаний; 
▫️расходов на маркетинг в размере 300–500 тысяч долларов; 
▫️прогнозируемых сроков окупаемости инвестиций.
Mika Games предлагает альтернативную модель. Фонд инвестирует в сами студии, получая долю в их бизнесе, а также отдельно выделяет средства на тестирование платного привлечения пользователей. Это позволяет разработчикам проверить удержание, монетизацию и окупаемость маркетинга, прежде чем привлекать более крупное финансирование.
Сейчас в портфеле Mika Games находятся пять игровых студий. Среди них — Guli Games, чья Legendale привлекла более 3 млн игроков, и Agonalea Games, разработавшая Punko с более чем 500 тысячами загрузок. В 2026 году фонд также расширил портфель за счет инвестиций в Bulberry Studio (Wargard) и LevelApp (Merge Dale, Sea Bloom).
Напомним, Mika Games начала работу в 2024 году. Тогда фонд также привлек 10 млн долларов при участии Flint Capital и объявил о планах сформировать инвестиционный капитал в размере 50 млн долларов. В апреле 2025 года Mika Games вложила 2 млн долларов в аргентинскую Agonalea Games.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** Mika Games, a Nicosia (Cyprus)-based gaming investment company founded by Wargaming co-founder Nick Katselapov (Nikolai Katselapov), raised a further **$10M** round **led by Boston-based VC Flint Capital** (the fund of managing partner Dmitry Smirnov), with participation from existing/new unnamed investors. Announced ~21–22 Oct 2026. Proceeds fund mobile F2P studios between *soft launch* and large-scale paid user acquisition — equity into the studio plus a separate pool for paid-UA tests to generate retention/payback data before bigger financing. Sources: [app2top.ru](https://app2top.ru/news/mika-games-privlekla-10-mln-dollarov-na-podderzhku-mobil-ny-h-studij-posle-soft-launch-244793.html), [dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/164737-mika-games-raises-10m-to-plug-mobile-gamings-soft-launch-funding-gap/), [therecursive](https://www.therecursive.com/wargaming-co-founders-mika-games-raises-10m-to-fund-mobile-studios-from-soft-launch-to-scale/), [pocketgamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mika-games-raises-10m-to-fund-mobile-game-studios/).

**[1] Who / numbers.** Round: **$10M** (described as a further/additional raise toward Mika's stated **$50M** investment target). Lead: **Flint Capital**. Mika Games founded 2023/2024, ex-Wargaming team. Portfolio grew from 3→5 studios in 2026: **Guli Games** (Legendale, 3M+ players), **Agonalea Games** (Punko tower-defense, 500k+ downloads; Mika put $2M seed in Apr 2025), plus 2026 additions **Bulberry Studio** (Wargard) and **LevelApp** (Merge Dale, Sea Bloom). Specialist UA financiers typically demand several months of campaigns, **$300–500k** of prior marketing spend, and a predictable payback window — the gap Mika targets. Sources: [gamesbeat](https://gamesbeat.com/wargaming-cofounder-launches-mika-games-to-invest-up-to-50m-in-mobile-games/), [spieltimes](https://www.spieltimes.io/gaming/mika-games-raises-usd10m-to-fund-tests-mobile-studios-struggle-to-run/).

**[2] Flint Capital — who they are.** Flint is a **Boston-based VC founded 2013** by Dmitry Smirnov (with Sergey Gribov, Andrew Gershfeld); distributed offices (Boston, Tel Aviv, Berlin, Palo Alto). Historic focus = **cybersecurity, fintech, digital health, enterprise SaaS** (portfolio: Socure ~$4.5B, Flo, WalkMe, CyberX→Microsoft, Voca.ai→Snap). Closed a **$160M** third fund in 2024 (prior vehicles ~$100M each, 70+ active companies). Mobile-gaming investing is **off Flint's usual thesis** — making this Mika re-up notable as a thematic outlier rather than a fintech deal. ## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Mika Games sits at the intersection of mobile-game publishing finance and venture — a "studio-funding fund" for free-to-play (F2P) mobile studios between soft launch and large-scale paid user acquisition (UA). Sector scale: the global mobile-gaming market is put at ~$137bn in 2026 growing to ~$289bn by 2034, ~9.7% CAGR (per Fortune Business Insights, as of 2026); mobile was ~55% of total gaming revenue in 2025. Venture flow into the sub-vertical is thin but resilient — ~$435M across 23 disclosed mobile-gaming deals over Aug-2025→Jul-2026, with a casual-games median round of only ~$4.25M (per newmarketpitch, as of 2026), i.e. a long tail of small rounds. Structure: fragmented on the studio side, but the *financing* layer around it is consolidating fast. Why now: global gaming CPI rose ~30% in 2025 (Europe +47%) — paid UA got expensive enough that studios can no longer self-fund the marketing tests that unlock the next capital round, opening a gap that specialist UA/studio financiers are rushing to fill.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs this model runs on: UA payback / cohort ROAS, retention (D7/D30), CPI, and — for Mika specifically, since it takes equity — portfolio studio count and title traction. Key players and *basis of competition* (price of capital / dilution / speed):
- **Mika Games** (protagonist) — hybrid: early *equity* in the studio + a separate *non-dilutive pool* for UA tests; 5 portfolio studios (Guli Games/Legendale, Agonalea/Punko, Bulberry/Wargard, LevelApp/Merge Dale, Sea Bloom). Positions on founder alignment + hands-on operating help, not just capital.
- **PvX Partners** — pure *non-dilutive* cohort UA financing; >$1.3bn committed to date, Unity partnership announced 2026-10-27 (same day as this news). Competes on scale + zero dilution.
- **Leus Capital** (Türkiye) — UA facilities, ~$20.4M committed to Spektra/Narcade (2026).
Protagonist position: *niche but differentiated* — Mika is one of the few taking equity AND de-risking UA, so it competes earlier in the funnel (pre-scale studios) than the pure financiers. Moat `(analysis)`: founder network (Wargaming pedigree) + operating know-how = intangibles/distribution, not scale — the balance-sheet players (PvX) out-scale it on capital.

**Comps & multiples.** This is a $10M fund top-up, not an operating company with revenue — so trading multiples (EV/Rev, P/E) are **not applicable**; "no data". Comparables are peer *raise sizes* in game-studio financing:
- [[PvX Partners raises $10.5M Series A for non-dilutive capital]] — $10.5M Series A (Apr-2026), UA financing; near-identical headline round size, different model (debt vs equity).
- [[Alinea Invest secures $22.5 million in user acquisition financing]] — $22.5M UA facility (2026), a financing facility not equity.
- [[Appcharge raises $58 million Series B for game commerce]] — $58M Series B (Aug-2025), game-commerce/D2C adjacency.
Read: Mika's $10M is *in-line* with the small end of this cohort (peer median round ~$4-10M), and its stated $50M target fund is modest vs PvX's >$1.3bn committed — i.e. Mika is a boutique, not a scale player. Distribution not computed (mixed instruments — equity fund vs debt facilities make the figures non-comparable); qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Capital-scale disadvantage / disintermediation.** At a $50M target fund (only $10M fresh) Mika is ~1-2 orders below PvX (>$1.3bn) and PvX just plugged into Unity's ad stack (2026-10-27); if non-dilutive UA capital commoditizes (CPI-financing providers cutting rates in 2026), studios may prefer no-dilution money and skip Mika's equity ask — margin captured by the financing layer.
2. **Portfolio concentration / early-stage execution risk.** Only 5 studios, success hinges on a few hits (Legendale's 3M+ players is the one real traction point); F2P economics are power-law, and the equity model amplifies single-title downside.
3. **Disclosure / provenance gaps.** Round participants beyond Flint Capital are undisclosed ("existing investors"), no valuation/terms, and founding-year and founder-name conflict across sources (note: founded 2024 by "Nikolai Katselapov"; web/GamesBeat: 2023, "Nick Katselapov") — treat specifics as soft `[UNSOURCED]`.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This is less a single raise than a signal that *studio-financing is becoming its own fintech sub-vertical* — equity-funds (Mika), cohort-debt (PvX, Leus, Alinea) and D2C/commerce (Appcharge) all racing to monetize the rising-CPI gap. Falsifiable thesis: if gaming CPI keeps climbing, non-dilutive UA financiers out-raise equity-style studio funds like Mika through 2027. Trigger to watch: whether Mika hits its $50M target and whether a portfolio title scales past soft-launch — absent a breakout hit, a $10M top-up at flat terms reads as a bridge, not conviction. What breaks the thesis: CPI deflation or a Mika portfolio hit that validates the equity-plus-UA model.
