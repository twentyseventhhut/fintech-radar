## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🌍MassPay chief executive outlines global money movement infrastructure strategy. Co-founder and CEO Ran Grushkowsky said the company uses multiple payment rails, banking partners and stablecoin networks to support international collections and disbursements. Its payment orchestration infrastructure automatically reroutes failed transactions, with MassPay maintaining 15 US sponsor bank relationships and more than 80 correspondent banking relationships.
