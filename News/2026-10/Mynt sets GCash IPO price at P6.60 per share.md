 MANILA, Philippines — Mynt Inc. on Friday set the final offer price for its initial public offering (IPO) at P6.60 per share, according to a disclosure to the Philippine Stock Exchange.
The final price covers the public offering of 8,027,409,600 common shares, with an overallotment option of up to 1,204,111,400 secondary common shares.
At P6.60 per share, the base offer is valued at about P52.98 billion, while the total could reach about P60.93 billion if the overallotment option is fully exercised.
The proposed IPO remains subject to the conditions set by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the PSE, including the SEC’s issuance of a permit to sell.
Mynt operates GCash, which is set to be listed on the PSE Main Board following the IPO.

# Context-enrichment: Mynt sets GCash IPO price at P6.60 per share
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 4/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On **2026-10-02** Mynt Inc. (operator of GCash) set the **final IPO offer price at P6.60/share** on the PSE Main Board — covering **8,027,409,600 common shares** base, plus an overallotment of **up to 1,204,111,400 secondary shares**. Base offer **~P52.98B (~$843M)**; with full greenshoe **~P60.93B (~$970M, "nearly $1B")**. Scheduled listing: **~2026-10-20**, ticker "GCASH" (not yet occurred as of today 2026-10-08). The June filing had an indicative ceiling of **P10.00/share** sizing the deal up to **~P92.3B**.

**+ Why this framing matters (the de-PR'd reality):**
- **This is a DOWN-SIZED IPO, not a triumphant one.** The final P6.60 is a **~34% cut from the P10.00 ceiling**; analysts had flagged P10 as too high to clear ([TechTimes 2026-09-14](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/327493/20260914/gcash-ipo-prices-october-1-analysts-say-10-peso-ceiling-too-high-clear.htm)). Implied market cap ≈ **~$7.0B** (≈66.9B shares × P6.60 ÷ ~62.7 PHP/USD; cap matches [Reuters/Yahoo "around $7B"](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/gcash-parent-mynt-prices-philippine-172052530.html)) — **below the ~$8B target** from May ([[GCash operator Mynt eyes $8 billion valuation in Philippine IPO]]) and far below the ~$10.7B the ceiling implied. Still above the ~$5B MUFG-round mark (2024).
- **~80% of the deal is a CASH-OUT, not fresh capital.** Base 8.03B shares ≈ **~1.6B primary + ~6.4B secondary**; the entire overallotment is secondary. New money to Mynt ≈ only **~P10.6B (~$169M)**; the rest (~P42B+) goes to **selling shareholders** (Ant's Advanced New Technologies, Bow Wave, Warburg Pincus, Insight Partners + executives incl. CEO Sazon). The PR anchors to the big "largest IPO" top-line; the growth-capital story underneath is small.
- **The "largest-ever" claim is conditional.** Full deal (P60.93B) only tops the **Monde Nissin record of P55.89B (incl. greenshoe, 2021)** *if the overallotment is fully exercised*; at base size (P52.98B) it is **#2**, below the record. (Note: the lower ceiling was partly enabled by the SEC's Feb-2026 float relief — large issuers can list as little as 12% free float, letting holders avoid dilution.)

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Maya (PLDT/Voyager/KKR)** — the direct domestic rival. GCash leads ~2:1 (GCash ~94M registered / ~40M MAU vs Maya ~50M). Maya has a **BSP digital-bank licence** and hit first-full-year profitability in 2025; its own IPO is reportedly slipping to **2027** (PSE, earlier Nasdaq dual-listing talk), valuation est. $2–3.5B — all **open**. Strategy contrast persists: GCash bets on the domestic PSE; Maya historically eyed deeper US liquidity ([[Ant-backed GCash operator Mynt prepares Philippine IPO filing]]).
- **SE Asia / EM fintech IPO track record — brutal, and that is the real comp:** GoTo (Indonesia) **~-90%** from its 2022 IPO, hit record low after IDX removed the price floor (Oct 2026), dropped from MSCI Indonesia; Grab **~-65/70%** vs debut; Paytm (India) fell **~-80%** post-IPO. The lone winner is **Nubank (~+74%)** — profitable, large domestic market.

**+ Why the lay of the land is this way (2nd order):** the thin-float Asian super-apps that listed into shallow EM exchanges cratered; the profitable large-domestic-market name (Nubank) rewarded. GCash sits in between — **genuinely profitable** (unlike GoTo/Grab at IPO) but listing into the **thinnest of these exchanges** with a tiny float. The P6.60 down-pricing is the market already applying the GoTo/Grab discount to the thin-liquidity risk.

## [2] Company history / fit
Mynt: founded 2015 (Globe + Ayala + Ant). Valuation ladder: ~$2B (2021) → **$5B (2024, MUFG/Ayala)** → **~$7B IPO (2026)**. The process: considered 2025 → **delayed to H2 2026** ([[GCash delays $1.5B Philippine IPO to second half of 2026]]) → **filing authorized/filed June 2026** ([[Ant-backed GCash operator Mynt prepares Philippine IPO filing]]) → **PSE approval 2026-09-19** → **final pricing 2026-10-02**. GCash is profitable: adjusted revenue ~P79.7B and net income ~P17.2B in 2025 (per press; standalone Mynt consolidation still **open** — most public figures are Globe's equity share).

**+ Why the company acts this way:** Mynt waited out 2025 and lobbied for the SEC float cut precisely to list at a premium with minimal dilution — the structure (mostly secondary, 12%-ish float) is engineered to **let early backers (Ant, Warburg, Bow Wave) realize gains** without flooding the thin PSE. The down-pricing shows the market pushed back on that premium ambition.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Freshness:** This note is the **PRICING** event — it adds **genuinely new, hard terms** (final P6.60, exact share counts, P52.98B/P60.93B proceeds, ~$7B implied cap, listing date, cornerstones) that did **not** exist in the June filing note. It is a **fresh follow-up**, not a reprint. Traction is real and citable: profitability, ~94M users, ~2:1 lead over Maya, and a bookbuild reportedly multiple-times oversubscribed with **20+ cornerstone investors** (~P36.5B committed: BlackRock, Capital Group, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, Schroders, IFC (P4.4B disclosed), HSBC GAM, plus domestic funds).

**+ Why the value-add is real but capped:** the cornerstone book and oversubscription are genuine demand signals — but the **down-pricing from P10 to P6.60 is the counter-signal**: demand existed only at a ~30% lower valuation. And because ~80% of proceeds cash out existing holders, the listing funds **shareholder exits, not GCash growth** — so the post-IPO equity story rests on existing profitability, not new war-chest firepower.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Next: SEC permit-to-sell → offer period (~Oct 6–12) → **listing ~2026-10-20**. The listing-day print is the real test, into a PSE that hit a 2026 low around Oct 1 with foreigners net sellers. Risks flagged: **thin PSE liquidity** (direct GoTo-style floor/float risk); **Ant Group / China exposure** (National Intelligence / Data Security laws over Ant's ~32% stake; Ant's own $37B IPO was killed in 2020); **BSP/SEC regulatory** pressure (gambling-access curbs on e-wallets given GCash's ~94% share); and valuation-vs-fundamentals (though profitability is the strong card; implied **P/E ~25x** is analysis, not a quoted figure).

**+ Why the market may go this way (2nd order):** the ~34% price cut is a **de-risking**, not a failure — it lowers the bar for a positive debut and leaves upside if GCash proves it is the "Nubank, not the GoTo" of SE Asia. But the thin float that protected insiders from dilution is the same thin float that makes the stock **fragile and manipulable** post-listing — a successful pricing can still become a volatile, illiquid aftermarket.

## Earnings Review

<!-- enrichment:earnings_review -->
**Verdict (headline read).** IN-LINE (IPO pricing, not a quarterly print) — this is the final IPO price-setting on prospectus/audited financials, which counts as results data. Mynt priced at **P6.60/share** (34% below the P10 max in the preliminary prospectus), gross base offer **~P53.0bn** (8,027,409,600 shares), up to **~P60.9bn** with the 1,204,111,400-share overallotment — the largest IPO in Philippine history. Implied market cap **~P442bn** (~$7-7.7bn), at **~25.6x 2025 earnings**. Underlying business genuinely strong (2025 revenue +47% YoY, NI +55% YoY); the "miss" is purely on valuation vs the earlier P10 ask, i.e. the market repriced the offer down, not a deterioration in fundamentals. Listing on PSE Main Board under ticker "GCASH", expected 2026-10-20.

**Key figures (with growth) — from the prospectus/audited financials.**
- FY2025 revenue **P79.7-79.8bn** (+47.3% YoY vs P54.1bn in 2024).
- FY2025 net income **P17.2-17.25bn** (+55.0% YoY vs P11.13bn in 2024); implied net margin ~21.6%.
- H1 2026 revenue **P43.27bn (+10.4% YoY)**; H1 2026 net income **P10.82bn (+7.3% YoY)** — note the sharp growth deceleration vs FY2025's +47%/+55%, consistent with the 2025 gambling/e-gaming link removal washing out of the base.
- Q1 2026 (per prospectus): revenue **P20.74bn**, net income **P5.6bn (+23.9% YoY vs P4.52bn)** — so growth front-loaded in Q1, implying a softer Q2.
- Morgan Stanley estimate (analysis, public, not company guidance): 2026 NI **~P21.2bn**, ~22.6% net margin, ROE ~28%.

**Operating metrics backing the valuation (as of 2026-03-31 unless noted).**
- **40.4mn monthly active users** (~55% of Philippine adults); ~41.5mn MAU as of June 2026.
- **62.4mn daily average transactions**.
- Payments **GTV P17.03tn in FY2025**; **P4.75tn in Q1 2026 alone, +23.2% YoY** — TPV momentum far outpaces revenue growth (take-rate compression / mix).
- Distribution: **2.1mn QR merchants**, 1.3mn cash-in/out locations.
- Financial-services cross-sell: **7.5mn active borrowers**, 16.9mn savings users, 9.0mn investment-fund users, 1.9mn equities users.

**By segment / driver.** FY2025 explosive growth was flattered by e-gaming/gambling-linked payment volume; the BSP 2025 directive forcing removal of gambling links from payment apps dragged H2-2025 profit to ~P7.1bn and is the primary reason H1-2026 growth collapsed to +10%/+7%. Forward growth is management-flagged to lean on **CreditTech (lending)** — so loan-book quality becomes the key swing metric, not payments take-rate.

**vs expectations / prior period.** vs the preliminary prospectus ask of P10 → final P6.60 is a **-34%** cut, a clear valuation miss / weak-to-moderate institutional pull on price (though book was oversubscribed on volume). No formal sell-side consensus EPS exists pre-listing [UNSOURCED for a Street number]; using the public multiple, 25.6x trailing P/E is a premium to the PSE but discounted vs the ~P669bn/$11bn implied at the P10 max. YoY momentum decelerating hard (FY25 +47%/+55% → H1-26 +10.4%/+7.3%). No prior earnings note on GCash exists in the corpus (IR DB holds only PRs/product/ESG items, no financial statements), so no internal `[[wikilink]]` trajectory available.

**Guidance / forward.** No company guidance issued (IPO quiet-period norms). Only third-party estimate: Morgan Stanley ~P21.2bn 2026 NI (+~23% vs 2025) at ~28% ROE (analysis). Plausibility: requires re-acceleration in H2-2026 after a soft H1 — hinges on CreditTech scaling cleanly. Management (CFO Evelyn Ng) tone confident on "depth and quality of demand" and a "diversified, high-quality shareholder base"; notably silent on the gambling-revenue hole and on take-rate compression (TPV +23% vs revenue +10%).

**Thesis-flags.**
1. **Growth cliff post-gambling ban** — fact: H1-26 revenue +10.4% / NI +7.3% vs FY25 +47%/+55%. Why: BSP forced removal of e-gaming links (2025); that volume was high-margin. Why it matters: the IPO is priced ~25.6x on inflated 2025 earnings, so the comp is a hard base. Second-order: if 2026 lands near MS's +23% NI it de-risks the multiple; if H2 stays at H1's ~7% pace, 25.6x looks rich.
2. **Take-rate compression** — TPV +23.2% YoY in Q1-26 but revenue only +10% in H1 → GCash is monetizing each peso of volume less. Second-order: pressures the "payments flywheel" thesis and shifts the burden onto lending.
3. **Secondary-heavy offer** — ~80% of the base offer is existing shares; only ~P14.9bn net proceeds reach Mynt. Why it matters: dilution-adjusted, this is largely an exit for Globe/Ant/existing holders, not growth capital. Second-order: weak alignment signal; post-lockup overhang risk.
4. **CreditTech dependency / regulatory beta** — forward growth leans on 7.5mn borrowers scaling; loan-quality and further BSP action are the key risks. Second-order: a credit-cost cycle would hit exactly the segment the bull case now rests on.
