LONDON, October 26, 2026 — Broadridge (NYSE: BR) today announced the appointment of Ervin Zaljević as Director, Regulatory Solutions, based in Luxembourg. In this role, Ervin will strengthen Broadridge's engagement with Luxembourg's investment funds and asset management community, bringing local market expertise to the company's global regulatory and technology capabilities.
Ervin will focus on strengthening relationships across the local funds ecosystem, combining regulatory expertise, market intelligence, industry engagement and strategic client support. His appointment reflects Broadridge's continued engagement with Luxembourg, a leading international investment fund centre, and its commitment to supporting clients navigating an increasingly complex regulatory environment.
“I'm delighted to join Broadridge and bring my experience across asset management, fund servicing and legal practice to its work with Luxembourg's financial services community,” said Ervin. “I look forward to connecting local market insights with Broadridge's global capabilities to help clients navigate regulatory complexity, strengthen operational efficiency and respond to change. I also look forward to contributing to industry dialogue and knowledge sharing across Luxembourg's funds ecosystem.”
“Luxembourg plays an important role in the global funds industry, and understanding the market’s regulatory environment and evolving client needs is essential to serving it effectively,” said Stephen Johnston, head of Broadridge’s Regulatory Communications EU/UK business. “Ervin brings a valuable combination of legal, fund servicing and regulatory expertise that will deepen our engagement with the local industry and help us connect clients with Broadridge’s global capabilities as the market continues to evolve.”
Ervin previously served as Head of Legal at BlackRock Luxembourg and as a board member of numerous investment funds and corporate entities. He also headed up the Fund Corporate Services team at RBC Investor Services Bank and practised as a corporate and investment funds lawyer at leading Luxembourg law firms. His background brings together legal, governance and fund servicing experience, alongside a deep understanding of Luxembourg's investment funds ecosystem.
