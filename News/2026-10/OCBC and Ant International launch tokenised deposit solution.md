## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
### [0] What happened (one-paragraph)
On **7 October 2026**, **OCBC** launched a **tokenised deposit solution** with **Ant International** for **always-on (24/7) corporate treasury management** between **Singapore and Malaysia**. The service lets Ant International mobilise **SGD- and USD-denominated tokenised deposits** for near real-time intra-group liquidity transfers outside traditional banking hours. It is delivered via Ant's **WhaleRTP** platform (blockchain-based wholesale settlement). OCBC positions itself as the **first bank** to collaborate with Ant International on this specific tokenised-deposit solution. Status: **live/launched** (not a pilot). Primary: Ant International newsroom + OCBC media release; corroborated by Fintech News SG/MY, Finadium, finews.asia. ([ocbc.com](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-tokenised-deposit-ant-international-treasury.page), [fintechnews.sg](https://fintechnews.sg/138687/blockchain/ocbc-ant-international-tokenised-deposits/))

### [3] Numbers / specifics

- Platform: **WhaleRTP** (Ant's blockchain wholesale settlement / "Whale" treasury platform).
- First corporate user: **Ant International itself** (intra-group liquidity across its own entities).

### [4] Why it matters / competitive read
Tokenised deposits are becoming the de-facto cross-border treasury rail in Asia, with **Ant International as the common hub** orchestrating a bank-by-bank roster (HSBC → UBS → Standard Chartered → Amundi/CACEIS → now OCBC). For OCBC, this is one plank of a multi-track tokenisation strategy (GOLDX gold fund Apr 2026; Swift-ledger deposits with Citi Sep 2026; $1bn blockchain commercial paper 2025). The "first bank to collaborate with Ant on this solution" claim is narrow — it refers to this specific SG–MY WhaleRTP product, not tokenised deposits broadly (HSBC/StanChart preceded OCBC with Ant). Key open question is whether Ant's "launch" announcements convert to third-party corporate adoption beyond Ant's own group liquidity.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Tokenised deposits = digital representations of commercial-bank deposits on DLT, backed 1:1 on the bank's balance sheet (distinct from off-balance-sheet stablecoins). Market sizing is a wide forecast, not a current figure: per Citi Institute (Tokenization 2030 report, Jun 2026, via CoinDesk/Citigroup), RWA tokenisation grows from ~$17bn today to a base-case $5.5tn by 2030 (range $2.7tn–$8.2tn); separately Citi projects tokenised bank deposits could support $100–140tn in annual *flows* by 2030, potentially rivalling stablecoins (base-case circulation $1.9tn) — a flow vs stock comparison, treat as directional, not a TAM. Structure: supply side is consolidated among large banks (capital + regulatory licence + correspondent network are the barriers); the orchestration layer (here Ant's WhaleRTP) is where interoperability value concentrates. Why now: (1) regulatory clarity on stablecoins (US GENIUS Act era) pushing banks to defend deposit franchises with on-balance-sheet tokens; (2) 24/7 settlement demand from corporates with cross-border intra-group liquidity. Why-ladder: banks fear deposit flight to stablecoins → they tokenise deposits to keep money on balance sheet → but a non-bank orchestrator (Ant) sits across all of them → value may migrate to the coordination layer, not any single bank.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: tokenised transfer volume/day, number of bank partners & currencies on the network, settlement latency, corridors live. Ant International's WhaleRTP is reported (per Ant/press, via fintechnews.sg & Ledger Insights, Oct 2026) to be supported by 20 banks globally across 17+ currencies (HK, Singapore, Europe, US). Recent peer moves with dates: Standard Chartered launched tokenised deposits for Ant's HKD/CNH/SGD/USD accounts (Dec 2025); HSBC launched TDS in Hong Kong with Ant as first client (Sep 2025), completed a Swift/ISO-20022 PoC with Ant (Dec 2025), and expanded real-time treasury to the Middle East (Oct 2026); OCBC completed SE-Asia's first cross-border USD tokenised-deposit txn on Swift's ledger with Citibank (Sep 2026, per fintechnews.sg). Protagonist position: OCBC is a *fast follower / catching up* — it is the first bank to do tokenised deposits with Ant on the Singapore–Malaysia corridor specifically, but HSBC and StanChart were already on Whale earlier. Ant International is *ahead* as the multi-bank demand aggregator; its moat is network/orchestration (one treasury platform spanning 20 banks → switching costs for both banks and Ant's own entities) `(analysis)`.

**Comps & multiples.** No deal size, valuation, or round attaches to this product launch → trading/round multiples not applicable; "no data" on multiples. Internal comps (same Ant-International tokenised-deposit theme, base corpus): [[HSBC launches tokenized deposit service with Ant International]] (Sep 2025), [[Standard Chartered rolls out tokenized deposits on Ant's Whale]] (Dec 2025), [[Ant International, HSBC and Swift complete tokenized deposits pilot]] (Dec 2025), [[Fintech Wrap Up tokenized deposits versus stablecoins guide]] (Apr 2026, the stablecoin-vs-deposit-token distinction). These establish OCBC as at least the 3rd named global bank onto Ant's Whale rails within ~13 months — a pattern of Ant signing banks serially rather than one exclusive partner. EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E = `[UNSOURCED]` / not meaningful for a feature launch.

**Risk flags.**
1. Disintermediation of the bank by the orchestration layer: Ant's WhaleRTP sits across 20 banks; the client relationship and interoperability value accrue to Ant, while OCBC supplies balance-sheet and a commoditised token — margin risk if banks become interchangeable rails.
2. Concentration / captive demand: the launch volume is Ant International's own intra-group treasury (Singapore–Malaysia), i.e. a single anchor client. "Live with one captive counterparty" is not the same as third-party adoption — de-PR: the release is silent on external corporate users, pricing/fees, and transaction economics.
3. Standards/network fragmentation: multiple parallel bank-specific deposit tokens (JPMorgan Kinexys, Citi Token Services, HSBC TDS, the planned US Clearing House shared token in 2027) risk non-interoperable silos; cross-bank, cross-corridor settlement (vs intra-group) remains the unproven hard part.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` Signals the Singapore–Malaysia corridor becoming a live tokenised-deposit lane and reinforces Ant International as SE-Asia's default multi-bank treasury-tokenisation hub — a land-grab, not a re-rating. Falsifiable thesis: Ant keeps adding named bank partners and currencies on Whale through 2026–27 (watch for a 4th/5th SE-Asia bank and non-Ant corporate clients). What breaks it: if the solution stays confined to Ant's own entities with no third-party corporate adoption disclosed within ~2 quarters, it is a captive-treasury tool, not a network, and banks retain the economics.
