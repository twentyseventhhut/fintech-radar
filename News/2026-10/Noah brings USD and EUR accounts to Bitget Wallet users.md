LONDON, Oct. 27, 2026 - Noah, the stablecoin payments infrastructure provider, today announced it is enabling virtual fiat currency accounts for Bitget Wallet’s 100 million users worldwide. This will enable the users to seamlessly receive funds in US Dollars and Euros into their self-custodial wallets using Noah’s regulatory compliant stablecoin payments infrastructure. The launch represents a significant milestone in the integration of traditional financial infrastructure with stablecoin-native payments, expanding access to global financial services for users across both developed and emerging markets.
How It Works
Through Noah's regulated infrastructure, users are provided with local USD and EUR virtual accounts that can be used to receive funds through ACH, Fedwire, SEPA and SWIFT networks. Incoming deposits can be seamlessly converted into stablecoins and accessed directly within Bitget Wallet, creating a bridge between conventional banking systems and digital asset ecosystems.
Why Now
The launch comes as financial institutions, payment providers and digital asset platforms increasingly adopt stablecoins as a new settlement layer for cross-border transactions. By combining local banking access with stablecoin settlement, the service reduces friction associated with international payments while expanding participation in the global financial system.
“For far too long, opening a dollar or euro account has been out of reach for billions of people around the world. In countries where high inflation erodes the value of local currencies, people often turn to the dollar and euro as a more stable way to save, yet access remains limited. With this integration we have, in one step, removed the obstacles to receiving these currencies and holding their value in digital dollars. This is the democratization of global finance. Stablecoins now let money move at the speed of the internet, with the compliance, transparency and reliability that regulated financial systems demand. We’re seeing a fundamental shift away from legacy correspondent banking toward programmable, real-time payment networks. Bitget Wallet is bringing that future to more than 100 million people, and any wallet or fintech can now do the same with Noah.” Shah Ramezani, Founder and CEO of Noah.
The Infrastructure Behind It
Noah provides the compliance, treasury, foreign exchange and settlement infrastructure underpinning the service. The company's stablecoin network supports compliant money movement across more than 60 currencies and over 150 markets, enabling customers to launch international payment services through a single regulated provider rather than navigating fragmented local banking relationships.
Ongoing Momentum
The announcement follows a period of significant momentum for Noah. Earlier this month, Western Union selected Noah to power its global stablecoin payments network. Noah also recently secured both a MiCA CASP licence and a PSD2 Payment Institution licence, allowing it to provide regulated stablecoin and fiat payment services across all 27 European Union member states under a single regulatory framework. The company recently closed a $38 million seed funding round to accelerate the expansion of its global payments infrastructure and regulatory footprint.
What Comes Next
Industry adoption of stablecoins is increasingly moving beyond crypto-native use cases into mainstream payments, treasury, payroll, remittances and international commerce.The collaboration with Bitget Wallet represents another step toward a future in which access to global currencies, payments and financial services is delivered directly through digital wallets, regardless of geography or banking status.
