# Ping An Digital Bank Completes HKMA’s Second GenA.I. Sandbox Cohort

ByMedia OutReach Newswire

#### _Strengthening Deepfake Fraud Detection, Unlocking Potential of GenA.I._

HONG KONG SAR – Media OutReach Newswire – 15 October 2026 – Ping An Digital Bank (International) Limited (“Ping An Digital Bank” / “PingAnDB”) has successfully completed the “Anti-Fraud Strategy Platform” use case as part of the second cohort of the Hong Kong Monetary Authority’s (“HKMA”) Generative Artificial Intelligence (“GenA.I.”) Sandbox, in partnership with Hong Kong Cyberport Management Company Limited. Through the sandbox, Ping An Digital Bank further strengthened its capabilities in detecting deepfake-related fraud, and explored the potential of an “A.I. vs A.I.” defense strategy, sharpening its fraud detection accuracy and enhancing customer protection.

**Mr. Ronald Iu, Chief Executive of Ping An Digital Bank**, said, “Ping An Digital Bank is honoured to participate in the HKMA’s second GenA.I. Sandbox cohort. The sandbox serves as an invaluable testing ground for banks to explore financial technology. Throughout the process, we received valuable guidance from the HKMA and shared practical experience with industry peers, advancing the development of anti-fraud efforts across the sector. This project successfully validated GenAI’s potential in deepfake detection, providing deeper insights into how AI identifies forged or synthetic faces across various scenarios. Sandbox testing results demonstrated a detection accuracy of over 96%. We believe the experience gained will help strengthen the industry’s ability to counter deepfake fraud, and build a more robust defence mechanism.”

Developed in partnership with OneConnect Financial Technology, the Anti-Fraud Strategy Platform leverages the proprietary technology of Ping An Group and OneConnect. The project focused on evaluating the platform’s accuracy in detecting deepfake technologies. By integrating cutting-edge A.I. facial recognition into the identity verification process, the platform verifies user selfies in real time to flag suspected forged or manipulated faces. This capability empowers teams to proactively monitor and prevent fraud, elevating the Bank’s overall risk management framework.

Looking ahead, Ping An Digital Bank will continue to leverage the technological edge of Ping An Group, increase its investment in A.I., and deepen the application of A.I. in daily operation, such as risk control and product innovation. The Bank remains dedicated to delivering a seamless, secure, and intuitive banking experience, striving to become the “user-friendly digital bank” in the mind of every customer.

# Context-enrichment: Ping An Digital Bank completes HKMA second GenA.I. Sandbox cohort
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
- On 15 Oct 2026 Ping An Digital Bank (International) Ltd ("PingAnDB", ex-PAObank, rebranded March 2026; wholly owned by Lufax, SEHK:6623/NYSE:LU; part of Ping An Group) announced it **completed** one use case — the "Anti-Fraud Strategy Platform" — in the **second cohort** of the HKMA's GenA.I. Sandbox, run with Cyberport. Tech partner: **OneConnect Financial Technology** (also a Ping An/Lufax-affiliated entity).
- What was actually tested (de-PR'd): AI facial recognition that checks a user selfie in real time at onboarding/identity-verification to flag forged or synthetically generated (deepfake) faces. Reported metrics: **>96% deepfake detection accuracy**, and (per Ping An Group/PR-Newswire release) **99% success rate defending against deepfake threats**, mapped to the "Anti-Deepfake" category of HKMA's e-banking security "ABCD" framework.
- Status = **completed sandbox trial, NOT a production rollout with adoption data.** No live fraud-loss reduction, no customer-count or false-positive-rate disclosure. "96%/99% in a controlled sandbox" ≠ production performance against adaptive attackers. The CE quote ("explored the potential", "will help strengthen the industry's ability") is forward-looking PR, not a results statement.
- **Why framed this way:** the item is a vendor-issued Media OutReach / PR-Newswire newswire (macaubusiness is a syndication, not original reporting). The anchor metric (96%) is a single sandbox figure with no baseline, no attack taxonomy, no dataset size. It is a credibility/marketing event for PingAnDB + OneConnect, timed to the HKMA's own 15 Oct 2026 cohort-completion communications — i.e. riding a regulator milestone. The "A.I. vs A.I." framing matches HKMA's own cohort theme (see [1]).

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **The sandbox itself:** second cohort = **27 use cases from 20 banks + 14 tech partners** (vs first cohort Dec 2024: 15 use cases, 10 banks, 4 partners — BOC, CITIC Intl, CCB, Citi, Dah Sing, Hang Seng, HSBC, Livi, SocGen, Standard Chartered; partners Aereve, Alibaba Cloud, Baidu, FORMS HK). PingAnDB is **one of ~20** participants, not the headline. The cohort theme was "AI vs AI" / adversarial deepfake defence — so PingAnDB's use case is squarely on-trend, not differentiated.
- **HK virtual-bank peers** (8 licensees: ZA Bank, EleBank ex-Airstar [Futu took control, renamed Apr 2026], WeLab, Livi, Mox, Ant Bank, PingAnDB ex-PAObank, Fusion). On profitability PingAnDB trails: ZA Bank H1 2026 net profit +44% YoY (~HK$70.6M, >1M users — see [[ZA Bank grows net profit 44% YoY in H1 2026]]); Mox ~HK$8.6M PBT; WeLab ~HK$14.6M adj. profit. PingAnDB discloses no comparable profit/user figure here — only a deposit base (~HKD 12bn / $1.5bn, March 2026 — see [[Ping An Digital Bank reports HKD 12bn in retail deposits]]).
- **Prior art on deepfake KYC:** real-time selfie anti-deepfake liveness is a crowded incumbent space (Onfido/Entrust, iProov, Jumio, FaceTec, Sumsub) already in production at banks globally. OneConnect's platform is parity-at-best on a mature capability; the novelty is the HKMA sandbox validation, not the tech.
- **Why the lay of the land is this way (analysis):** HKMA uses the sandbox to let banks de-risk GenAI adoption under supervision and to publish "practical insights" sector-wide — so a single bank's "96%" is a shared regulatory-learning datapoint, not a competitive moat. Being 1 of 20 means the marketing value decays fast.

## [2] Company history / fit
- PingAnDB granted HK banking licence May 2019 (as PAObank); rebranded to Ping An Digital Bank March 2026; HKD 12bn retail deposits reported March 2026 ([[Ping An Digital Bank reports HKD 12bn in retail deposits]]); Oct 2026 pushed up-market into private funds with JPMorgan AM + Aberdeen ([[Ping An Digital Bank enters Hong Kong private funds market]]); capital top-ups from Lufax (e.g. HK$200M, Aug 2025 — [[PAObank secures HK$200 million from Lufax]]).
- Fit: **logical.** Ping An's entire pitch is "tech-led finance"; OneConnect is its fintech-export arm. An HKMA AI-sandbox win is cheap, on-brand validation for a sub-scale virtual bank trying to signal differentiation vs more profitable peers.
- **Why (analysis):** as a loss-light but not clearly profitable neobank with a modest deposit base, PingAnDB competes on the Ping An tech narrative rather than price or scale → regulator-endorsed AI milestones + up-market wealth moves (private funds) are both plays to escape the thin-margin mass-retail virtual-bank trap.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novelty: low.** The capability (real-time anti-deepfake face verification) pre-exists commercially; the sandbox completion is a validation/PR event, not a new product launch. No disclosed production deployment, no fraud-loss or false-accept/false-reject data, no customer impact numbers → **traction = none disclosed**, only a controlled-environment accuracy figure.
- **Where the value/margin sits (analysis):** the defensible asset would be production fraud-loss reduction + the Ping An/OneConnect data and model edge — none of which is evidenced here. The "96%/99%" numbers are unaudited, baseline-free sandbox outputs; against adaptive deepfake attackers, static sandbox accuracy is the weakest form of proof.
- Honest read: this is incremental, PR-forward compliance/innovation signalling. Real weight would come only if PingAnDB later ships this into live onboarding and reports fraud-loss deltas.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- Plans (PR): "continue to increase AI investment", deepen AI in risk control and product innovation — all aspirational. HKMA will publish cross-cohort "practical insights", and has launched "GenA.I. Sandbox++" (first cohort Aug 2026) — i.e. the programme is expanding; deepfake/AI-governance will stay a regulatory theme under Fintech 2030 ([[Hong Kong rolls out Fintech 2030 blueprint]], which explicitly expands the GenAI sandbox for fraud detection).
- Regulatory backdrop (analysis): HK is leaning hard into supervised GenAI (sandbox, Project Noor model-auditing) — favourable for banks that participate, but it also **commoditizes** anti-deepfake as a baseline expectation rather than a differentiator. Second-order: once the regulator blesses and shares the pattern sector-wide, PingAnDB's first-mover marketing value erodes and the real contest reverts to profitability/scale, where it trails ZA Bank.
- Risk: sandbox metrics not holding in production; deepfake attackers adapt faster than static validation; no disclosed economics.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector context.** Ping An Digital Bank (PingAnDB, licensed 2019; a Lufax/Ping An Group subsidiary) is one of Hong Kong's eight licensed virtual/digital banks. The cohort sits inside HKMA's second GenA.I. Sandbox (run with Cyberport), spanning 27 use cases from 20 banks and 14 tech partners — including Ant Bank (HK), BOCHK, HSBC, Standard Chartered (HK), Livi and PAO Bank — clustered on customer experience, risk management and anti-fraud. A defining theme is the "A.I. vs A.I." posture (using AI to defend against AI-generated attacks), exactly the frame PingAnDB and OneConnect used for deepfake facial-auth fraud ([HKMA press release, 2026-10-15](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202510/15/P2025101500258.htm); [HKMA annex of use cases](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/media/eng/doc/key-information/press-release/2025/20251015e4a1.pdf)).

**Comparables.** Deepfake/facial-auth defence is a shared cohort priority — "fortified defence against fraudulent facial authentication" and "cross-sector fraud detection" appear across multiple participants, so PingAnDB's >96% detection claim is a sandbox test result rather than a differentiated moat versus peers. On commercial scale, PingAnDB remains sub-scale against HK's profitable digital banks: Mox turned its first interim profit (HK$8.63M PBT) in 1H 2026 on ~800k customers and HKD 20.9bn deposits; ZA Bank posted HK$70.6M net profit (+44% YoY) with HKD 22.2bn deposits; WeLab ~HK$14.6M adjusted profit (1H 2026). Sector-wide, the eight virtual banks serve 3M+ customers with combined deposits up ~42% YoY, and 2025 aggregate losses narrowed to ~US$192M ([Hubbis](https://www.hubbis.com/news/hong-kong-s-digital-banks-to-expand-wealth-services-following-deposit-surge); [TABInsights](https://tabinsights.com/article/hong-kong-digital-banks-losses-narrowed-to-192-million-in-2025); [FF News / Mox](https://ffnews.com/news/press-release-mox-marks-six-years-in-hong-kong-sustains-momentum-beyond-breakeve-878a2472)).
