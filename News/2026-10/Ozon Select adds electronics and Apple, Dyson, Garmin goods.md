# Context-enrichment: Ozon Select adds electronics and Apple, Dyson, Garmin goods
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On 2026-10-07 Ozon told CNews it expanded the assortment of "Ozon Select" — its standalone curated app for mid-to-premium "original brands" — into four new category clusters: Sport, Electronics, Children's goods and "18+". Named brands now present: Apple, Garmin, Dyson, Smeg, sporting goods Wilson and Head, kids' brands Bugaboo and Cybex.
- **De-PR'd reality:** This is a *category expansion of an existing service*, not a launch. Ozon Select itself launched ~Dec 2025 (first app release 2026-12-01; announced Oct 2025 per Vedomosti). So the real event is "Select widens from fashion/beauty/home into electronics + sport + kids," ~9–10 months after go-live.
- **The stated traction numbers (treat as vendor-supplied, unaudited):** 11M+ downloads in 9 months; 3,000+ brands onboarded; monthly customer count +~⅓ and orders +40% through 2026; 5M+ SKUs across 14 categories; delivery via 80,000+ Ozon pickup points. Director: Iloanga Ershova.
- **Why structured this way:** Select is a *curation/positioning play* layered on Ozon's existing logistics (same 80k PVZ). The "hand-picked, brand-rights-verified, quality-checked sellers" framing is the actual product — a trust/authenticity wrapper to escape the counterfeit/grey-import reputation of mass marketplaces. Adding Apple/Dyson/Garmin is a signal that "premium curation" now explicitly means *electronics authenticity*, the category most plagued by fakes and grey imports on RU marketplaces.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Wildberries** — market leader, ~45–47% of RU e-commerce GMV, mass-market (avg order ~720₽), ~3.8M orders/day. No comparable curated-premium sub-brand; its edge is price/scale, the opposite pole from Select.
- **Yandex Market** — explicitly positioned mid-to-high-end, cited ~32% of the mid/high segment; the most direct strategic rival for Select's target shopper. See [[Yandex Market closes ₽15K seller-referral program]], [[Yandex NewPay added to Russian installment-services registry]].
- **Ozon core marketplace** — Select is partly cannibalistic/complementary: it re-packages Ozon Fashion Lux SKUs plus new premium inventory into a separate app.
- **Why the lay of the land is this way (analysis):** With Wildberries+Ozon ~77% of RU e-commerce by end-2026 and YoY growth *slowing* as consumers cut non-food spend, the land-grab phase is ending; the next margin lever is *mix up-trading* — pushing higher-ticket, higher-margin premium baskets. H1 2025 demand for >35,000₽ items reportedly rose ~74%, the stated trigger. Select is Ozon's bet to defend the high-ticket shopper against Yandex Market rather than fight Wildberries on price.

## [2] Company history / fit
Ozon (NASDAQ/MOEX-listed; AFK Sistema a key owner) has aggressively stacked fintech/financial lines on top of the marketplace: Ozon Bank (now top-ranked on RU customer experience per WCIOM — [[Финтехно Ozon Bank tops banks on customer experience]]), a digital-financial-assets platform registered with CBR ([[Ozon Bank registers digital financial assets platform with CBR]]), a debut retail DFA placement ([[Ozon raises 1bn rubles in debut retail digital-asset placement]]), ATM doubling ([[Ozon Bank to double ATM network to 2,000 by end-2026]]), and a brokerage arm Ozon Investments ([[Ozon to launch brokerage arm Ozon Investments in autumn 2026]]). Financially, owner AFK Sistema pledged a large Ozon stake to VTB to raise ~320bn₽ and cut debt ([[VTB takes large Ozon share stake as collateral]]).
- **Why Ozon acts this way (analysis):** A commodity marketplace take-rate caps the multiple; Ozon's whole 2025–26 arc is bolting higher-margin, stickier layers (banking, brokerage, DFA, premium curation) onto the GMV engine. Select is the *retail* version of that same logic — trade mass volume for premium margin + loyalty — and it cross-sells the fintech stack (Ozon Bank credit/installments against higher-ticket baskets).

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Really new?** Minimal. The *app and curation concept* are ~10 months old; this is incremental category breadth. "Electronics on a curated RU platform" is not novel per se (every marketplace sells Apple/Dyson) — the only differentiator is the *authenticity/brand-rights verification* promise.
- **Traction:** 11M downloads / 3,000 brands / 5M SKUs are the only figures, all vendor-supplied with no GMV, revenue, or repeat-purchase disclosure. +40% orders and +⅓ customers are percentage-on-unknown-base — classic flattering-base PR.
- **Who captures the margin (analysis):** For high-ticket electronics (Apple/Dyson/Garmin), category margins are thin and brand-controlled; the value-add Ozon can actually monetize is *trust + financing* (installments/BNPL via Ozon Bank) and logistics, not product margin. If Select just becomes "a cleaner shelf for the same Apple SKUs," the premium is positioning, not economics — and easily matched by Yandex Market.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Ozon plans a standalone Select website in 2026 (beyond the app-only start). Backdrop: RU e-commerce growth slowing, non-food spend soft, duopoly consolidation near a ceiling — so premium up-trading and fintech attach are the growth narrative. Regulatory backdrop is tightening around marketplaces (recommendation-system scrutiny, marketplace-bank trust ceiling — [[Axenix study marketplace banks hit trust ceiling despite traffic]]).
- **Second-order (analysis):** The real strategic question isn't "can Select sell more Apples" but "can Ozon convert premium shoppers into Ozon Bank / Ozon Investments customers." If yes, Select is a top-of-funnel for the fintech multiple; if it stays a curated shelf, it's a marketing sub-brand with no durable moat. Counterintuitive risk: AFK Sistema's VTB-pledged stake and debt reduction mean Ozon is under pressure to show *profitable* mix, so expect margin discipline that could throttle the subsidy/promo needed to make a premium app stick.

## Earnings Review

> **Note:** this story is a marketplace-assortment item (Ozon Select adds Apple/Dyson/Garmin electronics, sport, kids), **not an earnings event** — no financials in the news itself. Ozon is IR-covered and recently reported, so its own latest results are folded in as material context on the issuer behind the headline.

**Verdict (headline read).** BEAT / accelerating · primary period is Q3 2025 (reported 2025-11-10): GMV incl. services RUB 1,102.2bn (+53% YoY), revenue RUB 258.9bn, adj. EBITDA RUB 39.0bn (+172% YoY), net profit RUB 2.9bn — Ozon's structural swing to profitability is holding, driven by fintech and higher-margin services; full-year 2025 guidance was raised to ~40% GMV growth and RUB 100–120bn adj. **Key figures — Q3 2025 (reported 2025-11-10, company release):**
- GMV incl. services: RUB 1,102.2bn, +53% YoY.
- Revenue: RUB 258.9bn (revenue growth lagging GMV by design — the marketplace mix shifts GMV to third-party sellers while the P&L captures higher-margin commissions/ads/fintech).
- Adjusted EBITDA: RUB 39.0bn, +172% YoY; EBITDA margin ~4.1% of turnover (up sharply from a thin base).
- Net profit: RUB 2.9bn — positive bottom line (vs the structural losses that characterised 2023–2024).
- Orders: 667.8mn, +79.8% YoY — volume growing far faster than GMV value, confirming lower average-order-value, higher-frequency everyday-purchase mix (the exact dynamic the Ozon Select assortment expansion feeds into).
- Active buyers: 63mn, +17.8% YoY.

**By segment / driver — fintech is the engine.** In Q3 2025 fintech revenue grew ~+198% YoY and fintech adjusted EBITDA reached RUB 14.7bn (+149% YoY); services revenue roughly doubled (+100%). Fintech is now the primary margin driver: for FY2025 the segment delivered revenue of RUB 195.2bn (+120% YoY), adj. EBITDA of RUB 65.3bn (+101% YoY), and 41.7mn active fintech customers. The investment read is that Ozon is monetising its marketplace traffic through financial services (Ozon Bank, Flexible Payment Plan / BNPL, deposits) at a materially higher take-rate than core e-commerce — the Select premium-assortment push widens the higher-ticket basket that fintech lending/payment products attach to.

**vs expectations / prior period.** No public analyst consensus exists for a MOEX-only name post-redomiciliation — all comparisons are YoY/sequential, not vs Street [no consensus]. Trajectory is clearly accelerating: Q2 2025 posted Ozon's first-ever (small) quarterly net profit (~RUB 0.36bn per TASS), Q3 2025 scaled that to RUB 2.9bn, and the trend extended into **Q1 2026** — net profit RUB 4.5bn (vs a RUB 7.9bn loss a year earlier), GMV +36% to RUB 1.14tn, revenue +49% to RUB 300.9bn, adj. EBITDA RUB 48.8bn (+RUB 16.4bn YoY), record adj. EBITDA margin 4.3% of GMV. **FY2025:** GMV growth delivered in line with the ~40% raised guide; FY2025 adj. EBITDA RUB 91.1bn (+~1,092% off a tiny base; ~2.2% of GMV); Q4 2025 GMV RUB ~1,260bn (+33% YoY). FY2025 net result was a small net loss (~RUB 0.9bn, per AKM/Interfax) despite the profitable quarters — a reminder the full-year bottom line is only marginally above breakeven.

**Guidance / forward.** At Q3 2025 Ozon RAISED FY2025 guidance to ~40% GMV growth and adj. EBITDA RUB 100–120bn (Interfax, 2025-11); actual FY2025 adj. EBITDA RUB 91.1bn landed just below the lower bound of the raised range — a modest miss on the EBITDA guide worth flagging. No FY2026 numeric guidance surfaced in public sources [no data]; Q1 2026 actuals (margin at a record 4.3%) suggest management is prioritising profitable growth over pure GMV velocity.

**Thesis-flags.**
1. **Fintech take-rate is the margin story (bull).** Fintech ~+120–198% revenue growth with EBITDA growing faster than revenue → operating leverage; monetisation of marketplace traffic, not just volume. Second-order: assortment moves like Ozon Select (Apple/Dyson/Garmin, higher ASP) raise average basket value, directly expanding the lending/BNPL/payments pool fintech earns on. This is why a "product" headline is thesis-relevant to the fintech P&L.
2. **GMV decelerating as margin inflects (watch).** GMV growth stepped down from +53% (Q3 2025) toward +33% (Q4 2025) / +36% (Q1 2026) while EBITDA margin hit a record 4.3% — classic growth-to-profitability pivot. Risk: if GMV deceleration continues, the equity story rests increasingly on fintech margin expansion, which invites regulatory/credit-risk scrutiny (reserve build, consumer-lending caps) that the releases are quiet on.
3. **Full-year bottom line still fragile (bear check).** Profitable quarters but a ~RUB 0.9bn FY2025 net loss and adj. EBITDA landing below the raised guide's lower bound → "profitability" is real but thin; one-offs/finance costs can still flip the annual number. De-PR: management leads with EBITDA/GMV, stays quieter on the net-loss full-year print and credit reserves.
