# Diálogo Américas: organized crime launders money via fintechs

> [!info] 2026-10-07 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: This Week in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[This Week in Fintech] 🌎 Diálogo Américasreports that organized crime is moving money through fintechs, crypto exchanges, and OTC brokers. In Brazil, six fintechs allegedly processed about $5.1 billion between 2022 and 2025 as "parallel banks" for the First Capital Command (PCC) and other criminal groups, and Chainalysis found that cartel, Chinese, and Russia-linked networks accounted for over half of illicit inflows on some Brazilian exchanges in 2025.
