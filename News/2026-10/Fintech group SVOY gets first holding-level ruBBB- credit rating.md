# Fintech group SVOY gets first holding-level ruBBB- credit rating

> [!info] 2026-10-22 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Финтехно

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Финтехно] Финтех-группа «СВОЙ» впервые получила кредитный рейтинг на уровне всего холдинга — ruBBB- со стабильным прогнозом от «Эксперт РА». Ранее рейтинги присваивались отдельным компаниям группы, а теперь агентство оценило кредитоспособность всей структуры на консолидированном уровне. В дальнейшем на регулярной основе рейтинги будут присваиваться как холдингу, так и отдельным компаниям группы. За последние годы группа вышла за пределы онлайн-кредитования и взыскания долгов: сегодня под брендом «СВОЙ» объединены Свой Банк, МФО Moneyman и Platiza, ID Collect, инвестиционный, страховой и технологический бизнесы. По итогам 2025 года рентабельность капитала группы составила около 34%, активов — 8%, отношение долга к EBITDA — 1,5х. Среди сильных сторон «Эксперт РА» выделило диверсификацию активов и доходов, высокую прибыльность и распределение источников финансирования между кредиторами. При этом сам уровень ruBBB- соответствует умеренной кредитоспособности. Зачем группе рейтинг холдинга? «СВОЙ»

### [0] What happened
On **20 October 2026**, rating agency **Expert RA (Эксперт РА)** assigned the Russian fintech group **SVOY ("СВОЙ")** — the holding entity **МКАО «СВОЙ»** — its **first-ever credit rating at the consolidated holding level: `ruBBB-` with a Stable outlook**. Previously Expert RA only rated individual group companies (notably MFO Moneyman at ruBBB-); this is the first time the agency assessed creditworthiness across the whole structure. Going forward, Expert RA says it will rate both the holding and individual group companies on a regular basis. `ruBBB-` sits at the lower end of the "moderate creditworthiness" band on Expert RA's national scale (one notch above the speculative ruBB+ threshold).

### [1] Who / what is SVOY
SVOY is the rebrand (announced Jan 2026) of the Russian financial holding **IDF Eurasia**. Under the single "СВОЙ" brand the group now consolidates: **Svoy Bank (Свой Банк)**, the MFOs **Moneyman** and **Platiza** (microlending), **ID Collect** (debt collection), plus investment, insurance and technology (IDF Technology / IDF Lab) businesses. It has expanded well beyond its original online-lending + debt-recovery core into a diversified fintech ecosystem (single product/IT platform strategy). Key FY2025 metrics cited: **ROE ~34%, ROA ~8%, Debt/EBITDA ~1.5x**. Expert RA flagged as strengths: diversification of assets and operating income, high profitability, satisfactory asset quality, acceptable liquidity, and diversified funding across creditors.

### [2] Why it matters / likely purpose
A holding-level rating is the standard prerequisite for **consolidated debt issuance** — i.e. placing bonds (ruble corporate/MFO bonds) on the **Moscow Exchange (MOEX)** at the group level rather than only through subsidiaries, broadening the investor base and reducing funding cost. It also signals maturation of the ex-IDF Eurasia group into a unified, rateable financial institution. Context: in **Sep 2026** Expert RA assigned a very high `ruAAA.sf` (structured-finance scale) to class-A bonds of **SFO «Свой Капитал – Финтех» (SFO Svoy Kapital)** — a securitization SPV — showing the group is already active in capital markets via structured deals; the new holding rating complements that by enabling direct unsecured issuance.

### [3] Numbers / facts to anchor
- Rating: `ruBBB-`, Stable outlook, Expert RA, assigned **2026-10-20**. First at holding (МКАО «СВОЙ») level in group history.
- FY2025: ROE ≈ 34%, ROA ≈ 8%, Debt/EBITDA ≈ 1.5x.
- Group brands: Svoy Bank, Moneyman, Platiza, ID Collect, + investment/insurance/tech arms.
- Rebrand IDF Eurasia → SVOY announced ~Jan 2026.
- Related: SFO «Свой Капитал – Финтех» class-A bonds rated ruAAA.sf (Sep 2026).

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** SVOY (the rebrand of IDF Eurasia, consolidated under holdco MKAO "SVOY") sits in Russian non-bank consumer lending / MFO + debt-collection + a licensed bank (Svoy Bank), funded heavily on the MOEX high-yield bond (ВДО) market. Structure: the segment is fragmented and regulation-driven — issuers rely on retail bond demand, so a holding-level rating is a funding tool, not a trophy. Drivers / "why now": (1) tightening MFO regulation — from 01.10.2026 the CBR caps high-APR lending via a "two loans per borrower" rule plus disincentivizing reserves on loans with ПСК >200–250%, which is pushing groups like SVOY to diversify away from pure payday lending into banking, collection, insurance and tech (per raexpert.ru MFO research 2025–2026; CBR, cbr.ru/press/event id=28242). (2) Rate environment: CBR key rate ~14.5% as of mid-2026 and easing, yet retail MFO investment yields rose to ~18.77% (02.09.2026, brobank.ru) — i.e. spreads for HY issuers widened even as the base rate fell, raising relative funding cost. (3) Market-wide, raexpert.ru forecasts micro-loan issuance to shrink ~5% in 2026 under regulatory pressure.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: ROE, debt/EBITDA (leverage), portfolio quality / cost of risk, and bond coupon vs rating. SVOY's own disclosed metrics (via note / Expert RA release): FY2025 ROE ~34%, ROA ~8%, debt/EBITDA 1.5x — strong for the tier. Key in-base / named peers in the same HY-lending-bond pocket: [[ID Finance raises €545M to expand into payments]] (sister lineage ID Finance, now scaling into payments), [[Lender Invest offers up to 14.5% via pre-IPO on SPB Exchange]] (a smaller platform going equity-route at a RUB 2.45bn pre-money), and the broader [[Финтехно Russian business microfinance splits into two models]] (commercial MFOs growing ~30% YoY but riskier). Basis of competition: cost of funding (rating → coupon) and regulatory agility. SVOY's position: a consolidator — ahead of single-license MFO issuers because diversification (bank + MFO + collection + insurance + IT) is exactly what Expert RA cited as a strength; moat is scale + funding diversification across creditors `(analysis)`.

**Comps & multiples.** This is a credit-rating event, not a valuation/round — equity multiples (EV/Rev, P/E) are **no data** (holdco is private; no public market cap). The relevant comps are intra-group / peer ratings and bond economics:
- Moneyman (SVOY subsidiary): Expert RA **ruBBB-** stable (confirmed Sep 2025); 4-yr bonds placed at coupon up to 26% / yield up to ~29.3% (moneyman.ru); H1 2026 net profit ~RUB 1bn, capital RUB 6.4bn, ROE ~35% (tadviser).
- ID Collect (SVOY subsidiary): Expert RA ruBBB- (upgraded Aug 2025; earlier BB+); bonds yield up to ~22.8%/4yr; H1 2026 revenue RUB 4.8bn, EBITDA RUB 3.6bn, assets RUB 33bn, 11 bond issues ~RUB 8.6bn outstanding (smart-lab / idcollect).
- SFO "Svoy Capital – Fintech" structured-finance class-A notes: Expert RA **ruAAA.sf** (Sep 2026) — note the .sf (structured) scale is not comparable to the holdco corporate ruBBB-.
Read-across: the new holdco ruBBB- lands **in-line** with its two main operating subs — the group rating does not upgrade the consolidated credit, it merely consolidates it. Arithmetic on bond cost: at ruBBB- SVOY's units price ~22–29% coupons vs a ~14.5% key rate = a ~8–15pp risk spread, consistent with the HY/ВДО tier where Expert RA flags BBB-and-below as the cohort most exposed to default in a downturn (finuslugi.ru; raexpert.ru bonds_2026).

**Risk flags.**
1. **Regulatory/funding-cost squeeze** — the 01.10.2026 ПСК caps and reserve penalties hit the highest-margin payday book just as HY spreads are widening; a ruBBB- issuer refinancing at ~22–29% coupons is structurally exposed if retail bond demand cools (second-order: forced deleveraging or coupon step-ups). 
2. **Tier is the ceiling, not a floor** — ruBBB- = "moderate" creditworthiness; Expert RA's own 2026 debt research notes defaults cluster at BBB and below, so the "first holding rating" headline masks that the group is still firmly in the vulnerable HY cohort, not investment-grade-safe.
3. **Model concentration / cyclicality** — despite diversification claims, the earnings engine remains consumer micro-lending + distressed-debt collection, both pro-cyclical; a consumer-credit downturn hits origination (MFO) and recoveries (ID Collect) at the same time.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** A holdco-level rating is a prelude to holdco-level bond issuance — watch for a new MKAO "SVOY"-branded placement (the consolidated rating lets the parent raise directly rather than only through Moneyman/ID Collect) `(analysis)`. Falsifiable thesis: SVOY prints a holding-level bond within ~6–12 months at a coupon inside its subsidiaries' ~22–26% range; it would be wrong if the group instead keeps funding only at the operating-company level, signalling the holdco rating is purely reputational. Trigger to watch: the first MKAO "SVOY" issue and whether the 01.10.2026 regulation forces a visible contraction in the MFO book.
