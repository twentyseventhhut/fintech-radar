# OKX launches unified EUR and EURC order book in Europe

> [!info] 2026-10-22 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇪🇺 OKX launches a unified EUR and EURC order book in Europe. Users can convert EUR to EURC at a 1:1 rate and settle trades in either asset. The setup removes the need to route trades through USD pathways.

European crypto traders should be able to trade in a way that feels native to Europe.
That means easier access to EUR, better support for euro-denominated assets, and a smoother path between fiat EUR and euro stablecoins. Today, we are taking an important step in that direction. We have launched a unified EUR order book that includes EURC for users in Europe.
Most Europeans manage their finances in euros and many would prefer to trade, fund, and manage their portfolios in their home currency. By bringing EUR and EURC together we are giving European traders more flexibility, whether they prefer to use euros or a euro-denominated stablecoin.

Our proprietary routing engine is now scaling to support EUR and EURC. Instead of siloing traditional fiat and stablecoin pairings into separate books, which splits liquidity and degrades execution quality, our architecture allows traders to access a single market pool while preserving the freedom to settle in either asset.

 Trade and Settle Fluidly: Manage trading activity in euro terms while retaining the freedom to settle in either asset. 
 Trade without added FX Risk: Eliminate the need to route trades through USD-based pathways, removing foreign exchange friction and protecting your PnL from unwanted currency fluctuations. 
 MiCA-Ready Infrastructure: Use a trading experience built from the ground up to meet European regulatory and operational standards. 
Europe’s crypto market is evolving quickly. As regulation, stablecoins, and user expectations mature, trading platforms need to become more local, more intuitive, and more aligned with how European users manage money.

## [0] What happened (one-paragraph fact base)

On 2026-10-21/22 OKX launched a **unified EUR + EURC spot order book** for eligible users in the EEA, built with Circle. Orders quoted against fiat EUR and orders quoted against Circle's euro stablecoin **EURC now meet in a single combined book**; at execution the user chooses which asset to settle in, and EUR↔EURC convert **1:1**. The stated aim: one deep euro-denominated liquidity pool (tighter spreads, less slippage) without routing euro trades through USD pairs — i.e. removing an implicit EUR/USD FX leg. OKX frames it as "MiCA-ready" infrastructure. Pair availability and access vary by region/account within supported EEA jurisdictions. Source: [OKX Learn](https://www.okx.com/en-eu/learn/okx-unified-eur-eurc-order-book), [OKX EEA EURC FAQ](https://www.okx.com/en-eu/help/eea-eurc-orderbook-circle), [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/okx-eurc-unified-euro-order-book/), [DefiPrime](https://defiprime.com/okx-unifies-eur-and-eurc-spot-order-book).

## [1] Why it matters / significance

- **Not a new mechanism for OKX — a new currency rail.** OKX pioneered the "unified order book" to merge fragmented USD markets, and in Apr 2026 extended it to fold Ripple's RLUSD into the same USDC pool (280+ pairs) — see [[OKX Partners with Ripple to List RLUSD with Deep Liquidity via Unified Order Book]] (if present) / the Apr-2026 RLUSD listing. This EUR/EURC book applies the same proprietary routing engine to the euro leg. So the "innovation" is incremental engineering, re-pointed at Europe.
- **Euro-native trading is the strategic prize.** Most European retail hold and think in EUR; forcing them through USD/USDC pairs adds FX friction and PnL noise. A single EUR-denominated book that also accepts a MiCA-compliant euro stablecoin is a genuine localization play, not just marketing.
- **Fits the post-MiCA cleanup of EU stablecoins.** Tether/USDT was pushed off regulated EU venues from 1 July 2026 ([[Tether exits EU market as MiCA blocks USDT from exchanges]]); ESMA then gave exchanges until **8 Jan 2027** to block EU customers from non-compliant stablecoins ([[ESMA gives crypto exchanges three months to drop unauthorized stablecoins]]). EURC is MiCA-compliant and USDC was just named the EU's first **significant** e-money token ([[EBA classifies USDC as first significant e-money token in EU]]). Leaning on EURC is the compliant path.

## [2] Key players & relationships

- **OKX Europe Ltd** — holds a full MiCA CASP authorization from Malta's MFSA (granted 27 Jan 2025; first global exchange to get one) plus a MiFID II entity (2025) and a Maltese Payments Institution (PI) licence ([[OKX secures European payments licence for crypto expansion]]); founder Star Xu touted broader EU authorization in mid-2026 ([[OKX confirms new authorization to expand services in Europe]]). OKX passports across ~28–29 EEA states.
- **Circle** — issuer of EURC. Circle France (ACPR-supervised EMI) is MiCA-approved for USDC/EURC custody & transfer ([[Circle France gets MiCA approval for crypto-asset services]]). Circle publicly confirmed the OKX integration (21 Oct 2026). This deepens the existing OKX–Circle relationship (they ran zero-fee USDC conversions back in 2025).

## [3] Market context / competitive landscape

- **EURC is the dominant euro stablecoin** but the whole category is tiny: euro stablecoin market cap ~$0.8–0.9bn in late Sep 2026 (ATH) — only ~0.3% of all stablecoins. Within that, **EURC ≈ 60–65%**, Société Générale's **EURCV ≈ 17–20%**; the two control ~82%. EURC's share more than doubled (≈17%→>50%) between Jan-2025 and Mar-2026. Sources: [BitcoinWorld](https://bitcoinworld.co.in/euro-stablecoin-market-cap-810m-eurc-dominates/), [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/euro-stablecoin-market-ethereum-solana-base/).
- **EURC is already widely listed** (Coinbase, Kraken, Bitpanda, Bitstamp, Bybit) — so OKX's differentiator is not "listing EURC" but **merging EUR fiat and EURC into one book with 1:1 settlement choice**, which few/no peers offer that way.
- **Exchange competition in the EU** is intensifying as MiCA-licensed venues (OKX, Coinbase, Kraken, Bitpanda) court users leaving restricted/exiting players ([[Coinbase and OKX lure EU users as Binance restricts services]]). A euro-native book is a retention/acquisition lever.

## [4] Open questions / what to watch

- Real liquidity in the merged book vs. marketing — are spreads/depth on EUR pairs actually better post-launch, or is EURC volume still thin?
- How many pairs and which EEA jurisdictions are live at launch (OKX says it varies).
- Whether 1:1 EUR↔EURC is guaranteed/free at scale or subject to fees/limits (FAQ hints at "standard fees").
- Does this meaningfully grow EURC circulation, or just reshuffle existing OKX euro flow?
- Competitive response from Coinbase/Kraken and from EURCV/other euro issuers (EURI, EUROD, Qivalis bank consortium).

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
### Red-team questions

1. Is this genuinely new, or just OKX re-applying its existing unified-order-book engine (used for USD, then USDC+RLUSD in Apr 2026) to the euro leg? The core tech is pre-existing — the novelty is the currency, not the architecture.
2. The only primary source in the note is OKX's own Learn blog (marketing copy). Where is independent confirmation that spreads/slippage actually improved? Circle's 21-Oct confirmation is also an interested party.
3. "Trade without added FX risk" — EUR↔EURC at 1:1 only removes FX risk if the peg holds and conversion is free/unlimited. What happens to the "1:1" promise under EURC depeg stress or if "standard fees" apply?
4. How deep is EURC liquidity really? EURC is ~60% of a ~$0.8bn euro stablecoin market — tiny vs. USD stablecoins (~$0.3% of total). Can a merged book be "deep" when one leg is this thin?
5. Which specific pairs and which EEA jurisdictions are live at launch? OKX explicitly says availability "varies by region/account" — the headline may overstate reach.
6. Does merging fiat EUR and a stablecoin into one order book raise MiCA/best-execution or settlement-finality concerns? Fiat and EMT are legally distinct — is netting them cleanly compliant?
7. Is OKX structurally dependent on Circle? If EURC faced an EU regulatory issue (it's now a "significant" EMT with stricter EBA oversight), does the whole euro book wobble?
8. Counterfactual: would a sophisticated trader not already achieve euro-native exposure via EURC pairs on Coinbase/Kraken? What's the marginal benefit beyond UX?
9. Volume vs. reshuffle: does this create new EURC demand/circulation, or merely relabel OKX's existing euro trading flow?
10. Reputational overhang: OKX was fined €2.6m in the Netherlands (2025) for registration failure and has a checkered compliance history. Does "MiCA-ready" marketing paper over that?
11. Why now? Is the timing driven by the ESMA 8-Jan-2027 deadline to purge non-compliant stablecoins (forcing a pivot to EURC) rather than genuine user demand?
12. Is there any disclosed economic arrangement (rebates, revenue share) between OKX and Circle that biases OKX toward EURC over rival euro stablecoins (EURCV, EURI)?
13. Does "settle in either asset" introduce counterparty/custody nuances (who holds the EURC, redemption path) that retail users won't understand?

Importance: 2/5 — A real, well-executed localization feature that fits the MiCA euro-stablecoin shift and plays to OKX's EU-expansion strategy, but it is an incremental extension of an existing order-book mechanism (already done for USDC/RLUSD), confined to a tiny euro-stablecoin market, sourced almost entirely from OKX/Circle's own announcements, and with no disclosed metrics proving the liquidity/spread benefits. Strategically sensible, not market-moving.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Euro-stablecoin is a tiny but fast-growing niche: the whole euro-stablecoin segment is estimated at ~$760m–$810m, <0.3% of global stablecoin supply (per Cryptobriefing / crypto.news, as of mid-Oct 2026). EURC (Circle) dominates with ~$497m market cap / ~443m tokens in circulation and ~60% share (as of 19-Oct-2026, per Cryptobriefing); up ~8.8% m/m and roughly doubled y/y on MiCA-driven institutional demand. Structure is consolidated on the issuer side (EURC ≈60%; the rest split across EUROe, EURCV/Societe Generale, bank consortia) but fragmented on the venue side. Driver / "why now": MiCA. Titles III–IV (stablecoins) apply since 30-Jun-2024 and the CASP regime (Title V) hit its hard compliance cliff 1-Jul-2026; USDT and most non-compliant stablecoins were delisted for EEA users across Coinbase/Binance/Kraken, and Binance exited the EU on 1-Jul-2026. USDC and EURC are among the few MiCA-compliant top-10 stablecoins — so a MiCA-licensed venue (OKX holds a Malta/EEA passport) that natively bridges EUR↔EURC is positioning into a regulatory vacuum left by the delistings (analysis).

**Competitive landscape.** Venue KPIs that matter here (none disclosed by OKX for this launch): order-book depth / spread, EUR-pair trading volume, EURC on-platform balances, and settlement mix (EUR vs EURC). Key players: MiCA-licensed EU venues — Coinbase (Ireland), Kraken (Ireland/Luxembourg), Bitpanda, Bitvavo, Crypto.com (Malta), Bitstamp, Revolut — competing on local-fiat UX, liquidity depth and compliance after Binance's EU exit (per Financemagnates / Yahoo Finance, 2026). Basis of competition: depth + native-euro UX + regulatory standing, not price alone. Recent moves: [[Coinbase and OKX lure EU users as Binance restricts services]] (Jun-2026) — both explicitly chasing the EU gap; OKX's own unified-order-book playbook was extended to USD stablecoins in Apr-2026 via the Ripple RLUSD listing (280+ pairs, one USDC/USDG/RLUSD pool, per TheBlock/OKX, 29-Apr-2026). This EUR+EURC book is the same proprietary routing engine applied to euro markets. OKX position: a fast-follower turning its existing unified-book tech into a euro-native edge — ahead on infra, but late on EUR liquidity vs Coinbase/Kraken's installed EEA base (analysis). Moat = the unified-routing engine (scale + switching-cost: traders keep settlement optionality without split liquidity); not unique long-term, but a real execution-quality differentiator today.

**Comps & multiples.** This is a product launch, not a deal — no revenue/round/valuation attached to it, so no trading multiple is computable here ("no data" on launch economics). Issuer-side context: EURC ~$497m market cap (not a multiple — circulating supply). OKX corporate valuation anchor: ICE took a minority stake at a ~$25bn valuation and OKX later raised at a flat $25bn (per internal notes; see [[ICE takes minority stake in OKX at $25bn valuation]], [[OKX raises capital at flat $25B valuation]]) — this is a private round valuation, not a market cap, and OKX does not disclose EU-segment revenue, so EV/Revenue = [UNSOURCED]. Internal euro-stablecoin comps for sector shape (not valuation): [[ODDO BHF launches euro-backed stablecoin EUROD]], [[Euro stablecoin consortium Qivalis expands to 37 banks]], [[Circle France gets MiCA approval for crypto-asset services]]. Distribution not computed — qualitative only; no comparable public multiple.

**Risk flags.**
1. Single-issuer dependence. The entire "euro-native" promise rests on EURC — one issuer (Circle) holding ~60% of a <$1bn market. If Circle faced a MiCA/reserve/redemption problem, OKX's unified EUR book inherits that concentration risk directly (second-order: liquidity + settlement both impaired at once).
2. Liquidity is unproven / undisclosed. OKX markets "deeper liquidity / tighter spreads" but has disclosed no depth, volume or spread figures and no comparison to peers; the euro-stablecoin pool it draws on is structurally shallow (<$1bn segment). "Unified" improves execution only if there's real EUR+EURC volume to unify — otherwise it's PR over a thin book (de-PR: announced, not proven).
3. Regulatory / passport dependence. The whole thesis is gated on OKX's continued MiCA/CASP standing (Malta passport). Any license or supervisory friction (ESMA/ECB scrutiny of large stablecoin usage is already flagged) would strand the euro-native positioning (second-order: the delisting dynamic that helped OKX could just as easily turn on it).

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Incremental, not a re-rating: OKX is cloning its proven USD unified-book into euros to capture the post-MiCA, post-Binance EEA gap and to deepen the EUR↔EURC on-ramp — a bet that euro-native crypto trading becomes a distinct product category, not a USD derivative (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: if MiCA keeps squeezing non-compliant stablecoins, EURC-denominated EU volume should visibly rise on OKX and peers over the next 2–3 quarters. Trigger / what breaks it: watch for Coinbase/Kraken launching their own euro-native unified books (commoditizes the edge) or EURC share/circulation stalling (signals euro-stablecoin demand is structurally capped, not just early).
