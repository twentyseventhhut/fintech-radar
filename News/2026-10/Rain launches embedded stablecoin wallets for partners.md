 NEW YORK , Oct. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Rain, the global stablecoin payments platform, today announced Rain wallets , embedded stablecoin wallets that partners can offer their users inside their own apps, with the ability to earn on balances to follow by end of year. The wallet is the account at the center of Rain's platform. It is accessible through the same integration that issues the card, powers rewards, and supports money movement, so a user can get paid, save, spend, and send from one balance.

 Companies bringing stablecoins to their users want them to work the way any other money does. For that to happen, a stablecoin balance needs to connect to everything a user wants to do with it. Rain wallets put that balance at the center of Rain's platform, so the full life cycle of money runs in one place, from the moment it arrives to the moment it is spent or sent.
Consider a freelancer in Mexico paid by a U.S. client. Her pay is sent in dollars to her virtual account and lands in her Rain wallet as stablecoins , with no manual conversion. She can spend that afternoon at any of the 175 million merchant locations where her Rain-issued card is accepted, or she can access additional ways to earn on balances directly in her app. When she needs pesos, she can send them straight to her local bank account using Rain's global payouts. The entire experience lives inside one app, and it's all powered by Rain's infrastructure.
"When a company builds with stablecoins , the first thing its users need is a place to hold them," said Pooja Shah, Head of Product at Rain. "Rain wallets are that place. Because they are integrated into the broader Rain stack, every balance is ready to use the moment it arrives. Partners can get all of it from Rain, or keep the wallet they have and plug it into everything else we run."
 How Rain wallets fit into Rain's full-stack platform 
The wallet is the account at the center of Rain's full-stack platform, the place where a partner's users hold their stablecoins and the balance every other Rain product draws on. Rain started by making stablecoins spendable through cards and is now powering all onchain experiences through the wallet . A balance can arrive through a virtual account or onramp, fund a card, be put to work, and be sent to a bank account in local currency without ever leaving the partner's app. For enterprises, that means one vendor integrating the full flow of funds, and every product a partner adds works with the ones they already run.
While Rain offers the convenience of an end-to-end platform, its infrastructure is also built to be open and interoperable. Partners that already work with a wallet provider can connect it to Rain's cards, rewards, onramps, and payouts. Rain wallets support multiple stablecoins and other crypto assets across major blockchains, giving partners the flexibility to choose which assets or networks best fit their business.
 Availability 
Non-custodial Rain wallets are available today while custodial wallets and additional earning capabilities will be available by the end of the year. For more information, reach out to [email protected]  or visit https://www.rain.xyz/product/wallets .
 Frequently asked questions 
 What are Rain wallets? Rain wallets are embedded stablecoin wallets that Rain's partners offer to their users inside their own apps. Each Rain wallet connects directly to Rain cards, virtual accounts, onramps, offramps, and payouts, so users can get paid, spend, and send from one stablecoin balance. Non-custodial embedded wallets, where users custody their own funds, are available today, while custodial Rain wallets will be available by the end of the year.
 What blockchains do Rain wallets support?  Non-custodial Rain wallets support twelve blockchains today: Arbitrum, Avalanche, Base, BNB Smart Chain, Celo, Ethereum , Monad, Plasma, Optimism, Polygon, Solana , and ZKsync.
 What stablecoins will Rain wallets support? Rain wallets hold USDC, USDT, other stablecoins and crypto assets on supported blockchains. Functionality may vary depending on the network.
 What's the benefit of a Rain wallet ? A Rain wallet connects a user's stablecoin balance to everything they want to do with it on one platform. Money that arrives through a Rain virtual account lands in the Rain wallet as stablecoins , can fund Rain card spending at 175 million merchant locations, and can be sent to a bank account in local currency through Rain offramps and payouts without leaving the partner's app. In the future, we plan to launch features that will let end users earn directly from stablecoin balances held in their Rain wallets.
 Can I use other Rain services without a Rain wallet ? Yes. Rain cards, and the rest of its stack, work with the infrastructure partners already use, including third-party non-custodial wallets. Partners that choose Rain wallets get card funding built in, ensuring a more seamless experience to power card spending directly.
 About Rain 
 Rain  is the global stablecoin payments platform for enterprises, neobanks, platforms, developers, and AI agents. Our technology allows partners to move, store, and use stablecoins instantly and compliantly through global payment cards, rewards, on/offramps, wallets, and cross-border rails. As both a Visa and Mastercard Principal Member, Rain issues cards that work at more than 175 million merchant locations in over 220 countries and territories. Built natively for stablecoins and trusted by more than 100 organizations worldwide, Rain delivers secure, scalable infrastructure that makes money move freely and instantly around the world.
