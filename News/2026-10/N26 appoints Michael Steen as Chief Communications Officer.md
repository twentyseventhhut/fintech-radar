N26 is narrowing its focus to its core European markets of Germany, France, Italy, and Spain to drive primary account growth and institutional maturity. The appointment of Michael Steen as Chief Communications Officer and the upcoming launch of N26 Aura signal a shift toward product-led scaling under the leadership of CEO Mike Dargan.

## What was announced

N26 is entering a new phase of growth centered on its four largest markets: Germany, France, Italy, and Spain. This strategic pivot follows the first six months of Mike Dargan’s tenure as CEO, during which the bank has worked to strengthen its compliance and governance frameworks. To support this transition, N26 has appointed Michael Steen as Chief Communications Officer, effective January 1, 2027. Steen joins from Deutsche Bank, where he was Managing Director and Head of External Communications and Newsroom, and previously held a senior role at the European Central Bank. He began his career as a financial journalist for Reuters and the Financial Times.

The bank is also preparing to roll out a refreshed product roadmap intended to increase customer relevance. This begins next month with the launch of N26 Aura, described as a new signature banking experience. The leadership team has been further bolstered by the recent addition of Marc Hess as Chief Financial Officer. These moves are part of a broader ambition for N26 to establish itself as a trusted digital primary bank in Europe, moving beyond its roots as a secondary spending account to become the central hub for its users' financial lives.

> "Building solid regulatory foundations, robust systems and trust at the core of how we operate has been a priority for me since joining N26. With these pillars firmly established, N26 is now well-positioned to accelerate towards our ambitions and deepen our relationships with more customers across Europe as their primary bank."

Mike Dargan, Chief Executive Officer of N26

## The companies involved

N26 is one of Europe’s most prominent digital banks, having played a central role in the neobanking wave that disrupted traditional retail finance over the last decade. Headquartered in Berlin, the firm has sought to challenge incumbent lenders by offering a mobile-first experience characterized by real-time notifications and low-fee international transactions. The bank operates under its own European banking license, which has allowed it to offer a full suite of retail products, including current accounts, savings, and credit.

The company has undergone significant leadership changes recently to navigate a more complex regulatory environment and a shift in investor sentiment toward profitability. With the appointment of Mike Dargan as CEO and Marc Hess as CFO and Managing Director, the bank is moving away from the rapid global expansion that defined its early years. Instead, it is focusing on deepening its penetration in established European markets where digital banking adoption is high and the potential for primary account status is greatest.

## What this means

This refocusing suggests that the era of neobanks chasing "growth at all costs" across dozens of disparate markets is officially over. By concentrating on Germany, France, Italy, and Spain, N26 is acknowledging that winning the "primary account" battle requires deep local relevance and ironclad regulatory trust rather than just a slick app. The hiring of a CCO with deep ties to Deutsche Bank and the ECB is a clear signal to regulators and the market that N26 is maturing into a traditional banking heavyweight. The challenge now is whether "N26 Aura" can provide enough differentiation to lure customers away from established national incumbents who have significantly improved their own digital offerings.

Original Press Release

N26 is refocusing on its next phase of growth and maturity: scaling the digital bank’s core markets of Germany, France, Italy and Spain to reinforce its leadership in European digital banking.

Six months into Mike Dargan’s tenure as CEO, with strengthened compliance and governance frameworks and a newly established management team in place, N26 will further sharpen its focus on Europe, rolling out a targeted product roadmap to drive customer relevance and primary account growth, starting with the launch of its new signature banking experience, N26 Aura, next month.

As part of this plan to drive execution, N26 has appointed Michael Steen as Chief Communications Officer (CCO) effective 1 January 2027. Michael brings extensive experience from Deutsche Bank, where he held the role of Managing Director and Head of External Communications and Newsroom. Prior to that, Michael served in a senior communications position at the European Central Bank. He began his career as a financial journalist for Reuters and the Financial Times.

In his role, he will work closely with the management team, in building relevance, resonance and trust among customers as the bank accelerates towards its ambition to be Europe’s Most Trusted Digital Primary Bank. With the appointment of Michael, alongside recently appointed Chief Financial Officer Marc Hess, N26 has taken significant steps in reshaping its management team to start the next chapter.

"Building solid regulatory foundations, robust systems and trust at the core of how we operate has been a priority for me since joining N26. With these pillars firmly established, N26 is now well-positioned to accelerate towards our ambitions and deepen our relationships with more customers across Europe as their primary bank." said Mike Dargan, Chief Executive Officer of N26.

"N26 has redefined modern banking for millions of European consumers. As the company enters this next phase of scale and institutional maturity, transparent communication and strong stakeholder trust must come hand-in-hand with growth. I look forward to working alongside Mike and our management team as we continue to build Europe’s Most Trusted Digital Primary Bank." added Michael Steen, incoming Chief Communications Officer of N26.
