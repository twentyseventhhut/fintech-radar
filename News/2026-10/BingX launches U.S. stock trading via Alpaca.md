PANAMA CITY , Oct. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- BingX, the world's leading multi-asset trading platform, today launched U.S. stock trading through an integration with Alpaca, a global provider of brokerage infrastructure and APIs. The new offering gives eligible users access to more than 7,000 U.S. stocks and exchange-traded funds (ETFs), expanding the range of traditional financial assets available on BingX and advancing its multi-asset strategy.

 Through Alpaca's brokerage infrastructure, eligible BingX users can access U.S. stocks and ETFs directly through the platform, using their existing crypto assets to fund trades without the need for fiat deposits or a separate brokerage account managed directly by the user.
Unlike tokenized instruments that track stock prices, the offering provides access to U.S. securities through brokerage infrastructure, including the associated custody, clearing, and settlement processes. It marks another step in bringing digital assets and traditional financial markets together with a unified trading experience.
 Key features include:
 Trade Stocks with USDT and USDC: Put your assets to work with no fiat, no wire transfers, and no additional brokerage accounts.
 Explore 7,000+ Stocks and ETFs: From established market leaders to companies shaping emerging market trends, diversify your portfolio on one unified platform.
 Get Started With Just $5: Access leading U.S. stocks and ETFs for as little as $5.
 Trade 24/5: Stay connected to the U.S. markets with trading access 24 hours a day, five days a week.
 Choose How You Trade: Place market or limit orders for greater flexibility in how you enter and exit positions.
"Expanding access to U.S. stocks is another step in making global market opportunities more accessible to BingX users," said Kevin Lee, Chief Strategy Officer at BingX. "As markets become increasingly interconnected, developments across equities, commodities, currencies and digital assets can influence one another. By extending access to U.S. stocks through Alpaca's brokerage infrastructure, we're giving users more ways to engage with these markets within a unified multi-asset platform."
"BingX has built a global community that is looking to access several markets and asset classes from one platform," said Yoshi Yokokawa, Co-Founder and CEO of Alpaca. "Our infrastructure and APIs are designed so partners like BingX can add U.S. equities alongside their existing offerings through Alpaca. We're pleased to support BingX as they bring U.S. stock trading to their users around the world."
To celebrate the launch, BingX is hosting a limited-time $200,000 Stocks Mystery Box , running from October 9 to 23. Eligible users can receive $10 in Tesla (TSLA) stock upon completing their first U.S. stock trade. The launch reinforces BingX's broader ambition to connect digital assets with traditional financial markets, giving traders more ways to navigate an increasingly interconnected global economy.

# Context-enrichment: BingX launches U.S. stock trading via Alpaca
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
On **2026-10-13** BingX (crypto exchange, HQ-of-record Panama City, ~40M users) turned on **US stock + ETF trading** for "eligible users," powered by **Alpaca's brokerage-as-a-service** rails. De-PR'd facts:
- These are **REAL US equities, NOT tokenized** — the PR explicitly draws that line ("Unlike tokenized instruments that track stock prices..."). Alpaca (a FINRA/SIPC-member, self-clearing broker-dealer) does the **custody, clearing and settlement**; BingX is a front-end/distribution skin. Confirmed externally: industry trackers place BingX in the **"real shares funded with stablecoins"** bucket alongside MEXC RealStocks / Bitget Stock+ / Binance — distinct from the tokenized bucket (Kraken/Bybit/Gate xStocks). (prnewswire; mexc.com/learn; paragraph.com/mexclearn)
- **Funding rail:** trades funded with **USDT/USDC**, "no fiat deposits, no wire transfers, no separate brokerage account." Min ticket **$5** (fractional). **7,000+ stocks/ETFs**, **24/5** trading (not 24/7), market + limit orders.
- **Marketing, not substance:** a "$200,000 Stocks Mystery Box" (Oct 9–23) giving **$10 of TSLA** on first trade — a CAC-subsidy acquisition promo, flagged as PR puffery, not a product fact.

**Why structured this way / what it reveals:** The load-bearing, PR-buried fact is **who the user is NOT**. BingX's own geo-restrictions exclude the **United States, UK, Canada, Singapore, Hong Kong, Netherlands, China, Panama** and ~13 sanctioned jurisdictions (datawallet; bingx support). So "U.S. stock trading" is sold to **non-US retail buying US-listed shares** — the value prop is *cross-border access to US equities for people who can't easily open a US brokerage account*, wrapped in a stablecoin on-ramp that sidesteps fiat FX/wires. The "real not tokenized" boast is the genuine delta vs most crypto-exchange stock products, but BingX **contributes none of it** — the regulated substance is 100% Alpaca's; BingX rents it. (analysis)

## [1] Competitors / peers
Two adjacent battlegrounds:
- **Real-equity-via-broker (BingX's actual category):** **MEXC RealStocks** (real NYSE/NASDAQ shares, USDT-settled, real dividends, "0-fee," via a licensed broker partner), **Bitget Stock+**, **Binance** — all let non-US users buy real US shares with stablecoins (mexc.com; cryptobriefing). BingX is a **follower** here, not a pioneer; MEXC/Bitget shipped comparable products in 2026.
- **Same infra vendor (Alpaca) distribution peers:** [[trii launches US stock trading in Chile via Alpaca]] (Mar 2026, LatAm retail, fractional US shares) — **identical mechanism, 7 months earlier**. Alpaca also powers Kraken Embed → [[Alpaca integrates Kraken Embed to expand crypto access]].
- **Tokenized-equity rivals (different mechanism):** [[Coinbase launches 24 7 US stock trading with Yahoo Finance]], [[Coinbase and Bybit explore US tokenized stock collaboration]], [[Kraken rolls out 24 7 perpetuals for tokenized US stocks]], [[Nasdaq partners with Kraken for tokenized stock trading]].

**Why the landscape is this way / second-order:** The "real shares" camp (BingX/MEXC/Bitget) and the "tokenized" camp (Kraken/Bybit/Coinbase perps) are **converging on the same end-user** — non-US retail wanting US-stock exposure inside a crypto wallet — from opposite legal structures. Real-share-via-licensed-broker is the **regulatorily cleaner** path (actual DTC custody, real dividends/voting) but it's **commoditized**: any exchange can rent Alpaca/DriveWealth and replicate it in a quarter. Tokenization is riskier legally but potentially more defensible (24/7, composability). BingX picked the easy, low-moat path → it competes on **distribution and incentives (the TSLA giveaway), not product**. (analysis)

## [2] Company history / fit
BingX (founded 2018; perps, spot, copy-trading, Chelsea FC + Scuderia Ferrari sponsorships) has been **systematically de-risking into a "multi-asset" super-app** rather than a pure crypto venue: [[BingX launches Visa debit card with Wirex]] (Jul 2026, fiat spend rail) and [[BingX names Enzo Fernandez global ambassador ahead of World Cup]] (brand/CAC). US stocks is the next adjacency.
**Why BingX acts this way:** crypto-exchange take-rates are cyclical and fee-compressing; bolting on equities (and a card) is the standard **"crypto-exchange → multi-asset brokerage" diversification** play to lift ARPU, trading frequency and stickiness without the cycle risk of pure crypto. Because the regulated plumbing is rentable (Alpaca), the marginal cost of adding equities is low and the strategic logic is almost forced — which is exactly why *everyone* (MEXC, Bitget, Binance, Bybit) is doing it at once. The move fits; it is not differentiated. (analysis)

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Novelty: low.** The only genuine delta vs typical crypto-exchange stock products is **real shares + real custody/dividends (not synthetic tokens)** — and that is Alpaca's property, already live via trii (Mar 2026) and matched by MEXC/Bitget. For BingX specifically this is a **me-too distribution launch**.
**Traction: zero disclosed.** PR gives **no users, no AUM, no funded-account count, no trade volume** for the stock product — only the 40M-user house number (crypto accounts, not equities adopters) and a time-boxed TSLA giveaway. "Announced/launched," not "adopted." Treat all adoption as **open**.
**Where the margin sits / what breaks it (analysis):** In this stack, durable value accrues to whoever owns the **regulated broker-dealer + clearing + custody** (Alpaca) and the **order flow / distribution** (BingX's 40M users). BingX owns distribution but **rents the regulated core**, so it is a **price-taker on the equities economics** — Alpaca takes the per-trade/custody economics, BingX monetizes the stablecoin float, spread and CAC-funded engagement. If Alpaca raises prices or signs BingX's rivals (it serves 300+ partners), BingX has no moat. The "real shares" edge also erodes as the SEC's 2026 innovation exemption makes compliant tokenized rails cheaper (FTX-linked Perpetuals, OKX/ICE). (coindesk 2026-10-14)

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- **Plans:** scale the multi-asset super-app; the TSLA giveaway (ends Oct 23) signals an aggressive acquisition push. Expect more asset classes (commodities/FX already name-checked by CSO Kevin Lee).
- **Regulatory backdrop:** the **SEC innovation exemption (May 2026)** and the broader tokenization push (NYSE/ICE-OKX, Nasdaq-Kraken, Securitize, Perpetuals) are **lowering the bar** for crypto venues to touch US equities — tailwind for the category, headwind for BingX's thin "real-not-tokenized" differentiation. BingX itself is **lightly regulated** (AUSTRAC registration; a Lithuanian/FCIS entity transitioning to MiCA) and **blocks the US/UK/EU-core/Singapore** — so this product lives in a **regulatory grey-to-permissive non-US retail market**.
- **Risks / second-order:** (1) **Suitability/consumer-protection** — pushing leveraged-crypto users into US equities via a $10-TSLA gimmick in loosely-regulated jurisdictions invites scrutiny. (2) **Counterparty concentration on Alpaca** — same single point of failure as every Alpaca-distribution partner. (3) **Commoditization race-to-zero** — "0-fee" (MEXC) is already the competitive floor, so BingX monetizes float/spread, a thin, contestable margin. Net: a defensible-strategy / low-novelty / unproven-traction launch — a feature ship, not a market-mover.
