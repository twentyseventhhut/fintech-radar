 Ecommpay Adds Klarna, Giving Consumers a More Flexible Way to Pay 
 Lending
 13.10.2026 10:17 am 
 Ecommpay, the inclusive global payments platform, has added Klarna, the everyday finance network, to its payment choices, giving merchants a simple way to offer millions of shoppers greater choice, transparency and control at checkout. 
 “Flexible payment options are fundamental to a strong checkout experience for e-commerce merchants and Klarna has been a popular choice for consumers and businesses,” explained Roman Tazetdinov, Head of Strategic Partnerships, Ecommpay.  “Used by millions of shoppers every month, we are confident adding Klarna to the Ecommpay payment platform will deliver real conversion gains for our merchants.” 
 "Shoppers today expect to pay their way: no faff, no friction, no nasty surprises," said Klarna Country Manager UK & Ireland Marc O'Fathaigh. "Ecommpay is a natural fit: we both believe good ways to pay should be available to everyone. Through a single integration, this partnership puts Klarna in front of merchants worldwide, expanding our reach and giving millions of shoppers more control over their money." 
 From small businesses to global enterprises across retail, travel and hospitality, fintech, mobility, digital goods and insurance, Ecommpay merchants gain access to Klarna’s flexible payment options including Pay in Full, Pay in 3 and Pay in 4 instalments, Pay in 30 days, and longer-term Financing, via one easy integration with no extra technical work or third-party systems needed. 
 “We know that many shoppers actively look for Klarna at checkout,” added Roman Tazetdinov. “Adding it to the Ecommpay payment platform gives merchants access to a large and engaged market, reducing hesitation at the point of purchase and giving customers another payment option at checkout. And it can be added to a merchant’s wider payment set-up in a single integration, eliminating unnecessary operational overhead.”
