 Родители могут открыть отдельные счета на каждого несовершеннолетнего родственника, а также получить увеличенный налоговый вычет
 
 В «ВТБ Онлайн» и офисах банка теперь можно оформить программу детских сбережений от «НПФ ВТБ». Договор оформляется за две минуты, а за 10 лет можно получить до 360 тыс. рублей господдержки.

 Для открытия программы нужно заполнить анкету в «ВТБ Онлайн» и внести первый взнос от 30 тыс. рублей. После этого счет можно пополнять в любое время и на любую сумму без обязательного графика. Программу можно открыть на ребенка или другого близкого несовершеннолетнего родственника, а на каждого ребенка — отдельный договор.

 Если один взрослый оформляет несколько договоров, софинансирование распределяется между ними пропорционально взносам. Размер господдержки зависит от дохода родителя: при доходе до 80 тыс. рублей государство добавляет 1 рубль на каждый рубль, от 80 до 150 тыс. рублей — 1 рубль на 2 рубля взносов, свыше 150 тыс. рублей — 1 рубль на 4 рубля.

 Родители также могут получить увеличенный налоговый вычет: по договорам в пользу ребенка в него можно включить взносы до 500 тыс. рублей в год. Кроме того, программа предусматривает ежегодный инвестиционный доход.

 Досрочно выйти из программы можно с возвратом всех внесенных средств. Государственное софинансирование и накопленный доход ребенок сможет получить через 15 лет после оформления договора.

 Роман Шаехов, заместитель руководителя блока розничного бизнеса ВТБ, отметил, что на детскую программу приходится 10% средств, привлеченных через цифровые каналы в рамках ПДС, а более половины счетов пополняются регулярно.

# Context-enrichment: Sber launches state-backed long-term savings plan for children
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

> SOURCING CAVEAT (load-bearing): The note's title and aggregated blurb attribute the launch to **Sber** ("News & Trends by Sber" channel: "launched online signup of a children's PDS with up to ₽360k state support over 10 years"). BUT the ONLY attached primary-source text (bosfera.ru) is a **VTB** press release about **VTB / НПФ ВТБ** opening online children's PDS signup (~2026-10-02). So the sourced detail (2-minute signup, ₽30k minimum first contribution, income-tiered co-financing ratios, Roman Shaekhov quote, "10% of digitally-raised PDS funds are the children's program") describes VTB, not Sber. SberNPF independently does offer children's PDS online via SberBank Online (section Накопления → Сбережения и пенсии). Treat the Sber-specific "launch" as **plausible but un-sourced in this note** — the de-PR'd facts below are VTB's; the Sber framing is a channel attribution, not a verified Sber press release. (analysis)

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
What this item really is: the **distribution/channel enablement of signing up a CHILDREN'S PDS contract remotely/online** — not a new product and not new state terms. The underlying "ПДС for children" and the ₽360k-over-10-years co-financing have existed since the PDS program went live Jan 2024; what changed in 2026 is (a) a legislative amendment letting parents open PDS contracts for minors and (b) banks/NPFs (VTB first per the cited source, Sber per the channel) wiring that into their apps for 2-minute online enrollment.

De-PR'd mechanics (from the cited VTB source + external PDS docs):
- **State co-financing: up to ₽36,000/yr for 10 years = up to ₽360,000** total. The "₽360k" headline is the long-standing universal PDS cap, NOT a children-specific sweetener. (external: minfin, npfsberbanka)
- **Income-tiered match (parent's income):** ≤₽80k/mo → ₽1 per ₽1; ₽80–150k → ₽1 per ₽2; >₽150k → ₽1 per ₽4. (cited VTB text) — again the standard PDS co-financing ladder, not child-specific.
- **Tax deduction:** for contracts in a child's favour, the deduction base rises to **₽500k/yr per parent** (vs the standard ₽400k personal cap; the extra ₽100k is usable only via the child's account), effective **from 2026-09-01**. Max refund ₽65k–₽110k/yr depending on the НДФЛ rate. (external)
- **Terms:** first contribution from **₽30k**, then top-ups any time/any amount, no mandatory schedule; separate contract per child; if one adult holds several contracts, co-financing is split pro-rata to contributions. Early exit returns own contributions; **state co-financing + accrued income release only after 15 years** (or the "wait 5 years to keep state money" rule tightening from 2026-09-01). Insured up to ₽2.8mn. (cited VTB text + external)

**+ Why structured this way / what it reveals:** The real change is a **regulatory + UX unlock** (minors made eligible; online/Госуслуги signup), which converts a previously branch-friction product into an app funnel. The "₽360k from the state for your child" headline reuses the universal co-financing cap, so the marketing overstates novelty: nothing about the *economics* is child-specific except the +₽100k tax-deduction headroom. The event is banks racing to capture the children's cohort as a cheap, 15-year-locked, sticky liability and a multi-generational cross-sell hook. (analysis)

## [1] Competitors / peers
Russian PDS/NPF landscape (see [[Russia's long-term savings program reignites pension-fund competition]]):
- **СберНПФ** — dominant distributor: ~68% of registered PDS accounts end-2024, >80% of Q1 2026 ОПС switchers, ~1mn new PDS accounts/quarter. Owns the retail funnel via the Sber app/branches.
- **ВТБ НПФ** — #2 by AUM; the cited source is VTB's own children's-PDS online launch (~2026-10-02), with VTB disclosing children's PDS = ~10% of digitally-raised PDS funds and >half of children's accounts topped up regularly. VTB's broader PDS inflow had ~halved QoQ in Q1 2026 → it is using the children's angle to defend share.
- **НПФ «Будущее», Gazprombank / «Благосостояние»** — large institutional players rounding out the field.

**+ Why the lay of the land is this way / 2nd-order:** PDS competition is a **distribution war, not a yield war** (2025 PDS yields clustered 15–22% with little spread). A children's contract is 15-year-locked → whoever signs the parent first owns a near-permanent relationship, making the children's cohort strategically valuable out of proportion to current balances. Sber's app funnel means it structurally wins any "we now offer it online" race; VTB's press-release-first posture here is a challenger trying to claim the headline before Sber's scale buries it. (analysis)

## [2] Company history / fit
PDS is a Minfin+CBR policy instrument (live Jan 2024) to channel household savings into long-term domestic funding — successor logic to the 2014-frozen mandatory funded pension. Growth: 2.8mn joiners 2024 → >9mn 2025; **>12mn participants and ~₽938bn attracted by mid-2026**; by 2026-07-01 ~13mn contracts and >₽1trn raised. The children's/online enablement fits the state's push toward the Presidential **≥1%-of-GDP (~₽2.28trn) 2026 inflow target** — a ~2.4x gap still to close, which is the real pressure behind every new signup channel. (see [[Russia's long-term savings program reignites pension-fund competition]])

**+ Why the actors act this way:** Under sanctions/closed external markets the state needs "long money"; it offers co-financing (10yr) + tax breaks, and banks-with-NPFs treat PDS as cheap, sticky, locked liability + cross-sell. Extending to minors both broadens the base and lengthens the lock (15yr) — maximal stickiness. Companion instrument: the June 2026 housing-savings ("жилищный вклад") law, the same "soft mobilization of private savings" doctrine ([[Финтехно: Russia approves new private-savings mobilization law]]). (analysis)

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
**Not a new product.** The genuine novelty is narrow: (1) minors became eligible to be PDS beneficiaries and (2) the contract can now be opened **remotely/online** (and via Госуслуги), both newly enabled in 2026. The ₽360k co-financing and income-tiered match are the universal PDS terms, reused. The only child-specific economic sweetener is the **+₽100k tax-deduction headroom (₽400k→₽500k base), effective 2026-09-01**.

Traction = early and thin. The only hard number is VTB's self-reported "children's PDS ≈ 10% of digitally-raised PDS funds, >half of accounts topped up regularly" — a vendor claim, not audited, and no Sber-specific children's figure is in the note. (analysis)

**+ Who captures the margin:** As across PDS, value accrues to the **state (long money) and the distributor (the bank's funnel)**; the saver locks money for 15 years for a co-financing match whose real (post-inflation) value is modest at elevated CBR rates. The children's wrapper mainly extends lock duration and deepens cross-sell — the value-add to the family is real but second-order to the distributor's. (analysis)

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- Expect every major NPF (Sber, VTB, «Будущее», Gazprombank) to roll out online children's PDS in parallel through late 2026; this is a feature-parity race, not a differentiator.
- The 2026-09-01 rule tightening (5-year wait to keep state money; ₽500k child deduction) is the policy backdrop steering inflows toward the GDP target.
- **Risk / counterintuitive 2nd-order:** marketing "₽360k from the state" as a children's benefit risks mis-selling scrutiny (the CBR already flagged ₽2.7bn of 2025 early-switch losses — see [[Russia's long-term savings program reignites pension-fund competition]]); a 15-year lock on a child's account is a hard sell if real yields lag inflation. Concentration, not competition, remains the structural reality: Sber's funnel likely absorbs most of this cohort regardless of who announces first. (analysis/hypothesis)
