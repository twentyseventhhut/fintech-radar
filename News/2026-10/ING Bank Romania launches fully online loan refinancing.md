ING Bank Romania launches 100% online refinancing, directly through Home’Bank
 ING Bank is launching a 100% online refinancing option, directly through Home’Bank, via the ING Personal personal loan. Customers can transfer their loans from other financial institutions anytime, anywhere, without additional trips to the bank and without paper documents, for financing of up to RON 200,000. 
 
Through refinancing, customers can consolidate existing financial products—such as credit cards, store cards, overdrafts, lines of credit, “Buy Now, Pay Later” (BNPL) loans, personal loans, and even, soon, mortgages—into a single loan. In addition, they can request an additional amount for important projects in their lives, ranging from home renovations to other personal investments.
 “In 2017, we were the first bank in Romania to demonstrate that a consumer loan could be granted 100% online, in just a few minutes. We then expanded access and added the ING Credit Card, the ExtraROL line of credit, and even ING Mortgage for new loans. Today, we approve over 13,000 online loans every month, through an experience that ING customers appreciate and recommend to others. Everything is available online, 24 hours a day: from applying for financing to routine loan-related operations, such as making payments, checking your balance, obtaining bank approvals, or changing the borrower structure ,” said Iulia Rusu, Head of Lending at ING Bank Romania. 
 More Than Just Transferring a Loan 
Refinancing through ING Personal is available in Home’Bank, 24 hours a day, Monday through Sunday. When refinancing through ING Personal, customers can obtain amounts of up to RON 200,000, which are automatically transferred to their checking account upon loan approval. Through this process, customers can optimize their existing costs, consolidate multiple financial products into a single loan, and, when needed, access additional funds for important projects in their lives.
 Control and transparency throughout the entire term of the loan 
The process doesn’t end once the loan is approved. Customers can manage their loan throughout its entire term directly through ING Home’Bank, including making early payments or paying it off in full.
With ING loans, customers know the exact costs even before they apply. The interest rate is fixed and guaranteed, tailored to the customer’s chosen options. 
 Unlimited simulations, with no impact on approval chances 
Customers need to compare options and scenarios when making an important financial decision. That’s why, in Home’Bank, they can run as many loan simulations as they want, without this affecting the amount ING can offer them or their chances of approval at ING Bank. This allows them to analyze different options and choose the solution that best fits their needs.
For approximately 7 out of 10 customers, the application review fee is 0 lei, based on salary agreements ING has with their employers. Eligibility is verified automatically, and the information is displayed during the application process.
 Nearly two decades of innovation 
ING Bank Romania was among the first banks to introduce 100% online personal loans in 2017, thereby fundamentally changing the way Romanians access loans. Since then, it has granted over 400,000 loans, and today, 9 out of 10 customers receive their loan instantly, in a process that takes, on average, between 5 and 8 minutes.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened (one line).** On 2026-10-21, ING Bank Romania launched a 100% online (end-to-end digital, no branch visit, no paper) loan-refinancing option inside its Home'Bank app, delivered via its ING Personal consumer loan: customers can move/consolidate debt held at other lenders into a single ING loan of up to RON ~200,000. (business-review.eu; thepaypers.com)

**[1] Facts / what's concretely new.**
- Refinancing is now self-service in Home'Bank, 24/7, with funds auto-disbursed to the customer's current account on approval; fixed, guaranteed interest rate disclosed before applying; unlimited loan simulations with no impact on approval odds; ~7 in 10 customers pay a RON 0 review fee (via ING's salary agreements with employers).
- Consolidation scope: credit cards, store cards, overdrafts, credit lines, BNPL loans and personal loans today — and "soon" mortgages. Customers can also top up for additional projects (renovations etc.).
- This extends, not starts, ING's digital-lending stack: ING claims it was first in Romania with a 100% online consumer loan in 2017, has since granted 400,000+ loans, approves 13,000+ online loans/month, and says 9 in 10 borrowers get funds on the spot in ~5–8 minutes. Spokesperson: Iulia Rusu, Head of Lending, ING Bank Romania. (business-review.eu; nocash.ro)
- Scale/positioning: ING Romania is ~#7 by assets (~8.4% share), behind Banca Transilvania (~22%) and BCR (~13.8%). (romania-insider.com)

**[2] Why it matters.** Refinancing/consolidation is the stickiest retail-lending battleground in CEE because it poaches balances from rivals rather than relying on new-credit demand. Moving the full refi flow into the app removes the last friction (the branch visit / paperwork) and turns ING's installed Home'Bank base into a lower-cost acquisition channel. The "soon mortgages" line signals the real prize: a 100% digital mortgage-refi flow.

**[3] Context — Romanian/CEE digital lending (internal + external).**
- Internal ING trajectory in Romania is payments-then-lending digitization: see [[ING Bank launches instant euro incoming payments in Romania]] (Dec 2025). ING's broader European app/payments push also appears in [[ING enables Wero online shopping payments in Germany]] and [[ING Germany joins Wero account-to-account payments network]]. (Note: the same-day [[ING Germany online banking down in unrelated technical fault]] is a DISTINCT, unrelated outage event.)
- CEE digital-lending comparables in the DB: [[Revolut reaches 3 million customers in Germany]] (neobank scale-up in Europe) and the broader [[This Week in Fintech UK and Europe fintech deal highlights]] thread.
- External competitive set is already crowded on digital refi/consolidation: **BCR** advertises a fully digital 100% approval flow in its "George" app for both home purchase and refinancing — i.e. ING is catching up on refi, not pioneering it (bcr.ro). **Banca Transilvania** pushes app-based lending via "BT Direct" (bancatransilvania.ro). **Revolut** (reportedly Romania's largest bank by cardholders, ~5M customers) offers app consumer loans up to RON 125k from 6.95%; **Salt Bank** offers fully digital personal loans up to RON 200k, claiming a 2m50s digital-approval record. (romania-insider.com; financemagnates.com; wall-street.ro)

**[4] Open questions / what to watch.** Timing of the promised mortgage-refi digitization; whether "100% online refi" is genuinely zero-touch for cross-bank payoff (settling the old lender) or still needs manual steps; pricing competitiveness vs Revolut/Salt sub-7% rates; and whether ING discloses refi-specific volumes (the 13k/month and 400k figures are all-lending, not refi).

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team / challenge**

1. Is this a genuinely new capability or incremental? — Incremental. ING already granted 100% online consumer loans since 2017; this adds *refinancing/consolidation* as a self-service flow in Home'Bank. New feature, not a new product line.

2. Is it actually live? — Yes; press materials dated 2026-10-21 describe it as launched and available in Home'Bank 24/7, not a "coming soon." (business-review.eu, bursa.ro)

3. Is ING first in Romania on digital refi? — No. BCR claims a fully digital 100% approval flow in "George" for both home purchase and refinancing; so the "first/innovator" framing applies only to ING's own 2017 consumer-loan milestone, not to online refi. Open on exact comparability of flows.

4. Does "100% online" include settling the loan at the old lender? — Open. Sources say funds are auto-transferred to the customer's current account on approval; they do not confirm automated payoff/transfer to the originating lender, which is the hard part of refi UX.

5. Mortgage refi — real or marketing? — Framed as "soon." No date, no commitment. Treat as roadmap signal, not shipped.

6. Are the headline stats refi-specific? — No. 400,000+ loans, 13,000/month, 5–8 min, 9-in-10 instant are ALL-lending figures reused as credibility; they do not measure refi uptake. Don't attribute them to this launch.

7. The RON 200,000 cap — competitive? — Matches Salt Bank's RON 200k digital personal loan and beats Revolut's RON 125k; so ceiling is competitive, pricing undisclosed here. Open.

8. Is the RON 0 review fee universal? — No. ~7 in 10 customers, conditional on ING having a salary agreement with their employer. Material caveat omitted by the one-line headline.

9. Any regulatory/AML angle to cross-bank debt transfer? — Not addressed in sources; standard consumer-credit/affordability rules presumably apply. Open.

10. Is this conflated with the same-day ING Germany outage? — No. Distinct events: [[ING Germany online banking down in unrelated technical fault]] is a German IT outage; this is a Romanian product launch. Confirmed separate.

11. Single-source risk? — Low. Multiple independent RO/EN outlets (business-review.eu, thepaypers, bursa.ro, profit.ro, bankingnews.ro) carried it, though all trace to the ING press release — i.e. one primary source, promotional in nature.

12. Does it move ING's market position? — Marginally at best near-term. ING is ~#7 by assets (~8.4%); refi is a share-shift tactic but a single-channel feature won't reorder the market vs BT/BCR/Revolut.

13. Is there a competitive-response read? — Yes: this is ING defending retail-lending turf against Revolut's app loans and Salt Bank's sub-3-min digital approval, plus BCR's George refi. Catch-up more than leapfrog.

14. What would make this a bigger story? — Disclosed refi volumes/balances migrated, a dated mortgage-refi launch, or sub-7% pricing undercutting Revolut/Salt.

Importance: 2/5 — A live, well-executed but incremental digital feature from a mid-tier (#7) Romanian bank, catching up to rivals (BCR/Salt/Revolut) rather than setting a new bar; single promotional primary source; no disclosed refi-specific traction. Worth logging for CEE digital-lending trend tracking, not a market-mover.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Romanian digital retail lending / consumer-credit refinancing. Household consumer credit stock ~RON 81bn (NBR data, all-time high RON 81.4bn in Nov-2025, via tradingeconomics/NBR, as of Jan-2026); Romania's fintech market projected ~€1.8bn by 2026 at >15% CAGR with digital-banking penetration seen >65% (per beaumont-capitalmarkets guide, 2026) — treat the fintech-TAM figure as a secondary/vendor estimate, not NBR data. Structure: concentrated at the top (Banca Transilvania largest, BCR/Erste, ING, OTP) but still low credit penetration — banking assets ~50% of GDP and ~3 in 10 adults unbanked (per techfinitive, 2026), so refinancing is largely share-shift between banks rather than market expansion. Why now: 2026 credit market is in "stabilisation and prudence" mode (AVBS, via business-review), making cost-consolidation / rate-locking products attractive to borrowers; the fixed-rate, fully-online debt-consolidation pitch rides the 2017→2026 digitalization curve ING itself seeded (first 100% online consumer loan in Romania, 2017).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: online-origination share, approval speed, cost-to-income, and refinance-in/switch volume (none of these disclosed as hard numbers here beyond ING's own stats). Key players & basis of competition — the race is on fully-digital self-serve flows, not price alone: BCR (Erste) runs George, "the only bank in Romania offering a fully digital mortgage flow" incl. refinancing, with ~95% of individual products sold fully-digital and 500k+ products accessed via George in Q1-2026 (per bcr.ro); Banca Transilvania pushes BT Pay + "Singura Rata" refinancing and is the largest bank (per BT/wikipedia); Revolut is the largest by cardholders (~4.5m clients, ~24% population penetration, per techfinitive) but search surfaced no Romania consumer-refinancing product — a distribution threat, not yet a lending one. ING's position: ahead on consumer digital-lending maturity (self-reported 400k+ loans since 2017, 13k+ online loans/month, 9 in 10 instant in 5–8 min) but catching up vs BCR on digital-mortgage refinancing (ING flags mortgages only "soon"). Moat `(analysis)`: switching-cost + embedded Home'Bank distribution and employer salary-agreement data (fee-waiver for ~7 in 10 applicants) — a data/distribution advantage, not a structural one.

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation/round/deal attached to this product launch → trading-comp multiples not applicable; "no data" on EV/Revenue, P/E etc. Qualitative comparison only. Internal comps (Romania digital-retail / ING footprint): [[Salt Bank launches Premium and Everyday plans in Romania]], [[Revolut launches Beneficiary Name Display service in Romania]], [[ING Bank launches instant euro incoming payments in Romania]]. These show the same pattern — incumbents + neobanks layering self-serve digital products into Romania — but none carry comparable refinancing economics, so no multiple is computable.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Macroprudential cap on volume** — NBR holds max DSTI at 40% for new household loans (35% median DSTI-O on consumer loans, Mar-2025, per IMF/BNR), with only ~15% of new loans exemptable; consolidation refinancing that adds "additional amount" bumps borrowers toward the ceiling, capping how much ING can actually convert. Second-order: growth is share-shift, not new credit creation.
2. **Share-shift, thin incrementality** — in a ~RON 81bn stock that's flat-to-stabilising (2026 "prudence"), a refinance product grows the book mainly by poaching peers; peers (BCR George, BT) have symmetric digital flows, so churn can run both ways and compress margin.
3. **Silent economics / credit-quality** — PR discloses speed and fee-waivers but is silent on rate level, resulting loan yield, and the credit quality of debt-consolidation borrowers (BNPL/overdraft/credit-card rollups skew to higher-risk cohorts). Second-order: fast fully-online origination of consolidation loans is exactly where asset quality can deteriorate quietly.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This is a feature-parity / defensive move, not a re-rating — ING is matching BCR's digital-switching flow to protect its consumer-lending lead rather than opening a new market. Falsifiable thesis: if ING's monthly online-loan run-rate (now ~13k) and loan-book refinance-in volume climb materially over the next 2–3 quarters, the digital-switching war is live; watch for ING adding the promised fully-online mortgage refinancing (the real battleground vs BCR's George) — absence of that by mid-2026 would signal the launch is incremental, not strategic.
