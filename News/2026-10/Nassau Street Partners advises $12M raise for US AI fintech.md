NEW YORK, NY / ACCESS Newswire / October 16, 2026 / Nassau Street Partners, a leading middle-market corporate advisory firm headquartered in New York, announces the successful completion of a $12 million equity capital raise for a U.S.-based enterprise artificial intelligence company serving the financial services industry.
The transaction, which closed last year, attracted investment from a combination of venture capital firms and family offices, reflecting continued institutional interest in artificial intelligence solutions designed to modernize financial services.
The company operates an enterprise AI platform focused on helping financial institutions streamline operations, improve efficiency, and leverage advanced technology across their businesses. The financing was structured to support the company's continued growth, product development, and expansion into new markets.
Nassau Street Partners served in an advisory capacity in connection with the financing, supporting the company's capital formation strategy and engagement with prospective institutional investors.
 "This transaction illustrates the increasing convergence of venture capital and family office investment in the artificial intelligence sector," said a representative of Nassau Street Partners. "The company's technology, scalable business model, and significant addressable market generated interest across both investor groups. By positioning the opportunity around its long-term growth potential and commercial applications, we were able to support a financing process that brought together investors with complementary investment objectives." 
The transaction highlights the growing role of family offices alongside traditional venture capital investors in financing emerging technology companies. As private capital markets continue to evolve, companies are increasingly seeking diversified investor bases that combine institutional expertise, strategic relationships, and patient capital.
The successful financing further demonstrates Nassau Street Partners' commitment to supporting high-growth businesses in accessing private capital through its network of family offices, venture capital firms, and institutional investors across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.
The identity of the company remains confidential.
 About Nassau Street Partners 
Nassau Street Partners is a New York-headquartered middle-market corporate advisory firm specializing in capital formation, private placements, mergers and acquisitions, and strategic financial advisory services. The firm works with founders, management teams, and institutional investors across a broad range of industries, connecting businesses with family offices, private equity firms, venture capital funds, and other sophisticated capital providers worldwide.
