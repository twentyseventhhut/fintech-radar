 Nubank has rolled out the AI-powered compliance platform of Cardamon , a Y Combinator-backed RegTech firm, across every jurisdiction where it currently operates, marking a significant test of whether automated tools can keep pace with one of the fastest-growing financial institutions in the world. 
The partnership gives Nubank’s worldwide team of compliance specialists a suite of AI agents that function as digital teammates.
Cardamon secured the Nubank mandate earlier this year and has since completed deployment throughout the bank’s operating footprint. The platform is designed to support the lender as it expands into new markets and introduces new products, while keeping it aligned with regulatory obligations in each region.
Speed sits at the heart of the proposition. According to Cardamon, its technology can map an institution’s specific regulatory position up to 20 times faster than legacy compliance systems or manual in-house processes.
For a business scaling as rapidly as Nubank, that acceleration could prove decisive in reducing the friction that typically slows market entry and product launches.
Nubank carries a market capitalisation of $70bn and serves more than 140m customers globally. Its rapid growth has made regulatory compliance a strategic priority rather than a back-office function, and the bank has turned to Cardamon to help it continue innovating without weakening its compliance standards.
Cardamon, part of Y Combinator’s Winter 2025 cohort, builds AI tools that help regulated businesses understand and manage their compliance obligations. The company positions its platform as a way for firms to simplify regulatory work in a sector where both the pace of change and the cost of error are high.
The firm said Nubank is one of a growing roster of leading companies that already rely on its technology, and indicated that further clients are expected to follow.
Nubank compliance expert Amanda Hauch F. Silva said, “Partnering with Cardamon allows us to enhance our regulatory capabilities, streamline complex compliance processes and empower our teams with intelligent tools that support faster and more informed decision-making. We are excited about what we can achieve together as we continue building a world-class compliance organisation.”
