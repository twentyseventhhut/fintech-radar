Rulen secures €1.8M to turn tax rules into action with AI
Italian AI startup Rulen (formerly LexSA) has raised
€1.8 million in its first funding round to further develop its AI-powered
platform for the tax and accounting sector and support its commercial
expansion. The round was led by VC Partners , with participation from Growth
Engine, AME Ventures, Zest through its Magic Mind vehicle, Alpha Venture and a
group of industry business angels.
Launched at the end of 2025, Rulen develops an AI
operating platform designed to automate tax and accounting workflows, helping
professionals reduce repetitive tasks and manage compliance processes more
efficiently. 
The platform integrates with existing software and third-party
systems, including the Italian Revenue Agency's online taxpayer records
(Cassetto Fiscale), allowing firms to automate processes from data collection
to review and document production while maintaining professional oversight.
The company aims to address challenges facing the
Italian tax and accounting sector, including a shortage of younger
professionals and growing compliance workloads. New registrations in the
profession have declined by 27.3 per cent, while professionals under 40 account
for just 15 per cent of the sector. At the same time, many firms continue to
rely on manual processes and fragmented software systems, increasing the need
for automation tools that can support business growth without a proportional
increase in staffing.
Rulen's platform is already used by law and tax
firms, specialist practices, groups consolidating professional firms and
multinational companies.
The funding announcement coincides with the
company's rebranding from LexSA to Rulen, reflecting its transition from an AI
research assistant to a platform capable of executing end-to-end workflows.
 Simone Hong , co-founder and CEO of Rulen, explained
that the company was created in response to challenges experienced directly by
its founders, particularly the difficulty of recruiting junior professionals
and the time spent on repetitive administrative work. He added:
 Our goal is to make AI adoption simple, concrete
and scalable. With this round and the move to Rulen, we want to accelerate our
transformation from assistant to agentic platform, capable of automating
end-to-end workflows without professionals losing control and verifiability. 
 Claudio Cama , co-founder and CTO of Rulen,
highlighted the company's plans to expand automation across additional
activities by integrating new systems and developing AI agents capable of
handling execution tasks independently, allowing professionals to focus on
higher-value decisions.
With the new funding, Rulen plans to accelerate
product development and commercial growth while expanding its team,
particularly in sales, marketing and product roles, including the recruitment
of additional tax specialists.
