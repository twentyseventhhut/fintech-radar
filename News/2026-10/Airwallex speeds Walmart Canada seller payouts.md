# Airwallex speeds Walmart Canada seller payouts

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇨🇦 Airwallex helps Walmart Canada sellers receive faster USD and CAD payouts. Canadian businesses can connect Airwallex to Walmart Seller Center to receive marketplace payouts into their Airwallex global accounts, hold funds in USD or CAD, and reduce eligible transfer and FX costs. Sellers can also use the funds to pay suppliers or manage business spending through the platform.

Frequently asked questions
What are the scenarios that will prevent successful payouts?
1. Entity information mismatch or connection error : Payouts might fail if your business registration number or country of incorporation on Airwallex and Walmart don't align. This could also stem from a faulty connection. 
2. Missing connection: Absence of a connection between Airwallex and Walmart can prevent validation of your business details. 
If any of these issues arise, Airwallex can retain the funds for 14 days, offering time for resolution before funds are returned to Walmart. 

My Walmart store connection is showing invalid status, how can I rectify?
If your Walmart store connection status shows as invalid, click the “Reconnect” button to restore a faulty connection.
