# Brazil tightens Open Finance partnership rules via Resolution 21

> [!info] 2026-10-28 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: This Week in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[This Week in Fintech] 🇧🇷Banco Central do Brasil and the National Monetary Council tightened the rules for Open Finance partnerships through Joint Resolution No. 21. Customers must now give separate consent for each partner company that receives their data, and partners can no longer use names that resemble banks or payment institutions.
