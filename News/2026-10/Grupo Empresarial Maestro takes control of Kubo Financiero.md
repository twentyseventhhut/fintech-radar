# Grupo Empresarial Maestro takes control of Kubo Financiero

> [!info] 2026-10-19 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: This Week in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[This Week in Fintech] 🇲🇽Grupo Empresarial Maestro (GEMA) took control of Kubo Financiero, the digital Sofipo, after the CNBV approved the roughly $33 million deal, effective October 19. Kubo will add Crédito Maestro’s payroll loans to its credit, savings and investment products, with Gerardo Piña as CEO.
