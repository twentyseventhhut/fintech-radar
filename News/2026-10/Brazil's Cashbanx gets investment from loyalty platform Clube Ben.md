## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** Clube Ben — a loyalty, engagement and relationship platform built by Proxy Media — made an **undisclosed, strategic investment** in **Cashbanx**, a Belo Horizonte cashback-infrastructure startup (founded 2019, Andre Giannetti). The deal is structured as a **convertible note taking a minority stake**, with a **commercial ("vesting") mechanism** that lets Clube Ben grow its equity if jointly-set revenue targets are met. The stated goal is to plug Cashbanx's cashback engine into Clube Ben / Proxy Media distribution channels and expand loyalty products across Clube Ben's client base. Cashbanx says it aims to grow its GMV/transaction volume **18x in 2026** vs. the prior year, targeting **>R$500M in 2027**. Reported 2026-10 by startupi.com.br ([source](https://startupi.com.br/clube-ben-crescimento-investimento-cashbanx-cashback/)).

**[1] Why it matters.** This is a **strategic distribution deal dressed as a financing**, not a classic VC round. Clube Ben brings a channel (>30 new clients in 2026 — Renault, Michelin, Unimed, Supermercados Zona Sul, Mantecorp SkinCare, MedSênior — and a 200+ client target for YE2026; 48% growth over 12 months). The revenue-linked vesting ties Clube Ben's upside directly to Cashbanx adoption, aligning both on volume rather than a headline valuation. It plays into Brazil's fast-growing cashback/rewards market (research houses peg the Brazil cashback-programs opportunity in the multi-billion-USD range, growing ~15-17% annually), where cashback is shifting from promo rebates toward payment-flow steering ([market databook](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/brazil-cashback-programs-opportunity-databook-100000196.html)).

**[2] Timeline / dated chain.**
- **2019** — Cashbanx founded in Belo Horizonte; white-label/API cashback marketplace for banks, fintechs and retailers.
- **Aug 2023** — Cashbanx raises **R$20M from SRM Ventures** (equity + tech support) to scale cashback capacity; SRM's André Szapiro and ex-Nomad Ernani Assis join its board. Target then: R$1B GMV "in coming years" ([startupi](https://startupi.com.br/cashbanx-recebe-aporte/), [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/gente-em-fintechs/andre-szapiro-da-srm-ventures-e-ernani-assis-ex-nomad-entram-para-o-conselho-da-cashbanx/)). **This is a SEPARATE prior deal — not the one being enriched.**
- **Oct 2026** — Clube Ben (Proxy Media) announces the **undisclosed convertible-note investment** with revenue-linked vesting; Cashbanx guides 18x GMV in 2026 and >R$500M in 2027 ([startupi](https://startupi.com.br/clube-ben-crescimento-investimento-cashbanx-cashback/), [Business Moment](https://businessmoment.com.br/clube-ben-cresce-48-e-investe-em-startup-de-cashback/), [Quasa](https://quasa.io/pt/media/aporte-na-cashbanx-nao-revela-valor-a-participacao-depende-da-receita)).

**[3] Players & landscape.** Cashbanx is a **B2B/white-label cashback-infrastructure** play (API, for banks/fintechs/retailers) — distinct from consumer-facing cashback marketplaces. The reference consumer comp in Brazil is **Méliuz** (public, Belo Horizonte, founded 2011); international analogues cited for the category include Ibotta, Rakuten, CashKaro, ShopBack. On the infra side it competes for issuer/retailer integrations against in-house and orchestration players. Internal comps in the DB are mostly consumer/payments LATAM rather than cashback-infra: [[WhatsApp and GenAI reshape Brazil's payments via PicPay and Nubank]] (PicPay runs large cashback programs), [[Binance and Mastercard launch crypto card in Brazil]] (card-linked rewards), [[Brazil's Bull raises $1.8M pre-seed for worker credit]] and [[Zippi raises ~$15.9M via securitized structure in Brazil]] (Brazil fintech funding comps, though both are larger/earlier-stage credit rather than loyalty).

**[4] Open questions / what to watch.** (a) **Deal size & stake** are undisclosed — magnitude and the vesting trigger thresholds are unknown, so economic significance can't be sized. (b) The **18x GMV** and **>R$500M 2027** figures are company guidance off an undisclosed small base — treat as marketing, not audited. (c) Execution risk: whether Clube Ben's ~200 clients actually route meaningful cashback volume through Cashbanx. (d) Competitive pressure from Méliuz/PicPay and issuer-native programs. Sources are PT-language startup press (startupi, Business Moment, Quasa) echoing a single company announcement; no independent financials.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Brazilian cashback is a fast-growing subvertical of the broader loyalty/payments stack: ~US$7.78bn in 2025 rising at ~15% CAGR toward ~US$14.67bn by 2030 (per Brazil Cashback Programs Opportunity Databook, via Yahoo Finance/ResearchAndMarkets, as of 2026). The adjacent Brazil loyalty market is ~US$1.89bn (2025) → ~US$3.57bn by 2030 at ~15.5% CAGR (per Brazil Loyalty Business Report 2026, via GlobeNewswire). Structure is moderately fragmented: cashback/loyalty value is being pulled out of standalone "promotional rebate" apps and embedded into payment flows — Pix, digital wallets, and bank/fintech apps (Nubank, Banco Inter) — so the strategic battleground has shifted to who sits closest to the transaction rail. Why now: Pix ubiquity makes real-time cashback-at-checkout technically cheap, and corporate loyalty operators (Clube Ben/Proxy Media) want payment-flow steering rather than points catalogues — hence a loyalty platform buying cashback infrastructure rather than building it. (analysis)

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: GMV/transaction volume routed, take rate on cashback flow, and attach/redemption rate across the partner base. Key players span (a) listed pure-play cashback — Méliuz (B3: CASH3, 30M+ registered users); (b) coalition loyalty/points — Livelo, Dotz (B3: DOTZ3), Smiles; (c) embedded bank/retail loyalty — Nubank, Banco Inter, Magazine Luiza, Mercado Livre. Basis of competition is distribution and rail-proximity, not standalone product. Recent move: Clube Ben (Proxy Media) reports +48% growth over the trailing 12 months and 30+ new enterprise clients in 2026 (Renault, Michelin, Unimed, Supermercados Zona Sul), per startupi.com.br / businessmoment.com.br. Cashbanx's position: niche infrastructure supplier catching a distribution tailwind — its moat is not scale (it is pre-scale) but becoming the embedded cashback layer inside Clube Ben/Proxy Media's channels (switching-cost moat if integration deepens). (analysis)

**Comps & multiples.** No comparable Brazil loyalty/cashback deals surfaced in-base (corpus "cashback" hits are non-Brazilian: [[PayPal offers 5% cashback on BNPL purchases]], [[Kard raises $15M for rewards infrastructure]]; semsearch down — OpenRouter 402). External public comp — Méliuz (CASH3), the closest listed pure-play:
- Market cap ≈ BRL 626M; EV ≈ BRL 520M (stockanalysis.com, as of Oct 2026).
- 2025 net revenue = BRL 460.2M (+26% YoY), 2025 adj. EBITDA = BRL 92.9M (InfoMoney / visnoinvest, FY2025).
- EV/Revenue = BRL 520M / BRL 460.2M = **1.1x**; P/S = BRL 626M / BRL 460.2M = **1.4x**; EV/EBITDA = BRL 520M / BRL 92.9M = **5.6x**.
These are low/cheap multiples vs typical fintech ranges (EV/Rev ~0.5–20x; EV/EBITDA ~8–25x) — reflecting modest ~26% growth and a Bitcoin-treasury overhang depressing Méliuz's rating, so NOT a clean read-across. Cashbanx is private and the round is undisclosed; a convertible-note with revenue-linked vesting means no implied valuation — multiple = **no data**. Cashbanx itself: 2026 target GMV +18x YoY, 2027 target revenue >BRL 500M (self-reported, not live — `[UNSOURCED]`/projection only).

**Risk flags.**
1. **Undisclosed, structurally thin deal** — convertible note, minority stake, revenue-vesting. Economics and valuation are silent; "investment" may be small and contingent (de-PR: announced, not a priced equity round). Second-order: signals Clube Ben is testing, not committing — integration could stall.
2. **Rail disintermediation / platform dependence** — Cashbanx's cashback value can be captured by whoever owns the Pix/wallet rail (Nubank, banks, Mercado Pago). As a thin infra layer dependent on Clube Ben's distribution, margin and continuity hinge on one partner's channels.
3. **Projection risk** — 18x GMV in one year and >BRL 500M 2027 revenue are aggressive self-reported targets off a small base; execution/credibility gap is high and unverifiable.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Signals a shift in Brazilian loyalty from points-catalogue coalitions (Livelo/Dotz/Smiles) toward embedded, payment-flow cashback — loyalty operators buying/integrating fintech rails rather than running rewards in isolation. Falsifiable thesis: if Clube Ben converts the note and expands the stake on revenue triggers within 12–18 months, embedded-cashback-via-loyalty consolidation is real; if the stake stays minority and GMV misses the 18x target, this was a cheap option, not a strategic pivot. (analysis)
