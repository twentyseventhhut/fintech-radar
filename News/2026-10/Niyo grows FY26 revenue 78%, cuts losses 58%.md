 Travel fintech platform Niyo’s income grew 78% year-on-year to Rs 178 crore in FY26, compared with Rs 100 crore in FY25, according to the company’s press release. 
 The company also cut its losses by 58% to Rs 33 crore in FY26 from Rs 78 crore a year earlier. Niyo said the improvement came from better efficiency, including the use of AI in customer service, operations, marketing and product development. 
 Niyo’s operating expenses rose 20% to Rs 201 crore in FY26 from Rs 168 crore in FY25. Employee expenses increased 17% to Rs 92 crore from Rs 79 crore. According to Niyo, the increase in employee costs was mainly due to the expansion of Kanji Forex , which operates under Niyo’s own AD-II licence.  
 Despite the higher spending, Niyo’s EBITDA loss nearly halved to Rs 32 crore in FY26 from Rs 77 crore in FY25, the press release added. 
 Niyo is also expanding beyond its core travel business. The company said its revenue is now coming from forex cash, remittances, flights, hotels, experiences, international eSIMs and insurance, among other services. It is also selling more forex and travel products to its existing customers. 
 The company has set up Finnew Global Solutions IFSC in GIFT City and received approval to operate as a Payment Service Provider under IFSCA rules. 
 Niyo CFO Gourav Kumar said the company is now focused on getting more value from its existing customers, improving efficiency and moving towards profitability. Niyo expects to maintain over 50% annual growth in the coming years and become EBITDA positive. 
 Niyo has raised over $160 million in funding to date, including a $100 million Series C round in 2022. Its investors include Accel, Lightrock, Prime Ventures, Horizon, Tencent, and others.
