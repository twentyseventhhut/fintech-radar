Scapia and Tourism Malaysia join hands to promote new destinations for Indian travellers
Mumbai, Oct 9 (UNI) Indian travel fintech company Scapia has partnered with Tourism Malaysia to launch Malaysia Calling, a four-month promotional campaign aimed at encouraging Indian tourists to explore destinations beyond the traditionally popular Kuala Lumpur and Genting Highlands. The initiative will focus on Malaysia's cultural attractions, natural landscapes and adventure tourism while offering travellers additional rewards and discounts. The campaign comes amid growing interest in Malaysia among Indian tourists. According to Scapia, flight bookings to Malaysia through its platform have increased nearly fourfold, while hotel bookings have doubled.
