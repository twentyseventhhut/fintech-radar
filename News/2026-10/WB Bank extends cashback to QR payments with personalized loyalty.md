## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** From 26 October 2026 WB Банк (the bank of Russian marketplace Wildberries, part of the merged RVB/RWB group) overhauled its loyalty program: it extended cashback from card payments to QR-code payments (specifically СБП/SBP QR, including online), and moved from a flat rate to personalized, user-selectable categories. Clients can now pick up to four cashback categories from a list that is generated individually per client, earning up to 10% reward. The maximum monthly reward stays capped at 3,000 points. Cashback is still paid in "ВБ"/"Ягодки" (berry) points — redeemable only on Wildberries at 1 point = ₽1 discount, i.e. not cash. Categories are chosen in the WB Банк mobile app or in the "Кошелёк" (Wallet) section inside the Wildberries app. Sources: [Frank Media](https://frankmedia.ru/311986), [RB.ru](https://rb.ru/news/wb-bank-obnovil-programmu-loyalnosti-teper-polzovatelyam-dostupno-do-chetyreh-kategorij-keshbeka-na-vybor/), [Клерк.ру](https://www.klerk.ru/buh/news/715532/), [bankinform.ru](https://bankinform.ru/news/143516).

**[1] Why it matters.** The move pushes WB's loyalty flywheel beyond the marketplace card into everyday payments (QR/СБП is the fast-growing Russian payment rail). By rewarding external spend with points that can only be spent on Wildberries, WB converts off-platform payment activity into future marketplace GMV — the digest's core insight: "loyalty of the marketplace becomes a competitive tool in everyday payments." Personalized categories also let the bank price-discriminate — offer different rates to different clients to steer behavior. This is a deliberate step up from a plain card-cashback scheme and tightens the WB Банк ↔ Wildberries ecosystem loop.

**[2] WB Банк trajectory (internal).** This is the latest in a steady 2026 build-out of WB's financial ecosystem. Priors: [[WB Bank launches savings accounts and consumer loans]] (Jun 2026 — accounts + consumer loans up to ₽500k); [[WB Bank names Svyatoslav Emelyanov acting board chairman]] (Jul 2026 — mass-retail risk operator inside RWB, with VTB's Georgy Gorshkov as partnership architect); [[Buyer to close 5% stake deal in Wildberries bank by October]] (Oct 2026 — VTB/RWB closing a 5% stake deal in the bank by end-October); [[WB Bank extends credit holidays for Wildberries sellers]] (Oct 2026 — seller-side lending relief). Together these show WB turning marketplace data, wallets, seller settlements and client traffic into credit/deposit/payment products — consistent with Expert RA's note that asset quality is a future risk zone as unsecured retail scales.

**[3] Competitive / marketplace-bank loyalty war (external).** Direct rival is Ozon Bank, which runs the same playbook (cashback, deposits, installments tied to Ozon). See [[Финтехно Ozon Bank tops banks on customer experience]] and [[Финтехно marketplace banks reshape small-business lending]]. 2026 landscape: Ozon promises discounts up to ~30%, WB offers ~5% base cashback plus this new up-to-10% category layer, Yandex Pay up to 50% in Plus points. Marketplace banks (WB Bank, Ozon Bank, Yandex) grant privileges — extra cashback, cheaper acquiring, installments — unavailable to traditional-bank cards, which is the heart of the regulatory fight below. Comparisons: [bankiros.ru](https://bankiros.ru/news/ozon-wb-ili-yandeks-pehj-gde-bolshe-keshbehka-i-menshe-ogranichenij-v-2026-godu-22717), [finuslugi.ru](https://finuslugi.ru/navigator/kak-ehto-rabotaet/stat_ozon-karta-ili-karta-wb-banka).

**[4] Regulatory overhang.** The biggest external context is the "banks vs marketplaces" conflict: major banks led by Sberbank demand a legal ban on marketplaces offering discounts for paying with their captive banks' cards (WB Pay/Ozon Bank), calling it unfair competition built on preferential acquiring rates. CBR Governor Elvira Nabiullina has backed restricting marketplace-bank discounts. Extending cashback to СБП QR (a rail banks and the CBR actively promote) is a notable move precisely while this discount model is under regulatory scrutiny — the reward mechanics may be a hedge against a crackdown on price discounts. Sources: [Kommersant 8214206](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8214206), [Kommersant 8196021](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8196021), [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2025/11/24/ozon-denies-central-banks-claim-that-marketplace-discounts-distort-competition-a91222).

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team questions**

1. Is "up to 10%" the headline cashback real, or is it gated behind narrow categories + a hard ₽3,000/3,000-points monthly cap that makes the effective rate far lower? The cap strongly limits real value.
2. The digest implies QR broadly, but sources specify СБП/SBP QR only. Does the reward apply to all QR payments or only SBP QR? (External sources say SBP QR — the note's "QR payments" is slightly broad.)
3. Points are "ВБ"/"Ягодки", redeemable ONLY on Wildberries at 1:1 — this is store credit, not cashback. Does the note risk overstating it as cash-equivalent reward?
4. How many of the four categories are actually user-chosen vs algorithmically constrained? Sources say the list is "formed individually" — the bank controls the menu, so "personalized" may mean bank-steered, not client-free.
5. Is this genuinely new, or a repackaging/extension of the March 2026 "first loyalty program" and the recurring 5%-on-everything promo that WB keeps extending month to month?
6. Does rewarding external SBP/QR spend actually cost WB Банк (it pays points) more than the incremental Wildberries GMV it captures? Unit economics unverified.
7. Regulatory risk: if the CBR/Nabiullina push to ban marketplace-bank discounts succeeds, does this points model survive — or is it the same mechanism under a different label and equally exposed?
8. Acquiring economics: marketplace banks are accused of preferential/cheap acquiring. Does the QR-cashback rely on that advantage, making it hard for rival banks to match?
9. Is there a hidden cost (annual fee, minimum spend, premium tariff) required to unlock the up-to-four categories or the 10% tiers?
10. Source quality: the note's only cited source is the max.ru/fintexno channel link (no full text). Primary confirmation comes from secondary Russian media (Frank Media, RB.ru, Klerk). Is any official WB Банк press release cited?
11. Does "activity on Wildberries unlocks extra categories" create a lock-in loop that regulators could view as the same anticompetitive steering they object to?
12. Scale/impact: WB Банк client base and QR transaction volume are not disclosed here — is this material to WB's financials or a marketing-led retention tactic?
13. Timing vs the 5% stake deal (VTB/RWB closing by end-Oct) and leadership changes — is the loyalty revamp a standalone product move or part of a broader governance/ownership repositioning?
14. Competitive response: does Ozon Bank (30% discounts) or Yandex Pay (up to 50% Plus) already out-reward this, making WB's move defensive rather than leading?

**Importance: 2/5 — rationale.** A concrete, well-sourced product/loyalty extension by a notable Russian marketplace bank, and a clean illustration of the marketplace-bank loyalty war and the live CBR regulatory fight. But it is incremental (cashback extended to SBP QR + category personalization, same berry-points store-credit mechanic, hard ₽3k cap), store-credit not cash, with no disclosed financials or scale and no official press release in the note. Meaningful as ecosystem signal, low as a standalone market-moving event.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector:** Russian marketplace-bank loyalty / everyday-payments land-grab. WB Банк (the banking arm of Wildberries, Russia's largest e-commerce platform) extends its loyalty cashback — up to 10%, up to four self-selected personalized categories — to QR-code payments from 26 Oct 2026. The strategic point is not the headline rate but the mechanic: rewards keep accruing as "Ягодки"/ВБ points redeemable only against Wildberries orders (1 point = 1 ruble of discount), with a 3,000 ₽/month cap. This pushes WB's payment rails *outside* the marketplace (QR/SBP at third-party merchants) while funneling the resulting loyalty back into marketplace GMV — a closed-loop that converts external spend into future WB orders.

**Where this sits in the Russian marketplace-bank race.** The direct comp is Ozon Bank, whose Ozon Card offers up to 25% cashback across (up to five, for Premium) categories on off-Ozon spend — a higher nominal rate and, critically, rewards that can be taken as rubles or "stars," i.e. far more liquid than WB's marketplace-locked points. Both carry the same 3,000 ₽/month reward cap, so WB is matching the structure while deliberately keeping its reward currency captive. Ozon has simultaneously been building banking "stickiness" infrastructure — doubling its ATM fleet to 2,000 by end-2026 ([[News/2026-07/Ozon Bank to double ATM network to 2,000 by end-2026]]) and registering a CBR digital-financial-assets platform ([[News/2026-06/Ozon Bank registers digital financial assets platform with CBR]]) — signaling both marketplace banks are trying to become primary, not promotional, banks. T-Bank's parallel experiment ([[News/2026-07/T-Investments launches loyalty platform paying shares for purchases]]) pays loyalty in *equity* rather than captive points, illustrating the industry's search for stickier reward currencies. WB Банк itself only launched savings accounts and consumer loans in June 2026 ([[News/2026-06/WB Bank launches savings accounts and consumer loans]]), so this is a very young balance sheet leaning on loyalty to acquire transactional relationships.

**The core risk — the discount trap (in-base thesis).** Axenix's July 2026 study of Russian marketplace banks ([[News/2026-07/Axenix study marketplace banks hit trust ceiling despite traffic]]) is the single most relevant frame: 85% of users see marketplace banks primarily as an embedded platform service, and 60% never use them outside the marketplace. Axenix's key finding is a trust ceiling — the stronger the discounts/cashback, the *weaker* the standalone banking identity, because users learn "this is a way to get a better price on the marketplace," not "this is my bank." WB's decision to keep rewards redeemable only on Wildberries deepens exactly this trap: QR cashback drives external payment volume, but by denominating the reward in marketplace-locked points it reinforces the embedded-service perception rather than converting users into primary-bank clients where salary/savings/daily finances live.

**Risk flags:**
1. **Captive-reward liquidity gap.** Points redeemable only on Wildberries are strictly less valuable to users than Ozon's ruble/star cashback or cash equivalents; at equal nominal rates WB's effective reward is lower, limiting the QR offer's power to move off-platform payment share for non-marketplace-heavy users.
2. **Discount-trap / trust ceiling (Axenix).** The mechanic risks entrenching marketplace-service perception, undermining the stated goal of growing payment-product usage "beyond Wildberries" and primary-bank conversion.
3. **Unit-economics / subsidy burn.** Up to 10% cashback (capped 3,000 ₽/mo) funded in points still represents a real marketing cost and GMV dilution on redemption; on a bank that only launched savings/loans in mid-2026, sustained cashback subsidies pressure a thin, unproven P&L — and the rate is already below Ozon's headline 25%, so matching competitively could raise the cost further.
4. **Regulatory / SBP-economics exposure.** Routing cashback through QR (SBP) payments ties the program to CBR-governed SBP tariffs and to the broader regulatory scrutiny of marketplace/ecosystem banking in Russia; changes to interchange/SBP fees or consumer-protection rules on loyalty could compress the model.
5. **Single-source, promotional framing.** The claim rests on one channel digest (Финтехно) plus vendor/press coverage; prior WB cashback moves have been time-boxed promotions (the 5% "on everything" offer was repeatedly *extended* month-to-month through Oct 2026), so "personalized categories" may be another promo rather than a durable structural change — watch for an expiry date.

**Not verifiable from available sources:** WB Банк does not disclose active-client counts, payment-volume or QR-transaction share, or the loyalty program's cost as a % of banking revenue; marketplace-bank is not in IR coverage, so no earnings grounding is possible. Reward economics (break-even on 10% point-funded cashback) cannot be independently confirmed.
