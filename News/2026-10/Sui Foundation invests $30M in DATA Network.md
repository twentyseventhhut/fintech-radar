# Sui Foundation invests $30M in DATA Network

Sui Foundation has announced a $30 million investment in DATA Network on October 12, becoming its largest institutional holder as the AI training data protocol prepares to move to Sui and shut down its independent blockchain.
Sui Foundation announced a $30 million investment in DATA Network, becoming its largest institutional holder.
DATA Network plans to retire its standalone blockchain and migrate existing tokens to Sui one-to-one.
Trace has registered more than 250 million contributions and processes over three million daily receipts.
Walrus will become the default storage layer for applications operated by DATA Foundation after migration.
Existing DATA token vesting schedules and governance rights will carry over under the announced plan.
Sui Foundation confirmed the agreement in an official announcement with DATA Foundation, explaining that the partnership will bring DATA Network and its Trace registry onto Sui’s blockchain infrastructure. The existing DATA token will migrate at a 1:1 ratio, while Walrus will become the default storage platform for applications operated by DATA Foundation.
The two organizations said the existing network will remain operational until the transition is completed. They have not announced a migration date, but plan to publish a detailed schedule and instructions for token holders before the upgrade.
 Sui Foundation’s $30M investment brings DATA Network to Sui 
Under the agreement, Sui Foundation will become DATA Network’s largest institutional investor, joining existing holders PIP Labs and DATA Foundation. The investment forms part of a strategic partnership designed to bring AI training data infrastructure onto Sui.
DATA Network currently operates its own blockchain, which supports applications involved in sourcing, tracking and licensing training information for artificial intelligence systems. Once the planned migration is completed, DATA Network will operate as a protocol native to Sui.
Its standalone blockchain will be retired, removing the need for DATA Foundation to maintain a separate network for the applications it supports.
According to the joint announcement, DATA tokens will transfer to Sui on a one-for-one basis. For every existing DATA token, the holder will receive one DATA token on the new network.
The foundations said token holders would retain economic benefits and governance rights at least equivalent to their current arrangements. Locked tokens and existing vesting schedules will transfer without changes under the announced plan.
However, the organizations have not disclosed the investment’s valuation, the exact number of tokens involved in Sui Foundation’s stake or the financial structure of the transaction. The announcement did not establish whether the full $30 million had already been transferred. Sui Foundation and DATA Foundation said they would publish further information about the holder transition before the migration begins.
Meanwhile, SY Lee, chairman of Poseidon and CEO of PIP Labs, will join Sui Foundation as a strategic adviser. Lee previously played a central role in the development of Story Protocol, which later became DATA Network. His advisory appointment was announced alongside the investment and technical partnership.
 DATA Network plans to shut down its independent blockchain 
DATA Network’s planned migration follows a major change in the project’s business strategy earlier this year. In June 2026, Story Protocol rebranded as DATA Foundation and changed its focus from general intellectual property licensing to AI training data.
The transition included renaming the original IP token to DATA and introducing a dedicated network for managing data ownership, licensing and payments.
As previously reported , the project had struggled to attract some large intellectual property owners because they wanted tighter control over their content and licensing arrangements. DATA Foundation subsequently concentrated its resources on infrastructure for sourcing and verifying information used to train AI models.
In its June 25 announcement , the organization explained that the new direction would focus on giving AI developers access to licensed information while tracking how contributors were compensated.
The October agreement takes the restructuring further by moving the protocol away from an independent blockchain. DATA Foundation said its existing network would continue processing transactions until the Sui migration is complete. The organizations have not published a shutdown date for the original blockchain.
They have not specified whether exchanges, custodians or other service providers will handle the token transition automatically. No mandatory action for token holders has been announced at this stage. The migration will require further technical and operational updates, with the foundations committing to release instructions ahead of implementation.
According to the official statement, the move will preserve DATA’s existing governance arrangements and token holder economics while transferring the protocol’s operations to Sui.
 Trace records 250 million AI data contributions 
The partnership brings DATA Foundation’s Trace registry to Sui. Trace launched in June 2026 as a blockchain-based system for recording the origins of information used in artificial intelligence training. The registry tracks the source of contributed data, the rights attached to it and the payments owed to the people who provide it.
According to DATA Foundation, Trace has registered more than 250 million contributions since launch. The organization reports that the system currently processes more than 3 million receipts per day on average. The figures were supplied by the foundation and have not been independently audited in the materials reviewed.
Applications using the registry include Kled, Numo and Oto. Kled operates a marketplace for people who choose to contribute information for AI development. The Sui announcement said Kled was onboarding more than 1.5 billion records, while Numo and Oto were supplying licensed information to AI laboratories and robotics companies. Trace creates records showing where information came from and the permissions attached to its use.
The foundation said contributors can receive payment when their data is licensed through participating applications. Through the planned upgrade, the registry will run on Sui while using Walrus for the storage of original files. Adeniyi Abiodun, co-founder and chief product officer at Mysten Labs, described the growing demand for properly licensed training information. “The AI industry has reached the point where the open internet has been read, and the next frontier is defined by access to datasets with the consent and provenance that regulators require.”
Abiodun said the partnership would bring existing data contributions and licensing activity to the Sui technology stack. His comments describe the companies’ intended application of the infrastructure, not an independently established guarantee about the quality or legal status of every dataset.
 Walrus becomes the default storage platform for AI data 
Under the agreement, Walrus will become the default storage layer for applications operated by DATA Foundation. Walrus is a decentralized data storage protocol developed within the Sui ecosystem.
While Sui records blockchain transactions and ownership information, Walrus is designed to store large files and make them available under specified access conditions. The companies plan to connect these capabilities so that AI training datasets can be registered, stored and licensed through the same technology stack.
According to the announcement, Trace currently records information about contributed files and their associated rights. After the integration, the system is expected to support encrypted copies of original datasets stored on Walrus. DATA Foundation said the arrangement would allow authorized users to verify records against the original information while maintaining restrictions on access.
The organizations described the planned storage system as a way to improve checks on whether a dataset has been altered after registration. Andrea Muttoni, CEO of DATA Foundation, said the project was moving beyond maintaining a transaction record.
He identified encrypted file storage and potential future verification tools for AI evaluations and model training as areas the organization intends to develop. Those additional functions remain part of the foundation’s development plans. Walrus has previously attracted investments and partnerships involving AI infrastructure.
In March 2025, Walrus Foundation secured $140 million in funding led by Standard Crypto, with participation from other digital asset investors. The financing supported the development of decentralized storage infrastructure linked to Sui, as covered in the Walrus Foundation’s $140 million funding announcement .
In June 2025, decentralized cloud provider io.net integrated Walrus for encrypted AI model storage , connecting the storage network with infrastructure used for AI training and deployment.
Sui has continued developing technology intended to handle high volumes of automated activity. On October 7, the foundation reported that programmable offchain tunnels reached 40,614,180 transactions per second during a demonstration at Sui Basecamp in Singapore.
The test involved offchain channels that settle to Sui mainnet, not ordinary mainnet transactions. CertiK independently observed the demonstration, while detailed results of the test remained subject to further review.
As reported in coverage of Sui’s offchain transaction test , the demonstration involved more than 10,000 tunnels supporting payments, games and messaging activity. For DATA Network, the next operational step is the publication of its migration schedule and token holder instructions. The foundations have committed to keeping the existing blockchain operational until the upgrade is completed, with locked and vesting DATA token positions transferring under their current schedules.

### [0] What happened (one-liner)
On 2026-10-11/12, the **Sui Foundation announced a $30M investment in DATA Network**, becoming its largest institutional holder, as the AI-training-data protocol prepares to **retire its own standalone blockchain** and migrate onto Sui (DATA token swaps 1:1; Walrus becomes default storage).

### [1] What is DATA Network (precise)
- **DATA Network is an AI-training-data infrastructure protocol — not a classic DePIN hardware network.** It sources, tracks, licenses and verifies the provenance/consent/payment history of datasets used to train AI models.
- It is the **rebranded Story Protocol**: in **June 2026** the a16z-backed Story Protocol rebranded to **DATA Foundation**, pivoting from broad intellectual-property licensing to AI training data, and renamed its **IP token → DATA** via a 1:1 migration ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/406175/story-becomes-data-foundation-shifts-focus-to-ai-training-data); [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/06/25/a16z-backed-crypto-firm-rebrands-shifts-focus-to-solving-ai-s-global-copyright-headache)).
- Core product is **Trace**, an on-chain registry (launched June 2026) that records dataset origin, attached rights and payments owed to contributors. Per the foundation: **>250M contributions registered, ~3M receipts/day** (self-reported, not independently audited). Apps on it: **Kled** (contributor marketplace, onboarding ~1.5B records), **Numo**, **Oto** (supplying licensed data to AI labs/robotics firms).

### [2] Deal structure (what's known / unknown)
- **Known:** Sui Foundation becomes largest institutional holder, joining existing holders **PIP Labs** and **DATA Foundation**. DATA chain to be retired; DATA token migrates **1:1** to Sui; locked tokens/vesting schedules and governance rights carry over; **Walrus** (Sui-ecosystem decentralized storage) becomes default storage layer. **SY Lee** (chairman of Poseidon, CEO of PIP Labs, original Story Protocol founder) joins Sui Foundation as **strategic adviser**.
- **Unknown / undisclosed:** valuation, exact token count in Sui's stake, cash-vs-token financial structure, whether the full $30M has been transferred, migration/shutdown date. Token/grant/equity split is **not disclosed** — framed as a token-holding + strategic partnership, not an equity round.

### [3] Why it matters (ecosystem-fund angle)
- This is **Sui ecosystem capture**: pulling a dedicated AI-data vertical and its on-chain activity onto Sui, reinforcing the **Sui + Walrus** storage/compute stack ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-10-11-sui-foundation-invests-30-million-data-network-418243)). Walrus itself raised **$140M (Mar 2025, led by Standard Crypto)** and has AI-infra partners (io.net integrated Walrus for encrypted AI-model storage, Jun 2025).
- Thesis (per Mysten Labs CPO Adeniyi Abiodun): the open internet has been "read"; the next frontier is **consented, provenance-verified datasets** that regulators require. DATA/Trace is positioned as that rights-and-provenance layer.
- Fits Sui's broader fintech/payments push — see [[Paga expands into tokenised assets via Sui integration]] (tokenized assets, May 2026) and [[Sui unveils RedotPay-powered Slush stablecoin credit card]] (stablecoin card, May 2026). Adjacent AI-infra demand signal: [[Prime Intellect raises $130M Series A for enterprise AI agents]].

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
### Red-team questions
1. **Is "$30M investment" even cash?** Valuation, token count and cash-vs-token structure are all undisclosed, and the source explicitly notes it's unclear whether the full $30M has been transferred. The headline number may be a token-denominated commitment, not deployed cash.
2. **"Largest institutional holder" of what — at what price?** Without a valuation or token count, "largest holder" is unfalsifiable; it could reflect a thin float rather than a large dollar stake.
3. **Is DATA Network a DePIN project?** No — it's an AI-training-data/provenance protocol (ex-Story Protocol), not a hardware/physical-infra DePIN. Calling it DePIN would be imprecise.
4. **Are the 250M contributions / 3M daily receipts real demand or vanity metrics?** Figures are self-reported by DATA Foundation and explicitly **not independently audited**. "Receipts" and "contributions" are not fees or revenue.
5. **Does any of this translate to recurring SUI demand?** Analysts flag the open question: a registry recording receipts ≠ sustained on-chain transactions or fee generation for Sui. Ecosystem "capture" can be activity that never monetizes.
6. **Why did SUI drop ~5% on supposedly bullish news?** Price fell toward $1 — market treated it as neutral/negative or priced it as dilutive/speculative, undercutting the "institutional confidence" framing.
7. **Is this a rescue of a struggling project rather than a growth bet?** Story Protocol pivoted twice (IP → AI data) after failing to attract large IP owners; retiring its own chain after ~1 year suggests the standalone model wasn't viable. Sui may be absorbing a distressed asset.
8. **Conflict / relationship risk:** SY Lee (PIP Labs CEO, Story founder) joining Sui Foundation as adviser alongside the investment — are PIP Labs' and Sui's interests being swapped rather than independently valued?
9. **Chain-retirement execution risk:** no migration date, no shutdown date, no confirmation that exchanges/custodians auto-handle the token swap — holder-transition risk is real and undated.
10. **Is "a16z-backed" doing rhetorical work?** The pedigree (a16z, Standard Crypto) signals quality but doesn't validate the AI-data demand thesis or the deal's economics.
11. **Walrus-as-default-storage:** is this genuine adoption or intra-ecosystem circular endorsement (Sui funds a project that then adopts Sui's own Walrus storage)?
12. **Regulatory claim ("consent and provenance regulators require"):** which regulators, which jurisdiction, which rule? This is a marketing thesis, not a cited legal mandate.
13. **Fintech relevance check:** this is crypto/AI-infra, not a core payments/banking story — importance to a fintech audience is indirect (AI-data + blockchain-infra adjacency).
14. **Is $30M large?** Against Walrus's own $140M raise and typical AI-data rounds, $30M is modest — more a strategic anchor than a market-moving capital event.
15. **Durability:** will DATA still be a native Sui protocol in 12 months, or pivot/retire a third time?

**Importance: 2/5** — Precise, dated, non-duplicate crypto/AI-infra story with a clear ecosystem-fund narrative (Sui + Walrus + AI training data) and a useful skeptical frame (undisclosed structure, self-reported metrics, SUI price drop). But it is a modest-sized, token-structured commitment in an adjacent crypto/AI vertical with limited direct relevance to a core fintech/payments audience, heavy on undisclosed terms and unaudited vanity metrics. Fresh, not a strategy-defining event.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** The deal sits at the intersection of two markets: (a) licensed AI-training-data/data-provenance, and (b) DePIN-style decentralized data infrastructure on L1s. The dataset-licensing-for-AI-training market was ~$4.8bn in 2025, projected to ~$22.6bn by 2034 at ~18.8% CAGR, with proprietary/custom-negotiated licenses expected to be >55% of revenue by 2026 (per dataintelo, via web, as of 2026 — secondary market-research source, treat as directional, not audited). Broader "AI training dataset" TAM estimates diverge wildly across firms ($3.9bn–$11.9bn for 2026, CAGR 16–33%) — no single reliable figure, so size = directional only. The DePIN vertical that DATA Network notionally joins was ~$18–20bn aggregate token market cap in H1 2026 (per Messari/CoinMarketCap, via web) — a crypto-native, token-valuation metric, NOT real revenue; sector on-chain revenue from paying customers was only ~$150m in Jan 2026, i.e. market cap runs ~100x+ annualized revenue, a structurally speculative sector. **Structure:** fragmented and early; value in AI data is being captured upstream (labeling/marketplace players like Mercor, Scale) while provenance/licensing rails are unproven as a paid layer. **Why now:** the stated driver — "the open internet has been read; the next frontier is licensed data with consent and provenance regulators require" (Abiodun, Mysten Labs, in the note) — is a real secular shift (copyright litigation + EU AI Act provenance expectations), but it is a seller's narrative and DATA is marketing to it, not proven to monetize it.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs for an L1 ecosystem bet: incremental on-chain transactions/fees generated, TVL, paying-buyer count, licensed-records volume, repeat contracts. For the data protocol: contributions registered, daily receipts, independent paying buyers. **Two competitive frames:**
- *L1 foundations competing for apps:* Sui is a small L1 — ~$4.8bn mkt cap (#36), ~$0.55bn DeFi TVL, ~$66m/24h DEX volume, ~$0.48bn stablecoins (Oct 2026, per CoinMarketCap/DeFiLlama via web). Solana dwarfs it: ~$16.2bn stablecoins, ~$2.47bn/24h DEX volume — ~37x the DEX liquidity. Aptos (the other Move-based L1) competes directly for the same Move-native apps. Foundation ecosystem-capital tactics differ: Aptos runs a $1m Thala Foundry DeFi fund and small per-protocol grants; Solana funds via Colosseum ($250k/company accelerator) + heavy institutional-fund flows (~$1.12bn cumulative by Jul 2026). Sui's $30m single-protocol token purchase is an unusually concentrated, large ecosystem bet by comparison — buying a flagship app rather than seeding many. `(analysis)`
- *AI-data provenance players:* the basis of competition is trust/adoption by AI labs, not price. DATA Foundation's protagonist position = niche, pre-revenue-visibility: Trace reports 250m+ contributions and 3m+ daily receipts (foundation-supplied, un-audited per the note). Moat claim = provenance network effects + Walrus storage integration `(analysis)` — but switching costs for AI labs are low and no exclusive lab commitments are disclosed.

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation disclosed for the $30m Sui stake (token count, implied protocol valuation, and whether $30m has actually transferred are all undisclosed per the note) → **implied multiple = no data.** Anchor: parent PIP Labs/Story Protocol raised $80m Series B led by a16z at a **$2.25bn valuation** (~$140m total raised; per Cointelegraph/CoinDesk via web) — so $30m ≈ ~1.3% of that last private mark, consistent with a strategic minority stake, but the DATA protocol is a token network, not PIP equity, so the comp is only a rough ceiling reference, not a like-for-like. **Risk flags.**
1. **Undisclosed economics / un-audited traction.** No valuation, token count, or financial structure disclosed, and the headline 250m contributions / 3m daily receipts are foundation-supplied and un-audited (per the note) — the stake could be priced off unverifiable vanity metrics; second-order: hard to judge whether $30m is cheap or a markdown-in-waiting.
2. **Activity ≠ SUI demand.** A registry's receipt counts need not convert to recurring SUI token demand — sponsored transactions + near-zero per-tx fees mean millions of receipts may generate negligible fees/value; the deal disclosed no incremental volume/revenue forecast (per crypto.news). The "activity coming to Sui" narrative may not re-rate the token.
3. **Pivot/execution risk.** DATA is a twice-pivoted project (Story Protocol → DATA Foundation, Jun 2026; now retiring its own chain, Oct 2026) that previously "struggled to attract large IP owners" (per the note). Migrating a live chain's tokens 1:1 with no published date, plus retiring its own L1, concentrates execution + migration risk; no exclusive AI-lab commitments disclosed.
4. **Concentration for Sui.** $30m into a single unproven protocol is a concentrated bet for a ~$4.8bn-cap foundation vs peers' diversified grant/accelerator models — flagship-app risk if DATA underdelivers. `(analysis)`

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` This is Sui buying a narrative — a flagship AI-data use-case to differentiate from Solana/Aptos on "licensed-data provenance" rather than out-liquidity Solana (which it can't). Falsifiable thesis: if the bet works, within ~2-3 quarters post-migration we should see independent paying-buyer counts, licensed-records volume and repeat lab contracts rise on Sui — NOT just contribution totals. Trigger/what breaks it: watch for the migration schedule publication, any named AI-lab offtake, and real Sui fee/revenue uplift; absence of disclosed paying buyers or a stalled migration date would mark it as a marketing re-rate, not a durable demand driver.
