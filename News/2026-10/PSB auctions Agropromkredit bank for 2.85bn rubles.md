 ПСБ выставил на аукцион банк «Агропромкредит» за 2,85 млрд руб. 
ПАО «Банк ПСБ» выставило на аукцион 50% акций банка «Агропромкредит» и 100% долей в двух компаниях — «Дэнсиборг» и «Блаусент», которым принадлежат по 25% акций кредитной организации. Начальная цена единого лота составляет 2,85 млрд руб. Торги назначены на 15 октября. Заявки на участие в аукционе будут принимать до 13 октября. Ранее совладельцами банка были уральские бизнесмены Артем Биков и Алексей Бобров (находится под следствием), но, как и ряд других компаний, банк был изъят судом в доход РФ по иску Генпрокуратуры, напоминает " Коммерсант ".
Согласно документам, начальная цена 50% акций «Агропромкредита» составляет 1,119 млрд руб., стопроцентная доля в ООО «Дэнсиборг» оценена в 843,4 млн руб., в ООО «Блаусент» — в 891,8 млн руб. Шаг аукциона составит 28,5 млн руб., задаток — 570,8 млн руб. Торги пройдут в электронной форме на площадке Российского аукционного дома.
На продажу выставлены 592,6 млн обыкновенных именных акций «Агропромкредита», что составляет 50% его уставного капитала. Согласно отчету об оценке, еще по 25% акций (296,3 млн шт.) принадлежало «Дэнсиборг» и «Блаусент». Таким образом, покупатель получит государственный пакет банка и две компании, владеющие оставшейся половиной его акций.
Банк «Агропромкредит», согласно документам, ведет свою историю с 1994 года и является на российском финансовом рынке универсальным банком федерального уровня. В августе 2009 года было создано АО КБ «Агропромкредит» в результате преобразования Коммерческого банка «Кредитный агропромбанк». В 2015 году банк изменил фирменное наименование. По данным документации к торгам, на 31 марта 2026 года в кредитной организации работали 328 человек. По состоянию на конец I квартала 2026 года региональная сеть банка состояла из 44 дополнительных офисов, расположенных на территории 28 городов.
Аукцион проводится в рамках реализации имущества, находящегося в федеральной собственности, на основании указа президента РФ. Собственником выставленных активов в документации указана Российская Федерация, продавцом выступает ПАО «Банк ПСБ».

# Context-enrichment: PSB auctions Agropromkredit bank for 2.85bn rubles
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
PAO Bank PSB (Promsvyazbank), acting as the state's agent, put up for a single-lot electronic auction a 100% economic interest in AO KB "Agropromkredit": 50% of shares held directly (592.6m ordinary shares) plus 100% of two holding LLCs ("Densiborg" and "Blausent") that each own 25% of the bank. Start price of the combined lot = RUB 2.85bn (50% shares priced at RUB 1.119bn; Densiborg LLC RUB 843.4m; Blausent LLC RUB 891.8m). Auction step RUB 28.5m, deposit RUB 570.8m. Bids accepted until 13 Oct, auction set for 15 Oct 2026 on the Russian Auction House (RAD) platform. Owner of the assets is listed as the Russian Federation; PSB is the seller, under a presidential decree.
- This is NOT a market M&A — it is a state fire-sale of a nationalized asset. The bank was seized "in favour of the RF" via a Prosecutor-General's (Genprokuratura) anti-corruption claim; prior co-owners were Urals energy businessmen Artem Bikov and Alexei Bobrov (Bobrov under investigation). Agropromkredit was one of ~38 Bikov/Bobrov entities (combined book value >RUB 134bn) nationalized by a Leninsky district court (Yekaterinburg) ruling on 10 Oct 2025 [theins.ru/news/297566, kommersant.ru/doc/8142465].
- **Why structured as "shares + two holding LLCs" in one lot**: the state did not hold 100% of the bank directly — only 50% directly, with the other 50% parked in two LLCs (Densiborg/Blausent). Bundling all three into one lot delivers full control to the buyer in a single transaction and avoids a residual minority overhang. This is the standard "deliver 100%, no stubs" packaging for these forced sales.
- **Why so fast / why PSB**: under presidential decree (the June 2026 Decree No. 389 regime on an "special order" for realizing federal property, which also *removed minority pre-emption/offer obligations* and compressed timelines), seized companies are pushed to auction months after seizure via PSB or Rosimushchestvo. The Insider explicitly calls it a "conveyor": a special list of ~27 assets for accelerated sale through PSB [theins.ru/news/297566, pravo.ru/news/264026, kremlin.ru/acts/bank/53296].
- Bank scale (de-PR'd vs the "federal-level universal bank" framing): small. ~328 staff (31 Mar 2026), 44 additional offices across 28 cities; assets ~RUB 10.9–22.9bn range, capital ~RUB 3.57bn, NKR rating BB.ru (positive outlook, May 2025) [banki.ru ratings, ratings.ru APKbank-RA-200525]. The RUB 2.85bn start price for 100% is modest — a sub-top-150 bank by assets.

## [1] Competitors / peers (comparable transactions, not product rivals)
This is a forced-sale-of-nationalized-asset event; the relevant peer set is *other PSB/state disposals of Bikov-Bobrov and similar seized assets*, not banking competitors.
- [[PSB fails third auction for nationalized Vektor Rail]] (2026-10-05) — closest sister: PSB as seller of a nationalized asset (Taicher group, seized by Genprokuratura), auction failed 3x this year for lack of bidders amid "not fully transparent" terms; start price RUB 20.3bn vs rumored RUB 6–10bn interest from Sber. Shows these state auctions frequently fail / clear far below start.
- Bikov/Bobrov agro-assets: sold for RUB 285m (≈half of start) to a loss-making micro-enterprise in Verkhnyaya Pyshma [66.ru/6517297, smi.today/3780502] — the bank was "valued ~10x higher" than those agro-assets. Direct precedent for how these lots actually clear: cheap, to opaque/small buyers.
- [[Belaya Skala buys 47.2bn ruble asset portfolio]] (2026-07-01) — RAD auction of a Raven Russia warehouse portfolio: cleared at RUB 47.2bn vs RUB 92.5bn start (near the RUB 46.3bn floor), single serious bidder. Same pattern: large haircut to reserve, thin competition.
- [[VTB to sell former Otkritie office to Wildberries-Russ]] (2026-06-30) — adjacent state-bank disposal of distressed/legacy assets to a politically-connected buyer.
- **Why the landscape looks this way (2nd order)**: a wave of 2024–2026 Genprokuratura nationalizations created a large inventory of assets the state does not want to operate; the June 2026 decree engineered a fast, low-friction disposal rail (PSB/Rosimushchestvo, no minority offer, 5-day windows). Thin bidder pools + compressed timelines + sanctioned-buyer universe → consistent clearing well below start price, often to small/opaque domestic buyers. The "competition" is effectively absent.

## [2] Company / fit (PSB's role)
PSB is Russia's designated defence-and-special-mandate state bank. Operating the disposal of federally-seized property is a mandate function, not commercial M&A advisory — it fits PSB's post-2022 role as the state's balance-sheet-and-agency tool. PSB here is agent/seller, not acquirer; it bears process risk (failed auctions, as with Vektor Rail) but not asset risk. Note the ownership tangle resolved via Densiborg/Blausent LLCs mirrors how the original owners structured control through nested holdcos — the state simply inherited and is now unwinding that structure.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
Low novelty. This is one more line item on an already-running conveyor of Bikov/Bobrov disposals (the bank is explicitly described as the second/later batch of their assets). The only genuinely new, citable facts vs prior corpus: (a) the specific asset = a bank (first *banking* asset in this batch), (b) the exact lot structure and figures (RUB 2.85bn, 50%+2 LLCs, step RUB 28.5m, deposit RUB 570.8m), (c) the 15 Oct auction date. Traction/outcome is NOT yet known — as of the 12 Oct retrieval the auction had not occurred; given the precedent (agro-assets at half-price; Vektor Rail failing 3x), a clean sale at RUB 2.85bn is far from assured. "Announced auction" ≠ "sold". Value-add of the *news* is informational (confirms a bank is now on the block), not a completed deal.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
- 15 Oct 2026 auction result pending (not public at retrieval): watch for (i) sale vs failed auction, (ii) final price vs RUB 2.85bn start, (iii) buyer identity (precedent says small/opaque or a strategic state-adjacent holder).
- Structural driver: the decree regime is built for speed and state convenience, not price maximization — so expect continued low clearing prices and recurring failed auctions for larger/less-liquid lots. For a small BB.ru bank, likely a single domestic buyer seeking a licence + branch network cheaply.
- Risks/silences: the PR frame ("federal-level universal bank", "history since 1994") flatters a sub-top-150 lender; deposit base/loan-book quality and any related-party exposure to the former owners are not disclosed in the auction note. Legal tail: Bikov reportedly challenged the nationalization (Supreme Court declined to hear) — residual litigation risk for any buyer is a plausible (open) deterrent.
- Sentiment (analysis): these auctions are watched as a barometer of how aggressively and cheaply the state is recycling seized assets; each cheap clearing reinforces that nationalization-then-fire-sale is now a routine, low-recovery process — which is itself a chilling signal for private asset holders, not a reassuring one.

## Top challenge / red-team questions (second-order)

1. **Is this actually new vs the corpus?** Partly. The *mechanism* (PSB selling nationalized Bikov/Bobrov assets) is already covered by [[PSB fails third auction for nationalized Vektor Rail]] and the broader conveyor. NEW here: the asset is a *bank*, plus exact figures and the 15 Oct date. Not a reprint of an earlier note → fresh, but low-weight.

2. **Did the auction actually happen / sell?** OPEN. Auction set for 15 Oct 2026; as of the 12 Oct retrieval no result was public. Given precedent (Vektor Rail failed 3x; agro-assets cleared at ~half start price), a clean RUB 2.85bn sale is not assured.

3. **Is RUB 2.85bn a real valuation or a formality?** Likely a floor/formality. Peer forced-sales (agro-assets at ~RUB 285m = half start; Belaya Skala near the reserve, not start) show these clear with large haircuts. Treat 2.85bn as a ceiling, not a clearing price.

4. **Why bundle 50% shares + two LLCs into one lot?** To deliver 100% control in a single transaction with no minority stub — standard packaging for forced disposals; the two LLCs are the nested holdcos through which the original owners parked the other 50%.

5. **Who would buy a sub-top-150 BB.ru bank under sanctions?** OPEN. Realistic universe = a domestic buyer wanting a cheap banking licence + 44-office/28-city network, or a state-adjacent consolidator. Precedent (agro-assets → loss-making micro-enterprise) suggests buyers can be small/opaque.

6. **What's the asset quality behind the "universal federal bank" PR?** Undisclosed. The note omits loan-book quality, deposit base, and any related-party exposure to Bikov/Bobrov. The flattering "since 1994 / federal-level" framing masks a small lender (~328 staff, assets ~RUB 11–23bn).

7. **Legal tail risk for a buyer?** Plausible. Bikov reportedly sought to overturn the nationalization (Supreme Court declined to hear); residual litigation/reputational risk for any acquirer of ex-seized property. OPEN on materiality.

8. **Does the decree regime distort price discovery?** Yes. Decree No. 389 (Jun 2026) removed minority pre-emption and compressed timelines (5-day bid windows) — engineered for speed/state convenience, not price maximization → structurally low recoveries.

9. **What does PSB actually earn/risk here?** PSB is agent/seller, not owner-of-record; it bears process risk (failed auctions) but not asset-value risk. This is a mandate function, not a commercial advisory win.

10. **Why does a bank being sold matter more/less than the other assets?** Marginally more — a banking licence + branch network is a scarcer, regulated prize than agro-farms, so it may attract a more serious bidder. But still a small bank; weight stays low.

11. **Is there a signal in the pace (second batch within a week)?** Yes (analysis): the acceleration shows the state is clearing the Bikov/Bobrov inventory fast and cheaply — a barometer of routinized nationalization-then-fire-sale, chilling for private holders.

12. **Could the state be the de facto end-buyer (circular)?** OPEN. In some of these disposals the acquirer is state-adjacent; cannot confirm for this lot pre-auction.

13. **Does the RUB 570.8m deposit (20% of lot) screen out buyers?** Likely narrows the field to serious/funded bidders — consistent with thin participation and failed-auction risk seen in peers.

14. **Any cross-border / sanctions-relevant angle?** None evident; this is a purely domestic RF forced sale. Not a fintech-innovation or payments story.

15. **Why is this low importance for a fintech radar?** It is a state-process/M&A item about a small nationalized regional bank, no product/tech/payments novelty, outcome unknown, and it extends an already-covered conveyor. Informational, not weighty.

Importance: 2/5 — A genuine, specific new data point (a nationalized bank now on the block at RUB 2.85bn, 15 Oct), but low fintech weight: tiny BB.ru lender, forced state fire-sale with no completed deal, no product/tech novelty, and it extends an already-documented PSB disposal conveyor whose outcomes typically clear below start or fail.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** This is not an ordinary bank-M&A deal but a *forced re-privatization* of a state-confiscated asset. Per The Moscow Times (via Russian press, 10 Sep 2026), the state has nationalized 800+ companies; finance.mail.ru reports ~$10.16bn of assets seized in H1-2026 alone, with ~100bn rubles raised for the budget from re-privatization. PSB (Promsvyazbank) is the designated disposal agent for seized assets alongside Rosimushchestvo; the Finance Minister has publicly pushed both to "improve coordination" because investors lack information — i.e. the state itself acknowledges the sales channel is underperforming (per finance.mail.ru, as of H1-2026). Why now: the confiscation of Bikov/Bobrov assets (Leninsky district court, Yekaterinburg, Nov-2025 per 74.ru/kommersant) has fed a pipeline of urals assets into the PSB auction channel. Structure: a bespoke, fragmented, decree-driven disposal process (Presidential decree, assets held by the Russian Federation, sold via the Russian Auction House) — not a competitive banking-consolidation market. Barrier to entry here is political/reputational (buying a confiscated asset) as much as capital.

**Competitive landscape.** For a small universal bank the relevant KPIs are assets, equity (book value), net profit and ROE; the deal metric is P/B (price-to-book), since banks trade on book not revenue. Agropromkredit (operating since 1994, 328 staff, 44 offices across 28 cities as of 31-Mar-2026, per auction docs) is a small federal-level universal bank. Credit rating: NKR affirmed BB.ru, outlook revised to positive (per ratings.ru, May-2025) — sub-investment-grade on the national scale. Reported financials conflict across sources and are NOT reconciled to audited CBR data: one source (mainfin.ru) ranks it ~150th by assets at ~10.9bn rubles with charter capital 2.24bn; another (zaimi.ru) shows assets ~22.9bn, equity ~3.47bn, net profit ~0.51bn [UNSOURCED to audited filing — treat as indicative]. Position: a tiny, niche regional player (~0.0x systemic relevance) whose only notable recent event was pausing its development/real-estate projects (RBC Yekaterinburg). No competitive moat; its historic franchise was tied to owners now under prosecution.

**Comps & multiples.** Deal structure: 50% of shares priced at 1.119bn rub; the two SPVs (Densiborg 843.4mn, Blausent 891.8mn) hold the other 50%, so the full lot of 2.85bn effectively buys 100% of the bank. Implied 100% equity value ≈ 2.85bn rub.
- **P/B arithmetic:** vs reported equity 3.47bn → P/B = `2.85bn / 3.47bn = 0.82x` (below book); vs charter capital 2.24bn → `2.85bn / 2.24bn = 1.27x`. Both inputs are unverified/conflicting, so treat the multiple as indicative only — but a sub-1x-to-~1.3x P/B is *cheap-to-in-line* for a small Russian bank, consistent with a distressed, politically-encumbered seller.
- **Internal comps (same PSB forced-disposal channel):** [[PSB fails third auction for nationalized Vektor Rail]] — PSB failed THREE times to sell a confiscated rail asset (start price 20.3bn rub, market talk of a 6–10bn clearing level, i.e. ~50-70% haircut-to-ask); [[Bank First to acquire PSB Holdings in $202.9 million stock deal]] (unrelated US "PSB" — NOT a comp, name collision only).
- External bank-M&A P/B comps for Russian banks: no free, verifiable 2026 data → distribution not computed, qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Auction-failure / illiquidity risk (highest).** Directly evidenced: a parallel auction of the *same owners'* four agro-holdings drew ZERO bids (ura.news, Oct-2026), and PSB failed the Vektor Rail auction three times ([[PSB fails third auction for nationalized Vektor Rail]]). Base-rate for this channel clearing on the first try is poor → the 15-Oct auction may well not transact.
2. **Title / legal-overhang risk.** The asset is a Genprokuratura confiscation sold under Presidential decree; a buyer inherits reputational and potential future-litigation exposure, and one former co-owner (Bobrov) is still under investigation — deters arm's-length financial buyers, narrowing the bid pool.
3. **Information / valuation opacity.** The Finance Minister himself cited investors' lack of information; public financials conflict (10.9bn vs 22.9bn assets) and no audited CBR figure is surfaced — a buyer cannot cleanly underwrite the book, depressing willingness to pay the 2.85bn start price.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Not a sector re-rating — a single distressed-disposal data point. Thesis (analysis): the PSB/Rosimushchestvo re-privatization channel is structurally bid-starved for encumbered assets; watch whether the 15-Oct auction clears at all, or (more likely, per the Vektor Rail/agro-holding precedent) is declared void for want of bidders and relisted at a cut price. Trigger to watch: result of the 15-Oct Russian Auction House session. Thesis would be *wrong* if a single qualified buyer takes the full lot at or above 2.85bn — signalling the channel is finally finding clearing prices.
