# «Яндекс» на фоне атак на дата-центры заявил о сохранении планов по ИИ

«Яндекс» сохранит планы развития ИИ и в ближайшие недели обновит «Алису AI», ежедневная аудитория приложения «Алисы» за месяц выросла на 14%, сообщили в компании на фоне атак на три дата-центра

Фото: Михаил Гребенщиков / РБК

Входит в сюжеты

В этой статье

YDEX 0,44%

«Яндекс» сохранит искусственный интеллект в качестве главной стратегической ставки. Об этом рассказал РБК директор бизнес-группы поисковых сервисов и ИИ компании Дмитрий Масюк.

«Искусственный интеллект — стратегическое направление «Яндекса», в котором мы уже добились значительных результатов. Мы сохраняем планы по его развитию и намерены ускорять внедрение ИИ в продукты и сервисы для пользователей и бизнеса», — заявил он.

По словам Масюка, число ежедневных активных пользователей приложения «Алиса AI» выросло на 14% по сравнению с прошлым месяцем. «В ближайшие несколько недель мы представим масштабное обновление «Алисы AI», которое значительно расширит ее возможности», — добавил он.

Video 2

ИИ-направление «Яндекса» включает пользовательского ассистента «Алиса AI», собственные языковые модели и инструменты для бизнеса. Компания развивает платформу для интеграции ИИ-агентов, которым можно поручать действия в других сервисах, и корпоративные версии «Алисы AI», в том числе для работы на инфраструктуре заказчика. Через Yandex AI Studio разработчики получают доступ к моделям для обработки текстов и документов. В сентябре «Яндекс» также опубликовал базовую версию языковой модели Alice AI Foundation LLM, обученной с нуля.

По данным Mediascope, в сентябре «Алиса AI» стала самым популярным ИИ-сервисом в России: за месяц ею воспользовались 59 млн человек, или 48% населения старше 12 лет. Месячный охват всех ИИ-сервисов достиг 55%, увеличившись за год более чем вдвое. Исследование учитывало сайты и мобильные приложения, но не чат-боты в мессенджерах и ИИ-ответы в поиске.

Заявление о сохранении планов развития ИИ прозвучало на фоне восстановления инфраструктуры после атак на три дата-центра компании. В ночь на 8 октября после удара беспилотников произошел пожар на объекте в Сасово Рязанской области. Его работу полностью остановили. На следующий день в Калуге вышли из строя несколько модулей, а в ночь на 11 октября был поврежден дата-центр под Владимиром, работу которого также приостановили.

На фоне атак перебои затронули «Алису», поиск, карты, такси, почту, платежные и другие сервисы. С трудностями столкнулись и сторонние компании, использующие инфраструктуру «Яндекса». В обращении к сотрудникам и пользователям компания назвала дата-центры своим «железным сердцем» и предупредила, что оценить сроки восстановления оборудования сложно.

14 октября «Яндекс» сообщил о восстановлении большей части функций «Алисы AI», «умных» колонок и телевизоров на YaOS. 20 октября компания вернула доступ к основным возможностям «Директа», включая создание и редактирование рекламных кампаний и управление счетом.

Компания также заявила о развитии распределенной инфраструктуры и систем резервирования, в том числе на независимых площадках.

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What's new.** On 20 Oct 2026, RBK ran a reassurance piece: Yandex's search/AI business-group head **Dmitry Masyuk** said AI remains the company's main strategic bet and plans are unchanged, with a "large-scale" update to the **Alice AI** assistant due "in the coming weeks." He cited **+14% MoM growth in Alice AI daily active users**. This is commentary/signalling, not a new product launch — a deliberate "we're fine" message delivered mid-recovery from the drone attacks on three of its data centers.

**[1] The attack backdrop (dated timeline).** This is the first sustained strike campaign against Russian data infrastructure since 2022. Overnight into **8 Oct**, a Ukrainian drone strike ignited a fire at the **Sasovo (Ryazan region)** data center; operations fully halted. On **9 Oct**, several modules at the **Kaluga** site were disabled (second strike in two days). Overnight into **11 Oct**, the **Vladimir-region** DC was damaged and shut down. Three of Yandex's six data centers went offline in four days. Internally the company called its data centers its "iron heart" ("железное сердце") and warned repair timelines were hard to estimate. See [[Yandex Pay still down after Yandex data-center outages]] and [[Yandex Cloud helps clients migrate to rival providers after outages]].

**[2] Blast radius.** Outages hit Alice, Search, Maps, Taxi, Mail, payments, Cloud, Documents and Tables; Downdetector logged 1,300+ reports. Third parties running on Yandex infra were also affected — a reminder that Yandex is Russia's "Google" and a single point of failure for consumer banking/payment flows. Recovery milestones: **14 Oct** — most Alice AI, smart-speaker and YaOS TV functions restored; **20 Oct** (same day as this note) — core **Yandex Direct** ad-campaign functions restored. Yandex waived Cloud + office-suite fees for **8–31 Oct**.

**[3] Financial + market reaction.** YDEX fell ~3% after the first strike, ~4% after the second, and overall shed roughly **₽77bn (~$918M), a ~5.4% market-cap drop** over two trading days. On the news day YDEX was +0.44%. On AI spend: Yandex does not disclose costs, but analysts estimated training the **Alice AI Foundation LLM** (80B params, 3B active, ~18T tokens, open-weight, released Sept 2026) at only ~**₽0.5–1bn** — small vs. the capex implied by rebuilding physical DC capacity, underscoring that the binding constraint now is compute/real-estate resilience, not model R&D.

**[4] State involvement + why it matters.** Digital Development Minister **Maksut Shadaev** called the attacks "unprecedented in scale" and said his ministry (with the Defense Ministry) helped Yandex migrate key systems to backup/external sites; geographic distribution of reserve capacity preserved service availability. Kremlin's **Peskov** (13 Oct) called Yandex "strong and powerful" but said loss estimates / a compensation mechanism were premature; Deputy Finance Minister **Sazanov** (19 Oct) said Yandex had made no support requests to the ministry. The strategic subtext: Yandex is pushing **distributed infrastructure and redundancy on independent sites** — the AI-continuity message is as much about investor/user confidence as about any new feature. Mediascope: Alice AI was Russia's most-used AI service in Sept — 59M users (48% of 12+ population); all AI services reached 55% monthly reach, more than doubling YoY. Related model work: [[TASS Yandex research aids Anthropic on AI self-propagation]].

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Russia's AI market is ~$3.57bn in 2026, projected to ~$21.4bn by 2031 at ~29% CAGR (per Spherical Insights / dcmarketinsights, as of 2026). The compute layer under it is capital-intensive and concentrating fast: ~$13.4bn across 128 planned data-center projects, but with ~$2.3bn of projects on hold mid-2026 amid high borrowing costs and grid limits (per The Moscow Times, Jun 2026) — capital and power, not demand, are the binding constraints. Structure is a consolidating duopoly: Sber and Yandex together ~62% of the Russia AI data-center market, Sber alone ~40% in 2025 (per dcmarketinsights). Why now: a "sovereign AI" push is pulling domestic big tech into self-funded capex just as the Oct 2026 drone strikes on three Yandex data centers (Sasovo/Kaluga/Vladimir) exposed physical concentration risk — reframing resilience/geographic distribution from a cost line into a strategic necessity.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: DAU/MAU reach and engagement of the assistant, inference volume (tokens served), and data-center uptime/capacity. Yandex position: ahead on consumer AI reach — Alice AI was Russia's most-used AI service in Sep 2026 at 59mn monthly users / 48% of the 12+ population (per Mediascope, via note), DAU +14% MoM, and Yandex AI Studio processed 597bn commercial tokens in H1 2026 (~18x YoY). Key players and basis of competition: Sber (GigaChat; largest infra buyer, raising AI investment 2.3x to ₽350bn in 2026 per search) competes on capital and enterprise/banking distribution; MTS/MWS and VK are smaller challengers; Yandex competes on consumer distribution (search, maps, taxi, devices) and an open-weights play — it published the Alice AI Foundation base LLM (80B params) in Sep 2026, trained from scratch. Moat: search + super-app distribution and first-party data loop (analysis); the strike showed the flip-side — that distribution rides on a small set of owned data centers.

**Comps & multiples.** Yandex (MOEX: YDEX) market cap ~₽1.38–1.47tn (per smart-lab/TradingView, Oct 2026). FY2025 revenue ~₽1,536bn (per Wikipedia/company); 2026 guidance reaffirmed at ~+20% revenue → FY2026E revenue ≈ ₽1.84tn, with adjusted EBITDA guided to ₽350bn (per Reuters/MarketScreener). H1 2026 was strong: Q1 rev ₽372.7bn (+22%), Q2 rev ₽386bn (+16%), Q2 adj. EBITDA ₽89bn (+35%). Multiple (market cap as EV proxy; net debt not separately verified → EV/Rev approximated by P/S): P/S = ₽1.38tn / ₽1.84tn ≈ 0.75x on 2026E revenue; ~0.9x on 2025 revenue. EV/EBITDA on guided ₽350bn ≈ 3.9x. That is cheap vs US large-cap internet/AI names (which trade mid-single-digit to double-digit EV/Rev) and reflects the Russia country/sanctions discount, not weak growth — ~20% growth at <1x sales is a classic geo-discount signature, so NOT an over-valuation flag. Peer: Sber market cap ~₽6.0tn (per stockanalysis), ~4–5x Yandex. Internal comps (same outage event, same name): [[Yandex Pay still down after Yandex data-center outages]], [[Yandex Cloud helps clients migrate to rival providers after outages]], [[Sber adds BPMN generation to Giga Agentic RPA platform]], [[Yandex open-sources YTsaurus Flow real-time stream processing]]. Distribution not computed (fewer than 3 clean public peer multiples); qualitative read: in-line-to-cheap for the growth rate.

**Risk flags.**
1. Physical concentration / infrastructure fragility — three data centers hit in Oct 2026 took down Alice, search, maps, taxi, mail and payments at once; "железное сердце" (iron heart) framing confirms single-point exposure. Second-order: an AI strategy that scales inference demand while the compute base is both concentrated and a kinetic target raises the cost and urgency of geographic redundancy.
2. Capex-vs-constraint squeeze — AI is the top strategic bet just as sector-wide data-center projects are being shelved over borrowing costs and grid limits; Yandex must fund distributed/redundant capacity into a tighter capital environment, pressuring the EBITDA-margin expansion the ~₽350bn guide implies.
3. No state backstop (yet) — MinFin said as of 19 Oct no support request from Yandex and the Kremlin called compensation premature; the company absorbs restoration and hardening costs itself, so resilience is a shareholder-funded line, not socialized.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** The strikes don't change the AI demand thesis — reach and token volumes keep compounding — but they re-rate "distributed, redundant infrastructure on independent sites" from optional to table-stakes, which is a capex story the market hasn't fully priced (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: resilience spend compresses the 2026 EBITDA margin vs the ₽350bn guide. Watch: the promised Alice AI update (next few weeks) as a demand tell, 9M/FY2026 capex and EBITDA-margin disclosure for the redundancy bill, and whether Sber's 62%-combined infra position lets it gain share while Yandex rebuilds. Trigger that breaks the thesis: Yandex holds the ₽350bn EBITDA guide and DAU growth through Q4 despite the outage.
