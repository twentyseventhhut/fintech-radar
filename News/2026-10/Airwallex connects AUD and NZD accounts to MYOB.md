MYOB Business Bank Feed
 
 
 Introduction 
The Airwallex and MYOB connection automates your financial bookkeeping by establishing a direct bank feed between the two platforms.
This integration automatically syncs your Airwallex AUD or NZD account transactions and balances directly into MYOB, eliminating manual CSV uploads and streamlining the reconciliation process.
This setup guide covers:

 Setup Instructions 
 Step 1: Prerequisites – MYOB Plan & Feed Availability 
 Step 2: Initiate Connection from MYOB 
 Step 3: Select Airwallex Accounts to Sync 
 Step 4: Complete Setup 
 Managing Your Bank Feed Connection 
 Setup Instructions 
 Step 1: Prerequisites – MYOB Plan & Feed Availability 
Before you start, check the following:

 MYOB product: This connection is available for MYOB Business (AccountRight / AccountRight Live). It is not available on other MYOB products.

 Currency: Only your business's domestic currency wallet can be linked — AUD for Australian businesses, NZD for New Zealand businesses. Multi-currency Airwallex wallets cannot be linked to a single MYOB feed; you'll only see accounts in your domestic currency during setup.

 Feed availability: MYOB limits the number of bank feeds available per plan (for example, Business Lite plans are capped at 2 feeds). If you've already used all your available feeds, you'll need to upgrade your MYOB plan before you can connect Airwallex — MYOB will prompt you to do this during setup if needed.
 Step 2: Initiate Connection from MYOB 
Log in to MYOB and open your Banking Hub (Banking → Banking hub) . Click Connect bank or credit card  
Find Airwallex in the list of supported banks, accept the confirmations, and click Next .  
You'll be redirected to an Airwallex-hosted account selection page to continue setup.
 Step 3: Select Airwallex Accounts to Sync 
If prompted, log in to your Airwallex account.
Review the Airwallex accounts shown on the account selection page — only accounts in your business's domestic currency (AUD or NZD) will be available to select.

 Select the Airwallex account(s) you want to connect to MYOB.   
 Note: The number of accounts you can select may be limited by your remaining MYOB feed quota. If you have no feeds remaining, you'll see a message asking you to upgrade your MYOB plan first.   

Select Allow to create the feed(s).
 Step 4: Complete Setup 
Once at least one feed has been created successfully, you'll see a setup confirmation and be redirected back to MYOB's Banking Hub. 

Until MYOB receives the first transaction in your file, the Banking Hub displays the financial institution name by default.
 When a transaction becomes available for reconciliation, a banner will prompt you to add a category. You can either select a standard category or create a custom category using the bank account name. The category name does not need to match the Airwallex bank account name. Categories are primarily used for accounting purposes. The account name provided when you create the feed is used internally by Airwallex services and is displayed during the onboarding flow.   
 Your first sync is scheduled automatically — no further action is needed. 
 Going forward, transactions sync automatically at least once every four hours. 
 Important: The BSB and account numbers displayed in MYOB for an Airwallex bank feed are dummy values. Do not use these details to receive or transfer funds. Use them only to match the MYOB account with the corresponding Airwallex account. 
 Managing Your Bank Feed Connection 
 How to view your connected accounts 
Navigate to the MYOB connection page: Select Settings → Connections, then select MYOB.   

View your active feed mappings, including account name, account number/BSB (for AU accounts), currency, and feed status.
Check the last and next scheduled sync times.
 How to sync historical transactions 
Navigate to the MYOB connection page: Select Settings → Connections, then select MYOB.   


 From the MYOB connection page in Airwallex, choose the option to backfill historical transactions.   
   

Select a start date — you can go back up to one year . 

Start the sync. You'll see in-app status while it runs, and be notified on completion or if an error occurs.
 How to disconnect 
From the MYOB connection page in Airwallex, select the specific feed you want to disconnect.  
Confirm the disconnection. Airwallex will notify MYOB to remove the feed.
Important: MYOB does not notify Airwallex if you disconnect a feed directly from MYOB's side. If you disconnect there, please also remove the connection from Airwallex so both systems stay in sync.
 Troubleshooting sync failures 
If a scheduled sync fails (for example, due to expired credentials or the feed being deleted in MYOB), Airwallex automatically emails the workspace owner and admins, and shows a persistent error banner on the MYOB connection page with guidance to resolve it.
If you see a "connection needs attention" prompt, this usually means the feed was disconnected or broken on MYOB's side. Reconnect by re-initiating the connection from MYOB's Banking Hub (see Step 2).

## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened.** Airwallex launched a direct **bank feed** between its platform and **MYOB Business** (AccountRight / AccountRight Live), auto-syncing AUD or NZD account transactions and balances into MYOB and replacing manual CSV uploads for reconciliation. Only the business's **domestic-currency wallet** can be linked (AUD for AU, NZD for NZ); multi-currency wallets are not supported on a single MYOB feed. Sync runs at least every four hours; historical backfill goes back up to one year. (Sources: Airwallex MYOB help article; airwallex.com/global.)

**[1] Why it matters.** This is a low-flash but strategically consistent move: Airwallex is wiring itself into the **two dominant ANZ SME accounting stacks** (MYOB and Xero). A native bank feed makes Airwallex "sticky" as a day-to-day operating account for ANZ SMEs, not just a cross-border FX tool, by closing the bookkeeping/reconciliation loop. It arrived **the same day** as a parallel [[Airwallex adds Bank Feed and Spend for Sage Intacct]] launch — signalling a coordinated accounting-integrations push across SMB (MYOB) and mid-market (Sage Intacct) tiers.

**[2] Scale / context.** ANZ is Airwallex's founding market (Melbourne-HQ'd). Airwallex reported **ANZ revenue up ~93% YoY** and global annualised transaction volume of **~$235B (+127% YoY)** when it named [[Airwallex names James Teodorini as ANZ head amid volume records]] its ANZ head (Jan 2026); global volume now runs near ~$287–300B annualised. ANZ governance was reinforced via [[Airwallex names Sir Bill English as NZ board chair]]. Accounting-integration lineage includes [[Dext and Airwallex launch Dext Payments]] (bookkeeping automation + supplier payments).

**[3] Caveats / unknowns.** Pure product/help-doc announcement, no disclosed adoption numbers, pricing, or SME-count impact. Constraints are real: domestic-currency-only feed, MYOB feed-quota caps (e.g. Business Lite = 2 feeds, may force a plan upgrade), and **dummy BSB/account numbers** in MYOB (not usable for funds movement). MYOB-side disconnects don't notify Airwallex, risking stale feeds. No competitive claim vs. incumbent AU/NZ bank feeds (ANZ, CBA, etc.) already in MYOB.

**[4] Watch next.** Whether Airwallex extends the MYOB feed to **multi-currency** wallets; attach rate of accounting integrations among ANZ SMEs; whether this feeds into AUSTRAC AML scrutiny ([[AUSTRAC orders external audit of Airwallex over AML]]) given deeper domestic-account positioning; and parity/expansion of its accounting-connector set (MYOB, Xero, Sage Intacct, Dext, QuickBooks).

8. Does this change Airwallex's competitive position vs. Wise, Revolut Business, or incumbent ANZ banks in the SME accounting-integration race, or just reach parity?

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Embedded finance inside SME accounting software (bank feeds / reconciliation) layered on cross-border B2B payments. ANZ cloud-accounting is a near-duopoly: in Australia Xero ~48%, MYOB ~30%, QuickBooks ~15% of SME cloud accounting; in New Zealand Xero holds ~70% (per envoice.eu / nzbusinesstools.co.nz, 2026). Structurally consolidated at the ledger layer, fragmented at the banking/payments layer — value accrues to whoever owns the data pipe into the ledger. "Why now": MYOB transitioned to Open Banking as the default bank-feed connection on 2026-03-01, adding 50+ connection options (per envoice.eu) — i.e. Airwallex is plugging into a freshly-opened, standardised feed rail rather than a bespoke one. The underlying cross-border flow is large and growing: Airwallex processed ~$287bn annualized TPV in Mar-2026, up >120% YoY (IR, 2026-03-06 / Connecting the Dots).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: for the cross-border engine — TPV, take rate, multi-product attach; for the accounting-integration layer — number of live bank-feed connectors and feed reliability/latency. Airwallex syncs AUD/NZD feeds automatically at least every ~4h with 1-yr backfill (primary source, help.airwallex.com). Players: (a) ledger incumbents Xero and MYOB own the SME relationship and the feed marketplace; (b) cross-border/B2B peers that also integrate with ANZ accounting software — Wise, Payoneer, WorldFirst (bank-transfer for AUD, direct-credit for NZD per worldfirst.com), plus ApprovalMax / Weel on the spend side (scalesuite.com.au). Basis of competition: not price on this feature but distribution (being discoverable inside MYOB's Banking Hub) and FX/transfer economics behind the account. Airwallex position: catching-up-to-table-stakes on the feed itself (Xero feeds are described as faster/more reliable; Xero is tripling feeds via Plaid), but ahead on the full multi-currency account + >90% of revenue from customers using >1 product (IR/CNBC) — the feed is a retention/switching-cost hook, not the product. Moat `(analysis)`: switching costs via embedded reconciliation + proprietary local-payments network; weak at the feed layer itself where MYOB owns the gateway.

**Comps & multiples.** Airwallex (private): ~$11-12bn valuation (Series H $320M at $11bn, Aug-2026 IR; ~$12bn secondary per Axios 2026-05-28), ARR ~$1.5bn (May-2026, CNBC/Connecting the Dots), TPV ~$287-300bn. Round-valuation EV/ARR ~ $11bn / $1.5bn ~ **7.3x** (round valuation, not market cap).

Flag: Airwallex at ~7.3x on ARR screens rich vs Wise 4.3x / Payoneer 2.3x, but is a private round mark (illiquid, preference-stacked) on much higher growth (ARR $1.1bn->$1.5bn in ~5 months; TPV +120% YoY) — above the public range is defensible by growth, not an automatic over-valuation flag. Comparability caveat: Wise/Payoneer are public and lower-growth; 7.3x vs 4.3x is a growth/liquidity premium, not a clean like-for-like.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Rail dependence / disintermediation.** The feed rides MYOB's Open Banking gateway (and Yodlee aggregation per help.airwallex.com); MYOB controls discovery, feed quota (Business Lite capped at 2 feeds), and can re-platform the connector. Airwallex is a guest in someone else's ledger — second-order: the ledger owner captures the customer relationship and can favour its own or a rival's feed.
2. **Feature parity, not differentiation.** Automatic bank feeds to MYOB/Xero are table stakes; Xero's feeds are seen as faster/more reliable and it is tripling feeds via Plaid. Risk: the integration neutralises a switching objection but adds little standalone pricing power — the real battle stays on FX/transfer economics, which this note is silent on.
3. **Scope limits temper the TAM.** Only the domestic-currency wallet links (AUD for AU, NZD for NZ); multi-currency wallets can't feed a single MYOB feed, and it's MYOB Business (AccountRight) only. The multi-currency value prop — Airwallex's actual edge — is exactly what's excluded from the headline feature, so adoption uplift may be narrower than the PR implies.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** `(analysis)` Incremental distribution land-grab, not a re-rating: Airwallex is wiring itself into the ANZ SME ledger as a default bank option to defend/grow its home-market account base ahead of a mooted 2026 IPO. Falsifiable thesis: if the feed drives net-new AUD/NZD account adds and multi-product attach in ANZ, watch for it surfacing in H2-26 regional revenue disclosures; thesis breaks if Xero (the ANZ leader) ships an equivalent native Airwallex feed or if MYOB throttles third-party feeds in favour of its own payments stack. Trigger to watch: Airwallex extending the same feed to Xero (the ~70% NZ / ~48% AU leader) — that would be the real volume unlock.
