# Ripple plans XRP-backed credit business in 2027

> [!info] 2026-10-03 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇰🇷 Ripple plans to launch a credit business backed by XRP in 2027, President Monica Long said at the XRP Seoul conference on Oct. 3. The service will use XRP as collateral for payment loans, requiring the token to be locked in lending pools for weeks or months. Ripple is currently running credit pilots with the goal of activation next year.
