## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] TL;DR.** In mid-October 2026 Bybit used a CEO roadmap moment to unveil **two distinct equity products** that this note conflates under one headline. (a) **Bybit Stocks** — real, direct-ownership US equities (1,000+ stocks/ETFs) via Alpaca, which went live 2026-10-12 and is covered separately in [[Bybit opens US stocks trading to global users]]. (b) **mStocks** — a *tokenised* equity product developed with **Mantle** (Bybit's affiliated L2), targeted to launch **~November 2026**, offering 24/7 on-chain trading of equity tokens backed 1:1 by real shares, usable as collateral in the Unified Trading Account (UTA) and tradeable via AMM liquidity pools on Mantle. The "launched 1,000+ US stocks AND developed mStocks" framing in the aggregated text merges the shipped real-share product with the still-forthcoming tokenised one.

**[1] Why it matters.** This is the clearest signal yet of Bybit's "New Financial Platform" pivot: it now straddles BOTH equity models — real-share brokerage (Alpaca rails) and tokenised on-chain equities (Mantle/Backed rails) — rather than choosing one. mStocks specifically weaponises Mantle (Bybit's own L2 ecosystem) to pull tokenised US equities into DeFi: 24/7 trading including weekends, two-way redemption, UTA collateralisation, and DEX/AMM composability. If delivered, it turns equity exposure into a programmable crypto-native asset and deepens the Bybit↔Mantle flywheel. The catch is legal substance: reporting indicates mStocks would be issued as **limited-recourse debt obligations by Halo Prime Limited (BVI)** — i.e. token holders get a creditor claim, NOT share ownership — a materially weaker wrapper than the Alpaca real-share product.

**[2] Internal lineage (prior coverage in base).**
- [[Bybit opens US stocks trading to global users]] — 2026-10-12: the *real-share* Alpaca product (enriched, importance 4). THIS note's "1,000+ US stocks" claim is the same launch; the mStocks/Mantle piece is the new addition.
- [[Kraken and Bybit launch tokenized US stocks]] — Jul 2025: Bybit's first tokenised-equity move (Backed Finance xStocks, 1:1, on Solana). mStocks is the natural successor, now on Mantle.

**[3] External facts & timeline.**
- 2025-06/07: Backed Finance's xStocks debut on Bybit & Kraken (Solana, 1:1 tokenised equities).
- 2026-10-12: Bybit Stocks (real equities via Alpaca, USDC-funded, 24/5) goes live.
- 2026-10-13/14: CEO Ben Zhou outlines the "all-asset" roadmap and unveils **mStocks** developed with **Mantle**, targeting a **November 2026** launch; 24/7 trading, 1:1 backing by real shares, two-way redemption, UTA collateral, Mantle AMM/DEX trading (per Cryptobriefing, Cryptometer, Bloomingbit, TokenPost).
- Separate but overlapping: a **Mantle + Bybit + Backed** announcement to list tokenised US equities (xStocks: NVDAx, AAPLx, MSTRx) on Mantle, with Bybit supporting deposits/withdrawals of xStocks via Mantle (per PR Newswire, FinanceFeeds). It is ambiguous whether mStocks IS the Backed-issued xStocks-on-Mantle product rebranded, or a parallel Halo-Prime-issued line — the sources do not cleanly reconcile.
- Legal structure (reported): mStocks issued by **Halo Prime Limited (BVI)** as **limited-recourse debt obligations**; Bybit supplies the trading interface and smart-contract ops but is **neither issuer nor custodian** (per Cryptonomist, Cryptometer).
- Availability: like Bybit Stocks, tokenised equities are **not available to US residents/US regions**.

**[4] Competitive landscape.**
- **Tokenised equities (direct comps):** Kraken xStocks (Backed-issued, Solana; 100+ names, targeting 500+); Robinhood EU (~2,000 Stock Tokens, but derivative contracts via its Lithuanian entity); Gemini (Dinari dShares, 1:1, EU since Jun 2025); Binance bStocks; Ondo Global Markets (largest issuer by distributed value); Securitize (Apple/NVIDIA/Tesla on Solana, NYSE trading planned). Market scale: ~$2.9bn distributed value across ~5,881 tokenised stock/ETF assets as of mid-Sep 2026, with Ondo, bStocks and xStocks the largest (per RedStone research).
- **Differentiators / weaknesses:** mStocks' pitch is 24/7 + DeFi composability on Mantle + UTA collateral — strongest on *programmability*. But the limited-recourse-debt / BVI-issuer wrapper is weaker on investor protection than Bybit's own Alpaca real-share product, and comparable in risk to Robinhood's derivative tokens. IMF has flagged tokenised equities as materially more volatile than underlyings.
- **Bybit's position:** late but hedged — running real shares (Alpaca) + tokenised (Mantle/Backed) simultaneously; Mantle gives it a captive L2 rail competitors must rent elsewhere.

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team questions**

1. Headline conflation: this note merges a *shipped* product (1,000+ real US stocks via Alpaca, live 2026-10-12) with a *forthcoming* one (mStocks, ~Nov 2026). Are these one launch or two? The aggregated text's "launched … AND developed mStocks" blurs a live product with roadmap vapourware.
2. Is mStocks the SAME thing as the Mantle+Bybit+Backed "xStocks on Mantle" announcement, or a separate Halo-Prime-issued line? Sources don't reconcile: one set says Backed issues xStocks (NVDAx/AAPLx), another says Halo Prime Limited (BVI) issues mStocks as debt. Which issuer actually stands behind the token?
3. "Backed 1:1 by real shares" — who custodies the underlying shares, under whose regulation, and who audits the 1:1 backing? If the issuer is a BVI entity, what proof-of-reserves or attestation exists?
4. Limited-recourse debt obligation vs ownership: holders get a *creditor claim*, not equity. In an issuer default or share-custody loss, what is the recovery path, and is this disclosed to retail as clearly as the "backed by real shares" marketing?
5. Bybit says it is "neither issuer nor custodian," only the interface/smart-contract operator — does that shield Bybit from liability while it markets mStocks under its own brand? Where does user recourse actually sit?
6. Launch date risk: as of 2026-10-26 mStocks is still "targeting November." Is there any confirmed go-live, or is this pre-announcement? Has the date slipped before?
7. Mantle conflict of interest: Mantle is closely affiliated with Bybit (shared ecosystem/treasury). Is routing tokenised equities through a related-party L2 a governance red flag, and does it concentrate risk in one chain?
8. UTA collateralisation: mStocks usable as collateral for crypto trades — what haircut/conversion ratio applies (undisclosed), and what cascade risk does equity-token collateral introduce during a crypto drawdown or an equity gap-down over a weekend?
9. 24/7 trading when NYSE/Nasdaq are closed: who provides weekend liquidity, how is price discovered, and how wide can AMM spreads get vs the underlying's last close? Is retail exposed to stale/mispriced marks?
10. Jurisdiction: tokenised equities are "not for US residents." What is the positive list of eligible jurisdictions, and how does a BVI issuer + offshore structure survive EU/MiCA scrutiny already hitting Robinhood's tokenised equities?
11. Dividends/corporate actions: do mStocks holders receive dividends, splits, voting? If debt-wrapped, likely only synthetic economic pass-through — is withholding tax (typically 30% on US dividends for non-US) handled or silently absorbed?
12. Redemption claim: "24/7 two-way redemption" into the underlying — redeemable for cash, for actual shares, or only swappable for USDC/crypto? Redemption mechanics and any gating/fees are unspecified.
13. Overlap/cannibalisation: Bybit now offers real stocks (Alpaca), tokenised mStocks (Mantle), legacy xStocks, and TradFi perps. Do retail users understand which product gives real ownership vs a debt token? Mis-selling risk.
14. Independent confirmation: nearly all detail is from crypto-trade press echoing Bybit's own announcement. Is there any regulator filing, issuer prospectus, or third-party audit of mStocks, or is this entirely PR-sourced as of 2026-10-26?
15. Numbers check: how many symbols will mStocks support at launch vs the 1,000+ real-share catalogue? The note implies parity but mStocks may launch with a far smaller set.

**Importance: 3/5** — A credible, concrete escalation of Bybit's equity push: it adds a *tokenised*, Mantle-based, DeFi-composable equity product (mStocks) on top of the real-share Alpaca launch, deepening the crypto↔TradFi convergence narrative and giving Bybit a captive on-chain rail. But it is docked from a 4 because (a) the headline double-counts the already-covered real-stocks launch ([[Bybit opens US stocks trading to global users]]), (b) mStocks is still *pre-launch* (targeting Nov 2026) with unresolved issuer/structure ambiguity (Backed xStocks vs Halo Prime debt), (c) the weaker limited-recourse-debt wrapper undercuts the "real shares" pitch, and (d) coverage is almost entirely Bybit-PR-derived with no regulator or audit confirmation.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Bybit's move sits in two adjacent markets: (1) crypto-rails equity brokerage (real shares bought with USDC) and (2) tokenised equities / RWA. Tokenised stocks are a small-but-fastest-growing RWA slice: ~$3.22bn of distributed tokenised-stock value as of 3 Oct 2026, up from ~$2m (Jun 2025) and ~$486m (end-Q1 2026); trackers differ ($1.3–2.5bn mid-2026), and the segment grew ~50% in one recent 30-day window — the fastest of any RWA category (per RWA.xyz, via cryptobriefing/eco.com). Total RWA on-chain value ex-stablecoins was ~$38.6bn on 27 Sep 2026 (per Stobox). Structure is still fragmented and issuer-concentrated — as of Q1 2026 four issuers anchored the category (Backed/xStocks, Dinari/dShares, Robinhood EU tokens, Kraken via Backed), with Ondo Finance holding ~60% of a ~$960m sub-segment in Mar 2026. Why now: the SEC's Sep 2026 "Innovation Exemption" plus Nasdaq's Mar 2026 rule change opened a regulated US path for tokenised NMS stocks, re-rating the whole category (per CNBC/Cointelegraph).

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: trading volume / notional, AUC of underlying shares, number of listed tickers, and custody/clearing counterparty. Bybit launched stocks trading live on 12 Oct 2026 — 1,000+ US stocks and ETFs (AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, QQQ), bought/sold in USDC, with an economic interest in the real underlying securities, cleared and custodied via Alpaca (US self-clearing broker-dealer) — notably NOT synthetic (per prnewswire, [[Bybit opens US stocks trading to global users]]). The tokenised layer, mStocks, is a separate, still-unlaunched product (targeted Nov 2026) built with Mantle: 1:1 real-share backing, 24/7 conversion, AMM/DEX trading on Mantle, and use as UTA collateral (per cryptometer/tokenpost — announced, not live). Peers: Kraken (acquired Backed Finance Dec 2025; xStocks >$25bn cumulative transaction volume, now paired with Nasdaq), Robinhood (EU tokenised stocks), Coinbase (earlier explored a tokenised-stock tie-up with Bybit — [[Coinbase and Bybit explore US tokenized stock collaboration]]), Dinari, Ondo. Basis of competition: regulatory legitimacy + distribution (Bybit's 80m-user base) rather than price. Position: catching up on tokenisation (mStocks launches after Kraken already live at scale) but credible via its user base + a non-synthetic, Alpaca-cleared cash-equities product. Moat: distribution/scale `(analysis)`; the tokenisation IP sits with Mantle, not Bybit.

**Comps & multiples.** Bybit is private and does not disclose revenue, trading volume (fiat equities) or valuation, so trading multiples = no data / `[UNSOURCED]`. IR grounding (Bybit 2025 Recap, per prnewswire 3 Jan 2026): 80m+ users, 24 proof-of-reserves reports — scale/trust metrics, no P&L. Reference points: Kraken's xStocks >$25bn cumulative tokenised volume (per crypto.news); the earlier Coinbase–Bybit US-entry talks were framed around a ~$25bn figure (per MEXC via [[Coinbase and Bybit explore US tokenized stock collaboration]] — deal value, not a market cap). Robinhood (HOOD) is the only listed pure-play (10-Q FY2026 on EDGAR) but its tokenised-stock line is immaterial, so no clean read-across. Distribution not computed — qualitative comparison only. Internal comps: [[Telegram Wallet to offer tokenized US stocks and ETFs via Kraken]], [[Bybit and DigiFT enable UBS tokenized fund as trading collateral]], [[Coinbase and Bybit explore US tokenized stock collaboration]].

**Risk flags.**
1. Regulatory / structural: the SEC Innovation Exemption requires tokens to carry the *same* rights as underlying shares and venues to permission users/pools — commentators flag that Robinhood's and Kraken's *current* synthetic-style forms may not qualify (per Cointelegraph). mStocks' 1:1-real-share, DEX-tradable design must thread this needle; a mis-step risks US exclusion or forced redesign. Second-order: the compliant cash-equities product (via Alpaca) and the DeFi tokenised product may diverge in jurisdictional reach.
2. Execution / announced-vs-live gap: mStocks is targeted for Nov 2026 and not yet live — the tokenisation, AMM liquidity on Mantle and UTA-collateral features are unproven; thin AMM liquidity would undercut the "24/7 tradable" pitch.
3. Dependence on others' rails: equities clearing/custody runs through Alpaca and tokenisation through Mantle — Bybit captures distribution but not the clearing or the token economics; margin and fraud/custody liability sit partly outside its stack.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** The post-SEC-exemption window is triggering a land-grab: crypto exchanges racing to bolt regulated US equities onto crypto UX, with Bybit choosing a dual track (real-share brokerage now, tokenised mStocks next). `(analysis)` Falsifiable thesis: Bybit's 80m-user distribution lets it scale equities faster than standalone tokenisation issuers — watch mStocks' actual Nov launch, its US eligibility under the Innovation Exemption, and on-chain Mantle liquidity/volume; if mStocks slips past Q4 2026 or is geo-blocked from the US, the thesis weakens.
