## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**[0] What happened (one-liner).** On 2026-10-20 Salesforce signed a definitive agreement to acquire customer-engagement/marketing platform **Braze (NASDAQ: BRZE)** in an **all-cash** deal valued at ~**$5.66B** enterprise value (headlined "$5.7B") — **$47.50/share in cash**, a **~58% premium** to Braze's 2026-10-19 close of $30.04. Braze shares jumped ~51% on the news; Salesforce (CRM) slipped ~3%. The deal is expected to close in Salesforce's **fiscal 2028** (which begins early February 2027), subject to Braze shareholder and regulatory approvals.

**[1] Deal terms & mechanics.**
- Structure: all-cash tender/merger at $47.50 per Braze Class A share; ~$5.66B enterprise value (Bloomberg/Salesforce headline $5.7B; one early outlet carried $5.6B). Equity value is somewhat lower net of Braze's cash.
- Rationale: fold Braze's real-time cross-channel engagement (mobile/web/email/SMS/WhatsApp), customer-data and AI personalization into Salesforce's Marketing Cloud and **Agentforce** autonomous-agent stack. Braze serves ~2,770 customers (Canva, Equinox, Instacart, Venmo among them).
- Market read: 58% premium + 51% BRZE pop signals a clear control premium for a profitable-ish SaaS engagement asset; CRM dip reflects dilution-to-cash / "is Salesforce overpaying for growth" skepticism and a pattern of expensive bolt-ons.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Deal at a glance.** Salesforce agreed (announced 2026-10-20) to acquire NYC-based customer-engagement platform **Braze** for ~**$5.66B enterprise value**, all-cash at **$47.50/share** — a ~58% premium to the prior close. Expected to close in Salesforce's fiscal 2028 (begins ~Feb 2027). Braze shareholders confirmed cash (not stock), per Salesforce's official statement and Braze's DEFA14A/8-K filings; an early wire headline calling it "all-stock" appears erroneous. Braze serves ~2,600–2,770 customers (Canva, Instacart, Venmo, Equinox) with a cloud-native, event-streaming architecture tuned for high-volume B2C real-time messaging across mobile/web/email/SMS/WhatsApp.

**Deal multiple — rich but not outlandish.** Against Braze's **FY2026 revenue of $738.2M (+24.4% YoY)** and ~$800M+ ARR shortly after year-end, $5.66B EV is **~7.7x trailing revenue**. On forward numbers it compresses: ~**5.7x NTM sales** (vs Braze's 2-year avg ~3.6x and the ~5.0x median software-deal multiple since 2025) and ~5.3x FY2028E sales (BTIG). So Salesforce is paying a premium to Braze's own trailing trading range but roughly at/above the current large-cap SaaS M&A median — defensible given 24% growth, but not cheap.

**Strategic read — AI-CRM consolidation, filling the "engagement" gap.** This slots directly into Salesforce's acquisition cadence of the past year: **Fin (AI customer service, ~$3.6B, Jun-2026)**, **M3ter (usage-based billing → Agentforce Revenue Management, Jun-2026)**, and informatica/data-layer moves. Braze adds real-time, high-frequency B2C engagement and first-party behavioral context — a capability Marketing Cloud has historically been weaker at vs. pure-play engagement tools. It also feeds the **Data Cloud / Agentforce** flywheel: more first-party signal → better agentic personalization. Management's public ambition is **$63B revenue by FY2030** (Bloomberg, Sep-2026), and bolt-ons like this are part of how that gap is closed.

**IR grounding (PRIMARY evidence — Salesforce FY2026, period ended 2026-01-31; source: Q4 FY26 earnings 8-K exhibit 99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000056/crm-q4fy26xexhibit991.htm; latest in ir_latest.json salesforce = Q1 FY27 8-K 2026-06-02, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1108524/000110852426000138/crm-20260527.htm):**
- FY26 total revenue **$41.5B, +10% YoY** (+9% cc); Q4 FY26 rev **$11.2B, +12% YoY**; subscription & support Q4 **$10.7B, +13%**.
- **RPO $72.4B (+14%)**, current RPO **$35.1B (+16%)** — large, visible backlog.
- **Agentforce + Data 360 ARR $2.9B, +200%+ YoY** — the strategic engine Braze is meant to amplify.
- Operating cash flow **$15.0B (+15%)**, FCF **$14.4B (+16%)** — ample balance-sheet capacity to fund a $5.66B all-cash deal without stress.
Context: at $41.5B of revenue, Braze's ~$740M is <2% of pro-forma top line — financially immaterial near-term; the thesis is product/data, not scale.

**In-base comps (Fintech Radar DB):**
- `News/2026-06/Salesforce acquires AI consulting firm Fin.md` — AI customer service, ~$3.6B; adjacent agentic-CX bolt-on.
- `News/2026-06/Salesforce moves to acquire usage-based billing platform M3ter.md` — monetization/billing for the "AI era" (Agentforce Revenue Management).
- `News/2026-09/Salesforce forecasts $63bn revenue for fiscal 2030.md` — the growth-ambition backdrop driving M&A.
- `News/2025-10/Singlife and Salesforce deploy AI agent for customer service.md` — Agentforce demand-side evidence.

**Risk flags (skeptical):**
1. **Multiple risk / price-to-perfection.** ~7.7x trailing (5.7x NTM) prices in continued 20%+ growth and clean integration; Braze's **net dollar retention has slipped to 109%** (TTM to Jan-2026) — decelerating expansion. If growth normalizes toward mid-teens, the premium looks expensive.
2. **Integration & overlap.** Braze overlaps Salesforce's existing Marketing Cloud; cannibalization, roadmap collision, and the usual SaaS post-acquisition sales-motion disruption are real. Salesforce's own CRM shares **fell ~3%** on the news, and multiple analysts (BTIG, Piper Sandler) **downgraded Braze** — street skepticism on strategic value/price, not just arbitrage.
3. **Long close window = execution drag.** Targeted close in FY2028 (into 2027) means ~a year-plus of customer/partner uncertainty and potential talent attrition at Braze before synergies land; regulatory/antitrust review of another Salesforce roll-up is also non-trivial.
4. **"AI-washing" of a mature channel tool.** Braze is fundamentally a messaging/engagement platform; the "agentic AI" framing is partly narrative. Durable value depends on Data Cloud/Agentforce actually monetizing the added first-party signal, which is unproven at scale.
5. **Capital-allocation optics.** Another sizeable cash acquisition while Salesforce runs large buybacks invites investor questions on whether organic growth is stalling and M&A is backfilling the FY2030 target.

## Earnings Review

<!-- enrichment:earnings_review -->
**Verdict (headline read).** BEAT (operationally modest; headline EPS flattered by a one-off). Most recent print is **Q2 FY27** (quarter ended 2026-07-31, reported 2026-08-26) — the freshest Salesforce results as of this 2026-10-20 Braze deal; the news item itself carries no new financials, so figures below are Salesforce's last reported quarter as the financial backdrop for the acquisition. Revenue **$11.35bn, +10.8% YoY** (vs public consensus ~$11.33bn — a thin beat). Non-GAAP EPS **$5.90, +103% YoY** (vs consensus ~$3.27) — but the bulk of that gap is a ~$2.6bn below-the-line investment gain (tied in part to its Anthropic stake), not operating leverage. FY27 revenue guidance raised to **$46.1–46.4bn** (from $45.9–46.2bn in May).

**Key figures (Q2 FY27, with growth).**
- Total revenue: **$11.35bn, +11% YoY** (and +11% constant currency); subscription & support **$10.8bn, +12% YoY** (+11% CC). Informatica (recently closed) contributed $456M total / $440M subscription.
- GAAP operating margin **20.5%**; non-GAAP operating margin **34.1%**.
- GAAP diluted EPS **$4.29, +119% YoY**; non-GAAP diluted EPS **$5.90, +103% YoY**. GAAP net income ~$3.53bn, lifted by a ~$2.6bn investment gain.
- Operating cash flow **$1.3bn, +71% YoY**; free cash flow **$1.1bn, +81% YoY**.
- cRPO **$33.5bn, +14% YoY CC** (~1pt ahead of guide); RPO **$66.3bn, +11% YoY**.
- Agentforce + Data 360 ARR ~**$3.9bn, +210%+ YoY**; Agentforce alone >**$1.5bn ARR, +240%+ YoY**.
- Capital returns: $364M dividends in-quarter; a **$25bn accelerated share repurchase (ASR)** program ongoing.

**By segment / driver.** Growth is low-double-digit and decelerating toward ~11% — mature core CRM. The genuine momentum is the AI layer (Agentforce/Data 360 off a small base, +210%+). The EPS blowout was overwhelmingly non-operating (investment gain); underlying operating leverage was modest, with GAAP operating margin only 20.5%. This is the backdrop against which Braze is being bought: a company paying up for growth/engagement capabilities while its organic growth sits in the low teens.

**vs expectations / prior period.** Revenue beat public consensus by only ~$20M (~0.2%) — essentially in-line. The EPS "beat" vs ~$3.27 consensus is misleading: strip the ~$2.6bn investment gain and the operating result is far closer to in-line. YoY momentum continues to decelerate vs prior FY26 quarters (Q1–Q4 FY26 all ran high-single/low-double-digit), so the trajectory is steady deceleration, not reacceleration. (Public consensus per StockAnalysis/Yahoo aggregators as of the 2026-08-26 report; EPS consensus figure is non-GAAP and comparability is noisy given the one-off.)

**Guidance / forward.** FY27 revenue **raised to $46.1–46.4bn** (~+11–12% YoY) from $45.9–46.2bn; FY27 non-GAAP operating margin guided ~34.3%, GAAP ~20.1%; cRPO ~+14% CC. Q3 FY27 revenue guided $11.42–11.5bn (+11–12%). Tone: confident on AI monetization, but the raise was small (~$0.2bn) and partly reflects Informatica consolidation rather than pure organic acceleration — management is quiet on when core growth re-accelerates.

**Thesis-flags (how Braze fits the financial position).**
1. **M&A as a growth substitute.** Organic revenue growth has slipped to ~11% and is decelerating; buying Braze ($5.7bn EV, $47.50/share cash, ~58% premium, ~2,770 customers) adds real-time B2C engagement to the Marketing/Data 360 stack. Fact → growth is maturing → Salesforce is backfilling the top line with deals (Informatica, now Braze) → second-order: integration/retention risk and a shift from the post-Slack "disciplined M&A / margins + buybacks" narrative back toward acquisitive growth.
2. **Balance-sheet capacity is there, but priorities compete.** FCF $1.1bn/qtr (+81%) and a $25bn ASR underway signal strong cash generation — a ~$5.7bn all-cash deal is affordable. Second-order: cash committed to Braze is cash not buying back stock; watch whether the ASR pace slows. (CRM shares fell ~3% on the announcement, consistent with "paying a 58% premium for lower-growth-stage engagement tech" skepticism.)
3. **EPS quality, not just level.** The Q2 EPS beat was a ~$2.6bn investment gain, not operating leverage — so the "record EPS" headline overstates core earnings power. Braze (likely dilutive to margins near-term, pre-synergy) pressures the very operating-margin story that has supported the multiple. Watch FY28 guidance (deal closes in FY28) for margin dilution commentary.
4. **Strategic coherence over financial accretion.** Rationale is capability (real-time engagement + AI decisioning across Data 360 / Customer 360 / Agentforce / SIForce), not near-term accretion. For the thesis this is an AI-platform land-grab: consistent with the Agentforce/Data 360 +210% story, but it raises execution risk and resets Salesforce as a serial acquirer again.
