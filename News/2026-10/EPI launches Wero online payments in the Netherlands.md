EPI Company initiates Wero roll-out in the Netherlands
As part of the migration process from iDEAL to Wero announced earlier this year, EPI enters a new phase with the roll-out of webshops' payment use case with all participating Dutch banks. Through a single integration of Wero, merchants will be able to reach a larger scale at European level while offering independent, sovereign payments to their customers.
 Brussels / Amsterdam, 26 October 2026 – European Payments Initiative (EPI) today announced the ignition of the next phase of the iDEAL to Wero migration with the launch of online commercial payments with all participating Dutch banks from 26 October. The ignition of the Wero payment service now allows payment service providers (PSPs) to progressively allow the deployment of Wero for Dutch merchants. From now on merchants will be welcomed to transition from iDEAL to Wero. Our strong ambition is to finalise the migration before the end of 2027 as this timeframe is the best guarantee to ensure a successful and smooth transition.
The launch of the Wero ecommerce use case will be effective for customers of ABN AMRO, ASN Bank, bunq, BUUT, Finom, ING, Knab, Nationale-Nederlanden, Rabobank, Triodos Bank, Van Lanschot Kempen on 26 October. Commercial payments will be extended in Q2 2027 with new payment management options such as subscriptions and pay on delivery.
Through its phased approach, EPI is allowing every merchant to work on its own deployment calendar and that of its’ PSP, while also offering additional time to integrate purchase protection which will be embedded in Wero for Dutch consumers by January 2028. This protection is being discussed to craft a solid solution with the support of all parties involved, from merchants to unions, federations and PSPs as well as consumer associations, banks and relevant institutions. The solution will be aligned through a series of workshops which will be hosted over the fourth quarter of 2026, and announced in the first quarter of 2027.
Smoothness, security and trust have been the guiding principles of the iDEAL to Wero migration from the beginning, to truly build on the foundation with the established use cases and simplicity of the solution while developing additional use cases and value-added services.
To support the ignition of Wero payments in webshops and pursue awareness initiatives, a new marketing campaign has been designed by DEPT Agency and orchestrated by Havas Media, and will be launched on 26 October, running until March 2027.
 Seeing Wero in the banking app of our Dutch shareholders and members is definitely the milestone we were looking forward to for the past year. Thanks to the continuous cooperation of all parties involved in the migration, we are entering this new phase ahead of the peak season, with the first merchants now joining the Wero journey - and we couldn’t be happier to observe the first transactions coming through. We are not just championing European sovereignty in payment, we are building it, creating new opportunities for commerce and empowering merchants to thrive. 
Martina Weimert
CEO of EPI
The deployment of Wero doesn’t require any action from consumers. Banks will keep their customers informed through its official banking channels. Neither Wero nor the bank will ever ask consumers to provide personal or banking information outside the secure banking environment. No new service should be subscribed to.
 About EPI 
 EPI  (or European Payments Initiative) is supported by 18 European banks and payment service providers. Beyond the shareholders, over 50 institutions in Europe are now members of EPI. They have joined forces with a common goal: to offer a unified mobile payment service, to all European companies and citizens, Wero. EPI intends to enable European consumers and merchants and to carry out all types of retail transactions simply, via a resolutely sovereign digital wallet. 
Find out more at  epicompany.eu and follow us on  LinkedIn 
 About Wero 
Based on instant account-to-account (A2A) payments, Wero further streamlines payments in Europe by eliminating intermediaries in the payment chain and the associated additional costs. Wero already supports payments between individuals, and progressively unveils payments to professionals (P2Pro). Wero is live for P2P payments in Belgium, France, Germany and Luxembourg, currently serving 60 million users. Wero also plans to expand into Austria. 
For retail payments, Wero is live in Germany, Belgium and Luxembourg with progressive roll-out in France throughout 2026. Point-of-sale payments are already live in Belgium and Luxembourg with further rollouts planned across other markets. The migration from iDEAL to Wero in the Netherlands is expected to involve around 15 million consumers by 2027. Additional value-added services will include merchant loyalty programmes, recurring payments and subscription management.  
EPI/Wero is also a founding member of the European Network for Payments (ENP), which aims to progressively connect payment solutions collectively serving around 130 million users across 13 European countries.
