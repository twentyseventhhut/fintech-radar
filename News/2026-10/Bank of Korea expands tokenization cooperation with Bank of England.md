Central bank to expand cooperation with Bank of England on tokenization


 Korea and Britain will expand cooperation on tokenized payments and settlement projects, including Project Agora and Project Hangang.
 
The Bank of Korea (BOK) said Tuesday it is expanding cooperation with the Bank of England (BoE) on tokenization projects.
Sarah Breeden, the BoE's deputy governor for financial stability, visited the BOK on Monday and exchanged views with BOK officials on innovations in payment and settlement, according to the BOK.
Breeden and other BoE officials shared updates on tokenization projects, including the global initiative Project Agora and Korea's Project Hangang, with their BOK counterparts.

# Context-enrichment: Bank of Korea expands tokenization cooperation with Bank of England
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 2/5._

**Freshness verdict: FRESH but very thin (a diplomatic visit, not a new deliverable).** This is a distinct, dated event — BoE deputy governor for financial stability **Sarah Breeden visited the BOK in Seoul on Mon 19 Oct 2026**, with the BOK announcing the "expanded cooperation" on **Tue 20 Oct 2026** (digest date 2026-10-21 = coverage/retrieval). It is NOT a reprint of the Project Agora notes ([[Central banks advance Project Agora cross-border payments testing]], [[BIS Project Agora advances tokenised reserves to real-value testing]]) — those are BIS-convened multilateral milestones; this is a **bilateral BoK↔BoE information-exchange visit**. So: fresh as an event, but de-PR'd it carries almost no new substance. No MOU, no signed agreement, no new figures, no dates, no new workstream announced — only "shared updates" and "will actively share experience / participate in global discussions."

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
- Breeden and other BoE officials **"exchanged views"** and **"shared updates"** with BOK counterparts on two already-existing programmes: the BIS-led **Project Agora** (tokenised commercial-bank deposits + tokenised wholesale central-bank money for cross-border payments) and the BOK's **Project Hangang** (domestic blockchain deposit-token / wholesale-CBDC pilot). The BOK said it "will actively share its experience and participate in global discussions."
- **Both central banks are ALREADY co-participants in Project Agora** — the Bank of England and Bank of Korea sit on the same seven-central-bank Agora panel (confirmed in the BIS May 2026 report, [[BIS Project Agora advances tokenised reserves to real-value testing]]). So "expand cooperation" is the activation/warming of a pre-existing multilateral relationship into a bilateral touchpoint, not a new tie-up.
- **Why framed this way / what it reveals:** the BOK press line leads with "expands cooperation on tokenization" because the underlying facts are soft — a courtesy central-bank visit with no binding output. The one genuinely concrete, datable fact buried in the external coverage is **Project Hangang Phase 2 going to a live deposit-token transaction test in Q4 2026** (per Korea Times/fnnews), but that is a *domestic* BOK milestone, not a product of this BoE meeting. The BoE angle is pure relationship-management: Breeden runs financial stability, and the UK is mid-way through its own tokenisation roadmap ([[Bank of England sets five commitments on tokenised markets]]), so comparing notes with a peer that has a *live* retail-facing deposit-token pilot is useful intelligence for both.

## [1] Competitors / peers
- **Project Agora cohort (the real arena):** BIS + Fed NY, Banque de France (Eurosystem), Bank of Japan, Bank of Mexico, Swiss National Bank, Bank of Canada (joined 2026), plus BoE and BoK — 40+ private financial institutions. Both parties to this "bilateral" are already inside this multilateral, so the bilateral adds little beyond goodwill (analysis).
- **Live wholesale-settlement / tokenisation rails elsewhere:** EU's **ECB Project Pontes** went live 21 Sep 2026 linking DLT platforms to TARGET central-bank-money settlement; Switzerland's SDX has settled digital bonds in real wholesale CBDC since Dec 2023. On a *live cross-border tokenised-settlement rail*, both BoK and BoE are behind the euro/Swiss rails.
- **On domestic deposit-token pilots, KOREA IS AHEAD OF THE UK:** Project Hangang Phase 1 (Apr–Jun 2025) ran ~81,000 participants / ~114,880 transactions; Phase 2 began 18 Mar 2026 (9 banks) with a Q4 2026 live deposit-token test incl. government-subsidy disbursement. The UK's equivalent (DIGIT digital gilt, tokenised sterling deposit pilots) is still pre-issuance (Q1 2027 target). **Why this matters (analysis):** the information flow likely runs *toward* the BoE — the BoK has real retail-facing adoption data the UK lacks, which inverts the usual "UK as rule-maker" framing.

## [2] Company / central-bank history / fit
- **BOK trajectory:** Jul 2025 the BOK *halted* its CBDC pilot amid the won-stablecoin bill ([[Bank of Korea halts CBDC project amid stablecoin bill]]); it then pivoted hard toward **bank-led deposit tokens / won stablecoins** ([[Bank of Korea urges banks to lead won stablecoin issuance]], [[Bank of Korea drops opposition to won-pegged stablecoin]], [[Bank of Korea wants gradual, bank-led stablecoin rollout]]). Project Hangang is the surviving, re-scoped wholesale-CBDC+deposit-token vehicle. So the BOK's tokenisation push has real domestic momentum and political cover.
- **BoE trajectory:** methodical sandbox-first programme — DSS, DIGIT, the May 2026 tokenisation Call for Input and the Oct 2026 "five commitments" ([[Bank of England sets five commitments on tokenised markets]], [[Bank of England and FCA launch tokenized wholesale markets consultation]]), plus a parallel stablecoin-cap workstream.
- **Why the two engage now (analysis):** both are converging on the same architecture (tokenised commercial-bank deposits settling against wholesale central-bank money) via Agora; a bilateral visit lets each de-risk interoperability choices early. Structural logic: cross-border tokenised payments are worthless without counterpart central banks adopting compatible rulebooks — so relationship-building across jurisdictions is a necessary (if unglamorous) input.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
- **Novelty: low.** No new agreement, instrument, figure, or timeline was created by this meeting. It is a status-exchange on two programmes that already existed and in which both banks already participate (Agora). "Expand cooperation" ≈ a warm diplomatic touchpoint.
- **Traction is in the underlying projects, not in this event:** Agora is at real-value testing stage (announced, not yet production); Hangang Phase 2 is heading to a Q4 2026 live deposit-token test — those are the datable traction points, independent of this visit.
- **Why value-add is thin (analysis):** the margin/value in cross-border tokenised settlement will be captured at the *platform/standard* layer (BIS Agora shared ledger, or whoever sets the interoperability standard) and by the settlement-asset issuers (central banks for wholesale CBDC; commercial banks for deposit tokens). A bilateral "we'll share findings" visit does not move where value accrues; it is coordination overhead. What would make a future BoK–BoE story genuinely weighty: a signed interoperability pilot linking Hangang deposit tokens to a UK settlement leg, or a concrete cross-border corridor test — none announced here.

## [4] What's next / sentiment
- **Next (datable, but not from this meeting):** BOK Project Hangang **Phase 2 live deposit-token test in Q4 2026** (incl. government-subsidy disbursement); Agora proceeds to real-value testing with an enhanced private-sector role; UK tokenisation Roadmap due "later 2026", DIGIT issuance targeted Q1 2027.
- **Sentiment:** coverage is routine central-bank-wire (Yonhap/Korea Times/fnnews) with no analyst reaction — a sign of its low market weight.
- **Why this direction (analysis):** the counterintuitive read is that the *Korean* side is the one with live adoption data and a clearer domestic mandate (post-stablecoin-bill pivot), while the UK is still in sandbox/roadmap mode — so despite the "UK convenes the world" framing on tokenisation, on retail-facing deposit tokens the learning likely flows East→West. Second-order risk for both: if cross-border tokenised settlement coalesces around the euro/Swiss central-bank-money rails (Pontes/TARGET, SDX), a BoK–BoE sterling/won corridor may struggle for relevance absent a shared settlement asset.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Wholesale cross-border settlement via tokenized central-bank money + tokenized commercial-bank deposits — a public-sector R&D race, not a market with a sized TAM (no reliable TAM figure for the wholesale-CBDC/tokenized-deposit niche — "no data"). The anchor, BIS Project Agorá, moved from prototype to real-value testing in 2026: in July 2026 ~28 central banks and FIs settled ~CHF 800,000 across 17 scenarios in six currencies, averaging ~80 seconds vs the 1-to-several business days of correspondent banking (per BIS/Ledger Insights, as of Jul 2026). "Why now": the forcing function is private digital money — BIS puts the stablecoin market at ~$315bn (per BIS, via Forbes, Aug 2026), pushing central banks and banks to build a regulated, insured, programmable alternative before stablecoins capture the settlement layer. De-PR: this specific BOK–BoE item is a deputy-governor courtesy visit + information exchange on Agorá and Project Hangang — knowledge-sharing, NOT a new build, mandate, or committed capital.

**Competitive landscape.** This is an initiatives race, not a revenue market — relevant "KPIs" are settlement finality/latency, currencies & jurisdictions covered, participant count, and live-value vs sandbox status. Two camps have emerged (per Forbes, May 2026, "multilateral CBDC interoperability is dead"): (i) **Western / Agorá** — 7 central banks + ~40 FIs, Bank of Canada joining in May 2026, atomic PvP across tokenized reserves + deposits; (ii) **China-led mBridge** — commercialization-ready, cumulative volume ~$55bn (e-CNY >95% of settlement), running outside the dollar correspondent system (per Reuters/The Block, Ledger Insights, 2026). Korea's own rail, **Project Hangang**, is a wholesale-CBDC settlement layer for commercial-bank deposit tokens; Phase 2 began 18 Mar 2026, expanding to nine banks, adding government-subsidy distribution and P2P transfers, scaling wallets toward ~500k (per crypto.news/FinanceFeeds, 2026). BOK's position: a credible mid-size participant bridging its domestic Hangang pilot into the Agorá/BIS forum — aligned with the Western camp, catching-up rather than leading on scale.

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation math — sovereign/BIS R&D, no equity, no multiples ("no data" by construction). Internal precedent comps from the base (initiatives, not deals): [[China advances mBridge digital payments system to rival the dollar]] (competing bloc), [[ECB advances to next phase of digital euro project]] (parallel sovereign CBDC), [[BNY explores tokenized deposits for payments network]] and [[Chainlink and banks launch Project Pangea for T+0 FX settlement]] (private tokenized-settlement rails), [[Bank of England proposes weekend RTGS and CHAPS settlement]] (BoE's own 24/7-settlement track that the tokenization work complements). Comparison is qualitative: Agorá/Hangang are ahead of the digital euro on cross-border wholesale real-value testing but behind mBridge on live transaction volume.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Multi-year, non-committal timelines.** BIS itself stresses Agorá is a research initiative, not a production network; this item is a visit, not a mandate. Second-order: years of pilots can be overtaken by private rails (bank tokenized-deposit networks, e.g. The Clearing House consortium targeting H1-2027) shipping to production first.
2. **Geopolitical fragmentation.** The Agorá vs mBridge split means no single interoperable standard — Korea must hedge between a Western-aligned stack and a China-led rail that already clears real trade flows; betting on the wrong (or on two incompatible) standards raises eventual integration/switching cost.
3. **Disintermediation by stablecoins / private tokenized deposits.** If regulated stablecoins and bank-run deposit tokens win the programmable-money layer first on reach and liquidity, slow-moving public wholesale-CBDC projects risk capturing only the inter-central-bank niche, not the broader cross-border value chain.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** Incrementally positive signal for the Agorá/Western tokenized-settlement camp — Korea publicly committing to share Hangang findings and participate in global discussions widens the coalition. Falsifiable thesis (analysis): if Agorá advances to a durable live-value corridor including an Asian deposit-token participant within ~12-18 months, tokenized deposits displace correspondent banking at the margin for wholesale FX; the thesis breaks if mBridge-style regional blocs scale real volume faster or if private bank/stablecoin networks (The Clearing House, USDC) go to production first. Watch: Agorá's next real-value phase scope, and whether Hangang Phase 2 outputs feed into a cross-border (not just domestic) corridor.
