Original Press Release

Long Arc Capital has named Vikram Chugh as the new Chief Executive Officer of advisor platform CircleBlack. Chugh joins the firm from Robertson Stephens, where he previously served as Chief Operating Officer.

The appointment marks a strategic leadership transition for CircleBlack, a leading wealthtech platform designed to provide financial advisors with integrated technology solutions. Both CircleBlack and Robertson Stephens are portfolio companies of Long Arc Capital, highlighting a internal leadership synergy within the private equity firm's ecosystem.

This appointment is a calculated move that moves the needle for CircleBlack by injecting seasoned RIA operational experience into its leadership. Vikram Chugh understands the pain points of the modern advisor firsthand. By aligning the leadership of its portfolio companies, Long Arc Capital is positioning CircleBlack to be more than just a software provider, but a core operational partner for the wealth management industry.
