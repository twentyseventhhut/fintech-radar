# RedOwl raises $5.3M seed led by Tidal Ventures

> [!info] 2026-10-14 · 1 упоминаний · 1 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇦🇺 Tidal Ventures has led a $5.3 million seed round for RedOwl, the Melbourne FinTech backed by Mastercard. The round gives the AI powered FinTech a valuation of nearly $40 million, with CP Ventures also participating. RedOwl previously raised $15 million in March last year.
