## Контекст

<!-- enrichment:context -->
**What happened.** Google officially announced the launch of **Google Pay in Uzbekistan** on 2026-10-19 (stated in Samarkand by Google Cloud's CEE government-affairs lead Marcin Krasuski); the Wallet app is expected to go live "in the coming weeks," with the list of supporting banks/cards not yet published. **Apple Pay** is reported by local media (sourced to unnamed insiders, not Apple) as possibly starting 2026-10-27 — unconfirmed; Uzbekistan is not yet on Apple's list of supported countries. So the "by year-end" framing is already partly overtaken: Google Pay is in, Apple Pay is imminent-but-unconfirmed.

**The enabling legal change (correct the timeline).** The blocker was Uzbekistan's **data-localization law**, flagged as early as Dec 2024 as the reason Apple/Google would not integrate. The fix came in **2026, not an earlier year**: President Mirziyoyev signed amendments to the Law on Personal Data on **26 March 2026** (Senate-approved) that abolished the blanket requirement to store all personal data on domestic servers — narrowing mandatory localization to biometric, genetic, and telecom-related categories only. This same reform package also unblocks PayPal. (The note's aggregated Russian text says "в марте" / "in March" — consistent with 26 March 2026.)

**Local schemes are the unresolved question.** Uzbekistan's domestic card rails are **Humo** and **Uzcard**. The decisive open issue in both Apple Pay and Google Pay is whether Humo/Uzcard are tokenized **at launch** or only Visa/Mastercard. The Central Bank of Uzbekistan (CBU) said in July 2026 it was working with commercial banks, Uzcard and Humo on technical integration covering both international networks and the domestic schemes, and that banks were still resolving card-tokenization issues. Domestic-scheme support is what determines mass reach, since most Uzbek-issued cards are Humo/Uzcard, not Visa/Mastercard.

**Competitive frame.** For Uzbek banks this is a new axis of competition on customer experience; for incumbent local payment apps **Payme, Click** and peers it is a potential threat, since NFC wallet tap-to-pay partly substitutes for QR/app-based flows. Related internal context: [[Alipay+ and Uzbekistan’s HUMO partner on cross-border QR payments]] (Oct 2025 — HUMO already pursuing cross-border acceptance), [[Kazakhstan launches interbank QR payment service]] (regional payments modernization), and [[Tencent-backed Uzum weighs London listing for 2027 IPO]] (signals maturing, investable Uzbek fintech market).

**Regional precedent.** Uzbekistan is a late mover in Central Asia. **Kazakhstan** got Apple Pay first in the region back in **Nov 2018** (Google Pay 2021); Apple Pay has since reached Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan. Uzbekistan (pop. ~37M, the region's largest) joining extends the NFC-wallet footprint to the biggest remaining Central Asian market. Apple more broadly has been expanding aggressively — see [[Apple Pay cuts $1 billion fraud, expands to 89 markets]].

## Челлендж / ред-тим

<!-- enrichment:challenge -->
**Red-team / open questions**

1. The Apple Pay "27 October" date rests only on local media citing unnamed sources — Apple has not confirmed and Uzbekistan is absent from Apple's supported-countries page. Treat the date as rumor until Apple lists it.
2. Google Pay was "announced" (2026-10-19) but the Wallet app, participating banks, and card list were NOT yet published — is this a live launch or a forward announcement? The note's "may launch by year-end" title undersells that Google Pay is already in.
3. Does launch include Humo/Uzcard tokenization, or Visa/Mastercard only? This is the single biggest determinant of impact and is explicitly unresolved by CBU as of July 2026.
4. What share of Uzbek cards are Visa/Mastercard vs. Humo/Uzcard? If domestic schemes dominate issuance, a V/MC-only launch reaches a small minority of consumers.
5. The announcement vehicle (Google exec at a Samarkand panel) is a PR/conference setting, not a dated commercial go-live — risk of conflating "announced" with "available."
6. Timeline accuracy: confirm the law was signed 26 March 2026 (not an earlier year) and that the 2024 references are to the *problem* (localization law), not the fix.
7. Interchange/economics: who bears Apple's/Google's tokenization fees on thin Uzbek interchange, and does CBU regulate this?
8. NFC hardware readiness — what share of Uzbek POS terminals accept contactless? Tap-to-pay value is capped by terminal penetration.
9. Threat to Payme/Click is asserted, not measured — do NFC wallets actually displace QR in a QR-heavy market, or coexist?
10. PayPal is bundled in the same reform but has a different (cross-border/remittance) use case; don't assume it launches on the same timeline.
11. Sanctions/geopolitical exposure: any constraint on Apple/Google operating given banks with Russian ties in the region?
12. Source quality: the note's body is a single Telegram channel (Финтехно) aggregation with no primary source text loaded — corroborate against Kun.uz/CBU directly.
13. Biometric/genetic/telecom data still must stay onshore — could that carve-out still block some wallet provisioning flows?
14. "By year-end" is a moving target already partly met; headline may need updating once Apple Pay either does or doesn't go live on/after 27 Oct.

**Importance: 3/5** — A genuine market-opening milestone for the largest Central Asian economy (~37M people) and a clean example of regulatory reform (data-localization easing) directly unblocking Big Tech payments, with knock-on effects for Humo/Uzcard, local wallets (Payme/Click) and the broader Uzbek fintech thesis (Uzum). But it is incremental vs. a Western fintech audience, the headline event (Apple Pay date, domestic-scheme support) is still unconfirmed, and single-channel sourcing caps confidence. Not a 4/5 until domestic-scheme tokenization at launch is confirmed.

## Market Research

<!-- enrichment:market_research -->
**Sector & drivers.** Central Asian digital payments — a fast-growing, under-penetrated market now opening to international wallet rails. Uzbekistan's digital-payments market is projected to grow ~11.14% (2024–2028) to a ~$9.49bn volume by 2028 (per Statista Digital Payments outlook). Adoption is inflecting: ~72% of Uzbeks used digital payments in 2025, up from 39% in 2021, and card-based cashless volume hit ~72% of nominal GDP in 2025 vs ~25% in 2021 (per Central Bank survey / KPMG, via Daryo & UzDaily, 2026). Infrastructure doubled — >68.27m bank cards in circulation and 430,762 POS terminals by Jan 2026 (per Kun.uz). "Why now": the binding constraint was regulatory, not demand — in March 2026 President Mirziyoyev signed amendments relaxing data-localization so most personal-data categories can be processed abroad (biometric/genetic/telco data stay local), which is precisely what unblocked Apple/Google tokenization infra (per Kun.uz, Feb 2026). Government targets 75% cashless share by 2030 to shrink the shadow economy (per Interfax). Structure: still consolidating around two domestic card schemes (Humo, Uzcard) plus wallet/super-app layer (Payme, Click, Uzum); entry barriers are regulatory (data-localization, CBU licensing) and network effects, not capital.

**Competitive landscape.** Sector KPIs: cards in circulation, cashless-% of GDP, active digital-payment users, TPV. Players: domestic card schemes **Humo** and **Uzcard** (the rails almost all local cards sit on); wallet/super-apps **Payme, Click, Uzum** (consumer UX layer); international networks **Visa/Mastercard**; and now **Google Pay** (officially launched 19 Oct 2026, per Kun.uz/Daryo) with **Apple Pay** rumored ~27 Oct but unconfirmed and Uzbekistan still absent from Apple's supported-countries list. Basis of competition: distribution + customer experience, not price. The decisive, still-open variable is whether Humo/Uzcard are supported at launch — the Central Bank said in July 2026 it was working with banks and both domestic schemes on technical integration (per search), but no bank/card list has been published. If only Visa/Mastercard are live at launch, reach is a small slice of the 68m+ cards; with Humo/Uzcard the effect is step-change larger. Apple/Google are entrants, not threats to the schemes — they ride on top of the existing rails and take a tokenization/wallet position; the real competitive pressure lands on local wallets Payme/Click/Uzum, whose in-app-payment UX moat erodes if NFC tap-to-pay via Apple/Google becomes ubiquitous (analysis).

**Comps & multiples.** No valuation attaches to this news (Apple/Google are mega-caps; the event is a market entry, not a deal), so peer multiples are contextual, keyed off the regional payments champions.
- **Kaspi.kz (Kazakhstan super-app)** — the clearest regional benchmark: ~$18.0bn market cap at $94.91 on 14 Oct 2026; P/E ~8.1x, P/S ~1.94x (per stockanalysis/Yahoo). A low single-digit P/E signals the market pricing in Central-Asia country/FX risk despite >54% margins — the valuation discount the whole region carries.
- **Uzum (Uzbekistan's first & only unicorn)** — $2.3bn pre-money round valuation, Oman-SWF-led $130m, Mar 2026, up ~53% from $1.5bn (Aug 2025); eyeing 2026/27 IPO (per TechCrunch, PitchBook, bne IntelliNews). Round valuation, not market cap; revenue not public → EV/Rev "no data".
- Internal comps: [[Tencent-backed Uzum weighs London listing for 2027 IPO]]; [[Eurasian Development Bank extends $70 million loan to Uzum]]; [[Alipay+ and Uzbekistan’s HUMO partner on cross-border QR payments]]; [[Kazakhstan launches interbank QR payment service]]; [[Mastercard cashless transactions in Kazakhstan rise 34%]] ($1.6bn Jan-2026 Kazakh Mastercard TPV, +14% YoY); [[Apple Pay cuts $1 billion fraud, expands to 89 markets]] (Apple Pay's 89-market footprint pre-Uzbekistan).
- Distribution not computed (mixed listed/private, revenue undisclosed) → qualitative comparison only.

**Risk flags.**
1. **Domestic-scheme support unconfirmed** — if Humo/Uzcard aren't live at launch, Apple/Google Pay reach only Visa/Mastercard cards, a minority of the 68m+ card base; the "significant event" collapses to a niche convenience feature (second-order: weak initial traction could delay full rollout).
2. **Announced ≠ live** — Google Pay is officially launched but the bank/card list is still unpublished, and Apple Pay has no official confirmation and Uzbekistan isn't yet on Apple's country list; the 27 Oct date rests on local-media sourcing only. De-PR: treat as "announced/partial," not fully adopted.
3. **Disintermediation of local wallets** — ubiquitous Apple/Google NFC tap-to-pay erodes the UX differentiation of Payme/Click/Uzum, shifting a margin/engagement layer to US platforms; also a regulatory/data-sovereignty tail risk if localization rules are revisited.

**What this changes (idea-lens).** This is a new-entry/infrastructure event, not a re-rating: it lowers the activation barrier for NFC/contactless in a market still card-heavy, and sets up a two-front contest — international wallets vs. domestic super-apps (Uzum/Payme/Click) for the payment surface, with Humo/Uzcard as the pivotal rails (analysis). Falsifiable thesis: if Humo/Uzcard are supported within ~3 months of launch, contactless penetration and Apple/Google wallet activations accelerate sharply; trigger to watch — publication of the supported-banks/cards list and confirmation of Apple Pay go-live. Thesis breaks if the launch stays Visa/Mastercard-only or Apple's date slips with no country-list addition.
