Anthropic is expanding its Claude for Financial Advisors platform through new integrations with FINNY, Jump, and Conquest. For fintech professionals, this represents a significant push toward vertical-specific AI agents that consolidate prospecting, meeting context, and financial planning into a single interface, reducing the friction of switching between disparate legacy systems and modern AI tools.

Anthropic has unveiled the next phase of its Claude for Financial Advisors initiative, introducing deep integrations with three key wealthtech partners: FINNY, Jump, and Conquest. These partnerships are designed to embed prospecting intelligence, client meeting context, and verifiable financial planning capabilities directly into the Claude AI interface. By utilizing these integrations, advisors can access critical client data and execute actions across their existing tech stack without the need to toggle between multiple platforms.

FINNY, an AI growth engine, has introduced its Model Context Protocol (MCP) connector. This allows advisors to analyze marketing campaign performance and enrich prospect data directly within Claude. Jump is expanding its footprint within the ecosystem through purpose-built skills that help advisors leverage client meeting notes and track decisions made during conversations. This functionality is intended to automate the drafting of follow-up communications by pulling context from intake forms and documents.

Finally, Conquest has integrated its SAM (Strategic Advice Manager®) engine into the platform. This provides advisors with access to client financial information and goal progress tracking. Unlike generative models that may hallucinate, Conquest’s engine provides deterministic calculations, allowing advisors to verify projections in real time. These tools collectively aim to provide a more unified digital workspace for wealth management professionals, allowing them to respond to client needs with data-backed insights without leaving their primary AI workspace.

Anthropic, the developer of the Claude AI model, has positioned itself as a primary competitor in the enterprise AI space, focusing on safety and specialized applications for professional sectors. The company has seen significant traction in the financial services market, recently expanding its reach into asset and wealth management to address specific regulatory and operational needs.

FINNY operates as an AI-driven growth engine specifically tailored for financial advisors, focusing on automating the prospecting and marketing lifecycle. The company provides tools that help firms scale their organic growth by identifying and engaging potential clients more efficiently through data enrichment.

Jump, accessible via jump.ai, provides a specialized context layer for client interactions. It focuses on capturing and organizing the "unstructured" data of wealth management—specifically the commitments and decisions made during live meetings and through various intake documents. This helps advisors maintain a continuous record of client intent.

Conquest is a wealthtech firm known for its Strategic Advice Manager (SAM), a deterministic engine designed to provide verifiable financial advice. Unlike general-purpose AI, Conquest’s technology is built on mathematical models to ensure that financial projections and goal-tracking data remain accurate and auditable for regulatory compliance.

This move signals a shift from general-purpose AI chatbots toward highly verticalized "workstations" for financial professionals. By integrating deterministic engines like Conquest alongside generative models, Anthropic is addressing the primary hurdle for AI adoption in finance: accuracy and verifiability. This puts significant pressure on legacy CRM providers and standalone financial planning software that lack native, deep AI integration. The industry must now grapple with whether advisors will prefer a "single pane of glass" provided by an AI orchestrator like Claude, or if they will continue to treat AI as a peripheral tool. Original Press Release

Anthropic has announced the next phase of **Claude for Financial Advisors**, introducing new integrations with **FINNY**, **Jump**, and **Conquest**. These partnerships bring prospecting intelligence, client meeting context, and financial planning capabilities directly into the Claude AI interface.

As financial advisors increasingly turn to AI to streamline their workflows, these integrations make it easier to access critical client information and take action across the tools they already use, without constantly switching between platforms.

**FINNY**, the AI growth engine for financial advisors, has launched its Model Context Protocol (MCP) connector. This allows advisors to analyze campaign performance, enrich prospect data, and access FINNY’s knowledge base directly within Claude.

"Advisors are increasingly turning to AI, and we want FINNY to be available in Claude, where they're already operating," said Eden Ovadia, co-founder and CEO of FINNY. "Our goal has always been to make organic growth easier and more accessible, and adding FINNY to Claude allows advisors to incorporate prospecting data, campaign insights and growth opportunities into their existing workflow without adding friction."

**Jump** is expanding its existing Claude integration with purpose-built skills. These help advisors leverage client meeting notes, track commitments and decisions, and draft follow-up communications without manually copying and pasting data.

"Advisors get the best output from Claude when it understands their clients," said Parker Ence, chief executive officer and co-founder of Jump. "Jump captures rich context from client conversations, documents, intake forms and more. Our connector and purpose-built skills in Claude for Financial Advisors give advisors another way to put that context to work. Whether they’re preparing for a meeting, tracking commitments they made or looking up details from a financial statement, advisors can use Jump and Claude together to do more for their clients."

**Conquest** has integrated its verifiable AI financial advice engine, SAM (Strategic Advice Manager®), into Claude. This enables advisors to access client financial information, check goal progress, and identify planning opportunities backed by deterministic calculations.

"Advisors are increasingly using AI to enhance the client experience, but the underlying data has to be accurate," said Rob Crnkovic, Chief Revenue Officer of Conquest. "Conquest was built to show up wherever advisors and their clients already are, rather than asking them to log into yet another system. Connecting Conquest data with Claude will empower advisors to respond to clients in real time, and every projection can be verified. That’s why Conquest is at the forefront in democratizing quality financial advice."

"Great financial advice starts with knowing the client, and Claude for Financial Advisors helps advisors put that knowledge to work across the tools they use every day," said Peter Nolan, head of asset and wealth management at Anthropic. "Bringing the goals, commitments and decisions Jump captures from client conversations into Claude can help advisors prepare for meetings and follow up with clients."
