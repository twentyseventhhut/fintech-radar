# Carta secures Luxembourg fund administrator authorisation

> [!info] 2026-10-13 · 1 упоминаний · 0 источника(ов) с текстом
> Каналы: Connecting the Dots in Fintech

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇱🇺 Carta secures authorisation to operate as a fund administrator and transfer agent in Luxembourg. The approval from regulator CSSF allows fund managers to combine fund administration, investor onboarding and reporting on Carta's existing platform, supporting cross-border fund operations through a single system.
