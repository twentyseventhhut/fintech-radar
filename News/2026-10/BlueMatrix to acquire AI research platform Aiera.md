Thoma Bravo-backed BlueMatrix to Acquire Aiera, Advancing Governed AI Distribution for Investment Research
Combination creates an AI-ready infrastructure layer connecting research providers worldwide to client AI systems, with governance, attribution, and controls
NEW YORK and MIAMI, Oct. 8, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- BlueMatrix , opens in a new tab. , the global leader in capital markets content publishing technology, announced that it has entered into an agreement to acquire Aiera , opens in a new tab. , a consortium-backed content delivery platform, to bring research authoring, governance and AI distribution together on a single stack. BlueMatrix 's acquisition of Aiera is backed by Thoma Bravo, the world's largest software-focused investment firm and the company's majority shareholder. Financial terms were not disclosed.
The combination answers one of the defining questions for research in the AI era: how can research move efficiently into buy-side AI systems while carrying clear authorship, governance and attribution? Investment managers today navigate multiple platforms to access governed research from different providers. BlueMatrix brings an established research authoring and governed AI distribution network serving more than 1,000 financial institutions and over 7,000 analysts worldwide. Aiera's technology, built with the backing of leading global research providers, offers AI-ready infrastructure for permissioned, transparently sourced and auditable access to proprietary financial content. Together, the companies intend to combine Aiera's technology and BlueMatrix's reach across the entire universe of published research, spanning broker research, expert insights, news, live events, transcripts and filings. The combined company brings the ecosystem together around a single, governed standard for research distribution in the AI era.
"When AI consumes research, questions around attribution, intellectual property and entitlements become critical," said David Nable, Chief Executive Officer of BlueMatrix. "Governed AI consumption requires research that is structured for machine-readable delivery, carries declared rights and persistent attribution from the point of authoring, and produces an auditable record of how it is consumed. With Aiera, we can extend AI distribution on terms set by the provider, governed from the point of authoring through to the query, while expanding our reach across the industry. We are excited to welcome the Aiera team to BlueMatrix."
"Aiera was built with many of the world's leading financial institutions around a simple conviction: AI would fundamentally change how investment research is discovered, analyzed and consumed, and the industry would need trusted infrastructure to make that transition possible," said Ken Sena, Chief Executive Officer of Aiera. "We've spent years building that access layer, with the governance, attribution and controls research providers require. Bringing Aiera together with BlueMatrix's global research network, and the backing of Thoma Bravo, gives us the opportunity to take that vision to an entirely new scale."
"Institutional investors are building AI capabilities at a rapid pace, and demand for research they can license into those systems has never been stronger," said Adam Solomon , a Partner at Thoma Bravo. "Our work with many of the world's leading financial institutions gives us direct visibility into this need, and we see significant opportunity for the combined company to address it. We are excited to support David, Ken and their teams as they drive expansion and innovation across the business."
The transaction is expected to close in Q4 2026, subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals.
 About BlueMatrix  
 BlueMatrix , opens in a new tab.  is the global leader in capital markets content publishing technology, trusted by over 1,000 financial institutions. Its secure platform streamlines authoring, compliance and distribution – driving smarter, faster collaboration across capital markets. Founded in 1999 and backed by Thoma Bravo, BlueMatrix operates from offices in Durham (HQ), New York, London, Paris, Edinburgh, Auckland and Timisoara.
 About Aiera  
 Aiera , opens in a new tab.  is an AI-enabled financial research platform that provides compliant, platform-agnostic access to proprietary financial content. Built in collaboration with leading buy-side and sell-side institutions, Aiera delivers permissioned, transparently sourced and auditable access to broker research, expert insights, live events, transcripts, filings, documents and news across the AI tools, internal platforms and research workflows institutions already use. Aiera is backed by ten leading global research providers, alongside lead investors Fin Capital and Flybridge Capital Partners.
