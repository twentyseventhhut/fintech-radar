## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Connecting the Dots in Fintech] 🇧🇩 UCB, Mastercard and Grameenphone introduce co-branded payment sticker. The prepaid sticker can be attached to a smartphone or another item, allowing users to make contactless payments by tapping it at POS terminals. Cardholders can also access cashback, rewards and other benefits.
