# MGKL shares and bonds plunge up to 25% in two sessions

## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[Финтехно] За 26–27 октября акции МГКЛ, владельца сети «Мосгорломбард», потеряли почти 25%, а отдельные выпуски облигаций — до 23% за одну торговую сессию. Доходность одного из выпусков в моменте превысила 90% — инвесторы стали оценивать риск невозврата долга гораздо выше.

[Финтехно] Сама МГКЛ утверждает, что продолжает работать в обычном режиме.

[Финтехно] ⬛️ Ещё летом у инвесторов появились вопросы к стоимости приобретённых МГКЛ компаний. В отчётности группы отражено 2,28 млрд рублей гудвилла — ожидаемых экономических выгод от покупок, сумма которых практически равна капиталу акционеров. Если приобретённые бизнесы окажутся менее ценными, чем предполагается, капитал группы может существенно сократиться.

[Финтехно] ⬛️ В сентябре «Эксперт РА» понизило рейтинг МГКЛ до ruB из-за высокой долговой нагрузки и вопросов к корпоративному управлению. Затем рейтинг был отозван по инициативе компании.

[Финтехно] За первое полугодие выручка увеличилась в 2,7 раза, до 27 млрд рублей, чистая прибыль достигла 553 млн. То есть компания зарабатывает на бумаге, но её текущая деятельность пока требует больше денег, чем приносит.

# Context-enrichment: MGKL shares and bonds plunge up to 25% in two sessions
_Analytical notes (not a post). Importance: 3/5._

## [0] What exactly happened (de-PR'd)
Over 26-27 Oct 2026, shares of ПАО «МГКЛ» (MGKL / Mosgorlombard group, MOEX ticker MGKL, 3rd-tier listing) fell ~25% in two sessions to an all-time low of ₽1.41 (down ~27% over the week; a seventh straight down session). On 28 Oct they fell a further ~10% to ₽1.26. Bonds dropped 4.6-29.2% across issues, with yields blowing out to 38.7-89.6% annualized — i.e. the market is now pricing a real probability of default/restructuring, not just a repricing.

The proximate trigger is NOT an earnings miss or dilution/SPO. It is that the Federal Tax Service (ФНС) blocked the bank accounts of **eight of MGKL's eleven subsidiaries** over tax arrears totalling ~₽1.22bn, with bankruptcy petitions filed by the tax authority against several of them. Per Vedomosti (citing FNS data), blocked entities include ООО «Звезда» (₽381m / in another source ₽319m arrears; bankruptcy case А40-205713/26 accepted 22 Jul 2026), ООО «Технотренд» (₽171.9m; bankruptcy case А40-181837/26 accepted 3 Jul 2026), ООО «Комиссионный-2» (₽381.9m), ООО «Главмосторг» (₽124.8m), ООО «Комиссионный-1» (₽140.5m), plus smaller blocks on «Команда МГКЛ» (₽1.6m), «Ломбард Аквамарин» (₽5.8m), «Ресейл» (₽14m), and АО «Ресейл-анти» (76.92% owned; ₽5.3m).

**Why this structure matters / what it reveals.** MGKL's response — that «Команда МГКЛ»'s debt is "technical" and will be repaid, and that bankruptcy proceedings on «Технотренд» and «Ресейл» were terminated, while declining to comment on the rest — does not address the core investor fear. The fear is not a one-off tax bill; it is that the subsidiaries carry the group's recently-acquired "businesses," and those same subsidiaries are the ones blocked / in bankruptcy and were at the centre of the summer goodwill controversy. Two red flags compound: (1) tax arrears of ₽1.22bn were accumulating since spring 2026 (bankruptcy petitions date to July) yet surfaced to the market only now — a disclosure-quality problem; (2) the market reaction (seven down days, 90% bond yields) signals investors fear the reported ₽553m H1 net profit and 2.7x revenue growth to ₽27bn are optically flattered by consolidation of entities that may not be genuine standalone businesses.

## [1] Competitors / peers
MGKL is Russia's first and only publicly-listed pawnshop/recommerce group (IPO Dec 2023 at ₽2.5/sh, ~₽303m raised). There is no direct listed pure-play peer on MOEX, so comparables are (a) other high-yield 2nd/3rd-tier MOEX issuers whose bonds reprice on governance/default scares, and (b) the broader Russian ломбард (pawn) and ресейл (recommerce) sector, which is fragmented and mostly private. Internally the corpus has no prior MGKL note; adjacent themes are gold-collateral lending ([[India's organised gold loan market reaches ₹11.8 lakh crore]], [[RBI updates Gold Metal Loans regulations]]) and Russian capital-markets stress under sanctions ([[Telegram's $500m Russian bonds frozen under sanctions]]) — useful only as backdrop, not comps.

**Why the lay of the land is this way / second-order.** MGKL's "first listed pawnshop" status was its equity-story premium — scarcity value as a proxy for a cash-generative, counter-cyclical pawn/gold franchise in a high-rate economy. That scarcity cuts both ways: with no peer to anchor valuation, the stock trades on narrative and disclosure trust, so a governance/tax shock de-rates it faster and harder than a name with established comps. The ~90% bond yield vs equity down "only" ~35% is itself a signal: fixed-income investors (via the АВО bondholders' association) are driving the scrutiny, and credit markets are usually the first to price solvency risk.

## [2] Company history / fit
Mosgorlombard traces a ~100-year brand history; the modern group runs three lines — pawn lending (brand «Мосгорломбард»), wholesale gold/precious-metal buying and refining («Лот-Золото»), and recommerce/resale («Ресейл-Маркет»). It IPO'd on MOEX in late 2023 and has been an active bond issuer: ~8 exchange-bond issues, ~₽5.6bn debt outstanding entering 2026, a 5bn-ruble bond programme registered on SPB Exchange (Nov 2025), and a ₽7.6bn five-year convertible bond placement. Expert RA cut the rating to **ruBB-** in Sep 2025, then to **ruB** in Sep 2026 citing high leverage and corporate-governance concerns; MGKL then terminated the contract and the rating was withdrawn (raexpert releases sep14d / sep12e).

The summer 2026 flashpoint: the Bondholders' Association (АВО) wrote to CBR chair Nabiullina (letter filed ~31 Aug 2026) questioning the ₽2.28-2.3bn of goodwill on the group's books — a sum roughly equal to the group's entire shareholders' equity. The letter highlights ООО «Ломбард Аквамарин» (goodwill ₽973m), bought Jan 2026 from a **related party** for ₽941m despite **negative net assets (-₽32m)**, where the whole operating perimeter was already controlled by MGKL — raising whether these should be consolidated under IFRS 10 (early control, no real acquired business) rather than IFRS 3, and asking CBR to audit FBK's review of the H1-2026 and FY-2025 statements.

**Why the company acts this way.** A pawn/gold take-rate business is low-multiple and capital-hungry (H1 operating cash flow was **-₽2.25bn** despite book profit, as cash went into inventory/pledged stock). To justify an equity multiple and keep issuing bonds, management needed the headline "2.7x revenue, record profit" growth — which the acquisition/consolidation of related-party entities conveniently delivered. The structural pressure (commodity take-rate → needs a growth narrative to fund debt) is exactly what makes aggressive, goodwill-heavy related-party M&A attractive to management and dangerous to minority holders.

## [3] Novelty / value-add / traction
This is a genuinely new, discrete event (the FNS account blocks + the acute 26-28 Oct repricing), not a reprint of the summer goodwill story — though it is the second-order detonation of that same fault line. The "value" to assess is really downside: the market is testing whether MGKL's reported franchise is real cash generation or an accounting construct.

**Why the downside is real, deeper.** Three independent signals now point the same way: (1) **Cash vs profit gap** — book profit ₽553m but operating cash flow -₽2.25bn; earnings are non-cash/working-capital-funded. (2) **Goodwill ≈ equity** — if the acquired "businesses" (now the blocked/bankrupt subsidiaries) are impaired, equity can be largely wiped, which is precisely why bonds, not just equity, are selling off. (3) **Governance trail** — rating cut then self-withdrawn, related-party purchase of a negative-net-asset company at a premium, and tax arrears quietly accruing since spring. Who captures value / who is at risk: minority equity and bondholders bear the gap between reported and economic earnings; the related parties captured value upfront via the ₽941m purchase. The multiple (scarcity-premium "first listed pawnshop") is exactly what breaks when disclosure trust evaporates.

## [4] What's next / market sentiment
Near-term catalysts: CBR's response to the АВО audit request (could force restatement/non-consolidation of Aquamarin → equity hit); resolution/escalation of the eight subsidiary account blocks and the live bankruptcy cases (А40-205713/26, А40-181837/26); and whether the ₽1.22bn arrears are actually cleared as "technical." Sentiment is sharply negative — all-time-low equity, ~38-90% bond yields, no credit rating. Company line is "operations continue normally; debts being resolved with tax authorities" — a liquidity/solvency reassurance that the market is discounting.

**Why the market may go this way / counterintuitive second-order.** The counterintuitive effect: MGKL's headline growth (2.7x revenue) is the *source* of fragility, not strength — because that growth was delivered through goodwill-heavy related-party consolidation now under tax and audit fire. A clean pawn/gold business in a high-rate economy should be counter-cyclical and resilient; the de-rating says investors no longer trust the reported numbers to reflect that underlying business. Base case is continued volatility until CBR/auditor clarity; tail risk is a consolidation-perimeter restatement that impairs goodwill and triggers bond covenants/restructuring. (analysis)
