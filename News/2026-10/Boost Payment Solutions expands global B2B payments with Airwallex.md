Boost's payment volume processed through Airwallex grew nearly 10x in five months as commercial partnership streamlines supplier onboarding, improves payment performance and helps lower processing costs 
 NEW YORK , October 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Boost Payment Solutions , a global leader in B2B payments, today announced a commercial partnership with Airwallex, the AI native financial operating system for modern businesses, to further enhance its international commercial card processing capabilities. The collaboration will help Boost streamline supplier onboarding and simplify cross-border payment processing.
Boost has built a global proprietary B2B payments platform designed to help businesses send and receive commercial card payments globally with greater efficiency, visibility and control. Through the commercial partnership, Airwallex is helping Boost build its existing international footprint by providing global processing infrastructure and acquiring capabilities that support Boost's continued expansion into new markets.
The integration combines the complementary strengths of both companies. Airwallex's global infrastructure supports a more streamlined and reliable processing experience across markets, while Boost brings specialized expertise in B2B and large-ticket commercial card transactions, helping eligible payments qualify for more favorable interchange rates and optimizing scheme fees. Together, the companies are creating a more consistent and efficient B2B card payment experience globally.
The Boost and Airwallex integration provides:
 Streamlined onboarding: A digital-first enablement experience that can bring international suppliers live in as few as two business days
 More timely and reliable payments: Lower payment failure rates, fewer retried transactions and continued optimization around on-time settlement
 Lower processing costs: Greater ability for eligible high-value transactions to qualify for favorable large-ticket interchange rates, alongside optimized scheme fees across European payment flows
 Improved reporting: More consistent, enriched transaction and remittance data across global payment volume
The commercial partnership has already seen significant adoption. Over a five-month period, Boost's processed payment volume through Airwallex grew nearly ten-fold, with more than 270 supplier accounts onboarded during that period.
"Global B2B payments shouldn't become more complicated simply because a business crosses a border," said Dean M. Leavitt, Founder and CEO of Boost Payment Solutions. "Boost has built a global platform designed around the unique needs of B2B payments, and our partnership with Airwallex allows us to make the infrastructure behind that experience even more scalable and consistent. By combining their global processing capabilities with our specialized B2B expertise, we can continue raising the bar for the customers and suppliers we serve."
"B2B payments are inherently complex, particularly when businesses are operating across multiple markets. Airwallex's embedded payments solutions are built to remove that complexity and provide the infrastructure they need to grow," said Philipp Reichardt, VP of Enterprises at Airwallex. "The nearly ten-fold growth in payment volume we've processed for Boost in just five months shows what's possible when Airwallex's global processing rails are combined with Boost's expertise in B2B payments."
 About Boost Payment Solutions 
Boost Payment Solutions is the global leader in B2B payments, with a technology platform that seamlessly serves the needs of today's commercial trading partners. Boost's patented technology solutions bridge the needs of buyers and suppliers around the world, eliminating friction and delivering process efficiency, payment security, data insights and revenue optimization. Founded in 2009, Boost operates in 180+ countries. For more information, visit www.boostb2b.com .
 About Airwallex 
Airwallex is the AI native financial operating system for modern businesses. We are building the future of global banking for a real-time, intelligent economy. We serve more than 675,000 businesses worldwide, either directly or through our platform customers. From startups to multinationals, our customers use Airwallex to manage their financial operations, or to build and monetize their own financial products.
Founded in Melbourne in 2015, Airwallex holds 85+ licenses across North and Latin America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, forming one of the most comprehensive financial infrastructures in the world. This regulated backbone powers Airwallex products at global scale, including payment acceptance, billing, global accounts, corporate cards, and spend management.
