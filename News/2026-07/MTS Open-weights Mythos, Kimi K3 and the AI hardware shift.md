## Агрегированный текст (из дайджестов)

[MTS] The reaction to the Mythos announcement and the eventual release of Fable 5 is already becoming hard to remember. A month ago, Andrew Curran wrote The Window Has Closed. He said what many other people were thinking at the time: Mythos represents such a significant step forward in capabilities that it might be, on its own, a source of durable advantage. He quotes the leaked Anthropic Series C deck, which says “We believe that companies that train the best 2025/26 models will be too far ahead for anyone to catch up in subsequent cycles”.
